Tradeskills Issue- Combining items that make No Drop item
The game needs to give a warning if 2 regular items are combined that will make a No Drop item!! I wasted 7 mil on an item last night that when combined with another item created a No Drop item. There was no warning or anything. The nice engineer that was helping me was as shocked as I was.

So weeks of work and 7 million credits are wiped away in a second of criminally bad design!

So, please add a box that says "These items when combined create a No Drop item. Do you want to do this?" Simple and easy and will save many people horrid losses. I was so angry I almost cancelled my account on the spot. This doesn't have to happen with one easy little fix.

Please look into it.

No, I was making the upgrade of the Solar Power Mechanic Pistol. I know the pistol itself is No Drop. And this is the 3rd upgrade level. When combining the Hacked Electrical Engineering Tutoring Device to the Disposal Unit Electrical Toolset (very expensive rare item) it creates a No Drop item! Now I can understand the gun is No Drop. But previous steps did not create No Drop items so why is this one? No warning, no mention anywhere. The other Engineer that was helping me with this one step was as surprised as I was.

But besides the point of pissing me off so much I almost cancelled my account. This is a feature that should be in anyway. When 2 normal items make a No Drop item there should be a warning. I don't think I'm asking for alot. I can imagine hundreds of players have experienced what I have. When it doesn't make sense and doesn't follow logic then I can only assume it was criminally poor design.

But I'm sure it was done out of a rushed work schedule. It sounds to me that Funcom is so under staffed and overwhelmed by Shadowlands that designing a minor item probably was an afterthought. For all I know it was a mistake or a data entry error that got missed. Whatever it was it cost me 2 weeks of work and 7 million credits lost.

And one more rant before I go off and cool down. Why are so many items No Drop now. In the old days this wasn't the case. This is not EQ (though it becomes more with each new addition). In EQ a level 1 char could equip a level 60 item. But in this game there are QL & stat reqs. on all the items. So why bother with No Drops. All you are doing is making Tradeskillers even more gimped. Since I'm playing an engineer I've finally started getting into tradeskills. But the lack of experience gained and so many interesting items No Drop now doesn't make me too interested. The engineer pistol is a perfect example. Here's a gun that anyone can equip but only engineers and traders have any hope to make with the stiff skill requirements. But its no drop. Wouldn't it be a nice item to make for players and sell? That's what tradeskillers do. But we can't. Why did they bother allowing anyone to make it if most people can't. It seems like a perfect item to make for players, but again we can't.

So to the designer of the engineer pistol, what were you thinking?
