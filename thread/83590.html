PVP MP Destroyed by MEZZ!
Us MP's all know how devistating mezz can be in a pvp situation... 3/12 profs have mezz.. not including the enforcer fear line on nanos.

1. MP should have a line of nanos to cast on pets that raise nano resist by a large amount on pets. This should have been done anyway with the anima line of nanos. I believe anima have the same type of affect as the enforcer "Rage".

2. Mezzing my heal pet? What foolishness is that?

3. Mezz pet is a waste of ip.

4. Mezz is insta cast for many chars.. and works as a stun for a good 6-8minutes. That is one useless MP.

5. The pets are crazy slow. Its not funny when an MP can run faster than his/her pet. I love watching my pet worm around for 10 seconds after i tell him to attack... just enough time for those mezz, root, snare classes to insta cast whatever they want a couple hundred times.

5. Of all the variety of nanos that mp's have.. (sarcasm).. our nano resist is blue and 4 points per lvl. Give us a self cast nano resist line. A nano class like MP has no root.. no mezz.. no snare.. no nano init debuff.. no area effect nanos. What we have instead are 3 pets.. 3 of which can be mezzed, rooted, snared, left in the dust by crazy runspeed, scared away, charmed and that cant hold an agro. We also get fully blue ranged skills.. blue melee skills.. blue inits.. blue nano resist.. blue dodge skills.. ranged clusters that fit right in the way of our nano skill clusters. And 1 "mezz" pet that does absolutely nothing in pvp and pvm.

MP's are walking punching bags.. if they and their pets (pets more importantly) cant resist nanos. As it is.. an mp in a large pvp battle situations must focus intently on his target to beat him.. ie: removing his use of nano.. before anything else can be done. We cant afford to be watching the other 9 people in the immediate vicinity who are able to put our pets out of comission. Its bad enough that debuffs stop us from commanding our pets...

I hope you have a plan in mind funcom. While raising the bar in pvp and pvm for other classes, you effectively ++gimp gimp gimp others.. the worst of which being the pet classes.
