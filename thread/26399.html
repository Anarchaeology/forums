And the Clan Guards will shot you on sight.

If you can't use the grid and don't want to do the walk through Broken Shores do the following:

Use the whompa in omni-ent to 4 Holes. Run north until you Zone. Then you are in Stret West Bank. In Stret West Bank is a zone called Holes in the Wall (somewhere northwest). From there you can Zone to Borealis. The Wildlife in SWB and 4 Holes isn't as dangerous as in Broken Shores. But for a lvl 9 it's dangerous enough But you don't have the Clan Guards on this route.
