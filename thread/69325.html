A Thesis of Law
There he stood again, in front of the glaring terminal. He slipped in a card, his name flashing on the screen. He pushed in a few buttons, and uploaded a document. He then removed the card and looked at what flashed on the screen:

It has been brought to my attention and I am finally ready to begin with my statements on the current state of law in Rubi-Ka. I believe it is mandatory to consider what is being said in these theses. Law in Rubi-Ka is corrupt, tarnished, and cannot be fixed unless a civilian base is established to repair its flaws:

The Thesis of Reynaldo Monilith Griffen
An examination of law

On this planet, this beautiful planet on which so many call home, there exists an evil not apparent to those not looking. Through the beautiful wildlife, the astounding sunsets, the amazing landscapes, we overlook the law. Does anyone take into consideration the law outside of the common eye for an eye?

For example, we'll take Omni-Tek. Look at the legal system which exists: nothing. The only law we know is if you are accused of something, you did it. Punishments are death only, and even though insurance exists, death is still a terrifying procedure that is not to be played with through a game of anarchy.

And that is what exists on Rubi-Ka. Pure anarchy, where laws are over-shadowed by the actions of the people, where corruption runs through the streets in the form of blood, where people die and people live with the constant fear of death and prosecution without representation. I still cannot look past the fact that people are constantly being accused of crimes that they might not have committed. I laugh at the integrity and sureness that Omni-Pol, Omni-Admin, and all the branches of Omni-Tek law have in their accusations.

Many years ago, on old earth, this very problem existed. Colonists, just like us, discovered a colonized land under the rule of a tyrant. But, unlike us, they fought and they WON their rights; their right to be represented, their right to be heard by a group of their peers and the right to be set free without fear of death or malicious prosecution, because they knew they were and were proved to be innocent. Know that, even if you know you are innocent, no one on Rubi-Ka cares to hear it. We unconditionally believe what authority tells us, and assume it to be truth.

This may sound quite drastic, but in no way is it thus. There may come a time in the future when you come home from a hard days work and are met with force. You are met with rifle barrels in your face, while people crowd around you, and you become a public embarrassment. You may be arrested without being told for the specific charge, without being told who accused you, and without the right to retain representation.

I am not here to be a radical. I am here to reveal the truth about the current state of anarchy that exists on Rubi-Ka. It doesn't matter if you support Omni-Tek, if you follow the Clans, or if you remain neutral. There will come a time when you will doubt the authorities and that will be a time when I will seek you out. Law is a precious thing, and law is impossible to forced upon a society: it must be accepted.

And law, in its current state, is not benefiting you. It is benefiting those who just want to fulfill their own ends. It is benefiting those who want to grab anyone off the streets at any time and accuse them of a crime they didn't commit just to prove they are always right.

And that brings me back to old earth. Many authors of the time also considered this, also understood this, and they were able without persecution to write down their feelings. Many authors touched on the fact that society could not survive into the distant future at the rate it was going, and they were absolutely right. The depiction of the future is accurate, because all of their novels stated that the future was an outright pathetic thing of corruption and haste.

But, criminals do exist. We cannot just let them go, and I don't hope to turn this into a document that they will claim to their benefit. This is why law is a process that is hard to accept. But, no matter if they are criminal or not, they deserve representation. Under what I hope is eventually established, under a glorious law system, all will have the right to be represented.

But I have talked enough about individuals. How will law benefit guilds and groups? Quite simply, law will be your mediator. All guilds have disputes, and through a fair system of justice I would hope law could settle them. Be it trade disputes, espionage, or any other conceivable dispute, it can be settled under law.

In closing, what I hope law will do is simple, and I quote from an old earth document, is simple:

Establish justice, insure domestic tranquility, provide for the common defense, promote the general welfare, secure the blessings of liberty, to ourselves and our prosperity -The United States Constitution

And with that, I do not want law to become an iron fist. I want it to become a flexible substance in which all workings of government can be sifted through.

With that, he turned around, card in hand, and walked off.
