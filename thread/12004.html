Avalon is the name of the zone, Camelot is the name of the city.

For a clan, once you take the whompa, look for the castle wall directly in front of you. Take the ramp up, through the gate, and over the lava moat, and you are there.

Camelot Castle is in two parts, When you first enter, you will be in 100% suppression gas, in the Knights living area. Obviously it will be perfectly safe to explore, as the only thing that can hurt you is your own stupidity (like jumping off a stairway, or something to that effect). To get to the actual area of adventure, you need to find the literal dungeon. There will be a dorway with a starway down, and you will know you are in the right place as the suppression gas will drop to 25% once you are on the stairs.

Yes, it is a political zone, so Omni and Clan visitors have nothing preventing them from shooting each other, other than the bigger threat.
