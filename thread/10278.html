First, I didn't mongo and heal/renano before the fight, I think that's cheap, enforcers already have a huge advantage. However I did mongo right off the bat once the fight was started, I was fighting a trader after all I expected to lose all my nano abilities. Tamuril imidiately began with the roots and I cast Beserk Rage, I had to sacrifice a lot for this nano and I love it, it buffs your speed and increases nano rez, and hits you with a 5 pt dot (to remove roots). This nano invariably removes the root fairly quickly and I was able to keep in contact with Tamuril regularly throughout the fight. I have 450 nano points, with that I can mongo and cast rage numerous times thruout a fight, aided by my use of ql 20 nano first aid kits. I used this tactic repeatedly, any time my health was 300 pts below max and my mongo wasn't healing anymore I remongo'd. Any time I was rooted I cast Rage and made it free in good time.

I killed Tamuril after a reasonably long duel. It wasn't an unfair duel, Tamuril is as twinked as I, she's wearing a coat, and she has a rookie title. She also specified before the fight that I wasn't to run away, which I took to mean she was used to fighting and winning and didn't want me running away on her. I never run anyways, dueling in the arena involves honor for me, running isn't permissable during a duel. I don't duel newb's, they arn't any real challenge, and when I win I'm gracious and chat with whomever afterward. I answer questions, I give tips, I tell them exactly how I beat them. When I lose I'm equally gracious, I congratulate the winner and enjoy the fact that I've encountered a new challenge.

After the duel while Tamuril/Doracles was making these acusations I even proved that I hadn't used any debuffs. I removed all the buffs I had, and stood and turned around to show that there were no nano effects on me. If there had been debuffs used I would have still had the little shiny nano effects circling my body. And yet Tamuril/Doracles wouldn't accept my innocence and continued with his accusations.

Tamuril wasn't played very well is the sad fact. A trader could have denied me both my mongo, AND my rage easily and yet I was never deprived or ransacked. But because Tamuril/Doracles can't seem to lose graciously I am forced to come here to present my side of the story. I have seen a number of Quizotic members posting on the boards and I encourage you to get Doracles to present his side of the story. I've never had any problems with the quiz before, Nishiki is even my friend, I don't wish for this to become anything more then it is.

Good day, and good hunting fellow rubikans.
