LTK
You can't stop the signal
Peace of the Air
To swim is a gift
and so says me
To roll with the waves
To break with the crests
the surface drift
is bliss you see
from here I can only
stand and wonder
Why is the land
so far from me?
Even though it's
beneath my feet it's
part of a thing
I cannot be.
In this aged
place of wonder

Peace of the air,
I want to be
Part of the land and
Part of the ocean

Wind does blow but
can't be seen
can't be dealt with
only sheltered
Wish I could be
part of that peace.
turning when wind
currents falter
Wish I could be
land air and sea
part of everything
what makes me
but I can only
stare and dream.
eyes grow wide with
hope and wonder

Peace of the air,
I want to be
Part of the land and
Part of the ocean

--Lan Tarryk Kozar
