After last patch, the chest loot is VERY different.
In chests(the few that aren't empty):
Newland/Mort maps.
Computer belts and components (NCU, CPU upgrades, Coolant sinks, etc)
Trimmers.
Vehicles.
House stuff (plants, fans, watebaskets etc)
Rings of power.

All the above stuff used to be extremely rare in chests.
Mostly, it was:
Weapons, Ammo, Armor, Tradeskill items, Nano crystals.

The items dropped by mobs seem to be more or less the same.

It looks like they decided to make rare loots not so rare by reducing the chance of getting other loot, and either on purpose or by accident turned armor, NC, weapon, ammo and TS item chances to 0. Newland/Mort maps had same chance, but their rate was not adjusted.

I hope this loot screw-up is accidental. They probably only wanted to even loot out a bit. Completely cutting armor/weapons/crystals from loot tables on purpose seems too heavy a change in missions dynamics to be left unmentioned in patch description.
