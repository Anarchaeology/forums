Inquiry to Funcom on the state of Tradeskills
#1) Profession skill sets

The allocation of tradeskills to each profession does not entirely make sense, though for the most part it is logical.

Why do engineers have a poor CompLit ability?

What is the basis of Chemistry skill?
Why is the Enforcer profession the best at it and Doctors mediocre?

Are tradeskills intended to be a Trader and Engineer thing only, or are most/all professions supposed to have a tradeskill roll?

What is the tradeskill roll of those who's tradeskills are secondary to the Trader/Engineer? (Doctor, Fixer, etc)

#2) Profession skill buffs

Why do Traders have the best engineering buffs rather than Engineers?

Why do Engineers have a good Ptech/Chemistry buff rather than Doctors?

Why do Traders have almost all the tradeskill buffs AND the best ones rather than a specialized or average tradeskill buff set?

Why do Meta-Physicists receive the superior Nanoprogramming buff and Nanotechnicians (at this time) not any?

#3) Tradeskill applications.

What is the intended application area of each Tradeskill?
For example chemistry: is that for medical chemistry, explosives, etc? or what exactly is Quantum FT & what is it supposed to be used for?

Will Mechanical Engineering have a single tradeskill use? (For Agents & Advents)

How will you implement a paperwork tradeskill?
