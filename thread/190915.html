Confederacy
Attention all leaders of Rubi-ka! I invite everyone to join a new institution that will be established to create order to the chaos we face on Rubi-ka. The confederacy wants YOU! No matter the size of your orgnization or your political ideology, everyone is welcome to this revolutionary new alliance. Below are the articals at which the Confederacy will follow, please look them over then contact "Marcai" if you are interested in joining!

Thank you

All organizations are required to sign the Confederate Alliance Pact (C.A.P) and to select 2 members of belonging to their organization to represent them. If 2/3 of the council feels that membership should be canceled or postponed, a vote will be held to decide.

1 ・瘢雹瘢雹 Voting
キ All members of the assembly are encouraged to speak about issues of concern. A representative that wishes to speak about a certain issue or have a voting called will have to submit it to the chancellor who will add it to the agenda. During the meetings each subject will be brought up on the agenda. Under no circumstance is an item to be left out or be considered unimportant. All organizations that have membership will be allowed up to 2 votes per voting session. The elected representatives must be the ones who cast the vote. In case of an emergency, the leader of an organization can take the place of 1 representative, but is only allowed 1 vote.

1 ・瘢雹瘢雹5 Order
キ The representatives who submit an item to the agenda will have 5 minuets to speak to the assembly. No members are to talk during this time. After the speech is over, there will be a 10 minuet buffer time for the assembly representatives to speak to one another and cast their vote.

1 ・瘢雹瘢雹6 Term in office
キ There is no limit to the term of representatives in the General Assembly. A leader may remove a representative if necessary, but must notify the Grand Chancellor. The Grand Chancellor him/her self has a 4 month term in office, but if the council disapproves of his/her job. A 2/3 vote will then remove the officer and will be replaced with another candidate from the prior election through a election vote.

1 ・瘢雹瘢雹7 Meetings
キ The Grand Assembly will meet 2 times a month. If more meetings are required then the Grand Chancellor will decide when. Emergency meetings can be called if notification is given 1 day prior.
