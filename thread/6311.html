24-7 the greatest trade and service organization on Rubi-Ka is recruiting now
You may ask yourself what 24-7 is?? Here is some answers:

24-7 is a trade and service organization on RK1. We are also a Roleplaying org., so our daily tasks is to make business running.
To do this we are running weekly events. You may have seen our buffstations at BB in O1 Entertainment. Other, more social events are also planned in the near future.

24-7 is at the moment a very small organization with only about 20 members. The activity in 24-7 is still very high because this is all very dedicated members, and they have very good knowledge of Rubi-Ka. So why do we need more members then?? Because not all profesions is represented among our ranks at the moment and we need more members to provide more and better services.

Now you may be among those that say you got no tradeskills or alike, but 24-7 also need bodyguards as our business often force of to move into hostile enviroments.

I bet you wanna join after reading this, so go to our homepage www.warders.net/24-7 and enter your data at the applicationform there. There you can find some more additonal info too.

If you have further questions you can either contact me or you can send a message to our guildchat by sending a /tell to our channelbot Twentyfour7. Twentyfour7 is also able to keep a conversation with you if evryone else is busy in mission or not online at all(if you are a member of 24-7).

The goal of 24-7 is fun for us players, and fame, fortune and glory for our characters.

Good Luck evryone!
