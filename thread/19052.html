Kill Tasks in AO?
Funcom should steal the idea of kill tasks from DAoC. In DAoC you can get a mini quest that involves killing a creature of a certain type. Once you get the task all you have to do is find a creature of that type and kill it solo. If anyone helps you out, it doesnt count towards your task. These tasks award a lot of experience but there is a limit to the number that you can do.

I love hunting solo. Especially going after some deep red mob that should tear me in two. Sometimes the mob I want to take down is surrounded by lesser mobs that I have to kill first. The point is that it is tons of fun. The only problem is that the experience gained is a drop in the bucket compared to what I could get in a group.

If I could get kill tasks, I would have something else meaningful to do when it is hard to find a group. Id also be willing go into PvP zones to do it. So why not add kill tasks to the game?

Id suggest that at the start of each level, your kill task counter would be reset. When you first start out the task should be relatively easy. As you completed tasks they would get harder. They should be structured so that you could reasonably get half a level through the tasks. After that point the tasks would become too difficult for the effort involved.

This would be a great way to reward the people who want to explore or risk a PvP zone but dont have the patience to try to get a full team to do it.

If more control is needed to prevent exploits, set a hard limit to the number of kill tasks that can be given out in a period of time.
