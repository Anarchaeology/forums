Calling all clanners on RK2
Greetings fellow Clansmen,

Nothing!!

Two weeks of amnesty and as many days filled with attacks from strange and horrifying creatures. Fourteen days in which the clans, once a firm military force, have deluded into a pack of babbling political clowns. Half a month of mayhem, and what have we heard from our leaders, the Council of Truth?

Nothing!!

If ye care for freedom, be there!
