I'd actually tend to say "no".

They wouldnt benefit in the two most important areas, actually taking a HP nerf, w/ nano skills remaining the same.

Look at this way, yes opifex would get "more" skills overall. But they wouldnt get those points, for the most part, in the skills that matter most. Sense isn't used much at all except in the speed tab, and the spy skills tab is useless.

Say JimBob is a Phixx3r:

At lvl 200 JimBob would, under 0/100/200 system I like, get
As Atrox: roughly 260 body dev x 4hp per = 1040life.
As Opifex: roughly +100 more to all evades
As Nano: roughly +125 more to all nanoskills

If you could wear 2/3 rings +100 evades +125 all nanoskills, or +1040 life at level 200, which would you choose?

While I'm not asking that Opifex be improved, I don't think a nerf is really necessary because the areas that their extra points help aren't "as helpful" as Atrox/Nanomage. AKA, speed skills and spying skills are not nearly as helpful in AO as HP/weapon skills or NP/nanoskills.

Interesting enough, theres only really 3 professions reasonable for Opifex... Fixer, Agent, MA. If this "fix" were to go through, I think Agent would be the only one consistently picking Opifex, with maybe a handful of Fixers.
