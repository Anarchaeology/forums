New Ideas/options for Orgs
I don't know if some of these have been suggested by others, but here are some ideas I had on improving Orgs.

1. Make meeting halls so that orgs could discuss things in peace. Here is how it could work: You rent a room in the meeting hall and a key would be generated for the entire org. You could then enter a meeting room and not be bothered by others. You could make copies of the key for anyone else who wanted to come too.

2. Make a message ability that would send a tell to everyone in your org. This would be leader only (or maybe let the second highest rank use the ability as well).

3. Add more org quests. It seems like at the moment all you do is go about in groups of 6 to mission/ hunt. I think it would be cool to make large missions that would require 2 or more grps of 6 to complete.

4. Allow different forms of taxes. you could have a form that would take a % of all the earnings you got off of looting monsters. I think it would also be nice to have it so that different members would pay different rates.

5. There is no #5

So what do you all think of these ideas? Any other suggestions for improving orgs would also be cool.

One solution to the meeting hall, is get a low level member to generate a mission (small, in a city), get them to give a high level member a key, they then trash the mision but don't complete it. Generate keys for other guild members as they arrive.

Ta-da - private meeting hall.

I'm sure a mechanism could be added to generate a 'mission' with a large single room in the current town/city. This 'mission' area would last say 10 hours or until the 'mission' icon was deleted. This would just require a minor tweak to the current mission system and some new 'meeting' terms in a few places.

I like the idea of the leader having /tell org message, or the idea of an org 'message-of-the-day' settable by the leader and which you see at logon or on issuing /org motd (leader would be anle to do /org motd message to actually set it.

Org quests = static duneons, for now at least. Maybe we'll get more later. Maybe something in SL.

The % of mob money looted as a tax option is a good idea, allow it to be set from 0% to maybe about 25% - would automatically scale with member's level...

The income tax would be a GOOD idea. What is trivial to a high level player is a weeks earnings to a newbie, so should newbies not org with high level players? A newbie kills a mob and he gets 1-6 credits for the kill. I sic my pet on a green in a mission, and I'm pulling 250 normally. I can afford a 1000 a day tax, a newbie'd have to kill about 200 mobs a day to pay the tax, (of course, then he wouldn't be a newbie anymore.)

An /org motd is a GOOD idea.

A private room for an org is less necessary. Open your friends window, untab everything but /tells and your org, and you have communications privacy. Just make sure you're holding your meeting someplace where you're less likely to encounter battlespam. I'd just hold them in the trade annexes of the general stores. Nice and layed back. Good setting that is probably little used as it should. I certainly haven't seen any meetings or parties in there.

Another one that we need. An /org list command to tell us who's on, on the whole server farm.

Maybe an /org members to list out the roster, (it is visible on the web page), and maybe who's on or when they logged off.

I'd give the /org members to everybody, but limit most members to telling who's on or off, and give the leader, and maybe officers, the information of when the last time on was, (so they can cull deadweight.)

Some other improvements. Loose the existing advantage(1, blunt skill), and just let the org leader drop a nanocluster in the box. Set the upkeep as 1/10th its price per day, per member. and one per skill/attribute. Maybe limit it to faded. Get some more restrictions.

There should be no fewer than 19 restrictions. One for each race, class, and gender (minus neuter, which is covered by race).
