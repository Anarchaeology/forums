Some additional info for the BIOMARE dungeon:

T.I.M. has a 10% chance (more or less) of dropping the HUD Upgrade: Personal S.T.M. (Likely even less with 14.5, as drop-chance was reduced)

The Lab Director can drop the Customized IMI Desert Reet 1000, a nifty pistol with burst + fling, does fire damage.

The Bodyguards can drop a Personal Safe, which can contain valuable items. (Rumors are that Grid Armor I sometimes drops from these.)

Neutralizers can drop Medic's Tank Armor (rare).
