Omegatalon climbed up onto the roofs above Borealis. From his spot he saw the group of Omni-Tek soldiers surrounding a prisoner. Members of Desert Winds and some citizens of Borealis were attacking, trying to free the prisoner. This wasnt their fight. Omegatalon had brought this on them

Final Act - Funeral
BOREALIS 15 Dec

Jabrak bent down and gently lifted Omegatalons body from the dusty street.

We will bury them near Azure lake, Angela and Omega wanted to be together forever. They shall be.

The party set off from Borealis, carrying the body of the dead opifex and the CPU, the person, Angela to their eternal rest. The procession stopped on the shores of the lake. Several members of Desert Winds and also some of their friends helped to dig a grave. Jabrak slowly lowered the body of Omegatalon and Angela into the hole.

Naxilite lowered his gun, he was tired, tired of death of fighting for no cause. Tired of young men throwing their lives away. He took a breath and spoke

Omegatalon and Angela, they gave their lives to protect us, to protect Rubi-Ka.
They wanted to live together.
They can not have that dream, but they shall rest together.
Here on the shores of Lake Azure
They shall sleep for eternity, undisturbed. Together in death as they were in life.
Their grave shall be a monument
A monument to the fight for our world
To live how we want to live


Rest in Peace Angela and Omegatalon



THE END
