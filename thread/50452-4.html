Hackers will most likely do their work in the safety of their very own apartment.

It is being decided whether it may be possible for hackers to be able to hack the locks of other people's apartments. This will mean hackers will have to use hit and run tactics to make sure the enemy can't send strike squads to his location if an enemy hackers successfully finds out where he is at the time.

There's enough good ideas here to make the booster pack now. I particularly like the idea of having a different template for each profession. As a martial artist I want a restrained, minimalist decor, which I'm sure that wouldn't appeal to many other players.

Paying "rent" is another great idea as a credit sink - the apartments would have to be good enough that people would actually want to pay for them on an ongoing basis though.

I also thought of having different lines of furniture, in both style and quality, with the better pieces only available once you reach a certain level. So for example you could have a rustic line (pine with minimal ornamentation), a traditional line (more elaborate hardwood stuff), a contemporary line (futuristic plastic and chrome), etc. Newbies would only have access to the cheapest and plainest stuff (bookshelves made out of cinderblocks and wooden planks? Maybe stacked milkcrates as an alternative?) but you can upgrade as you level.

Also, the furniture should be interactive, so you can put food in the fridge, clothes in a chest of drawers, tutoring devices on your bookshelf, and so on.
