Camping Sucks
The problem with the current system isn't the dungeons per-se, or the spawn times, or any of that. The problem is the items that are available there are generally better than stuff that is available else where. I've no problem with the mobs there dropping unique items, but they should be flashy show-off, look-at-me sort of things, not must-get-that-now sort of things.

Rare items should be rare because they aren't generated often, not because they are only available from an over-camped boss mob on a long spawn time...

Understand this FC, humans have a inherent propensity to want equality and fairness, if we perceive something as unfair, we get annoyed. We can even get annoyed enough to do things that others would see as extreme. In context of AO, there are (thankfully) limits on what we can do, but just because I can't nuke Camelot off the face of the planet doesn't mean I or other players are not annoyed.

Don't get me wrong, the static dungeons, and especially a dungeon like camelot have their place in the scheme of things, but they are not perceived as a fair means to distribute and restrict rare and powerful items by the bulk of the playerbase.

So, have that damn lizard drop QL200 armour that is much like other QL200 armour, but *looks* different, make it drop a sword that is better than most other sword, but only a bit, make it drop items that are just cool, but have no significant game-mechanic effect such as a purple yalm.

Something like the Alvin/Dodga quest is a good approach (just drop the stupid respawn times...) for handing out interesting items. Make the quest take a long time or involve travel to far flung and dangerous parts of the planet, make us work for our items.

Have dyna-camps be capable of spawning special named mobs on occasion (maybe after the camp has been hit hard) instead of a normal boss mob and have that be able to drop interesting items. Have them turn up just wandering about... Be creative, but I'm not paying you my monthly fee to have my character sit on its butt for hours.

All I'm going to say is this:

FC, take a clue from Asheron's Call. You don't need to make quests an impossible camp fest and frustrating expierence to make the loot worthwhile, valuable, or a status symbol.

You can remedy your quest situation by taking a few simple steps:

1. All unique mobs drop their loot every time.

2. Unique mobs respawn within 5-10 minutes.

3. Each person has a personal quest timer on the items they pick up. They cannot pick up another "sword of uber slaying" until a day, week, or month has passed.

4. Quest loot should never be the "best" and it should never be made to "balance" a class or skill. Quest items should unique and worth your time to get them, but they should never be the absolute best.

The best stuff should drop randomly out in the open. Anything that you know exactly where and when to get should not be that powerful. It just caters to the uber guilds.

These simple rules of design are what AC has followed, and it has worked wonderfully. Everyone gets what they came for, and gets out fast so the next group can enjoy the dungeon as it was meant to be seen.

Dungeons in AC were a great expierence, not something you had to force yourself through in order to obtain that phat uber lewt you can't live without.
