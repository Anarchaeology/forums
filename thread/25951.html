What are your Leet recipes?
Do you like to cook Leet? Well, post yer recipes here and maybe everyone else will try it


Deep Fried Leet

2 cups extra virgin olive oil

1 whole Leet, de-furred and gutted

Your choice of marinade and seasonings

First, marinade your Leet for at least an hour or so. Next, Pour in the olive oil and wait 'til it's hot. When it's hot enough, put in your Leet. Turn every few minutes 'til the Leet is golden brown. After about 20 minutes it should be done (but poke with fork to check because it may not take that long to cook it all the way through)

Serves: 1 person per Leet
