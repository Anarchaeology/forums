changing the reclaim process
I've played for a while now, and i have a few comments about the game mechanics vs the story/history of rubi-ka, as outlined in the timeline.

According to the timeline, the insurance terminals work on the process of cloning a new body at the exact moment of death, thus giving the soul a place to go besides the afterlife. Very nice plot issue there FC....makes the immortality of the player explained, but not completely dumb...

So, having read this, way back when i started, i was shocked to find that my clone, the new body for my consciousness, had all my weapons, armour and implants! WOOT....now i have duplicates...the cloning machine's toiler bots have made me new weapons, and when i get my otiginals back.....wait......these ARE the originals...hmmm......and how did my new, virgin body get all these implants! what gives!

Ok, i know, these items are there to cater to those that wear items MUCH higher qlevels than their skills normally allow...heck, my adv uses q78 Rider executioners, at level 62....and has since level 58. All self buffed, i might add.....

And i know that it is to make the game easier, but....doesn't it seem kind of silly to anyone but me? I think that the reclaim terms should do what they are supposed to....reclaim ALL of your posessions. The only problem i could see would be overflow in some cases.....but that could be handled by letting the reclaim hold items for you till you CAN get them....

The major effect of this would be, of course, on the over equipper. I have an agent character, so i know that overequipping is NOT cheating...it is a standard part of the game, intended from the beginning. The extent may have gotten a wee bit out of hand.....but there are ALWAYS people in games, and irl that are better of squeezing that last few points out of something. (my ex was GREAT at balancing his checkbook...always had a few extra $ at the end of the month....)

i see this as an improvement too. For the pvm fights, makes FC able to lower the stats on the monsters back to reasonable levels.....so at level 50 some characters don't get too wimpy for greys. For pvp, well.....there is risk in everything, no? when a self buffed goes up against a twinker, they usually die...i say usually, because skill and tactics can do much. So when the self buff dies, takes him about 10 minutes and 300 creds for the surgery clinic to get back to fighting trim. death should be more traumatic than a 2 minute rest anyways. and for the twink....well, he had a better chance in the fight....so a bigger risk/penalty for death - he has to find all those buffs again...and juggle em, calculate etc to get it all back. and from some of the posts i have read, that is what they like anyways..

Basicly, it would enhance the level of continuity between game story and game mechanics...with the added bonus of "balancing" pvp. The more over equipped, the easier the fight....but the bigger the risk. Sounds fair, i think.

It also gives BOTH sides in the debate what they want, kinda. The hardcore gamer will be able to re-equip easily enough (and keeping a backup may be a tactic now eh?) while the casual gamer has a chance still at having fun, for the hour or 2 they can devote to the game, without being ganked at every turn.

just my 2 cents, any and all replies welcome
(of course, constructive more welcome than whining...lol)
