Improved Missions.
As a newcomer to the game, (first post, please be gentle) I have to say its been great so far. Coming from 2 1/2 years of EQ (still there too) the idea of missions created just for me (or my team) is fantastic, and as such a large part of the game, I also have to say that I think the missions need some work.

So far, all they have been for me is;

Enter room, kill MOB(s), loot corpse and/or treasure chest.
Enter next room, kill MOB(s), loot corpse and/or treasure chest.
Enter next room, kill MOB(s), loot corpse and/or treasure chest.
ETC.
ETC.
ETC.

What a tremendous opportunity for this game to take an even bigger lead in the online game arena.

What about interactive missions and rooms? Where you not only have to kill a MOB, but figure out a puzzle, switch a lever, or trigger a computer system to access another part of the mission or open a door, position a bridge, ETC. In addition to that, moving trap doors in the floors, or rooms full of open pits where falling in means "death", Spikes that fall from the ceiling, or droid bombs that come racing out of a room when the door is opened only to chase you down and explode, taking half of your health? With the "no true death concept" you could really push some of the missions to an extreme and make them risky and tough (and in proportion more rewarding) I mean we've all seen these types of environments in FPS for some time now it makes a world of difference between running through and just killing everything, or actually having to think and figure stuff out while you're doing it.
And what about some more variety in the mission graphics? (Yes, I know that there seems to be different graphics for various regions) but I can't even begin to tell you how happy I was when after doing a number of missions in drab brown-walled rooms, I found one that looked like an old subway station, or even the inside of some huge dead snake, it was invigorating (even though it turned out to be the same old "enter room, kill MOB routine") What about levels to the mission with stairways or elavators, ETC. and how about obstacles in the environment that are actually useful, such as barrells, tables, computer consoles, ETC, that allow you to hide behind and use for cover when in a gunfight, or to jump out from behind or on top of to attack your opponent in melee, and MOBS that actually use the obstacles in the same way when attacking you, not just standing in the middle of the room and trading hits or exchanging fire (DUH!!!, thats just not how fights go!)

Being able to use the sliders (money-experience, open-hidden, ETC) when choosing your mission to adjust the complexity of these puzzles, or traps, level size, ETC, and a rough time it takes to complete any given mission would be a big plus also.

I am certain that these ideas have already been brought up and discussed (how could they not have?) I only wanted to bring them up again from a new players perspective to let those who are in charge of these things see that to some of us they are still very important.

I certainly hope that Funcom and the designers continue to improve what they have done here, as I can see with a focus on improving these and other gameplay issues AO will, without a doubt, truly become the best online game today.

Despite the 'bump' spam...
I'm still going to respond.

All these ideas are not only great, they are obvious. AO is a fantastic game, but it's also still so frustratingly short of its potential. I also played EQ for 2 years but finally jumped ship when Shadows of Sucklin came out. I do miss the wide variety of 'missions' in EQ (they were there, but they were called quests) that ranged from figuring out how to snatch a treasure chest from underneath a shark to chopping off the innkeeper's daughter's head to ferrying mail all over the world (I adored playing rogues ) and I hope that somewhere in the future funcom can begin to implement similar quests among the wide variety of apparently vestigal NPCs roaming around Rubi-ka...

But adding SOMETHING to the missions besides 'kill, loot, defuse, loot, heal, kill' really ought to be implemented since, as you noticed, all the parts are there (except elevators - I too have noticed they're nowhere to be found. But its easy enough to use a teleporter instead to reach other levels or hidden areas). Giving the mobs the AI to hide and dodge might be too much - their stats use math to achieve the same thing - but certainly some kind of puzzle or a stage 1, stage 2, stage 3 reward where you can use the art container or stolen nano you get from your stage one mission to access a static stage 2 dungeon that requires the item to access or complete, and which gives you a stage 3 reward to access yet another dungeon on the other side of the planet...

I believe the poll still has missions as the number one thing people want to have fixed, so I don't think Funcom is going to not know the players really want to see some further improvement of a good but potentially revolutionary aspect of the game. Trust me, if the game could be field hunting, teaming, travelling and social like it is now, and have missions that are almost like walking into Half Life? There would be no other MMORPGS on the market, period. No one would waste their time on anything else.
