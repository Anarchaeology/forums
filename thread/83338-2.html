Yes, it would. And that's the point, our shield has it's strenghts and it has it's weaknesses.

For instance, TMS shields us against direct damage, but when we fire at an adventurer their damage shields go right through our tms.

Soldiers have a fairly limited nanopool, especially as an Atrox, having something you can actively use more nanopool during a fight makes the nanomage better when it comes to actual combat, where in this case you would use quite a lot of nano. Our TMS cost a lot of nano to execute, omhh cost only 60, but in order to be healed up you need to cast it 10 times to maybe be healed for 2000-3000hp.

The whole idea of a soldier is damage prevention and damage output, tms isn't strong enough anymore to be any special anymore. Other professions are stronger with their buffs, as far as I see, soldiers are the only profession who gains a little with his buff. The positive sides doesn't quite make up for the drawbacks with the buffs.

Let's look at Enforcers, they have mongo, challenger and rage. The only drawback they have, is a relatively small dot and a nanoshutdown of 15-20 seconds (don't remember exact length atm). Mongo stack with essences.

Soldiers TMS and Omhh. Nanoshutdown 2 minutes so we have either tms or omhh not both. TMS also removes other reflect.

Of course finding a balance that is right is hard, but although my suggestion may seem overpowered I don't think it is, really.
