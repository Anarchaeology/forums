How do u kill the side bosses with only 1 team?

One thing's for sure, we need the doc to be able to do A&O..
Doc+adv+soldier+enf+nt+someone is the preferred team. The nukes from the bosses hits for max 6k-ish through RRFE. 3 Docs +trader+ soldier works too

The right-hand boss is the most difficult due to the minions. By hiding behind the pillar, you can fear those minions without risk of them fleeing up to Hezak. Gelid and the female next to Gelid is easier than the side bosses.

Most important is to avoid add, so pull those mobs closest to you first.

Killing hezak has 2 main strategies.
1. Pull him near portal. You need to either a) Keep a team to do the sides when they spawn, or b) Kill Gelid+friend, then kill the sidebosses before pulling Hezak, you now have 10 minutes.
2. Kill him at altar. Kill the female first, then Gelid, then rush Hezak.

If you can kill Hezak in 2 minutes, everything is fine. after that he starts to spawn Fanatics and do the really bad nukes with 17s anti-ch and 450 Nano-drain.

Use NTs to deal with Fanatics (Area nukes)
