I personally would also like to see some distinct differences between Omni and the Clans. Though they don't neccessarily have to be huge differences, or even affect gameplay so much.

For example, the dresscode idea. That's really cool, and can be made into a mainly RP thing. Suits could be created for each profession and maybe even for certain ranges of levels. The suit would be a back item, and wouldn't provide any benifits or protection whatsoever. Anytime that Omni's were inside a major Omni controlled city, they would be required to wear their suits. This wouldn't disrupt any gameplay aspects...only a back item, if one was worn, would have to be removed to put on the suit, and the suits would only have to be worn in cities, so omni's could take them off to go hunting or do missions or whatever.

Also, making the clans more reliant on tradeskills to acquire items is a great idea. I think the stock of clan stores should be reduced a little, make the ql of the items much more erratic and the selection of items smaller, but on the flipside make it possible for clanners to construct any weapons or armour they need. This would help make tradeskills make sense, since the higher cost and time involved in manufacturing an item would be balanced by the fact that you might not be able to get the ql and type of armour/weapon/item you need otherwise.

Just some thoughts. Though I don't envy Funcom having to deal with this issue. In the end, a lot of people will ***** and moan if the changes FC makes reduces their power or access to needed items at all, whether its a balanced change or not.
