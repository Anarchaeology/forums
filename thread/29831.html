Kaen-Archibald Kolt
My adventurer alt just gave up on all other pistols and switched to the Kaen-Archibald Kolt, a new pistol to version 14.2.

Not only does it do fairly nice damage at a speed so insane it sounds like popcorn popping, it has one other huge advantage over all of the other adventurer pistols: the QL is more closely in line with the requirements.

Max Agility, Sense, and Concealment. Cast Concealment Expertise and Pronouncement of Greatness. Put on a Cloaking Device. You can now sneak a mission for your next Kaen ... with, as far as I can tell, no risk, because I have jumped up and down on the feet of mobs 7 levels above me without breaking concealment.

Kaens are rare as mission rewards, but no worse than any other specific weapon ... quite do-able with ClickSaver. So when it's time to update my pistols, I generate two missions for the next "bump" up with Kaens as rewards, sneak them both, and voila, two pistols and a small stack o' cash, zero risk. (I do the same thing to update my armor, when I've got the patience. Seven armor pieces plus two guns equals nine missions to roll, travel to, and sneak ... a time-consuming process.)

Now that concealment is fixed, more or less, it means that adventurers and agents can now safely power-level, content in the knowledge that no matter how inferior their armor and weapons become, and how dangerous yellow mobs get to them thereby, they can always get free hardware to update themselves.

(I expect other professions to get a bit testy about this, any day now.)
