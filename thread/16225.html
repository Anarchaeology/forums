Teaming and targeting.. the neutral problem
Anyone who have ever teamed with someone from the other side of the conflict have one time or another noticed this problem. When you use tab to target an enemy you have to toggle all your team mates (and their pets) that's not on the same side as you. Now imagine being the only neutral in an omni team that have 1 mp and an engie, along with 3 other members. 5 players, 3 pets, and you have 8 potential targets when trying to find the mob that's attacking.

It have probably been mentioned before, but then it's worth saying again because it's rather an important issue.

People in your team should not be defined as hostiles no matter what side they are on.

There is no good reason for why they should be threated as hostiles.. we have chosen to team up against some other enemy, and are at the moment not enemies.

As a matter of fact, I for one don't want to be able to target my team mates using tab at all.. that's what I have F2-F6 for. The easiest way to fix this problem would be to simply not let us target team mates using tab. And to stop people from complaining, make it an option-box in one of the menues that we can check.

Target team mates using tab. on/off
