"We need to find where this clanner lives." Said Aktrez, more as a thought than an answer. "Halbert, pull up some info on her. Just send a message to Helpbot. It has the info we need to start. "Just ask it...."

Before she could finish her sentance, the rumble cut her off. She sheilded her eyes from the blaring sun, her heart pounding! Damn, had they been spotted? She stared up at the yalm, squinting to see the plates. OH! GSP!! Thank Ross!!!

The Yalm landed nearby and Gridfan came running from around the corner.

"What are you doing here Aktrez?" he asked frantically. "You want to be the next one killed?"

"What do you think!? I"m here to find Katrienae. MissMaul was OMNI! remember!? " Aktrez realized that she was being harsh and lowered her tone. "Have you heard any information?"

"No" Gridfan said as he shook his head. "I was busy with my feed when I saw Threx fly by and..."

Aktrez cut him off. "We saw him too. He was oblivious to the fact that we were even there."

"Yeah, that was the response I got too. I have no idea if he knows anything more or not. But, he looked like he may know where he was going."

Treylla snickered. "Or he was in a drunken stouper!" The party giggled a bit, a welcome feeling during this ordeal.

"I was just telling my crew that we need to find out more information about Katrienae. She has to be...."

All of a sudden a gun clicked. Aktrez looked up to find herself staring at a 7foot Atrox brandishing a weapon and holding it straight at her face.

"Well well well, what do we have here?"
