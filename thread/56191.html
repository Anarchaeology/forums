Trigger Quests - Prelude
...What is a Trigger Quest?

...The trigger sits at a table in the bar, waiting for someone. You're sure you've never seen him before. Why is he all alone? Perhaps you should say hi...

...The trigger stands leaning against the tree, looking out over the lake. She looks to be LD or something. Suddenly, for no reason, she throws out an emote...hardly a place to roleplay and "brush a hand through her hair"...you approach her...

...The trigger walks up to you, flashes you the bio-readout of another character, and inquires if you have ever seen that boy before...you say no, you're sure you haven't. He nods understandingly and proceeds to the next shop terminal to ask the next person a similar question...you start to wonder...

...Do you know who your trigger is?
