Leetas
A raid isn't all about the loot, it's also about having a good time with your friends. Raiding not only provides loot, but also strengthen the unity within a guild.

When the raid bots are raiding, a lot of people from different guilds go together. It's a point-based system, which means you get loot based on appearance. Of course, many guilds operate the same way regarding loot distribution, but that's not the point. When you raid together that much as you do, you get to know each other. But you really don't care if the next guy gets the loot, and be happy for him, what matters is getting your appearance point, and you know you're one step closer to that one item you want. This is a nice way for those in guilds who don't / can't raid i.e Smug/Fred/Mercs etc. It's effective, fair and simple, but you forget one thing: when you camp smug for the whole day, you also, in a away, block for other guilds.

I've heard one of the arguments being about "by giving more people the chance of getting more "uber" loot, omni becomes stronger etc". This is all wrong. Do you honestly believe that extra Azure sleeve, AF pants will make any difference at all out there on the battle field? The answer is no. The raid bots only make people angry, increased internal conflicts within the faction, and thus not making omni any stronger at all.

The way I see it, raids should be a guild-thing. That is the way it always have been. I don't mind guilds co-operating with each other, but it's the efficiency and greed of the raid bots, raid bots always having people available and camping i.e Smug for the whole day, that makes me mad.
