Are engineers really that bad?
Disclaimer: I have never played an engineer, so I don't really know what it's like.

I'm starting this thread to ask some questions about engineers. For instance: are engineers really that bad? Let's look at some advantages.

Tradeskills. They are proficient in some tradeskills that others are not proficient in. Not that big of a deal, but it can help.

Pets. Ok, many people would probably say pets are not an advantage. But aren't engineer pets the most powerful? I believe they have more hp and attack power (or CAN have more hp and attack power, eg with trimmers) than mp and crat pets. Tell me if I'm wrong, I don't know exactly. In addition, can't engineers heal their pets faster and better than mp's and crats can? Again correct me if I'm wrong. What this means, is this: even though their pets may not do top damage compared to, say, soldiers, they are capable of continuously withstanding a beating for a long time. This makes them ideal for one-on-one confrontations, and allows the engineer to be an excellent soloing profession, it seems to me.

Disadvantages: I'm sure these have been talked about before. I won't get into them.

So maybe what most people are saying, is that engineers are the worst in teams, because pets have crappy pathing in missions, don't do top damage, etc. But for other things, such as one-on-one soloing, don't they do quite well?

My point is that all professions are not geared toward doing "top damage"; each profession has a different philosophy. The most obvious example (to me) is the fixer; they have never been a damage-oriented profession. Rather, their philosophy is geared toward travel, looting, etc. Another example is the enforcer. They too are not viewed totally as a damage class, but also as one that can take immense amounts of damage. Or take the MP: their philosophy lies in increasing skills and in pet usage, not damage per se. So maybe (remember I'm saying maybe! don't flame me please, lol) the engineer's philosophy is one of good one-on-one solo player combat, combined with other extras such as tradeskills and ac buffs.

What do you think?

Engie pets have the most hitpoints and their damage output is acceptable enough. However, no pet can really taunt, so using them to tank is not the same as having an Enforcer or even a Soldier or MA on the team, as the bot will lose aggro too much to be reliable.

That's really the problem with soloing too. In any given fight the pet might hold aggro or it might not. It does best when it's the first thing the mob sees, although you'll probably still pull aggro off it for a while the moment you step into the room. If the mob sees the Engineer first, pets are hard pressed to persuade it to switch targets even if you just stand there on full def and don't fight back.

The last problem is that unlike Crats or MPs, Engineers have no "tactical" nanos at all. Just about every class has at least one nano line that it can use when things don't go according to plan, and some have several. Engineers have nothing of this nature which means that when things go wrong, the outcome is more or less down to luck.
