PvP in AO
PvP in AO is really bland to say the least because of Funcom's so-called storyline. Most fun from a game comes from conflict, not with NPCs but with other players. Funcom's idea of amnesty is taking a lot of the fun away. I hope it doesn't last for 4 years, because I certainly am NOT going to fight some 3rd party of NPCs.

As of now it seems like that's where we're being lead, to fight some party of monsters that oppose both OT and Clan. And let me tell u in advance, if they're not going to be player controlled it's going to be boring as hell. And I'm not sticking around paying my money to fight some more monsters.

There are 2 opposing sides, so where is the conflict? What use are PvP titles if we're all just one happy family? I don't know where you're taking us Funcom, but it better be good because right now at level 124 I'm getting real tired of hunting monsters.

That's the only action we get, we're not even supposed to fight the other side because of this amnesty thing.

Where is the conflict?
