PvP the Bigger Picture - Questions
I am fairly experienced at PvP in other games, and just got my first taste of it here on AO the other night. Just wanted to toss a few questions out:

Basically whats the point of it all, least in the bigger picture sense? Sure I can to go a 25% zone and run around fighting omni, but how does that fit into the larger story line?

For example is there a way that we can go and take an Omni outpost. Not just fight guards and other mobs, but actually change it from an Omni guarded outpost to Clan, or Neutral?

If the above isnt possible, then exactly how does our actions have any impact, other then FC sanctioned game events?

Well that seems a bit screwed up, for a game that claims we can shape the future, but we have no real ability to change anything? Seems like a rather static, pointless conflict that we as players never get to accomplish much more then gain some titles?
