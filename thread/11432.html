I myself don't approve of guard killing in general; as an MP, I usually set my healpet on the guard in a vain gesture.
It irritates me to no end when Omni come all the way to NL City to attack our Neutral guards. Even worse, the Clanners, whose midst we are in, supposedly the Rebels fighting for freedom, come over during the lunch-break and kill some neutral guards. Lovely. Can't condone it or rationalize it in RP sense no matter how much they smirk at my protests...

That said, *occasionally* there is a RP "reason" for Guard-killing.
A clan guard went nuts at Rhino Cockpit about a month ago, and started attacking neutrals, who of course at lvl 20 didn't stand a chance. I don't know if somebody dragged the guards from the bridge to the save point or whatever, but it wasn't pleasant to the neutrals. So our guild organized an expedition (well that's what we called it, OOC it was about 5 people who happened to be on the channel, trying to help a trapped friend. The team did a retailatory surgical strike on the said guard, and y'know what? Those guards haven't attacked neutrals for quite a little while afterwards.
'course, OOC, that's just the glitchy guard being eliminated; but we like to think we taught them a little lesson.

So if somebody thinks that ALL guard-killings ALL the time are Sin against nature, I humbly disagree; though I dislike random killing of guards in general, I think we played that particular instance about right, RP wise. Guards are an element of Rubi-ka, and there are situations where they get involved. If they attack my neutral friend for no reason, I think that's one good instance .
