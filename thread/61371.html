My personal oppinion:

Multiple GS' ain't cool. Too much hassle, too much griefing
possible, too much chaos...

Instead of concentrating on nerfing it to one GS, make it so that
Tarasque spawns more often perhaps, and fix the game so it can
handle more than 30 people in the same dungeon without the
game going into a stall...

Battles there are fun... but the griefing, ninjalooting and the
constant flames from each side about warping / cheating /
exploiting / harrasing has gone over the top.

Now a'day it seems -everything- is harrasment / disruption of
gameplay. FC needs to come clean and put out a CLEAR set of
rules and nono's.

Warping people into a playfield, overloading it way beyond it's
original limit is a given. That is disruption of gameplay since it can
temper with the very stability of the zone.

People sitting inside camelot preventing people from opening
another GS? Well, if you ask me, I think they are entitled to. They
pay 14.95 a month to park their ass wherever they want.

Also, if you look at it another way, I hardly think the current
GS1/2/3/4 situation is what the Devs intended and/or want at all.

People can be jerks towards eachother in games like this,
however, try not to scream wolf and do a petition every damned
time something annoys you.

I'm not going to say "This is War ffs, let people ***** at
eachother. ANARCHY!!". That's BS. This game needs rules like
everything else. Who would be here if it was anarchy? Not me.
Datts fer sure.

Instead of messing around with their coffee machines and ID-
extractors, FC should sit down and get some decent rules
together. A more detailed Rules of Conduct.

I'm sure it would only take me a day, if not less to make a clear
writing of rules on every thinkable situation. What we follow
today is way to vague and it seems as if -every- situation can be
turned into harrasment / disruptiuon.

Also, it seems that GM's themselves (even more visible in the ARK
program) give out different signals on what's acceptable and
what's not.

Clear guidelines.. That's all I ask.
