You know Omni can still group with Clan and will do it on a continuing basis, don't you? There has yet to be given one single solitary reason for an Omni character to attack a Clanner other than 'because I can'.

Apologies to all the RP'ers out there (myself included) but for the majority of customers Omni meant 'better equipment' and Clan meant 'better skills'. Nothing more, nothing less. Omni did not mean 'regimented orderly businesslike approach to profit and better living'. Clan did not meant 'fighting for the reduction of oppression and and a chance for one's own actions to affect a better tomorrow.' Why do you think I abandoned all my Clan characters? There was no 'purpose' behind being clan. Being Neutral in this game is the only faction choice that provides any purpose: survival (in-game mechanics) and flipping a continuous finger at the designers (out-of-game purpose).

The 'war' is a joke in terms of both game mechanics and the player's drive toward supporting it.

There is no 'war'. Wars involve coordinated military campaigns. Wars involve real repercussions to campaign successes or failures. Wars involve armies. We have no campaigns. No deaths. No armies. No hatred between factions. No cutting of supply lines. No prisoners. No infiltrators. No intelligence collection. No nothing.

We have gankers, posers, duelers, griefers, .... and now NPC's buzzing around like that stupid dog that would nip at your heals while you rode your bike to school. Kick the damn thing and it yelps away. How annoying.

If I can't have War, I'd at least rather have a game that I can play without tedium and annoyance.
