Funcom and Pistols and Adventurers and Overequiping
Hello,

There has been an ongoing discussion throughout this forum over the passed two months on the unseemly multi-ranged requirements on higher level pistols.

There has also been some concern on how there is an extreme disparity between pistols and all other weapons on the world of rubi-ka.

There has been rational discussion with ideas, comments, and suggestions. Most people just want this issue addressed by Funcom.

However, in the passed two months there has not been a single response to any of the following questions:

1) is there any plan on downgrading the multiranged requirment on pistols?

2) are there going to be any new pure pistol, pistol/fling weapons with increased minimum base damage inserted into the game. i have seen that new OT bio-pistol thing, but you need to have ranged energy which makes it unusuable to most pistol wielding classes (i think it is pistol/fling/burst/ranged energy but i could be wrong).

as it is right now there are three pistol dependant professions, i believe...the doctor, the crat, and the adventurer.

of these three professions, only one is completely dependant on the pistol...the adventurer.

because the adventurer has no true offensive nano or pets to rely on, a pistol wielding adventurer is pretty much useless unless equiping pistols at least 20-30 levels above the character. This only makes the adventurer equal to mobs his/her own level.

now...there seems to be a loud congregation of individuals who want all weapons to suddenly become useless if a character does not have the requirements to use it, regardless of the time and effort gone into trying to hold that weapon in the first place.

this would completely destroy the adventurer profession. or at least those who rely on pistols.

so this goes all back to the basic question...are pistols going to be improved...if so when...how...what about multiranged?

please answer.

pistols and other classes
I have read that the pistol issue is not just an adventurer problem, and while I agree to some extent that an improvement in pistols would certainly help other other professions such as engies, docs, and crats...it is primarily a pistol/advent problem.

Engies...for extra damage on top of what your pistols can do you get nice robots. At mid to higher levels, the robots do significant damage. VERY significant damage.

Crats...for extra damage on top of what your pistols can do you get your little android pet things. Again...at higher levels these guys do significant damage.

Docs...for extra damage on top of what your pistols can do you get your cold blast and poison nanos that do significant damage.

Advents...for extra damage on top of what our pistols can do we get...um...a damage shield? An extra 15 points of damage every time we are attacked for 100+ isn't really much of an offensive skill to fall back on. We get...a polymorph? +7 damage to attacks isn't exactly going to help out when you have a minimum hit of 3 points (which happens sometimes, even with QL 90+ pistols).

The pistol issue needs to be changed to make them more powerful, and while all pistol professions will benefit from a boost in pistols, the primary profession that relies on pistols is the adventurer. Engies don't NEED to rely on weapons after a certain level...the same with crats and docs. The pistols aren't the primary method of attack power coming from these professions after about level 50ish. The only pistol-profession that really needs the boost is the advent.

All of this is kind of irrelevant since funcom refuses to even address that a problem possibly exists with pistols. There have been a couple new pistols introduced, but nothing special. Nothing that has a higher base rate. Nothing that is just a pistol/fling. There is that OT Bio-Pistol thing that uses ranged energy which is like flushing IPs down a toilet for an advent. I saw some new pistol that does fire-damage and uses flamethrower ammo, but the damage really doesn't do that much compared to pistols that already exist.

So..funcom, please let us know what you are planning on doing with pistols? As someone above stated...there is alot of variety in pistols, and not much substance. Who uses a Bigburger, Inc. pistol anyways???
