PVP, 15 seconds, good-bad-ugly
Alright, I decided to post this here because I really didn't want to wade through 151 posts of it in the 13.2 patch thread. Anyway.

I like the 15 second rule for several reasons, but first I'll bring up a few reasons why people don't like it.


The main reason some don't like it is because it effectively gives the person gridding in the initiative.

Second reason is that it will let large numbers of clan/omni grid in so they can rampage over the zone.


Well.....I personally think those are GREAT things for the game. Let me explain. If the person that grids in has the initiative he can attack first. This would lead to <gasp> no grid campers, or at least less of them. They would be staying away from the grids and the pvp would be much more fair and interesting to both parties instead of just title gathering frenzies.

And now about the large numbers of clan/omni gridding in. I LOVE that. It would add so much more to the game story wise AND fun wise. Think of it, your sitting in Omni-ent or Tir selling your wares or constructing implants when a general alert sounds "THEY'VE TAKEN 2HO!" I've seen this a few times and usually a few people go. But with the new 15 second rule I predict that full scale battles (which for me are more fun) will become rather common. I know that people do enjoy single pvp more that battles, and those will still be there too...but AWAY from the grids. yipee .


Cheers
