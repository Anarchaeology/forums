Going full agg (all the way to the right) takes .25s off of both attack and recharge. Going full def (all the way to the left) adds 1.75s to both attack and recharge. 0s difference is at the middle marker.

300 RI takes .5s off of attack, and 1s off of recharge. So to get the Manex down to 1/1 at full agg you'd need (2.5-.25)*600 RI, or 1350. Here's something I wrote for the bot we will be debuting soon:

=====
Ranged Init, Melee Init and Physical Init all work the same way. Player experimentation has shown that for every 600 skill in the init, attack speed is reduced by 1 second and recharge speed by 2 seconds. People refer to the overall speed of a weapon by separating attack and recharge with a "/". The fastest a weapon can act ("capped") is 1/1.

The advertised speed of a weapon (fists start at 1.25/1.25) occurs with the aggdef slider at 7/8ths (87.5%) aggressive. For every 1/8th (12.5%) move of the slider, there is an effect of .25 seconds on both attack and recharge. Thus full aggressive speeds the weapon by .25/.25 and full defensive slows it by 1.75/1.75.

Example: OT Kerans Automatic Grinner
Base speed: 1.5/1.45

To get this gun to 1/1 at full agg, first subtract the .25/.25 bonus of being full agg, making it 1.25/1.20. Since attack is higher than recharge and gets less benefit from init skill, calculate how much skill is needed to drop attack by .25: 600 * .25 = 150. This would make recharge drop by .5 (double the attack benefit) to .7, but it gets capped at 1.

The benefit to raising your skill further than where you are 1/1 is that you reduce your aggdef slider to take more advantage of your duck, dodge, and evade skills, reduce critical hits on you, make you less susceptible to init debuffs, and let you equip a scope to increase your critical hit chance while mitigating the scope's init penalties.

Note that for inits over 1200, the effectiveness of each point above 1200 is reduced, but no one has figured out exactly how that works. 1200 would cap a 3.25s attack speed and 5.25s recharge speed, so for weapons with attack or recharge greater than that it is not clear what the necessary init is to get them to 1/1.

Incidentally, Nano Init works differently. For every 200 skill, nano attack speed is reduced by 1 second. Recharge speed is never affected.
====

EvilSpork's calculation was right, except for the niggle that 1350 won't get the attack down to 1 second because of the > 1200 nerf. Also, the neutral point is not the middle of the aggdef bar, at least as far as attack speed is concerned.
