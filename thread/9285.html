Prize Fights, and gambling big bucks
Has anyone tried setting up prize fights, and running a book on the odds?

I figure someone could set up prize fights in the arena between martial artists, with no armour, weapons, or healing shots allowed. Implants and nano tech allowed, just because it would be too difficult to police. Apart from that they fight naked, greek style. Level differences, track record, (and any info about implants etc) would contribute to the odds.

For really high level fights the prize money would of course be very high, as it would attract lots of high stake bets. Could even get some of the bigger guilds to sponsor fights, as they would be sure to attract a lot of publicity. It would take a bit of organisation, and probably wouldn't start making big bucks until it was very well established.

Any thoughts? I just feel that there should be more ways to lose my money through frivolous gambing in Anarchy Online.

I figure the problem would be, as you say ganking. But I also figure you could see people doing that, and as this is a professional gambling corporation thing, that would make the fight null and void. All bets returned and everyone hacked off with whoever tried to buff one of the fighters. If it looked like the player was involved, they would be banned from future events (Winners don't use nanos).

The other problem is that events would have to be organised at least a week in advance, and then players would be off levelling up in the meantime which would change all the odds.

Maybe it would be better if people created prize fighters specifically for professional gambling purposes. They'd get xp from the fights right? Am I right in thinking you can see when someone has implants with that whirly thing going around them? That would stop cheating.

The other problem would be fighters 'taking a dive'. Seems to me if you offer someone enough cash, they'd think about taking a dive, and there will always be people willing to exploit it. The gambling corp would therefore need hardcore backup from a mercenary guild etc, which would cost wonga.

I mean, this is primarily a 'new gambling opportunity', rather than a chance for players to show off their uber-character. I think it could be a success though, and really create some superstars out of players who were prepared to fight honourably. The reason I say just Martial Artists is because this is a gambling thing, it would need to be as standardised as possible. Might work with other characters, could expand into that later.

Okay, I'm thinking of trying to roll with this idea. If anyone is interested here is what we need:

1) Financial backing. Probably about 1000000 creds for start up. Investors could buy 'shares' in the company which would give them a share of the profit. No guarantee of profit though, although exact records of transactions will be available on the website. Mercenary guilds might consider exchanging their services in exchange for shares.

2) Some Fixers to help me sort this out. You fixers are moaning about roles etc, well how about being semi-illegal totes selling bookie slips?

3) Some people interested in starting off new prize fighter martial artists, only for fighting in the ring. I'll make you famous.

4) One pair of red pants/trousers and one pair of blue pants/trousers for the combatants.

(5) Somebody who really understands odds, and can help me work them out.
