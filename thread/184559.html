Even though leets are cute and have a limited vocabulary I would not go as far as calling them intelligent. Rubi-Ka has several species capable of imitating human speech but few of them actually know what they are saying. I would compare them to the parrot of old, that could be taught to talk by imitating sound.

As for sedating the Medusa I will have to object. There are other ways to communicating with this species without having to use drugs. Simply being a female will let you approach these creatures to study them in a natural environment. That way you will know that what they are saying and doing are not affected by the drug.

I would like to congratulate Zargin on his efforts though, as the study of Rubi-Kan life is a grand task and needs lots of researchers. There is still so much we don't know about our world and it's wonderful ecosystem.

If you have any questions Zargin, don't hesitate to contact the Desert Winds research group headed by Hibernaculum.
