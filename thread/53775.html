They are Educating Themselves
Yalm's have no trunk space. So I pack only the essentials, a few nanopacks and a medkit, and a some backpacks to hold the fruits of my labor.

Just north of the outpost in Pleasant Meadows there is a rouge camp of Claws. A place frequented by the elite of both Omni and Clan alike. They tear through mutants six or seven at a time. I just come to watch, maybe take one or two in hopes of catching the big prize myself.

But as I search through the carcass of my kills I can only wonder what the reason is that we are drawn here. The search for Grid Armor or Nullity Sphere instruction discs has become the lottery of our generation. Claws carry these discs, that's common knowledge. Do we ever question why? Why these mutated horrors always seem to have an unending supply of informational materials? I've seen them on the Shades and Tacs in Lush Hills, and the Eye Mutants have been known to carry them too.

The answer is clear, but not one that is spoken lightly in Rubi-Ka.

The mutants are educating themselves.

Maybe these horrific creatures are not the mindless beasts that we all make them out to be. At times they seem organized, even intelligent. And what does that mean? They are only growing in numbers. Are we overlooking what may be a growing threat to our existence here on Rubi-Ka? We have reduced these creatures to meat. A process that makes their endless slaughter one of sport instead of genocide. Yet, when i look in there eyes I see things that wake me from my sleep at night. We have the upper hand now, but I wonder how long that will last.
