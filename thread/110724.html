The ONLY Clan Benefiting from Mining
Yet another interesting dynamic to the "Notum Wars" has recently emerged. In my first analysis, I examined the role of Omni-Tek and how it is directly benefiting from the deregulation of Notum mining. However, there is another party in this little dispute earning just as many credits; if not more.

The towers used on privatized mining plots - for defense and extraction - are the original design of Omni-AF. However, at some point prior to the erruption of war, the Sentinels acquired these designs, and undertook full-scale production of these towers. The Sentinels do not mine the notum themselves; they manufacture and distribute the towers to private clan citizens so that they may do so.

However, as the Sentinels acquired these designs illegally, and are producing these towers illegally, they are not remitting ANY remuneration to Omni-Tek for the use of its legally-patented technology.

As an aside, it is interesting to note that prices at the terminals that sell the tower components are identical in both Omni-Tek and Clan shops (this analysis makes no statement of economic conditions in neutral territories). Given that, as a business, Omni-Tek is naturally selling these items at a slight profit (taking account of R&D costs of creating the designs, manufacture and distribution of the towers themselves), it therefore follows that the Sentinels are ALSO selling at a profit to the Clans.

However, the Sentinels need not "sink" the fixed costs associated with the designing of these devices. They have no debt as a result of project planning. It is thus likely that the Sentinels are actually earning MORE than Omni-Tek in the sale of these items.

Given the chaotic rate at which towers are torn down and errected, it is quite likely that the Sentinels' financial power is substantial indeed. And yet, all on the backs of the people whom they supposedly claim to protect. That financial power is the blood, sweat and tears of clan members. It is the credits we use to help our young ones, to rebuild our cities, and to create our society.
