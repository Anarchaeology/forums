Supa Leet
If you haven't been to the end of the book, don't assume you know which side is in the right.

Nascence Key quest:

The Unredeemed 'curse' the 'souls' of various horse-looking creatures by putting their holy symbol onto a device which fits over the eyes of the creature (the windows to the soul).

The Redeemed 'bless' the 'souls' of various horse-looking creatures by putting their holy symbol onto a device which fits over the eyes of the creature (the windows to the soul).

Only words which are different are:

Unredeemed / curse
Redeemed / blessed

But who's to say that the Redeemed aren't lying? Who's to say that the Unredeemed aren't actually doing GOOD by capturing the 'soul' of these creatures and saving them from some worse fate? The concept of 'bless' and 'curse' is based upon the diety or pantheon which is in CONTROL and thus has defined the terms.

Point is that you don't know!

What we do know:

The Redeemed left the world FIRST, fled it, and populated other planets with their seed.

Then the Unredeemed got kicked out (we don't know why) and followed the Redeemed, finally finding that the Earthlings were 'similar to them'.

The Redeemed, with their power and knowledge, had CENTURIES to manipulate human kind into believing that:

redeemed == white == good
unredeemed == black == evil

Of COURSE they made you feel that the Unredeemed were evil, bad, nasty people and that their practices were to be despised. Because that way you HELP THEIR CAUSE BLINDLY, based on FAITH.

Let me clue you in. The Unredeemed ask us omni to kill Redeemed things -- we do it. The Redeemed ask you clanners to kill Unredeemed things and... YOU DO IT.

If you were truly moral, just, perfect, holy, whatever, then they wouldn't condone you killing things, they wouldn't ask you to kill things and they certainly wouldn't REWARD you for killing things.

You have had a morality -- Earth's morality -- imposed upon your sense of good and evil. You're not on Earth. You're not even on Rubi-Ka. You're in a place where two sides are warring over control over POWER, quite possibly the single most important POWER in the universe. Neither is GOOD or EVIL, they just have different methods to express themselves and like it or not -- you are their pawn.

King's Pawn to rank 4. Your move.
