The role of the Soldier
Hey all.
Gaute has just sent this up to me and here is the answer for:

How do you view the role of the soldier, where is it now and where it is going in the next 6 months?



The role of the Soldier:

The primary role of the Soldier is that of the tank; being able to take and deal damage. For this purpose the soldier has cheap body development yielding a high hit-point count, and nano programs that enable him to sustain more damage than anyone else (partial invulnerability shields). This gives him an obvious role in teams as the focal point of enemy attacks. Who hasn't wished for a soldier shield when clicking the boss-room icon in a team mission?

The Soldier is also the long-range weaponry expert, with the cheapest skills in distance combat weaponry compared to any other profession. This enables the use of high output assault rifles and special attacks with solid damage (burst, full auto). The combination of these enables the Soldier to be a steady source of damage and also to solo adequately. They are not the ones who are supposed to deal the highest amount of damage over short periods of time, nor the highest overall damage, but they should be among the best. They are, though, among the hardest to kill.

In PvP, the Soldier doesnt have a niche as the best at something specific - but there are few professions with an equally predictable ability to survive massive amounts of damage inflicted on them at critical times. Since they are also using long-range weapons, they are not as vulnerable to roots and run speed reduction nanos as i.e. the Enforcer.

Firstly, may I add that despite the HP development being cheap/green, there is relatively little difference at the high end. Considering the amount of damage that is output by mobs and other high level players, the 200~ (+/-) difference is minimal to say the least. The crux of the point comes down to the amount of damage that is caused due to critical hits versus more expensive evades. Also, given the perchant for other classes having good HP buffs, shields, etc, the TMS is just a stop gap. Another issue is that of bracers and other means of damage deflection, and that 2 bracers will successfully reflect 40% of damage from anyone, never mind soldiers. Although soldiers get to augment this further through using deflects, because of soldiers having a lack of HP-support skills (healing, etc), these are pretty much balanced out so that most classes can withstand quite a substantial amount of damage. NS Mk1/2 is probably better than TMS in 6 member teams at this time, because the delays imposed on the team are minimal.

If you want to reach the point of them being able to truely soak damage, then boost the HP-line of soldier nano's so that this is shown to be the case.

quote:
The Soldier is also the long-range weaponry expert, with the cheapest skills in distance combat weaponry compared to any other profession. This enables the use of high output assault rifles and special attacks with solid damage (burst, full auto). The combination of these enables the Soldier to be a steady source of damage and also to solo adequately. They are not the ones who are supposed to deal the highest amount of damage over short periods of time, nor the highest overall damage, but they should be among the best. They are, though, among the hardest to kill.
Umm... firstly, I'd like to comment on the fact that there are very few AR's that are viable for a soldier. The current situation is that soldiers require themselves to throw away valuable attack rating, in order to diversify in anyway. The current crop of AR's that are proposed/developed for 14.2 are proof of this, with there being requirements for HW/FA/SMG. It's all well and good giving us the skills to use any weapons, but there aren't that many weapons to justify such diversification.

As for proposing that they should be "among the best", this isn't helped by the current scenario of NT's and MP's using HGB's, or adventurers and traders using Nova Flows, which happen to be the only two real options that a soldier has. Given the usage of such weapons by other classes, they are able to out-damage soldiers through the use of the "class" skills that they have chosen.

quote:
In PvP, the Soldier doesnt have a niche as the best at something specific - but there are few professions with an equally predictable ability to survive massive amounts of damage inflicted on them at critical times. Since they are also using long-range weapons, they are not as vulnerable to roots and run speed reduction nanos as i.e. the Enforcer.
Again, this is debatable at the moment (see the above comments), but the range issue is one that constantly nags the soldier profession. Because people can judge range through the GUI (the little red markers), root/snare classes can accurately judge the amount of space that they need to function. Base this against the weaponry that is currently viable for soldiers, then we are a weak class. We have the time that the TMS is running to ensure that we either kill the opponent, or have to run to a shop. With mobs, range makes very little difference whatsoever, as they will either pick you up at incredible ranges, or you risk the chance of pulling multi's for having range.

The current lists of things that soldiers are calling for would seem to bring soldiers to the point that Gaute deems we should reach in 6 months.

Soldiers don't want to be the best at everything, but we don't want to have to look at other classes that have the ability to "tank" themselves easily, thereby creating a gab between class support skills, thereby making them more sustainable in most situations. I don't believe we have any niches whatsoever, and that is probably the reason that soldiers are calling for change. Alot of us feel like blobs that just stand at the front, we hit TMS, then wait 2 minutes hoping that we don't get attacked during that time.
