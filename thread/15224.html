Profession Mishaps
A few weeks ago Garzu pointed out how Nt's actually were not the best nukers in the game. "lol, whatta riot" I though... Sure enough, after looking over speeds and total damage, he's right...

Strange I thought. Why would FC make a master of something...not the master of something?

So a few minutes ago I'm reading through the forums and I see somewhere that Advens and Traders heal just as well and/or better then docs.

"LOL, whatta riot" I thought. So I go check out the nano's.. and sure enough.... Advens can solo heal as well as docs and traders can teamheal better then us.

Now holdon.. does this seem a little odd?

So here I am, happily invested in a character I thought was the greatest healer I could possibly maker. Little did I know I'm second rate. Why on EARTH would anyone want to pick up a doc in a high level team when they could get a trader?

The trader heals faster.. heals for more, does more damage, and can buff the other classes so they can cast better nano's. Sure, they take a 178 pt dot, but it's not like it stacks and your constantly recasting.

I'd like my best trait to be more then buffing people when they ask for "+80 plz"
