Your pet leet? Did he run away? he questioned.

No, he ended up missing.

Tell me his name and last place you saw him, I will look around.

You know who.last seen in Borealis.

The locals should help you. I know my pet leet, he wouldnt leave the area.

That is not a problem.

No worries, the cage is ready.

Good. Your leet will be found with little trouble.
