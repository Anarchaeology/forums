Tir was freedom...
One day I was standing in Tir. As I looked over the water, and it turned purple things were running through my head.

I saw the beauty of the city. Its splendor inspired me. You would not see such a thing in a huge Omni city. The gentle waters, turned colors by the setting of a Blue Dwarf Sun. Almost a year ago this was. My eyes tasted the sweet sight of things hidden by huge walls, and towering high rises in Omni-1. My mind knew freedom, and it was Tir.

Looking over the city now, its not the same. The people are less colorful, just as the city is. It saddens me to see life gone from the Capitol of our people. It saddens me to see the empty halls of the Council building. Tir is no longer a place for freedom. How can it be free, when those who long the most for freedom can not walk its streets.

Time goes by so slowly, yet time can do so much...

A tear forms in Fixerben's eye as he slowly walks away from the place that he longed to be...had found...and now has lost.

Her soldiers began letting her know thier well-wishes, and something caught her eye. She tapped her comm off and leaned on the rail along the river-walkway.

She saw the sunset against the Stret River and memories flooded back. Before the war and the constant bloodshed. Before the Clans fell apart and became wild. On thier wedding day, they walked across this bridge together. He knew how she always loved to watch the sunset on the river. She watched the sunset one more time, and felt her missing husband's hand around her again. Then the sun sank below the horizon and the darkness settled in. She shook her head and continued her walk down the long road to her apartment.

Tir is no longer free. And it isn't likely to be again as long as the principles of freedom are beat down and trampled by invaders like the Sentinels who come to Tir as despots.

Tir was free when I was born. I remember Omni, Clan and Neutrals alike traveling freely over Rubika.

Sure there were criminals on all sides that caused problems.

But then instead of dealing with the Clan, Omni and Neutral criminals we decided to exclude all Omni...the first blow against freedom.

Then we sat and watched while Tir was invaded...YES, invaded...by the Sentinels who call all Neutrals their enemies. And yet more freedom evaporated in Tir.

Why shouldn't we fight invaders? We fight Omni...why not Sentinels? Both seek to limit or eliminate our freedom. If you kill Omni then you have no room to talk about those of us that kill Sentinels.

You can't claim to be for freedom yet also only apply to yourself. Freedom is for all the people of Rubika. We either set the example or we wallow in mire of despotism along with Omni.

We the Clans have very little now to distinguish ourselves from the Omni we fight. Why is our brand of despotism any better than theirs?

I couldn't have said it better myself. I'm not a native of Rubi-ka, I was born offworld, and came here seeking a worthy cause to ply my (admittedly limited) skills as a warrior to. I thought I'd found it in the Clans. The fight for freedom from an oppressive overbearing corporate rule seemed like the perfect goal to set my sights on.

Then came the Sentinels. At first I wasn't too worried, after all they barred entry to Omni-Tek employees which just seemed like common sense, considering that any one of them could be some kind of spy or saboteur, and limiting such activities seemed like a good idea. Then I started seeing the Sentinels policies towards neutrals put into play, and read the articles about Fisk's psychotic ravings.

I have since avoided Tir whenever possible, only entering the city when absolutely necessary and leaving as quickly as possible. The fascist, despotic,overbearing, murderous venom being spewed by the Sentinels has begun to infect the heart of what was once a noble endeavor. I came here to fight for justice and freedom. I once had only one real enemy in my sights, Omni-Tek. I now have another, more insidious enemy to face.

The price of freedom is eternal vigilance. And I will be ever vigilant of this growing threat to the freedom of all Rubi-ka.
