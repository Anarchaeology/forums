Trader Healing Nerf is overkill ....
Ok, first off Docs should heal better . Everyone knows that.

The reason Traders have always healed better was the recharge rate. I more sensible "nerf" would be to rasie the recharge rate slightly, and maybe bump up the Dot dmg some to match the lvl of the heal. And also Docs should be masters of healing, recharge rates need to be lowered for them.

Now our 1k team heal that has caused most of the controversy cannot be used self buffed till 190+ . That means we have to Drain at least once, or at lower lvl's as many as 3 times to use it. The recharge rate on Drains are high, and in that time people can and will die without a secondary healer. (Talking about non-tanking classes fighting higher lvl mobs) . It is not easy to cast these, but even so.. when enabled they are very powerful.

But I too agree our 1k heal dosnt fit the class .. I would be perfectly happy using the 800'ish heal .. even the 500'ish as it is now with higher requirements. But "Nerfing" us from best to worst healers in the game is overkill. With a long recharge rate, yes the dots will be more effective. BUT our heals will not be worth using! And your thinking of raising the DOT time?! So everyone minute we can use 1 heal .. hmmm .. Isnt that what first aid is for? In 1 min I can get two first aid kits off .. which would actualy heal better, and guess what? It wont DOT me! If this goes through I'll throw my team heals in my useless collection of Charms and Health Plunders.

So my suggestion: Higher Req with lower point heals or longer recharge rates. And remember, there is a medium. And thats where we should be as healers.
