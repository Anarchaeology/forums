I don't PVP. Before I went to explore Steps of Madness, I'd barely ever seen a reflect bracer. So I was quite pleased the other day when I found a 7% Bracer of Reflection - Physics on the mob I had to find for a mission. Really helped against the Lifebleeder type mobs in missions. Of course, it'll be near useless after 14.5

Here's a list showing Bracers of Reflection under 14.5

As you'd be able to see, the maximum reflect stays the same, but the % reflected caps at 7%. Bracers for Elements, Nano and Physics go on the left wrist, Energy, Melee and Projectile go on the right wrist. Bracers are also unique now, so you can't have more than one type of bracer at a time. You could have one for each type though.

Bracers of Shielding don't change however. So you could have the Bracer of Reflection on one wrist, and a Bracer of Shielding for the same damage type on the other. Still not nearly as good though.

What will I do with my bracers after 14.5? Melt them down and make rings out of them. The bracers themselves are nodrop, but once you melt them down, you can make a ring from the metal and sell THAT to the shop.
