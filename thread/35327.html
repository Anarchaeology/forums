Fixer nano suggestion
A low level nano used for escape. The pros being getting out of a nasty situation, the cons being the run back to where they want to be. Similar requirements for Grid Phreak yet scaled for lower level and not getting full Grid transferral.

Backdoor Escape

QL: ~5
NaCost: ~100
Dura: Permanent
Rng: 1m
Speed: ~5s
Recharge: 2s
AtkSkills: Matt.Metam 34% PsychoMod 33% Sensory Impr 33%
Requirements: Use: Fixer Psycho Mod from `~80 Sensory Impr from ~80 Matt.Metam from ~80

Description:
The body of the fixer is encoded into digital form and transferred through the Grid to their their apartment. This program will not be able to make a connection if the fixer forgot to pay the rent.
