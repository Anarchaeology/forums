Well, I tend to believe that Bliqz is right...to a certain extent. Certainly writing the constitution will be extraordinarily simple. The true first test will be gaining enough support from various clans to ratify it. However, if ratification occurs, I'm confident that the rule of law can be established.

Don't underestimate the rule of law. By providing a civil system for managing complaints and disagreements, both internally and externally, I believe the Federation will provide a strong foundation for its own survival. There will always be fringe elements that will balk at the structure imposed on them by ANY government, but the law provides tools and procedures for dealing with those people as well.

In short, if we all agree (as we seem to) that a stable Clan government is a positive step, then let's all work towards that goal. If we fail, we are certainly no worse off, individually or as a society, than we are currently.
