An Idea For New Clan Only Armor
This was a just some type of armor I made up, i have the armor defense values if you understand there relevance.

Vivalamosas Kevlar Armor

Vivalamosa, a valorous clan soldier wanted to take a break from the struggle on Rubi-Ka, and decided to sharpen his skills on Europa, a cold watery moon orbiting around Jupiter. While training he enhanced his Kevlar armor along with his Keelar sunglasses to suit his needs and preferences in the harsh environment. To swim the icy waters he added swimming skill, to avoid ice and meteor showers he added evade skills, enhance his stature he altered it to increase his size, to protect himself from the cold he added cold protection, but in the process he irreparably damaged the fire protection. After seven years he returned to Rubi-Ka with armor that intrigued all who saw it and since it became so popular among clan members him, and his organization decided to mass produce his it, for clan members only of course.

Requirements:
Stamina
Agility
Sense
Clan Wear Only

Modifiers:
Evades: skills (I havent decided how much, and what types but it would be nice to have armor that raises your evade skills)
Swimming: each piece +10 to swimming
Size: Chest 4%, Pants 4%, Sleeves Both 3%, Gloves 3%, Boots 3%

Armor Defense Values:
Imp/Pro: 1.25
Ene: 1.25
Mel: 1.25
Cold: 1.75
Fire: 0.19
Chem: .75
Dis: .75
Rad: .75

(For anyone who doesnt understand these values that means it makes this armor as in between Waitt Cyber, and Graft Armor in defense, also this armor was only intended to be from QL 60-200)

Vivalamosas Keelar Sunglasses

Vivalamosa, a valorous clan soldier wanted to take a break from the struggle on Rubi-Ka, and decided to sharpen his skills on Europa, a cold watery moon orbiting around Jupiter. While training he enhanced his Kevlar armor along with his Keelar sunglasses to suit his needs and preferences in the harsh environment. For the sunglasses he engineered them to add to his perception, and ability to navigate maps. Also he added as much cold protection as he could to them. After seven years he returned to Rubi-Ka with sunglasses that intrigued all who saw them and since they became so popular among clan members him, and his organization decided to mass produce them, for clan members only of course.

Requirements:
Stamina
Agility
Sense
Clan Wear Only

Modifiers:
Perception +25
Map Navigation +25

Armor Defense Values:
Cold: 1.75
