No, thats 100% true. And what I'm going to say isn't really a statement about this thread, or whether or not docs need to be toned down.

AO is just so simple that any item, or a nano, or a line of nanos could be deadly in anyones hands.

You can pick that apart by saying "what if an enforcer fighting you didn't go full defense" or "what if a fixer forget to make sure his grid armor had plenty of time left" but the simple fact is any half-intelligent doc out there is going to be pretty uber against anyone. Hit that CH button when you are too low, the regular heal when you are just needing 1Kish heal. Throw inti debuff at start of the fight and fire away while you keep the heals up.

Any half-intelligent enforcer with a EQB will be pretty uber against anyone. You pre buff with all the fortifications, rage (and mongo before hand if you like) and go out swinging, nailing specials and mongo/challenger when you can/want.

Any NT with an AE nuke will be pretty uber against anyone. Target one person, fire the nuke, switch to blind. Rinse, repeat.

Trouble is, when one this is so far overbalanced that even those with no real experience in PvP can overwhelm people just because they uploaded a nano or equipped an item, then its time for Funcom to rethink the balance of said item or nano.
