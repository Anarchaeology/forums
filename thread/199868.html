We do this why again?
In all our efforts to become truly free, what is it that we have gained so far? Cities in ruin, bloodthirst passed down through generations and numerous accounts of senseless slaughter.

Is there nothing we have learned? Fight the oppression, fight for freedom, turn into that which we loathed from the get-go over time and now those that wanted freedom are as bad as the evil corporation.

Lately I have seen several free spirits though, people that still think it is worth to live a life without hideous greed, people still willing to give insteadof take, while all the time keeping a watchful eye for those that are not yet ready to leave the safety of the citywalls.

One such person is still aligned with the corporation, for the sole purppose of being able to get things needed by followers of the clans that can not safely go to cities such as Rome and Omni-Entertainment. A sole fighter, loathed by many clanners that do not see past exterior, frowned upon by the other employees for never wanting to deal with them. He puts his life at risk for those that loathe him and he stands strong fighting for his beliefs.

He is helped by a friendly metaphycisist, who sees it as her fate to help those needing help, through which she every now and then puts herself on the line, since every time she helps her friend, she is frowned upon by those clanners visiting the one safe area they all can go to.

Over time I have watched more people doing these things, however it griefs me that it seems that the conflict will never end, for the simple reson that noone seems willing to fight the just cause anymore, but rather go killing many people in order to obtain items that may bring them more money.

This conflict has been put back, monetary problems is what drives people. They want to be the best they can be, yet they try to accomplish this by killing, by evaporating anything that stands in their way and through that, loosing their entire sense of purpose, other than the purpose of murder.

Has life on the face of Rubi-Ka become what is was so long ago back on Earth, when all people cared for was money? Omni-Tek seemed to think so, until a lot of rebels went against them and started to fight for the good cause of total freedom. Yet now we are facing massdestruction, senseless murder, killing for the sake of killing all over again.

People do not learn. Give them a finger and they will rip it off and sell it. Offer them help, they will never utter a single word of gratitude. Offer them freedom and they will kill everything around them. Apart from a few, there seems to be no real reason to stay on this planet, however a cruel twist of fate has bound me to this planet, as it has done with so many, sine we were not evolved, but bred to only be able to live on this planet, without the ability to leave as other breeds have.

They gave us minds, but stole the option of exploring the galaxy in order to fnid a more peaceful planet. Life is not looking bright, optimism is more than not smothered, pessimism is reigning.

I am willing to smile, yet it is covered by a shadow of worry. We're bound by fate to be completely obliterated whenever the natural resources have been taken away and the other breeds move on to other planets to mutilate. I pray for science to find a way to give us life off world, for the way we are consuming this planet and ourselves is frightening.

Am I a pessimist? No, I'm an optimist that is running out of optimistic possibilities to see a bright future. An optimist that is at the end of his wits and can no longer see pink in the sky when the sun rises or sets but only sees grey clouds forming over our future on this beautiful planet's face.

This can be home to all, if only we learn to share in the riches of the world instead of devouring it and spitting out every bit we deem unnecessary.

I wish you all a good day, one without senseless slaughter, while I go mourn lost friends and family, they are not to be forgotten. Live wisely, stay out of trouble as much as you can and become the best you can be through sharing whatever you can with whomever you can without greed. Only then will optimism for a brighter future have a chance.

Life as is is not beautiful. It stinks a little.
