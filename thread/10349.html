Injustice: We must not give up, Down with the Clans!
*** ATTENTION ***
*** ATTENTION ***

This is addressed to all Omni-Tek employees:

The time to organize is now. Stop laying down like lambs, stop accepting brutality and letting murderers dictate how you live. The time to fight back is now!

I have been an idle participant for too long. I have watched my fellow employees lose life, limb, and peace of mind to the clans. We cannot alllow this to continue. We must stop all trade with the clans, all formalities (NO! I will not "duel"! If you attack me you attack Omni-Tek as a whole!), and all communications. We must stand in the areas of conflict and prevent any clan entrance. And we must attack! Not two days ago I saw a massacre of unpresedented size occur in Omni-Tek's Trade district. What made it even worse was that all I could do was watch as vunerable guards were shot down by malcious clan members. I was constrained by the clanners explotation of the wonderous Omni-tek invention known as Suppression Gas. So I say if they commit these acts in one of our cities, let us attack ten of theirs is such a manner! We have the resources! We have the able bodies! What we need now is the will to stand up for our brave company!

So I invite you, my fellow Omni-Tek employees, to make a stand! I am no leader, I am no agent of change, I am a single voice that is crying for justice! And all that I ask is that you raise your voice in the same cry and together we may march across the sands of Rubi-Ka and forever rid this planet of all Clanners!

Signed,
John Doe - A Proud Omni-Tek Employee

Perspective
In the years since the Homo Atrox was patented, I've begun to wonder if their utility as miners is worth all the whining. For being such a big, strong product they seem to complain even more than the Homo Nano - and at least Homo Nanos have a point, being trapped on Rubi Ka forever, and being forced to compensate for their physical fragility by a (lets be frank) pathetic dependence on nanotechnology to have any usefulness at all.

Omni Tek makes few mistakes, but one of them was worrying so much about the ICC to the point that they allowed illegal workers' unions to form. The sad truth is that most clan sympathizers are just misguided youths who don't know any better and believe whatever their clan leader poisons their minds with. Conditions in the mines and in the cities were acclaimed by the ICC to be not simply humane, but outright luxurious for a mining colony on a desert wasteland planet. But the union leaders, who were certainly insurgents planted by Sol Banking the way I suspect the Dust Brigade will turn out to be, stirred up all this discontent and now, suddenly, Omni Tek is oppressing the poor Atrox. I'd as soon see the clan engineers set their pets free, or give them the ability to replicate unchecked. Not only is that a cruel thing to do to the well-meaning but simpleminded Atrox, it's certainly a poor business decision as well.

Ah, Mr. Punk, those are very good points too. But remember the Atrox were designed very early in the colonization process. As a geneticist I can say with some authority that in the beginning, it costs millions of credits per pound to build heavy duty labor bots from material that must be imported from offplanet, but it costs much less to grow your own workforce from dynamic DNA in the lab planetside. Atrox are still fairly cheap, even more so now because food is quite plentiful, but in today's Rubi-ka I believe you're right - the various non-AI workerbots not only don't revolt and don't hold ridiculous ideas about their 'rights', but they also last decades longer on their original powerplants and are cheaper to repair now than when the mine collapse cost us all that equipment years ago and set the Atrox off in the first place.
