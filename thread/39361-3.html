Tokens might be an answer.

If you are lvl 200 you can visit an NPC to buy a device. The devices are very expensive. One version of the device allows you to preform IPRs. The other version of the device allows you to earn extra IP.

Device type 1:
Buy the device for... say 80mil credits. It holds 500 tokens. Once a token is earned, right click the token on the device. Once the device containts the 500 tokens, return it to the vendor to receive your 1 IPR. You can buy a similiar device for 500mil credits. But this version holds 2k tokens and provides you with a complete IPR. (The high prices will reduce some of the currency problems at high levels)

Device type 2:
If you are comfortable with your character but might want to bump up a few skills, you can earn extra IPs by purchasing device 2. Its very expensive and, like above, it requires tokens. Lets imagine it comes in a variety of forms.

100mil credits = 500 token board that gives 50k IP when complete
200mil credits = 1000 token board that gives 125k IP when complete
500mil credits = 2000 token board that gives 300k IP when complete

We could imagine increasing the price of these boards dramatically to the billion credit range. You can also increase the IP BUT have it so you can only do one board. So, for example, a lvl 200 player has a couple tasks to complete. He/she must hord the credits for a long period of time to buy the board and collect tokens to fill the board. It would be time consuming but very rewarding in the end.

The combination of the two devices does two things. First, it allows players who mucked up to fix their characters by EARNING IPRs (not given but earned). Secondly, it allows players who DID NOT muck up to continue to grow their characters. So if Biff here screwed up and needs to earn IPRs, he will fall behind Jim here who didnt screw up and who is now earning extra IPs. Eventually they will both be the same but they will be months or years apart..... Giving Jim a destinct advantage for not scewing up.

Just some ideas.
