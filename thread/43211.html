Play 'cenobite tag' through broken shores. Cenobite tag is where you and your friends wear social clothes, get runbuffs but no other buffs, and put range increases in your NCU belts. Then you find a cenobite and aggro it by casting a general debuff or general taunt on it. Then run. Your friends try to aggro it (tag it) onto themselves, and run if they get it. You're 'out' if you die. The winner is the person who can make the cenobite chase them back to Home. It's a draw if everyone dies.
