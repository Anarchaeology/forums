Suggest new backpack type: Cloth bag
The ability to customize your character with different clothes and armor is one feature that is well implemented in AO. You really have the possibility to make your character look different from the rest.

However, as outfits consist of many different pieces, changing between them becomes a hassle. Even if I ideally would like to use different outfits for different occasions, I don't bother.

The solution to the problem is the Cloth bag. A Cloth bag would be a special kind of backpack with one slot for each of the slots in the cloth tab. This way you could define an outfit by putting clothes in the different slots of the bag. Changing to a new outfit would then be just right-clicking on the bag.

Good idea?
