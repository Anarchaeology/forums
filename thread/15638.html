Mail facility
It would be nice to have the capability to send mail to another character in the game. It is sometimes hard to keep in contact with players in other timezones, and a mail facility would facilitate that.

You could also allow for mailing items to people too. That way when you have that special item that your friend has been waiting for, you don't have to keep it in your inventory until you see them next.
