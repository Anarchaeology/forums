A question
All OOC of course.

Thyme,

You brought up something that I have a question on: When does someone have to request permission to post about an rp'ed scene?

The answer I use is simply this: If a scene is rp'ed publicly, then that is giving defacto permission.

It is done that way in real life all the time. An event happens, and reporters report it in the news, most of the time without the permission of the people involved.

In game, a player that does not want to do a public scene can always halt their part of the rp, or have it taken to a more private place. All it takes is a simple ooc or tell stating that the player does not want to do a public scene.

I personally have never had someone complain about a story I wrote as a result of a public scene over the 7+ years of online gameing and role playing in MUDs and MMORPGs. Which is why I wrote the story.

I could use more opinions on this, so I know what to do next time this comes up.
