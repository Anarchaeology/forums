I'm still wondering if there's an explanation for why all these nasty monster/mutants aren't killing each other off like it's their national pass-time. Obviously it wouldn't work from a gameplay perspective, but these are very large creatures so I imagine they'd have to eat a lot to keep their strength up. Plus they're very aggressive anyway, if not downright evil. So err... why aren't the different types of monsters killing and eating each other? And how do they perpetuate their species - seriously? If they get killed by military forces of any alignment whenever they wander too near an outpost or city, and if adventurers and explorers of all alignments are out killing them for fun and profit, and if they actually are eating each other in some kind of mutant food chain... how come there are still any left?

Of course, the 'explorers and adventurers' (aka Player Characters) are really nowhere near the wilderness areas and the big monsters... they're doing missions in Home and most rarely leave 75% suppression unless it's to raid Tarasque or fight in 2ho. But conceptually... monsters would seem to have a very, very high mortality rate.

Somewhat on-topic tying these things together, I'm pretty sure the COT and other Clans don't mind that much that OT isn't aggressively trying to deal with the mutant problem, at least not in their territory. Since it'd be a good excuse to have lots of powerful military hardware in or very near Council territory.

This is one thing I have never understood about a unified "neutral" force on Rubi-Ka. It seems that neutrals want independence and the right to live their lives as they sit fit. Well that is exactly the same thing that the Clans want. Where is the difference?
The big differance is how we wish to gain this goal. The neutrals believe very strongly in peace. War is the last option, one which we truely do not want to pursue at all costs. I have to admit it is something that I will buckle too if provoked too far, yet it is something I will do my upmost to stop, be that war against the neutrals, or war between the Clans and OT. For example the organisation for which I am currently holding presidency over. We are at essence a political organisation. We hold strong ties to both the Clans and Omni. Why? considering we get complained at by both sides for being too close to the other? All because keeping lines on communication between the two sides, keeping channels for which both sides can discuss peace and work together is whats good to benefit everyone on this planet.

But on the other hand, we have a rather intreguing military force to back us up. We have coalitions both with the other neutral organisations which you might of read about in the press, yet we also have agreements to support and protect (this is the funny part) both Clan and Omni organisations in mutual defence agreements...Negotiating agreements to get mixed Clan, Omni and Neut groups to work together, also a trying task but it is achievable. We also carry a urmmm, PvP element to our organisation. Yes its something we don't overall use as its something which goes against what we are.
