Competition in the desert.
In the streets of Borealis when the suns go down a whole new society comes
out, a society of thrill seekers, and daredevils, these creatures form into
gangs and their style of war is fought on the plasticrete, street racing is
a very popular underground sport, where only the best reign, and the worst
reclaim, there is no room for error as the slightest flinch could send one
tumbling to his death, in an effort to raise public opinion of this sport
and those involved, a local Yalmaha gang, "The Night Crawlers" have decided
to hold a public race in Newland Desert, all Citizens of Rubi-Ka are welcome
to this race and party, the President of The Night Crawlers, Logan "Griynd"
Barchus spoke to one of our reporters earlier today about the event;

"We're just trying to show people that we aren't some group of terrorist
misfits, that what we do is a sport and a safe one, so we're holding this
race and party to try and let everyone know that we mean no harm, we have a
ton of prizes planned, and a live DJ, the whole thing is gonna rock, nothin'
like a desert party either, me and my crew will be there too, so stop by and
compete or just say Yo."

The Night Crawlers have asked us to invite all interested Citizens of
Rubi-Ka out to this race and rave in Newland Desert, they ask everyone to
meet at Stolts Trading Outpost at 21:00GMT on June 27th, there will be a
10,000 credit entrance fee for racers, we have also been told there will be
some well known sponsors and great live music, unfortunately Mr. Barchus
would not give us any details on who or what we can expect when we get
there, but he did say it would be worth the trip.

(Editorial note: This is happening on Atlantean)
