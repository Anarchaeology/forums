MAW, about the grid. BC's reply cleared it up but think of kind of like an old telephone switching station, but for people not phone calls. It's sort of a central place where everything is connected, and you can enter/exit from specific areas, or a Fixer can just hack into the grid from wherever apparently.

As to why they chose that instead of something like a subway, it's the future! Who wants to wait around for an archaic train when they can travel in style and speed with the grid?

Quinn, about doctors, sure, everybody has kits, but they can only be used while not in combat, and the person(s) have to be sitting. They are in no way a replacement for life-or-death battle heals, just a means of helping reduce downtime.
