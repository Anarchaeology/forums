Be The Difference
Ask yourself this question.

How can I make a difference?

We all know coming on the forums performing a witch hunt pretty much gets us nowhere. We all know we can cry foul when we see something like what has happened recently in the great community of pvp'ers on RK1.

It's an insult. It's an insult to the honorable persons who would earn their title by fighting against others in the true spirit of pvp.

For some people its all about being some uber twink with a title they deserve because they took that lvl 74 to the max.

For others its about being the highest level, scariest, meanest, toughest, lowdown rootin tootin SMG weilding smurf this side of the Pacos. (and don't you forget it)

And for some others of the community. Its about having something you didnt earn and expecting others to respect you for it. Maybe its about being someone in a position of power who's frustrated about a bug they cant get fixed and they exploit that bug just to proove a point.

No matter how you candy coat it, its cheating. It's cheating yourself out of the right to be respected for your accomplishments and its cheating others out of their goals because they wouldn't exploit like you did.

How can we stop this title farming? Save funcom doing something about it, here is what we can do:

See someone doing something odd? Report it, immedeately. Petition them on the spot and put "suspect possible exploit" in your text so that the proper arks/gm's are alerted of the situation.

Never, ever let someone you dont know and trust "test" their new gun on you.

Stay away from bad elements such as those who would sploit their titles up. You know as well as I do that there are certain circumstances where someone could accuse you of being guilty by association.

Finally, never allow yourself to be drawn in to this "crowd" of individuals who are pretty much worthless for all purposes as pvp'ers. Why? Because as honorable pvp'ers, we deserve better people than them to associate with.

I challenge you, both clan and omni. Report these people, make it stop. Be proactive, be assertive. Make sure the ark / gm you call takes notice of this person. Don't have a 3 second conversation of what you think happened in one scentence, follow it up with an email to exploits@anarchy-online.com.

I'm directing MoK to do this. Why? Because its the right thing to do.

These title farming exploiters do not deserve to be stuck to the bottom of your boot, lets get rid of em.

Arg out.
