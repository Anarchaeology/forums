the basic high level hunting is:

cyborgs (mort)

ace camp (EFP)

meds (EFP and DAV)

it is more difficult to organize high level hunting teams, powerlevelers are addicted to same ol boring missions...

there is a ton of stuff to do and explore, one of problems with high level hunting is some of these spots such as EFP and SFH have a large mix of mobs, it is simply more risky and you run into mobs much higher level that randomly spawn, so your team could get wiped out by a ql 150-200 mob if not careful
