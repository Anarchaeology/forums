Guild Pacts
I don't think this would be that much of a hassle to implement, and from what I see it's kinda important.

Guilds have been signing alliances with each other for a while. There's been the Galway pact, the Argentium Coalition, The Black Alliance, the Athen Accords, the Enclave, the Horus Pact, and other multi-org groups that have come and gone. Why not support it in-game?

Buy a little Application form, have the leaders sign it, and there y'go. Got yourself an official alliance and *maybe* a new chat channel, though that may get a bit cluttered.

Any thoughts, objections, suggestions?
