An Engineer Idea
Hi!

After being in a battle against a QL250 tower that suddenly healed up the moment we turned our back on it, I thought.. how is that possible? Do towers regenerate? Maybe it has nanobots that run around inside to fix things? No. Maybe they couldn't think of any better way to do it. Ah ha! Theres the real reason.

And so I present you with a better way:

Nano: Tower Repair
Class: Engineer
QL: Varies. Perhaps have a QL50, QL100, QL150, QL200 and higher.
Attack: 600s
Recharge: 10s
Special: Target must be a tower. Target must not be in a fight. (And so on)
Description:
This nano allows the repair of all towers placed on your org's ground, one tower at a time. This is the only way of repairing towers, making Engineers a highly needed class in the Notum Wars. Though the above attack time states 600s, this can be modified by the damage currently inflicted on the target tower. As an example, a tower that has taken 50 percent of its full health will lower the attack to 300s.

Well, thats the general idea anyway. I personally think its an excellent idea (but I would, as I thought it up). I really can't see how a tower that takes almost an hour to knock down, is suddenly on full strength again after 1 minute. Its ridiculous. This would also give some loving to the Engineers, who, as Engineers, I'm sure would love this.

Comments/Thoughts?
