Weapon Variety...
One thing I'm noticing is that in terms of weapons, there simply aren't many viable choices. It's obvious when you see the Div9 so much...the X3....etc. It's not the lack of weapon variety, as there are tons of weapons in the game. It's the lack of feasible weapon variety.

I just hit TL5 not too long ago and decided that it was time for me to move from MA to Rifle. In prep, I had been saving every rifle I could get my hands on from level 140 onwards and noticed some very sad things....

The first X3 I used was a QL 132. Didn't think it was going to do too well because of the low QL but decided to try it anyway. Wow.

That QL 132 X-3, when compared rifle to rifle, outdamaged an entire backpack of QL 190+ rifles (the Zastaba and Caterwaul were the only two even remotely close). It became very apparent why everyone using Rifle had one of these.

Then I got a hold of a QL 160ish X3 (thanks Isooctene!). Did the same test. The only combination of rifles that could keep up was the Caterwaul + Zastaba.

So why haven't you updated the other rifles in the game after giving us one that makes them all look like stinky bronto poo?
