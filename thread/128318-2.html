Biggest problem is mobs don't spawn fast enough. At least that's the reason I won't hunt outdoors. Standing around with your hands in your pockets is not a fun way to enjoy a game.

I can't count how many times I've gone to help someone kill things and then all of a sudden ask the guy 'where's more?' and he's like 'well ... we killed of them, so now we have to wait'.

Likewise, I go to Central Artery Valley and kill ALL the bosses in a circle and get back to the beginning and there's nothing there. Gotta wait for some undetermined amount of time before they start coming back. It's just no fun waiting for some guy to 'pop'.

That's why we do missions. You grab a mission go in and there's something to kill. And then another and then another. The atmosphere is cramped and the challenge isn't really there (yet). But, you don't stand around using paid game time doing nothing.

The other problem is camping. That's the reason missions were introduced in the first place and the other reason they are so popular. NOTHING is worse than gathering 5 of your buds together and heading out to hunt Medusas, for example, and then showing up and there's 28 other numb nutz just like you who are camping what you wanted to hunt. Might as well log off. Same goes for the static dungeons now as well. Now that you can't get into a Smuggler's Den, for example that isn't full of campers, it's impossible to have a good dungeon crawl where ya work from beginning to end and have a sense of accomplishment without finding some uber-guild spawn camping the boss or whatever it is that was your goal.

I really wish they would make outdoor missions. Utilize something like the Neutral newbie ground layout. Get a mission key and use on some gate at a crevice in the mountains and you and your buds zone into a 'hole in the wall' box canyon where you can hunt stuff and even kill a boss. I know it's possible for them to implement this. Hell, gimme a mission saying 'some uber bad man/monster has invaded Newland backyard 7 in the Junkyard, your job is to kill him' and when I accept the mission and zone into the by with my key, I got some bastard rez-killing the Guide and Guard.

quote:
Originally posted by Haibane
As an old-time player (since release), I can definately empathize with Halyera's positive nostalgic memory of outdoor hunting. I recall a time at release when hordes of optimistic Omni-Tek employees flooded O Forest and Greater O Forest with hunting parties. Some of the best times I've had in this game were while hunting in Rubi-Ka's great outdoors and I find it saddening that some people haven't had the chance to experience the rush of dealing with multiple social adds
I couldn't agree more. I've been playing off and on (a lot of off, but much on lately) since October 8, `01, and I fondly remember hunting. It requires more skill, is more varied, and is more exiting then missions or even dungeons. Hunting requires you learn what mobs are docile, social, and aggro. You get and have to learn how to deal with adds. You're often in the middle of nowhere so there is nowhere to run. Open field fighting is much more fun then the "r2r" fighting of missions, allowing proffessions such as Agents to actually use some of their skills. Going room to room killing one mob at a time has become rather tedious and dull. I remember this thing called "pulling," where one person would go out, find one or two mobs, then pull them back to the team. Nowadays, people think pulling is when the team runs up to a mob and "pulls" it 20'. There was something very exhilirating about fighting 40' tall brontos, only to see 2 more spawn and come running at you.

I am still amazed at the beauty of the Rubi-Ka countryside and am constanly finding myself taking screenshots--more so then in any other game--so it's a shame that 99% of people miss it by playing indoors.

Basically, where players choose to go boils down to a simple formula. Does it offer the best loot or the fastest xp? If it doesn't offer one of these then people won't go there. So, give outdoor hunting the best loot or the best xp (or even just a lot of outdoor only loot), and you will see people return to outdoors. Funcom, if you don't like the idea of a mobs dropping loot, just make them drop "partially digested such-and-such", where the person who gets it has to go to an engineer or somebody to have it fixed, but once it's fixed, it's uber and on par with anything dropped at the dungeons or in missions, except that this is stuff that only drops out doors (this would be a boon to the tradeskillers).
