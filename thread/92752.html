To me, this board is the home for RP and it would have been terrible if it had gone away, but there really is some great information on the fan sites. So, let me plug my other favorite web sites and message boards.

AO Zone is run by Silirrion and has a bunch of great guides for newbies and advanced players. Silirrion is a great guy and very helpful to all players. I use his site pretty often.

The General Discussion message board at AOVault is a very friendly community with extremely knowledgable players like Beaker (creator of Helpbot), Silirrion (webmaster of AO Zone) and even Morb, (creator of ClickSaver). These people are among those who answer questions, no matter how basic, and encourage others who drift onto the boards to give AO a try. I'd say that we average around three or four people per day asking about what AO is like or if it has changed since they last tried it.

Before you go asking questions on that board, you might want to take a look at our player-maintained FAQ board. It's not a place for asking questions like the General Discussion board, but is more of a reference area for more commonly asked questions and topics like How do I blitz missions?, Missions: General Introduction, Changing Chat Channels With Scripts, Optimizing AO and fixing crashes and Scripts. There is a ton of helpful threads that you definitely are not going to find anywhere else.

Take a look!
