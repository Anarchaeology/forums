Little Johnny, a middle-classed teen from suburban America, really wanted to go to Walt Disney World. His parents, knowing the value of a dollar, refused to pay for the trip outright, but were more than willing to implement a set of chores and goals for Johnny to partake in to finance it.

Little Johnny thought that this was just "unfair" and that the people "who have been saving for a year now" will be able to go on that trip before him! The nerve!

So, Little Johnny hits the streets. As one woman walks by, he shouts "I need cash for a trip!". The woman replies "you're young and vibrant, why not go get a part-time job?". "Jobs suck!" Johnny replied. The woman continued walking.

Frustrated, Johnny decided to knock on doors. At the first door, an elderly gentleman greeted him. "Can I have some money?" said Johnny. "Why, of course!" said the old man, continuing, "I could use a fresh coat of wax on my car - It shouldn't take long, and I'll give you 5 dollars". "I want you to give me money for nothing." said Johnny. He was greeted with a door in his face.

Johnny returned home frustrated and crying. "Why won't they give me money?" he inquired. Johnny's father replied "People are expected to work for their money. But, if it'll cheer you up, here's 5 dollars".
