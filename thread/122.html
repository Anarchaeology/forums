The Enlightened of Rubi-Ka (TERK) - RP Clan guild on RK1
We are many who are unified in one single belief, that the world about us, Rubi-Ka, is a living and divine being. Each and every one of the devout shares in one thing, our full worship and respect for Rubi-Ka. Currently within our ranks there are some whom call the Divinity the Great Mother and others Bantz the divine spirit of retribution. And as our ranks swell so will our understanding, for the Divinity has many facets and there are many ways of worship!

As the ordained of the Divinity we act as the ordained and sanctified guardians, protecting the Divinity (the world about us) from those who would abuse and warp the Divinity's gifts. With fervent belief, we do not yield nor shirk our duties to protect the Divinity nor will we hold our hand where righteous retribution is justified.

As the chosen of Rubi-Ka, we act as missionaries in bringing the truth to the unenlightened! We will share our belief, preach our belief, and debate our belief, but we will not force our belief for choice is paramount to growth. A forced belief is not one that will be tended but will be held only so long as the forcing hand remains. The Divinity wants those who are willing for with willingness comes commitment.

While we attempt to ramain apolitical, we support the clans. For it is the clans who truly understand freedom and truth; both of which are key to the support of our core beliefs. Unlike the Omni slave mongers or the uninformed neutrals, the Council of Truth, representing the clans, has been supportive of our formation... perhaps a bit hesitant but supportive. For that we stand by the clans in bringing freedom to the people of the lands.

And this is only the surface of what the Enlightened holds in store for you. Join us in our ordained guardianship of the Divinity. Join us in a mutual journey of truth. Join us in our exploration of the sacred lands of the Divinity. Join us so that your immortal soul can ascend to join with the Divinity. Come to us and be Enlightened!

DarqOmen,
Prophet of the Circle of the Enlightened

OOC:

We are an RP guild on RK1. For more information please visit www.guildportal.com and search on TERK or The Enlightened of Rubi-Ka. You can also contact Selpats, Adolanabantz, or myself (DarqOmen) in game.

We are currently forming and are looking for those interested in roleplaying and belonging to a guild that is a bit different from your average clan.

As we are a fairly new clan, rest assured that we are an ideal guild to get involved with as there is need for those who wish to get involved and desire to have a say in the guild. A key precept behind the formation of the charter is a democratic style of guild, where every member has a say in the guild!

We plan in the immediate future to be spreading the gospel of the Divinity, both within the Clans and even to the heart of Omni itself!
