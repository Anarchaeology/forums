The idea is neat, as long as it is kept to a minimum. I agree with the point of the world being huge. Hell, I'm already looking at missions from Tir City to Newland City that are faster to get to than missions in Tir City to Tir City, and are worth more than 3x as much credits... but a +30 run speed or something minimal like that would be great for those 15 minute jogs through the boonies.

I do have to disagree about them being used as sport, like Tony Hawk. That would make the game, which is already bogged down in complexity, a little more complex. I think it would suffice to add a few special social animations in, to jump, grab, and to other minor tricks.

I also disagree with the simplicity of the devices. IMHO, there should be, say, 30 QLs, each designating the bonus to run speed, and each fitting for its level; eg, a level 10 char would be best suited to the QL 10 board, +10 run speed. That maintains a good % boost, except for at low, low levels, which you're only doing missions in city for anyway, so it doesn't matter. There should also be some amount of skill required to use one; staying on a bloody skateboard is hard enough, not to mention one floating 6" above the ground and going over uneven surfaces. Say, an agility/sense or something score fitting to the aforementioned QL. Another req is that when used, they should be a constant "run" deal. When you stop, it dismounts back to your inv. This would also make any zoning errors a no brainer.
