Engineer loving =P
I've been thinking about this idea for a while. Meta-Physysists can create weapons, shields, and pets, however they last a limited amoutn of time since they are nothing more than a semi-physical creation. However, Engineers basically "build" their bots, so why can't they build their own weapons and armors through nano? I'm not sure how they'd make weapons exactly (and I don't, armor plus weapons from nano for an engy is overpowering), but I have an idea for armor. The Engineer, using Mechanical engineering, Matter Metamorphasis, and Matter Create skills, they can upload special nanos that allow them to construct themselves a back armor that resembles their bots, a shell of a robot so to speak that you can physically enter and manipulate. These armors, lets call them Botarmor Shells, will come with possitive and negative side affects. Lets start with negative:

1. It's back armor, therefor you're screwed from using even a trenchcoat with this on.

2. These nanos will block the mat.create skill for a time span of about 30 minutes and Mech.engi. for 15. With blocked mat.create they can't make new bot shells, so they;d have to think ahead.

3. There will be negative speed buffs in all areas except nano resist.

4. Nano cost is increased depending on the bot type being worn. Gladiatorbot shells might increase it 80% and slayerdroid shells by 500% (or is that just plain too much? Maybe 20% for glads, 75% for slayers?)

5. These should take ages to equip/unequip (about as long as a computer deck) let alone how long they require to be made. 2 minutes attack, 1 minute recharge should work well (gotta recover, right?).

6. You cannot have more than one botarmor shell anywhere on your character (even in bank or backpack), so you wear it or let it collect dust.

7. Without maintainence, these botarmor shells will be destroyed(your Back armor slot becomes empty when the Botarmor shell's "hit points" reach 0).

8. They're NODROP

Now for the positive affects:

1. The AC these provide have no weak areas and are essentially better than tank armor and even battlesuits

2. Nano resistance is increased depending on the QL of the botarmor shell

3. These are meant to absorb attacks, thus can absorb up to 5% of any type of attack depending on QL and bot type.

Now the other stuff:

This armor is basically an inactive shell of a bot created big enough and manipulated in such a way that it is usable and wearable. Botarmor shells have HP. Depending on QL and bot type they should range from around 100-1000. The botarmor shells only lose hitpoints when they absorb damage (and they do that coinstantly), so they will require maintainence. A 2nd set of nanos should also be created that are basically self heals and increase the HP of the botarmor shell ~ONLY~ note, however, these can only be done outside of combat! Besides, you can't attack anyway when nano progs are executed, and these should take upwards to 25 seconds for attack. Just remember, the lowest QL might only have about 100 health to it and absorb 1% of every bit of damage done. So 1% may only be around 5 points of damage, but that's already down to 95 health! Well, anyway, that's my idea for an engineer "love" patch. Ideas, critisisms, and modifications, etc., welcome
