Lately I am puller a lot which leaves me somewhat lacking for cash as I am already off grabbing the next mob before the last body has even cooled enough to loot. I do not worry much about chests, as I know everyone on my team and any worthwhile items get announced by the designated chest looter.

Its too bad some folks get so worked up about loot as this game is so non-loot focused, with the exception of a few rare items. Pretty much anything you need you can easilly go off and buy or solo for yourself. If you need something you can't solo, pick a mission that gives it to you as the reward and get a team, all the other stuff along the way is just fluff.

I remember back in EQ when you were doing the grind in some spot that had a chance of some drops...the whole team would be like "Whoa...awesome!"(or something similar) when a cool item dropped, even if nobody could use it. Its just fun to see them is all.
