Warp Beacon question for Funcom
Warp Beacon is quickly becoming one of the most powerful and useful nanos out there. I have nothing against engineers in the least, but I would like to hear from Funcom why they decided this was more along the line of engineer nanos than fixer nanos. Fixers are supposedly THE travellers, the masters of the grid and movement. They are also horribly gimped, a state that I don't believe engineers have a good claim to. The second most obvious class to get this would be Adventurers, the explorers who are responsible for getting people in and out of wilderness areas. They are also quite gimped (though not as bad off as the fixers, IMO).

So why give something that so obviously fits into the bailiwicks of two professions, bailiwicks that you yourself defined, into the universe of a group that has nothing to do with movement and that seems to be relatively well balanced (though by no means dominant!)?
