Sounds like some good stuff coming up.

Again, i urge caution when playing with crit chances. Much of the play balance in the game has been built around crit chances, and when they go, certain balance goes with it.

Yes, MA's and Agents are affected. Also LLTS scope users (like most weapon toting high level players) are hurt. But that's the simpleminded view. These factors are obvious.

The less obvious effects include the power of healing being increased, and the power of limited duration abilities being decreased. To name a few of these? Short duration crit buffs (agent), TMS (soldier), Challenger (enforcer). The extended duration of combat makes these abilities less usefull as should be obvious.

If i had to expand the over-simplified list of winners and losers from this change i would say:

Doctors and other healers get top spot.
NT's and non-critting types get next slot. (depending on wether or not they use a supplemental crit weapon or not)

Soldiers get last spot - their #1 ability is even less useful - as it will expire early in the fight - especially at low level. Add this to their low-healing ability.. Ugh. On the bright side, they will have a little bit of time to see where the fight is going - it's just that the poor rooted barstads can't do anything about it lol.
Agents get second last. The crit-nerf/limited-crit-duration combo hurts them quite badly
MA's get third last. I would rate them worse, if it were not for their heals.

Not that i expect any drastic changes - i think it's more pertinant that these factors are considered in the future.
