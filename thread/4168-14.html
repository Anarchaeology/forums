My 2 cents about exploitment, IPs, concealment, etc
Please keep in mind that for every good idea I have, there's usually a glaring obvious reason looking me right in the eye. So go ahead, have your laugh, don't toast me too badly

I fully approve of measures to do away with exploitment. I never catch on to them until I see an explanation of how y'all got rid of it, but I did hear of one last night, a level 7 something-or-other with 110 mission tokens. 'How did he do this?', I asked. I thought you had to pretty much kill every monster in a mission in order to get a token now. As it turns out, he terminates before levelling, then re-enters the dungeon. So he's lost the xp, but he completes the mission, gets his token, thank you, drive through...

Seems like an easy way to fix that would be to put a minimum level to equip the shoulder bar...

About IP's.... why not allow people to recover the IP's anytime they wish... but not the full IP value? I restarted my adventurer 3 times.. the first time because I wasted tons of ips in stuff she never used past level 10, when she got decent weps. The second time, I figured out that melee was all fine and dandy, but since I always heal on my two-person team (with my agent friend), I really needed to be using pistols. Close combat *and* heals gets me the aggro, and gets my heals interrupted.

In addition, and this is where nerfage comes into play, some of the weapons, like the Freedom Arms, made it worth investing in the dark-blue-to-adventurer skill of ranged energy. Then they nerfed the damage/skill requirements. Was it a good idea? Yes, because that particular weapon was over-strong compared to other weps in the same quality-level. However, I was offline, moving, and missed the nerfage. I was surprised to come back, log on my third adventurer, and figure out that all that ranged energy was wasted.

I'm not saying you should make ips fully recoverable. I think that some, like weapons, might be 66-75% recoverable from the investment (i.e., if I raised my pistols skill to 100, I should be able to get back enough IP's to raise it back to 66-75 in the event of a skills wipe). Some skills shouldn't recover IPs, like nano-programs, primarily, I think, because there's less opportunity to waste. Maybe I won't use that jacket of blades buff, but I'm going to use the biomett skill it requires for other programs, like heals. Anyway, y'all get my drift.. I hope...

On to concealment... they've definitely fixed some of the problems. For awhile, my agent friend couldn't sneak in dungeons, which made it nearly impossible to use aimed shot. The monster was *always* aware of his presence, and half the time, aggro'd before we were even close to the door. But... I think the agent should be able to roleplay what a classic agent is: a master of disguise, including to other players. And I think that should include alignment. For example, an Omni agent should, at higher levels, be able to disguise himself as a neutral or clan, and infiltrate other player's meetings. Or extend the concealment to other players who invest in the skill, based on the skill level and actual level of the player. As another example, perhaps a level 50 fixer with max concealment the ability to conceal information about himself from a level 15 player, or change the ability of that lower-level to see him on-screen. I have no idea if this is something that is possible to do *shrugs* just an idea

Anyway, I guess I've talked your eyes off enough

try these formulas on all of your stuff:
(curr=current skill, buffs=buffed pts, req=item's required skill, debuffs=pts your skill can be debuffed)

no buffs:
100-((curr/req) x 100) = overequipped%

If you hit negative numbers, you're not even equipping up to your current level..

add your buffs:
100-(((curr+buffs)/req) x 100) = overequipped%

now figure in a debuff that could be used on you at your level:

100-(((curr+buffs-debuffs)/req) x 100) = overequipped%

and go look up what a debuff (remember, some stack) will do. Heck, anyone can even go get the standard -12 ones..
