If you aren't in the right area, or don't know the right names to put in the Location list, leave it alone. At low levels you won't get many missions that are far off, and if you do you can just press OK, and then START again and it starts searching again for missions.

Caterwaul <-----rifle is mission loot and sell pretty well at any level, I would add that to your Item list.

Anima <----short duration Damage increaser for the MP Attack pets, +36 at the lowest level, Ql 14. Very nice.

enfraam <-----the Mission only NT nano , it's a AC and HP buff for Nts, something most greatly Prized.

Distracting <----MP mezz pet, though this also gets 2 other nanos, which aren't so Rare, any Distracting Sphere types sell well usually.


Nadir and Obtru <--- the two types of improved Steel ribbed armor that are mission loot, available at QL 1

Vito <----Not vitos, Good Str stam armor that is Gold, and covers Chem as well as the Main types of AC well, available at QL 1

Beam <--- this one may cause you more trouble than not, it pulls the E-beamer, heavy gamma beamer, Etc, but the Atrox enf only Weapon is also a mission loot, and usually sells well.

SOL <--- Pulls all soldier nanos, but also the Adv only blades that sell well.

For Clanners, Keran <----The Fast firing SMG that Does Poison Damage that most low level Fixers want to get their grubby little mits on This is available in OT stores, but not in Clan stores, so it's Valid mission loot for Clanners.

All the above Items are available at level 14-->Higher.

Just thought I'd better point out that Nadir only becomes available in sets at QL50, although for some reason the sleeves show up at all QL's. Obtru starts at QL75 and isn't available at all below that level. That makes QL50 a good point to blitz full sets of Nadir and QL75 the same for Obtru, since people like to get this stuff on ASAP if they're using Sense/Agi armour.

I made my first million selling a full set (inc. both sleeves) of QL50 Nadir Steel-Ribbed armour.

If you're looking for Beams, use the search term "Support Beam" instead of just "Beam". That'll prevent CS from looking for E-beamers etc. Likewise for SOL's, use "SOL Fire" or "SOL Fire Executioner" instead of just "SOL". http://auno.org and http://www.aodb.info are both invaluable for checking spellings and exact search terms. If your search term doesn't produce unwanted results when you use it on one of the databse sites, it won't produce unwanted results in Clicksaver either.

Railex

That error message means you typed the names of the items incorrectly.
A. Make sure you have the spelling right.
"Worn Cyber Armor" is corret. "Worn cyber armor", "Worn Cyber armor" is not correct.
Every capital, dash mark, number, space, must be in the item name.

B. Check the number of times you set the search for. Make sure its not 0

C. First try click saver with all the sliders in the middle. And the difficulty setting in the middle.
Then try it with the mission difficulty setting a bit to the right.
If the difficulty setting is too low, CS will give you an error because some of the items
will not be found in a too low quality mission.

D. Make sure the mission setting is high enough to find the Quality Level of the item
your looking for.

This site lists items, and the QL of items including the minimum and maximum!
http://auno.org/
