Enforcer pre-SL 2HE weapon progression
Here's what Enf's did before SL. Well, from what I remember, anyway

lvl1-35ish Katana(just regular, old Katana, no fancy words before it)

lvl35ish-50ish Frost Scythe

lvl50ish-85ish Stygian Desolator

lvl85ish-110ish Frost Scythe

lvl110ish-161 ql200 True Katana

lvl161-185 Queen Blade

lvl185---- Enhanced Queen Blade

Now, note that this doesn't include SL 2HE weapons, as I'm not familiar with those. This also doesn't incluse Frost-Bound Reaper, which is from Inner Sanctum and also quite nice.....I'd probably put that in at lvl125 when you can first go to Inner Sanctum. Longmoon was also an old standby 2HE weapon for some.

Sadly, as mentioned above, the Smith katana, as well as Corpse Cutter, are Enf-only so you can't use 'em.

This was also taking into account the high Melee Init Enf's can have with Rage, so maybe Keepers would want to go with Longmoon at 110ish.

For a truely excellent guide, read this thread. Sure, some things in that thread won't apply to Keepers, but many *will*. And if you're on RK2, and reading that Guide helped you in any way, please send Deng a tell to thank him for writing it =)
