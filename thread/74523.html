This is possible already for teams
and here's how you do it.

You have to be in a group of at least two, but can be up to 6.

Have all members of the group in a different zone when the mission puller pulls the mission. Then cycle through all members until each member has 3 missions. Then each member updates the others once all members are in the same zone.

Example with a 6 person team set in Tir at the Fair Trade

Members 2,3,4,5,6 stay inside the fair trade while Member 1 pulls three missions. These missions member 1 pulls will not update on the other members screens. Member 1 then zones into the FT and Member 2 zones into Tir, and pulls 3 missions (the check for other groupmembers only extends to the same zone). Repeat this with each groupmember, making sure that the other groupmates are in a different zone as each mission is pulled. Once each member has grabbed 3 mission each member updates the others, thus resulting in 18 missions, which will all display in your mission window.

Its not possible in individual missions, but this is already possible in team situations.
