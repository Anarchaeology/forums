Omni-Pol is expanding its ranks!
Hello and well met,

the Omni-Tek guild "Omni-Pol" is now actively recruiting new members into its ranks. We are a department style guild, lead by me as President and several Generals who lead their own departments. as an Omni-Tek guild which is loyal to Philip Ross, we obey all orders given to Omni-Tek employees by Omni-Admin. The amnesty is active and we follow its rules. But: 2HO and 4 Holes are Omni-Tek property which must be protected from clans/Omni-Tek traitors who seek to overthrow the Omni-Tek presence in those parts of Rubi-Ka.
Omni-Pol Generals are those who have most power in Omni-Pol, due to the freedom they have regarding the handling of their departments. A member who joins us is Applicant for at least 1 week and might be promoted to full membership after this trial membership. After your promotion to full member, you will have the choice to join one of the guild departments and start your career in it by being active in the guild, loyal to Omni-Tek and Omni-Pol and showing outstanding interest in the benefit of the guild. To enlist, please check our website at http://www.omni-pol.de.vu and read the "How to join" section of the site. Please contact General Coldblooded (preferrably) if you wish to join and he will do the recruitment procedure with you.

Thanks for your interest and best regards,
