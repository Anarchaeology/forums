From reading the responses on this thread, I've come to the conclusion that those of you having such great success with this advice are experienced players who have created newbie alts in order to take advantage of Thedeacon's advice.

I say this because all of you talk about items (weapons, armor, etc.) and other things with which I, as a total newbie (have played for only a few days -- now a Clan Solitus MA at level 10), am completely unfamiliar.

I don't enjoy blitzing. I'm not a power-leveler. I enjoy taking my time in missions. I like to sneak up on mobs. I'm in no hurry to advance quickly. I prefer soloing to teaming because I can proceed at my own pace and in my own style.

However, I do prefer to earn my own way, and I have discovered that nanoprograms, implants, armor, and weapons are incredibly expensive for the beginning player. So, naturally enough, like you, I am eager to earn as much money as possible.

Using ClickSaver, I have learned that all is not "roses and sunshine." Most of the missions for really high cash are located in geographical areas that I'm unfamiliar with and that I dare not enter alone at level 10. For example, I went to Camelot, hoping to get a high-cash mission to Old Athen, W. Athen, or Tir City, areas in which I can do missions safely. The only high-cash missions available were out in the middle of wide open country, far from any city, in areas that I had never heard of, much less ever been to. So I have learned to content myself for the time being with local missions that earn from 500 to 1,000 credits, which I get in W. Athen or Old Athen.

I don't put the mission setting on 100% Hard, because I have learned that I die at the entrance when I open the door and all of a sudden three red mobs aggro me. I can hardly handle one red mob, much less three at a time. And it takes ages to heal after defeating a high-level mob. So I set the mission to 50-60% Hard, which works out very well, but, of course, earns me only half as much in credits as a 100% Hard mission.

The 100% BAD and MONEY settings work very well. The rest of the settings I leave alone, because I've learned that I get the most interesting missions (with the greatest variety of mobs, treasure chests, locked doors, etc.) that way.

Today I'm going to set ClickSaver to QL10 Concrete Cushion and see what geographical area comes up, or if any area at all comes up within, say, 20 tries. At 10 credits a try, the cost of finding an item adds up quickly. If the geographical area is out in Podunk County somewhere, then I think I'll forget about locating these high-cash items that Thedeacon has spoken of, at least until I become much more familiar with the geographical areas and with what-level monsters populate those areas.

I've written this not to discourage players from using Thedeacon's method. Far from it. I just wanted to present an alternative view to the "WOW! This is fantastic" responses, a view that is based on my own experiences as a complete newbie, so that other complete newbies won't get discouraged and think that they are at fault if this method does not work out as well as it appears it should.

I welcome any constructive comments, and wish you all Happy Hunting and Good Fortune!
