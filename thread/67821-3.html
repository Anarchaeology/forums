Interesting thread, I agree 100% with the side saying this is a dungeon for newbies, not for newly rolled twinked out characters played by veterans. I think a lot of the veterans can't rewind their own uberness and remember what it's like to be a noob. You are scraping for cash for everything, you simply won't know about what the best such-and-such weapon or armor is, and even if you did you probably couldn't afford it. The subway was put there for noobs, if you're rolling a new character to get in there for some loot that's good for high levels, then they need to address that and remove that loot.

But the point I wanted to add was, I think they did a great job on the subway mostly, if you think about the loot that drops, it's mostly only good for noobs. The morphing memory has a psychic check so real NCU is much better. But morphing memory ensures that a noob will at least have 1 NCU chip that is of equal QL to their level, or near it. Then you wont have as many noobs of level 15 with QL10 NCU memory running around.
The vegabond cloak is another example, its a great noob item, basically gives you a bunch of evades, but far better items are for higher levels.

Now comes what they did screw up. The NCU rings. I mean hello, its not nodrop, +20 NCU on a ring is pretty nice, so right now you know that the greedy twinks will be in there farming them, totally preventing noobs from having a nice hard monster to fight that drops a nice item for them. Sorry but that item is for the level 24 twinks, thanks for trying.

So for the most part they did a great job, I think they just need to remove the nucleus basalis NCU rings from the drop tables, or make it a nodrop. Or make some clever way, like with morphing memory, that its no good to higher levels.

Oh whaa I guess those farmers wont make 10 mil per ring anymore, but hey you know what, 10 noobs shouldnt have their good time ruined because of 2 twink, its just not good customer service. They can look forward to being outdamaged by people 100 levels above them in other dungeons. Leave the subway for the noobs please.
