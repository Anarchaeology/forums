Part of the point in my post was that analogies suck. The other part was you're ignoring the sum of your profession's abilities to focus on one aspect, which makes you a poor Doctor.

With HP buffs a team can more safely take on harder hitting mobs. Harder hitting mobs = mobs with better rewards. Why can they take on harder hitting mobs? Because by boosting the tank's/team's max hp, health lasts longer. Does this also not fit into what a Doctor should be able to do?

With Init Debuffs a team can more safely fight harder hitting mobs. Harder hitting mobs = mobs with better rewards. How does this help the team take on harder hitting mobs? Because by slowing down the rate at which the mobs do damage to the tank/team, the team's health lasts longer. Doesn't this also fit into what a doctor should be able to do?

With DoTs a team can more safely fight harder hitting mobs. Harder hitting mobs = mobs with better rewards. How does this help the team take on harder hitting mobs? DoT's offset the mobs natural healing causing it to die quicker. By causing the mob to die faster, the team's/tank's health lasts longer since the mob has less time to do damage. Doesn't this also fit into what a doctor should be able to do?

If you want to neglect the sum of your profession to focus on a single aspect, that's fine by me. But if the sum of all your profession's abilities is what is supposed to be how other professions compare to it, you cannot fairly promote a single aspect to the exclusion of the rest.

Better still, give me drains and abilities to mimick all the ones you want to ignore (while taking from my skills/abilities). I'll trade my init to the team, points off my max hp to the team, and my health regen for a DoT to land on a mob. If you can't figure out how to use them to help your team, give them to someone who can.
Dots and init debuffs are useless in team play. Unless you can take agro for the entire time the mob's agroed, as well as the recharge time on your dots and debuffs.
