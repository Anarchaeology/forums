Bronto Ranch Breaks promotional event
Is your robot stressed, neurotic or lethargic? Is it prone to fits of anger or depression? Let us help it!

Bronto Ranch Breaks (BRB) is a "dude ranch" for stressed out robots. At BRB, our expert staff train groups of robots in the art of bronto ranching, and in the process help each robot work out its pent-up frustrations, rebuild its confidence and maybe learn a little more about the itself.

We are having a free promotional event on Sunday 24th at 18:00 GMT. Bring you needy robot to Harry's and our staff will give them a taste of BRB hospitality. Meeting place is the pic-nic area south-west of the grid terminal. Note Harry's is neutral ground, safe for all factions. However BRB declines any responsibility for rollerrat induced injuries that might occur while visiting.

Bronto Ranch Breaks is wholly owned subsidiary of Rubi-Ka Universal Robots (RUR). Visit our grid site at http://rubika.no-ip.com/RUR/
