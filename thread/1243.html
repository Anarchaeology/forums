The Pillows
I play a Nano Tec, and keep seeing Nano Tecs running around with pillows. I know they give good bonuses to nano skills, but they look terrible.

You have managed to create a game that looks stunningly good, and yet you leave in such a ridicoulusly looking item. This lessens the experience for everyone who values the esthetics of the game.

Im not suggesting to get rid of the pillows as such, but replace it with something that would look cool.

Come on, you can do it. Design something else with exactly the same requirements and bonuses and just change it. Easy as that.

It sure would make my day along with a whole bunch of others.
