Neutral Leader Missing, Presumed on the Run. (FNN News Story)
Neutral Leader missing, Presumed to be On the Run.

A neutral Org, The Mercenaries of Hythloth, came to us claiming their Leader was missing.

Apparently, Earlier in the Week, Tim "Punkvision" Western, was in the process of completing a routine job in Omni Entertainment, when he was seized by authorities. He escaped and then went into hiding, dodging his would-be captors.

No one knows whether his captors were Omni-Pol, or another group of Omni Affiliates. What is known is that a short trasnmission was recieved by the Mercenaries, in which Punkvision claimed he was in good health, and not to worry. However, as stated by Mercenaries representative Kirk "Thepersonman" Pillot, "He always seems to make it back to us, but this is the longest he's been away."

The nature of the job he was doing is unknown, but it was discovered that the Employer was a man named Mr. Coteau. Mr. Coteau was unavailable for comment, and no one knows for sure who he is and what he wanted from The Mercenaries. It is believe that he as well has gone into hiding to avoid questioning.

The Mercenaries are in the process of organizing search teams, to scour Punkvision's last known whereabouts, Omni Entertainment. They as for any help they can recieve in their attempts to find their Leader. If you'd like to help in the search, or have any information regarding Punkvision's whereabouts, the Points of Contact within the The Mercenaries of Hythloth are Kirk "Thepersonman" Pillot, and Cyrus "Mercurialis" Ganzer.

Punkvision, if you are out there, be rest assured that your troops are concerned about your well-being, and are doing their absolute best to try and find you and bring you to safety. Use all your talents to stay alive, and free, until they can gather forces to get you.
