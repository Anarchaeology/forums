Old friend... new foe
It was during the meeting where a few Clan organization leaders and clan organization representatives sat to begin the talks of the idea that has sprung up towards the reformation of the Council of Truth when he came. One of the few disruptions in these talks, and for what Zane knew, one of the many upcoming disruptions. Bombings in TIR and then his former friend Lurai appeared to disrupt the show. Yet even after his disappearance, Lurai was in his head, beckoning him, after making his appearance in the initial disruption of these meetings. Lurai, a person he once called friend, now becoming a wall in between the road that Zane was helping to work towards... and that was a road for a voice within his fellow clan members.

"Come south to meet your death Leet!" The man cackled in his ear. Carl degraded the leader, confusing him, beckoning him to come to face him. Paranoia set in, Zane began to feel as if HE himself was loosing control. He began to feel weak, a feeling he hasn't had in some time, and that very feeling is his biggest fear. From the time he first arrived onto Rubi-Ka, he remembered the times he called out for strength, where he called out, wanting to get stronger, promising his family that he would. Time passed, and he did, but now it seemed as if he was loosing his strength and his mind all over again. This loss came in an outburst was made as the meeting as those there looked at him, this was followed by a mistell regarding a child he had recently taking under his care.

"Can't you feel the touch of death upon you?" The voice continued to call at him, to tug at his every will, his every feeling. It continued to push him further into wanting so very hard to make the man pay for his digressions. Yet Zane was slowly entering the stage of lunacy and anger.

Zane stood, making his speech as questions of his mental condition were posed and he did, not long before calling out and running towards the gentleman, waiting for him upon a hill south of their meeting place.

"I won't let you ruin this!" Zane said as he snapped at the man he once knew as Carl. Now having the Sabertooth take over his body he was lunging, and swinging his paws at him, trying to strike him down. As he did so, Magan belittled him jokingly wondering where he was going.
"Bad Kitty! Where's your pass?" She said teasingly as she did not know that he was in an epic battle against Carl. The beast swayed over Zane, he growled and drooled at the mouth as another paw arched for his foe, leaving his neck open for Carl's counterattack - a needle placed and his drug and hallucinogen was applied.

Anyone that came near became a foe... Usia his fellow family member, Jen his friend, and Anthony all who came to his aid were snapped at. The poison made Zane weak, and weakened his speech became nothing but growls of rage and snaps with of his fangs and teeth towards those around him. All of the scents he inhaled around him were too much for him, causing his head to further spin and his vision blur.

He knew he hit something or someone, and he had hopped it was Carl who he finally got a good scent on. Carl made the mistake to be still for a brief moment and talk his rhetoric. His attack towards the mad man only caused Zane to be flung into the nearby tree, after having struck him a second time. The first, Zane left a minor wound on his arm... the second reapplied that, but this time he was on top of his chest.

That was when the poison continued to settle in, further weakening his body. This poison, further revealed by the villain (in the traditional way that all villains reveal what they have done), exclaimed that it really was a combination of thousands of nanobots within his system, contaminating him, causing him to deteriorate. Yet Magan, being a good friend of his and his blood family Nexeus, did her part to save him overloading the nanobots within his bloodstream so that he could later dispose of them through natural means. When that time came he made a mental note to make sure that sometime in Carls future he goes through something that excruciating. Zanes saving grace through was his friend Jen who whispered into his ears who reminded him of who he was, having been a part of him for a brief moment once.

"Remember what you told me Zane!" She said in hushed tones, pulling his snout close to her neck so that he could trace a scent from her as she continued to plead from him. "Remember me Zane!"

She, more than anyone else knew him best, knew what he was going through because of the period of time that they had become one... in some sense. Her words reminded him of their experience in TIR, where he had to wake her up out of her daze, her scent reminded him of his friends and the reasons he was here to begin with. It was a combination of those two and her beckoning for him not to give up that caused him not to give up at that tree. And with a growl he sprung up, still weakened... but better than before.

"Thank you Jen," He said as she continued to explain it to her fiancé Anthony who was at a loss at what happened. Her explanation, he felt, was only accurate enough for anyone to understand. Yet he was grateful more than anything else... but began to see what this process was really going to entail.

It had been a couple of months now. Zane and Jenny had taken on an assignment together in Home, cleaning out a warehouse and reclaiming it from Omni-Tek personnel. They both needed someone to talk to, it seemed and maybe it was easier to talk while doing active battle than to sit in The Cup fiddling over cups of coffee. They had had a good night, and it was also the first time Jenny had seen Zane morphed into a sabretooth. It was quite peculiar, she thought, how his human instinct so perfectly blended with his feline instinct. His eyes, looking over her, with a glint she couldn't quite interpret...was he friend or predator? His paws, so big and powerful, claws embedded in the soft fur, claws that could shred her to pieces if he wanted to. She wasn't scared, but more...apprehensive? Zane was not Zane...more like...a hunting cat, the instincts of the wild.

The assignment had gone well, as the Omni-Tek personnel in the warehouse was underequipped and easy to take out. They had worked well together, herself, Zane and her manifestations. However, on the second level of the building, the resident personnel ganged up on them and Jenny, not really concentrating, had fallen to several shots to the chest. As death once again welcomed her, her last thought was that she hadn't told him about her faulty reclaiming lately.

She woke up in Tir. She was blind and couldn't move. Each breath of air was a fight for survival, as her newly rebuilt lungs struggled to draw oxygen for the first time. Through her comsystem, she could hear Zane calling her name, but she didn't have the strength to answer him. His voice became increasingly worried as she failed to transmit to him. Her vision started to return, slowly, and she only saw a dark fog with barely outlined lights, dancing before her eyes. Then she passed out.

Andrew and Zane stood over her, while she shivered. They had found her through her beacon, and now they were trying to wake her up. She opened her eyes, but couldn't see them properly, she tried standing, but her head was spinning so badly, she immediatly had to sit down. Through her haze, she heard Zane say to Andrew that he would morph her, trying to help her by making parts of him become one with her, let the animal form help the healing process. She tried to refuse, but nothing escaped her lips. Petrified, she saw Zane starting his chant, hearing his low, soft voice as he prepared the nanobots that would shape her body into a wolf. She wanted to cry out a warning but she couldn't.

She wasn't completely rebuilt. Her spirit was not fully integrated yet. Ever since her stupid stunt in Athen, trying to find Carls lost self, her reclaim process took forever it seemed. It had gotten a little better, but she was still weak, and not able to control an animal shape. As Zane completed the morph, the sudden change hit her immediately. The instinct banged in her head and she had two thoughts. Defend and run. She saw Zane and Andrew clear now, senses sharpened by her canine form. She knew they were friends, she knew it, and yet her whole body wanted to defend itself. The only way she could was to attack or to run. Andrew, the nanomage, executed a rooting program on her, she couldn't move...panic...panic... Without knowing what she did, she snapped at whatever came close, her teeth closing over a soft piece of meat...a hand? She heard Zane cry out in pain, but as his blood ran from his hand, she bit down even harder, almost biting his hand off, before being forced to let go by strong hands pulling her jaws apart. A deep, low rumble from her throat escaped her, as she partly understood what was happening. She wasn't in control, but she could feel Zane in her system, his ability to use animal instincts talking to her mind and body. She didn't understand.

Seconds passed that felt like days. Everything was moving slowly, but strangely enough, she felt better for each tick. The state of her body was improving, and she felt more in control, able to comprehend a little more. This was strange....the piercing headache she had been experiencing since the day in Athen was only a slur in the back of her head. Wow...this was amazing! She had to thank Zane for....Zane? Looking around, she saw him, one of her best friends, sprawling on the ground only feet infront of her. He was holding his head, his face ridden with pain. It was only then she understood. By morphing her, he had transferred some of his innate abilites to her...and gotten some of hers in return. He wasn't used to the sheer impact of seeing the backside of meta-physicism, like she wasn't used to the animal instincts being so strong and dominant. Neither of them could control what the other had transferred, and she could do nothing to help him. Nothing, unless she could get out of the morphed form.

With an enourmous amount of self-control, more than she thought she was capable of in her weakened state, she managed to cancel the shapeshifting program, her body tranforming back to normal. She grabbed Andrew and together they helped Zane get up, while waiting for the effect to wear off of him. She looked at the man she loved so much as a friend, understanding him better now...still feeling his presence inside, the animal instincts gone, but the cognition heavily in her mind now. She knew...she understood. And she would forever remeber the day they had merged like this.

Just south of the old fort where people had gathered to talk about the future of the new council stood a nanomage, a sabretooth morph and three women. Jenny looked at Carl...the young man she had been so close to, knowing that this couldn't be him...he would never have done this...the bombs in Borealis and Tir, the hatred so strong, trying to eliminate Zane, who was once his friend. What had happened that would change him so much? Was he fully in control, or was this the other one...the one she had talked to, feared and faught? The woman she knew as Usia, Zanes right hand, and from what she had heard, a fierce fighter stood close to Carl yelling at him, threatening him, barely able to hold back her anger. The second woman, one she had barely met, known as Magan, observing, holding back her manifestations. The anger was so thick..the demented words spewing from Carls mouth..things she could hardly believe he was saying..the two women trying so hard to stop him, to stop his clear intention of aniihilating Zane. She couldn't do anything...she couldn't stop him. She had tried to talk to him, shocked by his words and action, and he had struck her down, hit her hard so she would shut up. It was then she turned to the sabretooth lying very still on the ground, barely clinging on to life. This was not the everyday death. This was his spirit leaving his body prematurely and no reclaim process in the entire world could save him. She jumped over to him and kneeled down beside him. He tried to snap at her fingers but he was so weak.

- Sweetest Zane, can you hear me? I know you can... Please, Zane...please. Whatever it is he's doing to your head...please...

Behind her, the shouting was becoming stronger, and she heard Carl shout at Usia that this was it...the cat was dying right now. No! No...no no no...there had to be something she could do! She had to find a way for Zane to get back in control. The two other women were fighting hard with Carl to stop him and she decided there was only one thing she could do. Make use of Zanes abilites, force him to wake up. She heard the beloved voice of Anthony behind her, but there was no time, she couldn't answer. She wrapped her arms around the broad, furry neck and spoke softly in his ear, evading his constant snaps and ignoring his aggressive growling.

- Zane...listen to me. You can do this....remember when you told me about how the instinct took over? Remember when you told me how you change not only your physical self? How your feline instinct would blend with your human ones? How you could smell who is friend and store their scent..?

The feline stopped growling. He became very still and motionless. She couldn't tell if he was listening to her of if something else was happening, so she grabbed the ears and tugged at them, gently turning his head.

- You know me...you know me

She pulled the cats nose closer and placed his head to rest at her neck, feeling his cold nose so close to her main bloodvein. She knew it would only take a snip, and she would be gone, but she had to try, despite the incertainty, the danger.

- Zane...you know me...It's me...Jen....you know my scent...you've been part of me. And I've been part of you. I'm your friend.

The cat shook its head gently, pushing her aside and struggling to its feet. It was obvious it was still weak, but the eyes didn't look so delirious any longer. She got to her feet and placed a hand on the furry back, continuing the whisper, beckoning for him to wake up, for his instinct to lead him through whatever kind of daze and poison to the mind Carl had put him in. She looked directly at Carl, still fighting the two women, and now also her fiancè.

- Can you smell him? He's hurting you....can you smell his hatred and disgust? Don't listen to him....run by instinct, not command, Zane

It was all over so sudden. She saw the big cat lunging, taking a swipe at Carls waist, growling stronger now. She saw the familiar cloud of nanobots emerging from it, forcing themselves on to Carl, morhping his body. She saw Anthonys confused look as she egged the feline on, trying to call out the strong animal presence to take over, to defend itself, to hunt. She explained as best she could, her words not making much sense, as it was hard to explain something she didn't fully comprehend herself.

And then...it was over. Carl had escaped. Zane had chased after him. Both Usia and Magan were gone. And she was tired. Scared. Angry. Confused. Vengeful. She needed time to think, to process. Figure out some way to talk to Carl and ask him why....why he was doing all this. But she smiled too...for today she had helped a friend.
