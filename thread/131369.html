Aliens behind Enigma forest? EcoCrim investigates!
After some thinking and observations from my newly started EcoCrim, I have come to the fact that the Enigma Forest originates from an Alien Lifeform.

The things I have found as possibly evidence for this, is the following.

1: The Map of Central Artery Valley reveals strange circles and lines, shaped almost like the Earth phenomena "Crop Circles" populary connected with UFO`s. Of course, CAV is far away from civilization, and the chance for bullies or workers that want attention going to the dangerous place just for fun is unlikely.
My theory on this, would be the work by the Engima`s themselves, a way to contact their relatives and show them they have landed.

2: The Forest it self. Unatural, too symmetric and complicated to be a work of the nature it self or Omni-Tek. The Trees are living creatures too, something we only see here.

3: The Enigma beings. They seem to be similar to supposed Aliens when mankind lived on Earth. Their look, which is nothing like anything else on Rubi-Ka, can only be seen as otherworldly.

These three points is what I have currently figured out as possible proof of Aliens living active on Rubi-Ka, It is my hope that we will discover more about them and maybe find out where they come from.

There has been no visible progress in Enigma Forest since I started investigating it, but I will keep the case open and shed light upon any new events. Any form of threat to mankind or fauna on Rubi-Ka must be stopped before it can go too far, and I have yet to be sure of what these Enigma`s are planning.

Until then, take care.

Alien or not, the Enigma are a fascinating species. Their tree houses are some of the most wonderful sights on planet.
The Enigma seems to live in a perfect balance with nature. Even more, they seem to be able to get nature on their hand. What a difference with our over industrialised society. Maybe thats why they seem so alien to us.

If I look at the different kinds, I believe there they evolve. From sprout, twig, branch and limb to sapling and finally into tree. The trees seem to gather around some kind of totem or obelisk, engraved with undecipherable hieroglyphs. Did the Enigma raise them? Or are they relicts from a long forgotten past and do the Enigma merely worship them?

The obelisk seem to have some connection with notum, they resemble a notum crystal. The Braindogs, which Ive only seen around Enigma settlements, clearly have notum crystals on their head. This makes me presume that the Enigma are closer connected to Rubi Ka then we previously believed.

On one of my travels, I came across one of these weird characters found at remote places all over our planet.
