Changes to Anarchism Gov Form
I just recently participated in the creation of an "anarchism" type org with several other individuals. Our name for the org seemed quite appropriate given our emphasis on individual liberty and mutual aid, "Freedom." However, after experimenting with the org commands we came across a number of interesting issues that some of us had problems with.


First, we all seemed to like the fact that it took at least 3 people to kick any member. That seems to reflect on the decentralized nature of any anarchist organization, and still allow for the freedom of individuals within to disassociate when they choose to do so. I would personally prefer that the number of individuals required to kick be a percentage of the number in the org, but that is really a nit picky issue.

However, we quickly noticed that it takes only ONE person to either disband the entire org, tax the entire org, or switch the governing form and create a new org with themselves as the supreme leader! Now, I understand that the game might want to represent the fact that power can easily be grabbed in a decentralized network, and I admire that attempt, but this is obviously going to an extreme that does not represent how anarchist politics have ever worked in the historical past or even in a coherent theoretical framework. If it requires 3 votes to kick an individual, why does it require only one vote to effectively kick everyone or assume complete power?

I fear this will be especially problematic in the booster, I couldn't imagine that anyone would want to be part of an org where all the towers and mines could instantly fall under the control of one individual without any input from the org itself. Not only does this make anarchism difficult to play in AO, but it does not represent the nature of anarchism outside AO, which has always striven to reject power plays by single individuals. If the very root meaning of the word anarchist means a rejection of rulers, why would such an organization allow anyone to instantly become the ruler? I can't think of a single anarchist org in the real world in which any member could declare themselves king and instantly assume all power.

So I would really like to suggest that Funcom revamp the anarchism form of government to make it a more viable organization. Im not asking to remove all the quirks in it, or to make it impossible to function properly, just to make it more viable. I understand that anarchism requires trust amongst its participants, and that is a very good thing to represent, but you would still need to trust your fellow members if a vote was required to disband the entire org.

So that is my basic suggestion, make the votes required to kick a vote required to do anything in the org. Still allow everyone to make org commands, still have no ranks or titles, these reflect the individual power of anarchism, but dont allow the power of a single individual to -by default- override that of everyone else, that would be despotism, not anarchism.

If others reading this message could continue to bump it whenever it falls in the forum so that Funcom takes notice of the individuals out there who want to help each other without creating ranks and domination, I would greatly appreciate it.
