Cash Blitzing. How a team can do 500 mill in 4 hours
It's probably nothing *huge* to post, but I noticed a lot of people still blitz solo missions for cash. IMO that's a booring and slow way of blitzing.

In guild, once in a while, we make an event out of blitzing.
Team of 6 pick 3 team missions each with as many Anima of The Abomination as they can. (having backpacks ready) take on the missions giving 4-6 animas in BS/PM/Omni HQ (for omni)
and run like mad with GSF/Rage going.
in 1 hour, every member of the team should have about 20-25 Mill. Do this for 4 hours, and your team should've gathered about 500 Mill. Hey.. how about making that week a dirty run-week with the team, so every one end up with 500 mill

Btw... Giving the Animas in the end to a trader who buffs up CL max and sells it to tradershop.. Anima sells for 350Kish in normal shop... 500K in tradershop.
