I think it's a cool idea, but I'm a little worried about a couple aspects of it. I figure I'll wait until more info is released to decide exactly what I think, but I'm still bothered.

One, being the leader of a purposefully small guild with mostly low to average level players, we're concerned that this pack will basically do nothing for us. It will encourage guilds to be as high level and as large as allowed in order to maximize the bonuses you get from held areas.

I wish they would put in some organization alliance rules, because I feel that this might solve the problem. For example, if a number of small guilds can ally together and their guilds share bonuses from held areas, it will keep people from feeling like they need to be in the biggest and best guild out there.


My other problem is the whole "tower" concept. I'm worried that this is going to end up absurdly generic. This is a sci-fi game afterall. Can't we have giant stationary robots? Mechanized proton canons? If the world becomes littered with silly looking towers that shoot over night, I'm going to groan.

Not to mention how silly the idea of "solo" towers is. You can place solo towers that are basically pets and allow you train MOBs back to them? Ugh. Silly concept and even sillier when everyone in the game does it. Towers towers everywhere. How about adding new vehicles? Why is it that we have all this amazing tech and even have roads, but everyone basically runs everywhere until they can afford the one flying vehicle in existence? Give us more vehicles, maybe which allow combat and give some combat skill bonuses. Much more believable and fun than "solo towers that act likes pets!"
