The technical fixes is all ok, but WHAT ABOUT THE RP ISSUES?
We have milelong discussions about technical issues, why this bow isnt as great as that rifle, and how come this is so nerfed compared to that, but hey guys: Am I the only one who really do not care that much for technical details?

Ok, my sister is an adventurer and the pistols suck. SHE DOES NOT CARE! Ok, so some teenager with a main char has equipped his lvl20 char with 500.000 worth of implants, weapons and twinkled as much as an average olympics athlet, and run around in PvP-zones. I DONT CARE! Let the kids have their fun. The'll grow up and see the real beauty of RPGames, some day. Or they get tired and return to Quake. Byebye baby, I wont miss you.

What I DO care about, is the world I'm living in: Rubi-Ka. My friends, the roleplay within the guild, the explorations, the sights, the events that take place every now and then.

Im fairly strong, been around for a while, know my implants and nanos. I survive, and take my pride in returning back after a long travel, instead of just committing suicide by /terminating. So be it if some overequipped bountyhunter comes across and rip the spine out of me. Its a dangerous planet, Rubi-Ka. And I've got my dear Yalm to return me safely home.

But what do I return to?

Yes. The apartment. The ten items apartment. The apartment that looks exactly like all the other apartments, with no windows, no views, thats located in a newbie area that looks the exact same as all the other newbie areas of that side.

Well, I still invited some of my friends to a houseparty, and packed twenty people in there. It was a wild party, yes indeed, but the furniture didnt have to disappear like it did, in there. Thats a bug for ya.

My sister is a neutral adventurer! Why cant she have a cage somewhere in the woods? With all the beautiful graphics made for the missions, surely there must be plenty to choose from of existing graphics, to make a nice, crystalenlightened cave, or a small cabin somewhere where she can return home, to her own peaceful spot, where she can sharpen her Riders while watching the sunset. Myself, Id like an urban penthouse on the top floor of a building in the unofficial capital of the neutrals, Borealis, with a view from my balcony, to the waterfalls outside the city.

There should still be the free apartments, but what about building apartment complexes with genuine apartments offered for sale? Hell, this could be a traders only item, for them to buy and sell?

Of the missions Ive been in, Ive seen so many rooms that I would stop and look closer at. And Ive thought "Hey - I wanna LIVE here!"

And PLEASE - at least give me a sofa, or a bed? If not, a chair should not be too much to ask for?

***

So much for the apartments. Clothing is another issue. Its obvious that the Miir Fashion guys need some competition. They have not renewed their collection for ages. We all run around in the same clothing, looking like bimbos, pimps or - oh the horror - atroxes in string bikinis.

What about making a profession, either one of the current, or a totally new one, being able to design their own line of fashionwear, or craftsmen creating furniture? Or at least, please make local variations in dresses. Is it logical that the exact same clothing are to be found all over the planet? I dont think so! I think there should be a store, probably located in Omni Entertainment, where an independent designer makes high quality, high priced clothings for the jetset. AND SELLS TO ALL SIDES, not only Omnis. That weapons and other war equipment is restricted sale, is reasonable. But leisurewear? Does not make sense.

Also, I think there should be a smaller shop, in Borealis, where a neutral designer is offering her creations, at a limited number. When its sold out, its not aviable anywhere else than trade amongst other citizens, but she offers new designs. Some times shes probably sold out. Maybe shes off for a vacation too, at times.

And, finally, I beg you all, Omnis and clans: You are all welcome to our poor, but beloved capital, Newland. BUT PLEASE RETURN THE HOSPITALITY BY *NOT* KILLING OUR GUARDS!
