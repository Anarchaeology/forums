AnotherFixer
Supa Leet
First off, the MCS does not used Melee Init anymore. That was fixed two patches ago.

At high ranged init levels (800 or more, get a TIM scope to accomplish this easily) the MCS used in a hotswap combination with a REGULAR suppressor for FA attack is a great combination. Don't scope them though as they are too slow.

An even better combination is the Light Suppressor used in hotswap with a Regular Suppressor, again for the FA attack.

I use a Custom Heavy Suppressor myself. Forget about using the Heavy Suppressor until you're past level 150 though. The cost of raising the AR and FA skills is prohibitive until that point. The only downside to this gun occurs if you're using Grid Armor. Grid Armor is only truly effective when you can back down your AGG/DEF slider a good bit to avoid being hit by like-level players. The CHS is a bit slow to do this and as such, when you go more Defensive, you attack so slowly that many opponents can heal up a good portion of the damage you do.

Argulace currently uses a River 6 and Regular Suppressor combination from what I've seen. I'd assume he uses a big LLTS for this since the River sucks for damage unless it crits. At least its fast.

Pretty soon I'm going to start exploring the use of Duel Light Suppressors which should allow me to keep a higher rate of fire even when I'm nearly 100% Defensive in GA.

River 6 in my opinion is not very good now.

The Regular suppressor realy realy stinks now, when you FA with it you will hardly crit anything (crit more if you have a scope but then you suffer on the deffensive side) the regular dmg on it is crap so you would have to have all the apostulos praying for you to get enough crits on the FA to do any serious damage, in my experience, anyone with good evades will laugh at your suppressor FA almost everytime.

I tried the doual light suppressors, a ql200 and a ql143 and its good fast damage in pvm.. but for pvp sucks, sucks because damage shields will get you killed very fast, the low ql on the off hand is not worth it. If your oponent has a damge shield you are dead meat.

For my personal choice i use the MCS, its a powerfull gun with a fast burst recharge, the only downside is speed, this gun is not fast so you must have a good ranged init... i have 1k+ so i can use it and still play with the aggro bar, i dont need to be full aggro to get full speed out of it, but i can't also lower too much if i want to shoot fast. Bottom line is you have to know when to do what with your aggro bar. The fast burst recycle is very very important on this gun. My MCS burst will always do more damage then the regular suppressor FA, from my experience.

I also carrie arround other SMGs namely the sol chironis and others i can swap them all and i use it depending on the circumstances, if im initiative debuffed, the dmg type i want etc.

SMGs are not fast killing guns, in mass pvp its hard to get credits for a kill when everyone is empting spetials. If you want more spetials for mass pvp get a CHS, but you wont be able to self equip it, unless you go for a lower QL. If you want versatility, be able to swap guns. have high attk rate etc go for the MCS and other SMGs in secondary.
