Soleet
Alvin Quest - kill tons of bots, need to be 40 lvl +, and 4-8 hours of camping robot hot spots. NO team required

TIM Quest - 2 hour respawn, team required, longest road foreman dungeon. Usually camped by atleast 1 other team.

Director - Dont need to hit him since you wont be able to use anything he drops. Useless to a engie.

Smugglers Den - 2 different spots here. So ill break it down.

Mantis Egg - Atleast 1 full team of 125+ lvl's to get there, 30 minute respawn rate of queen, bad bot pathing.

MK II armor - alteast 1 full team 125 + lvl's to out damage other teams camping there. For a full set give atleast 3 - 6 days of camping since you will have to split the loot between the team. Multiple mobs so spawn rate dont apply.

Trash King - Spawn rate has been changing there but overall, very quick spawn, camped, used for alvin quest by alot of folks need to be atleast 100 lvl and teamed to even have a shot at getting loot rights though.

Camelot - Dont waste your time unless your in a HUGE guild and have the backing of aton of fire power. PVP area, alot of guild fights fun though Would say around 130 lvl plus and atleast 2 teams to put a dent in the boss though.

Medusae - 80 lvl plus a seasoned team can take out medusae pretty easy i started hunting them at 60 lvl but died from time to time. A good team helps, no camping, social now, and hunt male characters, females dont draw argo. Fun spot used to be "The hot spot" of the game till it was nerfed.

Claw - blah!
