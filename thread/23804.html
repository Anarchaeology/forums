It's Time We Met On Nuetral Ground
As most of you know Jobe was built by Omni NT's who later left Omni. It is considered Nuetral in the NT community, although Omni is still trying to hold onto it's ties with the leaders of Jobe.

As most of you know Jobe won't be implemented in the game until December.

I am not going to wait that long to meet my fellow NT's and surround myself with more of you who wield vastly destructive DD and AE nukes.

I would like to meet you on nuetral ground and discuss NT predominant teams, and in the future, an alliance of NT's that spans the Omni/Clan conflict. I respect the fact that you are fighting for whichever side you have choosen. But we do need to band together to improve our profession.

I am proposing a social meeting of NT's, a preliminary to future meetings, in Newland behind the grid access, near the hills. This is a nice open setting, near a grid, and on Neutral ground. I would like to meet a week from tomorrow (Sunday) at 4pm PST.

First I would like to meet everyone. Get everyone on everyone elses freinds list.

Then I would like to make a few NT predominant teams so we can do some missioning to get to know eachother and work on our team strategies. As many of you know 4 NT's, a Doc, and an Enf make a VERY powerful team. But you can substitute in any profession really. Even 6 NT's is a great team!

I am hoping that in the future this leads to a more tightly knit NT community, and eventually to an alliance the befits Jobe. Many of you can imagin how powerful an NT alliance could be.

This meeting is open to anyone, at any level. Please bring your ideas and an open mind. And a willingness to help the NT community learn and grow.



~Ankokujin
