14.2 and Engies
As a lvl 43 opi engie I can cast a Patchwork Gladiatorbot with my own self-buffs (I'm like two points off the next bot in skills). Except for a few implants (which really don't do THAT much for me because I can't AFFORD better ones) I'm a bone stock lvl 43 engie. My armor is pretty average, my bot is really nothing special, and my pistols are a joke.

BUT - if fixing the OE problem like FC says will allow them to also lower Mob AC's and HP's to what they really should be - heck - it won't affect me a whole lot except that either a) I can do easy missions even faster or b) I can finally hold my own in a midrange difficulty mission.

Overequipping was never an issue with me. I mean - what's the point? Every few levels or so I get a new, stronger bot. It's not invincible, but I don't expect it to be. That would ruin the game for me completely because I would feel like a serious cheater if I were running around with a bot completely out of my normal range. This way the game is still a challenge to me, and I'm not getting bored with it. If everything were suddenly incredibly easy because I had an uber bot I would lose interest. Besides even with the MP mastery buffs I need to build a better bot I still get my ass handed to me a lot of the time.

Yay to 14.2! Hopefully along the way FC may even fix the server sync and zone crashing problems so I can actually walk into a store again.
