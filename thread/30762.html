Agent:
Let's start with why I am writing this, to justify my suggestions, rather I would call them alterations..:

1)LOS is messed up for our special. If you put a skill check on a special, I expect it to work. No one else has to have checks, so if we do, it should work. I cannot AS anything unless I get out of visual range, put in sneak, and then walk up. DO you know how difficult a task this is, not to mention tedious, when in a group. Work fine in missions, because there's plenty blocking your view.

2)Consequently, this makes our 100% nano useless. I take it this was to make our AS's count. Our big damage rifles are 2.5/2.5, and have crap clips. No way we're gonna fire off a crit every time we cast it, we're too slow 1/2 the time. Well, how do you expect me to use this nano to crit on an AS, when I have to leave AS range, cast, then put myself back in sneak.. oh wait, I just tried an AS, but I put myself into sneak when I was in LOS. I have to wait another 8 seconds to sneak. But damn, the TC nano has ended! Oh well, I'll try again in 15 minutes.

3)The 15% nano, I crit once every 6-7 times I cast it. My class depends on crits, especially when soloing. I just can't understand how any of our new nanos is meant to balance us.

Not to mention the high lv crit nanos are almost impossible to aquire. What'd we get other than those? Candy coated crap.

4)Switch sides tool? Whatever! It's a damn application form with a different skin. Let's get real, no one wants to go undercover, and have to wait *6 DAYS* playtime to go back to their home, and be with their guildmates, and comrades. SO much for gathering intel, and coming home for dinner. I have to leave my character logged for several days, to do that.

5)Holograms? I don't even have to go there...

6)Baclavas, or whatever. Yeah, 1-3% crit increae on them, but the AC's suck compared to the helm I *could* be using in it's place. The bonuses are hardly worth the INSANE amount it costs to upgrade them.

8)And I still can't solo more than a green/yellow, one on one. Missioning is still terribly difficult. I pray you didn't think that those thign were supposed to aid in that. Did you even listen!? We couldn't solo, our damage sucked. You threw in tiny length nanos and thought it's fix that? Stupid..


I think this enough rant to show that you didn't help us any. This was not a fix, in any way, but I'll prepose new things for you, right here. Some that don't even have to do with agents. Write some of this down, it's *coming from an agent who actually plays this game*.

1)Put our crit nanos in single missions, as rewards. (I remind you these are supposed to help **balance** our class, but are only in team missions, but MA's get their in solo ones?) It's too hard to find a group that wants to do a certain, and inevitably not the QL they want to do for exp, mission so you can get a simple crystal. Most agents can only cast a nano at their own level. Why would a group go through a grey mission, just to help a single agent out? They don't, therefore, this MUST BE done. An agent cannot solo an even con mission, period. Nice try, but we can't.

2)Give us a >consistent< crit buff. Our class lives and dies by them. We have lower HP, and evades than any other weapon-combat class, and our damage isn't great. Not something major, but a good constant one. Our current ones are good for a temporary boost, but offer no help when in the mix consistently.
Perhaps 4 different ones. 3-5-7-9%'s. SELF ONLY, meaning no means of casting on crit-twinks who wanna PvP. *Simple enough!* And no insane NCU costs, Gawd!

3)Put skill reqs on baclavas. Sense/AGL, or even STR/AGL, and unlock the level requirement. That way, we can upgrade to a fair QL for decent AC's. (Mind you our evades are dark blue, as well as HP. AC's are important.) No worry! OE restrictions still exist. If it makes you feel better, it must be upgraded while not being worn. Paying to upgrade seems fair under these cicumstances, because it does yield a crit bonus, be it a VERY small one. And make them look cool. Christ, they are some ugly apparel....

4)Make x-3's shop buyable. An e-mail response from one of your "main guys" to my pal tekkor, when asked about this was that "it wasn't the bread and butter for agents". What? Idiot! You're not verrant, and I'm not stupid. I use a rifle, and I live and die by it. This could be the savior rifle for my class, so introduce it. *Period.* The IP saved on shotgun (Lovemakers are the best rifles, damage wise, no doubt.) could be used on HP, or evades, which we horribly lack in.

5)Fix the previously mentioned LOS problem. Otherwise out 100% crit nano is basically not of use, outside of PvP. (Think of the MAJORITY, for a change.)

6)Make the crat nano Face in the Crowd NOT overlap the Ruse line. Getting my 200-400-600 conceal replaced with *puke* 86 is a joke. It even says it's a superior program. Umm.. NO!

7)Why not just improve our damage line, instead of making two of them that stack. That made no damn sense.

8)Side change utensils you gave us. Give them concealment requirements to use, and knock off (8 MINUTES) for each point of conceal that they require to be used. 8640 minutes in a 6 day period. QL200 requiring 1000 conceal. So even a QL2000 would require you to be locked on side for about 10 hours.

9)Make team heals, auras, etc.. NOT break sneak. This is annoying in combat with teams, with the 10 second sneak timer. The sneak timer is fine, but only under these circumstances. I wrote the AO bug team, and got a response saying that I should be able to cast nanos in sneak, and that was that. Apparently he wasn't too bright, because I explicitly mentioned other professions casting the previously mentioned nanos, doing it.


New stuff:

1) Chemistry skill. Make it possible to construct stacks of ammo, via chemistry trade skill, that do a different damage type. And not just bullets, but other types of ammo, that can be sold. But, looking at implementation problems, it'd probably be easier to make a nano line along the same lines of MA's, that also require chemistry as a skill. A cold nano sounds nice. Self only? Maybe.


2)Give us a non-gen bow nano line. It's light blue to us, and most of them use AS, anyway, it's obvious it was intended to be a secondary weapon type for more creative agents. There are some great bows out there, but without a decent attack rating, they'll be essentially useless, without a good buff set.

3) A second sub-special for agents. AS recycles second only to DIMACH! Come on, we need something. HEY! You might make the special an special attack of a preselected damage type, based on the special shell type. Kill #1 and 3 with one stone. Chemistry skill check on the attack?

4)Make the TIM scope tradeable. Unique, to keep people form farming them, and owning a market, however. Hell, put them in the FIXER SHOPS! And make the fixer shop fixer only. Gawd! That'd build a huge bridge between fixer/agent, and hopefully get a little blackmarket action going. Heck, put the X-3 in there, too. Both classes should love that idea, as fixers would be making money, and agents wouldn't have to search half their lives finding the two.

(If not this, then make targetting scopes trade skill, and make it to where they can be altered to have a positive init, instead of a negative, using come parts and a screwdriver.)

5)Better debuff line. We have a dodge evade debuff, why not the rest of the evade lines, as well as init debuffs. That way, we can debuff mobs and get hit less quickly. We have low evades, ourselfs, and low HP, this seems like an interesting way to alleviate some of our pain. These must be effective. We're talking a -1200 buff for the highest, and lowest maybe a -200. I'm tired of seeing that nano in a doctors shop. They have heals, and can have the same amount, more with self buff, HP's than us. Right now we have no way to either heal ourselves effectively, or avoid damage. With crap HP/Evades, that's a scary combo for a combat class.

Non-agent-secific:

1)Add a RELOAD button! Come on already!

2)Make the ATK/RECHARGE time visible by shift-left clicking the weapon, and looking at it's description. I'd like to know, plain-as-day, what my weapon will be firing at.

3)Also, add a crit% box in the staus bar to see what our current % is. Of course there are intangibles, such as ARK rating over defense values, but I think people would like to know what their base percent is, before those factors.

**(OKay, I'm done.. headache.)**
