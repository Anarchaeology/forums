Xed: What a kickass sig, even now it makes me laugh out loud.

And all you need to do to make a script is make a file called 'blah' (or whatever, no extension) in the 'scripts' folder in your Anarchy Online directory. Then you put whatever commands and text you want in there. To call it in-game, all you do is type /blah, and viola!

So just copy those two lines from up there (the /tell and the /ignore) into a text file, name it what you want, and you're good. Then, you can make a macro to call it if you like (/macro blah /blah).
