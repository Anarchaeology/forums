Rare vs. Ridiculous
First off, let me say I agree that grid armor should be hard to get. But there is a difference between hard to obtain and dumb luck.

I have wasted a ton of time trying to find the instruction disc for mk1. I first started looking after early reports from Ballpin and Skorpius(sp?) made it sound like it was worth the spent ip on matter creation. I was not about to spend the ip for any of the other summon lines since they are all worthless. But from what I was reading grid armor was really worth it. Grid Armor was sounding like something that would help define the fixer profession.

I figured I would get mk1 and spend the ip on matter creation. If I thought mk1 was good I would then worry about saving up the ip for mk2. Note: at this point I was more concerned with saving up the ip than finding the ql 60 item.

The hunt was on. At first it was fun. I was sure there must be some trick to it. I went different places and tried different things. I was always certain victory was right around the corner. I was level 85 looking for a ql 60 item. I figured it would take some time but the odds were on my side. I was dead wrong.

It was during this time I had the chance to buy the mk1 instruction disc for 2 million creds. I laughed at the idea. After all I was level 85 looking for a ql 60 item. How hard could it be? In retrospect passing up that deal was the dumbest move I have made in AO.

That last statement is sad when you think about it. I am a fixer. I am supposed to be able to obtain hard to find items. HA.

I soon realized there was no trick to finding it. I just had to kill enough stuff. So began my merciless slaughter of gray conning critters. I killed everything; scooplets, diglets, plants, penpods, shades, claws, etc. etc. I found tons of instruction discs around the right ql, but the right one kept alluding me. Now I was certain there was some mistake and the disc drop would be fixed soon. I did find a ql 71 carbon rich rock so half the quest was done. I found the rock--that kept me happy for a while.

I got bored. I dusted off a level 15 engineer and started leveling him. I would kill grays an hour or 2 every night with my fixer and then play my engineer until bed time. I also went and got a couple levels with my fixer so I would have the ip needed for grid armor. I was certain I would find it soon or funcom would issue some kind of fix. Again I was wrong.

Time passed and my engineer got close to level 50. Cool, now I could look for the instruction disc and advance a character. Seems like a win-win situation doesn't it?

My engineers is level 66 now and has done nothing for close to 20 levels except kill stuff to find the mk1 disc. He has had no luck--this is ridiculous. This fact has become a sick joke with my guildies and friends.

So what is my point? The point is, I was optimistic. I gave Funcom the benefit of the doubt. I mean making a game is hard n' stuff right? But what Cosmik said earlier in the thread has proven me wrong. There will be no change. They are going to keep it the same way. This is how it was designed to be!!

Funcom must not be playing their game. I refuse to believe that when the devs came up with Grid Armor they figured the discs would auction off for 10-20 million creds and that the odds of winning in Las Vegas would be better than finding the necessary discs and rocks as loot.

I am not a whiner, ranter, or troller. So it is time for me to put my money where my mouth is. Cosmik I am inviting you to create a lvl 60ish character and join my engineer for a just a couple hours each day while he does nothing but search for the mk1 disc.

If at the end of 3 weeks you can honestly say you are still having fun I will shut up and go away. However, if at the end of 3 weeks, or around 40 hours of playtime, you find yourself honestly agreeing with me then promise you will take up the issue with the powers that be.

We can work out something with the time zones, and I do not expect you to join me on weekends. I will be very flexible. I just ask that you or someone else from funcom come join me for around 40 hours and watch me search. After that if you still feel it is a reasonable amount of time invested/risk vs. reward I will go away.

I think this is a reasonable request considering funcoms position on the issue versus what players know to be the truth. My guess--after 5 hours you will see our point.

Send a tell to Terkon or Tiyrk in game if you would like to join me.
