I'll scratch your back if you scratch mine
I have never seen this before and have never read about it, so I thought I would mention it ans see what you folks have to say about it.

I was in a mission building (cave) in Perpetual Wastelands. I left my bot killing some MOB and I poked my toes into the next room. Not far enough to be agged, but far enough to target, I hoped.

The MOB in this room was a something-or-other-Claw-something (sorry I don't have the exact ame).

The something-or-other-Claw-something said, "I'll scratch your back if you scratch mine."

Suddenly (I think by coincedence) I was hit hard from behind and had to retreat three rooms. By the time I got back, my bot was in combat with the something-or-other-Claw-something.

Whats up with that?
