Do away with skill locks after lvl 50
I will use the level 100 cap as an example since alot of people have gone through the cap.


THE LEVEL 100 CAP:

Once you get up to level 82 most of your main skills have been capped and you no longer can raise them until level 100. I lose the ability to put 90k IPs into my green skills since they cannot be raised. My characters become weaker between this period when they should be stronger and not very enjoyable to play since I cant get any new nanos or better pet etc.. till after level 100. I also would lose those IPs for weapon skills which after 14.2 would be very rewarding to keep.

I seem to be pretty much forced into putting IPs into other skills which takes away some of the freedom of developing my character the way I feel like it.

I would like to feel my characters growing throughout the 200 levels. I want to be able to have a consistancy of upgrading my nanos, weapons, pets on a constant pace.

Free us from these wicked skill locks.

It's not a mind game - it's to prevent extreme min-maxing. In my many years of playing RPGs, pen and paper and computer ones, I've seen people use min-maxing to create characters that were so "specialized" they would be completely non-functional imbeciles in anything resembling the real world. Warriors who put all their character points into strength and endurance so that they have the intelligence of tree sloths, or wizards smarter than Albert Einstein but more feeble than a parapallegic kid. This practice with the impossible characters it produces destroys any pretense of a game world. The game stops being about characters and becomes a spreadsheet. There's too much of that already and letting people min-max without any limit would just make things that much more boring and dull.

All in all the title caps are a good idea. I think the duration of the caps is debatable, but the idea itself is sound. Yes, they suck, but the overall effect is positive IMO (and yeah, I'm getting close to my next cap and worrying about how I'll deal with it).
