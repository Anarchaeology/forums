Iron reet can indeed drop both the DCD and the agro thing. The modified agression enhancer is not 100%, rather ~80%. The droid control device is 20-30% drop.
The shades of lucubration is not 100% either, it's more like 80-90%.
Regarding Kelly Fred, the chance to drop a sleeve is like 50%, then it's another 50% for each sleeve, they share the loot table slot, never ever saw both of them drop over more than 30 spawns. So basically, it's 50% for a sleeve to drop, giving each type of sleeve a 25% chance.
General Vivyan drops mother nitrogena, dust collector among other things. Exceptional Mother Nitrogena seems to drop 100% while the Dust Collector only 50% or so, only killed her a few times so I can't estimate properly.
Ljotur always drops CandyCane, the other loot table slot being occupied by one of the armor pieces + eye of the hunter.
Nelly seems to have a 20-50% chance of dropping a piece of azure, never bothered counting.
On Notum Soldier, the nophex drops about 50%+ of the time, a piece of armor about 90% of the time.
Professor van Horn drops the vest more like 10-20%.
Smug also drops sleeves/gloves/boots.
Tarasque seems to have 2 rare slots, one containing EoT, Aura Magnifier, Hollow Bone Bracer, Sinew (uber rare slot) and another with GoS, GPH, RBP (rare), heart the rest of the stuff being dropped randomly at each kill - usually he drops 2 items, but I've seen the chicken drop a single item, too.
The drop rate on the TIM scopes is more like 50% for each, they both occupy the same slot.
Coords for Nirtox are 3333x2222.
Hope this helps.
