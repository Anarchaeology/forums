Works just fine .

Two interesting things I noticed. Only rollerrats have three dying sounds and you won't find leets' folder. They do have sounds there but to find out where, follow Emiko's thread on Rubi-Ka creatures

Anyway, format is IMA ADPCM (or at least that's the name CoolEdit uses), it's 22kHz, 4 bit mono

Oh yeah, works even while you're running the game, you can't change the number of sounds for particullar event and game won't crash if you miss the wav format.

Enjoy!
