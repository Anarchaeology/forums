If you're an engineer (or otherwise have appropriate levels of the appropriate trade skills, mainly mech eng), you can refine those items and use them in the manufacture of jewelry, which you can sell for a lot more money than what the components add up to individually.

Cold Stone and Soul Fragment are both gems, IIRC, and with a Jensen Gem Cutter you can cut and polish them. The rest you should use a precious metal extractor on, to get their base metals. Then, melt with personal furnace, use wire drawing machine to make into wire, then ring template to make into ring. Add one of the above stones and sell back for large-ish money.
