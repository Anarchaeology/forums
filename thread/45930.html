I'm new to pvping and really don't like the idea of outside buffs at all. If you're fighting in one of the arenas and know the other guy has gotten outside buffs, then there's nothing wrong with trying to even the playing field. But, if you have no reason to believe the other guy has outside buffs, then you're being a dic for getting them yourself.

I was fighting a guy in the arena yesterday in what should have been a close match. Someone I don't know gave me a high level hot in the middle of the fight. I'm level 10 and was fighting with the kitchen sink buff. The other guy didn't stand a chance. If something like that happens again, I'm just going to cancel the buff.

Outside buffs should not be allowed in the arenas. If someone enters an arena with an outside buff, it should immediately and automatically be cancelled.
