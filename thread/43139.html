The reqs only vary with the ql of the implant. A ql 125 imp will have the same req number with 1, 2, or 3 clusters.

Which stat varies solely with which clusters you use.
Example:

Faded Creation Eye: req int
Bright Perception Eye: req agil
Bright Perception Eye with faded Creation: req sense

The order of assembly does not matter.


Yes it is possible to calculate the req numbers:

Stat: (ql-1)*((404-6)/199) + 6

Treatment: (ql-1)*((950-10)/199) + 10

well, it's close anyways. There's probably some creative truncating going on that I have not captured. Suffice it to say, my formulas should always predict high, if wrong.

If you want an easier way to calculate reqs:-

Treatment = (QL*4.72) + 5.28
(add a couple of extra points to be on the safe side)
