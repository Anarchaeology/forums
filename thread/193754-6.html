Here is why "The Notum Wars" is a seperate entity. It is unrelated to the "original" conflict and really has little to nothing to do with it. That's all there is to it.

The Clan vs. Omni war is about certain things. Notum Wars has nothing to do with any of those things.

Notum Mining on Rubi-Ka was opened to EVERYONE by THE ICC. (Read Headline: "ICC Loosens OTs Hold on the Notum") The Clanners did not cease Omni Notum Mining in a hostile act as part of the war. No. Notum Mining was declared by the independant ICC as being permissible for all groups to engage in.

The Notum Wars itself is nothing more than the internal struggles related to the ICC mandate. While tower wars themselves may be a result or symptom of the "war", they are not interdependant on each other.

The ICC mandate gave Neutrals just as many rights to mine Notum as anyone else. This has nothing to do with the Omni vs. Clan conflict. If Neutrals take a tower, they are simply excersizing their right to mine Notum as a citizen of Rubi-Ka. Any conflict related to the towers in question has little to nothing to do with the "war" or what Neutrals are really "Neutral" about.

I'm not sure what about, "The new regulations will effectively end Omni-Tek's absolute monopoly on the valuable resource" implies that a) Clans gain more rights to Notum than Neutrals or b) that there is any "official" standing for fighting over Notum at all. As per the ICC freeing of Omni's official lease on mining, mining is open to anyone--Clan, Omni, OR Neutral. This fact is independant of the "war".

Furthermore, the items complained primarily about in this thread are related to Shadowlands.. And there is really no question that Neutrals play as large a role in the Shadowlands story as either Clan or Omni. Therefore, I really see no reason for Neutrals to have to put up with being statistically inferior.

When you say stuff like "treating Neutrals like a side" in meaning, "We're going to attack them", you really miss the point of a multi-sided system. Not all sides have the same nature OR agenda. While there is a conflict between Clan and Omni, Neutrals being a viable side independant of that conflict does not instantly qualify them as part of the conflict. There is much more to the game than that--especially with Shadowlands.

I suppose this may be difficult for those who treat faction like, "Us, GOOD! Them, BAD!" however.. anyone who looks at the overall picture can easily draw the various distinctions and lines in the sand without oversimplifying the whole system into oblivion.
