Mission Map Saving
A friend of mine within our organisation yesterday mentioned that it would be wonderful if our mission maps were saved.

There are times when we crash inside the mission and come back to a place where we can't see anywhere we've been.

Other times, we invite a new member into the team and have to go outside to give them a key. Once we go back inside, we both have no map and have to get a third person to come down to the bottom level and guide us to where we were.

It would be so much nicer and a lot less hassle for the team members, if our mission maps were saved.

I thought this was a brilliant idea and thought I would mention it in here. I would love to see what others think of this too.
