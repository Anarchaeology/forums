OK, well this seems to be the sticking point:

Is the intelligence ability actually a measure of intelligence as we think of it in RL?

Fako's argument makes the assumption that it is.

If this is true, then without a doubt, nanomages have to potential to be more intelligent. Nanomages simply have a higher max INT than any other breed. Sure int can be augmented by implants, items, and buffs, but with the exception of a few these are available to every breed equally. Yes, with Odin's Eye a Solitus (and only a Solitus) MP can have a higher INT when maxed out than a nanomage, but with Shades of Lub. the nano wins again, so lets leave buffing, implants and items out of the discussion.

Max Int for breeds (unbuffed):
Atrox:400
Nano:512
Opifex:464
Solitus:480

If INT is in fact Intelligence, as the name clearly suggests, then yes, nanomages have the potential to be much more intelligent than any other breed. Given this, Fako's statement about the top 100 most intelligent people on RK being nanos is probably true, if there are at least 100 nanos that have maxed out INT.

If this assumption is true, then any arguments to the effect that nanos do not have the highest potential, or that a solitus can have as high an intelligence as simply false.

Yes, you can have an atrox with 400 INt and a nano with only 50, but the nano can indeed eventually have more intelligence than anyone else


So, is INT actually Intelligence as we think of it in RL? Well, the name suggests it is. Why would it be called intelligence if it is in fact not intelligence?

So, if INT is actually intelligence, then all of Fako's comments about nanos being smarter (potentially) are true, stating otherwise is either wrong, or assuming that "Intelligence != Intelligence".




I dont agree that the most intelligent should rule alone, but he has a point that the most intelligent often hold important and respected positions in a government. Advisors, councils etc are often as important as the "leader".

But, is it intelligence or wisdom that makes them valuable? I would say that it is both. Part of intelligence is cognative ability, which is essential for a leader or advisor. If you had a wise man without any cognative bility he'd be next to useless. Likewise if you had a genius with no wisdom, he would again be useless.

So, both are needed to be a good ruler (not get elected or be liked, but to rule well) along with a desire to rule well.

Follow me for a moment if you will:

If you need Intelligence and Wisdom to rule, it is safe to say that the rulers (councils, advisors, what have you) will be drawn from the most intelligent and most wise. You should select people that have high intelligence and great wisdom.

On Rubi-Ka, the most intelligent (following the previous assumption) people are nanomages. Some of them, if not many, are bound to be wise as well. Some of these are bound to have a desire to do good. Wouldnt they therefore make the best ruling council?
