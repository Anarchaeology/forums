Running out of Time...
05 June 29477 Late Afternoon

Tifanyx sat in his apartment, pondering what he can do in the next 57 days. 57 days is not a lot of time. Anger and sadness boiled inside him. Sadness because he cannot possibly accomplish everything he wants in that short time. Anger because he only has 57 days left. Anger because no one listened. Anger because no one but the BORPAC alliance truly cared.

Actually, a few others did care. They were the 3 closest to him. The two young Rubi-kans, Madtif & Xynafit, that he mentors, and his uncle, Hunter "Buglight" Locknane.

In the past few months, Tifanyx has infiltrated Omni-Tek and saved a countless number of newly arrived citizens from enslavement. He successfully convinced an Omni-Tek employee to join the clans and gave him a brand new identity. Eduardo "Konjer" Shelling resigned his post within Omni-Tek and became Alexander "Madtif" Markarian a rebel clanner. Madtif is a nanomage male. Tifanyx then met a young unnamed Opifex trader in Borealis. The Opifex was lost and confused. Tifanyx helped the Opifex tremendously. He fed him, bought him clothes and financed his first set of implants. The Opifex was so grateful he wanted to "mirror" everything about Tifanyx. His first step was filling out the Clan application.. The next step was to get a nickname. He really wanted the nickname Tifanyx, but he couldn't have it. So he did the next best thing. He "mirrored" it, and "Xynafit" the Clan aligned Opifex Trader is born.

The grim reality was beginning to sink in. After hearing the news from a few days ago, Tifanyx realized he can no longer help save the nanomage people. Sadly, this included his young protégé Madtif. Madtif is a bold young adventurer who is not afraid to speak his mind. Madtif shares many of the beliefs of the BORPAC Alliance and Tifanyx, but he believes there is a better way to ensure the survival of the Nanomage people.

Madtif walked into Tifanyx's apartment after a long day of hunting. Tifanyx, Madtif & Xynafit all share the same apartment in West Athen. Madtif noticed that Tifanyx was still depressed. He knew that the best thing to do was get Tifanyx's mind off the news from a few days ago.

Tifanyx was not in the mood for Madtif's over embellished hunting stories. Madtif ignored Tifanyx's protest and continued on with his story. He told Tifanyx the story of a young Solitus woman who rejected the notum infusion. A story he learned while in employ with Omni-Tek. She was born as a Nanomage, but her body fought off the infusion shortly after birth. It seems that she lapsed into a coma for an extended period of time. While in the coma, the her skin began rejecting the nanomage modifications. After a few years all traces of notum within her were gone. She had reverted back to her Solitus form.

Omni-Tek was shocked by this and would not release her to her Omni-Tek employed parents. Instead, they kept her in a small lab in Rome, for observation and study. To try and determine how she was able to revert back to being a Solitus.

Madtif, ever the optimist, told Tifanyx, if she can do that maybe he can too?!?

Tifanyx knew he was out of options. He set out on a mission to free the Solitus woman. The question is, will he have enough time? Enough time to find her, free her and discover how she reverted from Nanomage to Solitus.


57 days is not alot of time. None of this would have to happen, if people only just listened..


3 days earlier..


02 June 29477 Late Morning


No one wanted to listen. Maybe, just maybe, my death will open their eyes, Tifanyx thought as he heard the news of his terminal illness. At this point, all the notum on the planet wouldn't save him. His body has grown too weak. Two Clan doctors confirmed Tifanyx's worst fears one seperate occasions, one week apart. Last week, Katelin "Tracya" Cervenak, a nanomage physician, examined Tifanyx and told him he had about one month to live. What?!? 30 days? Notum deficiency? Tifanyx tried his best not to believe the diagnosis. He was in denial.

After a week of moping about, and not being to social with his Whisper`s Edge mates, Tifanyx decided to break down and tell one of his guildmates. She was an excellent physician. Clearly Dr. Hyland would tell him the truth. He can't be dying. He has so much to live for.

Clan doctor extraordinaire Kristin "Sorroe" Hyland had given Tifanyx a thorough examination. The results were still the same, but a little bit better. Sorroe told Tifanyx to get his affairs in order, as he had maybe two months to live, at MOST.

So, he is still dying, but he gained another month. Tifanyx screamed at Sorroe thinking there must be some mistake. He began spewing some classic BORPAC rhetoric. The planet is constantly being raped of its Notum. The mining must be stopped.

Sorroe explained to Tifanyx that the effort and the heart of BORPAC may have been in the right place, but their methods may have caused more harm than good.

The destruction of towers was a good way to stop mining, unfortunately it also destroyed the notum currently in the tower. The notum is collected into the tower, then extracted and sold. The extraction process also releases a portion of the notum in the air. When a tower is attacked, all the collected notum is incinerated, which means no extra notum in the air.
Without the extra notum in the air, nanomages will slowly die. Nanomages like Tifanyx.

Sorroe couldn't explain why Tifanyx was being so drastically effected. She just mentioned that had he not been so stubborn, he could have used Whisper`s Edge mined notum to survive. Tifanyx still held on to his beliefs and those of BORPAC, and never used the mined notum. He had his chance, but now it is indeed too late. The cause of death will most certainly be a "Notum deficiency".
