MA ranger? Nay!
I see the MA as a disciplined fighter with concepts etc. If advs and MA's would get to become the 'bow masters' or something, I would simply make these differences:

To emphasize the vision of the MA being a MASTER of archaic and traditional weaponry and martial arts, they should have masteries.

To emphasize the vision of the ADV being a RANGER, they should be able to make special bows and arrows from monsters and trade items.

For example a group deals with some anacondas and the adv take the monster parts and use a >>>new item to convert monster parts into arrows and things as such<<< to make poison arrows
Or you kill Scorpiods for 'disease' arrows, gargs for chem damage, rhinos for imp/projectile ammo (low to medium damage with high crit), same for blubbs (this time with very good normal damage and low crit), energy ammo from cyborgs, bleeders for radiation damage, etc. Maybe should the adv's get a nano line for 'arrow customizing' masteries? =)

That way MA's and ADV's would trade their masteries etc.

They could also BOTH build their own bows but with their differences. MA's would have hard-to-use skill bows (but wouldn't bother them cuz they would have the masteries) with very good attack/damage stats and specific types of ammo, while the ADV's would make up high flexibilty ammo usage with average damage/attack. Sounds fair? =)

And then make up a special bow creation with MA's and ADV's coordinating together to create a super uber duper bow =) Would be real fun! Then maybe lock up some of our skills etc and have chances of bow creation failures... stuff like that =)
