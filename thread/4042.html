A small practical upgrade perhaps?
I have a small proposal to make...

When u press "U" and get up the skills table, some numbers are green (if ur using implants or buffs) and some are red (in case u just died etc). Now, let's say I have 578 in rifle skill, and the number is green. That means I'm using buffs or implants. I'd be happy if I could just press shift + rightclick (or something) to see what was behind that particular skill! Like a small list telling me that Snipers Bliss was doing +50 (for another 30 mins perhaps even!), my faded longrange weaponry right-wrist implant was doing +25 etc, etc, etc.... U get my point? It's a pain going through all my implants to check what they are giving me, casue to be honest, i can't remember the exact amout all the time...

So in addition to click the implants to see what they r doing, I'd like to click my skills direct to see WHAT was helping me!

I hope this can be done... It doesn't sound to hard fixing it
