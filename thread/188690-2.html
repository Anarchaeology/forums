Balderac
Supa Leet
You forgot one important thing in your uber wish-this-were-true patch notes:

1. The inventory system is totally rewamped. The old inventory window is gone, and all items you currently carry now show up in a folder tree, with small icons representing the items to the left of the name of the item. You can create, move and rename folders that contain items. The folders represent what used to be backpacks. You can select multiple items in your inventory list and click a "Sell, Trade, Drop, Delete button. There is no longer a limit to how many items you can carry.

Example:

code:
---------------------------------------------------------------------
WEAPONS
      |______ [ICON] Q200 Freedom Arms 2019
      |______[ICON] Q200 Caterwaul Rifle
      |______[PISTOLS]
                         |_________  [ICON]Q200 Freedom Armos 2019
MEDICAL EQUIPMENT
      |_______[ICON] Q200 First Aid Stim (148, stacked]

[DELETE] [TRADE] [DROP] [SELL] [SELL ALL MARKED ITEMS]
