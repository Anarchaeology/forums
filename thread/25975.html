Red Tigers =Alus= Massacred
On Saturday, Bateman and Fletcher, LLC headed to Camelot castle for some medieval fun and frivolity. Most of us having never been there before decided to just go look around and explore. During our adventure we bulldozed through many skeletons belonging to Lord Ghasap. After much exploration we found Lord Ghasap http://ploskina.com/602/aofiles/Ghasap.jpg and proceeded to annihilate him.

On our way back to the entrance, we encounted many members of teh Red Tigers =Alus= guild. A standoff occured for a few minutes until we decided to try to negotiate a peaceful walk by. As I walked forward to discuss the situation, I was savagely attacked by Acewolf, and it was very apparent what there intentions were to Omni Tek Employees. Our guild, small and inexperienced, layed waste to these foul clanners. Meegah was our last victim
http://ploskina.com/602/aofiles/redt...usownzored.jpg

Lastly we went to the foreman dungeon and destroyed T.I.M. for the first time. http://ploskina.com/602/aofiles/TIM.jpg We would like to formally thank Jdog for the very large train he kept trying to bring us, Its nice to see Omni Tek has such honorable employees.. well I guess theres always a bad apple eh.

Was a good day to be in Bateman and Fletcher, LLC
