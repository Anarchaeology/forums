Idea
I don't see the damage in allowing a char to change the way his ip is distibuted at any time he wants. There are plenty of ways to detour exploiters from abusing this, one idea is a penalty for redistribution, you can redistribute but it will cost you something. Perhaps it will cost you 1000ip points to invoke a redistribution. This would give people an oppertunity to explore the game and cast the "can never go back" mentality aside. I know I would try many different things if I knew I could reverse my direction if I was not enjoying the direction I was on.

People will say well what about the ones that will reset and then use the ip to boost a skill to do something and then change it back (ie save 1000 moster parts and then reset to up pharm tech do the parts and then reset again)? Well like I said make the ip reset cost you something. Perhaps it is a cash fee based on your current level and it is very expensive to do. Say you would have to save for quite some time to be able to afford it. Another idea would be to have a hard time limit on reseting your ip. Say you could only reset your ip once every RL week. Would any of the people that exploit make a week long sacrifice of a gimped char to make some monster parts, or perhaps do some trade skills? I think not.

My point is that there is a way to give people the flexability to change there char without destroying the game. This could be coupled with a game balencing change that would have a lot less people in a uproar if they could change the way they built there char. Remember people you are paying for enjoyment here, would you enjoy yourself more if you had the flexability to try out some of the different things in the game? I would. As it stands I have no choice but to choose a path and stay on it for the life of the char or make a new one.

Oh and you forgot to mention the oldtimers out there that were wasting IP like mad on FA's that now cease to exist. This plays right into my point, everyone was using FA because they were higher powered than most weapons, and if you were not using then you were underpowered. The fact is there were other weapons out there that were close, but no one was willing to give them a try for the fear that they would be wasting there presious IP point. This model of fixed skill assignment breeds non-diverse gameplay. It is why everyone and there brother you see now is carrying around Vektor shotty's, cause no one wants to try the pistols out there and find out, man non of these come close to the shotguns and what am I going to do now? Same with the other waepons.

My 2 cents

Sorry but...
I think you'll find that the majority of people who call for the IP reset are those folks that made mistakes themselves with their IP's.

And those people dead against it are those that can see the potential damage it could cause to the world.

I can give an example.......

Bob finds a nice little car in a chest, but has no driving skill, so he pumps IP into this Dark Blue skill to allow him to drive it.

50 levels later, he finds a Yalm, so now pops some points into his Flying skills, also Dark Blue.

Now Bob will never use a car again, so those Driving iP's are wasted......tough. But then along comes an IP reset and lucky for Bob, he gets to get those Driving IP's back to spend elsewhere.

Compare this to Fred, who at the time of the IP reset still has a car, in 50 levels time, he'll have no chance of getting his driving IP's back.

This scenario is nobody's 'fault' yet Fred now has a disadgvantage over Bob due to these lost IP's.

This is just one example of the type of thing that will probably stop FC from ever doing a total IP reset.

You say there are people that thru exploits have max'd ALL their IP's? well I think FC should possibly find characters that have done that and reset just them.

Bottom line? an IP reset benefits all non-exploiting 'high' level character, while widening the gap between them and the younger or newer players. This is hardly likely to encourage new blood to join the game, if they know they can never be as good as the early folks who got the max benefits from the 'one time' IP reset.

Cordi.
