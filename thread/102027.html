Love stolen, not lost
Copied from:

Omni Underground Monthly: Lifestyles Section

Newland City  Some things are just not meant to be. At least that is what he tells himself.

Our story started in Omni Controlled territory. A young man, destined for greatness. An up and coming Fixer within the corporation, Jellobiafra came from a rich family. Omni-Tek employees for over a century. But all this fame and fortune could not stop the avalanche heading his way.

Months ago, Jello, as his friends called him, became bored with the Omni approved internet that gives employees and family access to shopping and entertainment. He had heard rumors of Clan and Neutral controlled media with freedoms unheard of in Omni-Tek territory. He contacted a friend Trader of his, and acquired the needed technology to hack into those systems from within the Omni net.

The art, music, freedom of expression displayed throughout this illegal outlet ignited the passion hidden so long within him. But Jello was unaware of just how passionate things would become. For on the third day of perusing the net, he heard a voice that would forever change his life.

Now, his notes do not state exactly whom this voice belonged to, probably to protect this person. We are left to speculate. And unfortunately, his notes are all that we have to go on. But more on that later.

By unbelievable luck, Jello was able to correspond with Ms. T (as he refers to her) on the bulletin board of an illegal entertainment site. She had taken up a fight between members of a hunting team that happened across an item of great wealth. Her passion for this incident immediately invoked the same in Jello, and he joined her in the fight. This writer does not yet know the outcome of this attempt for justice, but I shall look deeper when time allows.

Regardless, during this fight a bond between the two became apparent. And in an amazing twist of fate, a marriage proposal was put forth. Jellobiafra was taken aback. Could this be for real? he stated in his notes. A meeting was discussed and decided upon.

And that was as far as it would go for Jellobiafra and the mysterious Ms. T. For on the next day, as Jello attempted to cross into Clan territory using the grid, he found an elite Omni task force waiting for him. You see, the trader that Jellobiafra purchased the illegal technology from, had been under investigation. Because of Jellos high profile, the agents immediately recognized him.

Jellobiafra was immediately taken to an undisclosed location within Omni controlled territory. Using what sources say is an experimental new technology, Jello was mind wiped, erasing all memory of the woman who awoke inside of him the passion he did not know existed.

A week or so later, this office received an envelope that included all of Jellos notes regarding this incident. Where Jello is now and who sent the envelope to us, we just dont know. Rumor has it that the mind wipe was complete enough that an alternate personality had to be implanted. Jello, oblivious to it all, will never remember what transpired in those few weeks. And maybe, just maybe.. its for the best.

And how did Ms. T take being left standing, alone, awaiting her love to join her? We will probably never know. We can only hope that she reads this, and finds some comfort in the fact that he did not throw away their chance at true love because of some ill informed notion of Omni vs. Clan.

Maybe, just maybe, she will fight this too. And maybe, just maybe, Jello will be watching.
