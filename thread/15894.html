Solo difficulties: triple teaming
I don't know the degree to which this issue has been addressed, but here goes:

I find it quite frustrating that in solo missions, I most often come across a main room with three baddies in it. As soon as I engage one, his two friends join in the battle and suddenly I am fighting a 3 on 1 battle, and losing quite rapidly. Faced with these odds, I decide to make a tactical retreat out of the mission area to heal. I return a minute later only to find that all the damage I just did has been forgotten about and I am back to square one! The first time I thought it was a quirk or bug, but after subsequent attempts and numerous encounters, I have decided to believe that it is a simple gameplay issue.

So I decided to see if there was a reasonable way around this.

- First, I tried killing just one completely before running. This led only to the same reset mission, and my character dying most of the time before he could kill the first one.

- Second, I decided that attacking one at long range (with sniper rifle) from another room might provoke only that one giving me a 1 to 1 even battle. Result was still mostly provoking all three at once, and therefore same problem.

- Then I decided to write off the rewards from killing those three, and just sneak past them. Outcome here was mostly detection during the numerous times the room had to be entered, if not the very first attempt. This led to the same assault by the annoying triumvirate.

- So I then went back to the mission terminals, and turned the difficulty way lower. Here, I found that, in general, missions that didn't kill me instantly with this tactic had a setting low enough to deny me of any xp, as all enemies from there on were gray. Missions were then not worth playing at all.

- Turning the mission difficulty back up, I decided I needed help. So I put a message or two out on the "seeking team" channel, with no helpful replies most of the time. And anywhere I looked, I really couldn't find anyone to help me either. In the end, only 75% of the time could I find help, %50 of which stayed around long enough to be useful, and %45 of the rest who wanted at least half the corpses and took most of my chests. The only big advantage here was that I could turn the difficulty up a little higher without too much risk, and I ended up taking less damage (and thus spent less on healing items). But the end result here was that the time spent in finding someone to help in missions, combined with the apparent cut in xp and definite cut in credits and items, seemed to be an overall loss for me.

In the end, soloing gets overly difficult most of the time, and the only productive workaround wasn't soloing anymore.

So I ask:
Am I the only one who has difficulty with this? Have other people mentioned this already?
If I am just too much of a newbie to play well, then what on Rubi-Ka am I doing wrong? Is there a better way to attack this problem? Could anyone give me pointers then?

Frustrated and Confused,
AK

ok
Observation 1. Mobs aggro when they notice you.

Observation 2. Mobs notice you when you hit them

Observation 3. Mobs notice you when you hit someone in the room they are in (or close-by ie. doorway)

Observation 4. By turning Agg/Defbar to full def. you a) dont autoattack or b) hit way slower (=no autoattack)


Try this:

Goal: not hitting the mob/attacking the mob but letting it attack you then run away to a safe spot of your choosing

1.turn your AggDefbar to full def. then

2. move/sneak/whatever slowly into the room with the multiple mobs

3. hang back in the room-opening (showing your ass or /flip'ing them) until one notices you

4. run away like hell to 1-2 rooms away to fight the single mob there


Note: The reason for 4. is to try to avoid the other mobs/friends noticing you killing off their friend while they're having lunch...


I hope this will end your journey of frustration while soloing

Yep...
...follow the above advice.

1. Move in slowly to get one of the critters to aggro you. For this is usually have my map open and walk backwards into the room. This way I can get out quickly while keeping tabs on the critters (I have Monster Map upgrade) when I get the "Attacked by XXX" message. The reason you want to get out as quickly as possible is....

2. If they shoot you while in the room with their buddies, the others will aggro also. You need to get them totally out of the room and into a cleared room before you start the fight. Additionally, once you get into the cleared room, get out of a direct line of the doorway. Sometimes the MOB will engage you while standing in the doorway which will bring the others running. Just step to the side so that the MOB has to enter the room to get line of sight.

3. Kill MOB, rinse, repeat.

Its pretty easy to seperate the group with practice...

Here is some tips, but bear in mind that sometimes if they are grouped close you gotta wait for them to split up. I do this in the rooms that have 2 mobs as well.

Walk up slowly to the door and wait...
If you get the message: "Attacked by badguy" back away, and do not shoot him until he is in the starting room.

If you don't get the message, take another step in the room...keep doing this until one of the badguys attacks you, then run back in the room to lure him in.

If he's a shooter, you will have to move into a position where he can't hit you, thus forcing him to run into the room with you.

If you ever shoot him in the big room, most of the time his buddies will join him, so do not shoot until he is in the same room as you.

Hope this helps =)
