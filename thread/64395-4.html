Justification for extra IPR
Drabin, I respect your arguments, but your reasoning is flawed because you omit to include the fact that Funcom, when they stopped LLTS from dropping, clearly said they were *not* removing them from the game, so we should treasure the ones already there. To me, that sounds like Funcom intended them to remain in the game, the same way as the Freedom Arms 3927, as an item to covet and work hard to obtain.

It is not the players' fault that Funcom now decide to render LLTS obsolete with this nerf, thus going back on their word. Therefore, it is only fair to those of us who spent many many levels worth of IP in skills that will be of no value after the next nerf, be compensated for the wasted IP. IPR points already given at new title levels don't enter into the equation, as these points were not intended to rectify Funcoms's mistakes, but rather the players' own mistakes, IMO. Playing by rules expressed very specifically by funcom, is not a player mistake.
