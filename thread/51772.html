Goodbye?
OOC: (crossposted from RK2 Orgs as I have a feeling the majority of the people who'd have an interest in this probably aren't following the thread on the other board)
---


Karanya sits down the bags she packed for going off planet and mutters to herself.. "Damn them all, I will see this out to the bloody end.. and the end will be very bloody, of that I am certain.." She pauses to think for a minute, then steps outside, looking around carefully until she spots a young opifex woman approximately her size, with similar looks, and a slow smile spreads across her face.

"Excuse me, ma'am," Karanya calls out confidantly. "Ms. Summers?"

The young woman looks puzzled. "I think you've gotten me confused with someone else, my name is Alice Carver."

"Right, right," Karanya answers. "Ms. Carver, that's what I meant to say. Congratulations, Ms. Carver! You've won an all expenses paid off planet vacation.. I almost didn't catch you in time, your transport leaves in 2 hours! Here, then take your tickets, just sign here and here.. mmhmm.. let's see your identification.. there you go. Enjoy your vacation!"

Alice hesitantly accepts the tickets.. examining them closely she sees they look legitmate.. she shrugs and pockets the paperwork, not noticing that her ID card handed to her with the tickets is not the one she displayed to Karanya.

"Ah, there's your cab right there, Ms. Carver!" Karanya says, smiling enthusiastically. "Get right in and you'll be to the spaceport in no time at all," she says as she practically pushes the young woman into the cab.

"Have a nice vacation!" Karanya calls out as the driver speeds away.

Karanya steps back inside her apartment, and opens an encrypted com channel from the panel beside her bed. "Hey, my friend.. wanna make a delivery for me? ... Mmm.. yes.. ED-49-B.. to be put on board flight 9362.. leaving in an hour and forty-five minutes. ... I know it's short notice! ... Come on, man, don't disappoint me now.. it'll be lucrative, I promise. ... 2 minute timer after launch. ... Alright. What's the damage? ... TWENTY FIVE MILLION creds?! ... Fine, dammit, you'll get it. Now GO!"

... 2 hours later ... 20:15

Karanya stands outside her apartment, watching the star-lit sky in the vicinity of the spaceport... she watches the streak of the off-planet transport as it launches, and moments later a brilliant flash lights the sky as the transport explodes into thousands of bits of burning metal. She smiles, and languidly dons her Keelar sunglasses, moments before she disappears into the grid.. "To Tir, then," she whispers to herself. "To Tir."

Karen "Karanya" Vel

6 fixers and a crat...
...a small, dimly lit shack just outside Borealis - 01:00 the following night...

Karanya sits on a table in the corner, surrounded by a half dozen shady looking men and one woman, their faces obscured in the darkness.

"I've been screwed." she states simply. "I've got people moving in on my business.. on OUR business, letting me take all the heat while they reap the benefits.. it's time to put an end to this." Her voice lowers to almost a growl.. "No one messes with us and gets away with it.."

Heads nod slowly and a young male voice speaks from the back of the room.."What about Lane?"

Karanya turns to face the speaker, a glitter of anger in her eyes. "Don't even speak that name to me!" she snaps. "Last I heard he was shacked up with some clanner woman, and he's trying to send a proxy from among my adversaries to speak on his behalf.. quite a lot of nerve he has after I've put myself on the line for his sorry ass repeatedly."

"Some 'clanner' woman?" the young man's voice inquires.

"Ah, hell," Karanya responds with an exasperated sigh, "Some woman then, you know it doesn't make a difference to me which side of this idiotic conflict she's on.. no offense, guys. You all know I was born on clan side. Now let's get to work. You know who the mark is tonight.. this guy has been getting in on our business, all of ours, and it's time he's stopped for good."

Karanya digs around in her pack in the darkness for a moment and produces a small device, which she hands to a quiet man standing next to her. "You know what to do," she says. He nods, and takes the device from her.

Karanya turns to the other woman in the room. "We're going to need the assistance of Smithers for this.. just as an interface, he won't be damaged. Have him jack into this panel right here." She dusts off a portion of the wall and flips a switch revealing a lit control panel that bathes the room in an eerie green glow.

Senator Chan frowns, but nods assent and issues a command to the 'droid, who jacks into the control panel. "You know," Chan says, "He wasn't really designed for this.."

A wide grin spreads across Karanya's face and the men in the room chuckle softly. "Hon," she says, grinning at Chan, "We've been using things for purposes they weren't designed for since you were still attending diplomacy classes at Omni Prime."

"Shhh.." the man holding the specialized hacking tool says softly. "I'm working here, much more out of any of you and Smithers is going to be robot junk when my hand slips." The room falls silent but for the clicking of his tools as he works and the soft whirring of the 'droid.

"Accessing..." he says, then pauses a moment. "I'm in! Reverting insurance pattern to backup.. Kara.. how far back do you want to go for this?"

"Delete it," Karanya says softly.

"What?" he asks. "The job was to revert our mark to a previous insurance pattern.."

"I know what the job we were hired for was," Karanya says sharply. "Now delete the entire pattern, and every backup copy."

"Done," he says a moment later, wiping a bit of sweat from his brow and disconnecting the device from the 'droid, who steps away from the panel and resumes normal function.

Another man speaks up from the background, "Now what? We're going to kill him?"

"No," Karanya replies. "An accident will find our mark soon enough and we'll get some use out of him before that happens. Time to proceed with the rest of the plan.. care to fill them in, my friend?"

A man's voice speaks up, "Alright guys, this is how it's going to work.." He stops, interrupted by the sound of footsteps outside the building.

"Omni-Pol," someone says softly.

"Evac.. and don't forget Chan & Smithers.." Karanya says, fading into the grid with the rest of the conspirators.. mere seconds before the door comes crashing down.
