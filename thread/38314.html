Community answers - part 4
First of all, I have to excuse that some issues are taking a bit of time. This is mostly due to people being on vacation, and we don't want to give out answers without having them confirmed first.

I will (most likely) be off the rest of the week myself, but the other guys will be around. Might not be too much updates in here this week though, as they have other work to do too. Please bare with us. I think we deserve a little time off too.

Now, for today's issues...

Plastic surgery (...to allow people to change face on their character).
Submitted as a suggestion for the development team, though I'm not sure how much of a priority it would be. Might eb a nice money sink though.

XP limits
People have written to us wondering why they don't get the full XP reward at times (e.g. mission rewards). This is because there is an XP cap of 5% or 10% (depending on the source) based on how much you need to level. I was not able to confirm where each of these apply, but I believe 10% is for killing MOBs, while 5% is for mission rewards. Please let me know if you experience otherwise.

Wrong weapon initiatives
The list of the weapons currently in the reports (one for bows, one for other weapons:

Arwen MO-404 Grenade Launcher
BBI Faithful
Collins IX Bio-Energy Rifle
HSR Explorer 661
M-1 Bipower A
Manex Catastrophe
MTI - Russian Good Day
Netgun
Nova Flow - Mark I

Balanced Joint Clans Exocet II
Kevlar Bow
Oneida Razor Half-Bow
Reet-Tech Stryker X
Reet-Tech Venom Compound Bow
Sapphistic Bow
Schuyler Bow
Spring Inc Tube-Bow

And I think Bow-Blaster will be turned into a full-fledged crossbow, so it will keep Ranged Initiative. (Crossbows use Ranged Init, bows use - or should use - Physical Init.)

Got any not on the list? E-mail them to me, and I'll add them.

Loot messages
Specific suggestion handed to the designers. Hopefully a vicinity loot message will expose and limit - maybe even stop - ninja looting.

Trap damage
Trap damage originates from the one setting it off, and will thus make his own damage shields and reflection devices damage him even more. Reported as a bug, and needs some change in the trap system, I guess.

Custom made maps
People want to import their own maps to AO, and we're working on a description on how to do it. The first draft was a bit too hard to understand, and one of the QA guys volunteered to write an easier to understand tutorial or something. Hopefully it won't be long until it's on the site.

Unused part of original map
Some players are missing Sparta and the other areas not found on the current map, but being on the original map of Rubi-Ka. Unfortunately, none of these are scheduled for release in any near future, with the exception of... Jobe (Shadowlands Expansion).

Unique items problem
Doing a trade process which result in a Unique item, when you already have that Unique item, will lose you the components and not give you a new item. Bug reported. Be careful with those expensive components.

Same goes for completing missions that give Unique rewards, and already having the reward.

Sharing XP from locks and traps
We're hoping to have XP gained from picking locks and disarming traps shared with the entire team in 14.6. No promises, but 14.6 is what we hope for.

Targeting own pets
Suggestion for design submitted, and awaiting feedback on it. Basically, the idea is to use the F7 key to target and cycle through your pets. We'll see what happens.

PS: Pet issues (mostly pathing, but also this targeting thing) is #2 on Community's list of priorities.

---

Ok, that's it for now. Wish I could have more, but as said, vacation is putting a limit on information right now.

Btw, we will probably collect the information from these threads and put it in a closed summary thread later, to have it all available without having to read lots of replies. Not likely to get to that until next week though.
