What does each profession bring to a team?
Here is a list of what I think of when a proffesion joins my team. I am probaby missing some things by this is what goes through my head. I'd like to get a survey as to what others think each profession brings to a team.

Doctor: Heals, HP buffs
Enforcer: Tank, HP buffs, damage
Soldier: Tank, Ranged damage
MP: Nano skill buffs, healing pet, attack pet
Engineer: Big arse tanking Pet
Trader: Heals, Wrangles
NT: Calms, big finisher damage, HE, nano range increaser
MA: crit buff, very good damage (now), healing
Agent: Secondary buffer, Aimed shot
Crat: crowd control, charms
Adv: backup healer
Fixer: Run buff (well not really, I'm a Fixer and I have my own Run Buff)
