Omni-Pol Press Release
Multiple requests have been received on my comm. link for a statement from Omni-Pol. The following will document for public viewing Omni-Pols plans to assist in resolving this case.


Omni-Pol will as with any case stand by its policies and enforce them throughout. It is clearly stated within our code of practice that we will not divulge any information that is pertinent to an ongoing investigation.

Although the basic details have already been reported on Commander Fyarrs untimely death upon the discovery of his corpse the sewer district of Omni1, there are many factors that need consideration and further investigation.

After being contacted and performing a thorough investigation there is evidence that implicates both Dave Diamondcut Duster and Phoenixbird (active member of STORM); we are also perusing credible leads that implicate STORM aiding Phoenixbird and as being involved.

We are currently at a stage in our investigation where we are issuing warrants for the arrest of Dave Diamondcut Duster and Phoenixbird. We anticipate additional warrants will be issued which are likely to further implicate those higher ranked within the STORM organisation. Of course our official policy is that subsequent to any questioning, those involved will be released without harm. At this point Omni-Pol does not publicly offer rewards for information, we believe it is every Omni citizens duty to provide information that will help protect the cities and will help to decreases the crime rates.

If anyone has any information or was a witness to the appalling events then please contact our Public relations office, General Ukblizzard heads this and can be contacted on her comm. device. All information received will be treated with the strictest confidence.

Knowing that the clan organisations have no law enforcement or real leader to deal with, Omni-Pol will continue patrols in clan territory although on a more regular basis to seek out these criminals and bring them to justice. We will also make a formal request for assistance from our elite divisions. We would like to emphasise that we are not initiating an invasion of clan territory but that we are there to capture these wanted criminals. In light of this investigation though, officers have been issued orders to the effect that if they are attacked then they are to defend themselves.

We hope that through the combined efforts of loyal citizens with ourselves, that this case is closed promptly and law and order is restored.


President Gencop
