This has probably been said a few times, but goes for saying again.

1) Dualing with 2 chars from one account is an exploit. I know from the joys of getting disconnected, the software is designed to only let you log in once per account, no matter which domain you're using. And since you're exploiting a bug, it is therefore a banable offence.

2) Dualing with 2 chars on 2 accounts is completely valid if you own both accounts. If the second account is owned by a friend or family member, you are technicaly violating the EULA, but as long as nothing questionable is done (IE deleting items, deleting chars, being an ass to the rest of the players) there's no real way to detect this. This is more the player's responsiblity to take care of, more than Funcom's.
While playing EQ, I know of more than one situation where members of my guild were given items, because someone's boyfriend/girlfriend got upset, knew the other person's login, and went and gave all their equipment away. And Veriant's responce? "tough ", and sometimes banned the account, since the password had been shared.

This issue is not unique to AO, and pretty much everyone has the same answer. You want the convinece of having 2 chars on at the same time? Pay for a second account, or deal with it.
