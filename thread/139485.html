The "Good old days" Thread
Ahh, the good old days!

I remember my first serious character, Rawpower. He teamed with alot of well known highlevels back in those days. It was so cute.. I remember one person who have Experienced pvp-title atm and uber lewt.. he was so cute in his cyber-armour!
Some of the others had primus coat which was uber and very rare because allmost noone knew how to get one. The rest had a really ugly mixture of plaststeel armour and a medium tank.

Back in those days the lvl 30ish enforcer "Lasvegas" was the most known pvp'er or maby even the most known character in general on the entire RK1-server. He was like a legend and most people had heard of him.

Yalms was a rare sight and I never understood how someone could get so much cash. Getting a yalm was my dream and my idol at that time, "Looke" -70 adv, actually baught one and I SO wanted to be him when I saw him with his hiiigh ql Rider Executioners and yalm.. and primus coat.. I used to ask him to show his Riders so I could see the reqs because at that time I couldnt understand that someone could be uber enough to get those stats!

Everyone was killing stationary NPC's because in those days FC hadnt applied 1k-damage shield to them and they spawned again 20 sec after kill.

Mort was actually a scary place and the thaught that I, one day, would be able to SOLO mobs in that place sounded far out. Borgs wasnt even in the game back then.

Haha, I remember the day I got enough cash to buy a ground vehicle (50k). I ran around trying to lvl to 32 so I could get IP into vehicle-ground-skill. Then in the middle of the night I equipped it and I was driving around like mad and people actually said "wow" back then when I drove past. They said "wow" to my tankarmour too because only uber people had tank-armour. In those days lvl 70 was sky-high lvl so if u saw someone with enough skill to wear tank then u knew he was "uber!". What I remember most about that car of mine was my friend. He got really pissed off because I phoned him in the middle of the night just to say that I got a CAR in AO!

I didnt even know that it was possible to wear 2 sleeves at once back then. I saw that there was 2 sleeve-slots but for some reason I didnt get the message.

If I hadnt deleted good old Rawpower then he would still have had that HUGE Carrier-craft that buffed strenght by 150

People like Looke had a hard time finding QL 200 versions of their weapons because there was so few people in high enough lvl in those days to actually get a mission for them. Looke looked for a 200 Rider Executioner and asked me for help finding one. I tracked down another uber player, Tomtemor. She was an adv too and she had one of those ql 200 swords. I asked if she would sell it but they where so rare that IF she was going to sell it then she would want several hundred k's of credits which in those days was an insane amount.

Which brings me to how people got cash. Nr. 1 way to get cash back in those days was actually to complete a mission for the reward. I remember going to Avalon everyday getting missions that gave 13k cr as reward and in those days that was really good!

Dragongirl (200 MA atm and she has been for ages) pulled a bunch of lvl 90 mantises on my team, killing us all. I sweared that I would remember her name and tell people about her

WA was THE place to hang out. There was even more people in WA than anywhere else because EVERYONE who made a character would end up in WA Backyard Nr. 1. That was their original place of origin.

Huntinspot Nr. 1 was the beach just south of WA and that island with supa-leets on the other side of the river in Aegan. I used to go there with Rawpower when he reached lvl 50 and was concidered uber. I used to follow the small people around and smacking Rollerrats for them. Then they would thank me and call me uber. It was nice

Oooh I miss those days. Soldiers used Novaflows, NT's didnt use weapons and Stigma was THE rifle for everyone! OMG the Stigma was so uber!

And the best part was.. the fact that noone was whining about anything!

Times long gone...
I remember starting out and being told my friends to meet them in Galway Shire and then doing the small mobs there, gradually moving to tougher challenges, Hammerheads, TheCage with Scorpions and spiders, Alpha Females and Bulls near Bronto factory and finally Mantizes which started to feel like a bad exp.

Up till this moment I was exping mostly with rl friends, Izimazku, Gindokan, Rekkis and strugling to finance my new nanos, etc. Back then even 200k was quite much money and every new nano felt like it cost a fortune.

From Galway times I mostly remember my rl friends and a doc named Erica and the people lfg at Galway castle gate and when getting higher level, moving to other gate.

At this time I went to 2ho first time and gained some pretty fast levels there. Back then it was kinda secret place with only few Omnis hunting there. I made myself regular there as Manteze exp was starting to suck and met some early PvP people. Most of the people back that time in 2ho were PvPers.

With some of them I went to MMD to my first big PvP fight with 10 Omnis against 15 Clans and that was some fun. I still remember clans hugging safe zone and people shouting insults and how some rare clanner who had Yalmaha answered that atleast he was rich and dirty or something to which Omni replied that they give him 5M to buy some courage to step into 25%. I died few times but kept going back and finally made it Freshman which was quite rare at those times.

I continued to hunt in 2ho and more and more people started to arrive from Galway and finally made the 2ho gate the "new" meeting place of Omni's lfg. At best of times there were prolly up to 30 people at gate and as much or more hunting around 2ho.

From that time I mostly remember people like Gukky who was leading one of the best exp teams I've ever had there and MA named Pips who was pretty uber twinked compared to others and really rocked leading parties making fast but safe exp. He rarely made mistake that costed the life of party like some other party leaders.

I also remember how I started to feel bit like crap because Nano Init wasnt workin, many nanos were black holed, resisted or did min damage so I usually got like 1 nuke off to shade before it was dead and really didnt feel like I was doing my part.

Things that didnt work back then included nano bases range increasers, HE/CC (including hardware ones), Nano Init.. oh boy did the NT's suck

At that time I saw Cotorie doing 2700 AS on Shade near the 2ho gate and crowd go wild. OMG did you see that?! Actually many didnt know how good Agents were back then as they rarely partied with anyone and if they did, it was mostly with close friends. I then had Tabaal in one of the parties I was and saw how they rocked. Asked zillion of questions and started to think about Agent myself.

At the end of this time, some clans started to come to 2ho to hunt exp parties there. It is that time I came to known Omnifoxfire aka Itburnz (the old player not the new), etc and eventually came to be a friend of some.

Then the disaster striked. FC nerfed the spawn and visual in 2ho that made hunting there crap. People were confused and started alts, quit, etc. I finally managed to get to party who said there was good exp in 20K and off we went, roam hunting in the wilderness there as only Omni party visible.

At that time I made my decision to switch character though and well.. the age of innocence was basically over. By the time I leveled Agent to catch up, twinking was already pretty common and Lush fields was place to exp for lowbies and 20K had grown to be the "next spot".

What was different back then? It was all about outworld hunting, even roam killing. Camps were not very known and people roamed around wast areas killing stuff. It was very very fun.

There are some other people I remember (like that one doc who went clan after 2ho spawn was nerfed) but I really cant anymore remember all the names and hd crash took the pics

I miss those times and all the friends I met...

Oh and about armor.. well it was Cyber, Bau and Waitt (uber) and then Nano to _look_ uber

No whiners in the old days? Does the name Cptfallout mean nothing to you? :-)

But seriously, to an extent I agree with many of the sentiments posted here. I do miss the freshness and innocence of when the world was new, particularly as Techblast said, the joy of exploring on foot a world, living in constant danger and seeing things few other players had seen before. But that kind of thrill is by its very nature transitory; after the honeymoon period you either learn how to keep the game fun for you or just hop on to the next mmorpg to experience it all again and again.

As for whether people roleplayed more back in them olden days, it I guess it's all a very subjective. I know that for the first few months in AO, I only knew only a couple of other actual roleplayers and I could go on for days and even weeks without any significant in-character interaction. I looked hard for a dedicated roleplaying guild, one which did not simply equate roleplaying to mass PvP but there just wasn't much choice. Quite a few guilds, like the one I ended up joining, while claiming to want to promote roleplaying, simply never seemed to get round to it. By contrast, nowadays there is wide range of established roleplaying guilds with different styles and solid track records to choose from. Speaking just from my own experience, I would say roleplaying in AO has come along way and grown from uncertain infancy into full maturity. Of course YMMV.
