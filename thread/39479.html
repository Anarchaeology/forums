To be the best
"Violence isn't an answer to a problem, it's the only solution on Rubi-ka."

Those words still haunt my ears after all these years. I remember my Sensei first telling me that at my training camp. I don't remember my life before the camp. My past has never been more important then the reality I live in here and now.

I trained daily at the camp. It was hard at first. Everyone in the camp watched their own backs. Friendship was forbidden. Our purpose there was to be the best at what were to do. What we were training to do was kill. Savagely. Ruthlessly. Mercilessly.

At began when my Sensei told me to do weight lifts in the back. My arms ached after only a few sets and I gave up. "You're trying to work against the iron. You think the iron is your enemy, and you're trying to lift that which does not want to move." he said.

"When the iron doesn't move, it's the kindest thing it can do for you. It's trying to help you. If it flew up and went through the ceiling, then it wouldn't be doing you any good. It's not resisting you in the least. Work with the iron. The material you work with is that which you will come to resemble. That which you work against will always work against you, including yourself."

"Don't try to fight the pain, it is not your enemy. It is a calling to greatness. It's your body trying to pull you together."

My eyes open. I'm in my apartment. My body aches all over from the surgery I underwent yesterday. My old implants were removed, and custom built ones were put in their place. I open and close my hand in time with my breaths.

A static buzzed filled the air as my com line activated.
"Alphonso, how are you feeling?"
"Sore, but itching to get back to battle." I replied.
"Good to hear. Offically, things are still quiet, but as normal, there have been a few skimishes in 2ho and Meetmedere."
I grinned. "Good to see a war horse like me still has a purpose."

I exited my apartment to greet the moons in the Old Athen sky. I strenched on the balcony, watching the training grounds and thinking about the times I spent there testing my mettle on the leets and rollerrats that roamed there. I walked away from the apartment complex towards the city. I could feel tonight would be a good night.

I made my way to the nearest grid access terminal and plugged in. I was never really comfortable with the feeling of being digitalized. My body disappears before me when my eyes are blanketed in white, then the abstract becomes reality, and reality, the abstract. I made my way to the MMD point...

My body becomes real again, and I quickly activate a programing run in my NCU. Once my programing protocol runs through I head quickly into the center of MMD.

Suddenly, I feel my feet stop and my internal Heads Up Display flashes "Lesser Detain Customer is being run... forced action taken against NCU..." I quickly spotted my target, aparently the Omni had shadowed herself using a cloaking device. She leveled her shotgun at me and begin to fire her shotgun.

Her first blow merely glanced off my arm, but it was enough for me to break free from the hostile root. I quickly rushed her, and unleashed my fury in a flurry of kicks and punches. She ran another program on me, and I could feel my combat potential drop drastically. In response, I quickly flew my palm into the center of her chest towards a secret nerve point that when hit, would slowly drain her of health.

We were locked in a dance of death, until she knew she was going to lose. She quickly used another root on me and fled. I lost her on my HUD and cursed myself for my failure.

But the night was young..
