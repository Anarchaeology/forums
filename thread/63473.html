Please make a patch with NO NEW content!
Why would I love a patch with no new content? Because at the moment it feels like the new stuff takes about 99% of the developers time. And with the amount of small annoying bugs in the game 1% developer time for bug hunting just isn't enough IMHO. Please make a patch that's 100% bug-fix!

I have seen several statements about how much time it takes to fix and test bugs. Well... I'm a programmer myself and I can't stop thinking: How long can it take to fix a wrong initiative or a bad description on an item. That should be as simple as changing an attribute in the item-database.

If it takes several months to change a few numbers or attributes on a few items, I'm betting we will still have Yalm-bugs or problems with pet-pathing when AO has it's 4 year birthday.

I see lot's of other MMORPG's out there. And most of the ones I know about has one thing in common. If they see a bug, they fix it! And they do it now. Not in 2 months time... or half a year... or ??? If they see something like the org bank exploit the server goes down NOW! And they fix it at once. No comments like: We are aware of the problem and we hope to have a fix for the next patch... maybe... no garanties...

Don't get me wrong. I know why Funcom is putting so much energy into the new expansion. The expansion will be a new box on the shelves, which will make a lot of new players for AO. And that's great of course. But don't be fooled. With the amount of competition out there (no need to mention the upcomming Star Wars Galaxies, right) the new players will turn away just as fast if the bugs are too many and too severe.

I have personally introduced a handfull of players to AO. They all loved it more than anything. No other game in the world feels as social as AO. But they are all playing other games today. Simply because there were no way in hell they would wait forever for pet-pathing and similar problems to be fixed. Those things worked perfectly well in the games they came from.

So please please pleeeeeaaaase Funcom. Do something about those bugs. I don't really care about a ton of new armor or a new vehicle or whatever, if the game prevents me from playing with those items like they were supposed to! Who want to spend millions on a new Yalm just to find out that You can't get out of it 50% of the time. Or spend LOTS of hours to get that new ninja-droid. And when You finally get it going and tell the droid to attack some poor little leet, the droid just runs away and tries to **** a concrete wall???

From the amount of bug-reports on this bulletin board I'm quite sure that most people in AO would gladly wait another month for that new gun, if it meant that the time would be spend fixing bugs instead.

WOW look at all those words... And I don't think I really said anything new anyway

the larger crippling bug not mentioned yet
Pet pathing is my number one concern, and they may be able to fix it soon. (suggestion /pet warp)

There is a greater problem in that, the servers cannot handle large amounts of people in the same area. This may be much harder to fix as the fix involves the architecture of the game. It is crippling because it would be enjoyable to fight with more than 6 players, but my experience is that boss rooms are above the capability of the present design to do smoothly. I have a very fast computer, a very large connection to a good ISP, and a quick route to funcom. But when three of us were fighting a boss, we were still seeing lag errors.

The reason this is crippling, is that MMoRPG's have the potential for completely different ways of playing. FPS games have to restrict the number of players, and unfortunately so does AO. The frame count of my uber hardware drops incredibly when there are more than 10 people in an area.

Imagine for a moment though, that you could get 100's of people in an area, with 1000's of NPC or Mobs. The first and most obvious thing you could do in AO is a static battle line. everyday hundreds of Mobs could line up,and depending on the number and skill of the players that came, that day the battle would be won by x, and everyone would get some special bonus. Or imagine a siege dungeon. Or Camelot that flowed smoothly. Or Omni filled with hundreds of citizens, to interact with. Or wildlife that teams with lots of the little unkillable critters that add to realism. Or large scale protests, competitions, and so on.

Unfortunately I do not think AO could be made to do this at this time, unless some big company decided that they wanted to try out a new global network architecture to reduce lag, and to make a new type of viable platform on which to run games so they all feel like they are at lan speed (......hint.....HAL.+1....)
