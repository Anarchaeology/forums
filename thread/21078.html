From friends I started with - one is still lvl 37; I am 97 with my main char; another is 125; and one is a fast climbing lvl 154.

Many people seem to expect FunCom to provide fun for them. And indeed, FunCom is sorely lacking in that department from one perspective. Their events department certainly could give us a bit more meat ;).

But from perspective that I choose to take most of the time - FunCom has done their part.
They have provided us with beautiful, extensive, detailed, rich virtual world. They have provided us with communication tools that span the globe. They have enabled us to interact with this world and with each other.
True it is imperfect, but it's getting better every day, and as it is, it's already pretty amazing:).

Compared to kids that have nothing but paper and pencils (and still have fun:)), I would say that FunCom has indeed provided us much.

All it takes is some willingness and a creative spark - and you can have a BLAST on Rubi-Ka!:)
You can tell stories, interact, make events, explore, meet and have fun with people; be surprised, be saddened, laugh and fight.

If you haven't found those things in AO, any of those things, I am afraid you can hardly blame FunCom.

Sure, there are a lot of things we can blame on FunCom.
But almost all of them in the end fall into category of "mechanics", which should be (but aren't) secondary.
