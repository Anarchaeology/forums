Can we get a Top Ten?
Serious question here. Can we get a Top Ten list of questions answered regarding the Backstory of Rubi'Ka and it's politics, etc including the ongoing Story?

We wouldn't want anything other than what would be generally known or what a player would need to know in order to properly play his character within the Story.

Examples:

1.) Please define the political status of Newland City, Last Ditch, Borealis. What is the level of self-sovereignty and who is ultimately responsible for the ongoings in these cities.

2.) Who created the City of Wine? Is it possible to get to this city in-game currently and what it it's political affiliation.

3.) Please settle the discussion as to whether Jobe was created by NanoTechnicians only or through the cooperative efforts of the NanoMage breeded Engineers, NT's, MP's, Bureaucrats, etc.

4.) Is it safe for our characters to assume that all life on the planet is either imported, genetically-created, or arrived as stowaways on transports?

4.) What is the corporate structure of Omni-Tek at the very top? Is Phillip Ross appointed by a Board of Directors at the O-T Rubi'Ka level or via the next level up. Is the CEO of the Rubi'Ka division even elected or is he appointed by someone above his level.

5.) Answer the same for Henry Radiman. How is he elected and for what period is his term of office.

6.) Are there Clans that do not subscribe to the decision making authority of the Council of Truth. How much authority do decisions by Radiman and the Council hold over such clans as Sentinels and New Dawn?

7.) Why are there references in the Story literature and in-game to both Athen and AthenS? It's clearly stated that Omni-2 was renamed for a fighter during the last Civil War with the last name of 'Athen'. What's the deal?

8.) Obviously none of us can miss the big Temple and Pyramid in the City of Home. So many of our characters pass through there that it's kind of silly none of them have a clue as to why the Temple and Pyramid are there. Any backstory that our characters may be privy to would be appreciated.

9.) Is the City of Home, Clan or it's own faction? Some of us are under the impression that Home Guards are not necessarily CoT-affiliated but nonetheless at the same time bear no love for Omni-Tek. What's the deal?

10.) The Aquaan Queen is often seen swimming in the lake outside Newland. We also see an Aquaan Vicar (a religious figure) what are they doing visiting this little pond? What's the backstory on this species and where is their main base of operations?

Many of us are starved for information that let's us play intelligently within the game when it comes to putting our characters inside the 'real' world.

Anybody else got any reasonable questions that would help your character to properly play against the story. Or simply things you'd like to know about the world you play in?

4.) NO. Absolutely not. I talked to a GM about the nature of several creatures earlier, specifically the Eremites and the Bileswarms. I have seen the Eremite Altar in Deep Artery Valley and I asked about it. I was told that it predates omni-tek and that the eremites appear to be indigenous life forms. As for the bileswarms, they are indigenous life that has been warped by the notum to reflect the barren state of the lands they are found in. (EFP, SFH...etc..)
Just like in real life, facts and rumours are often confused with each other. And while a lot of Rubi-Kans probably want to find traces of alien life on their planet, the simple truth is that there is none.

Let me repeat that:

Humanity has found no evidence of alien life anywhere in the galaxy. Ever. Period. Maybe there's been the odd single-cell organism or traces of organic compound embedded in stone dating back millions, billions, of years. But nothing more than that. Humanity is alone - if not in the universe, then at least in this galaxy.

There are rumours. Of course there are. Some may claim that one lifeform or another has alien genomes. Or that certain strange creatures predate Omni-Tek's arrival on Rubi-Ka. Or that grand structures have been found, dating back tens of thousands of years, pointing to a hidden, and long vanished, alien civilisation on the planet.

They are completely and utterly wrong.

Scientists still hope to find something, anything out there. But Omni-Tek, and other corporations, have mapped the galaxy, and walked on thousands of planets, and still there is nothing. Nada. Zilch. Zip.

Still, simple facts - especially when those facts point to an absence of something - never stopped people from spreading rumours and lies, never stopped people from dreaming or wondering or cooking up elaborate conspiracy theories.

So go ahead. Knock yourself out. Conspiracy theories are always appreciated, and, hey, it's not as if Omni-Tek has never covered up the truth before...right? Right?

The truth is out there. Yes.

-Ragnar
