Trade Skills and Social Clothing...
Some items of clothing give minor adjustments +5 Cold Ac on a swimsuit for instance.
Would it be better to have a tailor like trade skill where other social clothes could be given adjustments.
Ofcourse these would be minor and in no way better than armor for Ac, depending on the difficulty, but perhaps they work very much like implants with nano Clusters, Where as a Ql 100 NanoFreak hood could give +70 to Matter creation, +35 Nano Pool and +12 Sense for example. This added ability to exxectively increase skills over AC maybe more beneficial and would allow people to customise what they where better and not feel ill prepared for social reasons.
Just an idea, Perhaps having them similar to Implants is perhaps too great but have unique abilities affixed to them according to what skills used in their creation
Quantem FT to create Flying cloaks, or Pharma tech to add regeneration and other skills to improve exp Bonus etc
