Meta-Con 4. Januari 2003 (RK2)
Hi All,

Bit of a cross-post but since Meta-Con is partly about getting suggestions from the MP Community to the Community Team, it's not entirely out of place here...

The next Meta-Con will be on the 4th January 2003. The Meta Con is a discussion forum for MP issues and ideas that will be fed back to FunCom via the MP Professionals to the Community Team. It will take place
  • * in the Reet's Retreat Fireplace Room (first door on the left after entering ground floor)
    * on Rimor (RK2)
    * at 21:00 GMT

Agenda so far:
  • 1) Heal/Buffing outside level range in Notum Wars

    2) Luxurious Rubber Suit: How useful are the benefits given the difficulty of getting it?

    3) Ams buff and Root breaker improvements.

The lovely and glamorous PsiRaven will be moderating.
