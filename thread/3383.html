I want my chainsaw.
Ok, so I've been sitting and thinking (I do that alot) about enforcers and adventurers and about their selection of weapons. the fact is, they stink. Maybe not statistic wise, but technological advancemen wise. Sure, you can tell me about composite materials, synth labs which produce edges a single molecule thick, and I'll tell you - so? The fact is, by the year 29745 we've been able to figure out how to create insane laser, rail, projectile, and so on weapons, we've been able to figure out how to get microscopic nannites to explode into a ball of flame, but we haven't managed to attatch a 2 hp engine to a chain to create a few rotating blades.

I mean come on, huskavarna, dewalt, craftsman, black and Decker have been doing this for years, by 29745 this technology is lost? How hard is it to get a rotating blade around here? Atroxes apparently don't mind dismantling buildings to use the support beams, but they didn't use a jackhammer to break the bedrock, or a rivet gun to attach the beams. Come on folks, this is relatively low tech stuff by todays standards. The closest thing to a technological advancement is a stun baton, and that has been used since the mid 20th century by farmers for hearding their cattle.

Perhaps funcom doesn't think that these weapons would be actually used. Tell that to Ash from Evil Dead 3: Army of Darkness, or Leatherface from Texas Chainsaw Massacre. Trust me, if all you had was an old millwuake drill you'd use that as a weapon if you could.

Oh I just though, is it an extension cord problem?

It's an, uh, "deninition" problem. The chainsaw does exist in the world of rubi-ka, as well as a few other lethal construction power tools. However, for reasons unknown to me, they ended up in the hands of the soldiers rather than the enforcers. Your chainsaw does exist, but it is an "assault rifle" with really poor range. Oh, and that rivet gun? It's called a bolter on Rubi-ka, and it too has been modified into a soldier weapon using assault rifle skill.

If you are looking for high-tek enforcer weapons, you may be interested in the next patch. Rubi-ka's inahbitants still can't figure out a way to attach a traditional bladed chain to their melee weapons, but apparently someone is in the process of creating a way to add mass-relocation bots to certain low-tech weapons. You might be pleasantly surprised in the next patch. (Or maybe unpleasently surprised. Can't really judge a book by it's cover.)
