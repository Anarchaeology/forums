Message Board Change: Read Receipts for FunCom
Hey - here's somethin I would like to have here. I want to have an option when I create a post or submit a reply, I would like to be able to get a generated email message letting me know when a funcom employee has read my post on the message board. I for one would like to know that you guys are actually looking at what people are saying. This should be something simple to put into the message board. Just have another checkbox down at the bottom of a submit post form that allows us to get read receipts from funcom. That would be pretty sweet!

All those in favor, please post a reply - maybe if we get enough replies here we could get something cool cooked up. Wouldn't you like to know that funcom people are actually seeing what you're writing?
