I think you've got that backwards.

I think Funcom listens to too many players. The fact is that a very large percentage of the changes they have implented were player suggestions. For example, the nerf of the FA pistols was completely player requested. The nerf of special attack damage was also completely player driven. You specifically may not have asked for the change, but the people on these boards sure did ask.

That is the problem. The people who post on this board are only a very small percentage of the people that play. For some reason, Funcom takes their opinion as representative of the entire player base. The problem with that is that happy people don't complain and ask for changes. Yet, Funcom is reacting to the negative comments of these forums in an effort to be seen as player-friendly.

What they end up doing is breaking business rules to make people less unhappy. Breaking rules of conduct only affects a very small number of players. Breaking a business rule, that drives game code, affects the entire player base.
