PigLick
Leetas
I know I may get some flak for this, but the people arguing that this change is going to hurt us in solo play really have no basis. The *team* heal shouldn't be usable for soloing, that's why it's a *team* heal. Also, as Nakomis said, the trader isn't supposed to benefit from the team heal, that's the whole point of the nano...he trades his health for the health of his teammates. If he trades his health but also gets healed, then it's not much of a trade-off is it? At that point, it's simply a heal, and I agree that traders shouldn't be better at simple healing than docs.

I'm pretty sure that neither the trader team heals or single-target heals used to work on the trader himself (which makes sense). In fact, I'm pretty sure no drain-type nanos were self-castable. You used to get a "You can't drain yourself!" (or something along those lines) message when you tried. Now, however, you can...heals, deprives/ransacks, etc. are all self-castable. Somewhere along the line they were changed so that they did affect you (possibly by mistake?) and now everyone's gotten so used to it that it seems that's how they should be.
