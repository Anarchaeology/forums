It is a bug and not just a visual one. Ask any agent who uses a disaffiliation rifle or similar weapon with a low clip capacity. If you do not manually reload, by dragging ammunition to your rifle after each battle, there is a very good chance that your clip will be empty. If you try to fire off a aimed shot, or I am assuming any special, you lose out on that special attack while the gun then auto reloads itself. The special is gone, the icon is grayed out and you have to wait for it to refresh before using it again with no benefit from it.

And it is a big deal, again for us who use a low clip weapon, as opening your inventory and dragging ammo to your weapon after each and every fight becomes quite tedious. Simply hitting R or some other key would be very much appreciated.

Anyway, since Cosmik understands our concern and seems to like the suggestion, no need for me to continue to defend the idea to those who do not understand the problem. I just wanted to clarify a few points and bring the suggestion up out of the pile, lest it be forgotten.

ok... cloudeh... The guns auto-reload while in combat. YES YOU ARE RIGHT.... if you kill the mob however with your last bullet in the clip of the gun will not use the autoreload feature until the next battle. So for people using a gun with a 5 bullet clip this means 20% of the time we will start the next battle with an empty clip and reloading. I can say this at least is true of all rifles. Now upon starting the next fight with a rifle... often that will be an aimed shot. There is an aimed shot bug that if you use your special with an empty clip (i.e. your last fight used the last bullet) you will waste your special and have to wait for it to recycle and all you get for using it is a chat line reading "reloading". In the mean time , the mob has aggroed you and you get to just wait for reloading while you are taking damage.

So basically there is a great demand for this feature.

I always mention things like this while in teams anymore, because it seems that most people are confused or don't understand about how frustrating and difficult it has become to play an agent. They see a nice aimed shot and think we are the scorge of AO... they don't notice how we spent half a fight trying to move into a position to get our Aimed Shot off which landed for 2/3rds of what we can crit for. They also don't see how we died in many missions trying to sneak for the uber item that is so high above us in level. They also don't realize how slow our guns fire, to make up for the sometimes seemingly high damage they do (you can relate to this with your hammer which is higher damage average and slower).

If I don't reload when I finish a fight with an empty clip then I ruin my next aimed shot. So I can either not use it or waste it or reload. I waste about 1/3 of my aimed shots per day... and I reload the rest of the time. Seems like such a waste and something so easy to fix.
