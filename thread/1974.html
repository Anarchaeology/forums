How about fixing the existing weapon?
Need to fix the grenade launcher.
Please adjust the Pistol, Assault Rifle and Grenade requirement for them as they are too high compared to the weapon's power.
I dont mind slow firing rate and the the glacial reload times. I really dont mind the outrageous prices of ammo. What I do mind however is very low damage ratio compared to the requirements to equip the weapon.
Without a doubt the Grenade launchers are currently broken. AoL, due to the IP constrains, does force some very cookie cutter approaches to support classes. Please adjust the requirement for the grenade launchers by about 15% down; I think it is pretty safe to assume that there will be no stampede to get into this new and improved weapons, because of its laughable 1 round magazine with very expensive ammo and pitifull range. This requirements reduction will make Grenade launchers just a viable, but still underpowered weapon.
Currently Grenade launcher is a toy of a weapon.
