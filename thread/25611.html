Why do they fight us?
Ok, it's late  That last clan fixer hit be harder than my mortal body could handle and my spirit is still in shock. I'm staring at the blue glow of the old faithful reclaim terminal in Harry's that's preserved my soul and reanimated my body so many times before. Again the question haunts my thoughts; why do they fight us still?

I am proud to be Omni-Tek through and though, but at first this was not the case. I began life a slave in the notum mines, a slave to Omni-Tek. There was an outcry and an uprising, we were not going to take oppression any longer. So we didn't.

There was struggle, conflict and much tension, but Omni-Tek yielded. They admitted their faults by abolishing slavery and forced labour. We were granted freedom.

So I lived in Newland for a while, as a neutral, but it wasn't the same. All the others spoke of was revenge. All they thought about was how to get back at our 'oppressors'. They formed blind pacts and swore their souls to war. They scared me. Who would want to harm those who had already paid their penance?

Freedom. That's what they wanted, and yet it's exactly the thing that they take from everyone by their very actions of vengeance. Omni-Tek is wholly not responsible for action taken against terrorists  dont force is to play our hand.

Many of the clans I have much respect for, I applaud their efforts. However, I cannot accept the fact that they stand next to those who would plot and scheme the eradication of all those who work for the benefit of a collection of people.

When will they see that the war is over? It has been for hundreds of years.. why do they still fight? Omni-Tek is no longer the monster it once was. Omni-Tek is not some evil all consuming foe. It is more a collection of vast interstellar resources dedicated to the benefit of all its employees, no matter how small.

We wouldn't need to fight if people just accepted the fact that Omni-Tek isn't out to get everyone. We aren't oppressing anyone, and if you say otherwise, I call you out to give me proof that we are. And don't quote "That Omni-Pol base over there is.." because if you read the Tir Accord, Omni-Pol is authorised to set up military forces wherever needed for the sake of global security. Not to plot the demise of your paranoid old grandma residing on the outskirts of Tir.

I fight for what is right, I fight for The LAW. I fight for the right to choose, and I fight for those who cannot. And let me tell you, the only ones who I seem to be fighting are rebels without a cause. Not a single clansman has given me a legitimate reason why he or she fights against Omni-Tek. So I call you out, rebels without causes, WHY DO YOU FIGHT US!?

[summons Taria to heal his wounds as he shoulder's his items from reclaim on his broad Atrox shoulders and slowly walks off home to his free and unoppressed friends and family in Omni-1]
