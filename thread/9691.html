on AoE effects
Area of Effect roots, nukes, snares, etc except mez's, will aggro everything they touch. They are all considered hostile nanos and therefore will be taken as an attack by the mob. Also, from what my NT friends tell me, AoE nukes and roots seem to cause more aggro than anything else in their arsenal. So be careful with your AoE roots, and use them when you're sure that all the mobs in the area can be dealt with or run from in case the root runs out or breaks.
