She nodded, and rested her head back against the cushions, closing her eyes. Then his words penetrated. Just in case? Her eyes popped open, she sat up quickly, but he was already upstairs.

She lay back down, and tried to think coherently, to logically assess what she knew about it. Its attacks had been harsh, but not brutal. It could have killed her, but it hadn't...

It had only attacked her when it felt cornered.

This should have made her feel better, but it didn't. Because she still wasn't certain what it was after. If it wanted information, or wanted help, why did it react so violently?

And why had it waited until Edwin had left, before showing itself?

She heard footsteps, and looking up, saw Edwin coming down the ramp, laden with bags. She had to laugh, it looked as if he'd packed everything but the kitchen sink. He was such a love. She wouldn't pursue the matter, not if meant unpleasantness for him. He handed her a bag, and she pulled out some clothing. Nobody else was around, so she quickly changed. Not that she really cared at that point - it really had been a long night.

She sighed at the thought of leaving their lovely Rome apartment. It was so convenient to the City, and so cozy. But.. they did have a lovely place up by the Stret River. And East Last Ditch was surprisingly cosmopolitan, in a subdued sort of way. And perhaps they might eventually return to Rome, take another place. She finished buttoning up her shirt, and picked up her bags. She smiled at him. She loved him so much. Everything would be all right, once they got to Stret.

"My love, I'm ready to leave when you are".
