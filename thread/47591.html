Anarchy Online Organizations Events Forums?
I'll make it short.

I've left CoA in the hands of Redruum's Player.

Why?

Simple, priority was to WAKE UP the storyline which has happened. It took quite a bit, and a LOT of heat from within the clans (those roleplaying for and against CoA)...and I thank you all.

As for my next Project...well, I'm DEEP in a lot of my clan's roleplaying events (which thanks to officers in my clan, they are too many to mention here)...however I believe a new way of communication should be available NOT just for Clan Leaders to use but other players (Omni includedl).

Why? (Deja vu)

Because we have not been able to make events in an ordely fashion as we used to do, too much BULLSH** going on, put 20 leaders in one room...and you know someone is going to frown and point fingers (I'm yet to see an exception, the main reason I avoid CCL Meetings, as other do).

This set of forums are only for CCL Members, what about the officers/advisors under you? there must be DOZENS of those players who want to help YOU create or run an event in your clan.

Keeping that in mind, I've created a new set of Forums, the "Anarchy Online Organizations Events Forums" .

Layout of Forums:

Anarchy Online General Forums
- Anarchy Online - General Forum
Any Rubi-Ka citizens are free to post here, be civil and keep it IC (in character).

- Anarchy Online - OOC Forum
Here all guest players can post OOC, so feel free to relax and talk about anything, from your video card to how your girlfriend has a better nano than you =P

Single Clan or Omni Only Events

- Background Settings Workshop
Here is where you can develop the in-character background story for your clan's event. You'll be able to get ideas, and suggestions from other Players to improve it.

- Pre-Event OOC Meetings
Here you can contact fellow Player to agree to time, date and place of meetings OOC through IRC, AIM, ICQ, in-game or any other form of LIVE communication to work on your event.

- Event Final Production
This is it! you got the background story set, the players ready to act and roleplay their parts, now all you need is this final stage. The FINAL Platn as well as any "last minute" changes, updates, etc. should be posted here.

Omni/Clan Crossover Events

- Background Settings Workshop
Here is where you can develop the in-character background story for the event which will include omni and clan organizations. You'll be able to get ideas, and suggestions from other Players to improve it.

- Pre-Event OOC Meetings
Here you can contact fellow Player to agree to time, date and place of meetings OOC through IRC, AIM, ICQ, in-game or any other form of LIVE communication to work on your event.

- Event Final Production
This is it! you got the background story set, the players ready to act and roleplay their parts, now all you need is this final stage. The FINAL Platn as well as any "last minute" changes, updates, etc. should be posted here.

Multi-Organizations Events

- Background Settings Workshop
Here is where you can develop the in-character background story for an event which will include several organizations, be it clan, omni or both. You'll be able to get ideas, and suggestions from other Players to improve it

- Pre-Event OOC Meetings
Here you can contact fellow Player to agree to time, date and place of meetings OOC through IRC, AIM, ICQ, in-game or any other form of LIVE communication to work on your event.

- Event Final Production
This is it! you got the background story set, the players ready to act and roleplay their parts, now all you need is this final stage. The FINAL Platn as well as any "last minute" changes, updates, etc. should be posted here.

Now you have two choices.

1) Use this as a tool, to run events without worrying about your organization not having a website (since these forums can help you exchange ideas, update people and send private messages to people you choose) or perhaps not having the time to update html pages or just you having a limited time to run your events...let those under your leadership HELP YOU!

2) Ignore I said anything, and continue running events the way you've been doing (if it's not broken, don't fix it right?).

3) Yeah...there was a third choice...BUY ME SOME WHISKEY AND VODKA AND GET DRUNK WITH ME IN ATHEN!

That's all folks!
