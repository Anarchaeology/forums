Re: Ph33r me?
quote:
Originally posted by Valerian
I only just started using the "Fear" line of nanos. What was the problem with them? I understand the need for a psy check as some of these crat nanos are quite powerful and could be used to fight much higher mobs and get insane xp (otherwise the resists of mobs would have to be upped and we dont want to see that )
What exactly was broken with "Fear"?
The reason both charm and fear were nerfed has nothing to do with their power. The problem is that the higher level nanos in the charm and fear line are only marginally better than their predecessors. Funcom therefore had two options if they didn't want high level characters doing well with very low level nanos (which was definitely happening), they could:

1) Give us incentive to get the higher nanos by enhancing the higher level nanos to make them more desirable as they have done with every other line of nanos in the game. This could have included greater range, greater duration or lower resistances.
or
2) Nerf them by providing a Psyche check on them so that they had a requirement in addition to nano resistance, unlike every other nano in the game. This had the added benefit of making it much more difficult for crats to be appealing to groups (since two of their lines wouldn't work against the level of MOBs that groups attack) and being more vulnerable to debuffs (since we can't go down to the next lower level of nanos in the line, like the other nano classes can).

We have asked a dozen or more times why they chose option two instead of option one and have been told, on two different occassions about three weeks apart, that they are checking on it.
