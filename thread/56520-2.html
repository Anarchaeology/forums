Here are a few multi-classing ideas:

1) when you pick a new class, you get to choose 2 or 3 skills that are cheap to the new profession and make those skills cheap for you. Ie, you could change 2-3 blue skills to green skills, based on the differences in skill cost between the 2 professions.

2) A nano lvl req that relies not on a level req on the nano but upon the lvl of the caster. This would only affect you if your first profession was not that of the nano you wanted to cast.

Assuming a ratio of something like 1:8, you need to wait until lvl 225 in order to cast ql 200 nanos. This works well because in order to cast anything useful to a lvl 200+ character, you would need to do some significant lvl'ing. Any worthwhile nano would need to be ql 100+, and so you'd need 12 or so lvls (no small feat given the xp curve) after 200 to cast anything decent.
And let's face it, nanos are the only thing seperating professions besides skill costs.

The same thing could work for weapons and armor, though perhaps on a more generous ratio, so that in order to use a ql 200 (or greater) weapon that is specific to your 2nd profession (something like a blood bat if you go enf), then you'd need 10 lvls of work in order to be able to use it, rather than an immdeiate payoff from choosing enf.

Anyway, just some thoughts, feel free to tell me why i'm an idiot

edit: a new PvP class would have to introduced, 200+ to make this fair at all.
