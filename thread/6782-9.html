And thats what I feel. Martial Arts... in its most simplest form, is the study of any skill that can be applied in warfare. It could be hand to hand combat, weapons, tactics, horseback riding/fighting, siege warfare, etc. There are many different schools, or forms, of Martial Arts. Some forms are more spiritual, some more physical. Each usually emphasizes the practice of such skills to perfection. Bojitsu, involves intensive training with the bo/long staff. Akido uses throws and joint locks to immobilize the enemy to prevent him from harming you, while Kendo is "the way of the sword". Even sumo is a form of martial arts It all depends on your own personal philosophy and what you believe in. So to say that Martial Arts is only suppose to use the fists (a single bright green skill), I think its wholely correct.

Point that I am trying to make is that weapons are in intergrated part of martial arts. If you dont want to use them, thats ok, but they are there as an option, and in my opinion, if you use them, you should be able to increase the damage you can do (not just as in IP cost justification, but a logical one too). I know that people like Jet Li can deal some major punishment with just his fists, feet, elbows, head, etc, but have them trained in a weapon, like a katana, and I feel he could do a lot more damage. If he has an equal skill with a sharp edged weapon, vs a not so strong fist, and it connects, whats going to hurt more?

I chose staffs for my martial artist, because I like the look, the feel, and the practicality of them. I could use just about any weapon like a stick, broom, pipe, and be just about as good, and much more versatile, then someone trained with a gun when they find themselves weaponless. But that does not mean I would force my way onto someone else. I do hit harder then a MA with a their fists, I dont get crit as much as my enforcer friend, I can run fast when not in my plane (they always say, its not how fast you can run from the sandworm, but who you can outrun).

Obviously, this is only my opinion, and I am not saying that my opinion is the only one, or the right one. I dont want to be jumped on by other people or accused of being too low a level

I do feel that like most other classes, martial artists should get some love, and funcom has promised that its coming. if it does, great. if not, then like life, I am going to do what ever I can to make the best out of it, and in some ways, thats a lot more rewarding.
