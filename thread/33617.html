Errr... Shouldn't that be 6pm Eastern? The Eastern time zone is on daylight savings (except most of Indiana) so it isn't EST but rather EDT.

EDT = GMT-4

So if it starts at 22:00 GMT, then it will be 6pm in New York.

I applaud GridStream's effort to make time zone conversion a priority by placing it on their front page, the "(This feature may not work yet)" after the "Using Daylight saving?" checkbox is true. This feature does not work on the Live Events section on the right of the page. Hence Thyme and DJ Rythum are both off by an hour.

But you're on the right track and you're doing better than Funcom. At any rate, better to err on too early than too late.
