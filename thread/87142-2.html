Bottom Line:

PvP and PvM are two aspects of the game. PvP is more enjoyed by a majority of the high level community, PvM is a hassle for good equipment and it's rare that someone 'likes' to do these things. The choice is completely yours, like it should be. However, gimping professions simply because of 'realism' disrupts the fun factors of the game. PvP and PvM are two product-promised aspects of the game, and whether you're and engi or a soldier you should be able to enjoy them as much as anyone else. The choice is eliminated for the bottom tier, and most of the previous tier.

So, what would happen? You already have a system like this, however there's less 'gimp professions.' Take two uber pvp professions for example, fixers and enforcers. Enforcer wants grid armor nerfed - fixer hates the fact enforcer has close to 600-700 more runspeed than his max, thus it should be 'nerfed.' So making a pvp system like this would bring your arses back to square one, the people who can enjoy pvp will pick the best professions for it and will prefer owning everyone as opposed to only a select few. There would just be less whine threads between uber pvp profs.

Case closed! There is no patch process for human nature, sorry folks!
