Well, after following this whole thread (yep, read the whole damn thing) heres what I'm seeing:

People are concerned that Traders will become far less valuable as the wrangles do not last long enough for them to be useful team members.

Agreed. I think that wrangles should last for at least 20, probably 30 minutes. Thats long enough for trader skills to still be highly valuable on missions, while short enough to prevent the whole crazy OE and +70ql Engi bot issues.



Many mid level (50-100) engineers are horrified that they won't be able to use mochams+wrangles for pets anymore.

So what? If you need a bot that much higher than your level, you dont have any play skills as an engineer anyways. You will still be able to use a MP or Trader buff from someone of equiv level w/o issues, assuming that you have good implants. With the abundance of extra IP (compared to most classes) that engis have theres no excuse for engis not having good implants. After lvl 30, any engi should have implants at least 20 ql over thier level, often as much as 50 ql. Since the implants are UNAFFECTED by the patch, do your homework and be happy with a great bot instead of an insane bot. I've checked. An engi w/ good implants can still use a equiv wrangle OR MP buff (+50-60 from 30-70, +90 from 75-110, +140 from 110 on) and not lose control of thier pet. Doing so will mean your pet will always be completely red to you.
My RL girlfriend plays a lvl 76 engineer, she gets her masteries and heads off to do missions. She can carefully, but almost always succesfully, solo 70-75% (ql 90-100) missions by using trimmers and precise, PRECISE bot control. Her pets will stay completely responsive after 14.2 when her masteries wear off. Don't tell me it can't be done, I watch her do it every day. Even with me having a higher ql atk pet that hits for more damage than hers, + a heal pet, + a occasionally useful mezz pet, + a better shotgun, I have more trouble soloing similar ql missions than she. Shes worked very hard to develop her bot control skills and its paying off. So, STOP WHINING and run some easy missions to you learn to control your weapon (bot).


Soldiers/enforcers wont be able to use such high ql weapons.

Using weapons at twice your ql is kinda silly. Added to this that you can still equip them carefully and get a decent bonus from them (be CAREFUL with the reqs vs sustainable skills) and you'll still be able to assault tough mobs. That does, however, require my limitations listed at the end of this post.

Some MPs are complaining.

Get a clue, MPs are so unaffected by this its not even funny. I'm playing a lvl 77 one right now, and this patch is irrelevenant.
(Aside from one of MY questions below)

PVP non soldier or enforcer players are VERY concerned about thier shotguns.

Yea, this is a toughy. I dont want to lose what I've just gotten used to. Been PVPing for less than a week, and am enjoying it. I think it needs to happen though, Docs and MPs w/ very high ql shotguns isnt really fair.


PVP players are very worried about ransack.

I think this is a legitimate fear. For weapon use it may be fair-ish, but for pets its horrendous. I think that ransacks should NOT affect pet response. After all, in PVP, even the pet classes rely on nukes and/or weapons, both of which would still be affected. And traders already have a way to deal with pets (root). In MP vrs Engi, the unmakes are pretty much useless against engis anyways, and in MP vs MP, its all about which pet attacks what.

Some questions I have:

What about scopes? I expect that they will be affected, and we're going to see a LOT fewer shotgun crits.

If thats the case, all MPs will be in SERIOUS trouble.

Conditions for accecptance:

FC needs to ASSURE the player base of some things before going forward with this patch.

1. Mobs WILL have thier HP reduced to something reasonable. NPC mobs have absolutely innane HPs. This ties into the soldier/enforcer problem to a great degree. You can NOT lower thier damage and not lower MOB HP. It's so unfair it borders on the immoral.

2. Nanite Resistance DOES work, and DOES proportionally affect your chance to prevent a debuff. If not, you can delete my accounts right now. I really like the game, but if you give the (still chaincasting on test) mobs a way to **** all my abilities w/o allowing me to do anything about it, why should I waste my time with it?

3. IP reset and skill stability. Both I and some other players have seen thier skills drop by a point here and there of late. One of my clan members lost a point in EVERY ability. Luckily he had screenshots and got them back. You need to fix this before the IP reset goes in. You also need to make SURE that the IP reset works. Period. Always. No exceptions. You had also better have a way to reimburse IP and the Redistribution points when it dosen't.

4. Economy. It s gonna be really bad out there when the patch hits. Everyone's stuff will have quit working to some degree or another. Docs are gonna be charging a mil per +80 treatment and still have 100 ppl waiting. Enforcers will be hounded to insanity. Etc... I think it might be appropiate to have either a large staff of ARKs on hand who can buff players (after all, why not? If it lets them OE a ql200 beam, it should be a "fair" in effectivness ql200 beam) or to provide players with some amount of credits to balance out the hundreds of thousands even mid (50-100) level players will HAVE to spend in order to get just thier implants back in. Personally, I think using the ARKs and the petition system is the way to go. It would be very nice if I could get in, confirm my math, and /petition need an IC, and essence, and a feline grace to put my implants back in, wait around for a few hours, then get a tell asking me if im ready and get the buffs.

Would it be abused? Sure, would it prevent a LOT of bad blood between both the players and FC and the players amongst themselves? Absolutely. Please, think about doing this.

How I would fix AO
Have no idea if it is possible from a programming standpoint, but I think the following would vastly improve the game. Comments, gripes, suggestions welcome.

1. Get rid of title caps and ip limits. Allow the player to spend as much ip as he wants in a given skill.

2. Change implants. Allow 1 implant slot available per 100pts of body developement, the first slot being free. The player decides which slot. Implant requirements would be based on the skills boosted by the clusters. Remove any and all body developement buffs and items that boost body developement. Implants would be quality neutral with only the cluster quality having any significance.

3. Implement the over equiping penalties as planned. Maybe even lower grace %.

4. Remove one of the inits (melee or physic). These should be combined.

5. Remove "fist" damage and attacks. Give martial artists a "style" weapon that they equip like an ordinary weapon that represents their ma attack. These weapons would be susceptible to duel weild requirements.

6. Adjust mobs as needed.
