As a trader of just a few levels higher than you, I say "go for it". Everyone once and a while I'm in town and someone asks for or buys a wrangle and that is fine. Unfortunately, it is not fine that I am constantly responding to messages or going "afk" in missions just to keep people off my back. We can't currently live off trades alone, so making money off of wrangle helps, but isn't the "whole game" (we still need to get xp - and people interfere with that).

The only thing that wrangling does is make people over-powered, which in turn makes Funcom "turn up the power" on the NPCs. This has a HUGE adverse affect on the casual gamer, as anyone who DOES NOT over-equip will tell you (read - uses implants about 30 levels higher and self-buff - casual over-equipping).

I wouldn't be fighting lvl 80 mobs with 5k hitpoints and 3k AC if not for Funcom compensating for over-equipping (remember that - Funcom made the mobs tougher because lvl 100 players were saying things were too easy - Well, they were wielding QL 200 weapons and armor).

So, we'll lose a cash cow of a nano - the skill wrangler. Maybe they'll give us something to compensate such as a team +3, +6, and +9 crit increases (remember that skill wrangling does increase your crit chances when used in battles already as it increases the target weapon skills above the defense skills of the mob - THIS WAS THE INTENTION OF THE NANO, TO INCREASE COMBAT POTENTIAL). Maybe they could give us a "baton of authority"-type nano (let's call it 'tough bargainer' or something) which increases the QL of loot by +5, +10, and +15 percent (so you'd get QL 90 loot and a little more cash from a QL 80ish mob).

Think about it. This is by no means a flame, but just another way of thinking about the problem. You say that killing over-equipping will kill traders. I don't believe that. If they do it right, killing over-equipping will only kill over-equipping twinks.

To elaborate on the potential role of the trader during a team battle:

Trader can health funnel a mob then;
transfer that health to the team via a health payment.
Trader can steal AC from the mob then;
transfer that AC to the team via an AC distribution.
Trader can plunder a mob then;
transfer that skill to the team via a wrangler.

This concept, the original design of a trader, makes sense (at least to me :P). The trader not only trades good, but also abilities.

While Funcom claimed the game to 110% complete, they did little in the way of game balancing and didn't figure that the combat wrangles would turn into ways for lvl 75 characters to equip lvl 200 gear.

I keep hearing traders saying "I can't find a group because I suxxors". Maybe I'm nuts, but I think this, in addition to distracts and roots (yes, charms are worthless) makes the trader a very useful member of a team!

Also bear in mind that people will *still* over-equip while teamed with a trader, so it only makes this profession more worthwhile and wanted.

I see us a winning, any way you slice it, with the removal of over-equipping.
