Boci's House of Sammiches
To hell with PvP! Engineers can wait! What we really need is Boci's Sammiches! Yes you hear me our very own "Boci's House of Sammiches" located in Omni-Entertainment. Clearly a priority above all else.

For years Brian "Boci" Fornataro has been perfecting his many recipes of Sammiches and is a clear winner in the taste factor. Not even the greatest minds in "Mongo Meal" or "Bronto Burger" can match.

Mr. Fornataro provides three excellent styles of Sammiches consisting of Original with Beef, Chicken, or Veggie (Veggie for those leet lovers out there!). He also provides three shakes that are to die for again and again. Those wonderful flavors are Vanilla Lovin, Roco Choco, and Tooty Fruity. Of course all of his Sammiches come with choice of Salt Free Potato Chips (Salt is icky!) and Fries of course.

It is a clear choice for the next installment on Rubi-Ka. I call you fellow citizens to rally with me so all can experience the fine tase of Boci!
