“A simple Question”
by Randa "Poetica" Whites

Lying in bed
With his arms around me
Tired limbs
Pulse still racing
He asked me a simple question
Should be easy enough to answer
If I dared to be true
“What am I to you?”
This was his simple question
Should be easy enough to answer
If I dared to be true
Time stopped for a bit
Possible answers raced trough my mind
I picked a safe one
It was partially true
“You don’t have to worry…
I’m not considering this a rebound affair”
I didn’t dare to be true
Because the truth is he meant way more
And what if I was to tell him
“I didn’t have to think twice…
Because I knew in my heart,
That if I didn’t let this happen,
I’d regret it
Maybe for the rest of my life”
He asked me a simple question
If I dared to be true…
But I didn’t…
I changed my mind:
I’m going to tell him now…
