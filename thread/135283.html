An Unusual Conversation
Greetings all!

I am a doctor with the Healing Hands here in Borealis. I had the most unusual conversation with another doctor this evening and I thought that I would pass it along. I was doing a little shopping at Fair Trade in Borealis when I turned to see a young nanomage doctor dressed all in black named Cyrongor. I had never seen him before. Without introduction, he began speaking to me. The following is a transcript of that conversation...



Cyrongor: Tis a shame we share the same profession yet with opposite goals.

Xarno: What do you mean?

Cyrongor: You live to heal others. I am here to release one's soul from the body ... permanent death.

Xarno: You disagree with the use of the reclaim terminals?

Cyrongor: Indeed I do.

Xarno: Permanent death will come eventually - why not use the terminals till then?

Cyrongor: I, among others, believe the soul is imprisoned because of these terminals.

Xarno: Who are these others?

Cyrongor: The few I trust who wish their names to be kept unknown. One day your soul shall be set free too...

Xarno: Then we will have to disagree.



And with that, he walked out.

Now, while normally I would chalk up such behavior to too much notum in the bloodstream, there was something almost ... sinister about it. As a doctor, I am of course concerned with any threat, real or potential, to the reclaim system. I know that the reclaim insurance logs have been sabotaged before. I might be over-reacting, but has anyone else been approached in this manner? Is this "organization" the product of somone's fevered imagination, or is there more to it? Is there a real danger to the reclaim system?

I am concerned.

Respectfully,

Xarno

Our souls ARE free!
Dr. Cyrongor -

What you are advocating is simply unconcionable. The cell scanners are a remarkable, wondrous invention. Think of the unique position that Rubi-Ka holds in the universe. The Solitus, Nanomage, Opifex and Atrox affinity for notum. The ability of nanobots to exist outside of ourselves. The ease and comfort the terminals give to those in the most dangerous professions: the rangers who explore the wilds, the guards who protect our cities, the scientists who research new nano-formulations. All of these and more rely on the terminals.

Also, think of the incredible philosophical and theological ramifications the mere existence of the terminals has already had. For the first time in history, we have incontrovertible, scientific proof of the life-force or "soul"! What was once a source of philosophical conjecture is now an established fact. This soul does not flee but returns to the reconstructed body. No where else in the universe but here does that happen. Perhaps YOU are mistaken. Perhaps the soul cries out to RETURN to the body and can only do that here. The implications of this have just begun to be explored.

Our souls will be "free" in their own time and need no assistance from you , sir.
