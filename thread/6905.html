Here's my opinion why
I posted this in another thread on the agent board about someone asking the same questions. Just my (and many others) general outlook on the imbalance.

Any given day you can go to 2ho and see tons of lvl 150+ Apprentice clanners, while High lvl omni pvp'ers are more rare. I have a theory shared by many on this issue:

- Omni players in my experience and from talking to others (on average) are older, almost everyone I know on AO, all my omni hunting friends, have families, kids, wives, etc. Also Omni is a structured corporation with strict rules, which is something Older people can relate to, stability in sacfirice for some personal liberties. This is something that younger players don't like, they want to be bad-ass rebel players who hate structure (generally). Most of the older omni players have to work 8 hours a day and usually have lots to deal with, so they don't like wasting their free time overtwinking constantly and shouting "i 0wnz j00" over the corpse of another player, they play to enjoy the social atmosphere and hunt with friends.

- Clanners from what I can tell (on average) are younger players maybe 12-18 or so, who are looking to do what most teenage players on MMORPGs do - PVP. Running around spending 8 hours a day online twinking out their characters just to kill everyone they see and spout childish insults at them or brag to fellow teenage friends about how many "n00b suckers" they "r0xx0red" tonight.


This is speculation backed up by a fair bit of reasearch and polling on player's ages and by viewing their maturity level in-game. No offense to either side, Im sure there are tons of middle-aged clanners with stedy jobs, wo just play for fun, and tons of omni kiddie grifers, but the majority i think, are as I outlined for both sides. This is why you see more clanner pvpers and more rich omni (older people with jobs can affoard to spend money on ebay creds a lot, trust me).
