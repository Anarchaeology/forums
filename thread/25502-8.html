Heads up!

I posted this in a thread of it's own to make sure it gets read as well because, I'm sure you will find, it is definately worth reading.

Straight from the mouth of Andrew "Blackmane" Griffin, nano designer -

""I've been reading the posts about what people think of the upcoming change and know that the 15sec rule is a pretty brutal change. So what is going to be done is this: the 15 second rule will stay, but rather than being for the entire line of team health transfers, it will just be for each individual nano. This means that if you execute Premium Delayed Health Payment, you won't be able to execute that particular nano again until the 15 seconds
are up, but you will be able to execute Exceptional Delayed Health Payment during that time. And then you could execute Sophisticated Delayed HP, while waiting out the DoT
for both Premium and Exceptional DHP, etc.

This will mean that the trader will have some team health transfer capability during this 15 second DoT period, but it will become rather dangerous because the DoTs will be
inflicting their damage. Having 2 or 3 DoTs on you at the same time will take some juggling skills on the part of the trader.""

So, the 15 second DOT lock will stay for reasons outlined here, but this change is a really good improvement over the previous planned change, I'm sure you'll find and agree. Comments? Keep 'em coming.

Re: Traders and Team Heals
quote:
Originally posted by Cosmik
Hey everyone - new information straight from my hot hands. Blackmane, nano designer, has written a small essay on team heals. It discusses why we are making changes to the Trader team heals and covers other commonly asked questions that have arisen in the last few days in reply to the team heal change announcement. It should answer any questions you have (of course, keep asking if there are any).

Onto the summary -

""Trader Heals

A very brief comment about the role of the trader.

The Trader in AO is not just a tradeskill character, but a trader in skills. The trader’s nano lines are set up so they can trade their skills to other members in their team, and make those people more effective. Or, they can drain skills from an enemy and make them less effective. Thus the trader is able to move around skills, armor class, health and nano points. But, the trader always pays a price for trading something to a friend. Now, on with the explanation about heals.


First, in regards to where the trader is supposed to be in the ranking of which professions are the best healers. The trader is meant to be after the doctor. The trader is meant to be after the adventurer. The trader is meant to be, very generally, in the same ballpark as the martial artist (at least when it comes to team healing). However, because of two problems with the implementation of the trader heals, they ended up being much more effective than they were intended. These two problems were that the introduction of the PvP 50% damage rule also affected the amount of damage the trader took when using a heal, and also that the trader was able to ignore the penalty when using their team heals.

The PvP 50% damage rule meant that the damage inflicted by executing the single target heals was cut in half, and the damage inflicted by the damage-over-time nano associated with the team heal was also cut in half. For the single-target heals this isn’t a large difference, around 115 points with the top level single-target heal. With the team heal, the difference is much larger, being around 670-odd points with the top level team-heal nano. However, it is the second problem associated with the team heals that causes the real problem, even if the 50% damage reduction never occurred.

The problem is that traders are able to completely ignore the negative effects of using their team heal nanos.

When a trader uses a team heal nano, a damage-over-time nano is placed on the trader. Currently, however, the trader is able to simply execute the team heal nano again and the existing damage-over-time nano will be removed and replaced with a new version. This can be repeated over and over again, allowing the trader to team heal for large amounts and suffer little real penalty.

The reason that the trader’s team heals were made as good as they are in terms of the amount that they heal and the low variance in the minimum and maximum healed, was because of this penalty. When using a team heal, the trader was meant to get the raw end of the deal. For example, the top level trader team heal, Premium Delayed Health Payment, heals 920-1144 points, but the trader is meant to lose 1352 points of health when using it. By being able to overwrite the damage-over-time nano, the trader was able to gain the benefits of the large team heal, without losing the health they should be losing.



We have been looking at fixing the problems with the trader’s heal nanos for a while, and are now in a position where we can ‘fix’ them. It is an odd coincidence that this occurred at around the same time as there were a number of long threads comparing the effectiveness of the trader’s heals compared to the doctor’s. I have explained above why we are fixing them, I will now explain how we are fixing them.

There are 2 changes being made to the trader’s heal nanos.

The first is that the damage inflicted upon the trader when using these nanos will be increased to the level it was before the 50% PvP damage reduction rule was introduced.

The second is that, for team heals, the trader will be unable to execute another team heal until the current damage-over-time nano has expired and inflicted all of its damage. Because we aren’t complete sadists, the duration of this damage-over-time nano will be reduced from 65 seconds to around 15 seconds. The damage inflicted, however, will remain the same. We are not going to rewrite the nano system to allow multiple copies of the same damage-over-time nano to run at the same time. During this 15 seconds, the trader can still heal others with their single-target heals.

Why not inflict the damage from the team heal as a single hit rather than a damage-over-time? That has been considered. The reason why the team heals got a damage-over-time was so that the trader had a chance of avoiding killing themselves instantly through using it. Because the damage would be applied over a period of time, the trader could then use a first aid kit, a health drain, be healed by someone else, etc to offset this loss of health. If it was done in a single hit, this could not be done. Inflicting the damage in a single hit could possibly kill the trader in a single hit, which is not something that we really wanted to do. The damage inflicted on the traders by the single-target heals is low enough that they did not warrant the same sort of treatment, so the damage from single-target heals is inflicted in one blow.


Something that we are considering for the future is that we may implement a second or even third line of team heals for the trader so they could cast another team heal during the damage-over-time period of the normal one. This would, of course, end up stacking another damage-over-time with the normal one - a very lethal choice for the trader to make. But, that is not something that will be done anytime soon.""

Thanks for the discussion thus far everyone. I hope this sheds some light on what is happening and future possibilities.

Green - Chemistry, Electrical Engineering, Pharmocological Technology, Psycology, Tutoring, Weapon Smithing,

Lt. Green - Adventuring, Swimming, Shotgun, Mechanical Engineering, Nano Bot Programming, Quantum Force Field Technology, Computer literacy, Perception, Map Navigation, Water Vehicle, Ground Vehicle, Air Vehicle,
