The Reethead Megabash! This Sat, March 23!
The last two nights at Reets have had such a great turnout that I have little option but to do it again! As announced on my show last night, we're going to have the mother of all parties this Saturday.

The Event: The Reethead Glass Floor Megabash!!

The Date: Saturday, March 23

The Time: 9pm EST (2am GMT)

The Location: Reet's Retreat on RK-1 (Smack in the middle of Stret West Bank), upstairs on the glass dance floor (that is, until it breaks)

I'll be DJ'ing there all night using a live-stream broadcast over winamp and playing nothing but hardcore industrial, techno, and metal from 9pm EST until whenever I need to pass out, and I give my personal garauntee that you'll pass out first! (hehe) As usual, I'll be doing plenty of commentary, watching the crowd, and taking requests!

The winamp address will be broadcast from within Reet's, so you have to show up to listen in!

I know all the loyal Reetheads will make it (especially since most of them already heard about it from last night's show), and I hope we get a lot of newcomers and first-timers, too!

If you have yet to see a Reethead bash, or if you've only been to one or two, then you should be notified that this will be the MOTHER of all Reethead bashes, and you can't afford to miss out!

We're gonna pack Reet's Retreat wall-to-wall and start bouncing and thrashing to the most hard-edged techno, industrial, and metal tunes out there, and we'll soon see just how strong that glass dance floor really is!

HOOT HOOT!
