Isn't it the protocol funcom's using that's the problem here, ultimately? From what I understand, they're using regular tcp packets, meaning there's error correction, crc, resending of packets, etc, going on, which creates a lot more processing load on the server... at least, I would assume that standard packets are more demanding than streaming UDP packets, which basically is more of a "get there if you get there" mentality...

At least, that's the way I understand it... I hate to say this, but look at Everquest... 3 years old now? And you can be in the midst of 100 people and only experience the rendering lag on your system's side of things...

Perhaps Funcom needs to take a look at the way other people are doing things. I know that even when there's 30 people in the same area in AO, things start lagging (and not hardware/rendering... I have the horsepower)...

New games *should* be a step forward, not 2 steps backwards.
