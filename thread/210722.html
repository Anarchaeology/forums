RUR Anniversary Celebration this Sunday
On the 15th of December, somehow or other, RUR will have managed to stay in business a whole year. To celebrate the occasion this Sunday, starting around 7 PM GMT, a few of us are going on a little pub crawl across Rubi-Ka. There will be a few treats, games and other activities to keep us amused along the way.

At the risk of sounding a little smug, let me just say what a magical year its been for us at RUR. Ever since our very first story was played out - Crattyjack's tale about a runaway, lingerie clad robot known as 'the Milly'  it was clear to us that RUR was going to be something special.

Mixing a bit of Asimov with a bit of Futurama, we brought to life stories of Luddite revolts, fiendish cyborg plots and bots gone bad. We organised events as diverse as the Science Symposium, the robot 'dude ranch' and multi-faction cyborg raid christened 'Operation Bash A Borg'. Our efforts on the neutral token board story were eventually rewarded by having RURs name mentioned in the description of the Experimental Cyborg Token Board when it was finally placed in game.

Of course we did not do all this on our own. From the very start, RUR was conceived as an open and outward looking guild. Rather than play the role of 'problem solvers', we were going to be play 'problem creators'; irresponsible screw-ups constantly getting themselves into trouble, sort of like professional damsels in distress. Our distress would of course be the hook through which we hoped to involve roleplayers from rest of the community into our stories.

At the time we had no idea if this approach would work. But the response from the community has always been terrific and since then we've had the privilege of roleplaying side by side with players from all factions, new and established, guilded or un-guilded. Simply put, without the response we got from the rest of the roleplaying community RUR would not be here today.

So, on behalf of the whole of RUR, Id like extend our warmest thank you to everyone who took part or supported our roleplaying activities over the past year. And if on Sunday you spot us wandering about, please do come say 'hello' and maybe share some cake and champagne with us.

Best regards,

Dabblez
Director of Rubi-Ka Universal Robots (RUR)
