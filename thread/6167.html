Actually not all games follow the path you describe. EQ which is the most successful of all MMRPG's uses a similar formula to AO. It's even worse actually. By the time you reach 60 in EQ you are lucky to be able to solo creatures that give zero exp.

Games use this formula to encorage grouping at higher levels.

AO also has more leveling at a faster rate than most games. This means more upgrading of gear also. Usually a sudden inability to beat an equal level monster can be overcome by upgrading your gear... not always an easy thing to do.

The title caps on skills levels also makes the pre-50 and pre-100 levels very difficult as you can not upgrade weapons and nanos to keep up with the increasing difficulty of monsters.
