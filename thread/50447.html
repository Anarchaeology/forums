Suggestion just for fun
I had an idea I wanted to share, which may not be original, but im not going to page through all these posts.

This would be strictly for fun and would not change gameplay at all, unless that was added to the idea.

Add some fun morphs to other classes besides adventurers at all levels.

For example:

enforcers: attach a morph to the mongo line of nanos, some kind of large intimidating creature

soldiers: add a armored vehicle to one of their AC nano lines, this one could add AC and reduce runspeed, but would be mobile

agents: modify the holograms to allow agents to easily shift into the different objects without the hologram being a separate object They should undetectable in this form but unable to do anything else.

engineers: this one is obvious attach a robot or cyborg morph to one whole line of AC or armor nanos

metaphys: give MP's a morph to turn into a cloud to float along with their pet friends

fixers: attach a phasing morph to their grid line which causes them phase back and forth between visible and invisible

nano technicians: add a morph into a stream of nanobots or maybe add graphics to represent a layered force field around them when those nanos are used

traders: It would be great to see a size change occur based on trader deprive, ransack lines, the more skill transfered the larger the trader would get and the smaller the target would become.


bureaucrats: I like either a size change or change in appearance here to go along with speeches that motivate or demotivate

ma: add a morph to different animals associated with ma, crane, tiger, snake, etc. Or perhaps a big overhead graphics that appears when one of the special attacks go off
