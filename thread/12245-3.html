Oh but I think a complete game mechanic overhaul IS needed.

Let me just list off some things I've seen in the past week.

* A level ~160 team without a critbuff, unable to kill one single Ace Ruffian. Eventually gave up and deleted the mission.

* A certain Neophyte soldier very irritated at having to zone 4 times to kill one single green monster, because of a lack of critbuffage.

*A certain Novice agent very irate and complaining that his critbuff was broken because he shot 6 or 7 times and failed to crit, only to feel much better a few minutes later when he crit 6 or 7 times in a row.

*Everyone over level 150, with the exception of Mayleen and her Rider Executioners, using crit weapons.

What exactly does this illustrate? It illustrates that for the most part, crits are all that matter at high levels. Without crits and/or critbuffs, a vast proportion of the most effective players in the game cannot even function at the barest levels. In PvP there are only two ways to do enough damage to kill anyone for the vast majority of players, crits.. and the burst/FA specials, but mostly crits.

I wrote a thread about "Make MA critbuff self only" once too. I see now that this is a very bad idea. People would leave the game in droves if 90% of players ability to do damage vanished overnight. No, I think we all have to be weened off crits somehow. The fact that the vast majority of weapons in this game do so little base damage most people would laugh at the thought of someone using em certainly doesn't help. The fact that high level mosnters HPs/ACs were increased to the point where nothing but crits can even make them blink doesn't help (I'm sure before this change crits were just hammering them into the ground, but I guess that funcom decided the problem was with the monsters defense and not with the players offense).

Anyway, before they go about changing anything.. there's one thing I gotta agree with.

Implement a fair IP reclaim scheme!!
And keep it permanent so we can change our charachters a bit every once in a while if we get bored.
