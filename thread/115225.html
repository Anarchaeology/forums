Division 9 RSGE is a military / science org is a belonging to Omni-Tek. We started as a collective science department within the company. After the loss of numerous invaluable scientists to the mutants and other dangers of Rubi-Ka, they were assigned under the command of General Barzillai, who took the scientists and trained them as soldier-scientists to fight and survive out in the wilds. All members, including those who are transferring in only to be scientists, go through an application period, consisting of a "Boot Camp," where you will be trained and educated for combat, military discipline and procedure. After that, you have your choice of inter-org departments, from our RSGE Dept, which is the core scientific body, which I command, to the Special Forces, who live for combat. We also have several other dpeartments in between which handle differing responsibilities.

With the outbreak of the Notum Wars, I will be honest, the direction of our scientific pursuits has shifted towards military applications. We spend a good portion of our time securing Omni-Tek intrests. If you are interested, contact me sometime when I'm not out in the field and we'll talk. Also, follow the link in my signature to get an idea of our history and learn more about who we are.

OOC:

Div9 is a military/science/RP org. We fight. ALOT lately *chuckles* We are not uber, but not pure RP anymore. We don't force PvP (except home tower defense of course) or RP, but both of those are very much encouraged (as in most pure PvP folks tend to get turned off by the use of rank and command, salute, show up in uniform to meetings, etc.., while pure RP's get tired of the constant fighting the we've been getting ourselves in.) A healthy balance of both is required for someone to really have a good time with us.

Anyhow, I'd encourage you to look at the history laid out on the site, read and chat on our Matrix (although its being rebuilt after some hackers wasted it recently in a non AO related server attack) and give me a /tell sometime.
