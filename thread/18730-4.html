Team Wrangles
OK, I'm a lvl 39 trader, so this applies to lower level play, but here it is anyway...

I undertook a team mission last night with an Atrox Fixer, an Atrox Sol and a Solitus Sol. I am a Solitus trader. We cruised through the mission and then got to the boss MOB. Now, can you guess what was more useful in this situation: my team heals or my team wrangle? My team heals were the ONLY thing keeping this team alive, as the boss MOB ripped into the Atrox like they were Leets. It's no good doing a little extra damage when you can't survive long enough to take down what you're attacking. In addition, Traders often essentially gimp themselves by providing a support role in teams. Most of the time, we are focussing not on combat but on neutralizing adds, healing, ransacking/depriving, and then actually doing some damage. Casting team wrangle completely takes away the ability to provide support in any of these other arenas, which often mean the difference between life and death.

As another point, you mentioned that a team should have a doc first to heal, and then a crat to mez and some other class like adv to support heal as a solution to filling up the trader's roles(s) so that they can team wrangle. If you include a trader in that group, 4 spots are already taken up, leaving 2 for sols/enfs/engis, etc. Not THAT is a gimped group, which would do far less damage than a group with one trader, one doc, and 4 main combat classes, even if the other group had the team wrangle running. The point is, a trader is a do-it-all support class, and can take the place of more than one other class in terms of support nanos. That's why we're valuable.
