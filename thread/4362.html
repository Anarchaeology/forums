The ONLY solution to overequipping (and solution to economy)
Remove the whole system of ql based items.
Then make items first of all BONUS giving, where a good, special, rare item gives a bonus, addon or other.
Make damage be dependant on skills+bonus(es)=damage/hit frequency.
A new gun=top quality, and best bonus. The older/more used it is, the lower quality, and thus lower bonus. This forces players to try to buy new weapons, and maybe sell for cheap prices a old gun to players. (special bonus might be high quality which keeps quality of gun for longer)
With guns+skills attack sorted on this, can damage be determined by Armor bonus. Good ql armor or armor with bonus again means lower impact.
Example of all:
100 in pistol skill and +50 bonus pistol=150 in pistol, meaning 150 in attack bonus, total.
Target: 75 in "dodge ranged" bonus
Result: 75 in total attack.
Now, 75 could mean 75% chance to get hit for example.(just example)
Now giving that you hit, it becomes a formula like:
Weapons damage - targets armor bonus.
Lets say target got a +15 armor bonus, and gun do 1-50 damage (yes, dice roll system, for those who are familiar with roleplaying systems)
Damage-armor=hits taken.
Let armor too be "used": The more hits taken, the more used, and in the end its useless/destroyed. Nanos can add to bonuses, give shielding etc. As a addon to profession, could AO have "repairmen", who can repair broken items.
Voila.

How to still make a differ on persons:
Levels of characters give bonus to skills, as it is per today, with skillranks and bonuses.
