The Borealis Charity Event (RK1)
((OOC Last edited 23-09 17 GMT - Pratical inforamtion posted further down in this thread))

Our beloved city of Borealis, capital of the Neutral communityhas been neglected far to long by the Rubi-Ka public services.
The Borealis utilities urgently require maintenance.
The lack of high-tech tools hamper our technicians in their repair efforts. The research of the high-tech new tool called "Profiteers Helper" has been stalled further under minig the rebuilt efforts.

Therefore The Independant Rubikans are organising charity to raise money to improve the public services of Borealis and to fund further research in the " Profiteers Helper"

On Friday 26 September at 22h00 GMT or 6 pm EST.
Location: Borealis - BackYard 5.

Several Expert Craftsmen will be present to help the citizens of Borealis with the New Jobe technology.
The newly discovered Jobe cluster will be transformed into ready to use implants by the experts.

The Experts Craftsmen have agreed to donate a substantial part of their "tips" for the renovation of Borealis and the research of the "Profiteers Helper".

Currently following craftsmen have confirmed:

I would like to call out to all Neutrals that make Borealis their home to come and visit the charity event and donate some of their Hard earned cash for this noble cause.
Off course any Clan and Omni citizens are as always very welcome too.
HELP the neutral community repair Borealis and fund our research for the "Profiteers Helper" !!!

-->Give 1 implant at a time to a tradeskiller

-->Get your implants sorted beforehand according to tradeskill requirements please.

-->To start we are limiting MAX 3 IMPLANTS to the Main Tradeskillers per visit.
