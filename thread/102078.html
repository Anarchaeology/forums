Omni-Tek organization reveals itself as traitors: "Name Withheld" to be hunted
The office buildings of Omni-HQ tower above Omni-1, shimmering in red and orange steel as the sun goes down behind the city walls. The bustling crowd of bureaucrats and Omni-Admin officials hurrying from their working places to the next capital transportation ship to return to their families in Rome or other districts of Omni-1 is still moving like a swarm of ants at the foundation of the largest tower in Omni-HQ: the giant and shining skyscraper of Omni-Admin HQ on Rubi-Ka. Although the day never ends in Omni-1, many of the offices are not lit anymore. The week has just begun and work is going slow on Mondays. However, the top levels of the tower still has a lit window here and there, scattered over the topmost part of the highrise where only the most important and high-ranked Omni-Tek officials have their offices and meeting rooms. An entire section of the building naturally belongs to Omni-Pol, the police force of Omni-Tek in general and Rubi-Ka specifically. Only those who had the honor to see the building from inside know, that late in the evening, most rooms are closed for employees and nobody works longer than neccessary. Thus it is quite unusual to see that at least 5 rooms in the Omni-Pol section of the building are still occupied.

The rooms are labelled "Omni-Pol Administration". Large offices with all kinds of luxury and technical equipment. Just as an Omni-Pol General or even higher ranked police officers expect a working place to be in Omni-Tek. Polished leather couches which are soft, although they do not look like it. Computer operated terminals, busy with compiling data, searching evidence out of all the video tapes fed to them through the day, office androids hurrying from one terminal to the other to check the status of them and adjusting some settings if neccessary. In other rooms flowers of the local flora, nicely arranged to compliment the layout of the private offices of the Omni-Pol command staff. One of the largest ones, secured with heavily armed Office-Security Bodyguard droids, has a sign on the black notu-glass door, reading "Monty Capo, President". Inside, no terminals, no busy droids, no noise. Eerie silence in a room only filled with an enormous desk and 5 leather chairs, arranged around a large notum-crystal meeting table. Each one labelled with a name of the highest ranking officers of the Rubi-Kan Omni-Pol branch: "Anthony Waddles", "Roberto Kenley", "General Fahrni", "Nick Wyles" and "Natasha Sosnowski". Non of the chairs are currently occupied, but they seem to be well worn from frequent use, although the leather is unscathed and glistening slightly in the dimmed light of the notum lamp, shimmering on the large desk. The desk itself stands in front of a large dark blue-black notum crystal window, which spans the entire wall. Looking through it, one can faintly see the plaza below the Omni-Admin skyscraper. The crowd of bureaucrats and officials is gone. Only a few late librarians hurry across the streets far below, trying to catch the last few shuttles to Jobe. Suddenly a robotic voice can be heard, coming from the entrance of the office: "Sir... the evidence has been scanned and filed for your review. May I proceed?". "Permission granted. Submit the datalinks to my terminal, I will look over the files later.", says a menacing sounding male voice coming from behind the desk. "That is all. Dismissed!" says the same voice, clearly angered about something. Scanning the terminal for incoming submissions from the data center, Monty Capo, the head of the local Omni-Pol Division leans back in his chair and waits for the submission to finish. The names of the files revealing only the name of yet another criminal organization which was recently revealed within Omni-Teks ranks: "Name Withheld" with the added suffix used within Omni-Pols data transfers: ".evidence". A faint smile crosses the mouth of Mr. Capo as he opens the files to study their content. A thought command to the Notu-Comm Brain Link device attached to the right ear of Mr. Capo causes the terminal to open the file conveniently labelled "Namewithheld1.evidence". A video clip begins to flicker on the screen embedded in the desk, obviously taken by an Omni-Pol officer during a heated battle, showing a few humans, opifex and atrox, running around in a hilly landscape and shooting either each other or notum towers in the background. The logo at the control tower which also appears for a second during the clip, clearly marks it as Omni-Pol property. A few of the attacking humanoids have their organizational information shown during the video clip: "Name Withheld", an Omni-Tek organization of low profile and no file with Omni-Pol until then. The video clip ends, shortly after a guardian-type tower explodes in a large beam of light, accompanied by a shockwave of notum energy. Mr. Capo shaking his head in disbelief, sends another command to the terminal, which opens the second file: "Namewithheld2.evidence". Another video clip flickers over the display embedded in the desk, showing more of the combat scenes and among the Name Withheld employees, a few Omni-Pol officers, defending the towers which are under attack by the Omni-Tek traitors. Engineer robots and metaphysical summonings fighting for the aggressors. The screen goes black with the words "Omni-Pol Evidence" appearing in the middle in white corporate letters.

As the video ends, Mr. Capo smiles slightly and makes some notes in an Omni-Pol data file which looks like the standard file for execution warrants. The title of the file being "* Name Withheld". The data transmission of the warrant is done instantly and all Omni-Pol outposts throughout Rubi-Ka receive their new orders: "Omni-Tek traitors 'Name Withheld' to be executed". With one last command, Mr. Capo adds another name to the execution warrant... 'Lida 'Templus' Pierce', ex-Omni-Pol officer. Then the entire evidence, including the warrant is sent to the InternOps offices one section below the Omni-Pol offices of the Admin tower through the naturally highly secure transfer lines of all HQ buildings. From outside the Omni-Admin building, all windows are black. Today is over in the office district of Omni-1, but a few of Omni-Tek's employees have gone too far during the last days...

Omni-Pol protects!
