Hm, let's see what I can answer on the fly a Sunday afternoon.

Cyclic shields: The lowest level one is in the shops now, and the others will be made available through other means. No ETA yet, as most work is concentrated on Notum Wars right now.

Dualwielding: If anything has changed about that (except how it is displayed) in 14.6, it's a bug, not an intended change. Looking into that.

Top buffs and self-cast: To encourage cooperation with other professions to get high nano skills, and because there will be more buff items in the game. We'd rather add a full line than introduce it nano for nano as we add more buff items. And letting nanomages have a benefit there is hardly unfair, imo.

Fixinf Adventurer: I'll focus more on lobbying profession attention when the question of the Professionals' future is answered. I wanna know what I am working with before going too deep into profession issues.

Deleting the profession: Nobody would seriously consider that. While I guess this is a show of frustration, try keep things constructive.

That's what I could take from the top of my head. I'll continue my discussion with the Professionals, and get back to everybody on that next week.

Dual wielding sequence was changed from:
1) Both Main/Off attack bars charge
2) Main attack
3) Main Recharge/Off Attack
4) Off recharge
5) Goto #2

To something like this:
1) Main attack
2) Off attack
3) Main/Off recharge
4) Goto #1

This may equate to a different time, depending on your weapon setup! If your on-hand weapon has a slow recharge compared to your off-hand, you will likely see a decrease in your hits due to this change. Before, the on-hand recharge was "eaten" by the off-hand attack, meaning as long as you matched on recharge vs. off attack, you would see a pretty optimal sequence. (Also, having a short recharge on the off-hand would help as well.)
