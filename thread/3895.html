Keeping characters on both sides - a great flaw
imho - the possibility to keep both clan as well as omni characters (and neutrals for that sake) is AO's big flaw. I had a lot more fun in the beginning, and you know why ? Because I felt like an omni and was getting into the role. That doesn't mean i would have to roleplay through my teeth - but I did in a bigger way play the role of an omni atrox enforcer then what i do now. The thing was... The clans was a mistery to me - I had never been there nor spoken to one. What I find today is that a lot of the people i meet have characters on both sides.. So HOW are they going to get sucked into hatred to one side ? I would love if your whole "family" of characters needed to be on one side instead. By this.. spying would actually make sence - as you can clearly see : now it isn't. If there are future warefforts (and I hope there is) that requires intelligence... Why bother trying to be clever when you can just create a lvl 1 clanner.

I do understand why you would want it open - to let people try a little of this and a little of that : but as long as the two sides have their own benefits there is a bigger prize in forcing people to _really_ side. Maybe you would want to give people a chance to defect at some crucial points ? lvl 15, 50 and 100 perhaps ? And then make that choice final ?

I am not going to get involved with a guild, because of the time and devotion it often requires - but i have a feeling I will by this actually miss out on everything around the rebels/omni conflict.

just my 2 cents.

well I don't think that is correct. Never heard it anyway - and nothing I've read from funcom indicates it. afaik, you can lvl til you drop with chars on both sides. My point was :

- I want bigger difference between clan and omni. I want them to have unique benefits.
- I want clanners to react in disgust when I enter a bar, and not go "need a team?"
- I want GM's constantly organizing raids on the other side, and i want traitors on my side warning the clans when we plan to strike.
- I want successful attacks to strengthen me/omni in some way, and I want the clans to suffer if they loose a battle.(prizes for the loosing side goes up, worse profits from missions etc.)
- I want to hear more propaganda about how devious the clans really are.

a bit late and im a tad tired - but anyway : I want to get some heat into this game. I feel like choosing sides in this game is like when you choose what breed to play in starcraft. Doesn't really give you any loyalty feeling now does it.

As for the storyline - I'm not sure what the plan is with the amnesty, but I sure hate seeing clanners team up in omni areas like they were our best friends. I think, in the end, it destroys the conflict.

I think AO has so much going for it, and the potential is there : I just see a couple of choices that funcom has made that prevents the game from being what I think it should be. And again : You might have a great plan which makes my points moot - but right now, I can't really see it. All I see is a graphically astonishing, futuristicly based, RPG style, advanced version of quake2 co-op.
