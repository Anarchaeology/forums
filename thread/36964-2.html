I hunt outdoors, solo usually, by flying around casually in my yalm until I see some monsters in my con range, then park and hunt. This is how I mainly play. I only do missions for specific nanos and armor and tokens. I level slower than most players because I play to relax and have fun exploring, that is my main thing, exploring. I am not in a competition to level fast to keep up with anyone. I park my yalm in especially pretty areas and run around on foot, sometimes on foot across half a zone, just looking at stuff.

There is so little lag outdoors, and so much vastness and such interesting monsters, outposts, dynacamps, monster villages, crashed spaceships, the planets in the sky, the thrill of hunting at night in a dangerous area....and when you run into other players who are doing the same as you, it is much more meaningful than just running into strangers in an anonymous crowded city. Outdoor hunters stop to share locations, tactics, stories, and just their opinions of how beautiful some location is.

I have a large list of dynacamps I have discovered, and try to note the lvl if I can, or make a note to come back to that camp at higher level. I like to hunt dynacamps and camps because: there may be boss loot, I like fighting multiple enemies, it takes concentration and tactics.

I also take alot of screenshots, which I use as wallpaper.

Indoor missions I only do because I have to.

Outdoor hunting is FUN and thats why I play AO, for fun.
