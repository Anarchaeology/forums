What I think should be done in PvP about bracers (longish)
This is a continuation of the "Time is overdue fix Healing VS Non-healing in PvP" thread that Caol started. I would have posted it there but it has long since turned into an argument. The last 3 pages are just people arguing over other people's previous posts and nothing new or constructive has been said.

People seem to be forgetting that all this fighting over healing being too powerful in PvP is not the heals fault. It is the reflect bracers that are causing the gap between damage and healing to be WAY unbalanced in PvP which give ANY class that can heal, yes mongo counts, such a supreme advantage over the others.
Its not that people canit do enough damage fast enough its just that after having 72%+ of their damage reflected its that heals are effectively made 7.142 times powerful compared to the amount of damage being done. (See Caols thread for an explanation of that number)

The following is my "Solution" as well as comments, suggestions, why I think it should be this way and stuff to back up why I think that. I have tried to keep it as fair as possible to EVERYONE.

I do ask that you read the ENTIRE post at LEAST once, preferable 2-3 times, before you start flaming or *****ing and reply with constructive comments rather then "J00 suxx0rs¡". Caol's recent joke thread about how heals are going to be 50% in 14.4 is an excellent example how people see one sentence then completly go nuts before they actually read the whole post. I am mearly posting what I personally think is the best way to deal with the situation and backing it up as best I can.

Also please do not start on the "what if the doctor doesn't have HE" or "what if I only have tlvl 4 bracers" or any other "What if"/special situations. The problem is at the high end and most of the time those "What it if" are meaningless since every person worth their salt will have those 21% bracer and HE running.

Eg) It was brought up before that "Well soldiers can heal as well they have OMHH". Big freaking deal. OMHH heals 200-300 health every 5 seconds, 90% of soldiers don't even raise bio to use it, on top of that and soldier in PvP is either deprived or under nano shutdown from the TMS. To date I have NEVER seen a soldier use OMHH in PvP for reasons other then to break a root.

These situations are usually neither likely to happen nor feasible and just cause more arguments and venturing off topic which takes away from the main point of the thread.

A complete nerf of reflecting part of reflect bracers in PvP.

Now before you get your angelicpanties in a knot please continue reading. Those flamers and trolls who are convinced to flame/troll regardless of what I type should stop here because the rest is my explanation/comments/suggestions/etc. Those honorable and fair people, please continue




Why the bracers?

As I had stated above it is not the damage or healing that is the problem it is the reflect that is widening the gap between the two causing heals to be too powerful.



________
Example1)

Lets assume that Doctor A has no reflect and is equally matched with Soldier A to the point where the doctor can heal exactly as much damage as the soldier can dish out. They would be evenly matched in a stalemate (actually the doctor could attack during the recharge of the heals which make the doctor win.)

Now if you were to give the Doctor 72% reflect the doctor could take on that original soldier AS WELL AS 3 other soldiers exactly like the first for a total of 4 soldiers (actually 3.57 soldiers in total, but you get the point.
_______




THAT is the general unbalance that is ruining PvP atm (aside from there be no fair PvP place to PvP for either side). It does not matter what year this is or what profession you are. NO profession should be able to take on 4 others who are each equally matched to the person taking on the 4.


The extreme reflects are pretty much crippling and class who can not heal in PvP and therefore must use pure damage to win rather then some damage and healing to back it up.



_______
Example 2)

It is pretty much agreed that a high level soldier is probably the best at causing the most damage in an instant amount of time. Personal record with a Westinghouse and 900ish attack rating is 18.2k alpha which took the whole 1.5 seconds to do. I Believe it was a 10k FA, 4.6k Burst and 3.6k Fling. I'm sure that people have done more, but that's my record which isn't too shabby. Now obviously way back that was too powerful in PvP so both damage was cut to 50% total and 40% of health max.

Lets just say my target has 8k Health. Pretty much any profession can get at least that much in PvP, while others can get way higher. Spydervenom PvPs with 11k+ health.

Now if they have 8k health and 30% reflect from RRF plus two 21% bracers and I just happen to get very lucky and have my record alpha I'll only damage them by about 2546pts of damage or 31.8% of their health.

72% reflect under 50% PvP is 14% effectiveness. ( 100%-72%)*50% = 14%
Max damage is 8000*50% = 3200 health (not that it matters)

Fling does 504pts damage
Burst does 644pts damage
FA does 1400pts damage.
Total 2528pts
___________



After unloading arguable the most powerful "attack" in the game I've damage the target for 2548pts damage after being EXTREMELY lucky in getting the best alpha I could ( I've done it twice and that was with UVC and a scope). I have not done one nearly as good since. Now call me stupid but I think anyone doing that kind of an attack, be it soldier/agent/ma/whoever, should be rewarded with a little more then having their target down to ~70% health.

And those are with lucky results. I did some weapons testing with the same gun on Kiantirsa and posted the results in Caol¡¦s thread. The average alpha damage was 915!!!. That barely take the targets health down to 88%. I Pity the f**l that tries with just normal damage to kill someone. (I used ** because "I pity the fo - ol" is a copyrighted trademark of Mr. T. :P )


So as you can see it pretty much gives a death sentence to any profession trying to actually kill another with that much reflect. Today in Camelot Myself, Spydervenom (woot for him coming back for a month) and a high level MA tried for 15min to kill Pomy, who is the greenest of greens to me let alone the others, even with 3 types of damage we could no do enough damage through the reflect to kill her.




Why not something else instead of bracers?

1. Nerfing the reflect portion of the bracers in make the playing ground more even. Although it is still skewed towards healing and not damage, its just not as muchas what is currently ruining pvp. If you just had a max of 30% reflect from RRF the results would have been different in the above two Healing and damage examples

Example 1) With 30% reflect. The doctor could take on an additional 0.7 soldiers and still be evenly matched. This is a little bit more fair as no profession should have the previous kind of an advantage.

Example 2) Total damage of that lucky power alpha would have caused 3202 pts damage (Target with 8k is down to 59.997% health. Even with the best alpha that is still not even close to an insta-kill. The most powerful player attack should actually be something that takes a bit to recover from and not laughed at. Using the average results I had posted before when I shot up Kiantirsa the results come out to an average alpha of 2287pts damage (target at 71.4% thanks to the max 40% rule)

Personally I think an alpha should do a little more about that but I'm trying to be fair here.



2. Nerfing bracers equally effects everyone since everyone looses that same ability to reflect. Where as "Nerfing" heals directly to 50% would only effect healers. It is true that heals would be relatively less effective but that's the whole problem in the first place. 1k heals will still be 1k heals not 500 and complete heals will still be 10k not 5k. Its just no longer that 1k heal can totally heal up 7142 pts of damage after it goes through reflects.



3. Gives some uniqueness back to soldiers. I, as well as other soldiers, picked a soldier because one of our string points was the ability to reflect some damage done on me. It pains many soldiers to see everyone walking around with 72% reflect


4. More fairness to the NTs. Their profession balancing nano, Nullity sphere, isn't exactly that special anymore when a soldier under TMS with bracers has essentially a 1 min 20 second nullity sphere in which he can move around and attack when the NTs nullity sphere lasts 12 seconds or so and roots them.


5. Removing only the reflect portion from PvP still allows the protection that is needed in PvM.


6. Balances the professions a bit. Its not very nice when say a Trader, who's official description states that they are weak in ALL combat types (damage, nanos, dots, etc), spanks an enforcer's butt for lunch before moving onto the soldier main course. Call me crazy but damage/tanking classes should have at least a fair chance against a "support" class. (I know people don't like the term "support class" but what else do you call a profession who's weaknesses are all combat abilities?)


7. More emphasis on strategy instead of pressing Q and watching TV while remembering to press your heal button every 30 seconds or so to completely heal up the damage done to you so far.


8. I would no longer take 2 min to kill someone in PvP (by which time they have chickened off to a safe area. Would by no means make it a quick fight either (as in the case that putting damage back up to 100% would to match healing would)


9. Less ganking? If people can't kill someone their own level to get their PvP titles because of reflect they'll just move onto the unluky lvl 75 people who they can snuff out no problem. Perhaps if the main PvP people coudl actually kill eachother they wouln't have to gank anymore.


10. No more Cloudeh whining and making excuses that he can't get refelct bracers even though everyone else over level 150 has no problem. (Sorry, just couldn't help myself :P )






Other Suggestions

The following should also be taken into concideration if reflect was nerfed.


1. Make sure that the reflect damage BACK part of the reflect bracers still works in PvP. It is the reflect away % that is killing PvP, the not 21pts damage that people get hurt back for.


2. Increase that reflect back damage to 0.25-0.33 pts per qlvl (reflect 50-66pts back for a qlvl 200). Reflecting an additional 100-132 pts damage back if you have the correct bracers on is an acceptable benefit. Any more would fringe on enforcer/advent damage shields

3. Increase the shielding on shielding bracers to 0.5-0.75 pts per qlvl (shields 100-150pts at qlvl 200) to make them actually worth something. This makes them worth choosing and now people have the choice of either saving themselves from damage or reflecting bits back at the attackers. I assume non-healers would choose shielding while healers might opt for reflect since they can heal the damage they didn¡¦t have shielding but use the reflect to further augment "their" offensive abilities.

Shielding 300pts off a 3.2k shot is a lot more balanced then reflecting away 2304pts from the same shot.
