Make all good stuff quest only
Alvin/Doga quest is a good step in the right way to do things.
All the best items should be given as reward for gathering random components from normal roaming monsters and should make anything currently special/"uber" (MK II, tarasque drops, etc) look silly in comparison.
It would:
1. make us go to some remote zones where only the non unique monster types required to be killed can be found.
2. Put an end to lame, boring, stupid camping at the same spot while waiting several hours for a specific rare mob to spawn.
3. change from daily missions which all look the same
4. and also give a huge exp reward depending on the mission level as an alternative to chain-missions which are currently the only way to get decent xp.

When creating these quests, keep the following in mind:
- no component required to complete the quest comes from a unique monster with several hours respawn rate unless it is for the ultimate PROFESSION SPECIFIC item that require to kill a different unique mob for each profession (this way not everyone is killing the same rare mob).
- npc who give the quests don't vanish for several hours.

We need more quests period and give us a reason to do them instead of camping for loot. The only way to make it happen is to have quest rewards better than anything mob dropped.

Keep bumping this post until Funcom does it if you like the idea.

Edit: More profession specific quests are a must have so we all end looking and being different. Currently it's everybody in senpai primus coat and MK II armor with a shotgun.
