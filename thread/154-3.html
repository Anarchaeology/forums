I've played the following classes Soldier, Doctor, Trader, NT and recently started on an Engineer.

TMS : x2 or x3 the current max reflect damage.

Reflect Shields : x2 or x3 the current max refelect damage

Weapons : Add some more weapons types, add some real impressive heavy weapons as well as assault rifles.

Tank Armour : Reduce the nano penality on Tank armour before we are all wearing those ugly coats. However a soldier should still be made to invest in raising their nano pool as well. Excuses such as "oh dear I've run out of nano how unfair", and then finding out the person has hardly spent any IP's in nano pool is rediculous.

Nanos : Give Soldiers a better self heal and self health buff, the current lines are a joke that only a gimpy Soldier would take. Look at giving Soldiers more weapon masteries but make them self only. MG and Shotgun would both be examples.

Weapons attacks : Unnerf weapon attacks, the whole point behind a soldier is to be able to do a frightning amount of damage though access to a number of special attacks, otherwise we'd all just head down the route of using a weapon with only 1 special, or with fewer weapon skills, not the 4-5 weapon skills they currently require.

Hint to Soldiers :
Invest in using Aggro enhancers to help keep your docs alive.
50-100 are your hard IP years after 100 you start having stacks of IP's again.

Hint to Others :
Invest in evade, dodge and duck.

To the person or persons who was thinking of giving up on his NT and go Soldier, go do it, bet you'd be back on your NT so fast the servers will not even have time to update.

Doc comment :
Why were Doc's made into PvP specialists with the recent patch, the whole point behind the doctor was healing and protecting others, not to PvP. It so goes against the image of a Doctor (Well at least the image I want in a Doctor thats about to treat me)
