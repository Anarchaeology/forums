Thank you!
Since this advertising our clan has expanded by 40%, all solid and active members. Consider joining the Mercury Dragons Clan, we're not the largest, we're not out for glory or fame, we're not terrorists, we're don't charge taxes, we are not politicians, we're not an "exclusive" clan, and we aren't going to fade in and out as so many clans have done.

We're a dedicated clan, our main goal is independece from Omni-Tek, not to kill and destroy everything on the planet to gain a "freedom" we already have, but to gain TRUE independence, have a real clan goverment body which we will be under, and not Omni-Tek's as the Tir Accord states (read carefully, it does say we're under their juristriction still).

We have the right to have our own goverment, to be able to trade freely with any corporation, including Omni-Tek, and to be able to mine in clan territory for Notum and sell it to the ICC as they are a neutral organization.

We also have the right to work for anyone we wish for, be it a clan or neutral company or even a corporation (Omni-Tek, SOL, etc). We should not be obligated to STAY with a company when we should be able to freelance.

We don't want to destroy Omni-Tek, they terraformed this planet and made it a home for most of us, yes...they did used our people as slaves in the mines decades ago, and they are yet to compensate for it...but what we need is CHANGE, not utter destruction.

Mercury Dragons Clan will FIGHT for CHANGE, we will fight Omni-Tek for our independence, we will fight Dust Brigade for the safety of our people (they are terrorists), we will fight the Cyborgs, and any threat to the clans.

Long live the fighters!
