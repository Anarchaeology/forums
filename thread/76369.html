Recon Race (atlantean)
This Thursday december 5th, Forsaken welcomes all clanners to The Recon Race.

Registration will be outside the Happy Rebel in Tir from 17:30-18:30 GMT (18:30-19:30 CET) Talk to Odeen for registration. If you have any questions you may also look up any other Forsaken members present.

The race will start at 19:00 GMT (20:00 CET).
Info and rules will be explained for the contestants from 18:30-18:55 GMT (19:30-19:55 CET).

All levels may join and have a good chance at winning 1 out of 3 money prices, winner gets away with 10 million credits.


Hope to see alot of clanners there, if not to join, to cheer on the competetors.

Good luck and hope you have fun.
