Programs and theirs req skill.
Hi.
I would like to discuss a few issues about the adv proff.

Nano skills:

After last patch , we got a bunch of new prg, mostley of them use psy mod as a req.
Before the patch we had only 2 prg (except for team prg now ) that used psy mod skill. Eagle eye and robust treatment.

I didnt raise my psy mod skill at all coz i didnt have any use of thos prg.
Many of our prg use Bio meta (heal prg) , matter meta, matter crea (shield) and Sensory imp ( poly prg)

That will make 4 basic skills that we have to raise if we want to be able to use thos prg.


But now when i have reach a descent level and have maxed out all thos 5 skills i scratched my head and said to my self:

What a heck,,, I think we are the only proffession that use 5 out of 6 nano & aiding skills. How can we possible be able to use all thos prg when we need to raise 5 skills?

I have tryed to do a descent mix of ql 200 implants to cover all skills, but how i switch and build i will never be able to use all prg, even if i reach level 200.
Either i have to choose to use healing prg ,or polymorph prg , or shield prg, or thos new psy mod req prg. But i can never use them all togheter.
And many of thos new prg is supposed to be used in combo whit the other prg. But i cant do it :/

I think something must be done if a level 200 player cant self cast all thos prg that is for his/hers proffession whit out any support from an MP ( mochams etc)
As a level 200 u should be able to be self supported.

And even if i get mochams,, I need around 2 or 3 mochams buffs that take up aprox 100-150 ncu space, then buff my self whit my prg i want,, but 100-150 ncu is tied to thos mochams so i cant use all thos prg i want anyway so it is a moment 22 here.

What we need is some sort of self buff prg so we can raise our nano & aiding skill or lower the req for our prg so we actually can use them.
As I said before, 5 skill out of 6 ,is to much to be coverd by implants ,as many of them goes on same implant.

I think it will be around 5-9 prg (not sure here) i cant self cast even if i hit level 200 and whit a good mix of ql 200 implants. And i cant switcht implants everytime i want to use a prg.


Something els i want to bring up is that,,, runbuffs,,

I though Adventures was supposed to be the speedy gonzales in this game but we have ony 100 runspeed buff ( if i dont count 1300 calias form parrot)
Isnt that a bit odd. fixers for instance , have nearly 550 run buffs.

And i dont get it, our polymorph prg (except for birdy shape) Lower the runspeed. I have never seen any sabretooth that run slower than a human ( hehe i have never seen any sabretooth at all ) but i just wanted to point out that instead of decresing the runspeed it should raise it instead.


Thanks

Ah, and just to add fuel to the fire.

Profession line usage:

Adv: 5/6, haveta raise the 6th for using chargers.
Agt: 6/6 for their own, much less other people's.
Bur: 5/6 (there's a low level crat nuke that reqs MM, but most just ignore it)
Doc: 5/6 for their main lines, 6/6 for Epsilon Purge, the NInit buff, and the treatment buffs.
Enf: 4/6, 5/6 for bluntmonkeys and init buffs, the 6th for nanochargers.
Eng: 5/6, 6/6 if bot heals are desired.
Fix: 5/6, 6/6 if GA fixer.
MA: 5/6, 6/6 if they want horde/cohort/str buffs
MP: 6/6
NT: 6/6. 5/6 if they don't care about layers buffs, stuns, and Enfraams.
Sol: 6/6, though the BM-based buffs are pretty useless.
Tra: 6/6, 5/6 if they skip their damage buffs.

So, for comparison, most professions do have about the same line usage as advents if they want all their nanos.
