Actually, the OE patch will have a slight nerfing effect on Traders.

Currently:
Traders can ladder Deprive/Ransack combos to massively increase their weapon skills. This allows us a chance to equip a weapon based on the laddered results of our weaponskills. Once equipped, we can run around with that weapon without having to repeat the deprives/ransacks.

Post 14.2:
Traders can ladder Deprive/Ransack combos to massively increase their weapon skills. However, now we'll be required to maintain those combos at all times lest the overequip rules come into effect.

The 14.2 overequipping rules will not apply to monsters, as Gaute has stated. Mobs follow their own sets of rules. Laddering the skill debuff nanos (ie: using a deprive/ransack combo to meet the requirements to use the next deprive/ransack combo, ect..) is monotonous, dangerous, difficult to maintain, and expensive as hell (for nontwinks).
