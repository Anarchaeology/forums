Hotkey Commands
This is very a very simple thing to explain, not sure how simple to implement.

The ability to set Keystrokes Commands and Access the Quickbar via a script.

The commands respectivly would be

/quickbar <n,n>
/key <a>

i.e.

I want to tell my script to do the following.

/quickbar 1,1 <---That is my heal spell 1=First Pane 1= First Slot
/say Now healing %t <--- Say who is being healed


or

/quickbar 3,4 <--- That is Mark of Risk 3=3rd Pane 4=Fourth Slot
/say %t, you now have been marked! <---Say who it is put on


or

/key X <---Activate key "X"
/say Nano Charging! <---Witty Comment
/quickbar 1,10 <--- My Nano Charger 1=Pane 10=Slot
/say Ready to cast! <---One more witty comment



Very simple, thousands of uses from these 2 commands for the scripter, and would add a lot of playability to our world...
