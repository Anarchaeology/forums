Changes to the Guild Chat System
I'd like to get some feedback from others. I plan to send a request to FC about making some additions to the guild system. I want to try to collect as many opinions and suggestions as I can before sending it though so that it actually has some weight behind it, hopefully enough to cause it to have an effect.

After trying a number of different guilds, I've finally found one which really works for me. The people are great, they're extremely helpful, there's a steady group of regulars that keep the chat going, etc. It's really a pleasure to be a member (err, Applicant) of this guild. My guild has recently suffered a number of defections of higher level players however. This is reportedly mostly due to the fact that these higher level players find it very hard to find groups within the guild. There are a smaller number of lower lvl players (below lvl 30), and a fair number of higher lvl players (lvl 100+). The majority of the guild is made up of players between lvls 40-100. We have two players over lvl 160.

I've heard a few people simply respond to the issue of a guild having problems retaining it's higher lvl players by saying that "High lvl players join guild with other high lvl players." What I hate though is that there's no real mechanism for manipulating a guild structure so that a guild can have high lvl members and lower lvl members. There's also no way for a guild to segregate it's members so that they can pariticipate in different ongoing activities within the guild.

My guild is split up into two (hopefully soon three) divisions. One division is devoted to the sciences. The other division is focused on defense and combat activities. We're looking into establishing another division which is responsible for the political and admininistrative activities (fundraising, bookkeeping, promoting/demoting members, maintaining the web site) involved in maintaining a guild, and it's relationships with the various other entities on Rubi-Ka (other guilds and individuals included).

So, we've mentioned a few ways in which the guild is divided (by level as well as by responsibilities). Yet there's only one chat channel for the whole guild. What I would love to see to address the issue of different lvl within the guild is the ability to have a channel for the guild as a whole, a general guild channel. Then maybe break it up so that there are additional channels for each title lvl. This way lvl 50-100 lvl members can hear and contribute to things going on in the general guild channel, such as lower lvl members asking for help from much higher lvl members. They can also keep focused on things going on (such as team recruiting etc.) with players within their title range without polluting the general guild chat. I think a system like this, or some modified version of it, would allow a guild to have members of all title levels while allowing an individual to be privy to and focus on issues within the guild pertaining to players near their lvl.

I think that that at least addresses the issue of how to maintain a guild with a much broader distribution of levels. Of the few things that I'll mention in this post, I think that that is the most important.

Now, onto the issue of having different divisions within the guild. I'll focus on the issue of guild divisions within the chat system as it pertains to our forming divisision of government and political affairs. Any political body must have meetings. Yet, the only real mechanism for holding quote/unquote meetings within the existing chat system is for everyone to gather in one location at the same time and chat in the vicinity channel, usually in a not completely private location. Or you can use some mechanism like an online forum (in real life) to share information among either all of the guild members, or a certain subset of guild members priveleged to access that particular forum. Well, to my mind the first option is usually terribly inconvenient. Getting everyone online at the same time is usually a difficult task to say the least (especially considering the fact that players are in any number of different time zones). And I think it would be fantastic to be able to chat with my fellow guildies (within the governmental division for the sake of argument) about governmental issues while going about my normal business(hunting, missions etc.) by having a channel dedicated to that specific division. Take this same concept and apply it to the other divisions of science and defense. It would be great to be able to have the leader of the guild as a whole delegate parts of his/her responsibilities by assigning responsibility for an entire division to a member. That elected official could then issue statements to his/her own division by chatting within the division's chat channel, not affecting or disturbing the rest of the guild.

I don't know how feasible it is to make these changes in one of the upcoming patches, perhaps the next major patch, which I expect will be 14.4 (14.2 for OE, a follow up 14.3 to fix the bugs in 14.2 and then a major patch for 14.4).

So, please post your comments to these ideas. And please do make other suggestions as to what else you'd like to see added/changed within the chat system, particularly as it pertains to guilds. And I'll keep FC informed about our ideas towards the end of seeing some real changes made in the near future.

Thanks for taking the time to read this.
