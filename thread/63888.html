new command /org mutiny
with the coming of the booster pack, many guilds are waking up to the sad state of their guilds. Many of the guilds I have been in have had absent leaders.

While this probably wont be able to change anytime soon, it would be helpful to guilds to have a /org mutiny command that removes the leader and promotes a new person to that place.

At present the only way to get a new leadership for a group is to form a new guild. The mutiny command would allow guilds to stay together when their leaders fail them.

anyone else like this idea?
anyone think why this is a bad idea?

I believe this can be a tricky thing. How can you be sure that your leader has abandoned you? Devil's advocate scenario:

Your guild leader hasn't been around in 4 weeks, hasn't left word with anyone in the guild. Fine, you say. Let's mutiny! The mutiny ensues, then a week later the guild leader shows up again, finds that he isn't a guild leader anymore and effectively the guild he helped think up, design and build isn't his anymore.

Why was he gone? Oh he was in the hospital cause he got hit by a drunk driver or something. So the first time he has a chance to log back in and let everyone know what was up, he finds his guildies didn't have any faith in him and jumped ship and took what he made

I've seen guilds that have leadership come up through the ranks cause the existing leaders never show up again. But usually involves at least a name change of the org to signify its at least a little different than the one it used to be.

Sure its a hassle when you don't know where the leadership has gone, or if they'll be back, but I think you sorta owe it to them since the leader OWNS the guild, to either wait it out, or disband/rename.

I think all of your ideas have merit.

The way it is now, with enforced leadership, that the leader cannot be changed without their consent, the like it or leave it, is one way to look at the issue.

I agree that the last thing I would want for someone who just took a vacation is to find out that all his work is gone.

But I would like a mechanism to counter the problems with having to leave. I have been a part of a terrible guild split, and it hurt so much, because now there were factions, and there is a tension when you see members of the other faction. The real mechanics of this game is communication, and the basic currency is friends, the application of this is that the guild is an easy communication group, and members tend to become your friends. When clan membership is severed for good or bad reasons, the chance of maintaining friendships with former guildiees becomes reduced. I dont want to loose any more friends because someone took a three month vacation, and forgot to tell his friends.
