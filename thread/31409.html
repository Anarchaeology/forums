An apartment idea
I just thought of a good idea on what to do with apartments. If apartments are stored client side (on your computer) instead of server side, it would allow the Funcom servers to be eased, as well as allowing more customization.

You could allow people to make their own art or wall textures to be used. You could also allow any number of items to be stored there. When you allowed others to enter your apartment, they would download the apartment from you, and then they could enter it.

I know that you could dupe items easily this way, but here is my solution around it. You store the closet server side, and allow only certain types of items to be placed (furniture, lights, plants)in the rest of the aprtment. You could have a limit of 10-15 items that could be placed in the closet. All non furniture items left on the ground when you left would be destroyed. You could than also make all furniture worth zero when being sold, so that no one would ever want to dupe furniture.

I think this would make the game a whole lot cooler, and would allow apartments to be used the way they should be. Plz bump if you like the idea
