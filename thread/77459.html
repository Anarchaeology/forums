Alliance Tower
New tower - Alliance tower

This tower has a list of guilds wich can use it.
You can add/remove guids to the list by tagging the tower and say /tower list addguild <guild name>.

Simply use /tower list remguild <guildname>

This tower must be weak, but you are allowed to build 2 or 3 on each area.

Dunno if they should be add to your tower control power, but it is open for discussion.

Being free to add guilds ain't a problem.. cause your towers also hit a 5% time. If you have ALL the guilds in the list, you will get destroyed sometimes.

This way alliances will work out.
