Didn't mean for this to turn into another breed argument, but since it has... well...

I started off with bot implants with my crat. Twinked him into a set of QL10s (okay, so I wasn't a GOOD twinker), then 20s, both with bot focuses. At low levels implants don't help a TON, but they do help quite a bit; every time I got a new bot, he was red to me, and barely ever dipped into the other colors before I was able to replace him.

Now, around 30ish, I decided to make myself a set of 50 implants. For some reason, I went calm/charm focus. Well, it was great, sure.

Except, my bot constantly dipped into the light orange or nearly-yellow before I'd get the next one.

Except, he couldn't hold aggro, even with Mudslinger.

Except, the charms ran out before the title cap hit, sticking me with the same charm for 10-15 levels.

Except, a calm is a calm, the duration is about all that changed; and I'd have had that calm two levels later anyway, at max.

Except, while my GREEN biomet/psymod/senseimp skills were rising fast, my LIGHT BLUE matcrea/t&s skills were lagging. On the flowchart checklists I have of my nanos, my bot lines were leaving big gaps.

The ONLY way I could keep aggro off myself was to charm a mob and send bot and mob in after an enemy, sitting in the next room over. My bot didn't bounce aggro effectively, thus he ended up with lots of health while the charmed mob was almost dead.

I just installed a set of QL70 implants, this time with a bot focus. My biomet/psymod/senseimp are as high as the QL50s made them before I swapped them, and the matcrea/t&s are nearly as high. And guess what?

My bot is now almost *10* levels above me; a little bit of work, and I'll have one 13-15 levels above me. That's pretty doggone good at a low level AND under a title cap.

My HP is higher than my NP, but both are well over 1000 (level 48, BTW). In fact, my HP is 1500+ with my life implants and maxed stamina/body dev. I was in a team with a nanomage NT with *500* HP. I guess he'd never raised his body dev... I had to laugh. Mobs can be critting me and I'll shrug it off as I bust out a calm. I like that security. And yes, I'm also raising evades, but anyone can do that.

I have to agree with Lucid here... the better your bot, the better off you are. Sure, you can't get the latest charm as quickly... but generally speaking, it's rare you'll be soloing a mission where you can't grab an enforcer or bot before you get a new charm. And as Lucid pointed out, the higher you go, the higher you can hunt, and the higher Psy gets (thus, no charms).

I don't agree that nanomage crats are worse. A carefully played and well-equipped (key point) nanomage crat can focus on nukes and pets and do some amazing things. I imagine with later title caps there are some other neat things that they can do before solitus crats can. It's a matter of playing style, really. I like to be a little bit behind on calms/charms, but equal in my skills with the bot, and have the stability I need to survive.

That, and I use Str/Sta armor and boost myself into it. Who needs Waitt or Nano?
