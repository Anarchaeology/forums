Don't remember where I learned this, but there's actually a little trick to placing items in your apartment: the item will get placed facing the same direction that your character is facing when they drop the item.

So if you want to place a painting on the wall, you have to have your back to the wall when you drop it. Just go out to 3rd person view to place your furniture.
