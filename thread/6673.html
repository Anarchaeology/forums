Dont see the point... but made me think
Dont really see the point of your idea... lets compare your scenario with the actual one

Yours: I do more damage with the same moves as I get to higher levels of MA skill
Now: I get access to moves that do higher damage as I get to highers levels of MA skill.

Nothing different. Just keeping the old moves, and frankly is not like the animations are so awesome that I would like to keep a variety of them

But thinking about fixing MA moves... lets see... what pisses me the most about the current implementation is that you do nano damage to the enemy and it can resist it. Thats not how I envision MA attacks... MA attacks are moves that you learn to program your body to do, and you spend some nanoenergy activating that so your whole body goes into the routine. The damage should be still plain old melee damage, not nano of any kind, as the nano should be all inside of you. But moves should have an evade check for completion; that is, a check made against the target evading skills to know if he parried, ducked, dodged or otherwise just werent there to get hitted. THAT check could have a different bonus, either to you or to the target, different for the move.
That way you could have, for example, moves of high damage but easy to evade (good for dispaching low level mobs but useless against the bosses), moves with moderate or low damage that are difficult to evade, ubermoves that are good in all aspects but only masters can get to know, etc.

Just my opinion.
