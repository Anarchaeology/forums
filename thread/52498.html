Okay, here are a few quests for clanners.

The first one is a simple for low lvls: just go to mongo meat or whatever it's called in Tir and talk to the new npc. The keywords are clear.. you have to deliver a package. Just say 'hello' or 'mission' or something.

The second is situated in Tir County in the northern outpost. There's a crazy inventor who wants you to get 3 parts: legs, brains and finally some other thing i forgot. You can find them on mutilated mutants (like Mutilated Shade or claw for example) These monsters are around Tir County. Another low lvl quest.

A third I heard of is getting a dog tag bag around Tir and collect tags from OT mobs in (greater) Tir County. I haven't completed that one yet.
