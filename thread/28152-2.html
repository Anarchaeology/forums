This is an interesting thread, and I agree that people do make or break this kind of game. My first character I kept to myself doing missions, and rarely teamed at all, and I was getting very bored. Next character I make is a trox, Hombrito some of you might have met him. once I got to level 40 or so I was giving out lots of 8 NCU essences to passersby, if you were gray to me and ran by you probably got it. Not everyone thanked me, but I didn't do it for a thanks and neither should any of you. Some did, and I was sure to show that I appreciated their acknowledgement. When you give a buff to someone who hasn't asked for it you never know if it will help them or just waste their limited Belt Space. My enforcer gave out plenty o buffs and I spent some one on one time with a few new characters who wanted advice, it can take a long time, but it's very rewarding, so to higher level players, try and pay attention to that lowly level 10,20,30 player, they might seem like a small fry to you, but help where you can.

I started this new MA (Martir) and he was a leech on society Until level 30 or so I didn't have any interesting nanos to give out, and until level 15 I couldn't even buff myself... I bugged people for MA expertise, and I always got it, with a good luck and a bow I'd say folks are plenty friendly. As long as I was polite and asked a specific person, I always got a good response. I asked for cash to get lockpicks around level 2, just 50 credits or so, and somebody gave me 5k, I'd say they were generous. I do tend to ramble so if you read all this thanks.

My point is that this can be a very welcoming community, there are people who are self-centered and don't respond, people who are just busy, but mostly you will find that people help how they can whenever they can, as long as you are polite and show you appreciate it. When it comes to paying for services and buffs, if the person is gray to you, it should be free of charge... it's stuff like this that made my experience so enjoyable, and also how I helped others when I was in the position to help. I've been a clan member, so it may just be on our side of Rubi-Ka but I doubt it, you will always find melons, and you will always find good people, Anarchy has a mix of both (it's to be expected) but there are defenitely enough good ones out there for me.
