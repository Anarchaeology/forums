Totally personal, and off-topic to the main topic of this post. But ... why is it that it seems that all of the Team Leads chosen for their professionalism, people like you who put time and effort into adding value to FunCom's product, and people like me who want nothing more than the best game that FunCom can produce are all so vehemently opposed to the kiddy'ification of AO?

Why does it seem that all the people with something between their ears have enough caring to almost pity what is happening to Anarchy Online?

Have you noticed the maturity level of the people who are pleading with FunCom to at least open a dialogue. The words chosen may seem harsh or even juvenile at times, but if you look deeper you see the words are very carefully chosen. I've learned a lot about who the adults are by reading some incredibly well thought out posts that are written with a passion simply drips of their care for the game.

Something is very, seriously, wrong, right now. I feel my own passion for the game draining away. I see my friends in game all of a sudden and almost in unison talking about which online diversions are being considered for the future. And for the first time ever, a long term commitment to Anarchy Online is not a given. In fact, the unspoken feeling is that we need to get ourselves together so we stay as friends in whatever next we decide to do.

None of us thought it would come to this. None of us understand what FunCom is doing. I have theories, but they won't be proven or disproven without the passage of time.

One thing for sure. They are not catering to the intellectual or mature gamer with any of the recent changes in game mechanics. The 'caring' seems to be gone. The game with each subsequent patch degrades to more of a commodity. Newbie rules, OE hack, Level Requirements on everything you look at, Story completely and utterly trashed.

It all appears to boil down to a desire to provide the EverCrack that can be easily counted on and forecasted in financial futures.

The soul of a game that was once full of it is gone. To make it worse, once the soul is gone, we're left with a game that isn't much of a game anymore.

If I want to play a game without soul, I'll at least grab a game that has 'game' value. Star Wars: Galaxies surely appears to be hollow in terms any good funk. But, if you look at the ruleset you'll see one hell of a game. Now, as for soul. Check out City of Heros.

Intra, Inocybe, Serena, DaveDread, Lucid, Xtrem, ...

the list goes on and on of the most respected people in the game and those who contribute to the gaming community as a whole and make AO more than it could ever have been without their presence.

Ignored.

As a matter of fact look down the entire list of 'Professionals' and find posts on their 'personal' opinions about what's going on. I'm not talking about their jobs as the 'Professionals'. Look at how these respected players actually feel. Then look at FunCom's response.
