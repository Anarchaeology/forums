Not Mentioned in 13.6 Patch Notes: Menu Windows Can Move (Some)
I found myself wondering if the menu windows (ctrl-1 to ctrl-9) could be moved, yet. It doesn't say anything about this in the patch notes, but it turns out that they can. They can't be dragged around, but they can be dragged up and down, which has the effect of changing what order they're in.
