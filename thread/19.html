Athens Accord
Congrats to Clan OpFor and Division 9 for accomplishing more than our the politicians have thus far - but be forewarned, there are those who shall disrupt and dishonor any accords not for the sake of justice or freedom but for the sake of anarchy or revenge. But this anarchy was coming anyways, it becomes a question on whether we face it alone or together.

And for those Omni who feel that supporting the clans runs contrary to Corporate well being - there are some historic truisms you must understand. War and Anarchy run contrary to profit, deficit spending occurs to support any kind of war effort or even "police" action. This diminishes our profit margin and lowers the return for our investors. Also, unhappy and bitter workers or those meerly stressed out by labor strife [even those 100% loyal to omni] become less productive, much so. This again raises medical and sick leave costs, and again reduces the profit margin. Keep workers relatively stress free, happy and productive - and they will pay off in income per worker big time. Enlightened self-interest based capitialsm, learn it - love it. As long as the "rebel" clans pay their fair share - let them have their northern zones. Let them pay the liability claims of disgrunteled miners, the insurance, ICC work safety compliants, surprise inspections and the complaints of abuse (and there will be, whether its deserved or not). Mining is a tough, dirty and dangerous business and always will be. Because of this many of us to sympathise with some of the grieviences of the clanners and wish to see this resolved and establish safe, equitable and profitable transit through all territories through Ruki-Ka for all it's citizens. Omni-Tek will become a stronger and more profitable corporation because of it.

The stipulations I would require of the "Council of Truth" is to somehow help defray the costs of ongoing Terra-Forming of the planet and to negotiate a *reasonable* percentage of Notum sales from the northern zones based on a permenant sub-lease from Omni-Tek. I believe there are no provisions against this in Omni's lease with the ICC. In the end, we both get what we want and quite frankly perhaps more. Any taxes or duties in the northern territories would be strictly between the Council of Truth and it's citizens. Omni would deal with the Council as the Corporate/Political level, Council would need to continue it's own policing and such - but we could conduct joint military ventures as possible. Well perhaps getting far to ahead of ourselves, the Athens Accord is the first step in a long process towards prosperity for all.

Omni-Tek is your friend,

Avatarius
