On RK-1, I have a 74 twink that fights duels 100% self-buffed. In all but 1 of my 20 or so fights, my opponent has agreed to duel self buffed, and I had no reason to believe they werent. Not everyone on RK-1 is scum : )

Also, I dual wield Gamma Ejectors (radiation damage), so I light up with a little smile when I see carbonum armor. My advice would be to mix and match armor to get a good overall AC spread.
Don't totally abandon carbonum, just don't wear a full set.

I agree with the implant suggestion - you should be able to get higher implants in. Try tweaking your implants to get a skill req that matches your highest abilities. I adjust all of my implants to be either agility or stamina. Also, you can get into higher implants by using 'stepping' implants. Usually if you get a set of stepping treatment implants and/or stepping agility/stamina implants, then you can get much higher.

In terms of the damage question, it's probably a weapon of choice issue. Although I don't have the exact specs on a QL 28 Ithaca, I believe it's a crit dependent gun. Most of the people you're fighting have maxed their evades and I doubt you are carrying enough crit%+ to see results. You might try switching to a shotgun that carries a better normal damage.

Good luck to ya : )
