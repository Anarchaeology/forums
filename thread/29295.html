Sneaking and Invisibility
First off id like to say ive tested this while doing ipr and with 5 different second parties(as well as a 3rd in a few instances)

If someone(1) sucsesfully sneaks and disapears(lets say 1 has 2000 conceal) and someone else(2) canot see them(lets say 2 has 6 perception);
If 2 zones then comes back he can see 1 even though he still sneaking.
If 2 runs around a corner or just out of the line of sight of 1 then back in the line of sight he can see 2.
If 1 is out of the line of site of 2 and sneaks then becomes in the line of site of 2, 2 will always see 1.
If 1 sneaks then zones to the same zone as 2, 2 will always see 1.
If 1 is in a PvP countdown and tries to sneak 2 will always see 1.
Ok now lets say 1 has 200 in conceal and 2 has 200 in perception.
If 1 sneaks sucsessfully and dissapears from 2. 2 will not be able to find him with the search button.

I tested this while doing ipr with skills rangeing from conceal skill 40-2000; and perception skill from 30 to a little over 1000
