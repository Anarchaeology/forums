So much land and no life!
I feel in the future the dev team should enhance how you interact with the world. The first step is to enhance tradeskills. I am tired of the idea of kill kill kill. Ok this is a war, but can we have something to keep us interested? Something we can use to roleplay? Why are missions the only means of surviving? ugh I hate missions and being forced to do them is sick.

Ok so you added a bunch of neat things for people who want to profit on making nano crytsals. But must it be impossible? Why aren't there mines in which carbon ore can be dug out?

Little things like this would make Rubi-ka a more interesting place. I would like to see actual miners going out of town, not to fight, but to actually mine some materials for tradeskills. It would be nice to see slave droids helping these miners.

How about player owned shops? I hope these things are at least in mind with the dev team.
