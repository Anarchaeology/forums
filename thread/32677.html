Another anti-pathing mission type to look into
Cosmik has mentioned that they want to know what kinds of rooms/mission types cause pet pathing problems so they can fix them like they fixed the dreaded cave missions. I would like to nominate the indoors mission set-up where the floors curve upward at the walls in halls and some rooms. I have pets getting stuck in that floor/wall curve all the time. They will just stand there with their feet below the floor graphic and will not move for /pet follow, /pet behind or /pet attack. In these types of missions I have to /pet terminate at least 2-3 times.

Though the biggest pet pathing problem right now is that they can't find the dang door. That's why some groups are now preferring not to bring pet users into missions.
