Guild Bank needed
Hi,

Having the responsability, in my guild, to keep any item for the guild, and having the guild size raising rapidly (as up to now 65 different members), it'll be difficult to manage all the stuff i try to keep for the guild, and give it back to lower levels. But this way of doing is very important for a guild, this help our members to save a lot of money.
I propose to rapidly allow a special Bank account for any guild created, and also ameliorate the bank. The simpliest way, after having thougt about a lot, would be to have a kind of "Windows Explorer" tool where any people using the Guild Bank (and why not allowing this to anybody's bank ?) can create/delete/modify folders, and put in there all the stuff. This would ease so much a lot of things. I bet some people will ask this for their own bank.

Try to thing twice about it, I dont think this would require a long development period
