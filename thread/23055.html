Chaotic Order (RK1/Clan) - Open Recruitment
We are a small-sized guild, with an active (nightly) core of 40s-50s and a couple of individuals in their 80th level of achievement. We are mostly a social guild and group up regularly (but not exclusively) to go perform tasks for the clans. Many of us are RL friends or have come from other games together. If this sounds like the kind of family that you would like to be a part of, please give myself or one of my officers a message in game.
