You got it griemdaall, but nobody seems to be able to figure it out. A team wrangle, ESPECIALLY the higher level ones will VASTLY improve how they perform, it is BETTER than a damage buff, depending on the level of the mob your group is facing, it is a crit buff too. How good is a +96 team wrangle? Well, about every 4 attack rating increases the damage your weapon does by 1%. So say i'm a fixer using a mausser which is 100% smg, every point in smg is 1 attack rating. So I get 96 more attack rating from the wrangle. That is 24% more damage my mausser does. Damage buffs are NOT affected by attack rating. Whether I have 50 attack rating or 800 attack rating, it only increases my damage by what it says. So a wrangle ESPECIALLY at higher levels is much better than a damage buff. And since the higher your attack skill is in comparison to a mobs evades, decides how much you will crit, if the wrangle raises your attack enough, you are critting more too.

Sure, the trader is sacrificing HIS ability, but in return he greatly enhances the effectiveness of up to 5 teammates. The damage dealers do more damage and possibly crit more. The doc can use better healing nanos, the mps/engis can use better pets, the NT's can use better nukes, everyone benefits, even the trader because now his uber friends can kill stuff faster which means he gets exp faster.

But if the traders want to go on being naive of this or feel like they are giving away something for free that they could be making money off of...well, they're just missing out.
