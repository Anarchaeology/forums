My two cents
Personally I would tend to agree that killing your own guards is bad in almost all cases. OK you're bored and you off a guard to see if you can do it, fine. I like to test my level sometimes too (Usually by taking on a deep red of some type shortly after leveling. Then if I die I don't lose much.) KrazyKanuk whose n00b got shot by a guard and brought back an Alt and toasted the guard, I fully understand thier position and approve. That guard was certainly out of line and deserved to be toasted. Other than that it is basically showing off and does not impress me at all.

However I don't see the problem with killing enemy guards. I have seen groups of Omni's frying guards all over Tir and then heading over to Newland City to continue their killing spree. In their wake are dozens of people *****ing and moaning. Why? (BTW I am sure the Omni's have seen clanners doing the same in Omni 1 etc.) If they are showing off, so what, if they are RP'ing more power to them. I don't whine when someone kills roller rats (leets yes, but they are cute and cuddley and killing them is a shame unless they attack you first ). I sit back and watch them, sometimes they are a mob and you realize that level doesn't equal skill, other times they are well organized and working as a team and it is a joy to watch.

My favorite was a group that had an Engie, Adv. and a doc (there were two other tank types there but I didn't identify them). The Doc was doing his job and watching the health, the Bot was tanking, and the adv. was helping and watching the doc's health. It was a ballet of death and destruction. A beautiful thing. I look forward to the day when my Guildmates and I can go to Omni-1 and put on a Clan performance there. I only hope we are as coordinated and deadly in our ballet de morte.

Remember death is not permanent and I am sure the guards get to use the save terminals for free right before their shift begins

And yes, when we hit Omni-1 you can be sure that it will be listed as a surgical or terrorist type strike to protest the abuses of the evil Omni corporation. RP'ing is the best reason to kill enemy guards (although that revenge thing rates closely).
