Is there a way to get the time and place of GM events?
I'd like to propose that they be some kind of forum or thread explained the activities of the Greenies (GM's) and how others could get involved with them. I always read about how this happened here and that happened yesterday, but it seemed that only the people who happen to stumble on the event are actually part of it.

Since this is the only type of story related things going on, besides player done ones that only members of the RP'ing guild are involved in, it would be awesome if a posting of time/place of a GM event (or even a hint of one) were thrown up in a thread where any and all could get involved. Maybe not so much as posting the exact time, but like maybe a riddle to give a clue about an event time and location.

Just an idea.
