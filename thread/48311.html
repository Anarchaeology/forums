The Fixer Code Learn it While You're Young
Ok this message is for all fixers, but specificaly those on RK2. We Fixers there especially the old school fixers have a code that has been around since the first days of the game when we were all gimped and hanging in Newland facing lag and even harder times soloing. We fixers began to form friendships that transcended sides. We were there for one another and over a period of time we all formed a code. All of us fixers from the old school live by this code for the most part so I think its fair to let the new fixers that have influxed the community in on them.

1. You help a fellow fixer regardless of side out in any way that is with in your means.

2. You never attack a fellow fixer. If you duel a fellow fixer always give them the option of leaving the battle if it looks like they will die. Dont kill a fixer if you dont have too.

3. You never lie to a fellow fixer, nor cheat them. Honesty among fixers is one thing that is prized above all.

4. If a fixer is with his/her friends they should be treated with the same respect you would give your fixer brother/sister.

5. Fixers must have fun at all times its the one thing fixers should never forget.

I know these little rules sound corny but they have been in place for a long time and have helped structure a community of fixers that are awesome people to talk to. Next time you see an old school fixer say hi if your a youngin. You'll see what I mean by the code of the fixers. For the most part all the fixers on RK2 strive to live by this code. The only thing I've seen that happens is when you reach 200th you seem to be drawn to the darkside where you no longer care about your friends and find wealth and greed to be your new ally's.

Ok thats enough of this. I love all the ingame messages I get so please dont stop sending them but keep in mind I'll get 20 to 30 messages in a minute while in combat, so If I dont respond back very fast dont be offended I'ld never ignore you I am just in Tell Hell.
