A ref is and icon which carries info about the original.
Target a person, hit the appropriate hotkey and you will get a a pawn-shaped ref/icon in your Knowledge View inventory. Shift-click the ref and you can reference the info that was available from the person's ID box. Similar works for items.
To make a kill mission. Take a ref of Bob and place it in the objective ref slot of the Mission Creation window.
