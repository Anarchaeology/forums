Nerfing Low Light Targeting Scope
We're going to nerf the critical chance bonus on the old Low Light Targeting Scope to be equal to the Targeting Scope - Vision Enhancer.

We're looking into adding other bonuses to the Low Light Targeting Scope, to ensure that it stays as valuable as it is now. Suggestions for this is appreciated, and will be brought to the designers.

Open thread. Feel free to discuss.

Possible Suggestions
1) add 1 million credits per QL per scope that each character has; or
2) add a hefty XP bonus that scales with the scope QL; or
3) reduce the init penalty of the old scopes by a good amount; or
4) 2 IPR per character to reset WS/EE (since the new scopes aren't worth the IP); or
5) +2 point to all ranged skills per QL of scope (using 5 QL chunks like the LLTS and ELLTS already does). Having +400 to a ranged skill (ql195-200 scope) isn't going to replace the crit chance, but it will give you 40 more points of dmg (roughly); or
6) more than one of the above.
