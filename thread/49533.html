Rotating server Reset times?
I doubt this will ever happen but I may as well start a thread & see what goes down.

I live in New Zealand (GMT) I have seen a few ppl cry about the servers going down during there playing times (peek) But what about us, the ones who play in offpeek? We are constantly hit with server resets of 3 hour downtimes at least once if not twice a week, this comes slap bang in the middle of our peek playing times.. every week!
I am not complaining about the resets themselves, I do know it is a needed part of the system to keep the servers running smooth but here is my ask......... can we have a rotating system where one reset is GMT & the other is at a different time?

Bahahahahahahah yeah I know we have a *bleepn* bleep* show of having this done, but hey guys & gals... just spare a thought for us who constantly have our gaming time interrupted so you can play uninterrupted.
