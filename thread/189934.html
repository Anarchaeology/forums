Newland City Benefit and Fundraiser (EVENT! Rimor)
Event! Newland City Benefit and Fundraiser (Slave Auction)

Date has changed:

On Saturday, November 1st, 18:00 GMT there will be a party and benefit to help fund ‘city improvements’ in Newland City; held in Reets Retreat to accommodate the crowd that is sure to attend. It has been proposed that it is lack of funding that may be responsible for the city’s current state of low economy; therefore all funds raised will be presented to the city in good faith.

Now for the fun part, not only will there be dancing and bumping elbows with people of every faction, you will have the opportunity to bid on them to ‘own’ them for day! Yes, that’s right, volunteers from all over Rubi-Ka will put themselves up on the block for this worthy cause. Need someone to clean the dust bunnies out of your apartment, buy yourself a little helper. Need a shiny new set of gear, buy a fleet of fixers! Always wanted to march around like a real member of Omni Pol? Bid on a set on a set of Omni’s finest and ask them to march in time.

Dress in your finest, and come with some spending credits for an evening of fun and lighthearted entertainment. Hope to see everyone there.

-Fali

--------------------
OOC Event notes: All ‘slaves’ will have the ultimate discretion as to what they will do or won’t do. Sexual harassment or abuse of the good nature of any slaves purchased won’t be tolerated and it will result in the loss of your slave and no refund. Please come ready for some light hearted RP and lots of fun!

Those that wish to volunteer their time as a slave please contact me in-game or leave a message in the ooc thread here: http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...hreadid=189935

All final purchase prices will be noted and posted here after the event, who will be the top seller? We shall see!
