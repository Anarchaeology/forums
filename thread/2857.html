I've never complained about MAs before as a class because crats and MPs and such have had such a legitimately hard time in "balance" issues, but here we go:

Thank God I have my healing programs, because they're the only way I can survive a fight at lvl 40 with a light orange mob. I know that at high levels yellows and limegreens will give you a run for your money in any class, but it was my understanding that pre lvl50 was supposed to be a cakewalk.

And it used to be for my MA, too. Now it takes several nano-stims worth of self-healing just to get through a fight with half of my HP left.

I'm not a powergamer, as can be shown through the fact that I still have a lvl40 as my main char after a couple months of play, but the combat effectiveness of the MA has been degrading steadily over the course of the last five or six patches.

Please slow down the downward slide of the MA. With the improvements in mob ACs and damage output, I'm performing at lvl40 about as well as I was at lvl30, though I've kept my MA stats pegged all along.

I'd much rather see XP awards go down than for the mob difficulties to get any worse.
