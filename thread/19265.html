Great Idea for implementation to enhance gameplay!
I think that there should be a option in the team menu to turn on a 'Damage Calculator' if wanted..

Possibly it would even show how much damage mob did to whom.. At least an option.
This would give us something to base optimized groups on and excellent for improving yourself!
Would also add an ever so slight needed added enjoyment to the monotany* of current grouping. We could have tiny short little games of trying to outdamage each other and such. Seems like fun to me.

Just a thought that I personally would Love to see implemented..

Bad Idea. Almost as bad as giving titles for solo PvP.

You can't base and individual's performance in a team based on damage. Just like how people try to gank and prey on weak foes to get their so-called titles, people will be all about doing 'damage' to put themselves in a better light if anything like this ever was implemented.

Doctors would be more interested in crit buffs and getting more rounds out of their shotties. MA's would be less selective when timing their Dimaches. Our bureaucrat would be more into Nuking and less into crowd control in order to not look like a drag on the group.

The best evaluation of group performance comes from the group's impressions themselves.

Now if you want to evaluate your OWN perfomance during a day of play, I think that a chat log parser similar to the one I used to use for DAoC would be a great 3rd party tool that would be readily supported by the community.

Knowing you average damage versus certain MOB's, number of Fumbles, average special damage, true Crit percentage, etc. is all available by parsing the chat log. This knowledge could actually be useful when developing your character.
