Yep. It's not just you.
Those are three of the four on the short list: A-x000 Soldiers and Elites, Lifebleeders (et seq), Tentacle Mutants, and Parpsalums (et seq).

The soldier bots have more hit points than a building, phenomenal armor, and every other attack does double damage.

Lifebleeders do radiation damage, check for crits versus your Nano Resist (I'm told) so they crit like mad, and hit at 1.0/1.0 speed.

Parpsalums do poison damage, have 1.0/1.0 speed, and insane range.

Tentacle mutants do chemical damage (which stops being a problem once you get real armor on), and every other attack does double damage.

They mentioned over in Community Corner that the designers have been encouraged to rethink how they "con" these. No word on when, if ever, anything will be done about this, but Funcom has been made aware of the problem - vociferously.
