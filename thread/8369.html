Funcom should make it so you ca alwats reset your IP's but give some time/level/cost limit. You would first have to unequip everything, including armor, implants, bracers, and token boards, you would only be able not to unwear certain rings. You would then have to issue a command, and then the next time you die, all of your skills would be reset with the exception of Map Navigation (unless they design it to reset your maps too, which brings me to another point, but I will get to that at the bottom)
After your resurrection effects wear of, you could be able to take all those wasted IP from Pistol and put in Shotgun or whatever, I personally would not put any into Parry and ot as much into Physic Init. It would give you the chance to reevaluate your character, and perhaps come out alot more able than before. Most of us have at least one character that we regret putting IP into certain things, and as of that, we play that character less. Perhaps some time limit like 24 hours of (semiactive) Playtime. Or perhaps you would have to gain a level (not easy for 100ish+), or induce a fine of some sort depending on your level (perhaps 50,000 CR per level). But don't forget, resetting IP's would be a pain in the first place, as ALL of your main Attributes require to be raised beforehand.
As for Map Navigation, well, if this skill would be reset on an IP reset, you would have the command you issue erase allmaps except for the starter maps. Or perhaps implement someway that maps do not work if you do not have that certain amount of skill. I see people now take a set of Map Nav implants and upload all the maps they can, this is time and money consuming as well as the fact that it does not raise Map Nav too high, so I do not seee this as a Spoil/Exploit/whatever.
