An Engineer's Diary
An Engineer's Diary:

21/08/302002 - 10:23:21
Finally I have been able to gather the components for my new robot, and the plans for its brains matrix. Soon, the days of a stucked and run-away robot are over! Those factory made nano's is as flawed as ever. Now its time to clean up the errors.

21/08/302002 - 14:40:07
I made it home, no thanks to my current robots help. Ran into a mad OT mob and sent the bot at him...and saw the damn mechanical piece of junk run the other way and get stucked in a table. I ran my arse off and escaped the mob. Then I terminated the bot, the sight of it made me sick, and I would assemble a new one anyway, a new and IMPROVED one!

21/08/302002 - 16:10:18
After getting some rest and something to eat, I sat down and started to program the new brain implant for the robot. Using the same component as the original, I improved the bots logical functions, making it smarter. This way the damn thing wont run the wrong way, and get confused.

22/08/302002 - 00:06:55
Finally! The brain has been reprogrammed and is finished now. I have installed it into the new bot-shell. I have activated it and it responded perfectly, avoiding all obstacles I have laid in its way, and acts like I want it too! Now to the finally test: The Field Test!

22/08/302002 - 06:10:34
Its with great difficulty I write this, since im laying in bed, warped into nano-bandages all over. The test was a catastrophic, I can't believe it, and there can only be one reason! I got to the mission area with the bot and saw the huge OT mob and I ordered the bot on it, and stepped forward to attack...The mob attacked and I realised that the bot was nowhere! Getting almost beaten to death, I managed, finding the bot waiting outside the mission area.
After examine it I have only one conclusion about the bots: They are NOT stupid, they act the right way. They KNOW that the mob is going to beat the crap out of them, thats why they run! Its not an error or anything...DAMN technology!

24/08/302002 - 11:33:43
Got out of bed and been reprogramming the bot again. Its stupid but I think it might work. Of course I will miss get toast in the morning for a while, but I can manage it. Going to buy a new one later.

24/08/302002 - 11:35:17
Threw the rest of the dismantled toaster out.

* Diary end *

Footnote: Still problems with bot, when passing the bakery. Check into the problem later.
