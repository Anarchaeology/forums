Idea for concealment missions
Whoa, ok, let's think about this...

A check box for a mission. Concealment, or not. Change the rules a bit if it's a concealment mission...

Make it HUGE, like MASSIVE

Make the mobs a touch less responsive to a broken conceal if it's immediately re-initiated

Make it so you can only take one mission at a time, make the reward more balanced, say, worth 2 other missions...

Basically, find a way to force people to take a similar amount of time doing concealment missions as someone else would have to spend doing another type of mission, but make it worth it...

And if that box isn't checked, then concealment works the way it does now. On grays. Perhaps a touch less effective even.

Just a thought, but one with potential, perhaps?
