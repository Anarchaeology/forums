The Omni-Entertainment Bombing (The Great OT Lie)
By now everyone has either heard about or seen the after effects of the bombing of the appartment complex in the city of Omni-Entertainment. It is relatively easy for the ignorant OT executive, soldier, policeman, or citizen to point an accusatational finger at 'the enemy'... The Clans.

Afterall, the Clans have been known to kill innocent OT employees and nuetrals at the drop of a hat... So they must obviously be the culprit?

Let me attempt to show you the situation in a different light.

Omni-Entertainment is an area covered by a high concentration of Supression gas. As we all know, Supression gas inhibits one's agression and prevents he or she from inflicting injury uppon another living creature. The density of the Supression gas determines the limits that one is able to express agression and the ability to inflict harm upon other living beings. In areas where the ratio is 25% gas and 75% 'natural' air it is possible for one to attack another living human based solely upon the differing political and philosophical outlook. At 75% gas to 25% normal air, it is possible to attack wildlife, but not other human beings. At 100% supression gas no attacks are possible.

Consider this for a moment, Omni-Entertainment is an area under the affects of 100% Supression gas. This means that there is no possible way for one to inflict harm upon another living being within the city.

This leads to the conclusion that the bombing is either a full fabrication composed of falsity, or that the levels of the Supression gas were somehow lowered in Omni-Entertainment during this bombing... Now ask yourself, who has the ability to lower the levels of Supression gas within Omni-Entertainment?

Omni-Tek...

Are they truly your friend?
