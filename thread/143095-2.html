Re: Combat pass is great idea!
quote:
Originally posted by Nozick - WMUB!
Anyway, I still like the Combat Pass the best, it'd just need a PvP-level check on each Nano-effect.
Again, thinking as a programmer, I believe that would put a lot of overhead into the program. Because in order to perform that check when we equip the combat pass, realistically speaking the PvP level check routine will have to be tagged onto the "wear item" routine, and that routine is most likely be executed every time an item is worn. Therefore, the wear item routine will have to check to see if the item being worn is the "combat pass" and then if so, perform the buffs check.

However, tagging the buffs check routine onto the PvP flagged routine will guarantee the buffs check is performed only when it's needed. It's also only logical to do any kind of PvP related routines when a player is PvP flagged.

I like the combat pass idea too and it would definitely solve more than just low level outside-buffed twinks. But still I believe it's more complicated to implement than simply performing a check when a character is PvP flagged.
