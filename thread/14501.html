Fixer Shadow Attendant
Saw a post below and instantly thought it would be referencing this. Since it didn't I decided to slap this idea together (it might be a bit rough around the edges)

Standard Fantasy RPG element is to have a mirror image of yourself to fool your opponent.

With the Fixer being so in tune with the Grid (hell, when he's in Grid Armor he's not even on the planet) and them supposed to be so 'shadowy' wouldn't it be fair to give them the ability to manifest some sort of 'clone' of themselves to aid them in messing up their enemies?

I mean, if they are really in the Grid while they are in Grid Armor and what we see is only a beacon, why can't there be another (less powerful, limited) beacon created? Seems to be right up the alley for one who is supposedly fixed in the land of deception and shady dealings.

Imagine the PvP benefits to the Fixer if it were implemented in such a way as to be able to send in your doppelganger and while the opponent was fixated on the false image start unleashing roots or bursts as your tactics dictate.

We want all professions to be somewhat unique. Heck it was the Fixers that got screwed most by having their abilities given away. Therefore, we don't want them to be another 'pet' class (hey I play two of the pet classes). Now DAoC did have one cool class, the Theurgist, he made pets that were shortlived and essentially disposable.

I would propose:

- that the doppelganger look exactly like it's creator and show the creator's 'Info' window when targetted (who?, huh? which one do I attack?)
- have the same gear as far as feasible by the game
- mimic the creator's skills to a certain degree
- be almost insta-cast and low cost
- maybe hit the creator for some lifepoints upon creation
- lock a seldom used skill like maybe MattCrea for 5 minutes.
- be limited to 30 second or so duration
- only accept 'guard' and 'attack' commands

Nice feature that doesn't unbalance the game, fits within the profession's profile, and best of all can probably be implemented within most of the game's current codebase.

Hope I'm not speaking out of turn by proposing this feature for another class. I just thought it kind of fit those guys and would add a unique element to the game and variety and freshness to those that play that profession.

Is it my post you are talking about?

I posted a similar idea. I was talking about what I thought the agent hologram device might do.

Now that I think about it, they should make a fixer device that creates a mirror image of yourself.

This thing would basically project a double of yourself off to your side. This illusionary double moves in perfect sync with you and preforms all the actions you do (such as running or firing).

For realism and balance purposes you will NOT see any bullets coming out of the hologram double. In PvP this is how they will distinquish the real from the fake.

I though of this device as more of a solo'ing tool, not a PvP tool.

When activated the clone has a chance of drawing argo away from you. The QL of the item determines how often, and how long, the clone can draw argo off you.

This would be a powerful balancing tool for fixer's in combat as the very high QL ones could effectively double your lifespan because your clone draws argo 50% of the time.

Nah, Zero, check the date on my post above. Just another example of an idea that fits the profession and helps them out within the framework that FunCom painted for them that gets no response from FunCom or the intended audience.

Reducing damage to the genuine article and allowing him to damage the target with impunity until he's flushed out of the shadows.
