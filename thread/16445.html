In Game Movie Theaters and Gambling!
My first post! Hey everybody! I am having a blast playing AO, with all of it's flaws, it's still the best thing going, and I've tried them all!

OK, here are my crazy ideas that will probably never get implemented. Why not have some kind of futuristic movie theaters, (with an AO/Rubi-Ka flavor), that have real streaming video playing on the screen? FUNCOM could buy the rights to crappy horror movies or some such, edit them down to little, grainy, shorts and stream them into the game. How cool would it be to meet your friends in a dingy movie theater in OMNI-E or Tir and make fun of some bad slasher flick? Bandwidth hog? sure it is, but it would be cool!

GAMBLING! I have seen tantalizing hints that FUNCOM maybe intends to put gambling parlors in the game. I have seen store signs that say, "Shop of Fortune", or, "Casino", but still no way to play blackjack or craps in the game. That is something all MMORPGS have missed out on is the ability to play little "mini-games" with others or against the casino for money. You could play chess in UO, and that was great, but we can do better than that. How about an all night game of poker with your guildmates? All night poker in a movie theater?

Anyway, dream on. Thanks everybody, and I'll see you in RK2!

bugs...
>>"gambling is a good idea, movie theatres... ehh no. but bugs should come first, just keep that in mind"<<

Well, the movie theaters are useless, sure, but so is Baboons and the other "dance" clubs and bars. It's just another thing to do in the cities that allows for more roleplaying. Think about it, the citizens of Rubi-Ka have to do something more than drink and dance for entertainment! And, it shows a little more animation in the backgrounds and adds atmosphere, which the game already has in droves.

As for bugs coming first, well, there will always be bugs in a game of this size and complexity... always. Because there will always be updates and content additions, new bugs will always be introduced when the old ones are stamped out. That's just the nature of it. If they only worked on bugs, nothing else would ever get done!

The live teams of all these games have special departments of coders that hunt down bugs. These people are not slowed down by the other parts of the team that work on World Developement, or class balance, etc. Everyone always thinks that if a MMORPG works on some new dungeon or something, their favorite pet peeve is being neglected because of it. Relax, there are more than two guys working at FUNCOM.
