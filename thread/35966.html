Are you looking for a few good men? Then look no further.
Attrition over the past couple of months has seen the number of active members in the Southern Cross guild reduced to a mere handful. Rather than recruit more members, a simpler option is for us to close shop and offer our services to a worthy Omni-Tek guild.

All our players are located in Australia, so ideally we would anticipate joining a guild which has a reasonable number of players located in the Australia-Pacific area, say GMT +7-12. Or just have players who never sleep.

We are active AO citizens who enjoy participating in guild events. Men of action, we like nothing more than a good fight. Although, being Aussies, the occasional beer isn't bad either.

Being long time associates, we'd like to migrate en masse to a new guild. We would envisage transferring our main characters to the new guild and perhaps, after a settling in period, a select few active alts.
