RUR (Rubi-Ka Universal Robots)
Omni-Tek, Rubi-Ka 1

RUR is a new, small and somewhat dysfunctional robotics company fully owned by its members. RUR's hopes of success in the marketplace rest on its innovative designs, first among which is the "Quantum Positronic Thingy"(QPT) brain its robots come endowed with. This gives the robot a greater and more intuitive type of intelligence, which in turn makes for improved human-robot communications. It also has drawn upon RUR the unwelcome attention of competitors, cyborgs and worse of all, creditors.

/ooc
The purpose of this guild is to run roleplaying adventures with a robotic theme. The guild was conceived by a group of veteran AO roleplayers looking for fresh roleplaying opportunities for new or unused characters.

Our aim is to outward looking and structure these adventures in a way that non-RUR roleplayers can easily join in the fun without having to commit too much time to it.

Just to be clear:

* We do not asking people to play NPCs or "monsters" for us. Nor do we ask them to work from a script. What we offer are genuine roleplaying hooks for your own characters to do, well, whatever it is your character deems appropriate.

* You do not need to join RUR to take part. In most instances your character will be hired by RUR for a specific job as an external consultant, contractor or whatever. This will not effect you current guild status.

* These are not be not PvP events.

Oh course, if you do want to join RUR, that can be arranged too.

Anyway come visit our Website. If nothing else, its worth a giggle I think.

Red Tape of Omni-Admin
Argentum, 4-Holes, RK-1(Omni)

Desc:
Red Tape is an Omni-Admin affiliated organization that coordinates with other Omni departments and organizations into working as a cohesive whole. The old notions of warfare have completely changed since death isn't fatal on Rubi-Ka. Brute strength alone will not forge a lasting solution. A lasting solution can be achieved through diplomacy and moderation. We work with those that have the foresight to realize it.

Obj:
An organization that is active in Rubi-Ka's politics. Our group of talented employees work dilligently to help secure a bright prosperous future for the company and its employees, helping make a difference in the world for the betterment of the planet's citizens.

History:
Founded on November 24, 29475. Officially affiliated with Omni-Admin on Sep 10, 29476. Member of the Omni-Tek Security Council (OTSEC) which played a part to help bring the Dust Brigade menace under control.
