Tab a teammate
Hi all.

I got a question. I know Clans and OT's are suppose to be enemies, but we can still team and do missions, hunts if we wants to. Consider that this is possible you could say that everyone in the team are 'friends' So I wonder why the tab funciton in missions/hunts still select a member of your team that is of another affiliation.

Wouldn't it be easy enough for FC to make a check on the tab and only select those outside of the team?

I am clan and if I team with a neutral or OT the team side goes to neutral which means that the mobs in the mission will be from all factions. But a clan mob attacks me even if I am not fireing first, so the clan mob see's me as an enemy. But before I can fire back I have to either tab a few times or ctrl-tab in case the mob is clan. Selecting the mob using the mouse ain't always that easy since everyone in the team that fights tries to find a location where they can fight the mob.

So. Is it possible to remove eìther the possability to team outside your side or remove the tab cycle for those in your team?
