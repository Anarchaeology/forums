Angel of Night Inbalance.

Like... Angel of Night.... AE damage thingy..... one click u damage multiple mobs for a mediocre amount of damage.. then u'd need to wait for quite a long time until u can use it again.

Now why wouldn't anyone use it then?

I'd rather use "flower of life" when i'm fighting 3 orange enforcers and get my reflection shield going...

Angel of Night is nothing more then eye-candy as to impress group members who ignore u half of the time anyway......

My suggestion: Make Angel of Night a AE damage "Attack of the Snake" with exacly the same Re-use, but 3 times the Nano consumption.

If i use angel of night 3 times, then there is no way i can kill the other 2 orange enforcers. U cannot kill an NPC with 5 shots of "Attack fo the snake" because u'll be dead by then if u don't attack using ur normal MA skills, thus meaning the other 2 enforcers which have ganged up on u, won't die..so it won't hurt game balance.

This change would make "Angel of Night" more spectacular and useful.

And if u think it's 2 strong, no way..cuz Minimal damage would be 1. Spread out the damage to the 3 orange enforcers evenly.

Like: Enforcer 1: 120 damage
Enforcer 2: 1 damage
Enforcer 3: 34 Damage

I cannot keep chain using this attack cuz i'ma run out of nanoes and that's expsnesive to upkeep. So u see what i mean? It would be a nerf in itself not to make the attack too powerful .i'd rather conserve nano points to heal msyelf.
