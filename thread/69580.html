Improving the tradeskill system
The backround for writing this post is that I feel the tradeskill system in AO is flawed and is in great need of improvement. Judging from other posts and ingame conversations, this is an opinion many players share with me.

In AO there are two possible ways to get experience. One is to kill NPCs and the other is to use tradeskills. The first is vastly superior to the second, and is in reality the only viable route.

How can this be changed?
A fact of MMORPGs - and a fact that has been stated several times in FunCom articles - is that there must be a risk vs reward calculation. Higher risk must be equivalent with higher reward. One important issue that is hidden in that statement is what the players risk to lose. The obvious answer is xp, and in case of PvP items as well. But both of those are in reality only an investment in time. It is time the players do not want to lose, and it is with as little time as possible the players want to advance their characters.

Dealing with tradeskills, there is no risk involved. You have a strict border with two possible outcomes: failure and success. Failure leads to no loss at all (except a few seconds to click on the objects) and success as a consequence is rewarded with almost no xp at all. So to get a higher reqard for tradeskills, there must be some risk involved.

Now for some of my ideas for how to do this.
The first way that springs to mind is to implement the already existing overequiping rules. With 100% of the needed skills meaning 100% chance of success, 80% giving 80% success rate and so on. But in the case of failure, there should be a penalty. Depending on how the dice roll (as it really is in the background) turns out, one would have varying degrees of failure. A small miss would not have any consequenses, but 'rolling' far below what was needed could result in the loss of the items involved, destruction of the tools involved or even death in case of assembling weapons.

The problem I see with this is that no one would ever try assembling items that lead to a less than 100% success rate. Another option is to simply let item assembly take time. One would then invest time, and could be rewarded as such. The obvious problem here is that it will be simply boring...

So how can it be done then? Perhaps a combination of the two? And the possibility to rule out the worst cases of failure. This could ie be done by introducing a new building for the controlled areas coming in NW. Let guilds place a "workshop" where tradeskillers can work with their items under a controlled enviroment. Assembling items in the workshop would not increase the chance of success, but would rule out the possibility of destroying the involved items.

Anyways, just a couple of my ideas. The tradeskill system need attention, and this may or may not be a good way to do it.
