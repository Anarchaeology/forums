Ie docs, crats and mps love NT's for their humidity extractor.

One notable thing about this thread, besides the complete lack of any answers from Funcom to the original questions, is that it seems that we have mission crats and hunting crats talking past each other (to oversimplify both categories). Crats, and I'm sure they are not alone in this, are played very differently in missions than when hunting. I think some of the disagreements here as to what works and what doesn't stem from this difference in playing environments.

And some of the disagreements stem from people assuming that life as a crat at level X is the same as life as a crat at level X+50 or X-50. I see this on all the profession boards. There is a big difference both in what you can do and what your opponent can do in just 20 or so levels. Heck, there was a huge difference last week in my effectiveness at level 65 vs. today at level 68. I could solo 75% missions at 65 with an ability to charm 80% of what I would find. At 68 I was run over with a steamroller in a 75% mission and had to run around like a chicken with my head cut off to find a yellow I could charm.

There are points in a crat's life where we actually feel like our profession might be implemented: When we first get Monofilament Whip, for a couple of levels after we get a new 120% charm, in those wonderful few levels after we get an increase in our IP/level and can put points in those necessary Blue skills (MapNav, Perception, RunSpeed, Conceal, FirstAid, B&E). But the rest of the time I honestly believe we are at a sharp disadvantage to other professions because of a lack of implementation and the nerfs.

Missions vs. Hunting
Scorus,

I do agree with you about the talking "past" one another, you see it in all the forums, my other "main" character is a 69 adv. , the level 30s wondering why the level 80s are complaining, etc; Obviously its FC's balance issues [profession, high vs. low game, insane mob scales, team vs. solo etc] that cause this, so it's not easily addressable.

As for charms, again I'm young but Ive been doing team missions, about 60%ish so missions are mostly orange, rare reds, some yellows. We have a trader, an engineer and myself - so we let our pets do alot of the work. I charm the yellow or orange, and our 3 pets go to town. Because were picking "easy" missions relatively speaking, the few times I've goten the charm cant be cast message I've managed to debuff the Mob then charm.

I prefer hunting in general but the pet gets slaughtered pretty easily and I could charm little or nothing. But then again the opponents were 20 levels above me, so should be able to charm? Tough call, they were mostly deep red to me, Funcom always said "Red" was to be feared and probably should always win in a solo situation, or certainly have the odds stacked in its favor.

But - because of overequipping and the desire to be an important part of the team - as crats we seem to be in an akward position. We can "Uber twink" to some extent with implants, we can have Nano Masteries placed upon us before missions, but we cannot "overequip" our pets, DD's or charms on an ongoing or extended basis. Engineers are in a similar unfortunate position with their bots.

So what do we do? I like this idea: "A powerful Psychic debuff", its reasonable anyways and would be a quick fix that could easily be put into the next patch as a short term solution; But why should "monsters" be high in psychic anyways?! I would think human NPC's would naturally be high and creatures would naturally be low. At any rate; I agree if we are to be more viable in full teams, or charms need to be more doable when hunting team type targets [which is almost always red to us and beyond our psy checks].
