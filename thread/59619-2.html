quote:
Originally posted by Martek
They should not do this important addtiion half-way. If they're going to post loot messages, then they should also implement alpha-looting (and other game-enforced team-loot options) on chests.

Otherwise chests will become the point of contention. With a body, well if it was my turn to loot, the team may have previously agreed that whatever you loot is yours - rare or not. But with chests you do not have that game-enforced "random fairness". It's whomever gets to that chest first.

Without a game-enforced system for alpha or leader looting on chests, there will be a lot of conflict about whose turn it was, who the rare or otehr item belongs to, etc.

There has been past discussion about how to implement such a system. Yes it would be different, maybe harder, to implement on chests than on mobs. The order of mob-death is the trigger for whose turn it is on mobs. Chests don't have any such trigger. But some system is necessary; especially if we are going to get loot messages.

It will be a lot easier to accept someone finding a GA disc in a chest, if I knew it was their "fair" turn, than if I thought it was simply because they were putting the needs of themselves ahead of the needs of the team by looting while others were fighting.

If we get loot messages on chests without some in-game enforcement option on whose turn it is, I think it will encourage teammate looting of chests ahead of cooperatively fighting of the mobs.

So while I am (somewhat) for the loot messages; I think they need to be coupled with some team-loot order options on chests.

I have never been able to figure out what the big deal is with ninja-looting, alph-looting, etc.

I do like this addition (though agree it would be nice to have options for it).

I guess it is just because when I team, I tend to start teams and lay down the rules up front:
-For normal items, if the character you are playing can make use of it (besides selling it) then do so. If it replaces another item (in the case of weapons/armor/etc) then offer up the item it replaced.
-If the character you are playing can not make use of an item, offer it up to see if someone else in the group can. If not, its yours if you could put it to use (on an alt or whatnot) or anyone elses if not.
-If you get a second item of a category (armor/weapons/nanos/etc) you pick which one you would rather keep and offer up the other one. If no one else needs/wants it, its yours too.
-For very rare items, those who need them the most get them so long as there are not others in the group that could make good use of it and the finder has not gotten another good rare item (if he has, then one must be offered up). If no one needs it, the finder can keep it.
-Anyone who pisses and moans about not getting an item gets booted. I dont care to spend my game time fighting over random items.

I could care less about fair as I prefer to go the route of "who can make good use of X".

Needless to say, this method helps find like minded people that are there to have a good time and help the group out and weeds out those that are there for the Ph4t Lo0t and could care less whether someone actually needs it when they could sell it instead.

I think this change will help me by virtue of letting everyone in my groups know that I am being straight up with my rules...as most times, I make it known that I loot and divvy up all the goodies. Anything no one can use I sell and split the cash up at missions end.

-Religion
