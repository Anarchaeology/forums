Re: questions about battle suits
quote:
Originally posted by spaztic
... while in battle was that I was taking the same amount of damage as with my regular armor. eventhough the battle suit had about 400 more ac in most areas. what gives with that? ...
That one's easy, if we're talking about missions or hunting. Monsters, robots, and NPCs get their level added to their minimum damage. Infinite armor won't lower damage per hit below minimum. And battle suits lower your evades. So in a mission, a battle suit will get you hurt worse than even lower-quality (but still adequate) armor. Bites, doesn't it?
