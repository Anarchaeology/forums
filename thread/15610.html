Interesting thought.
Interesting thought, but no, it won't work that way. You see, they'd have to have an OE weapon in the firstplace, and second, if you wrangle them, it would raise their skills LOWERING the % of OE on the weapon. So if they were OE'd, they'd benefit from your wrangle. Your thought would require you to wrangle them, have them OE a weapon, have the wrangle wear off.. then you might see an affect on their offensive dmg capabilities. Wouldn't work since you can't force someone to equip something.

However, if you were a Trader with an OE Shotgun for example....

If you wrangled someone else, so your skills dropped, bringing your percentage of OE above 20% or more, you would see a reduced dmg output on your SG.
