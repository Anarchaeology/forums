I think outpost takeovers could work. I posted the idea elsewhere, but here we go again:

The problem with outpost takeovers now is that you can kill all the guards you want, but fresh troops are simply warped in.

But what if outpostes in 25% and 0% supression had a "Security Office." This would be where the guards check in for their shift, get their assignments, check out APBs, etc. Wepon control (gun encampments and security droids) would also be in this office.

There would be a number to guards inside this building, and perhaps some holding cells with prisioners.

As the base siege is in progress, the ultimate goal would be to get inside and take over this security office, not an easy thing given the troops inside. (And since they're guards, you get no XP and no loot for killing them.)

Somewhere in the security office is the control system. A talented Fixer, Engineer, or someone with a high enough CompLit and B&E can access the system, and guards from the other side will warp in instead of the guards of the side that was holding the outpost.

Mission Terms and Insurance Terms would also change. (That's the real tricky part, anyone saved there would either resurect into a hostile area, or they're insurance records would have to be transfered to one of the startup cities or something)
