It's really simple. you want to know when Tarasque is up? Just type: /tell clannet respawn tara1 (or tara2, tara3, etc.).

Then when it's about 1 hour before spawn time head out to camelot and help us fight Omni. It's usually either Jdude or Marlark doing invites so seek one of them out. Stand in front of them and /greet them hello. They will join you to the private channel.
