One thing to think about, MORB: the number of item rewards is no longer fixed at one. I saw a mission on test last night that had NINE rewards. That's how I ended up with 9: I drew a 2-reward mission that, by freak of chance, had an aggresssion enhancer and a deflection shield. So it expanded to all of both.

On the other hand, the possibility of missions giving ridiculous numbers of rewards was apparently planned for, because once they all showed up, the "mission reward" part of the mission info box suddenly had its own scroll bar.
