Incentives
It is not very hard to see that FC wants get a "pull" in the market through the retail sector.

FC has not succeeded selling subscriptions in the retail sector before (when everyone can download it through the Internet). This "stunt" creates incentives for the retail sector to push not only NW, but Anarchy Online out in the shelves again - and FC will start selling more subscriptions.

I think this is a clever move from FC, IF their distributors did a good job (which obviously is not the case).
