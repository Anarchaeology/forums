OK, I have to chime in on this one...
I have been following this interesting little saga, and I must say that this entire issue has proven why proper communication is the vanguard of quality customer service.

My take on this "tempest in a teapot":

1) Kudos to the "investigative efforts" of those who brought this issue to light. Without their diligence and the resulting hue and cry, this "incident" would never have been properly addressed.

2) Make no mistake, it HAS now been properly addressed. A FC Director has posted an official confirmation of the incident as well as a company apology. While one would hope that the employee responsible has been either fired or at least "relocated" to a position within FC where s/he cannot again abuse their position, that is an internal matter and FC has no need to inform us of the specifics...(despite how cathartic it would be for all of us to know the steps that were taken).

3) Shame on those who jumped on this opportunity to bash a company or individual worker based upon very limited information. Think, people. Gather more information, make inferences and posit assumptions, but don't claim you "KNOW EXACTLY WHAT HAPPENED!!!" when you don't. Sometimes when you do that you are right...and most of the time you are wrong. Don't call people like that FC Exploit Team Lead liars when they are just doing their job and trying to help.

4) Cz's temporary ban of Merkaba715 on the main thread was 100% justified. See point #3.

5) The player running Illuvatar may, in fact, have "done nothing wrong" as some folks claim, but s/he clearly attempted to facilitate a cover-up. That in itself implies some sort of collusion, and encourages the "appearance of evil". Since s/he apparently feared reprisals and tried to downplay the incident and cover it up, that player will now have to deal with the consequences of their actions and the resulting conclusions the rest of us have drawn after witnessing the "unusual" forum self-editing spree.

6) The character Illuvatar most certainly SHOULD have been moved back to RK2. Sucks for the player, but the transfer was clearly an "ill-gotten gain", and the only fair step is to undo the misdeed. For those who are too dense to comprehend why, consider this fictitious analogy, an old ethics chestnut:
quote:
I work at a bank, where I am a head teller. I have befriended one of the bank's customers (let's call him "Examplatar"), and I like him a lot. One day while Examplatar is in the bank making a deposit, he complains to me that he doesn't have enough money to buy that new car he so desperately wants. Because I like him, and because I have easy access to all of the bank's cash, I decide to take $10,000 out of the vault and deposit it in my friend's account. Sure it's against the bank's policy, but Examplatar really wanted the money. And since the banking corporation I work for makes billions in profit every year, no one will even notice.

But someone does notice. Another customer sees Examplatar driving around in a new car, and pieces together his recent boon. The bank at first cannot figure out what happened, but eventually the vault cash is counted and the account transfer logs are checked, and my deed is discovered. The bank releases a statement about the incident, circulates a memo to all bank employees reminding them that corporate policy strictly prohibits giving "extra" cash to "some" of its banking customers, and reprimands/demotes/fires me.

Should Examplatar be required to return the $10,000?
The answer to this question is clearly "Yes". Sure, we would all like to be $10,000 richer, but when such "gifts" are gained illicitly, they MUST be rescinded, otherwise the system breaks down as there is no "down side" to being on the receiving end of such an illegal arrangement. Even though in our analogy Examplatar did nothing wrong himself, to allow him to keep the money would not only be unfair to the other banking customers who didn't have a friend working as head teller, it would also encourage a system of favoritism and partiality.

7) I want to move DrPhil to RK1. RK2 is dead. Long live RK2. But I will wait until every player has the opportunity to do the same.

8) Kudos to FC for not deleting these related threads, and to Cz for helping to keep us informed as they got to the bottom of it all. This is clearly an embarrassment for the company (as it should be), and it would have been relatively easy for FC to make these threads "go away" (Forum DB crash, anyone? ) until the furor had died down. But they have handled this incident properly and IMHO, we should all now return to our efforts of making AO a better game.

- DrPhil
