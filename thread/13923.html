Not sure that's feasible....
(Sorry SilkeX, You know I agree with you most of the time...)

With the way AO appears to handle texturing the models, I don't believe this is really very feasible. It would require layering textures and lots of Alpha Blending. From what I can see of the client program's display, it appears that texturing is done strictly on a 1 texture to 1 model-part method. They just have lots of different textures (which is why the game is so huge on your HD!)

Making the engine support multitexturing and alpha-blending on the models would make having a GeForce3 or better GPU a requirement just to run the game.

Rings could be implemented fairly easily, simply by creating 2 hand textures (maybe one, that could be mirrored) for each race/gender (one for each major TYPE of ring, each ring having it's own textures would be way too many new textures). Then they would be the 'social-armor' equivalent of gloves.

Sounds like a great idea, I just don't think the engine can (in it's current incarnation) handle such things.
