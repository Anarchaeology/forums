New Avec Leet Virtual Tour
A Virtual Tour of the Avec Leet art exhibition is now available from comfort of your homes. Just visit grid reference: http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/srsart00.htm

Details of the exhibition below.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The "Avec Leet" Collection (29,415 - 29,450)
A retrospective of Rubi-Kan painting
The Modigliani Art Gallery, Omni-Trade

Between 29,415 and 29,450 a group of Rubi-Kan painters produced a series of works collectively known as the 'Avec Leet' school. The goal of this artistic movement was quite simple, to revisit the works of the Old Masters in Rubi-Kan key by introducing the leets motif. In essence this can be seen as the Rubi-Kan artists taking possession and control of their cultural heritage. While this premise has often been dismissed by critics as 'shallow' or 'provincial' what really brought this movement to life was the sheer genius of its main exponents, like Tinka and J.J. Eek.

The first, major retrospective on 'Avec Leet' painting in twenty years is now on display at the Modigliani Gallery in Omni Trade. Visits can be arranged on appointment mailing gsoylent@hotmail.com or leaving your details below. Tours are free on Mondays and Wednesdays (18-21 GMT). A complete tour of the gallery takes between 20-40 minutes. Additional tours can be arranged for other times, but there will be a fee for these payable when the booking in is made.


Sergio "Modigliani" Portello - curator.
