Players want more clubs, bars and other "social stores"
Pretty much everyone I meet wants to go party or hang out in AO. Unfortunately people are tired of dancing at Baboon's & Reet's. Plus we need a few more of these with style here and there.

Here are my specific suggestions:

New Dance Clubs:
Old Athen (They're restoring it right? it's the biggest clan city and needs a nice hangout. Tir already has 2 anyways, time for Athen to get one)

Rome (One of the main omni cities, needs a coffee house/chapel to fit in with the motif and for the stressed execs to relax in)

Camelot (Needs a rowdy 0% bar for all the knights to, ah arm wrestle in)

Hacker's Club House (Something only accessible in Borealis with B&E or through the Fixer Grid)

Some random hideouts (Remote places that are hard to find, but interesting little getaways. Maybe some shack of a bar way out in Perpetual Wastelands with an insurance term inside )

Plus open up some more of the clubs in Omni-ent please. Baboon's could stand the competition actually admitting members.
