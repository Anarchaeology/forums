Nat, unfortunately, labeling bags like that isn't a solution without a serious overhaul of the database. The reason for this is the same reason people had to remove items from their Pioneer backpacks before they modified them with a screwdriver. Each item in the game has it's own database entry. If one single stat on that item changes, it gets a new DB entry.

Going back to my Pioneer backpack explanation: For the sake of argument, let's say that the Pioneer Backpack's database number is 10006. As soon as you modify it with a screwdriver (to get the -1% in height effect instead of +1%), it is no longer a Pioneer Backpack. Now it's a Modified Pioneer Backpack, DB entry 10007. That was why they warned people to remove all their items first.

Changing the label for backpacks would require a totally dynamic database, that is able to be written to dynamically by the client. It's the same reason that I explained in the thread a while back about people wanting pieces of paper so they could pass notes in game. It just wouldn't work with the current database.

As far as it being expansion pack materiel, well, I don't quite think they are going to rework the database. Ever. Redoing the database itself prolle wouldn't be a problem, however changing all the call routines in the game programming would take forever. In a game that is so totally dependent on items, database decisions are made during development, then they run with them for the duration of the game's life.

Good idea though. It's been kicked around for about 8 months now though. Just thought I'd let you know why not to get your hopes up about it.
