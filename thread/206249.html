'Tis the season
'Twas the night before Notum Wars,

'Twas the night before Notum Wars, when all through the game
Not a feature was different, it all seemed the same;
The guildies were teamed by the grid terms with care,
In hopes that great towers soon would be there;

The newbies were nestled all snug in their beds,
While visions of notum chips danced in their heads;
And my wife in her biomech, and I hoping for green ping,
Had just settled down for a long night of camping,

When out on Lush Fields there arose such a clatter,
I sprang from Omni-Trade to see what was the matter.
I got my X-3, from out of my bank,
Jumped into my Yalm and put on my tank.

The moon on the mist of the new-fallen rain
Too bright for my night vision goggles, the pain!
When to my wondering eyes what appears,
But a notum mine tower, and eight clanners with spears,

How foolish they were, to think they could build,
All I needed to do, was call to my guild,
More rapid than fixers my teammates they came
I tuned my communicator and called them by name;

Now Dyna! Now Jardus! Now Magbyte and Dreben!
On Kwautrawn! On Hyach! On Athawn and Bayken!
To Harrys, north of the mine! Rally at the gate in the wall!
Now blast away! Blast away! Blast away all!

As dry leaves that before a hurricane fly,
We let loose our ammo, it lit up the sky,
So up to the tower the Omni they flew,
With a pack of grenades, and a shield disabler, too.

We swarmed the tower, even jumped on its roof
The tower was damaged, it went down with a poof.
As I brought down my rifle and was turning around,
Down came a flurry of notum dust with a bound.

We sprang to our yalms, to my guild I gave a shout,
Rejoicing and commenting on the successful bout,
I sent a tell to the Sentinals, ere I flew out of sight,
THIS NOTUM IS OURS, IT IS OURS BY ALL RIGHTS!
