Moved from: http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...288#post702288

I don't want to hi-jack the thread, but you just gave me an idea.

First, I would like a large cage with an exotic animal in it. Let the animal change at some fixed interval. Sort of like a carnival side show.

Second, we have been discussing possible token board changes on other threads, not going to into that except to say.....

5000 tokens, a life size statue with your name on it, with hopefully some likeness, gets dropped randomly in Rubi-Ka.

You can pretend it was made and stolen by the opposition and dropped somewhere.

You could have your own quest to find it, with xp as a reward and you, and only you, could pick it up and transport it to display in your apartment or an approved location.

The object would be impregnated with a sensor, but a poor one. You could only detect it once you are in the correct playfield. Unfortunately, the detector can only sense presence, but no direction. So you will have to hunt the entire zone.

You can even hire a group of noobs to locate it for you, or just ask around.

At the very least, a statue would be cool, the rest of the details were just add ons, take em or leave em. Hey, you could even just build a big arena to display them in.

To combine two threads, the statues could all be dropped in the propsed "no yalhama zone" and you will have to venture by foot into perilous territory to find your statue.
