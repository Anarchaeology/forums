A constructive suggestion re: visual/audio
This is a transcript of an email I sent to funcom's feedback department -- I figured I'd post it here too to see what people think (I'm aware there are more important issues with the game to worry about first, but my idea would still be nice to see someday).

Anyway, here's my email to them:
--------

Hello, I recently just got SL after leaving AO for several months..
overall I am relatively pleased with the expansion, despite the somewhat troubling bugs and other problems I'm sure you've already heard plenty of.

Anyhow, my email is for a small .. cosmetic... suggestion I suppose. I was very excited about perks (and like them) when I first heard of them. I choose a bunch for my 136 fixer and had fun practicing with them in a few solo missions. I was pleased with their ability..

But I have one minor complaint I guess.. they seem rather.. flat? For instance, I have level 6 (i think) SMG perk for the fixer and enhanced dna around level 6 or 7 as well.

However, when I activate the neat specials on my Reinforced Slugs or use Jarring burst, there doesn't seem to be any exciting audio or visual effect from them... reinforced slug gives me a tiny little white swirl barely visible -- but there is no added visual effect to my shooting or new sound to add that extra "oomph" to the ability.

Jarring burst is also very 'dull' in appearance -- it does damage and stuns, but there's no neat Audio or visual effect to it ... it would be cool to see an animation of a bunch of spraying lead or something or even simply a neat sound effect when the special goes off.... the ehnhanced Dna ability that lets me use a short HOT every 5 mins is useful, but there's nothing to suggest visually or audibly that it is going off.

My suggestion, is if you ever find the time, is to add at least a small visual and/or audio effect to the Specials on perks ... right now, when I play in Rubika with my friend who is a fixer too, you even can't tell any difference between us (he doesn't have SL) ... he keeps asking when I will use one of my special abilities and I told him I am always using them.... but he can't really tell because there isn't a visual or audio sound to make them seem as neat and unique as they are.... right now the
only difference between me and him is that I can do more damaged -- it would be nice though if it was somehow displayed or heard.

Thanks.
