What's the beau of these class/profession thing?
Well, seems that almost all MMRPG and MUDS need you to choose a class/profession - in fact I think all MMRPG need you to do so (dun know of any MMRPG that dun have a class/profession and if u know pls tell me, very interested).I just can't see the beauty of this system. (Actually very sick of it)

Why can't they make it classless such that if you want to be able to do something, you put your mind and time into learning it and in the end you can do it.

E.g. Like in the Arcanum game where it is classless, if you want to be able to make a weapon, just put your skill point in it and with enough, you are able to do it. If you want to cast a spell, put your skill points in it and there again you can do it. This way, I can create a really unique character (eg an dabbler in magic and technology in Arcanum though such characters sucks in game play but rocks in RP)

I also believe that profession is something I cannot understand. In real life, you might change profession. (E.g. You can be a technician but can change to a beaurecrat if you want to and put your heart and mind into it. )

This is also one thing I dislike about D&D RPG. The thing I like about Champions the Super RPG is that there is no such class thing. You can be a martial artist and because of some circumstances become a psychic. (Just buy the psychic powers, however, you must give a convincing story before the GM will allow you)
