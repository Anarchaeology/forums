MAs getting dogged in PvP
Not I don't often complain about my MA, because I love using him, but in PvP conflict, he just plain sucks.

Let me first say, that for any melee user, a player that can ROOT is a bane for us. Seeing as how MAs are almost forced to use their fists at this point, a root alone will pretty much be the end of me.

Secondly, ac and half damage, ouch right there. If I hit the guy, not only do I have to contend with doing half damage, but it's also less because of the armor. So I will probably do minimum damage, and of course I can't overequip my fists and twink to high heaven like all these other PvPers.

Thirdly, the once-useful dimach. This is a freakin joke now. If they don't parry it, I might be able to do 40% of their life with it, which they probably will just shrug off since that's about the most damage I can do.

Fourthly, I believe when I use bwt it does half damage every tick since it's considered a special. So that's about as effective as a standard DoT. MAs can heal, but usually I'm taking such a pounding at this point, I either have to try to heal and run, or run and heal.

If anyone has a right to complain about PvP conditions, I think it is the MA. I have seen doctors, which should be horrible in a PvP fight tear others to shreds. All I ask for really, is a way to break roots, so that I can at least hit my opponent for some measly damage. Beyond that, they'd have to overhaul so much stuff ...they might even have to take time aside from fixing all the little nuances they waste their time on, like screwdrivers to adjust size for pioneer backpacks. (woopie...)

Even against monsters, my MA has become a joke. I lvled him to lvl 50 before "Minimum Damage Artist" became the nickname. Now that I'm lvl 80, and even more crippled than before, I have to hang him up. I enjoy PvPing, if not for the strategy of it, but I'll have to reroll a whole new guy to have any chance of competing. To the others that are currently screwed with PvP, I feel your pain, oh do I feel your pain. But, more likely then not, you have a better foreseeable future than the MA. If we didn't have sub g and vulnerability seeker, I don't think anybody would bother even adding us to a team.

I hope they fix PvP.
