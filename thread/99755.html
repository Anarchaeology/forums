AOChatSounds update
http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sh...ht=AOChatSounds

AOChatsounds plays a sound when a phrase appears in your Anarchy Online chat window. This is useful (to me) for knowing when a special attack becomes available or a when buff expires during a hectic battle. You can potentially specify a different sound for each special or nanoprogram. It does not respond to messages from other players, just messages that are generated by AO.
The way you specify what phrases to watch for and what sounds to play is by editing chatsound.ini. The included chatsound.ini has some examples of how to set this up. Basically, you put the phrase to watch for on one line, and the FULL path to the .wav file to play on the next line. No blank lines are allowed. After you have entered this information into chatsound.ini and saved it, run AOChatSounds. It will display some information about what chat/sound pairs it found in your ini file, then it will wait for Anarchy Online chat text to process. Run Anarchy Online (doesn't matter if you run AO before of after starting AOChatSounds). and do something that would produce the a phrase that you entered in the ini file, and the sound should play. Entering the phrase directly in your chat will not work, the message must be generated by Anarchy Online.
