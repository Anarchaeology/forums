Visions of the past
Footsteps echoed in the concrete canyons that made up the streets of Omni-1, the rain clouds high above blanketing the city in an endless downpour and blocking out the moon ands stars. Valaik made his way slowly along the streets, his thoughts drifting where they wished much like the clouds high above danced on the winds. Making his way up a ramp Valaik looked around, the wall that seperated the Omni-1 Entertainment district from the sewers was just north of him and he moved along the walkway to sit in what he assumed at one time had been a clothing shop, now long abandoned. Sliding down the wall to sit on the hard concrete ground Valaik continued to look north to the sewers, a place where he had spent a good deal of time during his early days on Rubi-Ka, though the memories comming to mind were far from pleasant. With a sigh he pulled a small recording device from his coat and set it beside him, turning it on and looking back out at the wall.

"No matter where you make it to in life, never forget where you come from. Good thing to remember, far too many folks become obsessed by power or money to the point where they forget where they came from, what they believed in, and who helped them along the way. Like it or not its something I've seen happening to me up until lately." The words rolled from Valaik's lips in a softer voice than usual, the sadness he felt in his heart comming through clearly though he didn't intend that to be the case.

"I've done alot that I'm not proud of and I can't take it back but I can make sure it doesn't happen again. Should tell ya something when your friends tell ya to hide and your wife gets up in arms at ya for nearly getting arrested. Can't say I blame them though, prisons never a nice place, no matter what world you're on. Thought of having to look at Ravia, Thyme, Trist, Don, Trell, or anyone I care for through a set of bars is more than my heart can take." Valaik leaned his head back against the wall and closed his eyes, rubbing them with his hand a moment before continuing.

"It all goes back to remembering where we came from. Can't forget your past or you end up making the same mistakes over and over again. Guess I didn't remember my past as well as I should have. Now's as good a time as any I guess." He turned his head to look to the north, to that wall and what lay beyond, to memories of the past.

"Guess the best place to start is the beginning..."

Verin IV was a mining world rich in natural resources, lacking in just about everything else. Verin wasn't excatly the kinda place you would expect anything to live, nothing lived their naturally because of some fluke of nature, a world covered in barren plains, mountains that stabbed into the sky, and endless wind blasted deserts. Other than what the corporations came to mind the only thing that Verin IV was famous for was its storms, merciless sandstorms that could strip off layers of metaplast on a vehicle just as easy as it could rip the flesh from bones. That's why the domes were created, to protect the miners or more specifically the corporate headquarters and the resource processing and shipping facilities. No one lived outside the domes, if you tried you died, and that left approximately seven million people crowded into an area large enough for three or four. The mines were connected to the domes by a massive series of subsurface tunnels that housed the maglev system that everyone but the priveledged few used to get around.

There were actually 5 domes, three of them used for housing the residents, one for the corporations to handle their business, and one for entertainment. Each dome had an official name but unless you were talking to a corper they had other names. Hab's One, Two, and Three were the living domes, the largest three domes taking up the majority of the city. The Corp Sector, the corporations dome, was the next largest and situated on the far side of the entertainment dome from Hab two. Finally there was Haven, the entertainment dome, nobodies sure where it got the name but that's what stuck, and that dome was where you could find anyone or anything to make you happy. Each Dome was connected to the ones nearby, the city was arranged like a large arrowhead, with the habs making up the point and Haven and the Corp Sector making up the shaft. Hab Two was the point of the arrow, connected to both of the other Hab domes and Haven, if you wanted to get to Haven, you went through Hab Two so naturally it became the home of the more respected and powerful members of the city.

Corp Sector was a small city in itself, corporate enclaves and villas filled the areas around the centrally located headquarters building and south of the wall that seperated the better parts of Corp Sector from the resource center north of the wall. Citizens didn't just walk into Corp Sector like you could anywhere else in the city, if you wanted to enter the dome you had to have one hell of a good reason and more important, a clearance pass. Corporate Security was a creature all its own, seperate from the cities standing police force and by far more brutal when it came to enforcing the corporations will. Hephaestus Mining Corporation leased the planet Verin IV from the ICC and ruled it with an iron hand, workers did not strike, there were no mentions of open revolt, and if there were they didn't last longer than a visit from Corporate Security.

The Rest of the domes were run by The Council, a collection of the cities elderest and most loyal employees to the corporation. They ran the cities day to day operations and kept the peace. Their hand was the cities police force, a group that most would describe as criminals given a job on the other side, and they dealt with crime swiftly and largely without trial. That's one of the things about the frontier worlds, the rest of the galaxy doesn't really care what happens there so the corporations could get away with murder, literally. The police regularly patrolled the streets and skies of the domes by foot and with the help of destinctive, heavily armed transports.

Haven, the last of the domes and by far the most frequented of them. Half the size of each of the Hab dome's three kilometer diameter, Haven was the mixing ground for the corporate employees, Corp'ers as they are commonly called, and the rest of the citizens, everyone was welcome in Haven but the Corp'ers definetely got preferential treatment. Everything you could dream of to make you happy was found in Haven, the streets lined with casino's, clubs, and restaurants. The streets were illuminated in every color imaginable and highlighted with anything from silver to platinum as long as it sparkled. Behind that bright, clean facade lay a darker world that everyone knew of but few admitted to knowing.

That world was where the more extreme pleasures flowed from. A criminal underworld is what many police forces joke about but few really have to deal with but on Verin that's exactly what there was. The Grosk Syndicate ran Haven from behind the scenes, controlling the crime in their own way, making a profit from it along the way, headed by an extremely paranoid and cruel man, Vladomir Grosk. His men collected protection fees from the entire Haven, mysterious firebombings usually marking those locations that refused to pay or simply couldn't. The corporation knew that the syndicate existed but couldn't do much about it other than strike out when they had an opportunity, each side did this but always were careful to keep it from growing into open warfare between the groups.

A civilian transport flew low over the buildings of Hab Three, traveling fifteen meters above the highest of the buildings and no more than one hundred meters above the streets below. Aboard was Gabriel Krohs, the pilot of the civilian transport that he used to taxi people around the domes providing for his wife Vennessa, who sat next to him in the transport, and their son Garret whom they were heading home to see. Gabriel was an expert pilot, respected by many of the other transport services for his skill behind the controls of his ship. His wife Vennessa was his copilot, together they headed their own business, Krohs Transport Incorporated, which handled a large percentage of the traffic to and from the other domes of the city. The pilot turned and smiled to his wife, reaching out to squeeze her hand tightly as he moved their transport between the buildings that lined the street that their appartment was located on.

Below on the street a young man was sitting on a low wall, watching the citizens walk by on their way to some unknown destination, but his eyes were watching something else, a group of three making their way up and down the same area of the street. A smile spread on his face as he saw one of the three, a woman in her late teens, bump into an elderly man and then appologize, giving the man a charming smile that he tried to return before moving off. The boy shook his head as he imagined the face of that very same man later when he found out that his wallet and credsticks were missing, professionally lifted from his person by that nice young lady. Then he heard the a sound he was very familiar with, a high pitch whine that accompanied a subsonic rumble, the engines of his fathers transport. He looked up and smiled brighter as his eyes picked the ship out down the street and watching it erupt into an expanding fireball as the ship exploded high above the street.

The explosion picked the rear of the ship up a moment before the engines added their combined fury to the explosion, their fuel joining in to fuel the growing fireball. It took less than three seconds for the ship to be completely consumed by the fireball and then secondary explosions began as fuel storage tanks began to detonate, raining metal and fire onto the street below, sending terrified people scrambling for cover in every direction. The boy, Garret Krohs, could do nothing but watch as the ship began to lose altitude fast, a rolling fireball above the street. What was left of the transport smashed into the ground one hundred meters from where he stood, sending out a shockwave as another major explosion, probably from the primary engine core, lashed out. Garret was thrown from his feet over the wall, putting it between him and what was left of the transport now sliding down the street in his direction, pieces of metal from the last explosion cut into his leg but the wall shielded him from the bulk of it. Garret slid back against the wall and knew without any doubt that his parents were dead, could hear the burning transport not so far away from the wall he was against.

...

"I was 14 Years old. I watched my parents ship get annihilated by an explosion. Even then, sitting there with my back to that wall I knew, I knew without a doubt in my heart that my parents were killed. My father was much to careful to have missed anything that could have caused an explosion like that. I sat there for a long time thinking of what I should do, trying to figure out what had happened, little did I know the answers would basically walk up to me."

Part 2
His eyes drifted up to the sky, watching the transport ships hauling their goods back and forth above the city, the long trains of containers carried along their course by the small tugs. There passage slowed because of the storm over the city but not stopped, they carried on their work. Valaik reached up to scratch his chin as he let his eyes fall shut, resting his head against the cold concrete wall behind him.

"I loved my parents a great deal. I know how happy they were even on that world. I vowed right there on the street to find who did it and make them pay. Funny how fate sometimes agrees with the hairbrained schemes of youth isn't it ?"

Garret stood from where he sat against the wall and wiped the tears from his eyes onto his sleeve, then moved down the alley to the back door of the building that housed his appartment. Each step seemed more difficult than the last as he made his way to the lift that would take him to the top floor, moving inside he hit the button he wanted and let the doors slide shut. The lift was fairly slow but finally arrived on the top floor. The doors slid open slowly and he stepped out into the cooridor and made his way towards his apartment. A few steps away he noticed the door was open and knew he hadn't left it that way. Moving much slower Garret made his way to the door and glanced in, a large man in a synth-leather jacket was knocking things around.

"Da kid ain't 'ere. Les go already." spoke the man in the jacket as he turned to look at someone out of Garret's line of sight.

"Yeah, Kid was probably with mommy and daddy on the ship. Warms my heart. Warmed theirs too when I set off that blasting charge, they roasted big time. Lets go tell the boss." said another person in the room with a deep laugh, Garret could feel his anger growing as he listened to their words.

The man in the jacket turned towards the door and Garret ducked out of sight quick enough to avoid being seen, then ran as quietly down the hallway as he could, moving to the nearest vent openning and tugging it open then crawling inside, facing out before closing the vent. From his position Garret could see down the hallway to the door of his apartment. Both men walked out and Garret took a long look at the both of them. The one in the jacket was fairly tall and muscled, his face marked from forehead to chin by a long scar that crossed over his right eye, which was a blank white orb. The other was dressed better, a dark blue suit and tie, he looked like a typical Corp'er to Garret except for the lightning bolt tatoo he could see towards the back of the mans neck, just above his collar.

"Do it." ordered the man in the suit and "Lefty" as Garret had named him drew a small canister from the inside of his jacket and rolled it into the room, closing the door before the two walked off as if nothing had happened.

Garret lost sight of them but heard the lift doors open and shut. Carefully he crawled from the vent, making sure the two men were gone before dropping to the floor and staring at the apartment door, wondering what the canister was. He though he recognized the canister from somewhere, maybe from one of the street gangs he normally watched, he wasn't sure but began moving towards the door. The hallway was filled with the concussion and roar of an explosion as the canister detonated and sent out a wave of fire throughout the apartment, ripping the door free and smashing it against the wall opposite it. Garret was thrown from his feet as the shock wave hit him, growling he crawled to his feet, thinking how mad he was at getting knocked from his feet by an explosion twice inside fifteen minutes.

Shaking his head to try and clear the ringing from his ears, Garret stumbled down the hallway away from the burning apartment towards the front of the building. There was a window at the end of the hall that overlooked the street and Garret made his way there, slowly but surely, the ringing in his ears slowly fading away. Reaching the window he sat there looking out, the scene below caused his heart to miss a beat. The remains of the transport were no longer burning, a fire control vehicle and a pair of police patrolers having blocked of the street. Pedestrians, police, and firefighters all moved around the wreckage and then Garret saw them. Across the street in an alley the two men that had killed his parents were standing but not alone, with them was one of the policemen. Garret watched as "Suit" as he had been named handed the policeman a small package and clapped him on the shoulder before turning to walk down the alley away from the wreckage, Lefty following close behind.

Garret stood there watching for a moment, his eyes drawn to the destroyed transport. He shook his head a few minutes later and knew that he had to leave, he had to let them think he had died in the shuttle. His hearing clear and his head no longer clouded he made his way down the service stairs to the street and made good time across this section of the city to an abandoned store. Moving to the back door Garret slid a few of the boards aside, chuckling to himself how easy it was to make the place look as if it was still secure, and moved inside. This was his home away from home, a place to be alone with his thoughts and watch the world. In the corner he moved a section of the flooring and removed a small bag, then layed them out on the counter to make sure everything was there. His lockpicks, a few food containers, and a small energy pistol were layed out and examined. Most he had borrowed from the local gangs as he learned how to steal and the pistol he was particularly proud of, stolen from the head of the toughest gang in this area. Carefully he placed everything back into the bag and and looked out the broken windows.

"Even then I knew what I had to do. I had to learn as much as I could about the way things worked in the world and the way things worked in the shadows. I was already a pretty good pickpocket, I would study those on the streets, examining their methods and picking them apart. I learned to break into places from a member of one of the local gangs, same one that I stole the gun from too." Valaik chuckled lightly as he sat there, listening to the rain.

"So I set out on my glorious quest for vengence. A simple teenager against a world that would kill without hesitation. I learned the hard way that was exactly what I had to become. So I fell in with one of the local gangs, learned as much as I could from them about how things worked, practiced my marksmanship, and practiced my thieving. I had a natural apptitude for taking what didn't belong to me, can't say that I'm proud of the fact but it did keep me alive for the next few years. Breaking in to houses and stealing small items, pickpocketing folks in the Habs, and even a few larger robberies with the gang." spoke Valaik slowly, his head resting against the wall and his eyes closed, his arms resting on his knees.

"Five or six years past and I had moved on. I had my own apartment and I robbed people to get by. The whole time I had watched for Suit and Lefty, the ones that had so changed my world. My thoughts usually didn't stay on them for long though, I always wondered who the man was that told them to kill my parents. I asked myself that big question of teenage years alot, Why. During those years I learned that Krohs Transport had been taken over by another company, Ovilias Transport Group. I remember my father talking about them, he didn't like them and had told me about how they wanted to buy his company. He said that he would never give up flying and Ovilias could keep his money. That's when I started to figure things out." Valaik stood and stretched in the doorway to the long abandoned store, watching a droid fly buy and continue to the west, his eyes sweeping the surroundings as he began to pace.

"So I did some looking and I found out where Ovilias had their headquarters, a nice little section of Hab One. I started watching the place when I could, I even stepped up my robberies figuring I'd need more money soon. So days turned to weeks and I watched..."

Garret had purchased a small apartment in a highrise overlooking the Ovilias headquarters and began living there, keeping an eye on the building through a viewscope he purchased. After a few high profit robberies Garret decided that it would be best that he lived off what he had for a bit and let the police quiet down, they had been close to capturing him on his last break in and he had barely gotten away. So for now he would focus his entire attention on finding the people that killed his parents, a much more satisfiying task. Night and day he walked the streets around the area, learning every street and alley until he could navigate with his eyes closed, and also watching the people around him. His gut told him that the reason his parents were dead was here or would be soon and he was determined to be here when it happened.

One dark night Garret made his way into a popular local club, tipping the doorman generously to get inside, and hoping to find some decent information. He had began to carry the same energy pistol he had stolen years ago with him, it was a powerful yet compact weapon that had served him well for years but upon entering the club he saw that inside he would be unarmed. A large scanner stood inside the entryway and beeped to indicate he was armed. A bulky man holding a shotgun indicated that he should hand over all weapons to a rather cute female clerk and walk back through the scanner. Garret did so with no complaint, handing over his pistol and a small throwing knife to the clerk and passing back through the detector. This time it did not go off and the armed guard waved him through.

Moving down the hallway and up a flight of stairs into the main area of the club Garret instantly knew why it was popular. The entire dancefloor was alight with lasers projected from the ceiling, the rest of the rather large club was expensively decorated with plenty of seating and a live band on the far side of the dance floor from him. Moving to his left he heading towards the bar to find a nice vantage point to watch the rest of the club. Ordering a drink and flashing a fake ID that he had created for himself, Garret watched the floor and searched for familiar faces. His drink arrived and he sipped on it slowly, it burning his throat badly as it went down.

"Now I think I could grow to like this stuff." Garret said to himself as he ordered another sake from the waitress in a decidedly revealing transparent dress.

As his drink arrived he spotted someone at one of the booths sitting alone, sipping from a glass with a mostly empty bottle on the table. The man was wearing an Ovilias Transport jacket and never looked up, staring into his drink between sips. Garret smiled and asked the waitress to bring a bottle of what the man was drinking to him at that booth. Moving through the crowd Garret dropped into the seat across from the man, who looked up and stared Garret in the eyes.

"Whatcha want ? Can't you see I'm busy ?" growled the man, his breath stinking of alcohol.

"I'm just here to share a drink with you. Look like you've had a rough day." said Garret calmly and then smiled as the waitress showed up with the bottle, tipping her nicely as he payed for the bottle.

"Rough day ? That old bastard Ovilias fired me. Didn't like the way I flew the transport ship down from orbit. He's the one that ordered me to fly the damn thing through a class two storm." blurted the man as he held out his glass for Garret to pour him a bit more of whatever it was he was drinking.

"He fired you for that ? Must really be a bastard. So why you wearing an Ovilias jacket if he fired you? Oh, excuse my manners, The names Garret, Garret Clasen." spoke Garret as he leaned back in his seat.

"Eh ? I'll be damned, I'm still wearin' it. Name's Floyd Kealty and yeah, he fired me for not flying the ship smooth enough through the storm he ordered me to fly into." said Floyd as he pulled the jacket off and tossed in the corner between himself and Garret on the rounded bench.

"So old man Ovilias just got back on planet today huh ? Probably why I haven't seen him around."

"Yep, bastards been offworld doing somethin' or other for the past couple years. Company was great without him here and now he's back and first off I get booted. Ain't that a hoot ?" growled Floyd as he took a long drink, leaning back deeply into the padded wall of the booth.

"Yeah, but I'm sure you'll find a better job, specially with Ovilias back on world. Well, I hate to drink and run but I gotta go. Nice talking to you and keep the bottle." spoke Valaik with a wink as he stood and began to walk away.

"Thank ya! You're a good kid ya know." yelled Floyd, barely heard over the music of the club, never noticing his missing jacket.

Garret made his way out of the club, collecting his knife and gun at the door and walked two blocks the opposite direction from his appartment, the Ovilias jacket held in his arms, and then cirlced back to his home.

...

"That night I knew things were starting to change. Soon I'd get a shot at the men that destroyed my life. It was also the first night in a long time that I cried."

Running his fingers slowly through his hair he decieded it was a bit warm for the coat and pulled it off, tossing it down beside the recorder. Taking a deep breath and releasing it slowly Valaik looked out and saw a couple walking, hands locked together with bright smiles on their faces, and he couldn't help but let a smile appear on his own face.

"Even on this planet, with all its conflict and hatred, you still see love. Gives me hope for the future. As for the past, I had learned the man that was most likely responsible for my parents death was back on planet. Hunting season was open and the prey had come home..."

Two days passed after Garret had met the ex-pilot in the club with no sign of Lefty, Suit, or Ovilias. Then it all changed with the arrival of a hoverlimo at the entrance to the Ovilias building. Garret snatched up his viewscope and zoomed in on the buildings entrance, waiting to see who got out. First out was old man Ovilias himself, a rather fat man that needed a cane to help walk, he looked just like the picture Garret had found with maybe a bit more weight now. Next men in suits climbed out, Garret didn't recognize either of them, they looked like typical corporate lackeys. Finally another man in a suit climbed out, this one was bulkier than the first two and then something caught Garret's eye. On the back of his neck was a black tatoo shaped like the end of a lightning bolt. Garret's heart began to race in his chest as the man turned and he saw his face, the face of the man that had killed his parents.

The man didn't come up in any of the records he had accessed about Ovilias, so he was most likely someone that did the dirty work when Ovilias wanted something. He watched Suit walk inside and then set down the scope, quickly dashing around the apartment picking up food and drink and placing them near the window, setting himself up to watch for as long as it took. He picked up his scope and began watching the building closely, glad he had picked a room with an angle on both the buildings entrances. Surprisingly Garret didn't have to wait long, just over an hour later Suit walked out the side entrance and stood there, moments later being met by Lefty. Garret couldn't help but grin, both his targets were right there. Garret quickly grabbed his jacket and pistol, sliding it into a holster in the jackets interior, as he quickly ran out of his apartment and down the stairs to the street. When he got there he looked to see if the men were still there.

With a smile he saw they were, both were talking and Garret made his way slowly closer to them, taking up a position in an alley nearby so he could keep them in sight. His dark clothing helped him blend into the shadows as he observed the two, watching them and noticing how both were relaxed by their body language, they had nothing to worry about, yet. With a slap on Lefty's shoulder Suit turned and walked back inside while left moved off down the side street with Garret following in the shadows. Garret was careful not to get too close to his target, keeping a good distance back but close enough that Lefty would never be out of his view for more than a second at most. He followed him to the maglev trams and onto one, keeping at the back of the compartment as the tram rocketed beneath the streets of the Hab and into Hab Two where Lefty got off.

Lefty seemed to be making his rounds, stopping at a few shops and talking to a few people that stood out to Garrets senses. Most of them were probably the local drug or stim dealers and the shops were probably handing over protection money to Lefty. Garret though about that and it made him uneasy, it meant that Lefty was probably connected. That didn't stop him though, he trailed him for another fourty-five minutes until he entered an apartment complex, moving in fast to keep track Garret followed him inside. Taking a huge chance he climbed into the same lift as Left, being so close to one of the men that had killed his parents made his stomach tie into knots. He wanted to draw his pistol and end it there but he knew if he did that the others that were responsible might get away, so as much as it pained him he remained passive. He touched the button for the floor above the one Lefty hit and tried to act tired and distant to keep from drawing attention.

Lefty got off and took a right out of the lift as Garret let the doors shut and the lift move up to the next level. Getting off he looked for the stairs and found then quickly, heading down one floor in the hopes of catching Lefty entering his apartment. When he glanced out of the door down the halway he saw that it was empty and slide back into the stairwell. Making his way down to the street Garret thought back to all the tech wizards he had met and decided to visit one that lived not too far from here. He was careful to confuse his path as he made his way to Sparks' apartment, a tech expert he had met a couple years back, and hoped he would have what he was looking for. He walked around to the back of a large apartment complex and down a flight of stairs to a large steel door with a viewport in it. Knocking hard on the door he wated for the port to open, when it did a pair of eyes stared back at him.

"Who are ya and whatcha want ?" spoke a nasal voice from beyond the door.

"Sparks, Open up its Garret Clasen, I'm in need of some of your toys." spoke Garret with a smile as the door was thrown open.

"Damnit, how many times do I have to tell you they are not toys!? They're works of art!" yelled the skinny man known as Sparks that had openned the door.

Garret grinned and walked inside, Sparks closing and securing the door behind him. The two talked and went over what exactly Garret needed, not surprisingly Sparks already had something just like it ready to go, for a price. The micro-camera recording system cost Garret about one third of the money that he had saved but if he was right it would be worth every penny. After paying Sparks Garret made his way back to Lefty's apartment building and up to his floor. Making sure no one was watching he planted the micro-cam so that it could view the six doors down the hallway. The camera transmitted to a handheld display unit that Garret pulled out and tested, satisfied that it was working he took the stairs down to the bottom floor and to a cafe a few blocks away. Finding him a quiet booth Garret ordered some dinner and some coffee to keep him alert he pulled out the display and watched for Lefty.

It took four hours of watching before Lefty left his apartment and when he did Garret smiled and left the cafe, catching sight of Lefty moving down the street away from him and the apartment. Garret moved into the apartment building through its rear entrance, taking the lift up this time and scanning the hallway, then checking to make sure the camera was still transmitting before making his way down the hall to Lefty's apartment. Pulling out his lockpicks, that he was thankful stayed in his jacket pocket, he quickly unlocked the door and moved inside, drawing his pistol after replacing his lockpicks. Carefully Garret checked out the apartment to be sure no one was here and then moved to find himself a place to wait. As he searched he found a familiar item and carried it with him for later use. Finding a large chair down the entry hall in the apartment from the door and out of direct sight.

Garret was glad for the coffee four hours later when Lefty stepped off the lift and turned towards his apartment. Drawing his pistol from its holster Garret made sure it was loaded and ready then stepped closer to the wall. Hearing the door being unlocked and opened made Garret's heart race, now he was getting a chance to pay back one of the men that had taken his parents from him, had taken his future. Lefty stepped into the main room and turned to the left, staring right into the barrel of Garret's pistol. He made a move for his own weapon but Garret took a step forward and kept the weapon aimed at his head.

"Don't even try. Now drop the piece and sit in the chair or I'll add another scar to that pretty face of yours." said Garret quietly but with enough feeling so that Lefty knew that he wasn't joking and slowly took Lefty's weapon from him as he handed it over and sat in the chair, staring back at him hard.

"Boy, you is dead." said Left as Garret moved to stand in front of him.

"If you want to avoid that very fate you'll start off by telling me who your buddy in the suit is, the one with the lightning tat on his neck." spoke Garret, his gun pointed directly at Lefty's good eye.

"Lightnin'? Oh ya mean Kas ?" said with an almost confused tone.

Garret got a name, Kas Markal, and soon after where his apartment was and his first proof that Markal worked for Ovilias.

"Alright Lefty, thanks for the info, fun times over though." with that Garret extended his arm and put one energy bolt into Lefty's forehead, burning an hold through his head and the chair behind it.

Garret took a deep breath as he looked at what he had done, up until now it had just been an idea in his head, but now that had changed, he was on a path there was no turning back from. His heart was still racing in his chest as he moved next to Lefty's body, picking up the item he had found earlier and placing it in his lap. Setting the timer on the side of the canister for five minutes Garret stood, holstered his pistol, and left. Closing the door behind him and taking the stairs down to the bottom floor. He heard the muffled blast as the canister exploded as he openned the rear door of the building and despite the act he had just done, he smiled.

Killing him is one of the acts I'm not sorry for. He gave me the name of the next man. He would give me the name of the next. Valaik rubbed his eyes softly as the first rays of light from the first of two sunrises of the morning came to him over the walls of Omni-1 Entertainment. Spinning on his heel Valaik moved to pick up his cloak and recorder, tossing the cloak over his shoulder and keeping the recorder in hand as he made his way down to the street and began heading south through the red light district. His eyes wandered from the neon lights to the dark alleyways as his feet carried him along, moving towards the bridge that would take him to Omni-HQ but turning farther south as the guards came into sight, making his way slowly towards the docks. Valaik passed beneath a large archway that lead from the cleaner streets of Entertainment to the less organized and seemingly dirtier area of the docks and made his way to the nearest one, walking slowly down the stairs that were still slick with the previous nights rain and down the pier, moving to the end to stand looking out over the water and towards the rising sun on the horizon.

"I guess that's when things really started to change for me. First person you kill is never something you forget, it burns into your mind and eats at you. Many can't take it and shy away from any sort of violence they see, trying to avoid confronting the face of the person that died at their hands. It didn't happen that way for me. His face haunted my dreams and from time to time still does but I did not hide from it, I knew it had to be done and that it was only the beginning. Maybe if I knew then how far it would go and what it would cost me I might not have done it. Then again, maybe I would have, I have many things to smile about here..."

Another night came into being on the dark world of Verin IV, the last shift of miners were released onto Haven and the city began to glow with lights of all shapes and colors, bathing streets in an eerie often ever shifting glow. Three days had passed since Garret had killed Lefty, Rudolf Palino he had later found out, and Garret was making his way through Haven. The night before he had recieved a message from one of the local information brokers, also a frequented fence, informing Garret that his services were needed for a special job and that if he wanted in on it he'd have to show up at The Bend, a club on the Haven's north side in a higher class section. Garret climbed out of the transport and made his way inside, the building being a striking clash to the standard clubs that littered Haven. Its plain brick facade with a single small sign was all that announced that this club even existed to the outside world. The Bend was a private club that a very select few got an invitation to enter, so when the club had been mentioned in the message it had imediately peaked Garret's interest.

Garret made his way to the door, glancing up and down the street and noticing how quiet this section was compared to the rest of Haven, barely anyone was here unlike the typical packed streets of the other sections. Turning back to the door Garret placed his thumb on the plate next to the door as the message had instructed him and with a click the door began to swing open, revealing a very large man standing just beyond.

"Evening Mr. Clasen, You'll wish to make your way to the back room where Mr. Kansiar is waiting on you." said the bouncer, for lack of a better description, in a deep gravelly voice.

With a slight nod Garret made his way into the main area of the club, a large room with booths lining a small dancefloor. All the booths were shaded so that unless you were to walk right up to them it is doubtful you would ever see anyone inside. Making his way in front of the large and decidedly well furnished bar that took up the east corner of the room Garret pushed through a curtain and into the back room. The major feature of the room was a large conference table, at it was seated five men, two on the far end and three more closer to himself. Kansiar, the broker that had contacted him, waved him to have a seat with the other three men on the opposite end of the table from himself. Glancing at the faces of all the men, trying to place them and remember their names, Garret made his way to the far side of the table from the rooms entrance and sat down in a rather comfortable chair.

"Gentlemen, You have all be asked here because you have made a name for yourself in each of your lines of work and at the same time have avoided drawing too much attention for those same actions. Each of you has a skill that is vital to the success of the job that we have planned and if I have your attention, I'll begin." spoke Kansiar as his eyes moved to those of each person around the table that returned his gaze and nodded slightly.

"Very good, as most of you know bringing explosives onto Verin is about as easy as walking outside the dome in a storm naked and surviving. What we plan is to acquire a sizeable quantity of the corporations own blasting charges. These charges are stored in Corp Sec under heavy guard. Our plan is to insert you into Corp Sec in place of the standard shipping vehicle that transports them to the mines." as Kansiar finished he pushed back from the table and stood, his suit hanging loose on his skinny frame as he began to walk slowly down the table towards Garret and the other three men.

"If I have your agreement and cooperation I'll go into better detail about each of your jobs. Each of you will be payed five hundred thousand credits for your part if the items are delivered. So are all of you in ?" Kansiar stood near the middle of the table, his eyes studying the men.

Each of the four turned to look at the others, their thoughts ranging from typical paranoia to outright greed at the thought of making a half million credits for a single nights work. They studied each others faces, trying to judge what the others would decide and more importantly if they could trust the others to not stab them in the back. To a man they all decided that they could not. They did decide that they would all participate and each man signaled this my looking Kansiar in the eyes and nodding slowly to which Kansiar's smile grew wider and wider. Garret was the last to agree and with a clap of his hands Kansiar moved back down the table to sit next to the other man who looked out of place here, the cut of his suit making him look like a corp'er.

"Mr. Davidge, You are an expert pilot and claim to be able to drive anything that has been made. You will be driving the transport into and out of Corp Sec. Most likely on your exit you will be pursued by Corporate Security and will have to avoid capture. I will get you full specifications on the vehicle and have someone take you to see it tonight." said Kansiar to the man sitting directly across from Garret, a large muscled man that looked more like a boxer than a pilot.

"Mr. Varns, you worked with corporate security at the very facility we are planning to breach for five years up until your security clearance was revoked because of possible ties with the Grosk Syndicate. You will be invaluable. You job is to help the team bypass security, locate the items as quickly as possible, and delay the teams discovery until the last possible moment."

"Mr. Sotto, your expertise with heavy weaponry will most likely be a necessity during the escape from the facility. I have no doubt the team will be discovered. When that happens they will do everything they can to stop you. If all goes well your transport will already be away from the facility and they won't be able to mobilize enough units fast enough to stop you. The units that do follow will need to be dealt with, that's where you come in. The transport you will be using has a few heavy weapons mounts and it will be your job to use them to maximum effect." Sotto smiled broadly and leaned back into his seat, crossing his arms as he listened to Kansiar go on.

"Finally, Mr. Clasen. You have made quite a name for yourself as a thief. I have made a great deal off of what you have brought me over recent years and your skills are among the best on Verin IV. Your job will be to break through the security system and keep the alarms from sounding until the team is well on their way out. If the alarms are sounded before them escape will be nearly impossible." with that Kansiar once again looked to each man and then smiled.

"To tell the truth I don't know if it was Lefty's death or that meeting that really changed my course. They both set things in motion that sooner or later that I lost control of. Actually now that I think about it, I know when my course in life changed. It was when I saw a fireball in the sky that had moments before been two loving parents and when in that moment I had given into the hatred and the desire for revenge came. To this day I have no idea what my life would have come to if they hadn't die or if I hadn't wanted revenge. I probably would have died on Verin IV, never knowing the people I now call friend, and that makes me regret my past mistakes a bit less. We had three days of planning before the job would go down and for the moment I was happy to take my mind off revenge and focus on work..."
