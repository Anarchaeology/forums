The Omni-Gen Story (Please read if youve got the time =)
First off, a big thanks to Stormrazer and Missmaul for getting involved in this, I'd love to get more people in it. I really belive its a great story. Im going to speak in perspective to explain the past, and once we get into the present, ill begin with 3rd person.
About 11 years back (give or take a few) a skilled engineer found what was belived to be a child, half dead, in the middle of a massive clan vs. omni battle. It was nothing un usual, but he went against his orders and rushed the kid back to an aide tent. Made with expert craftmanship, these implants brought the boy back to a healthy condition, if not a better one. A few years passed, and the engineer had a son of his own, "Damon (Technon) Timas". After cutting through red tape 3 feet thick, the engineer "Rincord Timas" legally had fathership of his new son, David Timas. Little did he know that through the expertise of clan hackers and coders, that name would spell disaster for the rest of their lives......

Chapter 1- The Beggining
Life when by peacefully for a few months, things couldn't of been more out of the ordinary. Until one day 3 men in back suits busted down Rincord Timas' door, demanding their child of the clan... "Their Loakii". Rincord spent his dying breaths defending his children from the 3 strangers, as they ran off to the shadows, Rincord gave them one last smile. This was the last they ever saw of their father. Homeless children were commen in cities like Omni-Ent, and some parts of Trade, but rarely in Rome. They were quickly spotted and snatched up by the authority's. As David and Damon sat back in fear, they heard gruff voices speak of "jobs" and "Omni seeks more loyal soldiers", while hearing others yelling back in unision "Theyre just children!!". David and Damon froze in their tracks as a tall, slender, and someone ill looking man walked into the small room, he said a few words in undertone, barely audible...but deep down, David knew he was never going to be the same again. The man passed a small chip of somekind, like those containing credits... the eyes of the men and women dilated as they slipped the generous offer into their pockets. The deal was sealed. David and Damon...or as they would be know...#7 and #12.... were about to be changed forever....


Chapter 2- The Experimentation and Liberation

Alot of things have happened in just a year. Life as an Omni-Gen, as the faction called themselves, went from bad, to worse. The 2 children, now were no longer children, but two young men, working hard to push eachother along each new day. Various side effects caused a few of their fellow Omni-Gen Guniea pigs to *vanish* as if they never existed. David and Damon wondered if life could get any more frightning. As months passed, other #'s simply faded away, and which each passing day, more attention was being placed on David, or as they called him #7, "Their Loakii". It seemed the only reason poor Damon(#12) hadnt been "scrapped" was because of his importance to #7.... with out eachother neither could survive.... Horrible things went on behind those big steel lab doors, Damon, or "Technon" as the Omni-Gen middle men called him, feared for his brother every day, and sought a way out. People were making quite a scene about the screams heard at night, it was drawing quite a bit of attention, and some of the"Guniea Pigs" were getting rebellous, showing sides that frightened the Dr. and his research assistants. He knew he had to find a new lab- a place to continue his work- for he knew that this child, this man, it was more than #7, it was really "Loakii". His fears came true one night as shot rang out 3 levels below him, he dropped his vile and screamed in agony as it burned through his flesh... for a short second, he pondered his choices, as if to change his ways... but no, he simply grined in delight, despite the wrenching pain he surely felt shooting throughout his body. Omni-Gens weak security was no match for Omni Pol's barrage of weaponry, nor the mutants and various experiments that turnt against them. Damon himslef found an assualt rifle and shot his way to where his brother was being held... but to his deepest dissapointment, he was gone, along with the Dr. and all his associates...he had been to late...

Chapter 3- Prez Pulls The Plug On Omni Gen
Damon would make it his lifeswork to find his brother- but not without a little help, thats where I came in..... {http://www.anarchy-online.com/charac...e/prezidential]

Damon Timas came running to my door, one late afternoon, he was family, I had to let him in. I hadnt seen him in years, i was relieved to see him ok. I told him of what he had missed in the time he was away, and the fate of his father, and he told me his disturbing story, that has changed me to this day.... I wanted to personally put a bullet in the head of the man who did this. Well, I don't want to keep you too long.... im sure your already getting droopy eyed from the behemoth of text you just read. Long story short- i spent 3 years of cutting red tape, being where i wasnt suppose to be, seeing info i wasnt suppose to see, talking to people i wasnt suppose to be, and getting in enough trouble to put me away for a long, long time. Finally, around 5 am, snoozing on the key board, I awoke to a deathening shrill of success- I had hacked the location of Omni-Gens current base. I was so thrilled I fell back in my chair, and scrambled to the door, where I immediatly contacted man-at-arms, with enough weaponry to start world war...eh.... 73 is it? We tore down the iron doors, and pulled the plug on Omni-Gen- for good, at least I had hoped...
With a few classy words and a sharp tongue, I pulled the trigger and sent the bullet between the eyes of the monster that started it all....

Or so I had hoped.... Omni Gen- I will find you!

[Note: Too keep those phreaks from finding him too easy, I gave him an Alias- Darin Somani...and what do we call him around here? Well, Loakii to be exact...]
