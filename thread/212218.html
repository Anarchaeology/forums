A Day in the Life
Dear Diary,

I woke up this morning to find jellybeans in my nose. Apparently, Ditzie is growing more tired of my snoring. Oh well, it's still better than the olives from last week.

On the way to work, I accidentally ran over a Chirop. What is wrong with those crazy birds? They seem to always fly right in front of you! Ha ha! Who am I kidding? You know me too well, Dear Diary, and I can't fool you into thinking I didn't run it over on purpose!

It was pretty boring at the office. The only excitement was when I cussed out Mr. Huston for not making another pot of coffee after he finished the last cup. He just sort of looked at me, but I think he respects my assertiveness. A promotion soon?

After work was finally over, I decided to fly into Old Athen. As usual, there was the gaggle of Clanners flocked on the hill. And, as usual, I dove down, flying by them really fast, blowing my horn and sent them scurrying all over the place! It's the simple things that bring us the most pleasure.

I stopped by the Bronto Burger to pick up dinner tonight. Ditzie loves those burgers (as do I) but I think we're eating too many of them lately and that can't be healthy. I might have to cut down on my Athen flybys in order to allow more time to make proper dinners. Ha ha! You caught me again, Dear Diary!

Ditzie and I drank a little too much wine with our burgers tonight (yes, Dear Diary, I know it's weird to have wine with burgers!) and, afterwards, Ditzie turned us into Leets and we went swimming in the fountain in Rome. It was a lot of fun until the guards arrested us. I swear, as if Leets could REALLY chew through Elite armor! We finally got the whole mess straightened out and were released on our own recognizance.

Finally, we got home and it was time for sleep and, of course, to say hello to you, Dear Diary. Here's hoping for a jellybean-free night!

Nevver
