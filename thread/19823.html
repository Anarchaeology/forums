Patch Notes of Nikki Dial -- Pornstar
What can I say? Team missions, Nano Wire, Home changes, MMD grid reqs, Hidden nerfs, and everything inbetween. Ill try to make my point long and to the point, and discuss what the good and the bad is and why :OP

Team missions
In concept they are a good thing. Many changes can be made to change the reality of team missions though.

Boss room
The boss room needs a side room you can warp into, instead of directly warping in to fight him. This may sound unneeded, but how many people died randomly because of lag yesterday??? The only thing this did was allow NPCs to camp the grid like PCs did at 2HO. Adding a side room to allow your team to set up for a good fight would only add to fun, not decrease it.

Floors
Pets need to be able to travel with you to the next floor.

Playfield shifts
Come on, this patch was about a month late and still no one caught this? I cannot see how you would be shifting anyways since its just a new room, anyways this needs to be fixed.


Nano Wire
Hmmmm after 2 weeks of players telling you Nano Wire didnt exist ingame anymore, you finally end up listening to us and fixing it. Im tired of hearing stupid ARKs and GMs telling us players that everything needed is ingame and working properly. I think we know better than they do that crap is wrong and has been wrong, and gets worse after each patch it seems. Just like the Dodga quest, Funcom finally addressed this and said it was broken, after about 3 months :O( Start listening to us we know what we are talking about.


Home
Weeeee Home now has less lag than before. How long will it last though???? The fact remains when you bunch the players into 2-3 zones lag will be seen in great amounts. The only real method of fixing lag is spreading us out. The only way to do that is add more grid exits and more whompas to ALL zones. Otherwise it will just be a few weeks until everything is back to the same old bad things.


Meetmedeere
Weeee basically just made another Arena. Lowered the grid reqs so even twinks can get there to gank without too much trouble. Nice job, exactly what this game needs.


Treatment Nerfs+++
The idea was to cut down on twinks. Hmmmm I dont think its a good idea to put in hidden nerfs such as this when you havent even seen how the OE patch will effect the game. Is it me or does this not sound right? If you have an announced change coming up that could go sooo wrong and you sneak in a change the patch before it happens the greatly affects the OE patch, doesnt it make your chance at disaster all that more great??? Like I said it doesn't make sense to me to change something that has been ingame and accepted by Funcom for so long then change it at the absolute wrong time. Either way this nerf will be seen even more in the upcoming patch.


Promised unique Spawns
Well this patch promised more rare disks and crystals that where previously almost impossible to get. Today only one rumor of one rare disk has been posted. Out of the 10 or so super rare disks 1 of them showed up yesterday. Given a year there would be 365 disks found or approximately 36.5 of each of those disks. This means instead of the 2-3 people per year that are allowed to be balanced ingame now about 38 per year will get to feel balanced.

This just doesn't make sense to me in any way. Funcom designs the game so everyone can feel balanced. Yet they take certain professions balancing items and tell them, Fat chance finding them." You will be lucky to be balanced. Not only that the loot is random. How many Ninjas will start popping up??? How many people will not tell their team what they just looted and go ahead and sell the Disk for 100mill??? You are only going to cause trouble with this.


Smugglers Nerf
HEHEHEHE, only thing I can say is people got what they asked for. You asked for an end to camping. :O)

As I stated in other threads, you can NEVER stop camping by making things spawn less. If people said it was hard to begin with and people where bunched up at one area that tells you its TOO HARD. Not that the times need to be decreased.

The truth is people on average spent 15 hours camping for a set. This is a WHOLE lot of time spent doing something completely boring. The solution is NOT to make it even harder. This could have easily been avoided by adding the spawns on more types of mobs AND increasing the spawn rate on them. Also in adding MK1 on the lesser mobs like the dogs and bots, and leave the MK2 on the NPCs. Instead though you made a bad situation even worse.


What more can I say, Funcom never listens, or maybe its us, the paying customer, that never listens. Both of our messages are clear, just no one hears.



A game is meant to be fun. This game has potential to be Fun. Only thing is the keep screwing everything up and makes it into more work.
