I see MPs asking for Roots and/or other forms of crowd control, now I see someone asking for a HE workalike? Come on guys...be relistic here. You can't have almost every nano line in the entire game.

This is not meant to be negitive, just realistic. I hope pet-pathing gets fixed soon, because that's a major issue for 3 profs right now. (MPs and Engis especially...) However, you can't expect to really get any more "features" in the mean time... MPs have a TON of abilities, including the single most important one in the entire game. MPs can do rediculous damage with an attack pet and a decent weapon combined, soak better than any casting class due to a heal pet, instantly kill off any chance for their opponent to cast nanos (in PvP), are the only class which can self-cast all their nanos (at an absurdly low level, no less), never need a Yalm, are the best Nanoprogrammers, and have a few extra things tacked on (like Evade buffs and Init debuffs) that other fighting classes can only ask for.

Pathing is the biggest problem with MPs, not any lack of features or abilities...and for that, I hope it gets fixed ASAP. =)
