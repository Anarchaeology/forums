Hey, that's almost the same I thought today while I grumbled about pet pathing. Here comes my concrete suggestion:

There are up to three new items (something like a helmet or glasses, all UNIQUE) which cannot used during a fight with your character. You have to sit down to use them and you loose all abilities to fight during usage. You control your bot fullscreen, so you really have to find a safe place to hide. During remote control usage you can control your pet just like you would control your own character in 1st person view. Of course your remote controlled pet has to stay in your area.

Control Device B/W
- applicable to androids (or better)
- your screen is black/white, so you see hardly nothing when using during night or in dark places

Control Device
- applicable to gladiatorbots (or better)
- normal color view

Control Device NV
- applicable to guardbots (or better)
- night vision screen
