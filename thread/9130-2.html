Soleet
I have a few comments since the discussion seems to have degraded to arguing over the same points.

First, it seems that the prevailing feeling here is that NTs have to spend gads of IP on evades and any other direct combat type doesn't have to spend on anything. The fact is that everyone has to spend IP on evades. In the same way, everyone would now be expected to raise nano resist.

The problem with nano resist is this:
With other evades, the chance of completely evading an attack from anyone of comparable level over 100 is slim to none. The reason you raise this, as several of you have said, is to avoid outrageous crits.
Nanos don't have crits (you also stated this and it is not in contention). So either it hits or it doesn't. That is all well and good. Unfortunately this still fits into the normal evade scheme where actually resisting a nano for anyone that doesn't keep the skill maxed is negligable.
You do not keep evades maxed from what I hear. Only high enough that it is fairly certain to avoid crits.

In addition, as mike stated, factoring in resists or interrupts in PvP (yes I know all you do is large scale battles...of which I rarely hear or see) and PvP only is not logical. You just have to hit the first time. That would give any reasonably skilled NT enough time to not only recast the same nano (assuming you have maxed init and have implants that aren't out dated) but you would likely also have time to regain nano (in about .2 seconds it is fairly easy to hit 'sit' 'nano charger' 'stand'.) Or in some cases you may have time to cast a lower level speed-DD. But we'll assume that you can't. You can still chain cast.

Ok, fumbles. Fair enough. So it happens. That throws a little bit of chance in there. Black holes? Bug. At least it sounds like a bug to me. If we're considering how this effects combat, we should be talking not only the day the roll out happens, but if the change will still make sense when they fix problems and get the game to what they wanted it to be from the beginning.

If black holes didn't happen, would it still be balanced? I'm not saying either way because I don't know how often this happens.

Mass PvP. In a team vs team combat...or even a large scale combat, who would your priority target be? The doc? The enemy NT, so you could stun them before they hit you or your doc? I doubt your first priority target is the tank. Sure, we'll still be hitting, but basically this will make all battles turn into "I'll stun the doc, tanks smash the doc, then we go grab a beer."

It make sense and it a good tactic, but think of who this is really going to effect in your so heavily emphasized ppppppppvppppppp.

I personally think that it will make PvP shorter in personal and group battles. I think that it would make it less fun for everyone. What if you were the target. In group or solo PvP?

Battle starts.

You start casting but are stunned right away.

You stand there while the doc dies, the tank dies, you die.

Sweet, glad NT's can stun.

The final point I'd like to make is that you state that you have been stunned by melee combat. Does it happen every time you are hit? You don't like being critted on, but it isn't as common as some would believe.

If the stun effects were added to these nanos with the same chance of effect as a normal combat stun or crit, that would be great. But if it turns into every attack stunning or not going at all. I think that is far too binary.

I'm not saying that it will or will not ruin PvP, but I am saying several people appear to be using skewed logic, or not seeing all of the facts. I agree that NTs need to be made more powerful, but I think that if you start a thread to look at the facts of a situation, you should look at more facts than the ones you think of on your own. Not only the ones that you see, but the ones that others point out to you. Unfortunately this appears to be more of a "you can't prove me wrong because I am setting the assumptions and designating the rules on which you can debate" type of thread.
Guess I'll go back to my home forum and leave you to continue saying the same thing louder and louder.

Have an enjoyable day.

(and again, I do not claim to have all the facts, but I can guarantee that no one else does either.)
