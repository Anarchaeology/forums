The point is more that the ToTW/IS weapons are - compared to current game items anyway - overpowered either for pvm or for pvp. And the vast majority of these weapons (including 100% of the ToTW weapons) are melee.

Tacking more specials on to them would be like giving a flingshot to the manex.

However having brass-knuckles as 'invisible' (looks like you aren't wielding) 1hb weapons with low multi-wield requirements and the brawl and fast-attack specials is a good idea. Scaling them against MA damage with moderately higher max hits, equal min damage, slightly slower speed, and a much lower crit would make dual-wielding them pretty viable PvM for a lot of professions. Docs and MPs, for example, have 4/level 1hb.
