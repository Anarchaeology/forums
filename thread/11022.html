Stealth change to unique items
Since Funcom won't tell anyone about this major rule change, then I guess I will. Hopefully I will get something wrong here so that someone from FC will be forced to actually post something on this change.

While it was not listed on the patch notes, you can now have only one of each UNIQUE item in your inventory, regardless of QL. Thus if you have a Flurry of Blows, for instance, and someone tries to give you a higher level Flurry of Blows, you will receive a message saying something to the effect of "character already has the unique item." It doesn't matter if the item is equipped, in your inventory or in a bag.

I'm guessing that the ramifications of this will only become apparent gradually. One of them is that you will no longer be able to dual-wield IQ Rings. I'm sure there are a lot of other items like this that we will have to change our thinking on after this stealth change. Hopefully Funcom will give us some documentation on this change soon so that we know how it is supposed to work. Otherwise we don't know what is a bug and what isn't. Maybe that's the point...
