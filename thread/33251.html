How about adding Boss MOBs to Solo Missions?
Poll:http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=33253

Of course it would have to be different type of Boss Mob, one that is much weaker and focused to give the solo player a challenge and a chance to get some of the new loot introduced into the game recently.

This could be done similar to the way team mission boss rooms are set up, (or even just have the MOB wandering in a special large room that gets created with the regular mission layout).

The Boss would probably be hard enough to have it alone in a room, or AT MOST with 1 extra henchman.

The Boss would also only drop 2 items to make up for the lowered difficulty of the boss.

Funcom has to realize that not every player has the time to find a team, and run through lengthy team missions.
