Individual Missions are ruined - this must be addressed
Since patch 13.8, quite a few of us have noticed the change in mob behaviour.
http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...threadid=16701

Now that TEAM missions have been introduced with patch 14.0 and I have gone through several of them, I am of the strong opinion that the mob AI was snuck in earlier into INDIVIDUAL missions, prior patch 14.0.

Funcom, you surely cannot be that ignorant of the type of gamers you have playing this game of yours. There are many who do both solo and team missions and for all sorts of reasons. Some only do team while some solo a lot. Bear in mind that casual gamers do not have 3-5 hours to spare to do the kind of team mission concept you just introduced. The amount of time it takes to do a high level mission now only suits the the most ardent with time on their hands.

I do both solo and team. I solo a lot too because I find it a more convenient way to work towards my 1000 token board. And I am not alone, I know many like myself who do likewise.

So I am giving you feedback and I want a response - INDIVIDUAL missions are frustrating to do and this is making AO terribly terribly un-FUN for me.

The total lack of focus in the AO storyline is already such a major disappointment ... lack of guild housing and other org features ... broken static quests ... heavily camped theme dungeons ... no fix for my profession .... and now this.

So read this feedback for all its worth. Eyes are watching your response.

Why was the mob AI changed for INDIVIDUAL missions?
And will this change?
