Long ago, deep in the vast desert, a child and her father sat upon a small rock formation, watching the twin suns set beneath the horizon. The adult was now aging into his late middle years, grey hair cropping up and complimenting the worn wrinkles upon his brow. The cool blue eyes of the man reflected the years of struggle and battle he had survived through. Turning to his daughter, he could see that she also carried with her his blue eyes...yet they were free from strife and worry. They were innocent, pure, and looked upon him with unconditional love.

"This is beautiful, father" she said, turning back to the dance of the twin suns. "I love it here."

The older man nodded. After a moment of silence he said "Then that is why you must fight."

"Fight?" the child said, turning back to her father.

"Yes. Omni-tek would take this from us, take this from anyone. They would destroy this world for their greed, and any who stand in their way. Like us." Her father grew silent a moment, reflecting on his life of warfare and strife. Damn Omni-Tek, damn them for taking his home, his friends, his family!! They would pay for their crimes. "You must fight them, for they are evil, and care nothing for the beauty of human freedom..for the beauty of the world...even the beauty of a sunset."

The girl looked at her father, with mixed emotions of fear and understanding.

"They killed your brother, and caused your mother to die when they attacked our village. They care nothing for you or your family, your homes, your freedom. You are nothing to them, a number, a disobedient employee. That is why you must fight. For your freedom."

The girl turned back to the sunset, letting all her father had said sink in. He knew well that moment, for he had sat there once, many years ago, as his father had said the same things to him.

---

High above the city streets, a son and mother stood looking out through a window. Below them spanned a massive city, a testament and example to human achievement. Tall skyscrapers reached up from all around them, and the whole city glowed with a magnificence in the last rays of light from the suns of Rubi-ka. The view was beautiful, watching the sleek yalms dart across the sky, people milling about in the park below, and the illumination of the city as dusk came and the citizens turned the lights on.

"This is so cool mom, I'm glad we moved to the city." the young boy said. His mother smiled.

"One day, I'm going to go to school, and then I'm going to join an awesome department. I'm going to work in that building there" he said as he pointed out one of the largest skyscrapers. "I'm going to be successful, and my family will never have to worry about anything. I'm going to make Omni proud and be a great worker. Just like daddy."

The mother continued to smile at this, though in her green eyes worry and sadness were all too present.

"I'm going to join Omni-pol, and I'm going to arrest all of those terrorists." the boy said after a moment.

"Luke..." his mother began to say, but the boy continued. "I'll find the ones that..that...bombed us...our home...and killed daddy. I'll find them and make them pay. I'll make them all pay. They are evil, terrorists. They are thieves, trying to take this beautiful world from us. All we want is to better man kind...to bring this to everyone," he said as he spread his arms to the window. "And because of this they terrorize us? Animals..."

The mother could only sigh. It was not the first time she heard these words...her late husband had said them first. And his father before that.

---


Are the ghosts of our past truly gone? You need only look into the eyes of your children too see them haunting you. For children are innocent, are pure. But they can be molded, changed. Can you change the hatred in a man, when all his life he was raised to hate? Can you create a peace in the minds of people, who have learned only to fight?

The answers are not in what we did, but what we are doing. To free our children from this war, you must first release them. Not drag them into it. For they love you, and will follow you. And their children, will follow them.

Demongun wakes up on a bench on Rome Red and slowly sits up.
"Man what a headache... Never again bench, never again."
Quickly he slides his helmet on and starts his morning patrol, breathing the fresh air, squinting at the sun. The park was peaceful and quiet, as usual.

*Whom-Pah!*

The chatter from Bronto Burger hit like a heatwave, turning down the com-systems in his helmet, he felt more at peace with it. Walking behind BB, taking a left turn, then a right. Streets are clear, people running by at times, nothing out of order. A left then another right, the thumping music from Baboons in the distance, nothing wrong, all is quiet. A left turn. The soft music from the Rompa catches his attention as he walks up the staircase and enters.

Nods to the lady in the first hall, opening the door to the mensroom, disqusting - yet nothing out of order. Mainhall of Rompa, just the regulars, all quiet. He turns around and heads back out.

Carefully he looks around, nobody outside. He jumps off the bridge between the two building and walks towards Baboons. Nothing's changed from before.

Inside Baboons there's nothing but the regulars either. Two dancing folks on the deck, atrox in a dress, DJ atrox. In the back there's nothing but dancers and salesfolks. He sighs as he leaves Baboons.

South-East, then east, nothing at the northern park but some friends. He smiles and nods to them, and continues to the southern park - Nothing out of order as he passes the platform, nor the Bronto Burger yet again. The southern park is empty as ever, just some brat, and an engineer tinkering with his bot.

*Whom-Pah!*

Trade... He heads north to the grid, all peacefull by the veterans gathered. Tired he heads to the Omni-Pol HQ and drops his helmet in the first hall, in the corner, running his hands down his warm, sweaty face, freshening it with some water off a sink.

Taking a look around there's nobody here of interest either. Knocking on Uk's officedoor, no answer - He walks in, and places his badge on her desk, turns and walks out, but stops in the doorway - thinking to himself.

"What they did to Lieutenant Marcai was irrasional... It wasn't right, they should only have given him a warning... A peace conference..What's the harm..?"
Vividly he thinks of his two children, the twins, Emy and Marine, and of his girl, Millie.
"Do my kids have to grow up in a time of war? Do they have to put up a fight against the clansmen and women? Do they have to protect Omni-1 from bombers and assassins? Will they be victims of perma-killing weapons...?"

Demongun turns and watches his badge shining in the morninglight of the sun, from the window in General Uk's office.
Saying outloud; "What Lieutenant Marcai had good intentions, I would have done the same, for the kids."
He walks over to the messy desk, filled with papers, and the badge. He carefully touches it with his fingertips. "Will me being an officer of the law help protect my friends and kids from crazy asssassins, or will it only bring more?" Picking up his Sergeant's badge, looking at it. "Would I be of better use somewhere else protecting them?" He sighs heavily and puts his badge back in his pocket and leaves the Omni-Pol HQ.

"I'll leave it up to them."
