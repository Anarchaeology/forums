Agent fixes LOL, How to further screw up the class
Concentration line:
They are all 100% crit buff. They only last 4-6 seconds but as said, they
are 100% crit. You WILL crit with this nano, and can get off some shots
(including Aimed Shot) in that time. The highest one also does +300 damage. They are insta-cast.

Sureshot line:
They are from 15-30% crit buff. They last between 15-20 seconds and can be used once every 2 minutes. They also buff damage, rifle and aimed shot as they go up.

The last one, for example, buffs aimed shot by +100, rifle by
+50 and damage by +200 with a crit increase of 30. The cooldown period of these buffs is NOT being done with a nano shutdown (like the enforcer challenger line and MA controlled destruction line), so Agents can still cast other nanos during this cooldown period, just not others in the same line.

So they take away our ability to cast the 1hour 24% crit buffs and give us a 20 second version instead thanks alot for that Funcom :P

Now to further screw up this situation they give us a 100% crit buff that lasts 6 seconds assuring we will be able to instakill anyone in pvp with an ithaca yet these chanegs dont help in the least for pvm.

So let the nerf cries begin...14.2 Agents = Best pvp profession and worst pvm profession, I didnt think it was possible but Funcom somehow figured out how to accomplish it.
