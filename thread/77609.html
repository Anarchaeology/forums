If your going to have 5% zones then allow for formal alliances
Refering to my previous post on omni bretherin getting attacked by omni towers in a 5% zone. It is not possible to get teams going with allied orgs to avoid this situation when battle is in progress.

Solution is to allow orgs to have formal alliances working within game. This would allow for an alliance chat and perhaps set a limit of 10 orgs can be allied. It should show up when you ID the player as to who your allied. Most importantly you would not be open to fire in a 5% zone where it is controlled by an alliance org and could not target alliance members either in any zone. (except arena)

At least this way if you have an org that you may be at war with even in the same faction you can still attack their towers because they are not in your alliance.

DaOC has this type of alliance setup and it really is only used for communication purposes but it is so much more important for AO considering these zones.

thoughts?

FC pls look into this

Oh I have thoughts all right...
Yes! But I claim we should be allowed to have cross faction alliances as well.

My idea is along this line:

Each controller carries a 'friends list'. Each name added to the list stays there for 30 min after which time it can be refreshed.

If you attack a controller you are banned from the friends list for 30 min.

You can add yourself to the friends list by bringing up a controller menu and declaring yourself an ally.

If you declare yourself an ally then the following happens for 30 min:
1. The towers will not attack you.
2. You can not be tab targetted by owners of the tower or others on the friends list.
3. You can not buff or heal those not on the list.
4. You can not attack the towers nor friends nor owners.

If you are not on the friends list then:
1. The towers will attack you.
2. You can be tab targetted by all those on the list.
3. Once you attack anyone on the list or in the owning guild then you are automatically banned from the list for 30 min.

You might also have only a short time to add yourself to friends list before towers open fire...this prevents 'spys' from lounging around in the zone.

This way Neuts, Clans and Omni can all aid one another.
