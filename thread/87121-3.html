I don't have much to say about the PvP situation. I feel like certain classes have advantages in every situation. Are most classes going to beat an enforcer if they let him get close? No. But what do you expect?

What I do want to say: I used to think of Agents as skilled assassins. After reading this thread, and some experiences in the arena, I think some of them are more like cheap, backstabbing vultures. In mass PvP, popping someone whos already knocked around or in a fight is the way to do it. Theres a war going on, and anything goes in those situations. An Agent staking out a piece of 25% zone, waiting for a kill is actually very cool, and almost makes me want to play an agent.

In the arena however, I like to think that you're there to fight an honorable duel, on the terms arranged by the duelists. Not to be randomly ganked by some scumbag too weak to fight like a true warrior. I personally attack on sight any agent I see ganking in the arena, and I never go to the arena alone, because I know theres lowlifes out there, just waiting to gank a victorious combatant in his weakened state.
