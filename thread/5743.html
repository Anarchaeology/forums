Ebay
I don't think Funcom has Officially and CLEARLY stated their stance on the issue. Sure there is a line about it in the TOS agreement at the end of a paragraph of crap but nothing specific. More in the lines of protecting their interests until they figue out what to do. Here is what I mean.

quote:

9. You may not use any third party software to modify this Software to change Game play. You may not use our intellectual property rights contained in the Game or the Software to create or provide any other means through which the Game may be played by others, as through server emulators. You may not take any action which imposes an unreasonable or disproportionately large load on our infrastructure. You may not sell, buy or auction any Funcom characters, items, coin or copyrighted material.


See what I mean one line and REALLY EASY to miss the line. Not only that with zero statements or enforcement of it that it can easily be thought of as a NO RULE. Something put in to protect their intellectual rights until they figure out how they would want to do when the time comes.

Well here is my stance on it and then I want an OFFICIAL RESPONSE FROM SOMEONE THAT MATTERS. Nothing less than the top guys will do on this one. Sorry cosmik and others, but this is a real rule needing real clearification now. Not something spit out with no thought and no authority to make the statement. So I am asking for a CLEAR AND FINAL statement on this so I won't get banned if I start selling crap :OP

Here is what I think others think of ebay, that are against it.

1. Unfair advantage -- Actually in a game that already has unfair advantages due to, time available, Hardware, Friends/guilds, Connection, Knowledge, other characters, and a ton of other things you need to give people as many chances to succeed as possible. In a fair world everyone would be limited to a set amount of time that anyone could reasonably play in a week. The game wouldnt depend so greatly on Hardware and connection and have a reasonable response to most equipment. Guilds wouldnt be so important to characters developement so you could easily solo if you wanted to. Nothing could be transfered inbetween characters to make noobs unbalanced. The game would be easy to learn so you wouldnt have to spend so much time figuring everything out. Of course we cant do all of that. So our only option is to give every player every advantage to succeed without throwing the game out of whack.

2. Ruins the economy -- Actually no it doesnt. Actually the money goes to noobs that usually spend it in machines more than we would if we kept it. Not only that poor planning ruins the economy and not selling creds on ebay. It's just another place to get your money that was already there to begin with.

3. Gives exploiters a place to sell their ill-gotten gains -- Actually the exploiters will STILL sell on Ebay, they will just do it with a dummy account. Doesnt stop them from exploiting and selling, just makes it so honest players cant do it.

4. Look at all the other games ruined by Ebay -- Actually no game has ever been ruined by ebay. Every MMORPG is still around and going strong despite Ebay. Not only that it wasn't ebay that even threatened the games it was bugs. Heck look at diablo the most exploited game in the history of online games. Yet people still sell items on ebay and still play the game. Not only that it was exploited from hell and back long before Ebay came along. People still played it though because it was a solid and fun game.

These are the major points as I see them. There may be more but these are the major ones that always pop up. The good points of ebay are pretty good and numerous though.

1. Account stability -- Ebay allows players to sell off their characters. This is a good thing because it gives funcom a continually growing customer base since accounts are often bought by currant users that keep their currant accounts. Instead of the continually rebuilding of account stabilty if accounts arent allowed to be passed on. This makes it so the company isnt always worried about going out of business since it continually gets more cash instead of continually rebuilding their cash flow.

2. Out-of-Work-jobless-bastards -- Well it gives them a justification for spending too much dang time on a game :OP Sure they should go out and get a real job, but you know thats not happening :OP

3. Cuts down on the work needed to battle the Ebayers -- Plain and simple it will happen. It's more effective to monitor for the abusers and let everyone else do what they are going to do anyways. Heck it's simple enough to get ebay to pull an infringing auction, But it doesnt end there. They will go to other auction sites trade channels, bulletin boards or any other spot of the day. Heck look at the EQ fiasco, never stopped the ebayers there just made Verant spend a ton of missspent time trying to stop people that had grown used to selling items on ebay. So why not look for the people selling cheats, wayyyyy to many items and start making their lives harder than restricting yourselves to only banning irregularly.


Heck those are a few points I came up with. Im sure there are a ton more out there. Whatever the decision I would like one soon so we will all know the rules as you intend them.
