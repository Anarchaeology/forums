The Atrox Bareknuckle Enforcer? But why?
I gives me grate pleasure in intorducing my new progect-The Atrox Bare knuckle Enforecer. Hold on im not suffering from any "conditions". Please tell me what you guys think of the following theory:

And the other responce is why be a Martial Artist without all the heals and bonus Chi-yin-yang-hironuken-goodies?"
Well this is how it should work(I hope): First off, fists ROCK!!!
Admitedly, while many MAs are raving about the new Hagerzine(you know the word im trying to spell)Claws are great, most of the O.G. Martial Artists I have seen swear by the empty hand.
Now the next reason, why not be an Atrox MA? Well i have seen a small few, and they do really well. The major complaint is the Lag in the Nano department. Most Enforcer Atrox dont experience such a dramatic nano disability.
