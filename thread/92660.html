I cant comment on the pet thing, but I do know how NR works, without using numbers (since we are not getting those from fc, hehe).

It's attacker nanoskills as AMS vs defender NR multiplied by an unkownfactor of aggrobar-setting. Usually the shift of balance is when you have more NR and full def then the attacker has AMS that you have a good chance to resist.

In short, it works just like evades.
