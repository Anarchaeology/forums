Item Recovery
I believe I can explain the reasoning behind the missions in which the target item need not be recovered, only described and scanned with your NCU. You see, a recent development in cryptography allows nano-bots to encode information on the surface molecules of nearly any physical object. An NCU scan will recover the encrypted data, and destroy the nano-encoding on the item in the process. By this mehtod, your superiors will recieve highly classfied information without the knowledge of their operatives. Secrets
Dear Journal,

I have heard no more from the hacker that suggested items could be inscribed micro****ally and/or in Notum. Maybe these new encryption codes will keep out further prying eyes, I can't let wind of my investigations reach back to this Coop I dread what might happen if they were to find out my identity, I only hope they get raided enough by other independant parties to keep them off guard.

On to darker matters...

It is indeed coming clear that there is a deeper darkness to matters on this planet. This substance called notum seems to draw out the worste in people. Not only that, it may even corrupt and effect the very soul of those who are exposed to it for great periods of time.

Not only that, but new contacts within Omni Corp have ventured for me into the subways for me, reporting back some of the shadows which lurk beneath the cities of Rubi-Ka. I've heard tell of caves in the wilderness which seem full of madness, but now, this madness seems to be emerging directly under the places of human populations. The aberations in these realms seem to be directly linked to these affects described above. Wandering souls bodies without minds. Is it the lack of notum in these recesses the cause? Or is it simply the fact that notum's hidden workings has been caught in these recesses, never before seen by the eyes of the sane? Until now?

I can not push from my thoughts the possibility that these secrets are what has brought the ICC to this wasteland of a planet. Since the Clan uprisings, this notum hasn't shown its head among the Omni, this may possibly be in part to the Omni taking more precautions in their mining operations, also they use mutants now to do their dirty work deep in the mines, since it seems these mutants are little more than animals, their minds are simple in their tasks. Therefore, they don't seem to be affected by notum's side affects.

But the early Clan miners, what has become of them? Does Omni know more about the effects of notum on the body than they are admitting? Did these two who were permenantly killed, know something the Clans and ICC didn't want them to reveal? Did THEY know something about the fates of the early miners?

One more thing. This notum seems to have the affect of "trapping" souls. One frightening thought on this... What if this notum was placed here for a reason? If it seems to draw out the evil in the hearts of men, with each layer of notum stripped away, could there be an evil far below the surface of this planet meant to be kept trapped?
