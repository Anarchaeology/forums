Forewarning
I'm sure many of you have read about or seen Chimera on the gridfeed. As some may know, we are currently engaged in several activities to help make the future more secure for the Corporation. Currently we fight an ongoing campaign to clear Stret West Bank of clan mining facilities. We have and will continue to exterminate members of the terrorist alliance BORPACT whenever opportunity presents itself.


We fight and bleed every day in order to keep Omni-Tek safe. But security begins at home.

Members of Chimera have noted some rather... unnerving events at some of the local community 'hang-outs'. Namely, the insistence of certain Omni-Tek individuals that they be allowed to fraternize with the Clans.

A war is being fought. If a member of one faction chooses to associate with the enemy, then they are a threat. Chimera is dedicated to seeking out these threats, and terminating them with extreme prejudice.

To those of you who would cavort with the enemy, note this:

You are being watched.
