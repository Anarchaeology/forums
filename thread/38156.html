Hmm... I play AO to escape from real life, but since the comparison was made...

In real life engineers are more into the "creative" end of things, actually putting the pieces together and and marketing them are pretty much up to the other guys. That's not to say that an engineer can't turn a wrench or use a rivet gun or mix up some chemicals, he has to in order to develop new products and processes.

Another mis-conception is that "Trader" in AO is equated to "Merchant". His main ability seems not to be in the sale of goods, but in the trading of skills... from him to you, from his enemies to his team-mates and so on. So skill manipulation in general should be his strong point, be it nano skills, attack skills, "trade" skills or whatever. I dont have a problem with this.

What I do have a problem with is that there really isnt anything in the game that gives the engineer definition.

Ideally engineers should be the ones you have to come to for improving/modifying things in the game. For instance: wouldnt it be nice to be able to remove the burst or sneak attack requirement from a weapon so that someone could wield it? or add some chem AC or a bit of reflect to elite armor?
This doesn't happen.

My opinion.

I am not all that negative about our skill pattern.

The best buff is not worth a penny when you are not allowed to use an item or nano before level 'x'. Down to buffing machines - looks like shotgun MPs without a healing pet (not to sad about that, actually )
