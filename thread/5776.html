Anti-Root Nano Proposal
I would like to request that funcom makes a General (i.e. found in the general vending machine or simply non-profession specific) Nano formula that gives us ... just to throw out a number a 60% chance to break any root formulas that are effecting us. As I and I'm sure many others are getting really sick of getting rooted just as you kill someone in a mission then not being able to move for upwards of 2 minutes this would come in most handy. I would also like to stipulate one condition so it doesn't throw the balance of the roots out of whack, it can only be cast when you are not in a fight. Now this feature is implemented with vehicles, it shouldn't be too hard to transplant the code for this to use with the anti-root nano. Maybe make also higher level versions of this that has a higher percentage chance to break the root, the percentage being in place to represent your ability to affect hostile nano programs, that and just to make it a bit more up in the air so there is a chance you can run out of nano points attempting to break the root and you just have to wait it out. Please consider this idea, we need something to thwart the after combat root blues.
