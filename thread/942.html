Some suggestions about missions
I wanted to make a few suggestions about making missions more fun and interesting as they're quite boring and repetitive right now. Basically, if missions would take more time and pay a lot more, I would prefer that over the state they are in right now. Make missions that have multiple steps so that they're interesting. Example; I'm at the avalon mission booths. I get a mission which is to go meet an informant in Omni-1 Entertainment, who will give me some special item or documents to infiltrate an omni-tek base in Clon****. I grid to Clon**** and go to the base, infiltrate it and do whatever is needed inside. Then inside I find some mysterious notes that tell me to evacuate quickly and go to Tir etc. etc.
Finally, when I complete the mission, I get paid a nice, decent amount of cash (200-300k for the distances I traveled at lvl81)
Now this would at least make missions more interesting and since you go to different places throughout, it would at least take away the current stale and boring atmosphere of missions. Of course, this idea could be improved more but I'm really tired can't think of anything else =)
