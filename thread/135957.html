Monster descriptions
I think there should be flavor text in the monster descriptions. I can think of two ways this would improve gameplay. First, it adds to the roleplaying experience. Second it gives players something to do if they are caught in a seemingly booring bit of combat. If you agree, please reply to this board saying so, that Funcom may see player support for this.
