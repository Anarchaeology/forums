Battlespam Fix
I have noticed alot of people complain about the quantitiy of battle spam they encounter. This arises from an number of situations. Common ones are:
-> People starting fight in the arena and then moveing into a crowded fight in the middle of town

-> Noobs (in the Backyards) often find it difficult to chat when there are lots of others fighting while they are trying to figure everything out

-> People chain killing NPC guards

Anyway it seems things of this nature annoy quite a few people. At the same time, there are many times when battle spam is usefull (I.E. Team missions). Now of course experienced players know that you can turn off most battle spam by unchecking other hit other in the Misc tab of the options window. Many players don't know this however. And even if you do it's somewhat cumbersome and time consumeing.

Maybe if we could have a macro that does this for us. Like possibly /spam on/off. An even better option would be to let us customize an alternate set of chat window options. For instance if I wanted to RP, I don't want Battle Spam, Team Channels, Shopping Channels, and the like. If there were a way to turn all this off at once that would be nice. Could be more complicated though. But making a macro to switch off the other hit other shouldn't be hard at all I think. Anyway. Just something to think about, it seems to me to be an easy way to improove the quality of life on Rubi-Ka. So post your thoughts and CZ... If you get a chance, your feedback is welcome to.
