What happened to the "impartial media"
I'm noticing an increasing trend in the news periodicals of Rubi-Ka. It seems that the reporters for publications on both sides find it acceptable to use their articles as a place to state their personal opinions. Correct me if I'm wrong, but it was my understanding that media does not attempt to outwardly take sides. They are meant to disperse facts, not represent a specific interpretation of those facts. The purpose of an editorial is for a newspaper to state an opinion aside from the facts. When every article you print is an editiorial, it makes having a separate editorial section a little redundant.

This problem is far more of an issue in the clan's Voice of Freedom, but I see it happening in both publications. It really needs to stop. These newspapers are virtually the only source of information for many of the citizens on Rubi-Ka and by erroneously skewing every little event to one view or another you eliminate the ability for people to interpret all the facts in their own way. I would expect this of Omni, but it is precisely what we're fighting against on the side of freedom.

My complaint is much more geared towards the reporter for the Voice of Freedom. I've spoken with Sandfield numerous times, and I think he is as objective as he can be. However, such cannot be said for the clan's reporter.

I understand that the media is never truly impartial, but there should at least be an -attempt- at being impartial. Bringing up personal opinions of people and actions and telling little stories about yourself is disgustingly impartial and has no place in any periodical other than TAG.

A general press rule of thumb is never to use the words "I", "you", "me", etc. This means things like "I for one" or "I feel" or other things that make it clear that you are stating an interpretation of facts or presenting a direct question to the reader. You'd think this much would be obvious.
