Being able to purchase different layouts would probably be the simplest solution. I've seen a lot of nice apartments on missions, as I crept through them, ambushing and brutally murdering the hapless occupants...

All the furniture is fixed, of course, but that still allows for a little personalisation. The 10 item limit, while still undesirable, becomes less of an issue if you have the basic furniture built-in to the apartment.

A couple of things that would improve the basic apartments:

Something to sleep/sit on in the unfurnished apartment, such as a basic bedroll (seen in missions, again). A proper bed would be nice, but as I'm sure you've noticed, apartment furniture is like the mission chests, you can walk right through it.

Storage that worked. I've got a fridge, a couple of plants, a clock radio, a painting and a drawer unit in my appt. Neither the fridge nor the drawer works as a place to put things. I'd like to see lockers, chests etc. that could be used to store the enormous amounts of spare stuff that the tradeskill professions tend to generate. The bank's fine as far as it goes, but it rapidly becomes disorganised.

There's more on this subject in MissMaul's sticky thread.
