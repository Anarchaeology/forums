Neutrals have the ability to play as most of those types so they end up looking pretty confused as a whole in the end.

Since this is primarily a combat game I think this option should be made more viable: "are mercenaries, outlaws, and private armies fighting anyone and everyone that they can for personal profit."

Everybody can already target them in a 25% zone, I imagine it'd be really fun if neutrals could attack anybody as well. Just think if there was a DB-like Anarchist guild that was very well organized and could have a chance at taking camelot away from both Clan and OT. Or if your smallish Clan or OT guild wanted some Camelot loot but don't really have the chance against Storm and Trinity (just examples, who owns Camelot changes on a regular basis). Just hire some tough as nails neutral mercenaries to fill up another team to give you the advantage.

Furthermore, Borealis could be made into a 25% zone (yeah, I know it'll never happen and the role players won't like this). It'd be the best place to get illegal stims and such but you'd have to watch your back at all times. I suppose humanity in general isn't mature enough to make this viable though.
