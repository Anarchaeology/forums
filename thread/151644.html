Weird little plants...
Weird little plants have been ruining crops on RK lately; especially have the farms in the Andromeda region been hard hit. These are said to be most resilient little weedlings, immune to any kind of planticides and given they have shot roots in fertile ground, turn into ferocious, carnivore and almost alien looking hulk of a catch weed.

We have not been able to get any eyewitness information from the extermination work, except it is said to have been exceptionally hard. Only thing that is known for sure is that in the end Omni-Med and OTAF evacuated and closed off all infected areas and sent in a joint task force to eradicate the pest. A spokesman for the OT department of agriculture says the emergency is now over and the farmers back on the plantations in their usual routine.

If people come across any su****ious looking vegetation, they are advised to stay clear of it and contact the nearest authorities.
