OOC: RP Rambles....
Now this is more of a Utopian type view fo role playing. So don't flame me because I do not even play like this all of the time. I often find myself breaking character while in teams, and while hunting. This is only my opinion, not how I actually play. It is however, how I feel I should play.

Why do I level?

Well, as I state in most of my characters profiles, they all seem to enjoy travelling. In these times it is a must to have some sort of combat skill or you may never really make it outside of a town, or guarded area alive. I power level, and no I don't see anything wrong with powerleveling and RP'ing. When I go out and hunt I se that as a break from role-playing, I consider that time spent on the lines of battle be it against Clan, Omni, or Neutrals. My main two professions I play are a doctor, and a soldier. Thus most of their time should be spent on the field of combat.

Lack of noticeable RP Guilds:

Now there are allot of guilds who claim to RP. Whenever I talk to these people I find them dropping OOC, or using the common wOOt!!!, Leet, Uber terms most regular power levelers use. Table top games always seem to have a constant storyline going, and in a table top game if you wanted to try to kill everyone in the world you could try. So you cannot gain any ground on anyone in this game. Sure you can become a higher level and pound someone. But all it gives you is bragging rights. You can't actually harm that person.

I feel RP guilds need to get together and form a common committe. They need to come up with some storylines to go along with but no run int o FunCom's ideas.

When I first logged into AO after coming back I saw Drakios, a "green name" gettig involved in RP... I nearly cra... well I am sure you know what I nearly did. My point is I thought that it was awesome. Funcom allowing GM type player to get involved! Well after awhile I went looking for those green names trying to figure out what was going on. I got allot of useful information, but I still haven't found a way in. I still haven't been able to crack the barrier, how can I make myself a player? DiamondCut Dave owns a club. I thought yeah I could somehow work under him, or even Omni-Pol! There are so many things I can do! Well... Nobody plays as much as I would like. I just don't see the faces I need to see to get involved, and I try too hard.

I soon learned that the many things to do weren't really that many. In fact there are only a few. Thus, I am starting my own little storyline. It's open ended and hopefully the end result will be a group of Role-Players who will enjoy RP'ing and RP quite often...

Someone in a previous post said something about re-inventing the wheel. I thing this is true. We need to sit down and figure out what will work and what will not. We need to figure out how to get people into storylines... draw people in.... use the dark side of the force for once to manipulate people into our plots and schemes... trick unsuspecting people into storylines and quests and pray they like it. I would love to walk into Rompas, or Reets and just BS about things going on in Rubi-Ka, not things going on in real life.

I think Grid Steam Productions is a great way to do this. They run a show EVERYNIGHT. That in itself is amazing. I don't go to Reet's everynight they are there, but I am a frequent listener. I think they should be the focal point to help round up RP'ers. Start setting up recruiting and learning events to teach people how to RP. Start setting an example by cutting down on the RL chatter and start coming up with stories in game.

Things that are too common:

Marriages, guild wars, lovers tales, and lost person quests.


Things we should see more of:

Covert-Ops missions to obtain a certain item from group A, which can secretly be used from Plan A, which in turn sparks Group B to plan an espianoge mission to spy on Group A, and hopefully obtain the information needed to steal the item in question, as well as figure out Plan A, and stop it. There can be no deaths, A certain Police Force, *coughs* Omni-Pol *coughs* has been watching both group A, and group B very closely. Murder would spark a raid or a mass investigation of the groups.

Complex stories like these are what will draw people in, maybe. The problem is all the organizing required to plan out something like this. Allot of times peop;e pre-write this. I don't believe in pre-writing. What is role-playing if the story is already decided? Let the groups play... see what they come up with... make it a game of the people writing the story against the people playing the roles.

The key is finding ways to get EVERYONE involved. If I knew how to do that I would do it. I am open to put my head together with leaders of current RP guilds to come up with ways to help the community come closer together.

2:30 AM, this may have been like more of a ramble then a post but I hope a brought up some decent points, and I hope people will get in contact with me. Even if you flme me that's fine... I am open to constructive critisism.

A matter of style
It is all very much a matter of each person's style. The guild I'm with, for example, probably wouldn't be classed as a 'pure RP' guild. While we try to stay in-character when we meet one another, our guild channel is extremely OOC - and much of the time people will simply be levelling, or going and experimenting with PvP, or similar.

For different people there are different 'thresholds' of RP. I know many people who consider it shocking that someone would ever dare slip out of character, while I know others who RP only at events. Either is, in my opinion, fine.

* NEVER involve someone in a RP event against their will. By all accounts give them opportunity to join, but don't thrust it upon them.

Running a player-event and GMing a table-top game are two very different disciplines. In one you have a set number of players whom you know well, and no danger of outside intervention. On the other side you have a possibly huge number of players, vast quantities of unknowns and a high possibility of intervention. You've also usually got at least two 'GMs' involved, unless your event is entirely within your own org.

So yes - reinventing the wheel is required. And, IMHO, you get the best results when the events players of everyone involved talk about the event first and actually plan it together. Yes, that may well mean OOC revealing your secret dastardly plan to someone on the 'other side' - but believe me when I say the end result is much more enjoyable, much more smooth-flowing RP events for everyone concerned.

As for 'RP' guilds, how many of the following criteria have to be met before a guild is 'RP' ?

* In-character during events
* In-character when in the same location
* In-character in guild chat
* In-character in /tell
* In-character always unless that particular send is delineated as being OOC

For me, I'm happy with just the top two - which is what I usually play.

Something to help the ball roll
Something we (Omni-Tek Blackwolff Operations) have instigated is an 'event planners' board on our forums. This is a board that has limited access - basically you have to be recognised as a member of an events planning team. Re: my earlier post, this would effectively translate into 'GM for player event.

So far this has proven to be extremely effective. Rather than limiting this to only one or two people, we've got it open to about 8 people within BWO and a bunch of people outside of BWO. When people have ideas for events involving BWO (both internally or externally) they get posted on this board, and from there a smaller subset of the posters then go on to plan the event in more detail via e-mail and private messages.

So far we've only done a couple of inter-guild events via this method. The most recent event we ran with Dragons Coalition was exceptionally fun all round - and I feel that a lot of it went so smoothly because of the 'advance planning' that went in. There were select players on both teams who knew in advance the 'overall event plan' and who worked to keep things flowing in the right direction. For those of us 'in the know' there wasn't anything particularly surprising in the event, but there was the incredible buzz of pulling off an event which everyone participated in and everyone enjoyed.

If anyone else is planning on running inter-guild or inter-faction events, I strongly recommend a org.-specific events board. By making it org. specific you can focus on the needs of every org. involved.

Dr Phunk - I think you need to be careful about classing some types of event as too common. People rescues are one of the best ways to introduce people to RP... especially leader rescues - its an easy RP adrenalin trigger. And likewise - although I think leaving as much to chance as possible is great - if you are running an event with another guild -you sometimes need to railroad the story a bit to make sure there is enough pace to the RP to make sure people don't get bored and go back to leveling.

The advantage of the common events is that they can be used to build on for the future... for example Dragon Coalition have yet to react to the fact that Mech's guild BWo arrested their leader...but they surely will at some point and story will build. My point is that you need that first "common" event as an emotive hook for people who are intersted in RP but not really sure what its all about.

There are a million ways to RP in this game, and they are all valid. There are equally a million things that haven't been done by FC to make RP easy that could have been done. But as the game stands its still perfectly possible to make things happen and many people are doing so. Don't give up hope just cos its not visible!

A Committee on the other hand will only ever be frustrating as lots of people who want to run events in different ways discover they can't work together. Co-operation between a few guilds at a time on the other hand can work and does seem to be providing a lot of people with a lot of fun.

Seek and enjoy... thats what I would say...
