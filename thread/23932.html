Please listen to the role players
Please listen to the feature requests by the role players, take them seriously and get your a** in gear making some of them.

I don't think their cries for features that will support events and role playing have been given the attention they deserve. I am not saying that you shouldn't fix profession or make more high level content ect. - but the role players should be given much more consideration.

There is a very simple reason for Funcom for doing this as the roleplayers will be the people paying the money they depend on in they coming years to keep the game running. The power levellers, monster hunters, PK'er etc _will_ move on to the next game with better graphics or cooler features. Those who stay with the game are the role players that find something more lasting in the game.

This summer there is Neocron coming out and next year we will see Star Wars Galaxies just to name to sci-fi MMORPG coming out. With each of those there will be a great loss of players. Those who stay behind will be the role players ... if there are some features that will support us in building a good RP environment on Rubi-Ka.

The plain truth is that while the role players are not immediately threatening to leave the game (like so many other fractions of players on the forums) they are needed to keep the game alive. Personally I cannot see myself playing this otherwise good game in a year if there are not wastly improved RP features put in place.

-Neph

Story? Bah... it'll never happen...

I've seen better back-stories for FPS shooters... and more developed plots on the backs of cereal boxes...

What FC has is a story that's 1-page long, and they're stretching it out to take 4 years to tell?

I've done a bit of RP, but as far as the "official FC story" is concerned, the game is just a blank template for RP'ing. Sure, there are a lot of good "featuers" that allow you to RP just fine - as long as you ignore the official story.

I was digging through my game CD's the other night, and I realized that while playing Planescape: Torment, I usually experience in 5 minutes the amount of story I've experienced in AO in over 500 hours... :/

The AO story has 0 draw for me... I enjoy casual RP'ing (I'm not hardcore, in other words, but I try to make RP'ing at least 10% of my game experience - which is a lot I think for AO ). I also enjoy the fun things in AO. I think exploring in AO can be fun, I think some of the sights/places are cool, I think it's generally a neat environment to play around in... I think FC deserves kudos for creating a fun environment in that respect, actually.

But if all I wanna do is log on to "mess-around..." Well, I can buy The Sims to do that for 40$ and forgo the monthly 12.95$ charge, thanks very much. If FC wants to retain customers long-term, it needs to do some work on its story, and I don't mean writing another "AO novel." I mean when I do a mission in the game, I want it to affect the story, or have a direct relation to a story event.
