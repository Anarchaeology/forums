Portable insurance terminals and save pills
Many of the missions are usually in the wilds and far from a safe save spot. And the game needs a money sink. Why not sell portable save terminals that only works on team members. Place the terminal in the entrance room. The terminal should only have a limited amount of uses, and only be used once per person.

The terminals could be quite cheap (a few thousands), but when saving, the price should be quite high depending on lvl. When the terminal has been dropped in the entrance room it cannot be picket up again.

If the character dies he/she will be respawned at the reclaim terminal closest to the mission area. Just hope that you don't get caught in a killer loop

Save pills should work much in the same way, but they should be very expensive and be level specific in some way. So if you buy twenty and suddenly increase in lvl very fast, you might find them too low for your use.
