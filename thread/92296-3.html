I have been an avid roleplayer for the almost a decade now (damn i am actually getting old here), and I always try to roleplay a bit to get to know the area.

For instance, my current character, Aloque, is a somewhat pessimistic nano technician who wants to prove his own pet theory of sub infusion. He hates the Clanners because they shut down his research after some accidents, and loves Omni because they give him the chance to work on his theory. He feels very loyal to them because of this.

Now what happenend IC is that I started talking to a few Omni people explaining sub infusion theory (whatever it may be) to them, and stating that I was desperatly short of funding. I was always being perfecly 'Omni' polite, calling everyone mr., sir, ma'am, dr. or whatever. Offering to field test equipment for them, share research results etc. Sure enough one of them took an interest and after a while gave me (level 4 at that time) a staggering (for me at least) 250.000 credits for 'research funding' and a backpack full of various items to 'test'. Most of them QL 70 or above.

So now Aloque is walking around with his subinfuion tech proto types, complaining that he has needs access to even more technology. Also I am handing out 'subinfusion' items to new players asking them to field test it for me. Most of them like the concept. Altough I get the occasional 'thanx d00d' response and nothing more.
