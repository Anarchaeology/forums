I want to sum up some other things and thoughts that I have, these things are not all related to nanos.

  • - Take out the calculator and estimate the IP-cost of all these new nanos. We don't want to wind up in a situation where we have to max out both Psy.mod and Time&space to make use of them because there aren't enough IP for that.

    - Make cat smaller or give us some way of shrinking it while indoors. I constantly get complaints about blocking the view for other people while doing missions and I myself also find it too big.

    - Take away nightvision on the Wolf forms. It serves no purpose what-so-ever. Night vision goggles cost 100 creds for those who wish to use them.

    - A whole lot of these new nanos are self-only. I guess and hope that that fact makes them better but I would still like some means of contributing to a group with buffs.

    - Consider giving the cat a slight HP-buff as well. It would make it attractive for buffing other players and would aid us while soloing.

    - No new morphs introduced. A slight disappointment, but I understand it would give you more work than just doing new nanos...

    - First person view on at least cat should be looked over. When using bob camera, the whole scrren is jumping while fighting. When I use no-bob-camera I have a hard time seeing through the mouth of the cat.

    - Charming animals beside leets-for-fun. Really consider this, we are the rangers and the wilderness guys. Shouldn't we be close enough to nature to request assistance from more animals?

    - AC buffs, our line of AC buffs stop at lvl123 (I think). Why?

    - "Make flurry fly" has been a slogan for a long time...

    - There are some issues with the animation of the current morphs. I don't have the details but the cat still have the 'sputtering effect' when using two pistols at least. The morph appear sometimes to get locked up when attacking, making it shake and it stops hitting.

    - Pit lizard, landing and taking off doesn't work very well. Need to be looked at.

All-in-all, I'm looking forward to the 14.4 patch with great interest. Please release as much info as you can before 14.4. I would like to know what's up so I know how I should the IP-reset.
