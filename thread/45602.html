I'm afraid that in this instance, it does. Normally I take anything on Virral's site as gospel, but in this instance it just doesn't work.

Hitting the trimmer in combat causes one of two things to happen:
  • Some or all of the mobs switch aggro to the bot pretty much immediately,
  • Nothing at all.
Regretably the second answer is all too common, but when an aggro trimmer works it works straight away.
