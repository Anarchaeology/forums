Borealis Underground - Recruiting (Australian/Kiwi/UK/European players particularly)
The Borealis Underground is a neutral organisation devoted to the benefit of our members. We participate in activities ranging from contracted arms consultant to supplier of various pharmaceuticals to our strong and growing client base. Were a criminal guild that welcomes all forms of expertise. Sure, perhaps our numbers are few at this stage, the goods we peddle, not the highest quality available, but this organization is run by a strong leadership determined to see us achieve our objectives.

We like to call our group the family. Some of us may take a slightly moral stance, like Thawne, others are willing to sell anything to anyone for a price, and some of us have a real attitude. Its always fun, its quite often interesting, and its always in character.

New to the criminal way of life, but always been curious? We can help.
Looking for a change to the endless mob hunt? We can help.
Wish to RP an interesting idea with like minded individuals? We can help.

Were currently planning our first major event: An Underground Market. All sorts of items will be sold at this event and it promises to be a real bash

So if youre interested in pursuing the criminal life, give us a /tell planetside, and we will endeavor to set you on the right path.

The BU is Atlantean only atm.

/tell Thawne if your GMT +8, +9, +10 or +12
(Australian/Kiwi and Asian players)
or
/tell Frostmane if your GMT +0, +1, +2
(U.K and European players)

or email mattius@iinet.net.au

For a further insight, visit the Borealis Underground website. Keep in mind a good proportion of the site is for members only.
