That's odd. I spent the last two hours there (as Slotine) and only encountered one cursing shouter - triggered by mention of Tir. The other weird annoyance was a fully armored table dancer (another customer) that jumped on the table I was at.

The "let everyone have a fair chance at running things" comment is misguided as everyone DOES have a fair chance of running things there. The only thing stopping someone is common courtesy. Which luckily, a great many other RPers do have. However, if you wish to forgo courtesy, there's no reason why you couldn't setup shop there yourself.

As for the occasional outbursts and poor behavior of certain customers, I don't think it's fair to blame it on the girls working there. They've been courteous to the extreme, promptly asking those who come in if they need drinks, and discretely asking shouters to please keep it down. If the unruly customer continues, then what would you have them do? Throw them out? Is that 'fair'? - to use a word you're so familiar with.

Indeed, I was even surprised when one of the Vixen girls was asked to table dance at a private booth. Knowing that some people find such things distasteful or are uncomfortable being near one, she was polite enough to ask all the others sitting around the table if they would object.

As for Meister's comment "FC is far away from having a good RP game.", that's only because strong RP can only occur when both parties are consentual. Vixen's girls know this well, as evidenced by the fact that they ask before doing. They don't force a bottle of Omni-Champaign down your throat. Nor do they start table dancing in front of you unless you agree.

No, the only BAD RP I see is from someone who wants it their way regardless. If you want to talk about maturity... that's the thing to look for. Not something as silly as a string of letters that happen to form a curse word.
