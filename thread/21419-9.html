I don't want to be uber but I do want to be balanced. And I really really don't want to be nerfed again. So I have a question for all of you, what is an agent?

Most agents that started from release would say that an agent is a sniper that uses concealment to pick and choose his battles. Starting off as many battles as possible with aimed shot and the outcome of the battle is dependent mainly on how often he crits. We aren't tanks, we have little to offer a team other than our damage and pulling capability. Our health is low and our evades are low so we are more team dependent to level. Our primary role in a group is to pull for the group and attempt to cut the mob down to a manageable size by the rest of the team at the beginning of the fight. Very similar to the role an NT generally plays but with some slight variation in the technique. The new crit buff nanos and many of the other new agent items would help us achieve that and be unique as an agent. I don't want to have to rely on going a different profession in order to find a team. I want to be effective in a team based on my agent abilities alone. I should only need to switch to a different profession to use buffs that help compliment my core abilities or to help compliment the core abilities of other professions in the group. Frankly if I had to choose what needs to be improved and what could be reduced in effectiveness then I would choose the alternative profession nanos. I really grow tired of getting nerfed everytime I find a way to be even somewhat useful in a team. And as long as we can use another professions nanos every profession will be jealous of this eventhough we can't use them as effectively as they can due to the penalties associated with using an alternative profession nano. And this jealousy just causes us to be more and more gimped. In order for us to have an identity currently or a role in the group we are forced to borrow the identity of another profession because we no longer have our own identity anymore.

What does it take for people to stop asking for nerfs? The reasoning behind asking for nerfs against something due to its effect on PvP is complete and utter nonsense. PvP will never be balanced for 1vs1 combat for every profession. PvP balance only stands a chance if it is balanced toward group PvP. The idea of dueling belongs in an arena and shouldn't consume the essence of PvP. PvP should mean something more than just some title next to your name. There should be something at stake when you PvP and until there is a good reason to PvP its only good for boosting ones ego about how "uber" they are. The only way it will ever be balanced is if its balanced for group PvP. Just as the rest of the classes are now being balanced for group PvM.

Once balance is achieved for a profession while in a group then and only then should solo play be looked at for that class. At lower levels mobs are weak enough that a player can achieve enough of an edge over mobs to be able to solo. But in higher levels we only have an edge over mobs when we play in a group. Currently none of the professions are truly "fixed" or balanced yet. Right now the only profession I would even consider remotely close to balanced yet would be the MA. They are no where close to being "fixed" but there is a solid backbone for them to being balanced in the future. Healing capability, good evades, good hitpoints, good combat nanos, low base chance to crit with MA attack, and a high crit buff usable for extended periods of time. All in all a very good backbone for being balanced in the future.
