A call to all RK2 Omni Org Leaders
I would like to suggest structuring a bit the way Omni orgs (on RK2 where I am) work together.

It could be done in different ways, but here is one proposal:

I thought we could create the RK2 Board of Directors.
* each omni org would would have 1 representative (some mentionned having conditions and not opening it to each and every org, hence focusing on active ones - eg. orgs with at least 20 members)
* we would have regular meetings together (weekly? monthly?...)
* creating such a Board of Directors would not be a pb with the official storyline (we wouldnt say that Ross is chairman of the board etc..)

Example of use we could think of for such a Board:
* Organize large-scale events
* Influence the storyline
* Voice OOC remarks to FC
* Settle issues between Omni Orgs
* Have a common voice when talking to the clans
* etc...


Let me know what you think, this is an idea, we can do it differently, but i think it would be good to have it started somehow. Or /tell me in the game to discuss it live (/tell nikh)
