OOC: Thoughts on Omni Fixers
Here's something I was pondering in another post on AO Vault earlier today. Thought it was more fitted for this crowd.

--
One thing I've always wondered is what an Omni fixer is (I'm sure Nevver can help with this). To me a fixer is someone who "fixes" things, no questions asked. Someone whose specialty is to bend the rules to solve problems, as well as having plenty of helpful contacts in the shadier parts of society. Also, it would be a very individualistic person, who doesn't take orders and don't care much for hierarchies.

Han Solo is the archetypical fixer in my mind, but it's also a cowboy type character (Han Solo is a lone rider, after all).

How does this rhyme with swearing allegiance to the company, which is all about rules and hierarchies and The Law?
--

Along these lines, I've been thinking that it would be reasonable if some professions were only availbable in some factions. It would differentiate the factions more and make one or the other side more attractive to certain personalities. Any thoughts on that?

Well... I reckon that all the 'shady criminalstuff' can seem not very compatible with the vision of OT in itself at first sight, but all in all, I don't think it's really a problem. OT is a very big company, and I'd doubt everybody in there completely plays by the rules. You need the shady ones to counteract the shady ones from other factions. As Myers wrote, you need the hackers to counteract the other hackers. You need people who are willing to take some risks in order to obtain info. And Aniee has the point too, because if thinking along a logical RP line, OT has the money, and the best means to obtain it. After all, it's easier to hack your way in if you're already in the place, so to say, and have certain access. It's certainly appealing to know that you'll be able to obtain the right tools for your job. And in a certain twisted way, having a little control over your head can divert attention and allow you more maneuvrability.

On another hand too, I'm not sure at all that NO crime exists within OT. Let's get honest, the company must most likely have its lot of corrupt Crats, of weird Docs doing their stuff in their dark little corner, etc etc. A Fixer in this has to act slightly differently from a Clan Fixer, but in the end, there will always be people in need of certain... services. Even if in a way, maybe the OT Fixer would best fit the hacker theme and the Clan/Neutral one the smuggler theme, so to say. They can live on the fringe of the company, operating in less watched areas. Or be employed full-time, on the contrary, maybe as the counter-hacker, maybe to play the shady type and obtain things better than they would if they were 'officials'... Is it really a barrier to imagination? I don't think so...

Allright. Now who here hasn't understood yet that I am an OT Fixer?
