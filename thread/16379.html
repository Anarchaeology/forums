MA-Confrence about Issues
Well we saw it work for MP's they got together, started discussing changes and made a big enough force that funcom had to listen to them. They just started having meta confrences and discussing problems they had, and stuff they wanted added... why not do the same thing for MA's?

FC is starting to fix us somewhat, but we still have a lot of problems out there.

One thats big on my list right now is getting MA special attacks to get there NoDrop and Unique taken away. It really makes no sense as to why they are nodrop in the first place. Now with the new unique changes it makes them being unique a pain in the arse also. Currently when you find a new MA attack or buy one, if you have one in your invintory already it just deletes the NEW one. As it stands you have to delete your old one, or put it in the bank (if there isnt one there already) to upgrade to new one. Last night i spent 2 and half hours and 37k on a mission terminal to find a new BwT. Upon completing the mission i forgot to delete my old BwT so i didnt get my mission reward. Do soldiers have to delete there guns when they find a new one? No. Then why do fist MA's (and possibly soon weapon MA's) have to delete our old "weapons"/attacks when we upgrade? Unless Funcom can give me one good reason why they are nodrop and unique in the first place...i want this changed. Giving us the option to sell/give away our old special attacks would be nice.

Another issue we have is parry. As far as i have seen, and others have seen, it doesnt work. or it doesnt work often enough to be noticable.

Riposte. Another practicly worthless skill. i have 200somthing in it and i do a fearsome 2 damage back to the target for 1 or 2 hits.

Dimach. It is great at low levels, but the higher you get the less return we get from the IP we invest in it. Im level 98, have around 300 points in dimach. i crit on brawls for 1500 at highest, 1300 or so on punchs. dimach hits for 1000 then locks for 30 somthing minutes. Yes its a good skill and worth it to invest in anyways, but compare it to full auto. Full auto can do more damage then dimach and has a 5 minute timer? (correct me if im wrong on this one, as i dont play a character with full auto)

Evade's and Evade nanos. id like to see evade made more effective. along with that our MA evade nanos dont seem to do much since the raise in points is so low.

Sharp Objects. Where are they? I've seen it mentioned that its a low priority for the devs. If your going to make it a low priority, then make bows a lot better so we have a viable option when we are rooted at lower levels. As it stands now, MA's cant really fool around with ranged weapons till 100+ without gimping other needed skills.

Bows. Special arrows was a step in the right direction, but they still need work to be made a viable option. Possibly a bow with MA as main req, and being able to punch while using one? (possibly even brawl?)

more MA weapons. currently to my knowledge, there is no 2 hand edged and 2 hand blunt MA weapons. Possibly some glove-like weapons and fist weapons like brass knuckles or spiked knuckles. Right now the only real viable MA weapon is the torture tool.

MA special attacks while using a weapon. Cosmik mentioned that this was in the planning. possibly give us an ETA on this?

Oh and a small one, but would be a nice touch. Make us kick more often!!! in my 90 levels, i have seen myself kick 3 times. Im getting tired of being a boxer =P

theres probably a lot more issues out there, post replys with your ideas, and comments on what i have posted here or just your support.

-Xenok level 98 Opifex Clan MA

lets see
Just off the top of my head, here's a rundown on things that have been fixed:

* Attack of the Snake now works above QL100.
* Bow Special Attack has been implemented.
* Fist damage has been increased quite a bit.

Things that Funcom is officialy working on:

* Fists going on Phys Init instead of Melee Init.
* Fists making damage appropriate to the fist nano running.
* Dualwielding fists/MA weapons.

Things that Funcom has hinted they are looking at:
* Parry/Riposte getting an overhaul.
* Letting us do MA special attacks while wielding weapons.
* Recharge on MA heals.

Things not commented on by Funcom:
* Efficiency of Dimach (Drops dramatically at higher levels)
* Efficiency of Nano Resist (Wierd that the scourge of the nanocasters dont have a NR buff methinks)
* Efficiency of Evades (MA's have taunt nanos. Evades are our tanking capability. Does it work well?)
* MA special attacks NODROP/UNIQUE. (I can live with the NODROP, but UNIQUE tag? Boggle.)
* Damage output of MA weapons. (Quite sub-par now. Could perhaps be helped with Phys Init fix.)
* Sharp objects not in game. (I would like my shurikens with vanilla. 8P. Please.)
* General state of MA Special Attack damage output/MA skill lock time. (Need I say Angel of Night?)
* Flower of Life MA special attack not working QL100-199. (Need I say Gah!?)
* Cohort and Horde nanos broken. (...)
* All the other comments in this thread.

So, we have 3 things fixed and 3 things in the works. Now, these were all major issues (except the AoS thingie perhaps) but there is plenty more to be done with the MA class. They started in the right end thou, we are all very happy with causing more damage.

But personally, I started as an MA to dodge, duck and be a nimble little friggin annoyance that opponents just couldn't hit and kill. Right now I'm a slugger. A boxing slugger.

As for fixers having the best evades, Hell ya! They need all the help they can get. But they dont tank much, do they? They dont have taunting nanos, do they?


I think it all boils down to what a MA is supposed to be. In my opinion, we are the 3rd tanking class, the healing hybrid.

Now, our healing sure cannot keep up with incoming damage and evades doesn't help avoiding that damage well enough. High Parry and Riposte skill might get our shields running for a few seconds, but shouldn't these skills be useful in their pure form?

1 on 1 selfbuffed I would guess that the MA holds his ground quite well, but put a crit buff on any other profession with a weapon and MA's are pretty dead meat unless we get in range and pop off a Shen.

Hehe I'm ranting. Anyways, the basic point of this is that Yes! We got a good damage output boost, and more is coming with phys init/fist nano fixes. But No! I say we should NOT stop asking for things.

We need to define what we are supposed to be, and then ask for the tools so we can be just that.
Are we the "scourge of the nanocasters"? Hardly.
Are we doing the "most damage over time"? If the other professions doesnt get our critbuff, perhaps.
Do our nano lines make sense? "Give Life: 100", No. "Give Life: 1000"? I would use that if a teammate is dying.
Is it fun that everybody are using BoP until they can use BwT and then switch to Shen when in PvP?

If we get the tools, then we can diversify and find different ways of using them.
