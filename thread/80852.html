The story of Tinwolf
Hello fellow residents of Rubi-Ka!

I've grown much since my early days on Rubi-Ka, and thought that others might find the experiences interesting to read. To ensure that I'm on the right track with that belief, I've decided to post the beginning of a story that I'm working on. Any and all comments are welcome, positive as well as negative, so long as they are constructive. Here then is the beginning of Tinwolf's saga. If there is enough interest I can post further installments as they are completed.

==============================================
SECRETS OF THE WIND --PROLOGUE (By Tinwolf, thuldar@hotmail.com, First Draft October 10th, 2002)
==============================================
"This'll never do, Maria."

The man spoke softly but with a quiet conviction in his voice. Shaking his head ruefully, he looked down with sadness upon the remains of what had been one of his finest creations, its delicate circuitry showing through its once-sleek metal skin, now just an assortment of useless components. At least in HER eyes, he thought bitterly. The setting sun cast a deep crimson over the vast oval field that only moments ago had served as a battleground for all manner of creatures. If there was one thing that this little piece of hell was known for, it was these bloody games. An engineer by trade, Benjamin had had to make do with contract work ever since his company had been folded into a larger competitor and then forced to downsize. Leaving his cushy corporate life behind had been the most torturous decision of his life; but the principles that Durotech had stood for had been swept away through the acquisition, and his life's work had been neatly packed into a corner by Mirabelle and quickly forgotten. He wasn't about to allow his revolutionary designs in artificial canine battlefield companions to simply go to waste on the say-so of a few bean counters. But these accursed arena battles were surely showing the weaknesses in his creations. There was a great deal of work to be done before any of them would ever be truly battle worthy. He caught a glint of metal from the corner of his left eye and looked up just as the last of the spectators were flying away from the arena, presumably on the way back to their shuttles and then to their starships silently orbiting the planet like ancient sea creatures of old. He quietly cursed his luck and spat on the ground, causing a tiny cloud of dust to rise up where the spittle touched the ground.

"How was I to know that the micro-fusion reactor wouldn't be able to draw enough power for the jaws or evasive routines? Damn it all, why NOW of all times?" He thought back to the promise that he had made with that little toad of a man; without a doubt, Samson would be out for blood after this little turn of events. He slammed his fist into his left palm with a loud meaty thwack. Ignoring the sudden stinging in his hand, he turned around to face his wife. All around them, worker bots of all shapes and sizes were already moving in to take away the remains of the dead gladiators. He shooed away a winged bot as it landed on the silent form of Kira. Absently, he noted the beauty of its perfectly symmetrical body and tiny dragonfly-like wings. Sighing to himself, he bent down to examine Kira's damaged optical array and thought about his predicament.

Thinking of the difficulties in his life brought him back as usual to thoughts of his wife. If there was ever a more graceful and elegant woman to be found on this little dustball, Ben had yet to find any that approached Maria's natural beauty. With long, shiny black hair that reached down to her hips and chocolate skin that perfectly matched her hair, she was quite a stunner. Her legs were her best features in Ben's mind. Lovely, tapered legs that looked sexy in just about anything had been high on Ben's list of must-haves in a wife at the time he had met Maria; he still couldn't believe his good luck. He really was a lucky man. Taking stock of himself, he wondered yet again what it was about him that had made her accept his wedding proposal so readily. He wasn't an ugly man; he regarded himself as something closer to plain or perhaps even nondescript. But for all that, he did have a good physique. Part of his upbringing as a pure-strain Solitus, he supposed. And he was good with his hands, the awareness of which had encouraged him to follow the path of an engineer. It had been a life of creation and discovery, and he had loved every minute of it. No regrets there. But now their marriage was very nearly on the rocks; it wasn't that she didn't love him anymore (far from it, if last night's lovemaking had been any indication), just that she wanted them to return to something approaching a normal existence and he never seemed able to deliver. It was becoming a real problem between them. To complicate matters even further, Ben had been required to get a loan from the local loan shark Samson to pay for much-needed maintenance on Kira and to provide for some research funds. This last series of battles was to have paid off the debt to Samson in full, and yet Ben found himself worse off than before.

Maria simply let out a quiet sigh as she mussed Ben's short sticky hair with both hands; Alvaris IV was famous for its scorching summers and this evening was no exception.

"Sweetheart, I really think it's time to give up these dreams of yours. We have to make a new life for ourselves, and I hear that Omni-Tek is offering some very reasonable conditions for anyone willing to make the trip to that new mining outpost of theirs. It's time to start building a HOME again..."

Ben quickly got to his feet, a dull fire evident in his eyes.

"What, this Rubi-Ka you're so fond of? Honey, do you know what you're asking me to do if I ever accept such a position? First off ," and he spat on the ground before continuing, "there's the issue of Omni-Tek itself. Durotech was built on the principle of research efforts that would benefit all of mankind, not just certain market segments, something which I'm sure you've noticed Omni-Tek hasn't been the slightest bit interested in doing. They're a finacial empire all unto themselves, constantly using talented people and spitting them out when they're through with them, and I want no part of it. I just want the opportunity to further develop my creations." He was becoming frustrated with these seemingly never-ending debates; why couldn't she see that all he wanted to do was to provide a range of useful security systems for the common man? Was that really so out of touch with reality?

"And I suppose you're going to tell me next that nanobots WEREN'T one of the greatest inventions to ever have been introduced? How could you forget that Omni-Tek were the forefathers of nanotechnology? What's next? That these, these THINGS," she shuddered as she spoke the word and pointed her sandaled foot at Kira, "are also a 'great contribution to society'? Do you really expect me to believe that you'll be the saviour of humankind?" A look of disgust crossed her striking face so quickly that Ben almost thought he had imagined the whole thing. "If you don't sell off one of these prototypes soon, we won't have any money for food. Is this what you call 'stability?' If you can't get your act together, don't expect me to be around much longer..."

It was true, and something that Ben grappled with every night, long after Maria would go to sleep. It was also becoming an increasingly annoying point for her to have to bring up. As if the lackluster performance of his prototypes wasn't bad enough, the couple were becoming increasingly dependent on the meagre earnings that these little arena games were bringing in. Originally designed for Durotech as replacements for flesh-and-blood security units, the research efforts had been cut short by the untimely arrival of Mirabelle. Still, Ben had simply stubbornly assumed that his prototypes would have had natural merit in the eyes of potential investors. But everytime that Kira or one of his other creations would suffer extensive damage, he was faced with the prospect of having to sell them off as spare parts to cover basic living expenses or hope for the best and see if they could go on to win a few matches. This time he had opted for the latter and the results could not have been more disastrous. Caught off-guard while engaging a twelve-legged arachnid from Paltonia in a deadly dance of death, Kira had been nearly split in two after a massive Tyraxnoid had slammed into her side and immediately caused an overload of her internal systems, nanobot repair systems notwithstanding. But really, to be fair, this had simply been the icing of an especially distasteful cake; all day long, due to an irregular output from the micro-fusion reactor, Kira had simply been unable to avoid what would normally have been clumsy, ill-performed strikes. His temper had already reached an all-time high long before discovering the faults in Kira's jaw mechanism. At least her ability to partially match her surroundings to confuse any attackers showed promise, Ben thought. That was something, at least.

Clearly, something had to be done. And fighting with his lovely wife over this would get him nowhere. Ben was nothing if not level-headed. But there was also an explorer deep within him. He had tinkered with all sorts of machinery in his life, occasionally coming up with things that he would always believe to be very useful to society, but would turn out to be of limited use to anyone. But this time, THIS time he knew without a shadow of a doubt the he had stumbled across something of value. He just needed the means to see his vision through. Perhaps Rubi-Ka would offer him the answers he needed...

"You're...right, of course. It's just..." He turned his face away so that his wife couldn't see the tears beginning to well up in his eyes. To think that all of his hard work had come to this... " It's just that these little guys aren't just random pieces of spare parts for me, honey. It's like they're my children." His hands clenched into balls of flesh, and he turned back to face her once again. "I'm sorry for putting us in this situation, Ria. Maybe I DO need to look at my options now." There. He had said it. As his hands unclenched, he thought of the future, in particular of the far-away world of Rubi-Ka. It was settled then, at least in his mind. Maybe he could make a new generation of mechdogs there...

"You haven't called me that in ages. I've missed it for so long..." Her fingernails gently stroked his face, and Maria's face brightened visibly at the suggestion of leaving Alvaris IV. Homeworld or not, this was no place for a lady, and she certainly considered herself to be a lady at the very least. She leaned forward and ever so slightly bent down to kiss her husband's forehead. At least she was finally making some progress with her husband the dreamer. Dreams COULD come true, after all...

********************


The hardest thing for Ben had been leaving Kira behind. But the debt to Samson had been considerable, and his cash reserves had run dry; and, as he had predicted, the pudgy little man had tried to accept Kira at a lower value due to her damaged condition. In the end, they had settled on Samson accepting her on the sum value of her components prior to her last series of matches in the arena. Judging by the excited gleam of his eyes, Samson had been fully aware of the considerable deal that he was getting, and waived the remaining portion of the loan. That made the loss of Kira all the more unbearable. She had been his finest work to date, and there was no doubt in Ben's mind that Samson would try to sell her off to any interested party, or possibly even try to manufacture an entire line of Kira-grade mechdogs (as he tended to think of her). And Ben would be powerless to do anything about it. Samson was Kira's master now.
