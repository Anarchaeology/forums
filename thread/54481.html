Cz, Cosmic: Profession Descriptions
quote:
PvP strength
Not all professions are intended to be equally strong in PvP. Some are good at PvP (which technically is more or less a solo game right now), others are better in teams or with e.g. sneaking or trade skills. (No, that does not mean Agents and Traders are supposed to be worst at PvP and teaming. It's an example.)

I'll try bring the issue of profession balance up with the Professionals. Just never expect all professions to be balanced in all areas. Some will excel in some areas, some in others. Some might be ok in all, but master of none. We simply don't have 12 different areas where professions can excel.
I think as paying customers we should be given more profession related information.

Don't take me wrong. It is fine, if you do not intend that every profession is suitable for PvP, AS LONG AS you inform your paying customers about this. They should have the right to know which profession suits their playing style the most. Fancy names often misdirect people towards completely wrong profession for them.

Please outline what are professions strenght vs weaknesses in the character creation chamber aka trade skills, solo, player vs player, team, multi-skilled etc. This should not be too much to ask.

This would also help you guys to retain some customers. It would not probably be able to heal old wounds, but at least avoid problems in the future, so that people would not have unreasonable expectations for their professions.
