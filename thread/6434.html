Mech/engineering: Building cars and planes
Not really many do take any advantage of the tradeskills, exception is probably only to try a special gun or build the rare nano. Problems are several, so i will not go any further into that part of it, really.
But what COULD be done, is to insert an option to build cars and planes. Actually building it would cost somewhat lesser (or same) as regular vehicles, and the time spent building it would be far more than doing any large mission. XP would of course be good too.
Also, a sideoption of this would be to make the vehicle no-drop. I see no reason in why this could affect economy anything, but you could actually add some more gameplay, and have cars and planes as rewards or prices in contests AND make tradeskills more wanted.
Oh, dont forget, if the parts to MAKE the vehicle would be same as buying the vehicle, disregard arguements on giving low levels expensive vehicles, as you just as well could give the low level the money for it... This feature would mostly be for gameplay, variation and XP (building nanos etc for fun is... just for fun. Lousy XP and the money needed is too darned high)
