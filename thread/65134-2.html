Originally posted by FunCom
We have decided to make the following game balance changes:

- reloading a weapon while in combat gives an advantage to users of weapons with small clips, as there is no reason to use a weapon with a large clip. you must now zone in order to reload.

- the ability to sneak up on a mob and surprise it by attacking is not an intended feature. initiating combat is no longer possible, as this makes xp-gaining trivial.

- an engineer was reportedly able to get an enforcer to 75% life in pvp. this has been fixed. engineers may now only attack themselves in pvp areas.

- when zoning into a mission, all mobs will now warp to the staging room to remedy the problems with pathing.

- due to pathing issues, we have disabled movement abilities on pets. this is to coincide with the previously stated change. missions should be much less of a headache for pet professions now. enjoy!

- we have observed that professions with psychosis (mezz) abilities have an unfair advantage. previously, when a player calmed a mob, he was then able to sit and restore health and nano. this is no longer possible. players must now zone in order to kit.

- we have noticed that players have been able to obtain experience by combining store-bought tradeskill items. in theory a player may reach level 200 by purchasing and using tradeskill items. this was not intended, so stores will no longer function.

- players zoning into missions with warping mobs and immobile pets are no longer able to move.

- in an effort to fix the economy, all items ingame are now nodrop and unique. in order to avoid saturation, mobs will no longer drop loot or credits.

- an exploit involving mission generation has been fixed. players were able to obtain missions to reasonable destinations for useful equipment and credits. to fix this, we have disabled engineer pets.
there you go. straight from the horse's mouth...
