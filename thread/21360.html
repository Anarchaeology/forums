Player Event Do you want to be a part of it ?
Ok the lack of interesting events on Rubi-Ka is starting to bother a lot of people. I guess there is something to be said in the line " well if you think you can do better than do it yourself"

So we are going to give it a try run our own mass player events, create own events where the players take the roles the GM's normally would have. The story (yet to be decided but we have a few ideas) would try to have different elements both roleplaying and p vp as well.

The story will be played out over a number of days and weeks with different things planned each day. The events will be linked and how you do in one event can effect another.


The story characters will have a background profile pubished and everyone will be able to follow the via news stories on a website each day.

We will be looking for people to play certain roles, guards, lawyers, gang leaders, criminals etc etc ( of course depends on the story) but also people can take part via the guild just for the P v P for example. The roles will be based around the idea of the guilds and the role the the guild might play in the faction they belong.

We will not be playing Ross, Radiman etc or anyone above guild sort of level so as to not confuse the main story.

But you must be willing to fit into the idea of the story, and accpet the fact your character might not ( and prob will not) be a main character or a hero ( so to speak) in this event. You may even be asked to take the role of the bad guys or end up on the losing side by design.

We will also be looking for people to help out with the story ideas etc etc.

So if you are interesting in be a part let us know post a bio on you current character, what they do where are they from, maybe details of an adventure you have been on. Oh yes there are roles for both Omni, Clan and neutral.

The whole idea behind this drive is to improve the level of RP and get people interested in the story until FC decide to go back to it.

Please include me....
February 17th 29450 I Katelin "Cyani" Kerans was born to Harlen and Amalia Kerans. Employed by Omni-Tek, Harlen and Amalia were Nano-Technicians living in the Rome Blue district. Harlen, a very well known scholar and high ranking Omni-Tech Professor of Nano-Technology Sciences was a stern and very disciplined Nanomage. Amalia, an Omni-Tech Officer assigned to Harlen's research team was quite the opposite showing a more compassionate side.

My relationship with my parents was very close. My mother Amalia shaped my ideals, beliefs and soul. My father Harlen shaped my fortitude, discipline and mind. As I grew older my fathers persistent drive for perfection and lack of compassion pushed us farther apart. My mother and I became closer, and though my father loved me dearly his persistent force to drive me to be like him forged a bitter wedge between our family.

Not the typical Nanomage satisfied with laborious studies and isolation, I was labeled as a free spirit and somewhat reckless, preferring to socialize and experience the outside world from my sheltered life and Omni-Tek research institutions. This did not bode well with my father, especially considering his stature and place in the Nano-Tech community.

Educated by Omni-Tek, along with my fathers rigorous extracurricular studies I quickly mastered the ability to manipulate nanotechnology. I quickly grew tiresome of the tedious, cold calculations and research and in a heated debate with my father one evening regarding my conduct as a scientist, and foremost his daughter, he stated that I did not meet his expectations. Following an exchange of harsh words spoken in anger, my father instructed me to leave and not return should I see fit to pursue what he called "my foolish endeavors". My mother torn at the turn of events pleaded for my father to understand, unfortunately at our wits end I left Omni-Tek and my family in Rome to follow my heart and create my own life.

In my relentless pursue to find a new home, I found myself in a pleasant city known as Borealis. Small in comparison to the Omni Rome district I had been raised in, I found Borealis very quaint and well suited for my new life. Alone in a new city I quickly tried to adapt to my new surroundings. My first acquaintance was one of a kind Doctor Malcom Neblett, or Drtiny as his friends like to call him. The kind Drtiny answered many questions regarding my new home and his views on neutrality. He also noted his affiliation with an organization known as The Independent Rubikans and their strive for independence and freedom to not side in the conflict at hand and to live in peace. Intrigued with the thought of an open minded organization and the opportunity to meet other like minded individuals I did not hesitate to join when the kind Drtiny presented me with the option to do so. And so my life begins..... Free ... Neutral..... Independent.........

Katelin Kerans, simply "Cyani" to my friends....
