15 Men on a Dead Mans Chest...
It was quiet in the aftermath of the storm. Quiet, so that the steady drip of water falling from leaves sounded like heavy gunshots. Water gurgled and pooled in places, slowly flowing into the Stret catchment, and beyond.

The storm had come quikcly the night before. A rare, powerful storm that surged over the mountainous devide between Stret West Bank and Athen Shire. It had collected itself and with renewed rage, hurled itself upon the unsuspecting landscape. All night it had raged, like an angry animal. Leets, Reets, and even passing Atrox had to duck for cover in the blinding maelstrom. More than one ill set-up mining platform had been tipped over, jetting blue hued gas and hot, viscous liquid into the air.

But now, morning had come. Silence and the steady patter of rain on vegatation. A leet popped its strange, bug-eyed head from a burrow and chirped cautiously. It looked about, sniffed the air, and skipped out among the thrashed leaves and fronds of grass.

Something caught the Leet's eye. Something glinting from a newly made crack in the rocky, sandy surface of the hillside overlooking the Cobalt Shores. With inbred caution, the little creature skipped and hopped closer. Again it sniffed the air, then shrugged what passed for its shoulders and started digging.
