Some suggestions on putting the story into the game literaly
1) Put a faction oriented newspaper (say 5 credits to get a weekly copy) into the game with all the parts of the story in it that apear only on the web site at present. To add some kind of flavor to the game.

2) get a few players to act as "ears" or reporters, to do player to player stories for the paper, like hunting tips, strategy discussions, or meet so-and-so of guild X stories. Would be nice to have some kind of pulse in game besides the daily level grind, especialy on RK-2. I know some of the players would maybe like to get famous in game or be infamous.

It would be nice to actualy see some small story elements that have nothing to do with funcom or the story, but are about the players characters and there adventures killing some godzilla sized mutant that was about to eat meetmedere, lol.

3) have the "story' characters show up once in a while doing something from the writen story, maybe a few random encounters with players.

Say Radiman takes a walk around Tir or something. Maybe even a few NPC vs NPC events like this "dust-bunnie" group actualy causing a real disturbance for a change, maybe slap a few burn marks from a bomb here and there, then we get to read it and go see it in game not on your web page.

4) A real functional and moderated working in game message board, nothing fancy, but a way to do business or make arangments in game, even and especialy usful for guilds to recruit and attract players without channel spamming or that annoying tactic of whompa-farming. Maybe we could get some mileage from the player made missions portion of our interface, a few quick relabels and some tooling and poof, we got a postit pad in the game ready to use.

I think this would give the world a lived in look of functionality. Putting them indoors, say some of these clubs we have all over the game that are collecting dust? Nothing realy fancy is needed, a funny graphic picture/painting like thing on the wall in a back room or out of the way spot. Or even a new terminal if you wanted to get real fancy and impress us.

If lag is a issue then only load the board to client when its viewed, since it would be in a sub area like a club that would help control lag I think to a degree, also limit how much text and how often one can post, and cycle the board, say it resets every 48 hours? Really, we need more reasons to loiter in other spots besides the whompa areas, lag there is horrific at peak times, anything to move people away is a good thing.

Another good reason for this in game is it can be tied into speciffic classes or levels, in addition it would be a nice thing for guilds to have in there HQ if that ever comes about.

5) ok, we have these news channels, but absolutly nothing on it, or no one using it, atleast not since I came back last month to the game. Maybe a weekly update of some kind? like a interview brocast with some of the "story" characters, this I can see tying in to the paper concept were you could read the interview afterwords, but it would add a nice interactive part thats sadly missing, to have people listening in game to the story. Heck why not in special cases let a few of the reporters I mentioned above use the channel say when a big fight happens in 4holes to kind of give us a play by play of the action.

6) How about a MOTD ( Viewing needs to be optional, there are those few anti-social hermits out there. ) for big story type events, ( sorry but to date besides the pumkin incedent I have never seen a event yet. I would like a heads up so I can maybe enjoy what ever does happen at some point )letting players know it happened or is going on or something.

Really I think what I suggested at least the paper part is deffinatly one thing the game needs to liven things up.

I have attended wakes that were more socaily engaging than most of the interaction I have seen so far in this game, and thats sad.

I see no logic to not having some kind of equivalent to many of the comon news items we use in every day life in the game. 90% of people start there day by reading a paper or watching the news or something like that.

Look at why we do that, were not just curious, its a physical need to know what happened in the world while you slept or worked at your job, so you feel conected to the world. Well guess what? I don't feel connected to the game, we need to fix that guys, please.

The reason the game seems so bland to many is that we are cut off from knowing everything thats happing around us. Its like living in a big city with a bag on your head 90% of the time.

When I come into the game there should be a thing or item there that will bring me up to speed on the last few days or events, so I can get a feel for game life again, and climb back into my character easier. I know there has to be something going on some were on Rubi-ka that is news worthy material.

We as people first players second, need to feel connected to our factions side some how before we can give a rats bahootie about this conflict.

I truly think that this one little thing is what really makes the game so different on the home end as apossed to the Dev's end of things, they know more about what goes on than we do, and its very different experience for players playing the game then it is when they log in and play.

We really do need a Rubi-Ka version of CNN or the nightly news to stir things up. Don't expect us to acomplish much on our own with whompa gossip. Once or twice a day give us some tidbits of some kind, and see if it does not indeed pick up a bit in response.

Actualy, another idea just struck me too, but I don't know how big a chat group can be. Maybe even a player run news channel of some sort run off a chat group with timed brodcasts and a set of runners who go out and invite people to the gruop at given spots.

But hey, no slacking on Funcoms end, we still need offcial versions of this stuff in game. Maybe some Ark's could help some volunteers to get a channel up and runnign to test this out?
