My first time on Rubi-Ka...
All I remember is that I was cruising around in my new Apollo 747-XF, it was the year 3024 on earth back then, and I was on my way to the planet of Naboo to drop of some goods. All of a sudden I heard something crash to my rear wing and I discovered an asteroid crashed into me. I tried to repair the problem, but it was no good.. I was going down fast towards some kind of planet and was about to crash. Everything turned black, and a black hole fills my memory
When I came back to conscious I was sitting in some kind of strange space shuttle Around me sat weird looking people, and I was wondering where I was. When I tried to look on my watch to see what time it was, I saw I myself was a weird looking creature My skin turned blue and my watch turned into some kind of a computer. What the . I said Back to the world again? I heard Where am I? I said, and I looked up, A friendly (also blue) face was looking at me, it was a man who was probably around my age. He told me that after my ship had crashed the people of Rubi-Ka picked me up and where able to reanimate me with some kind of Reclaim device Back then I didnt understand a word he was saying, but I nodded and listened carefully to what he was saying
Then the ship landed and I was asked to step out, doesnt seem like a hard thing to do, but it proved me wrong Almost the moment I stood up, I fell again It was like I never walked before. The other people helped me get out, and I stumbled true the big gates of the space port. In there all kinds of people started talking to me, I tried to answer all of them, but it was a hard thing to do, I walked towards the other side of the big hall, and saw 3gates, I obviously needed to walk past one of those gates But witch one? A woman asked me if she had to help me I answered that I would like that, and she started to ask me questions. After telling her what my favorite animals and colors where, she told me to walk into the gate witch had a sign above it saying Clan. I picked the Omni gate, because I thought that would be more interesting. I had to step into some kind of teleporting device and I got morphed into something that looked most like a boxing area
In here I put my hands in my pockets to find all kind of strange devices. The things where labeled Treatment pack, First-Aid Kit and more. I started to talk to people and ask questions about where I was. They told me I was on Rubi-Ka, and that I would have to fight to survive, I got out of the boxing area and saw a Leet I never heard of them before, but it seemed like a very nice animal. I tried to pet it, but it wouldnt let me, instead it began attacking me, biting me, scratching me, so I gave it a bang on the head. It wouldnt help, the beast killed me in less then 10seconds
Right before I died I thought this is it, this time Im really going to die, but I was wrong again. 3seconds later I respawned in the boxing area again I tried attacking another Leet, but got killed again. I asked somebody how I could get to another place without dying, they told me I should start learning stuff. Words like IP and XP where flying around my ears, and I didnt understand a word they where saying. They explained it to me carefully, and I started to get myself ready for battle again. Again I saw the Leet, only this time, I was the stronger one. With a big scream the Leet fell down, and died. I robbed the beast, and found some money. An easy way to get money I thought, and I started killing more Leets.. I killed a few more, and was getting a hand of money already when I felt a strange thing sparkling to my body. I aged, I was 2 now.
I started killing some more, and in a short time all the beast in the area where dead in a few seconds I walked back to the Boxing area and stepped into a portal. I picked an apartment, and explored it a little, I saw another portal, and stepped into it. I looked around me Everything was so huge, all kind of people where talking to me, and the NCU on my wrist was vibrating like a computer gone mad
I was in the city, ready to do some missions, ready to explore the world of Rubi-Ka

(Im sorry for my bad English, but hey English people dont speak Dutch, do they? )
