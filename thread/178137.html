Naxilite's Journal - Shadowlands
Personal journal of Eldridge McCovery

September 11

Rubi-Ka has been abuzz recently, everyone has been talking about JAME, Jobe and The Shadowlands. In my time in Omni-Tek I had not heard of these things, but now they were public knowledge. JAME claim to be an independent group of nanotechnicians, scientists and metaphysicists working to open some transdimensional portal. I dont know, this sounds like so much madness to me. Still I am out on a limb at the moment, I think I will see what all the fuss is about. JAME have sent brochures to almost everyone, it seems they have placed a whompa in Borealis..interesting.

Jobe whompa in Borealis
There are two whompa stations in Borealis, JAME have placed the second in the north east corner of the township, it seems to be busy tonight as a number of prominent citizens are here. I dont inquire after their business as its probably sordid or illegal knowing Pryo and Ike. Heh I dont know if they would appreciate me writing that, well they will never know. We chatted for a while though, but I was anxious to begin this journey so I made my excuses and entered the Whompa.

Jobe whompa in Jobe
I step from the whompa into the arrival area and my breath is taken from me. Looking up I see 3 gigantic rocks hovering in the air, lights glitter from the many buildings covering the rocks. I move from the whompa and down a short ramp. Looking behind I see three whompas, one for Rome, Borealis and Athen. It seems JAME want Jobe to be open for everyone, an attitude I agree with. In front of me are three elevators, each of which goes to one of the rocks. I take the nearest one up to The Plaza area of Jobe.

The Plaza
The inhabitants of Jobe like these extravagant white costumes. I speak with one Lady Giljabet Skellibjalla, they all seem to be Lords and Ladies here, strange who do they Lord over in their city in the sky? Giljabet is busy and will only tell me that the portal leads to the shadowlands and that I should see the portal technician for more answers. I do not press her for more details, from what Dr Neblett told me they have a simple way to deal with over inquisitive people and drop them in the lake near Newland, that serves his stinking clanner arse right. Continuing around the plaza I come across several shops and Jobe Real Estate offices. I enter the offices and after speaking with the people in there I am given a key to a modest apartment in Jobe! Excited I find my new home and am impressed by the multilevel comples. Laughing I call up Dr Neblett hah you should see the one they gave me Major he pans the camera around his luxurious apartment resplendent with its balconies and views. I am happy with my place though. But this isnt getting me into the Shadowlands, leaving my new place I search for this portal.

The Portal
Its not a long search, which I like. A crowd had gathered at a small walkway, on which stands the energy vortex that is apparently the much-vaunted portal. I speak to the technician and he tells me that I must pay a small fee, 25000 credits, to enter the portal. Its a small price so I hand over the money and he runs a nanoprogram on me that will allow my body to pass through the portal. This is quite a show really and worth the money alone, I take a step forward and enter the portal.

The Shadowlands
I entered the portal and my body was wracked by unknown forces, dazed I fell forward onto a cold metal surface. Looking up was amazing, a gigantic rift filled the entire northern sky, a gigantic wheeling vortex of some sort. Had I just passed through that? Standing up I see that I am in some sort of arrival platform, more people appear and run off down a narrow walk way towards a grassy area. A number of buildings of an unusual appearance are there, I walk towards them. This place is amazing and feelshmm..more real? Yes that seems to be right, but how can it be more real than real?
Oh my god, this road is a bridge, this side of the portal is like Jobe. I am on gigantic floating rocks again, but floating over what? Looking over the edge there is nothing but blackness below and the rift above me.
The buildings form a small town, perhaps some sort of control area for this place, which I have learnt is called Port5. Well I didnt come here to look at buildings, I ready my rifle and head away in to the wilderness. There are hills here, I can get the members of Desert Winds fit again by making them climb them for a few hours a day, they should like that. Battlefalcon is looking a bit portly so it would do him gowow

This is a sight to greet you, it seems to be a beam of blue light pointing up into the sky, It touches down somewhere in the distance. It doesnt look too far Eldridge, get to it soldier. I cross over the hills and pass down into a wide valley, what looks like ancient ruins cover the bottom of the valley. I doubt they are inhabited now, this place seems empty.

Famous last words Eldridge, dont let the rest of the gang hear how you were caught unawares by two humanoid beasts. I dont think they were totally alive though, their bodies faded away after I killed them and they had sightless eyes. And Eldridge, come prepared next time <note to self nano/health kits and stims do not work here, look into alternatives>.

The beam of light was coming from a pit in the ground, weird thing. A strange wailing noise came from it and the light was not hot or cold and I could walk into it safely. I have no idea what it does, probably not good though. I hope I dont want any kids.

Yuttos live here, who would have thought it. These dont seem so timid as the ones on Rubi-Ka, mind you they are twice as tall as I am. The one I spoke too told me the light was an Incarnator, but he did not tell me what its function was. I wasnt in the mood to experiment either. When it came apparent I didnt want to buy anything from him the Yutto was very uncommunicative. Oh well, I shall explore some more.

Those ruins looked interesting, so I head there first. They are of a gothic type of architecture, huge pillars and walkways, I can not find any clue as to their origin, who made them or why. I dont think the Yuttos could have done this, could they? As I moved through them I found they were not uninhabited though. Beings I have not seen before, strange glowing animals, more humanoids really resided there. Some had huge red glowing wings, others had an organic armour and some sort of weaponry but did not seem intelligent. Other winged creatures carried axes and maces I think. I did not perceive any intelligence from them though. But they had the ability to use these things they held. My Target Acquisition Bots (T.A.B.) did not recognize them as a threat though so I felt quite safe despite their dangerous appearance. There was little else in the ruins though, so I left them and headed for the white buildings I saw on another hill further to the south.

As I climbed the hill to the village I was once more struck by how odd this place, the Shadowlands, felt. So different to our own world and galaxy, I wonder if the physical laws are the same here, I have already found my nano and health kits dont work here. I wonder if anything else is inoperable.

Ok Eldridge, Parrot form no longer allows you to fly, so dont try to jump off a cliff like that again! Hardly covering yourself in glory are you? But man, the view! I am so glad I remembered my holocams, everyone will want to see these pictures. But this view, its a beach stretching as far as the eyes can see in all directions and some sort of tower? Out there on the shore? Who builds a tower on the sand? Another mystery for this mysterious place.

Well the beach is home to some freaky things as well, I watched a family of huge ape like animals forage for food in the sand, they were marvellous beasts and moved so gracefully it was amazing. I could like it here I think. They seemed to be using a rock as a shelter I think, but the rock was split in two. Like it was a meteor that had crashed here and split on impact, the exposed core was glowing red. I felt no heat come from it though, but I took some rock samples for the lab rats.

So much to see here, but my destination was the tower and it was still some distance from me. Only way to cross this beach was in wolf form for the speed. The tower was black obsidian and reached high into the sky. Yuttos were using it as a trading post, or a home? I wasnt sure. But why was there an eremite statue here? Are the two species linked in some way? The Yuttos were friendly enough though and allowed me enter their tower, the Tinker Tower they called it.

Inside the tower
It is dark and steamy inside the tower, and larger inside than out? The Yutto are sure filling me with questions but are light on answers. Well one day well work that out I am sure. I dont feel safe here though, the light or something. I cant shake off a feeling that this place is dangerous somehow. I ready my nanoprograms as best I can, I can not recharge my nano and head on. A large corridor spreads south and north, I can see movement on the scanner and its not human.

It most definitely is NOT human, the Yutto share this tower with huge spiders and mutants, they did not attack me though, but I dont think they saw me. I dont want to risk a confrontation so lightly preapred, Its time for a strategic withdrawal Eldridge. I do not attack and explore as deep as I dare before leaving unharmed.

The Brink/Journeys end
The sea seems to stretch forever, as I approach the shore the sky visible darkens and a sensation inside of me tells me this is The Brink. I find it hard to see here, a gigantic ring of fire is in the sky, the rift is there too. This land is mysterious and beautiful but dangerous, like something from a dream. But it is cold here in The Brink, cold and dark. I can not see well and my foot slipped on some gravel. I was too close to the edge, I had forgotten I was on a rock floating in the air and I fall.
