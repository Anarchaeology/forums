Player-made mission maps: A real possibility or just a wistful dream?
I am so sick of doing randomly generated mission maps, they have no character, no unique flavour, no personality!!!

Well I was thinking... many games, especially in the 90s, often included 'map builders' with their games to add replay value and whatnot. How hard would it be for Funcom to write a little mission mapmaker program which subscribers can download from their login screen and make maps with?

This would basically allow players to make the 'blueprints' or 'floorplans' for missions(though not MOB or loot placement of course). The map author would then submit the map to Funcom, whom would test the map and, if approved, add it to the servers.

So, when you go to a mission booth and obtain a mission for yourself, there is a probability of your mission having a player-made map, then all the rest of the MOBs, loot, etc generated randomly as per normal.

In the entry chamber of these player-made maps, you could have a little plaque, when checked would reveal information like so:



~The Gauntlet~

Initially the idea is very good. Although I'm familiar with a few of the mappers who helped build the world (which has made some impressive work earlier), the dynamically generated missions leaves a lot to be desired.

On the exploit issue, this will never be a problem. No way is any player submitted material going into the game without proper testing by the staff at Funcom, and any "exploiting" available simply wouldnt pass inspection (any more than is what already in missions by the Funcom mappers themselves).

Designing complete missions is a little far out though, but I dont see why simple rooms can't be made. As I see it, a mission is generated room by room - meaning a ground layout is made with a room # and how many exits that room # has. The game will then pick a "3 exit room, north/east/west" from a batch of premade rooms of a certain flavour (like brown cave) to fit in that spot. I know of a few similar mods made for Q/Q2 that did this quite well. There also appears to be a dynamic layout of monsters too - ie, "big grey cave room with exit south/north" has 3 monster spawns. The game will place monsters in these spawns based on player level and mission difficulty/type picked. The same would hold true with chests and items inside, and/or locked/trapped.

Additionally, I don't suspect the creation of the mission geometry is far removed from the majority of fps games out there, so building basic structures, either straight in a 3d program, or using already existing "map programs" shouldnt be that hard to implement into AO.

Even with the latest "fixes" to the random generator, mission rooms are still overly static and most players have seen the same rooms over and over again. You know the dynamic generation of missions is quite lacking when you even get several rooms sewn together in the same fashion time and time again. The few times a "new" room appears you usually stop up and explore it, taking shots and sucking in the change - more of these would certainly freshen up mission considerably.

Having players submit room templates would certainly be a great feature unrivaled by any other mmrpg and a great push from Funcom making AO the most wanted game again =)
