orgs are about ppl that
* are nice to disucss with, about the game or anything
* give (not sell) equipment, buffs or tips
* make easy groups to hunt or mission with
* etc.

Orgs are also about
* RPing (more or less depneding on org)
* organizing internal events
* and having more weight than by oneself in the AO world, hence more chance to participate in events or storyline

Although everyone is of course free to play AO as they feel it, I really think you may lose one important dimension of the game if not in an org. And actually the vast majority of pple are in 1. (But once again, it's up to everyone to choose!)
