20K Hunting Spots
here is a quik guide to hunting at 20K (Pleasant Meadows)

50-60 VB'S (Virusbuilders) 700 X 1900
SW on road from 20K, or just NE of outpost by ferry to
lush, good xp at low levels, but only supports 1 team

50-60 EYE CAMP 1100 X 2500
just NW of 20K, great spawn, sometimes see n00bs
pulling train all the way back to guards at 20K gate

55-75 SHADES 1800 x 1200
by far most popular hunting in PM, with great spawn, can
support several teams, SE to river crossing, then due S

65-75 CRIMS (Hardened Criminals) 1700 X 2100
east on road from 20K, alternative if u get bored of shades
or bothackers

70-85 BOTHACKERS 800 x 800
from 20K, SSW to river, then due S
along with shades, popular for hi lvls


when you start to get bored of NW mine in lush, or just want alternative to level 50-65 hunting, try vbs or eye camp, after level 60 or so, shades is most popular, exp starts to cap out at around 70-75, then bothackers is good til around level 80

since shades is by far most popular hunting in PM, and can support several groups, unlike other hunting spots listed here, it would be nice if it had some unique spawn like the lifebleeders at lush NW which make that hunting ground so popular (Chaos Minions? which would pop up whenever someone brings in a slayer-droid to kill the entire shade spawn)

hope this guide helps, hopefully someone can post a thorough guide to lush op? i forget what levels for SW pipe, mines, etc.
at lower levels, i used to hang out at the outpost in Greater Omni Forest, i remember hunting bags (rock blubbags) just east of Omni-ent, then moving on to find groups at the outpost to hunt muties (at the Horseshoe camp mainly)

HAPPY HUNTING!!!!
