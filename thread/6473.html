It'd be nice to see shields and bucklers as viable equipment.
Pardon the vaguely annoyed tone of the message, but I'm completely boggled. These have to be one of the most absolutely useless pieces of equipment in the entire game.

Consider:

1) The absolute lowest Multi Melee requirement I have seen for a shield or buckler has been about 150. Yes, that's even at QL 1-5. Utterly out of the question for anything remotely resembling a low level. I can multi-wield a couple of 1-handed edged weapons by level 6 or 7 (as an adventurer; not sure who else has MM as an easy skill, maybe enforcer).

2) The damage is atrocious. A QL 21 Cast-Off Energy Buckler (for example) has a range of 2-15(3). This actually isn't bad at all, because the whole idea is defense, not offense; at least, until you look at..

3) Stupid AC. Just.. -stupid-. It doesn't make any sense. QL 60 of the same buckler type as above gives you an extra Energy AC of.. wait for it.. 14. Fourteen. At quality level 60. A -shield- does this. Shoulder pads gives you more.

I know people come onto the boards and say, "Geeze, this is such an easy fix, why don't you set your team of code monkeys on this already!" But come on. Comparably, I can't see how difficult this is. Either:

A) Lower the MM requirements drastically. Like, Quality Level = Multi Melee requirements. That might make up for paper-thin AC bonuses.

B) The preferred solution, make the AC bonus actually substantial.


Sigh. One of those things I doubt will get changed in my lifetime, but I like to dream.
