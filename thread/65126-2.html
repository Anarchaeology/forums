quote:
Please correct me if I'm wrong, but if, say, a Clan agent uses a neut undercover kit, that clanner will permanently become a neut?
Yes.
quote:
Also, if you do switch sides, do you automatically revert to your old side after the side skill unlocks or do you have to do something to switch back?
You have to use an undercover kit (or application form) of your old side to switch back.
I'm not sure if an application form deletes the tokens of the side you switch to or from, or both, so the undercover kit would be the better choice.
quote:
That would be a way to be a neutral and still keep your board?
When I tested (14.5), the undercover kit did neither unequip the board nor were the effects disabled. But since the description of the kit states that you can not keep using your board, this could be an exploit. Better ask a GM before you go neutral with your board equipped.
