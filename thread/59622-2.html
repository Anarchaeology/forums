That's not totally true...

The skill reqs for the Advent and Fixer ones were already fairly high. Even a 195 Advent can't self-cast this self-only nano without sacrificing almost all their other nano skills due to implant swapping.

Also, if you notice... the Points per PM/SI on the Enforcer set is actually LOWER than the Advent Ballad nano. This is completely contrary to what you just stated. Obviously, the reqs on the buff compared to the skill budget are based on how much the nano actually does buff the skill--so what you say doesn't make much sense when comparing the Advent and Enforcer nanos.

The only one that seems truly "in-between" is the Fixer one. The Advent one has lower reqs, but buffs a lower amount as well. The Enforcer one is nearly identical to the Advent on in terms of its ratio. The Solider one is insane compared to any of them.

Also, I question the logic of all this "skill budget" talk. First of all, Shadowlands has no annouced release date, and has been pushed back over and over again. It seems very unwise to make players of the game NOW suffer due to an upcoming patch that they may or may not buy, which may or may not come out any time in the near future.

You are taking buffs that are needed for a class to fix balance issues, and forcing people to buy Shadowlands to gain access to these balance fixes. That is very unfair, IMO.

Also, if the skill budget is so screwy in Shadowlands, some severe changes are going to need to be made. Otherwise you'll have Engineers casting Extreme Prejudice at level 15 and laughing at all the level 194 Advents that are trying to gain 29 million XP to cast their +100 buff.

So, the question is...why not revamp the reqs after Shadowlands comes out? Seems like the obvious way of doing things...but, as it stands, you're basically trying to strong-arm broken classes into buying (and waiting for) Shadowlands to get balance fixes.

The other question being, having insane reqs AND level reqs seems silly as well. If the level reqs are there to maintain when someone is supposed to get something, the req could be 1 PM/SI...it doesn't matter. Likewise, if you raise the req so much based on the "new budget", there doesn't really need to be level reqs, now does there?

Nanos based on the "new skill budget" probably shouldn't have level reqs, and nanos with level reqs probably shouldn't have outlandish reqs on them. And no profession fixes before Shadowlands even has a release date set should be based on the "new skill budget" that only exists somewhere in Blackmane's head.

Just my take on the issue...
