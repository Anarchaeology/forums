In the end, it will be Humanity that either prevails or perishes. Humankind has in our dark past been effectively wiped out, but in the end Humanity eventually prevailed. It's now 20 thousand some odd years later and we mustn't forget crawling out of the caves not once, but twice. 'Freedom' on Rubi'ka is not about what symbol is on the badge of your local police officer. 'Freedom' is not even being allowed to choose the flavor of your coffee in the morning.

Freedom is about the right to remain Human. There's something special about Rubi'ka. Earth appears to be only a stepping stone. We reserve the right to kill each other. It's only Human. What we have to remember are the times we set aside aside color or political ideology to maintain our very existence. Killing on Rubi'Ka is senseless. It's not a means to an end. It's a waste of time.

For me to see a call for Unity amongst the Clans I only have to chuckle. Unity for what purpose?

To win?

To win what?

Democracy? Self governance? Control, power? Notum? Respect?

Deliverance?

Working against anything in the 30th centuray AD and the 70th century or so since the first Human culture brought themselves back together bears little weight in my way of thinking. I'd rather see Human anarchy than what I've seen of the inhabitants of this planet lately. One new Order after the next will not bring you the Nirvana humanity may or may not deserve. The Clans unifying themselves and 'liberating' Rubi'ka serves no greater good.

Henry Radiman should be sought out and conferred with. If you won't let him lead you, he should at least be given a chance to confer understanding. I trusted that man an in the name of Humanity almost took up arms for his cause with a blind faith. And we all know that blind faith is only true faith.

I could never consent to allow my abilities to aid the efforts of whatever it is the clans purport to be fighting for at this point in time. How can you ask for Unity when you don't even have a purpose? Or is Blind Faith being asked to be placed in the hands of someone besides Radiman?

At one time a man named David Marlin delivered Humanity from a great Evil. That evil is gone. There is no Evil on Rubi'ka, only shades of grey.

The Clans had half a planet and no more War than Humanity has ever allowed themselves. It was thrown away with the snap of a finger. I remember a golden age on Rubi'ka. I remember a time when we were all citizens of the planet who could at most be likened to supporters of different sides of a sports competition. Rivalry.

Now, we have hatred. Now, we have prejudice. Now, we have non-acceptance. Now the world is dark. Life is not rich. The mornings are not embraced, but rather accepted.

The call for Unity in this thread is a call for further polarization. That can not be good.

Ancient folklore: there were those that had stars on their bellies and those that had none. Cut them both. The both bled alike.

I'm tired of bleeding.
