It's funny you mention this. I decided a couple days ago to go for the Snakemaster, and got mine last night. i believe Fopus uses one as well, so watch it.

As far as skills. Im capped out and QL180 impanted for rifle and aimed. I do not use a LLTS as I have opted for the speed using the TIM scope. I did try the ranee and my buffed shotgun is 497 atm. I think the reqs for it are 530, whcih should put me close enough to do max damage.

I can also self buff myself into any sniper rifle in the game (besides a small ransack needed for the Ranee). I can self buff into a QL 200 Diss rifle, which after extensive testing, I felt was a better gun for over all damage. Sure the Ranee hits hard, but the diss seems to hit hard more often. A normal crit on the Diss rifle seems to be around 1800-1900 vs the Ranee 2600 or so. But it seemed like I would hit the 1800s twice as much, and the minimum damage of the diss is a lot more than the Ranee. I don't get the 10k aimed shots all the time with the Diss rifle, but I never got one with the Ranee. It hardly matters. I will be equiping the Snakemaster in a couple levels.

Good luck against Fopus... he is easy to find. Check MMD. If you see Etin in 2HO, give him a snake bite for me, but watch him... He found an exploit for roots, and is not affected by them... He doesn't even miss a beat when you root him... It's quite a site when you root him 3 times, and he keeps chasing you down with the glowing bright green root aura around him.
