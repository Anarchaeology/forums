I like this idea of more tokens rewarded per mission as title lvl increases. And with the way the mission system works right now, it even makes sense. Solo missions at newbie lvl are the ideal size. There aren't 100 tiny rooms with a mob (or 2) in every single one. Past lvl 60 or so missions just seem to get larger and more difficult, hence more time consuming.

When my soldier was lvl 5, she could clear a 50% mission in less then an hour, easily. When she hit about lvl 70, a mission of the same difficulty would take 3 hours. Why? Missions increase in size, mobs increase in power.

There are the exceptions. Very rarely I get small 'white cave' missions and they don't take very long to complete. If these were the norm for missions at my lvl, I would have no cause for complaint, but the chance of getting these missions are slim, regardless of how far I set the slider to OPEN.

As for being teamed in a mission, I think that if you are teamed when completing a mission, and the mission holder receives a token, everyone in that team should receive a token. In order to avoid exploits with high lvl characters teaming with low lvl characters to receive tokens, just make the token act as XP. If someone in the team is too low lvl or too high lvl, the token isn't granted.
