Regarding gimpness
Here's what happens in every game. The achievers experiment and figure out which class/combination does the most damage or levels fastest or is most essential. Every other class is therefore "gimped", because playing those characters is a rough ride. the peeps complain about it, demanding the game is broken, the boards are populated with messages . . .

But there are explorers and socializers and other types of people who want to play as well, and they find ways to play. And people do play gimped classes and combinations, often to the highest levels. And people still love their hybrids--note that the fixers have more postings on any of the official boards than any other class. For some reason, the people who play them are passionate about them.

Game designers know that it takes all types to make a game successful, so they design different types of characters. They also know if there was one way to be uber, everyone would do it, following the same path, and then the game is considered broken. They also know that at least a substantial minority of players do not levle their characters as fast as possible, but try this and that, often deleting and restarting and forever staying a lowbie.

So the end is not the goal, nor is the road. It's the game. Have fun. Kill what you can. And never trust Omni.
