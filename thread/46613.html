But this one is mine.
My Beam is my best freind, it is my life.
I must master it as I must master my life.
With out me my Beam is useless, With out my Beam i am use less.

I must weild my beam true,
I must weild it better then my enemy who is trying to kill me.
I must panncake him before he kills me
I will!.

Before God I swear this creed.
My Beam and my self are defenders of RK
We are the masters of our enemys.
We are the savers of my life, so be it!
Until there are no enemys, but peace.
