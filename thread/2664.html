Wednesday 5th 20:30 Baboons
Time 20:30 GMT - Until you can bored
Location Baboons - Omni Ent 750,750
Theme - Old times and a new start.

For those that know rolplaying and have been around AO for some time will remember the parties we used to have in baboons.

Baboons was the first club to open and for a long time the only club. Baboons was special not due to its location, or the way it looked but by the people that used to go there.

In the past few months baboons was left empty as people drifted away and the parties stopped. It is sad to say that a lot of people on Rubi-Ki now never had the chance to see a baboons party.

Well as the question of roleplaying comes to the front in AO, people are wondering what else is left in the game. We are returning to where it all started baboons.

This is not a massive party as in the days of old, or a disco but just a party for the people that used to come to baboons to meet up. It is also a chance for the new players to come along and meet some of the longest running roleplayers on Rubi-Ki. A time to hear old stories, and a time to start your own new stories.

I cant say baboons will be going back to the way it was. But if we get enough support from you the players, from the guilds. We just might be able to create some entertainment for you.



Also if your interested in running parties or would like to hold guild events, or you want to help in any way or you think we can help you then please get in touch.
