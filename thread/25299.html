Reference: Map location
Just like being able to make a reference of players and items, a "location reference" would be handy. This feature could be assigned to a hotkey similar to the current "create reference" hotkey.

Say I found a hunting spot and I want invite my friends to join me. I could then make a reference of the location I am at and drop it in the chat window. The reference info should explain what zone and coordinates I am at, and my friends could add it to their own references list.

Right-clicking the location icon under references could bring up a flag on the main map and a guideline on the compass, similar to missions.

It would also be nice if reference icons could be labeled, so that one could keep track of favorite hunting spots, cities, dungeons, bars and nightclubs, organization meeting places etc.
