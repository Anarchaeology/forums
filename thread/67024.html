Taxes needs to be lvl based!
There is a big problem about how taxes work.

I am leader of a guild of fairly sized guild that includes levels ranging from 11-200. Now this is all nice, but the problem is when I try to find what kind of taxes I should set. The 150+ people can afford taxes of 20k per 2 hours gaming time, no problem, but its just a "little" steep for the sub 50 players.

Preparing for the Notum Wars where money will play a greater part and a guild will need more money to buy towers, the taxes need to be working properly.

I think there should be a base tax that is based upon your current level, for example setting the tax to 100 creds will deduct 500 creds from a lvl 100 player, while deducting 50 cred from lvl 10 and 1000 from a lvl 200. Either the factor could be modifiable by guild leaders, or it could be a set factor made by FC.

I feel this NEEDS to be done, please tell me you will, Cz

Tax rate per rank lvl
Cz, while your suggestion seems like a good way to let players pay taxes according to their org. status, it might still not be the most fair solution for players (I assume we are talking about character, not account) who rise quickly to high org status due to their social/political skills, while not yet being major cash generators. Actually, someone devoting a lot of time to RP and guild activities will have even less time to go missioning or whatever to make the money he/she needs. Likewise, a lvl 200 'applicant' to a guild will have substantial funding to pay a high tax rate.

My suggestion:

A similar, yet different system, that is based on Title level rather than org. Rank, that allows the guild leader to set different tax rates for the each title level. Also, the option to set individual tax rate to compensate for the event that a high lvl member rerolls and moves all the money to a low lvl character. All this should be visible somehow to all guild members.

Could this be implemented?
