Shocking report on Omni Hospitals:
Shocking report on Omni Hospitals:

This afternoon I was sent by the galactic health & welfare society to look into some rumours (given to us by an anonymous clan’s man) about a Omni-Tek hospital. In the avelon sector on Rubikai.

I arrived and went undercover and started my research, carefully sneaking through the hospital, on my unannounced inspection.

I was shocked to find that not only does Omni employ Drones as medical staff, but these drones upheld none of the standards required by medical personnel (ie. Personal hygiene, wearing clean clothes ect.)
Further I found several cases where garbage was thrown in a corner. It seemed that part of the hospital had been made into sleeping happitat for the drones, it might be good to make sure there is always personnel nearby, however it is disturbing that their living quarters was not separated from the rest of the facility.

On my inspection I found several locked doors, and was shocked to find that they covered several skeletons (my preliminary DNA samples strongly imply that at least some of those persons reported missing has been found. However more in depth analysis is needed)
Also there seemed to be a serious rat problem on the hospital, the rats was hiding in door panels and in equipment containers.

I felt that I had learned all that I could, but would give the personnel a chance to explain the situation, (after all there might be some logical reason??)
As soon as I revealed myself, to the employer at the entrance to the facility, he shouted for a few of the other employees, and they started beating me up. I only by great a stroke of good luck, escaped with my life.

I suspect that Omni-Tek will be hearing from the galactic health & welfare society!
Until matters has been further investigated I recommend all rubikai citizens to stay away from Omni-Tek medical facilities, if you are sic take an aspirin and call your local doctor.!
