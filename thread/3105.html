battle info hiding
i wish ppl didn't have access to so much info about us when they asses us. it would be nice to let others see only the info on us that we are willing to share.

it would make a fight more interesting. if a caster meets an MA on a battlefield, they know that root is a relatively safe tactic. if a u meet a caster on a battlefield, u think that your chances of finishing him off quickly are good.

if u run into somone and can't see any useful battle info on them, u don't know who you are up against. it might be an MA with a ql 2 gun for show and misdirection; it could be an enf wanting to lull u into thinking a root can make her ineffective. it could be someone a few lvls below u or a lot of levels below u; it could go the other way and u could be taking on a char 200 lvls over your's. ( i know u know there is a lvl limit to who u can fight, stop before u go there )

so many tactical subtleties open up.
