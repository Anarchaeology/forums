Expoit in teaming
Yesterday, while defending a base, I saw a level 130ish trader in the 75% zone, on the border of the 25% zone. He ran across the border, and at the time, was teamed with someone too high or too low for anyone to attack him. He stayed teamed until he was able to pick off the lower levels, including me, safely.

This is pretty cheap. My 110 NT could team with a level 50 player and be completly safe to simply walk into an omni base and scout it out. This is ridiculous! I think an individual player, who isn't teamed, should be able to attack another player in a team even if they are teamed too/high or too low. On the other hand, the player in the team should not be able to attack back until they left the team.

I'm sure I'll get several opinions on this, so fire away!
