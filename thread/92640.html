New patch equipment and character balance
To start, one aspect in character development in AO I've so far liked much is that the system does not favor the ones knowing the ins and outs of the development mechanics. If you're a gimp at level 100, the situation can be remedied with reasonable effort (unless you have made horrendously stupid errors in IP distribution) and you can correct the situation.

However, looking at the items most likely to be found in the low level and high level dungeon I feel I'd like to drop my level back to 60. For instance, the new implants look quite nice (like http://www.aodb.info/showitem.asp?AOID=204651), same goes for some rings as well.

It'd be quite ok if the new high level dungeon had similar, higher level copies of those. It's not a big difference, but I hate the idea of another character being tougher and having better equipment only because he's created later.

Anyone have any idea (experience with previous patches, though Dev reply should do five too) on the matter?

Point being..
It's not a matter of twinking, and I'm only playing a single character. also I don't consider myself being solely picked upon with these changes. Also, I'm not complaining when FC adds new, undoubtedly very nice dungeons and items into the game.

What I'm complaining is that, as someone already said in the thread, that it would be nice to have upgraded versions (they don't have to be identical, but similar enough) in the high level dungeon to the ones found in the lower level dungeon.

Why? Because I don't want to feel bad about not having access to nice things they add to the game, just because I've made a good effort in gaining levels. I'm not saying they should make the items droppable, I like there being nodrops so that the lower level dungeon isn't run over by rude high levels stealing all the fun from low levels. There are those too, you know.

I don't want to end up noticing I'd have to remake the character I like very much just because characters who were created later are better just because of that.

As I said in my post, so far I'm liked very much the way it is now. You can make mistakes, or not optimize your character for the first 150 levels, then shape up and put some days of effort into it, and have your character emerge as good as those who have been twinking their characters all the time (nothing wrong with that either).

When I was still playing MUDs some 7-8 years ago many of them had character development mechanics which made the character tougher at higher levels if you knew how to do it. A simple example was giving out xp or development points based on say, intelligence stat. Of course many times, even if you were making a Conanish barbarian, you raised intelligence as high as you could, to net more development points, resulting in a tougher barbarian fighter.

While this item issue is a small one compared to that example, the cause of worry is the same. I meant to ask if anyone knows if a character who is created post-14.7.8.2 will be tougher than one created and levelled above 60 before that.

While some of the items in the excellent Anarchy Mainframe do have low and high level versions, some don't, and some of those look like as if they'd be very useful even as low level versions to a higher level character.

Usefulness aside, according to the icon in the Guardian Tank Armor for instance tell me it'd be an extremely good looking armor for my Enforcer, despite the low ACs for a high level character. Completely black, gleaming black tank? I love it! But I can never get it so I'm upset. Not too much, but still.

This was the point.
