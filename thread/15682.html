two issues

1- not being able to sit when rooted. this is the silliest thing i've seen. it is bad enough when you can't move for about two minutes, but i can't even heal myself. i think this is something that should be changed soon

i understand that these are not important in the grand scheme of things, but they are highly annoying. any respose would be nice.
