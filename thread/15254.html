Zero,

You sound totally frustrated and disgusted with this token fiasco. I am too, as I feel lots of hard work I did was pissed away.

So, since I will get 1000 tokens way below lvl 200, I wanted to be able to get something for those extra tokens.

As we have seen, some people simply must have everything or they feel gimped and cheated, so higher token boards giving health, nano, etc would lead to the spiral you described.

That is why I said to make it ceremonial items which would provide no tangible benefit, so no one can say they are nerfed if they don't have the highest token board.

Lets say that higher token boards are created, with 1000 giving the last tangible benefits, and the rest ceremonial items. At this point, if people start whining because they cannot get the flaming purple crown of obsessive/ compulsive mission runners and want higher token rewards, then we know what kind of losers play this game. Self serving, lazy, cry babies.

At this point Funcom will give me a special nano- Essensce of Eeyore- which I will execute on these players and turn them into the jackasses they are!
