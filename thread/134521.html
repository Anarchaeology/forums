Items in the 14.8.2.2 database by mistake
We've noticed a lot of people are looking at - and discussing - a multitude of items that came with the latest update on Test-Live, i.e. 14.8.2.2. I'd just like to inform people that these items are not for the 14.8.2 update which is currently scheduled for the live dimensions May 28th.

The database in the 14.8.2.2 update on test-live was exported by a mistake, and while the new items since 14.8.2.1 are things we're working on, they're not meant to go live for some time still. Some of them are in need of small or big adjustments, while others are just test items.

And remember that an item being in the database is no guarrantee whatsoever for the item being available in the game. We won't change our way of working the database just because some clever players figured out how to rip the data.
