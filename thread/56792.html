The clan is like a pit bull...now even thought you love that pit bull and wish him well, the first time he bites bites you, you have to put him down. If your dog has rabies, then the only humane course of action is to put him to sleep.

What these cretins are trying to do is bring that feral rabies ridden dog to the table and try to explain to it why not to bite us? A dog that has lost it's mind to desease will never be something you can reason with. It might be calm for a while...but sooner or later it will attack your children. As a loyal soldier of Omni-Tek, i firmly protest this new peace talk.

Don't get me wrong, i wish for peace to reign on Rubi-Ka as much as the next loyalty citizens and employee of Omni-Tek, but not at the cost of the lives and rights of our people. These feral dogs calling themselves a clan will use this peace agreement to strenghten there forces while launching covert attacks on us using mediums such as the Dust Brigade, for one.

Oh they claim ignorance, as they too were attacked. Come on...that's just so ICC will believe that they are poor victims as well. This peace agreement will only shift the balance of power in there hands. They will use it to harm us, and will hide behind it and ICC's skirt.

Instead of wasting our time and energy in these ridiculous peace treaties, that they will NEVER respect anyway...we should concentrate on gathering evidence to expose the Dust Brigade as a faction of the clans and present this evidence to ICC. Then, once mother steps aside, we can deal with the evil hell spawned anarchistic dogs as they deserve.

When will you realise that Rubi-Ka is not big enough for both factions. And if the clans won't co-exist peacefuly with us, then it is our duty to ensure that future generations won't have the threat of war hanging above there heads. I'm sure Mr. Philip Ross will never, willingly, agree to this ridiculous peace agreement. It's an invitiation to disaster.

I've spoken my mind. You debate your peace talks behind a desk, while we loyal members of Omni-Tek give our blood, sweat and tears in the trenches.
