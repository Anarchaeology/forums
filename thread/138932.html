Ye olde useless skills
Adventuring: Slightly decrease the range of which monster mobs (only) aggro you dependant on how much skill you possess. Make the skill more useful by implementing a bonus of run/swim speed and concealment equalling ie 5% of the advent skill total. Only works in non-urban areas.

Trap Disarm/Perception: Implement locked doors with disarmable alarms/traps. Make it possible to disarm cameras.

Vehicle, Water/Swimming: Implement offshore areas with gargantuan oilrig like bases/areas and underseas grottos. Submersible vehicles or excellent swim skill would be needed to avoid drowning.

Vehicle, Ground: Implement some cool bikes (motorcycles/hoverbikes) with speeds that put normal ground vehicles to shame. Let bikes equip/unequip faster and allow equipping while aggroed (slower than otherwise).

Tutoring: Let this skill affect the xp gained for team mates of the tutor. Only use the person with the highest tutoring skill in any team. Each member would gain ie a +0.5% bonus xp (of standard xp reward) per 100 tutoring skill. Let tutoring skill allow the tutor to team with characters normally outside teaming range.

Grenade/Throwing: Implement some monstah grenade launchers with superior range and high AoE damage (low min dmg). The largest versions should have Heavy weapons requirement on them as well. Implement lighter versions in form of hand grenades with short range and AoE damage.

Knife or sharp object throwing: Implement some kewl throwing weapons with limited range. To make it more attractive implement some viable piercing weapons that can be used in both melee and ranged combat. When thrown the weapon should return like a boomerang.

Hope I covered all useless/near useless skills, what do you think?

That would only make it a nuisance I think. Maybe some areas only reachable with water vehicles. I frankly really don't see how swimming can ever be useful in game, since you can't fight while you are swimming.

If they make it so that you can fight while swimming and impose penalty or bonus on your abilities based on your swimming skill, now that would make it useful.

Imagine having towers in large bodies of water and then having your guild with swimming maxed. Talk about geographical advantage. And I think that's way cool. Heck, even the swim speed is a huge bonus in that sense.

As for adventuring, like others mentioned they can make mob less aggressive with an adventuring check. Also, it should give some kind of overall skill or abilities bonus according to the character's adventuring skill. Run speed, evades and concealment comes to mind immediately, perhaps weapon skills as well.

For the unused weapon skills, they just need to release some cool weapons that are comparable to other popular choices (or at least no so far behind).

I like the VG idea on being able to equip during aggro. That would only make sense that you can hope on a bike much easier than getting in a yalm.

Tutoring is just a bad concept... it implies transference of skills from one player to another and that's fundamentally open for exploits. I do like the xp bonus idea.
