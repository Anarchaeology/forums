Upstart Company buys out Fair Trade *edited version*
A new company has formed, known as West Tir Acquisitions, Inc. Its initial endeavor was to purchase the rights and ownership of the Fair Trade line of general stores. This Franchise includes a major Basic quality General store in Tir, and an Advanced quality General store in Borealis City. At the moment, th company has secured ownership of the Tir branch, but due to a unforseen change in the leadership of Borealis City, new negotiations must take place between West Tir Acquisitions Inc. and Rubi-Ka Asset Management, who has recently gained power in the City of Borealis. The store in Tir will retain the same name, however the service will be extended. The CEO of West Tir Acquisitions Inc., Director Myles "Tahrus" Barchus had this to say during an interview shortly following the official announcment:

"Fair Trade will still provide the best quality service and the best quality products to all citizens of Rubi-Ka, whether clan-affiliated, neutral, or Omni-tek employees. The goal of West Tir Acquisitions is to revamp Fair Trade, and expand the franchise to include Omni-tek accessible basic stores, and to open more stores through out Rubi-Ka. You should not notice an immediate change in the service and availability of items. We will gradually change the franchise, so that there is never a cease in service. We wish to keep our stores open so that you may still conduct business in them, rather than shut them down to rebuild. I will personally inspect all stores as soon as possible, to determine what needs to be changed and what new additions can be feasibly made. Please feel free to lend your suggestions if you happen to see me around. I will welcome and consider all constructive comments and criticism. More information regarding this franchise will be released at a later date."

Director Barchus also added, in regards to the store in Borealis City,

"West Tir Acquisitions does now own the Fair Trade basic store in Tir, but now must continue negotiations in Borealis City in order to secure the rights to that particular store. The reason being is with Borealis City under new leadership, the contracts signed by the former regime are deemed null and void, and new contracts must be drawn. Therefore negotiations between myself and the CEO of Rubi-Ka Asset Management must begin." "However," the soft-spoken yet charismatic CEO added with a confident smile, " I am more than confident that an agreement can be reached swiftly, and West Tir Acquisitions will be soon able to provide the best customer service to all Rubi-Ka Citizens, and our vision for Fair Trade Borealis will become a swift reality."

Again, West Tir Acquisitions Inc. has secured the rights and ownership of the Fair Trade franchise. Expect to see the CEO of West Tir Acquisitions Inc., Myles "Tahrus" Barchus making his rounds in Fair Trade, and feel free to lend your own suggestions as to changes that could be made in the store.
