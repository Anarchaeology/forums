Galway County - A travellers report
Today, yours truly Vitniri, junior member of The Mockers can reveal shocking news from the Bronto industry in Galway.

It all happened the night before last while I was scouting Galway County for intelligence gathering purposes. Suddenly while travelling westwards on the road, vastly over the regulated speed limit, I stumbled onto this sign. I started sneaking to get a closer look at this new mysterious facility. It turned out to be the factory where all of Rubi-Kas Bronto Burgers are manufactured. The guards were not openly hostile but they clearly didnt want to answer any questions. Upon examining the facility I came across an assembly line with the familiar Bronto Burgers coming out. I also discovered that the standards for hygiene were less than satisfactory for a food plant. The worst discovery however was yet to come. All around me I could see and hear the cute little minibrontos munching on their grass, little did they know of the horror that was awaiting!

Once the machinery needed more raw materials the gate would open and the medieval slaughter methods employed by the Omni-Tek personnel would commence. Something had to be done and I searched among my uploaded programs for a suitable tool. I used a program to lasso a larger group of minibrontos and YES! the entire heard decided to follow me as I guided them out of this terrible place. The guards and especially the Drovers seeing their livelihood disappear were not too happy about this sudden development and your brave fixer had to run guiding the brontos out.

Once outside however I discovered that only a few brontos had decided to follow me into freedom and I recklessly digitised myself out of there in contempt. My dismay was great as I thought of these creatures while visiting my favourite Food Provider. Maybe stupid creatures deserve to die horribly to become tasty snacks?

After feasting on a delicious Bronto Burger with cheese my mind was set, they had it coming! As the gentleman I am I complimented my hostess for the evening before calling it a day. That is all for now, if your undercover fixer reveals other important news, you can count on hearing it on this channel.
