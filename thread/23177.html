Get some ppl together to help you...
Join a guild or get some ppl to help you level. What I mean by this is get a high level doc or MP to be there for healing support, a tank class to finish the kill of a mob (they help you kill the mob once you have done more than 50% of the damage), etc.

Some ppl with 2 computers, and good connections, pay for 2 accounts. Then make a doc (easiest class to level IMO) and get he/she/neut up to a pretty decent level and have the doc follow your new toons around providing backup healing, etc.

There are many ways to "power level". However, I think the best and quickest way to level is to just find a hard core team and play till your eyes hurt. =P

The key to powerleveling is simple.

CREDITS!

If you have those then you have what it takes to get everything you want. When I make a twink to powerlevel I give them a full set of Q50 implants for the skills they will be using. CL belt items that require about 90ish computer literacy to equip, which would give me around 40-50 NCU. Every store bought nano and possibly every high level nano that profession will need. Twink items like Concrete Pillows, Shades of Lucabration, Treatment Suit, Rat Catcher Goggles, Pioneer Backpack, Q50 coats, and anything else that will help. And lastly a weapon if need be.

I will also do other things along the way, like making new implants, getting new guns, finding a low Qual Yalm, and other things. The trick is having a high level character to run most of these erands for you and having around 20m extra to waste.

You spend about 2-3 days twinking and about 3-4 days actually leveling for about 5-6 hours a day to get to level 100 in a week. Ofcourse you can do it faster but why hurt your head and possibly waste time when you can do it the slower way and get done in a week.

The next trick is to NOT get rid of anything you just made for your Twink. When you are done with it either trade it to a guildy that twinked for your new twink or save it for the next twink if possible.

So if you want to get ahead of the game then goto WWW.EBAY.COM that will get you ahead fast.

As for knowing the game??? Well dont worry about it. Not alot to the game so powerlevel until it isnt fun anymore. Then you will know you have reached the highlevel game and can either quit, *****/moan or start another twink.
