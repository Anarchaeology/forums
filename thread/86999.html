The "N" word...
Man... Let's stop crying nerf already. Enough is enough.

There are people on these boards who have been playing this game for over a year, some of them 1 1/2 years.

I think its time we all sat back and evaluated what the word nerf does to other people, instead of being so selfish to call it out whenever our skills do not match up.

N.E.R.F. = 4 letter word.

Lets be a bit more concious about using it arbitrarily.

Why? Because its not funny anymore. People are sick of it. I'm tired of hearing it, I know a ton of other vets in this game who are also sick of hearing it.

I'm running out of IPR points... How many of you are also running out ? How many more nerfs will it take to get people to realize that the only true answer to imbalance is to fix the weaker profession up to par with the others. Not degrade a profession (any profession) which was long time worthless and now has a fighting chance.

I think we could all be much more productive by not taking the often easy route and crying nerf. Instead we could be pro-active and apply our various experience into 1 pool of knowlege. Thus giving FC a resource to assist in balance, not making the problem worse.
