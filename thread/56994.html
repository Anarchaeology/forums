Just An Idea
Ok, I've read some posts about how people want indoor places to pvp. And I think that would be a really good idea. So, while sitting in camelot castle tonight, I came up with a good idea. Atleast I think its good, I've had "Good Ideas" before that have been shot down lol.

Anyway how about making the entire castle 25%. So that you can run throughout the entire place fighting omni/clan. Not just the dungeon. Its a huge place, tons of places to camp, plus there wont be as much zoning in the deep parts of the castle. And even if someone tries to run when they are back in the library, there could be omni/clan all over the place to stop them. I'd be more of a war like atmosphere.

The "Man-At-Arms" might be a problem. If they attacked omnis it'd make it unfair for them. So maybe something can be changed so they werent in the castle or something.

Anyway, if making the entire camelot castle a political zone would be too much of a problem, why not make a new castle of some sort for pvp. I think it'd be awesome. And even more exciting if team pvp was more prominent.

So, post your responses. Ideas to add on. Flames. Just see if we can get anywhere with the indoor pvp scene.
