Keeping the same weapon for your entire life
I have recently come back to AO after about a six month hiatus. Things are vastly improved...and finally...fun.

One thing that would increase this even more would be an emotional attachment to one's primary weapon. Alas, this is impossible due to the quality level being static.

When I came back to the game, I got the impression that a sufficiently skilled weaponsmith could take a quality level 5 weapon and improve it. This really got me excited about things again.

If you find that lucky weapon in a dungeon or dropped off a mob, especially at lower levels, and you know you'll be able to continually improve it's level to match yours (depending on the amount of credits and effort you want to invest), it would give more of an immersion factor. If you really want to have fun...let players name their primary weapons Hey, if Bilbo can do it, I want to as well.

To take this a step further and increase the bond between character and weapon, allow for a chance to completely loose said weapon on death. For instance, you die, you go get your thing from the Reclaim, only to find out that Heavy Grinner you've been nursing along for 20 levels has been lost to the winds of time.

Thoughts?
