Token board request -please
Simple post for Funcom. Please add more Title levels, you can decide what you want to give for them.

I am at 320 tokens and will reach 400 at about level 92 or so. As a solo player in this game, I don't have many advantages. I don't have guild members to give me those >125 level weapons, implants and armour and shopping for it is very time consuming. I don't really have the type of schedule which permits team play or guild activity.

I do lots of missions and get lots of tokens. All I have to shoot for is the title levels and its bonuses. I like that incentive. It seems a waste to have to go from 400 to 1000 for the next raise. I think title level should be raised every 100 tokens, just to keep that incentive real. Even for me 100 tokens is a significant achievement. Since I don't team I can't take high level missions to get that high level booty, so I need some perk.

I really don't care what is is, so much has already been posted on this. I just want you to keep that carrot out in front of this old mule.

Some official response would be nice. Thanks.

Final note: gameplay for me was almost at a standstill (<50% missions) till some very nice player gave me upper level lead pipes to use. I can't expect pennies from heaven like this on a routine basis.
