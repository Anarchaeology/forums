EDIT: Btw, a word on calcuttas. Any team bought in the calcutta has the option to pay half of the bid to the buyer for a chance to win half of the calcutta money. So lets say Talanie bought Pogobear/Kasean for 14 mil. Now Pogobear/Kasean has the option of paying Talanie 7 mil (1/2 of 14) for a chance to win 1/2 of the entire calcutta pot if they win. Also, pooling credits with someone else to outbid someone in the calcutta is allowed. However, the original buyer of a team has the right to refuse any offers after fixtures are announced.

Finally, let me know if anyone likes the idea of rewarding 1st and 2nd place for Calcutta pots, instead of winner takes all.
