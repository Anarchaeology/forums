Sounds like you're using a Vektor. It's a very crit reliant weapon. You really need to pump up your chance to crit to get those to work. MA buffs and scopes both help.

The Ithaca is a more common shotgun these days. Still crit reliant, but has aimed shot - a very nice special.

If you want to avoid crit reliance, the Home Defender is a better option, but still does significantly more damage with a crit.

There's a new shotgun that may appear next patch, the Maw of the Abyss. It will give better non-crit damage. But it requires flingshot.

Oh, pushing the AggDef slider further toward the right should not increase the damage per shot. It should increase your number of shots, which will increase your damage/sec.

It also has the effect of decreasing your evades :/ So, if you push the slider to the right to get more attacks in, anyone/anything that attacks you will have a better chance of hitting and will be more likely to get a crit.
