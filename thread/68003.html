Something Screwy going on!!
With the dissaperance of the Council of Truth (OT Kidnapping), and the increased security at all Omni Grid and Whompa Locations, it would appear that Omni-Tek are about to launch a full scale assault on the Clans.

Already, any Omni Employee can wonder the Streets of Tir with impunity, whereas Clanners are killed on sight for going any where near Omni Tek claimed territory.

Now we have the rumours that Omni-Mines have discovered a huge Notum deposit within Clan Territory, and everything becomes clear.
