PVP question which will be ignored
Here's a question for anyone at Funcom to answer.

You've been making lots of changes in PVP in the previous month or so, in an attempt to fix it or make it better.

The end result is that even less people engage in PVP than before. Instead of only 1 player in 1000 PVP'ing, now it appears to be more like 1 in 5000.

So the question is, doesn't this tell you that maybe you're not making the right changes?

Why aren't there hundreds of people at any one time PVPing and fighting great battles? There are many hundreds or thousands at any one time shopping, doing missions, fighting in groups, etc. Why is almost no one PVPing?

I agree with Zierow. The only MP games I have ever seen with working, balanced PVP are FPS games like Quake, Unreal and the like. I have never seen or heard of an MMORPG with a PVP system worth mentioning (imho) and I would be very surprised if Funcom can pull it off. Nothing against them, its just that after years of watching other mmorpg franchises try to develop a *decent* PVP system and consistently failing, I have come to believe that PVP just does not work in the MMORPG genre. RVR (Realm vs Realm) is a new concept (at least to my knowledge) and daoc is working on that. Perhaps not so much a new concept as it is an admission that PVP in MMORPG's is a futile endeavour and *this* is an alternative approach to the game element. I've heard mixed reports of whether it has merit or not as an alternative to basic PVP. I guess time will tell. Perhaps FC should be looking at alternatives? It would be terrible to spend years of resources on a PVP system that never ends up working as expected (as have other MMORPG's), when it could have been spent on an alternative approach or even just abandoning it and increasing the resources used to perfect the PVM element.....
