ease of use.
Ok here ill try to touch on all the professions that you found intereting since i have played them all a lil bit.

Crats: Can be expensive chars to maintain if you dont have any idea on how to generate a decent income on your own. However, I am currently playin a crat and am now level 31. These chars are just plain fun once you hit lvl 10-15.

Pros: Attack Pet (low-medium strength), Charms (1 stable(lasts for 2minutes+) and 1 unstable (at level 31 mine lasts 44 seconds) allows to charm a monster in mission or in the field wiht a Psychic check (ie their psychic must be 99 or lower)) Mez's (causes the monster to stop attacking all together for a length of time and will not enter combat mode unless attacked) roots, exp team buffs, crit team buffs, always welcomed in a group, main role = crowd control.

Fixer: The thief class, easy to maintain since very few skills require lots of cash but nano skills are dark blue and hard to train specializes in break and enter and trap disarming.

Pros: Warp to grid nanos (great for travel pretty much transport you to an area wher you can get to most towns in seconds), run buffs, good trade skills

Nano-Technician: Main mage class, has lots of nukes and AE (area effect) nukes. Can be very daunting for a beginner due to low life and high maintenance costs.

Pros: uhmm...... go read the NT forum and try to filter thru the crap to find some cuz i cant think of any (sorry NT's i tried and failed.... you guys need some serious lovin, just my opinion pls dont flame me if you disagree)

Trader: Root + Shoot and debuff the crap outa anything. These guys are also one of the main tradeskill classes. Properly equipped and setup (IP wise) these guys are deadly since they can reduce an enemies fighting abilities to almost nothing while at the same time increasing their own abilities drasitcaly.

Pros: Right now they not sought after as much in groups and are considered buffing whores (lol) but after the no OE (over equipping) patch will be sought after greatly in groups for group attack buffs, so the group can equip higher level weapons. These guys are also lots of fun with roots since i find them damn funny, the onyl hard thing is that roots break easily so it takes practice to nail technique down.

Engineer: Pet class (strong pet) with great tradeskills.

Pros: damn that pet is sweet if you can get buffed into a good one, always sought after for groups because the pets make decent tanks. High maintenance costs due to always upgrading pets and pets cost creds to summon.

Meta-Physicist: Pet Class (weak-medium pets) however you can summon 3 of em at any point in time (1 attack, 1 heal, 1 stun) and so they can be very useful and when you get that heal pet easy to solo with.

Pros: Once again easy solo char due to healin pet, sought after in groups cuz they the only class that gets great buffs for all nano skills (masteries can be learned by level 30 that add +50 to any 1 nano skill) but once again can be expensive due to upgrading pets.

As has been said earlier in this post, if you want the ultimate supreme self reliant char for your first char, then go Martial Artist. They have the lowest maintenance cost, well rounded nano formulas, and great dmg if not using a weapon (past 120 martial arts skill you can use weapons that have a martial skill requirement to ADD to your dmg as well) these chars are great for solo or group play since in a group they become the backup tank and have a nice crit buff. These chars are great for learning your way around Rubi-Ka and generating income to dump into a more advanced (strategy wise) character.

I know the post was long but i hope it helped. If you really havin a hard time gettin the all important creds though and you play on Rubi-ka1 give Uglyfrank or Tinycrat a shout and ill give ya some pointers.
