Please, for the love of Ross, give in-game grid information!
This is so monumentally easy it defies explaination as to why it isn't there already.

Problem the first:

There is -ONE- mention of the Grid that I have found in the (online) manual; a small line in the description of the Fixer that says something to the effect of, 'Fixers can use "The Grid".' That's it.

Problem the second:

Nowhere is there a listing - or a way to find out - unless it's eluding me - of how much Computer Literacy the different Grid terminals (and their exits, once inside) require. The only source of information I have found is an UNOFFICIAL FAN SITE. I apologise for the capital letters, but it's rather appalling when one of the major features of a game can only be learned about from a third-party source.

There is not even a message that pops up when you can't use one! There's the blip-blip-blip sound, and that's it!

This is a severe drawback because the Grid seems to be a major mode of transportation, especially for those who don't want to blow five million on a little flying car (and the associated IP to go with it); whom-pas don't go everywhere, while there are grid access terminals in every zone (that I've seen; correct? incorrect?). Computer Literacy is far more cost-effective and useful overall than Vehicle Flying, as well.


Solution:

Superbly, mind-numbingly easy. Two small fixes, in order of the problems:

1) A short book in the Bookstore Terminals giving a brief rundown on the Grid; what it is, what it does, what you need (Computer Literacy) to use it.

2) Each terminal gets a unique description; by "unique" I mean they get a line added to their target information ('t' or shift-left click) that says, "This terminal requires X Computer Literacy," where X is whatever number you need. It doesn't even have to be exact! It could say something like, "This terminal requires about 70 Computer Literacy to use," and it would specifically need 75 or such.

I don't remember if there's a message saying whether you need X amount of CL once inside the grid to use an exit; if not, there should be.


Anyway. This just sort of really, -really- annoys me; it's one of those basic, underlying modes of transportation in the game and there's no official information on it. Bugs the heck out of me.
