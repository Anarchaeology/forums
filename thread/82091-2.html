Speaking of burst and burst recycle..

I recently switched back to pure SMGs from using a CHS for a while. In pvp I use GA and go full def, relying on burst and try running in and out of range bursting every 7-8 seconds.

At my level, my RI is at 1k+ and my burst is maxed at 850 something selfbuffed - so the recharge is fast. Burst, root, out of range, recharge, run in, burst, root, and so on. Init debuffs dont really matter as I dont use normal attacks in that sense.

Results? I've killed doctors and traders(!) this way. I even tried against an NT friend (not using the anti-GA nuke tho) going to 50% - we agreed that I'd have won 2 times out of 3.
