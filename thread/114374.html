Soldier..a newbie take on the profession
First of all I would like to say this forum has been a wealth of knowledge for me and i would like to say thank you to all who have posted here with their information. I have a level 15 soldier Atrox (I like the extra HP's) although i do have to admit i have twinked him to the max with my 75 level fixer ie; and i believe this is a good thing to do for anyone starting a new character in my own opinion. I went a hit all the QL5 to 10 Dynacamps to get the things beforehand that i feel would be benificial to my new character, also i had QL10 implants in my soldier at level 3 and at level 15 i have a custom set of QL35 implants in him as well of course i required buffing also was lucky to find almost a whole set of Sekutek Chilled Plasteel Armor very good i believe at the level that i am at. One of my better finds albeit via a loaner was a QL1 Heavy Supressor which i had in his hand at level 2. He now uses a QL16 Heavy Supressor which i think i will try and stay with this line with a Nova (which i have not found yet) as my Alpha, although i have heard not to try and use this (Heavy Supressor) until a higher level, but as long as i keep finding them in my QL i figure what the hell. I get "burst" "fling" and "full auto". being an Atrox i figure i can handle the "full auto". I do incredible damage for the level that i am at. I made sure to max my base abilities every level and keep an IP reserve until level 14 which i dumped into my weapon and nano skill to be able to use TMS MKI, plus a few other nano's. I really like this profession and hope to accelerate with him. Once again thanks go out to all who have posted here.

Hehe good luck with your soldier! Yes, if you have a steady supply of HS' its fine, but do know at later levels you want to switch to a more "pure" gun that asks soldier weaponskills. SMG is more a fixerskill; we cannot buff it and implant it really well like we can with AR and RE.

If you reach level 30ish or so you might want to check the shops for a Novaflow-Flashpoint combo (latter one to do mighty alphas) or get lucky with a team-mission and get a div9 gun (or buy one from the shopping terminals).

I used HS for quite some lvls, I think up to lvl 80ish at least. When "twinked" those guns really pack a punch, but that goes away with higher levels.
