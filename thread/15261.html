Travel in this game is crappy.
This post is just my own opinion on this matter. This game hardly has any exploration because travel is soooooo easy. Hop into the whompa..POW! your across the world in a different city. Or..jump in the grid...and digitally float to far away lands in a matter of seconds. I came from EQ, and I like the feeling that I am actually crossing a vast world. Now even though EQ may not have had the size of this game...the traveling was much more effort demanding, and promoted exploration in the world. My personal opinions on this are as follows:

1. Get rid of the whompa's!!!! Yes I said it! Get rid of the blasted things and confine chat channels to their specific city. Without Whompas you'd have to OMG!! actually travel in the game and not instaport!

2. Raise req for the grid. Its pretty sad that you can access the grid at around lvl 15 :/ and on top of that whompas?? Completely destroying any ideas of exploration..why explore when you can whompa???

3. Add the subway system to replace the whompa's. At least this would make travel in the game seem that way. And not an effortless thing.

4. Maybe spread the nanos out a bit..maybe make certain nanos only available in certain citys. EQ did this..you will never find all your spells in one city. I personally think that was a good system that promoted a lot of exploration.

These are my personal idea's..feel free to comment on them if you choose to.
