Grid Armor - Bugs/Information
Important stats: LVL 175 Fixer (Opifex) Max Title Evades + QL 200 Evade driven implants. QL 200 Suppressor, 871 self buffed attack rating.

I have had the opportunity now to extensively test Grid Armor MKIV. I'm posting my findings here so that hopefully my experiences will help all of you decide whether or not Grid Armor is something you want to invest IP in.

PVM: Seasoned - Veteran - Real Mean up to lvl 199 mobs. (Unbugged results)

Grid Armor is very effective when it works. I've noticed that comparing Grid Armor to wearing QL 200 Elite/Pol/Primus (I have worn all 3 in PVM for extensive time periods) is apples and oranges when and if you are hit by a mob. Average damage when hit by a mob is about the same. I do seem to take a bit more damage on average in Grid Armor than I do in QL 200 armor when I'm hit.

Grid Armor does however allow me to evade being hit at all more often than not. When it works, doc's don't waste much nano on healing you.

Grid Armor MKIV does not allow a fixer to solo missions at high levels because of the current bugs. If FC were to fix those bugs, it would in most cases.

The bad news -

Bugs: Something causes grid armor to fail horribly if you get hit by a mob for critical damage. Examples below.
_________________________________

Mob: Veteran Techwrecker
Mission QL: 190ish

This mob can crit you up to 2120ish in Grid Armor when the bug happens. The worst part is that once the mob gets one of these bugged hits, they just keep coming and the mob will continuously hit you for this uber damage until you die.

This mob hits around 1280 max crit damage on me in QL 200 armor.

This mob hits around 1380 max crit damage on me in absolutely no armor, no protective AC buffs or shields whatsoever.
_________________________________

Mob: Seasoned Poacher
Mission QL: 190ish

This mob can crit you up to 1800ish in Grid Armor when the bug happens. The worst part is that once the mob gets one of these bugged hits, they just keep coming and the mob will continuously hit you for this uber damage until you die.

This mob hits around 990 max crit damage on me in QL 200 armor.

This mob hits around 1140 max crit damage on me in absolutely no armor, no protective AC buffs or shields whatsoever.
_________________________________

Mob: Real Mean (Any Melee/Projectile/Energy Damage Mob)
Mission QL: 196ish

This mob can crit you up to 2400ish in Grid Armor when the bug happens. The worst part is that once the mob gets one of these bugged hits, they just keep coming and the mob will continuously hit you for this uber damage until you die.

This mob hits around 1450 max crit damage on me in QL 200 armor.

This mob hits around 1600 max crit damage on me in absolutely no armor, no protective AC buffs or shields whatsoever.
_________________________________

Now, this bug is not every time. This bug is not easy to reproduce because when it happens there is absolutely no warning whatsoever. During the testing phaze of MKIV for me, this has happened about 40-50 times in total. This includes all missions since I got the MKIV Grid Armor at lvl 167. 8 Full levels of missions went into those numbers I presented, the damages from the QL 200 armors were taken from my previous levels and the damages while not wearing any armor is something I have slowly assembled during the MKIV missions when the armor expired or I took it off to do a "lets see that again" test.

I thought initially that this bug had something to do with GA expiring and not dissapearing. That is not the case, this bug happens on grid armor that is 2 minutes old. Summoned inside the mission, summoned outside the mission... makes no difference. Agro to full def/full agro makes no difference to the bug, when you get nailed the first time just pray your doc is on it and really really good at what they do or kiss your exp good bye.

I then thought, hmm... maybe its my run buff or something. Nope, that was also a dead end. Augmentations? Nope, dead end.

I petitioned this bug and managed to get in contact with GM Ashed a while back. Ashed said he was going to put some MKIV on and go test it. I gave him details, promised him some good screenshots and that was that. I have since petitioned 4 seperate times to get in contact with Ashed to give him the screenshots. My petitions are either deleted or I am told that Ashed isn't online. I have emailed the bugged screenshots to bugs@anarchy-online.com... Not sure what they did with them.

No official information from FC has been made available that I am aware of on this bug. Make no mistake, this is a show stopper. If this is allowed to continue, GA is worthless because you can be going along just fine and have 2-3M exp in a mission, then bam your dead with no chance of saving yourself, including an evac.

Now for the worst news yet:

Grid Armor MKIV: PVP

FORGET IT! I'm not sure how it could be related, if it is related to the bug explained above but EVERY SINGLE hit you take in MKIV Grid Armor during pvp will be a crit. Bottom line, Grid Armor = worthless for pvp in any situation, even against lower level opponents. You might as well pvp in your newb suit.

Special attacks also do max damage vs MKIV.

Summary:

Grid Armor MKIV works well, when it works for PVM only. As for PVP, don't kid yourself... This is about the worst possible thing FC could have given us to protect ourselves in PVP. It acts as a damage multiplier, not reducer.

Would I spend the IP again on MKIV Grid Armor knowing what I know now? Yes I would, only because I really do believe FC will eventually wake up and realize that there is something VERY wrong with it and fix it. If it actually worked like it was supposed to, it would be worth using.

Here is the downside for lower level fixers. You have to make a choice obviously with your IP distribution. If you build around Grid Armor, your base atribs will suffer and thus your AC will be horrid after the OE patch when you try to equip non-social armors for pvp or to substitute if the GA bugs get worse with new patches.

Advise: Get your base atribs up there first, then worry about grid armor as a secondary. The reason I advise this is because we know AC actually does something in PVP and it doesn't add nearly 40% damage in PVM when a phantom bug occurs.
