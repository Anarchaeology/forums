If at least 75% of the posts in these forums were the least bit constructive, maybe the devs would have the time to actually read them.

Usually a good idea is followed by a massive number of flames (not always though) making it hard to sort out the goodies.

If the only reply you can think of is: "R U Nubi?" or "Come back when you're 150+ and über like me", maybe it's better not to post at all and let the people with the constructive ideas share these with the devs on the "Future Anarchy" forums.

The bottom line is: If you keep it constructive, the devs are probably more likely to take your ideas into consideration.
