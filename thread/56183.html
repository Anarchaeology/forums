Adventuring
I've read some suggestions about adventuring, but I have not read all, so please be gentle



When I think of adventuring, I think of beeing able to stay out in the wild for a while, without having people around you begging for buffs all the time.

So.. Some suggestions..

Setting up a camp
You're out in the wild, you decide that enough is enough, and wanna sit down and chat for a bit with some friends (guildchat, whatever).
You find a safe spot, you cast a Camp nano that puts up a tent, after 3 minutes, the tent is up and you're safe from any lurking mobs around.
(Adventurers could get a team version of this, and maybe added regeneration as well as a small hp boost afterwards (due to beeing fully rested), or maybe they could get a camoflaged version to hide deep behind enemy lines :-D.)

A long delay would mean that you cant put up a tent right in a spawn, you can however go AFK in a political zone, if you need to help out some friends with advice, or you're just one of those guys who like deserted places.
A soldier, 1 man camoflaged tent is also a possibility, and I'm sure a design team could figure some more stuff out.

CANNOT BE PUT UP WHEN IN FIGHT (to avoid exploiting)
a 30s-1 minute unequip time would also make it so one cannot pop right out when one want to.

and make it so one automaticly sits when in tent, so one can log off in this position. (nano casting restricted of course, who the h... stands up in a tent anyway)


Beeing able to spot mobs where you dont have a map
New map upgrades could allow people to see mobs in areas where they dont have a map(some maps have too high MN skills), whereas every X adventuring gave you the ability to see up to Y lvl mobs.


Tracking Tool
A tool based on adventuring alone, where the player goes into /apachi and are able to search for what creatures are nearby.
The QL of the tool decides how high LvL of mobs he will find, but he should allways be able to find higher ones than himself without problems.



tnx to Naraya who got me onto the Tent thingy

anyway.. some of these suggestions may probably seem silly, but I'm sure someone are able to work further with 'em and make it useful..
I would especially love to have that tent

Alright, my thoughts.

When you are inside this tent you
a) Cannot be attacked
b) Can't attack anything else
c) Must not be in a fight already.

Rightclicking the tent should transport you inside. You get added healing, and maybe a little health boost- Like the guy above said.

Also, in case you exit the tent in a political zone, or near auto-aggro mobs, you get 15 seconds of 'run time'.

Oh yeah...I wanna store stuff in my tent.

In addition to that, I'd like a campfire that can heal HP faster as well...like a tent, but a lower-level nano and no protection from enemies.
