Fun times in 2HO...
I just got to say that this past Saturday was a kickass day to PvP in 2HO.

Someone shouted that there were a few Omni's in 2HO and they were getting spanked and needed help. So, we mustered up the cavalry, a bit slower than usual, but we got them there.

Vergil, Tiros, Silversomething, Univers5 (?), who else I miss from you Omni's? Were all putting up a damn awesome fight and got us clanners held up in the bunker and picking us off one by one when we zoned out.

I zoned out of the bunker and targetted Tiros and saw he was in range...I cast crit, AS 1536, Fling 1325, crit 1K, crit 1K, Silversomething jumps in and BAM I'm dead, but I see Tiros with like a sliver left of HP. Sad thing is, no one zoned out with me to finish him off...

Well, I rez, head back, and we then got the plan to make a mad, organized rush all at once...

Well, that was awesome, clans all zone out, about 20 of us, and start obliterateing the 15-20ish Omni's outside. We pushed them back to the whompa...

I'm not saying that clan is better than Omni or vice versa. I just wanted to say that both sides fought well and held ground for a long time.

I had a lot of fun and hoped that everyone did too...

Bring it on!
