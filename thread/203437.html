Rubi-Ka`s first Superhero takes the mantle!
Special Report by Rimor Extra News(REN) today, the 20th November!

Martial Artist Engelen has today, in an official statement, confirmed that she will step up as a Guardian of Rubi-Ka, protecting and helping every man, woman and atrox in need, wherever the need is requested.
Engelen`s own words are:

"I wish to make Rimor a better place to live, and if there is anything I can do to ensure that, it would be my deepest pleasure"

Martial Artist Engelen, highly skilled in Close Combat and Martial Arts, Survival, Technology, Detective skills and investigations, has today offered Rimor her help. She hopes that more will follow, and that people stand up and fight for others, against any evil that threatens the safety of the inhabitans of Rimor.

As a final comment, she says:

"I will not only focus my fight against the biggest powers, but will also do my best to ensure that everyone`s everyday needs are fulfilled, in any way possible."

And with that, we wish Engelen good luck on her first day, as the first official Superhero on Rimor!
