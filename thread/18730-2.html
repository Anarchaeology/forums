You did the math, and you did it wrong...

Take an enforcer, level 80 wielding a 120 beam with an attack rating of 450.

His average damage (before scaling by attack rating) ranges from 22 - 320.

On average then he will do (( 320 - 22 ) / 2) + 22 multiplied up by his attack rating. Thats 171.

Now the formula for attack rating is (1 + atk/400) * weap_damage

So ave damage is (1 + 450/400) * 171 = 363.375

Now at around lvl 80 a +56 wrangle is easily achievable for trader (you dont expect us to be getting mochams or trying to run multiple ransack/deprives to wrangle higher do you?). OK with QL125 implants higher level wrangles could probably be cast but lets use +56 for an example.

Now, after +56 is added to attack rating the same enforcer does:
(1 + 506 / 400) * 171 = 387.315

Thats around 6% more not your wildly off 44%. Do you really think that PvP enthusiasts wouldnt be queuing up for a wrangle just before a fight if it were that good? Clue, get one. Soon.
