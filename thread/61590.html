The Reformists Present... Reet's Parties every saturday!
The Reformists have recently decided to host parties at Reet's every saturday night at 10pm EST till the beats and booties quit groovin. We have and will spend much time gathering and providing you house, trance, techno, dnb, and your requests for songs you would like to hear. Here is some info on the next event...

Event Title: Party at Reet's
Dimension: Rubi-Ka 2
Date: 2002-10-26
Time: 22:00:00
Timezone: EST Eastern Standard Time

Event Description
Come join the fun at Reet's this saturday and every saturday. Whether you come to dance with your favorite partners or listen to the slamming beats you will find yourself a good time. With music by The Reformists Radio/DJ Soletrain in the genre of trance, techno, house, dnb, or whatever you want to hear to get you in your dance groove, we will find time to play it and make your musical experience most enjoyable. Strap up your heels, pick your fro, throw on your Katara's to come get down and dirty on RK2. Hope to see you there! For more information contact Soletrain ingame or at the e-mail xozicide@rochester.rr.com/visit The Reformists website http://reformists.ionichost.com.

We are looking for more DJs and people to run relay servers so we can stream out our music to more people than we can currently. If you interested drop by our website or email Soletrain.

Hope to see you there, and enjoying yourself.
