Ah ... I guess I could try and "explain" why they ended up with this odd amalgam of Future and middle ages - But it's kinda complicated. So I'll try a simplified version of it.


Basically , it stems from the basic 4 Arch types of characters and how to apply basic Team tactics.

The only way to avoid this odd thing, is to let EVERYONE be good at ALL of it .. so basically you end up with just 1 Class that everyone plays .. better known as the Tank Mage on most MUDs and the likes.

However, if you want to split the different skills and abilities so you get diverse characters, you also need tactics so the poor Doctors is not stomped by a monster while the Enforcer is standing 30 meters away with his Gun, shooting and shouting at it.

Thus you quickly end up with Melee weapons.
And the only Melee weapons we know of that was any worth, are those used in the middle ages.

In order to make a Fighter act like an WW2 Tank, is to give him Melee weapons so he will actually go up in front to shield the weaker characters.



Thus we wind up at the basic Arch types of characters :

-Damage Dealers (Thieves, Archers, Riflemen, Wizards, Bazooka's ... etc.)
-Damage Takers (Fighters, Tanks, Roman Pike-and-Shield men, Powered Battle Suits, Paladins ... etc.)
-Supporters (Doctors, Field Medics, Clerics, Shamans, Tactical Commanders .. etc.)
-Crowd Control (Enchanters, Hackers, Fire Supporessors, Tear Gas teams .. etc.)


Those are the basic Arch type classes for simple MMOG fighting. If you are strict, you could say there are more arch types, since we haven't touched the entire subject of the industrial side of a fighting Nation ... so I guess we would have stuff like Factory Worker and stuff ... but almost noone would play as a Factory Worker in a Sci-fi/Fantasy MMOG computer game.

You will also notice that I took all sorts of types and put in there, not just AO types, but also EQ types and real world types .. even a few sci-fi types ... yet they still fit because, surprise, surprise, they are Arche Types.

In fact, I think we have all the Arche Types in todays millitary, except for the Crowd Control one, which is sorta unique to Games since it Requires "Magic" of some sort to to, though there are some crude approaches to it with tear gas and tranquilizers and stuff.




There - That was the simplest I could do it.

In literature, science fiction is generally differentiated (and more respected... the good sci-fi that is) from fantasy because it usually has a theme of foreshadowing the effects of the faults of humanity. (Orwell's 1984, Huxley's Brave New World for example).

Stylistically speaking, there are no restrictions on science fiction. It can be completely "fantasy" if it wants. It can fall away from the literary definition of science fiction and even deviate from taking place in the future (Star Wars). It also isn't necessary for technology to exist (post-apocalyptic).

So I guess what I am trying to say if FunCom can do whatever they want with Rubi-Ka. Be it the presence of magic (errrrr... "nano-technology"), powerful swords or the like, one really can't argue with the validity of the science-fiction claim. Now, I'm not saying it is or isn't lame as hell, but it really just boils down to personal taste.
