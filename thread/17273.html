I'm taking a stand. (long post of my email to funcom)
The following is taken directly from my e-mail to feedback@funcom.com, regarding their "no refunds policy"on lost in-game items, and a number of bugs which are becoming more frequent.

Hi,

Earlier today I was killed by the "undead, still fighting after he died" mob bug that has appeared since the 13.8 patch. You can see the screenshots of the chat dialog here, here, here, here, and here. These are all 100(ish)K jpgs created directly from my screenshots, and are included to allow you to verify that I haven't edited it - the original png files are available should you need them. An edited image showing just the chat dialog is here, which is included for convienance.

As you can see, I was attacked six times, for a total of 794 damage, after the mob was reported as dead. The "Target can't be in a fight.." mesages are from my attempts to use heal kits to stay alive. As you can also see, I lost 97,648 xp as a result. This is no huge quantity of xp, but does represent a considerable input of time as I was soloing for items. As any medium level player knows, soloing at level 80+ is not an easy task. It had taken about an hour to get that xp, and I was about 1/3 into the mission when I died.

Having been sent to the reclaim in Rome, I took the screenshots and /petitioned about the issue. I waited for about an hour and an ARK arrived to talk to me about the issue. The conversation can be seen here, here, and here. Again, these are 100k jpgs, but the bottom line is he said "we can't refund xp".

First off, I have no complaints about the ARK - he was very polite and was unable to give me the xp because thats your policy. Basically, I'm not exploiting or trying to gain anything I don't deserve. What I'd like is my xp refunded. As the ARK said, "This is a known problem and we are working on fixing it." While the 96k I lost is not a huge issue, its the point that is important. The problem is yours - you wrote the code, you failed to uncover and fix this bug during testing, you have not given the playerbase any information regarding when this may be fixed, and you punish players who are affected by it.

Plain and simple this is unacceptable. I am aware that the game has no guarantees, and that the reason behind your "no refunds" policy is the danger of exploitation but the fact remains that you punish the majority to prevent a minority from exploiting. In addition, you have taken no action against players who have exploited - it seems your opinion is "if you can do it you've got away with it".

As a paying customer, I would like to see some action taken in the following areas:

1) The "no refunds" policy. It is a simple matter to re-evaluate this and impliment methods of allowing refunds of lost items / xp / cash under certain circumstances. A blanket "no refunds" policy is not acceptable, particulaly in cases where the fault is the game code and there is clear evidnece to show this.

2) Testing Procedures. Each patch brings new bugs that, quite simply, should have been discovered in testing and fixed pre-launch. That is the purpose of testing and it is failing badly.

3) Customer Care. The level of customer care at Funcom is of major concern to many of your customers. Many of the problems we, the players, are experiencing would be much less of an issue if you were more forthcoming with information regarding future changes, debugging, and other game issues. Examples include the undead mob bug, the items-going-missing-in-trades bug, the increasingly frequent client crashes, the zone-crashes, the killed-by-a-mob-two-rooms-over bug, and so on. If you told us that you are aware of these issues, that would be a start. If you gave some indication beyond "were working on it" that would be great.

4) I want my XP back. I've shown that I'm not exploiting. There is no reason for me to suffer because your code was not tested to satisfactory standards. Give me my xp back.

The problems with Anarchy Online are rapidly overtaking the benifits of playing. Let me make myself clear - I love this game. Or rather, I love what this game could be. There are many issues and I fully understand that you cannot fix everything all in one go, nor is that what I'm expecting. The changes proposed in the forthcoming 14.0 and 14.2 patches show that you are working hard on the game problems - well done. If you'd just deal with those issues most important to the players first, or explain why not, then I'm sure you'd see a lot less unhappy customers. As an example, you have not explained why you devoted the dev time to the Camelot static dungeon when a large number of game zones are unavailable. If you were trying to improve high-end gameplay you could easily have thrown in hunting areas in the as-yet unopened zones.

Unfortunatly, I'm running out of patience with this product and a company that seems incapable of communicationg with its customers. As a result, I am going to close my account unless steps are taken, by you, to improve the situation. As I've said, I'm not expecting a bug-free, perfect game, but moves in the right direction. Perhaps you could start by addressing the concerns I have expressed in this email.

Regards,


I have posted this to the forums because it is an issue that affects many people. I'd like your feedback on this email, and I'll post any replies I recieve if and when they arrive. Thanks for your time.
