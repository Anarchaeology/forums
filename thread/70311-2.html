Quick numerical analysis of Tarasque spawn times.

Given that under normal circumstances, servers go down most often on Wednesday in the morning for the GMT time zone, and assuming generally are back up by Wednesday in the afternoon for the GMT time zone.

When the servers come back up and the 18h spawns are out and ready to get whacked, it will be early afternoon for the GMT time zone, mid-morning in the EST and CST time zones, and early morning in the PST time zone. Any person who has a job or who attends school full-time in all four of those time zones cannot be on to get the goodies, but it's very safe to assume that the people who can be on, kill the big spawns as fast as they can.

18 hours later the mobs spawn again. This time it will be Thursday mid-morning in the GMT time zone, early morning for EST and CST, and excrutiatingly early for PST. Once again, people who have to sleep, or who have a day job, are kept out.

So then 18 hours later the mobs spawn again, very early Friday morning for GMT, very late on Thursday night for North America. Some people in North America might sacrifice a few hours of sleep to camp their items now... but they still will have to compete with the people who don't need to work.

So out of the first four spawn periods of the 18h mobs following the 'standard server reboot' on Wednesday, people who have day jobs - in all time zones - will realistically be able to make an attempt at one of them.

On the weekend all bets are off, but then, there is more competition on weekends too, by and large.
