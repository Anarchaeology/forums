Neat idea.

I had a similar idea to this, that worked more along the lines of a factory. Basically, I was tired of shift clicking to change rocks and suck into prepared crystals. I came up with the following idea:

A device, that you can put in your apartment to mass produce certain items. It HAS to go in your apartment, nowhere else. This would encourage people to actually USE their flats. It works according to your tradeskill at the current moment. Basically, if you have a backpack full of like 30 Carbonrich Rocks, all ql10 we'll say. You are only level 5 though. Let's say that your friend also gives you a backpack or two of ql10 Rocks. So now you have started to amass this collection of rocks. Bow would it be a pain to shift click each one, neh? So instead, you right click on the machine, and it opens a window like a backpack. Then you proceed to put in whatever you want processed, be it rocks, gems, metal, or monster parts. After it's all loaded, we'll say a max or 60 items, hit the "Process" button, and the magic begins. Suddenly, on the floor, or in a window like a chest whatever, your completed product is presented to you.

Now comes the argument of how to get XP from it. Considering that it would have to work off of your skill at the time you press process, it's kind of tricky. Also, the fact that it is automated and you don't really have to do anything factors in as well. We'll use our numbers from before for the example.

Without machine:
90 Carbonrick Rocks, ql10
4 steps(I think) to create finished crystal
5*ql=xp per rock per step.
5*10=50 xp per rock per step
50xp*4 steps = 200xp per rock
90 rocks * 200xp per rock = 18000 xp

With machine:
90 Carbonrick Rocks, ql10
1 steps using machine to create finished crystal
3*ql=xp per rock per step.
3*10=30 xp per rock per step
30xp*1 steps = 30xp per rock
90 rocks * 30xp per rock = 2700 xp

Yes, I know this isn't a whole lot of XP. But with all the time you can save by using the machine, you could be out leveling!

Just my thoughts
