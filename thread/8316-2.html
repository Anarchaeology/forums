Try to understand what happend, it would take me one hour to explain myself clearly on that point.
Naive behavior would be that one does not understand the current situation and assumes certain things based on the constraint set which they currently interact with, and not on the constraints which will be imposed later. As a character increases in level, and have access to certain nanos, skills, guns and ammo, these items as well change. A mausser for a first level character and a mausser for a 138th level character are very different. There is a much finer scale where differences occur. Heck there may be points in your carrer where you decide not to carry a mausser. Point being, intereactions in the environment change. It is for this reason that assuming that if a mausser is a successful gun at low levels that it must be a successful gun at high levels (and therefore complaints about it are unfounded) is not so wise.

For further example, you know that Nano Skills are tough to raise the higher you raise them. Now as far as an analyisis goes you can only raise them so high, as you have a finite number of points to allocate, and they can only be raised to finite levels. Raising them early on in the game gets you the nano skills sooner, however this means that other skills whcih you could have invested in, will suffer. Now at lower levels, the system is forgiving, and there is a lot of leeway built in so any wrong choices early on are easily coverable. However, as the system becomes less forgiving, and the IP requirements of even your green skills grow, you wind up with a necessity to keep a somewhat balanced character, balanced between abilities, nanos, evades, weapons, and other skills. Failure to maintain that balance leads to a violent realization when you encounter a weakness. For instance, SEB is hard to navigate until you have a 200 in map skill, or learn the map very well - this allows PvPers to take advantage of your confusion at lower levels. Not being able to open chests means that mission loot is not optimized. Low evades mean that mobs crit more often - and even a really good HoT won't save you if the mobs do it that frequently. Low weapons skills will make combat take longer. That may not sound like much, but mobs have a lot of hitpoints (and they used to have unlimited nano too); the effect is that they have more of a chance to crit you, you use more bullets, first aid can be used once - and twice if the battle is scary long - and you probably don't have that many hitpoints left in that case, and the mob has a greater lenght of time to cast a heal spell, root, debuff, slow or otherwise deadly/annoying nano. Combat becomes a bit of a dice throw. One would expect a fixer to be good with dice... they are, just not those dice which are a mission of fatestealers...
Lastly, you can skimp on nanos. Since fixers have a nano scheme which forces them to focus, the temptation is to focus on the weakness - however, by doing so too much - you can drastically hurt your ability to succed in other necessary directions. I know this, I've made my share of focusing too heaviliy on nano mistakes. It is overcomeable, it just takes time and patience.
