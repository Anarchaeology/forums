Yeah - all the big selling items got price nerfed - battlesuits used to sell for loads, now not much at all. Also rare nanos have come down in prices. My little secret
13.6 is the magic word.

with this patch, a series of new nanos made their way into AO (Animas, e.g). Their (sell) value is much higher than of comparable items and all of them are single mission loot! (-> Clicksaver)

So, until FC normalizes their value as well, you will get 'good' cash for them. Not comparable to the old battle suits, but still better than the rest.

Professions (and nanos) ordered by value (DB):
MPs: Creations, Summonings, ...
Crats: Speeches
NTs: Warps, Enfraam's
Fixers: Bootleg, Smuggler Shipment, GA (cough, selling such an instruction disc to a shop might not be the most profitable solution ...)
Engis: Sympathetic and Disruptive auras

Anyway, each piece of ql150+ loot (armor, guns, etc.) yields 25k - 40k+. Two or three bags full of such stuff - one large brown cave mission -> one mil. Just doing a couple of missions will usually provide you with a mil or more (at the appropriate level, of course)
