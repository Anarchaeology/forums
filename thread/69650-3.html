re: general perfect team discussion
ok, this is my first post on this board, but I have been playing for a bit... but i have this tendancy to start new characters all the time, so I don't have any over 50. However, I HAVE played almost every profession at least a little...

In my experience, observation, and calculations, the perfect team consists of...

6 relatively well-built characters played by cool people who know what they're doing and are interested in team-wide goals. The last part sounds like a corporate slogan but it just means that they don't run off if they get bored, share what they find, and stay involved when theres any fighting going on.

It also helps (at lvls under 50 anyway) if you have:

Enforcer who knows how to get and keep agro (incl apropriate equip and nano);
NT with good dam who knows how to lose agro, if necessary;
Doc that doesnt fight and knows when to use team or solo heal (it's just a matter of watching the bars, but a lotta docs don't seem to get it);
3 others who meet the above criterea.
