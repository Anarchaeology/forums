Unanswered questions
The universe hates me. Why do I know this? Because everyday I'm left with unanswered questions.

Like why in the world am I about to try to steal some vessel to get off of Rubi-Ka?

Okay, sure, I know part of the reason. Because someone is trying to kill me, and permanently.

I replay the little message that Young sent me, wondering if there's some detail that I missed.

Replaying voice and text only message received today at 23:41

Configuring to known voice patterns
Colour coding selected
Playing message (please turn off all other audio communications to avoid any confusion)

Colonel Jae-joon (Omni-AF) : ...your pardon sir?
Unknown voice : You heard me, wipe his insurance

Of course, Mr. Unknown just had to be the guy who invited me to join Omni-Tek. Course, what I'd like to know is why the sudden change of heart?

Colonel Jae-joon (Omni-AF) : Mind if I ask why?
Uknown voice : Because I thought he'd be useful, and now I realise he's still a threat. No further questions. He's a clanner, always has been, and I want to make sure that when he dies, he stays dead. Make sure it looks like an accident.
Text message received at 23:41:04 : i trust you're getting this haiiro
as you can see things are not looking good in your favour
get out of this city as soon as possible
Colonel Jae-joon (Omni-AF) : Affirmative sir. I'm on it right...

Message ends. Would you like to play the message again?
Would you like to view any other messages?
Have a nice day.

Bloody cheerful computer.

I rise from my crouching position, and look down at the ground far below me. Yeah, when they built these towers in Rome, they built them pretty high. (Oh sure, state the obvious. I'm going to blame the lack of oxygen up here for that stupid remark)

I give a slight chuckle. Sometimes I kill myself.

I look up at the freighter above me... really far above me, and feel a slight sense of unease.

Why oh why didn't I try to steal a Yalmaha first, and then try to steal a freighter?

I sigh, and raise the grappel gun (sure, it's old techonology, but at times like this, I don't want to depend on some transportation device, especially since someone could've hacked it, ensuring that my atoms get spread across Mort or something.).

I take aim. Very, very careful aim. Target is approxiametly 486.782 meters away, with changing winds, and you know, I'm just going to let the computer figure out the proper trajectory.

If Young and Mr. Unknown are watching me, my scanners are definetly not detecting it. Here's hoping. I only have two shots... hell, make that just one.

I pull the trigger, holding my breath. I see the grappel stream out of my vision, the ultra-thin wire helping me trace its movement...

Good, perfect attachment.

...crap, the freighter is moving away. I desperately attach the retractor to my Omni-Pol armour, wondering if I'm wrong taking this risk, then I push the button, wondering if the crew would notice some odd human shaped form fly up to the hull.

Maybe they'll think it's a bird.

And I'm desperately hoping no one saw me.

"Simple, and elegant, Young. What is he doing now?"
"Well, I've tracked him climbing a commercial tower, and took a risk in assuming he would attempt to hijack a tower. I've already took my Yalm up there and arranged a suprise for him. That, and I have a press release ready for the press."
"Excellent. I'm glad you haven't let me down. Young, this calls for a little celebration."

____________________

The freighter was over Galway Shire now. Looks like my pessimistic side was wrong, and that they weren't tracking me.

When I come back here, I should remember to buy Young a beer, or something.

Then for a split second, I see the hull start to rupture, and then a bright light...

____________________

Omni-Pol inspectors have concluded that the ship was carrying contraband weapons, as the intensity of the explosion could not be attributed to mere equipment failure. The number of crew cannot be determined, as all organic material aboard has been virtually vaporised, however, it was reported that an officer under command of Colonel Jae-joon was inspecting the ship, and that due to unknown circumstances, the officer has failed to reappear at any reclaim terminal. The most likely explanation is that somehow something interfered with the officer's life force, and thus what was aboard the vessel shall remain a mystery. As we speak, Omni-Pol is analysing the miniscule shards that have survived, but their current statement is that they are not holding their breath for any firm results. And now for the weather. Tomorrow, expect..."

The force of the explosion nearly knocked me off the building... didn't know they would be so enthusiastic about killing me... a shard of metal had passed through my shoulder, already being healed by my nanos... slowly though...

I smiled mischieviously... hell, if I didn't suck at sneaking around, I could easily be an agent... or a fixer...

Reloaded the grappel gun... took aim for a different ship...

Here's hoping my death seemed real enough to them...

"Your attention please, we are now about to dock with the station. Please get ensure that all your carry on luggage and pets are in order. Your nearest flight attendant will be happy to assist you if you are experiencing any difficulties whatsoever..."

I slowly stretched my arms, wishing Rubi-Ka wasn't so bloody far away...

How long has it been anyways since I left here? Hopefully long enough...

"Umm... Commander?" said an Omni Trans employee. Atrox. Probably security detail.

"Shiseiji." I had him a document pad, with the imposing insignia of the Omni-Armed Forces displayed on it.

...somehow, the ship I was on was sent to bring supplies to a hot combat zone, somewhere's in one of Omni-Tek's new colonies... apparently the planet was being attacked by some unknown force, and due to it's isolation, and the fact that things were getting a little desperate, any recruit was accepted, no questions asked.

And with the high casualty rate, and the lack of ressurection facilities, I was rapidly promoted to the rank of Commander, despite my inexperience. Funny how things like that happen.

Finally reinforcements arrived, the situation was contained, and they decided to transfer me here, "since due to the Commander's ability to command inexperienced units in suicidal battles, what better place to send him than the planet with one of the highest death rates?"

Well, I knew that I'd have to come back here anyways...

After all, there are some people here that I'd just love to meet.

I was put on the next military shuttle to Omni-1. Due to classified circumstances, Omni-Armed Force officers were a rarity, and with the priveleges of my rank, I actually had a little bit of freedom for an enlisted soldier.

Regretably, as there wasn't really an Omni-AF presence on Rubi-Ka, I wasn't exactly privileged to be as well equipped as Omni-Pol officials of lesser rank.

I stepped out of the doors and into the pouring rain.

Well, here I am...

Now what.

"Now what?" Damn, feel irritated. Overloaded with work, and I swear that...

The computer gives me another reminder that I have a message waiting. I step out of the.. cleansing facility. (It is not a shower, despite what the manufacturers claim. How the hell can you call something that only usues 500ml of water a shower?) and try to find something suitable to wear in the chaotic mess that is my living quaters.

The computer gives a friendly beep. I lift two fingers at it in return, in a not so friendly fashion.

Settling for a black outfit, "Computer, display message." and searching for the bloody insignia, I look up to see a flipping text message. No visuals, no sound... text. Can't they just send a message that pretends it's from a human? At least a cg image would be nice...

...and they want me to go to my office now? I was just there, it's way after hours...

I put on my coat, informing the universe that if it isn't about a clan invasion, I'm going to go on a homicidal rampage killing everyone that looks at me funny.

I storm out of the apartment, waiting impatiently for the next shuttle to come by, mentally trying to kill the speakers playing that exceedingly happy jazzy tune.

...okay, so the jazzy tune isn't that bad. I could even get to like it... maybe...

Bout flipping time a shuttle comes by. "Omni-Trade sector, temporary Omni-AF Headquaters." And the computer acknowledges my request, and the screen changes to the latest weather info.

You'd think I'd be in an office in Omni-HQ, but of course, something had to happen to push all new armed forces officers into some building in trade. Course, I swear I'm the only guy. Hell, I am the only guy. No secretary, no junior officers, just me, and an empty floor, and surplus equipment.

So not only do I appear to be the only Omni-AF soldier on this entire planet, my secondary goal besides saving the world is guarding crates of... stuff.

Classified stuff.

Classified stuff that feel the wrath of my non-existent telepathic fury.

Finally got to the office... (at least I can choose an office with a window so I can see the view. Traffic and more buildings.) feel like some corporate executive in a bankrupt business or something...

Right, let's see what was so important to get me all the way...

Open door, see superior officer, salute.

"Ah, Commander Shiseiji. Good to see you." said the holographic general sitting in my chair.

"General, this is an unexpected suprise."

"I'm sure." He said with a smile. The smile stayed on his face as he rose from the chair and offered it to me. "Have a seat."

I sit. So I have a general in my office... not good.

"And how are things on Rubi-Ka going, Commander?"

My hands fidget for something to do. I ignore them. "Well sir, having just arrived here, all I can really say is that it seems that the tide of war has tilted slightly in our favour. Other than that, you'll have to see someone with more experience..."

The general snapped his fingers with a start, "Almost forgot, I failed to introduce myself. Commander Shiseiji, I happen to be Major-General Perry, the commanding officer of the military operation you were involved in before you came here."

"Pleasure to meet you. I didn't even know we had someone so high up there."

Folding his arms, he turned to look out something. "Well, back then I was a colonel, but that nasty business did result in a lot of promotions all around. You yourself advanced quite rapidly."

...well, what is there to say...

"Well Commander, let us get down to business."

"I take it this isn't about the lack of military resources Omni-Armed Forces has on this planet."

"Sorry Commander, but Rubi-Ka's military budget is currently focused on Omni-Pol, so that the people can feel more secure here. No, actually this is about your personal life."

Okay.... that's definetly something you don't here everyday from AF command...

"I'd just like to remind you that as a commander, you should try to keep any personal problems out of your work, for example, personal vendettas against... oh say, someone who tried to kill you or something."

...

I think my heart just stopped.

He turned to look directly at me. "Commander, I understand that you may be experiencing some difficulties, but let me assure you, that we are doing something about it. Situations like this take time, so I hope you'll have patience."

"Yes sir." ...can he be any more cryptic? Something tells me I should do a sweep for bugs again...

"Other than that, I'm sure you know how to handle yourself. Good luck, Commander, as in these times, I'm sure you'll need it."

With that, his image disappeared.

I lean back in the chair, thinking. Well, this is an interesting development.
