Battle Suits add at least 50% to nano cost and only give ACs that are equal to a set of any other "good" armor.

The only advantage to Battle Suits is you only need 1 suit to get the benefit of a whole suit of armor.

Cons:
1. Can't wear bracers - hence PvP useless
2. Can't wear shoulder Items - Now totally useless
3. Add 50% to Nano cost with a QL 200 Omni-Elite Suit...yeah 50%!
4. Can't "mismatch" armor types to cover the weaker ACs and improve your overall protection.
5. Have a 30 second equip timer so taking it off to buff is a pain in the ***.
6. All battle suits use Agility-100%/Strength- 90%. How about a Str/Stm version? or an Int/Psy version? or an Agil/Sen version?

quote:
Originally posted by Meligant
Battle Suits ... only give ACs that are equal to a set of any other "good" armor. ...
Actually, it's worse than that. The other day, while wearing a complete set of QL78 Flowers Tech, I happened across a QL78 Urban Battle Suit. The UBS had slightly better protection, I think, in one or two ACs. But in the main ACs, the Flowers Tech suit had about 10% more armor ... without anything in the back or shoulder slots.

But yeah, web cloaks are still hard to find (bring back Shopbot!), and a complete set of Primus Decus is nine pieces. Which means that even with ClickSaver, that's nine missions to find, then blitz, sneak, or run. That's a lot of time investment in an armor upgrade.

But considering that the BattleSuit lowers your run speed, lowers your evades, lowers your effective nanopool, slows you down sitting down or standing up like an atrox with arthrititic hips, doesn't show your weapon, doesn't have the skill or attribute buffs you can get from some armor types, and deprives you of all emotes ... the only reason I can think of to wear one is if you're willing to be a gimp in order to look different. Everybody puts one on ... once. Almost nobody keeps them.

Funcom "de-nerfed" them in 14.4, intending to make them usable. They also dropped the store price on them tremendously, making them no longer worth running missions for (thereby making it even harder to afford a Yalmaha), but that's another argument all together. But you still don't see a lot of people running around in them, do you? It's because they're still not worth it.

They do have one gimmick that makes them amusing in rare circumstance: wearing a battle suit is like wearing an adventurer morph in that it keeps you from becoming an inverted pyramid icon in the grid. Once in a while you'll grid, and see some leet the (subjective) size of a person or a battle suit the size of a building clumping around. It's kind of disturbing the first time you see it.
