Well, they say the plug is permanently pulled on the videos. But ... they still owe us a Spring season.

The idea of the Story was to run it like a TV show but using all forms of media. The Web, In-game events, Items and the like that come at Patch time, etc.

Then they were going to lay low and let us play with the aftermath of the 'season' and wonder about the resolution of the 'cliffhangers'.

That first set of animations could have been considered Fall '01 season.

Regardless as to whether or not they are going to continue to present us the next season using CGI video, they owe us the story in some form or another.

I'd be equally as happy with a nice storyboard (edit: 'comic book style' for those who don't know what a storyboard is) that had a new chapter every couple days as I would with text like that which accompanied the Cyborg Dungeon release.

Most of all I'd like the game to settle down to the point where they could do weekly small content patches and provide us with an in-game mini-browser datapad to provide content in-between patches.

In a nutshell, to answer your question, the videos ain't coming back. I'm scared the story might not be coming back either.
