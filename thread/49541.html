Unfortunately, you pretty much have to just keep cycling through the shops. Was a tad easier when you could whompa from Tir to Newland directly, making a Old Athen->Tir->Newland->Borealis loop not entirely too painful. Old Athen is the best area in general for hitting the shops; three basic, three advanced and two superior.

As for implant versus cluster QL, the safe bet is to have a cluster no lower than 87% of the QL of the implant. The actual limit seems to vary just a little; I put a QL167 cluster in a QL200 implant when the lowest I really expected to work was a QL170 cluster (85%). The safest bet is to use clusters no more than 10% lower, this allows for possible QL promotion of the implant -- if a nanoprogrammer's skill is far greater than what the prorgramming requirement is, the QL of the implant can jump. This seems to not happen for QL50 implants and lower; I am not sure at what point it starts happening. It seems to have nothing to do with the quality of the cluster.

You can always use a cluster of higher QL than the implant with no limit. Thus a QL1 implant will readily accept a QL200 cluster, but what a waste it would be.

These are the shops I usually look in when searching for clusters:

Tir
Two superior shops (one south from the west gate, one on the north or west end of the arena)
One advanced shop (that I know of, just north from the west gate)

Newland City
One combined adv/sup shop

Newland Desert (whom-pah from Newland City)
One combined adv/sup shop

Hope (whom-pah from Newland Desert)
One combined adv/sup shop

Borealis
One combined adv/sup shop

Athen (whom-pah from Tir)
Two superior shops
Three advanced shops

West Athen (grid)
One superior shop
One advanced shop

I only look in superior shops since I won't find clusters of high enough ql in advanced shops anymore (I only use ql200 implants now), so there may be more of those around that I haven't mentioned.

If the shops are empty (e.g. no people) I sometimes just step outside and wait 2 minutes before I go back in, at which point the shop inventory has been restocked. If there are people inside it takes a lot longer (I've heard it's 2 hours but never checked for obvious reasons ).
