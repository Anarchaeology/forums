kurtz
Two problems actually. The first, is that if you had the org building next to (or in) an org tower, only those orgs that had towers could have org buildings.

The second follows from the first, and is that if you lose your tower you lose your building. While, as Blue Cat notes, this may encourage defense of towers, most likely it'll also encourage people not to add much value into org buildings, and the original poster's idea would be lost.

Both are problems if you're going to have real org buildings that had things like the org armor bank, general meeting rooms, etc. Also, I think it make more sense to have them in a city.

Rooms in Towers in a interesting addition and could be seen as sort of tactical rooms, but they shouldn't take the place of org builds.
