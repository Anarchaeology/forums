A Simple Yet Effective Alternate OE Fix -- Everyone Wins!
Hi,

I thought up the system below recently. I think it is much better than the current OE fix, and also much better than the current state of the game.

The key benefits are:

* It doesn't make high level equipment useless (unless you're very high level)

* It makes it possible to carry a variety of equipment and switch guns or whatever based on the situation (this is something I hate about the current OE system -- once you put something on, you *never* want to take it off).

* It makes buffs useful.

* It makes debuffs useful.


***********************************************

Here's how it would work for armor and weapons:

* "To Wield" requirements are half of the current wield requirements

* "Optimal" requirements are equal to the current wield requirements

* "Max QL" is equivalent to the current QL

* "Effective QL" is calculated as the lesser of:
-- the Max QL
-- the highest QL of the item that the character could
wield with their current stats

* When you wear/wield the item, the "Effective QL" is what measures your damage, ROF, AC, etc.


Examples:

* I am a level 50 soldier. I find a QL 100 Novaflow.

* In the current system, I would not be able to wield the rifle without extra help -- normally I could only wield a QL 70 Novaflow.

* In the new system, I can definitely wield the QL 100 Novaflow. However, it will only function as a QL 70 based on my current skills.

* I get a buff from a friend, and now I have my skills shoot up to QL 80. Because this is still lower than the QL 100 max of the Novaflow, the rifle now acts as a QL 80 in my hands.

* I get a superbuff from another friend, and now I have the skills to handle a QL 110 rifle. Because this is higher than the max QL 100 on the Novaflow, my effective QL is only 100. If I can find a QL 110 or higher rifle, I can get the full benefits of the weapon.

********************************************

The main drawback here is that I'm not sure how it would work with NF and pets and everything else. NF I think could work a pretty similar way. Not sure about ther other stuff though.

Well, what do you think? Please post your feedback, especially if you like this.
