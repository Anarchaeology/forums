Yes, IP cost remains the same.

But yeah, you cap a skill. There are title caps as well as breed caps. Title caps mean simply that you have a cap for each title (lvl 15, 50, 100, 150, 190, 200) where you have time to raise other skills. I.E.: at around lvl 40-45 you will hit the cap on green skills if you max them, at lvl 50 (next title) you will be able to raise them again.

Breed caps are simply the max of points you can have in your base abilities like strength, stamina, etc. Depending on the color of the skill these stats cap at 400 to 544 (check http://antiguardians.org/breeds.php for details).

Still you can raise these stats with implants and buffs.
