Sean Roach, I'll check on that. I believe we did a clean-up of those organisations a while back, but apparently that one got away or there's still a bug in the org leader system.

Firedrake, we take the arguments for the nerf and see whether they are well founded or not, and compare whatever thing people want nerfed to the original intention of the system.

Then we see whether we look into other solutions to try avoid a nerf, compare the work load and functionality of each solution, and go with the best one, which - unfortunately - can be nerfing.

And considering that only a small minority of players in MMORPGs ever give feedback, we have to do with the information we do get, objective or not.
