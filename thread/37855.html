We, the South Rubi-Ka Society are a dedicated roleplaying guild. All of our members are roleplayers; frankly there would be little point being the the Society if you were not into roleplaying.

As a civilian org our adventures focus not so much on the OT/Clan struggle as on internal OT issues, like drugs and poverty in Omni-Sewers or the morality of professional leet fights. I 'm sure this is not what most people signed up to AO for, but it amuses us and its kept us busy for the last six months.

Anyway you can read more about the Society at our website (link below). We are always happy to welcome new roleplayers in our ranks.
