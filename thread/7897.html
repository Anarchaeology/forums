Don'tcha think this nonsense between Clan and Omni will be settled by late next year at the earliest? It should be obvious that they're going to let Clan and Omni fight for a while until a third party steps in that requires the two sides to battle together for survival of the planet.

FunCom has spoken to the fact they want players to be able to play monsters as early as late this summer. That seems a logical step towards manning the A.I. of alien invaders or 'mutants gone smart' that will be needed to make this possible.

It's been repeated time and again that FunCom simply can't let either Clan or Omni 'win' the game. It's bad enough the loss in subscriptions that 12.6 caused. The fallout from having half your playerbase obviously 'losing' would be the final nail in their financial coffin.

I look forward to whatever plot twist they come up with that will unite Rubi-Ka (however tenuously) against a common opponent. I think I've heard the word 'scum' a little to often for my tastes. It gets old after a while.
