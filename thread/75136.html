Ye old token board nano
Just remembered this one...

A very long time ago (around 12.0 or so) the token board had a built in nano (different depending on nr of tokens) that added an "aura" to the char. If I remember correctly black for Omni and white for clan. That was the only thing it did... oh wait healing 1 also
I think it was removed due to the great strain on the HW at the time.


Well, this would be a great addition with NW (or PvP in general). As it would be very easy to see who is your enemy and who is not.
And with the upgraded/tweaked gfx engine with 14.7, this should be able to handle to increased load.
Well, in worst case make it possible to turn off in settings or something.


So please bring it back!
