Suggestion - automatically upload mission coordinates
A very simple gameplay enhancement would be to have a mission automatically upload its target coordinates to your map when you get it.

The bit where you have to open the missions window, right click & hold, select the upload icon, then get rid of the window... it's tedious. It doesn't add anything to the game. You *need* to do it in order to complete your mission, so there's no point waiting for the user to explicitly run the upload. You know he's going to have to do it anyway, why not just automate it?

This little process adds nothing to the game, and it is confusing for new players (I wasted my first three missions in the city before someone explained the situation to me).
