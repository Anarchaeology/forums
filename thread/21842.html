Armor Bonus
I thought this would be a pretty cool feature to add to AO.

Add AC, xp/kill, nano, hp, or skill bonuses to Armor when a user has eqquipped a full set of one type within a ceartian ql of eachother.

IE Increasing Imp/Proj or Energy AC above the normal increase provided when wearing Omni-pol armor if a User has eqipped a full set: Helmet, 2 sleeves, body armor, pants, and boots that are all between ql 70-80. Or providing health, stamina, and treatment/first aid boosts when wearing a full set of biomech, or organic armor.
It would give players a reason to go hunting for armor of one type, other than just looking stylish, and provide yet another aspect of game play that makes AO such an addicting game.

Feedback always welcome

As it should be
But thats not necessarily a bad thing. There would have to be some sort of a trade off for wearing all one type of armor. Yeah you'd get an extra bonus but you would also be vulnerable to one type of attack. Besides no one will be forcing you to find and equip all one type of armor, but there would be an added incentive to do so. I'd Love to see an armor bonus like +%chance to find +100ql items. or +50% chance to crit. The downside would be vulnerability to chemical or Disease dmg. That way it evens out the game play so that none gets a glaring advantage....other than just looking down right cool. This would be especially fun in pvp. If you're going up agianst someone who has all of one type of armor with a particular bonus, you can equip armor that would counter that and/or weaplons that would exploit that armor's weakness, adding an element of strategy to pvp.
