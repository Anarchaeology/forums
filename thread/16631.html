Looking for a Clan Guild
Hello:

My character Dauhuai is a level 39 Engineer.

As an Engineer I see my role as two fold. My Pet "Whitney" can make me an asset during group play (without Whitney I am pretty much a wimp). I also have the benefit of some excellent trade skills which allow me to make implants, provide some buffs, and eventually make weapons to help equip other players.

My on-line persona is that I am an idealist. I do not believe in the destruction of Omni-Tek but I believe in self rule for the clans and the neutrals. I also believe that all races and classes have their place and should be treated with respect and equality. Being an idealist I tend to freely share what I have gained rather than seeking to exploit others. I believe in spending some time each "day" helping out new players in the Newbie yard with minor buffs, healing, useful equipment, and money. A pittance to me is a major help to a low level (1-5) character. I especially help out new Engineers and Doctors since I have a soft spot for those classes.

I am opposed to blatant overbuffing since I believe it brings imbalance to game play.

The Guild I am looking for would ideally be:

1. Composed of people dedicated to intelligently helping each other out.
2. Have a loose organization relying on personal integrity rather than many strict rules. Although some rules are necessary. The only guild I tried tended to be overly dictatorial and while requesting input from members the leaders never responded to those inputs. I am sure they were overwhelmed but I still left the guild in frustration.
3. Utilize offline e-mail or a common access point in either Tir City or Newland to meet and exchange ideas etc. Or something along this line.
4. Would have a sense of who they are and why they are. I like the RP aspect of the game and look to the guild to help immerse me more in it.
5. I do not mind paying taxes on a percentage basis or if it is low enough that it does not stop my player from purchasing the items he needs or his philanthropic donations to low level characters.

I would be happy to submit to an interview etc.

You can contact me when I play: usually between 5-8 pm PST or you can e-mail me at dpendle93555@yahoo.com
