Small Gameplay Tip (AOMD)
This is a small tip for people who don't know which nanos to watch out for at the mission terminal (assuming you are using AOMD):

Go here and save that page.

Copy all the formulas on that page (I edited out the QLs but I don't think it's necessary) and paste them into the "watch" list in AOMD (use ctrl+v).

This is probably a pretty obvious tip, and I don't know if that nano list is the most complete one around, but I figured I'd post it anyway. Hope it helps someone.

I think that editting out the qls is VERY necessary. AOMD just does a text search as far as I know, and you won't find a nano formula that has, for instance a "[120]" in it's name.

Also, there's no reason to bog down your AOMDv1.4final with a huge list like that. Use Wildmind's AOMD1.5 version http://homepage.ntlworld.com/chris.wallbank/ and you can have the results color coded! That way it's VERY obvious what is rare and where it is! (You may still have to edit the "special.txt" file to put in what you want to highlight, but it's so worth it.)
