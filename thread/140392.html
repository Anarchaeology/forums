Hi there

Currently there is no check box or such that will automatically stack items of same ql.
Sometime they stack, sometimes they dont

For splitting larger stacks, press ctrl+shift and move you mouse to the left or right. There will be a number increasing, when you are satisfied that this is the desired amount you want in your new stack, just release the shift+ctrl combo.
