Adventurer concerns about 14.6
Here's a little time line.

-About a month or so before 14.6: Patk9 vs Earthmonster (190+ enf). Winner: Patk9

-2 days before 14.6: Patk9 vs Spaxz (180ish Trader). Winner: Patk9

-Patch arrives, rematch between Patk9 vs Spaxz. Winner: Spaxz with ease at 50% hp remaining.

-Rematch after patch between Patk9 and Earthmonster. Winner: Earthmonster with 2.5k hp and 2k absorb tank ready to be used.

-Patk9 runs into hermy in 2h0 and dies. Loses Neophyte.


All 3 of those fights after patch cause me to lose pvp points and I blame it on a few things ive heard lots of rumors about. Not taking away from any of the people that killed me they are all friends and respectable skilled pvpers. But before this patch I dont remember anyone being able to beat me with that much ease in 1v1. Possible issues:

1) Melee initiative bug: Ive heard rumor that you do not hit as fast anymore no matter how much initiative. Ive also heard it may be due to agg/def setting bug where it is registered as set in the middle (50%) of bar at all times.

2) Mult Melee bug: I tested using 1 byom sword in arena and i hit 24 times in a span of 1 minute. I also tested 1 sol fire executioner in a span of 1 minute and hit 25 times. With both on I hit for a total of 32 times in 1 minute, shouldnt it be more along the lines of ~49?
My guess is that mult melee isnt working like it should/used to. Making it look like 2h edge is a way better option if your just gonna hit at a set time per minute.

3) Flurry of Blows: Well it lives up to its name now, it blows. The agg/def part was the best part of it IMO, having weapings hit at 1/1 are nice from the init but putting yourself at risk for that extra few hits is important vs key profs like MA and Doc that you have to catch off guard or they wont die at all.


Some kind of explanation to these bugs would be nice to know before i decide to trash my account over a years+ work.

P.S. Dont even get me started on our nano skill problems we had before this came up.
