i'm taking numbers from the theoretical break point of 1 second attack/1 second recharge.

under that scenario, you should get 1 shot every two seconds. 1 sec attack + 1 sec recharge = 2 total attack time.

the way that Cz described, and most people understand how multi wielding works, you get a attack/attack/recharge both at the same time. so for two weapons that are 1/1 you get 2 shots every three seconds. 1 sec attack + 1 sec attack + 1 sec recharge = 3 seconds total recharge.

that's a 33% increase in the rate of fire. but the only way to get 33% increase on damage is using two identical weapons. however, because it is only possible to get a ql160ish weapon on the offhand (maybe 170) of the decent pistols (BBIs, River Seasons XPs, Electrocium V2/V3s) you can never achieve the actual 33% increase.

lets do an example:

ql200
main hand example gun
100-100 (1) damage

using this as an example, with only this pistol i should fire it 30 times in a minute with my damage at 100 points a shot, for a total of 3000 points of damage.

with two guns we'd have

ql200
main hand example gun
100-100 (1) damage

ql160
offhand example gun
80-80 (1) damage

with conditions at the optimum you'd get 40 total shots, 20 from each gun. so, 20 shots x 100 damage = 2000 points from the main hand gun, and 20 shots x 80 damage = 1600 points from the offhand gun. for a total of 3600 points of damage. that's only a 20% increase in damage for a skill that has the is maxed out at level 200, with 200 implants, and advent buffs. even if you could get the offhand weapon up to ql170, it still is pretty crappy considering the enourmous IP sink.
