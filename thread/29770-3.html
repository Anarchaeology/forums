with recharge currently bugged, the amount you spend IP-wise will never go away, even if you have a debuff on that lowers it to the negative bonuses. for example, say you have a 3/3 weapon, and 600 (the max) spent IP-init, but your overall init after scope and debuffs is 0. your weapon will swing at 3/1, since the recharge is unaffected, but your attack rate is based on the total amount.

this will be fixed in 14.6 though so that recharge is affected by bonuses just like attack, so the scope penalty will be even worse, but higher positive amounts will mean more too

multiple melee is only used to equip - it has nothing to do with your attack rating, etc. the change you see in the "stats" menu in your attack rating when you equip a weapon is simple to explain - your previous listed AR is for your fists, and the new, lower one is for your claw. the menu can only display one AR at a time, so it always shows the last equipped weapon - in this case the claw.
