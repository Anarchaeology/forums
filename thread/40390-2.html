There was no where to zone so if you got the heat on you you better have the stuff because no zone was gona save you, no zone hoping, the day when i was there we were 4 clanners, Me, Snraf, Nessecity and Sylense, we killed all the omnis there, almost 20 of them. But that is behond the point, the point is, everyone that was there had fun, there were even two omni atrox that were gray to me that came back two time and died but i bet they had the time of their lifes, the fights were fair, strategies were used and they made a diference, noone escaped death by zoning, only by employing strategies and by having their friends pull the enemies off their back when they were going down. It took us hours, hours of the greatest pvp fun to defeat all the omnis, we basicaly took them down one at a time and it was a very hard fight. I remeber Zerosignal, an omni MA i forget the name he was high lvl at the time i think he quit now, many of the big pvp names of the time were there. Angelicnova was but a litle MP at the time and i bet she had great fun too.

The thing is, after the crisis was over noone ever put their feet there again, there was no porpuse anymore.

FC as got to make a zone where there is something worth controling, something that whoever side controls can feel proud about it. Given that there needs to be at least one staging area for each side, there also is a need for those zones not to be too close to the actual point of interest.

Taking in exemple sabulum, if the interest is the city, no zones should be present in the city, make one zone North with a grid and a bunker where clanners can stage their attack, and one zone South with whompas where omnis can stage their attack. On foot you should never be able to go from the staging area to the interest point in less than 2 minutes, take in consideration a on foot speed of 1800.

By this whoever controls the city and is pushed back, is pushed back to their staging area and there will be no zone hopign inside the city, once the defeated party is pushed back into their staging area the victors will back down to the city which is the point of interest so there will be no fights and zone hopping at the staging areas.

This kind of setting is what we need for large scale batles.
there is:
1) one staging area for each side
2) space for maneuver, scouting and approaching startegies between each staging area and the point of conflict
3) no zone hoping at the point of interest
4) no incentive or point at fighting or zone hoping arround the staging areas since it accomplishes nothign and the point of interest is 2min away.
5) dead people take a litle longer to come back to the conflict, its a bit more realistic.
6) the point of interest is still close enough to zones not to make it too isolated and boring to go to

one more thing you could do to make it interesting is make Banks and outdoor vendors, something cool to be in.

Now all that is missing is someone at FC that will make this happen. there is so much empty room in mort for exemple or in Prepetual wastelands, there is a clan grid in mort and a whompa usable by omnis in Hope(also mort) they are like 4 minutes away from each other, why not make a contruction of some sort, a military facility or an old abandoned fort that looks very very cool so people would want to fight there. Its not dificult at all to make things happen, just need to dream a litle and then do it
