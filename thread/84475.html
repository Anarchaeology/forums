Every fixer should max or nearly max int. Each nanoskill is based 80% on it, and it will save you loads of ip even as an atrox. Before 14.2, I was only upping int enough to raise my nanoskills (kinda like what I do for sense/RI now ). With 14.2 and the full IPR, I maxed my int before raising any of my nanoskills. I was amazed at how much free ip I had to play around with after maxing all my important skills. When your nanoskills start costing 700+ ip a point, every point you gain from intelligence and your NCUHI is a blessing.

Let's see, I'm also focusing on getting all my nanos as soon as I can. Major Suppressor, Blackmarket Perscription and Detailed Medical Claim at 75, etc. If I had to choose (which I did have to do at lower levels, and might have to do in the future) I would always choose HoTs - and body dev, the HoT's sister skill - over everything else. You can't shoot or run if you're dead. Weapon buffs come next, along with roots, snares, and damage buffs because they share the same skills as other buffs. Runbuffs are last, though still a high priority. I haven't put points into runspeed for a long time, and with LGV I have 576 runspeed at 76. Good enough for now until I get more ip to throw around.

Since I am an atrox and my sense and psychic are dark blue, I only put ip into them when I need to. I keep my agility and stamina maxed, and all my implants are agi/sta-based. I raise strength when I need it for armor, though it is at about 85% already. This is of course going to be different for other breeds, but remember that int is very important for nanoskills (especially for fixers and other dark blue nanoskill'd profs), body dev is entirely based on stamina, and it's easy to make implants that use agility.

[edit]
Oops, forgot NCU buffs and GA. Since the reqs on our NCU buffs are so insane and they're bugged anyway, I'm not making any special efforts skill-wise to get them, I'm just uploading them as I get them.

Like Kas said, only put points into MC once you have the GA disc in hand. The other summon nanos are a joke - literally. Summon that ammo, baby!
[/edit]

To find out how much NCUHI adds at a given level, type in the QL in the box on the nanoskills site I posted above. It's +8 to all at QL25.

Int Boost (from the basic general nano shop) is +12 int. You can get them for all 6 stats.

Expertises are also from the basic nano shop and add 20 to almost any skill in the game (dimach, multiwield, and map navigation being the only exceptions I can think of). I personally can't afford to raise my nanoskills high enough to cast the best ones without expertises. It's a pain but you get used to it. It's not like we have a shortage of NCU, even in teams.


The snare-breakers are:

QL10 Scatter Bonds [SI=62, TS=58]
QL21 Scatter Bonds (Other) [SI=119, TS=107]
QL53 Burst Bonds [SI=284, TS=242, Lvl=50]
QL63 Burst Bonds (Other) [SI=347, TS=299, Lvl=50]

and so on... do a search for all fixer nanos added since 14.6 on http://aomainframe.info to find them all (ignore the GA nukes which show up in the list, for some unknown reason).

The root breakers (self-only) are:

QL73 Escape Captivation [PM=409, TS=354, Lvl=50]
QL169 Master Escapologist [PM=871, TS=767, Lvl=165]
