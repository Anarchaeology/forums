A new(?) guild for adventurers (primarily)
I don't know if this has been tried, but I had the idea the other day. I got the idea when I ran to Galway and saw a Galway Ranger. My thought was for adventurers, and anyone else who just felt helpful, to set up a sort of rangers guild for Omni forest, Greater Omni Forest, Lush Fields, pleasant meadows, and foul plains, with the "rangers" of appropriate level patrolling each.

I'm sure everyone has had somebody come along, at one time or another, in their AO lifetime, and help them out in a big way, and wished thet person would have been around later. Well, this is just the up-scaled version of that.

The main problem I have, is that I just don't know enough people, who aren't already involved with guilds, and would be up for something like this. I'd like to know if there are others who would be interested in this, and get some feedback on any other topics involved.
