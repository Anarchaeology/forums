I don't think I know as much as many people on the boards. Both MP boards and other profession boards. I still learn something new from other people almost every day. But I don't think that personal knowledge is the only key. Personal detailed knowledge of the game is good, but if I don't know something I'll just ask people on the boards. Being on the Professionals team isn't a chance for us to fight for our own ideas and opinions... it's a chance to represent all of the opinions in our profession to the best of our ability.

The Professsionals team should be able to collect the many different views from the boards and represent them all. It requires being able to see things from other peoples' perspective and to represent that fairly and honestly to the dev team... even when the general community views are in conflict with your own personal views.

Obviously, no human being can ever do this perfectly. We all have our own opinions and for me, I'll keep expressing my opinion on the MP forum. But in my communication with the Community and Dev teams I'll work hard to present the views of everyone to the best of my ability. When I'm asked a question by FunCom about something specific, I'll post to the MP board and ask people to say what they think. That way, I just become a channel through which everybody has a voice.

Several of the team members have already done this. We've been asked to put together an initial report on the overall status of our professions. The first thing I did was go back through the MP board archives for the last 100 days and write down all of the major issues and solutions that people have come up with. Yes... I re-read every thread. The second thing I did was make a post to the MP board inviting everybody to state their own personal view on what issues we have, what solutions we'd prefer and what their wishlist might be for future change.

Once everybody has had a chance to make their voice heard, I'll take all of these elements and encapsulate them into a single document for the Community, Dev and Design teams. This is how I'll continue to work as a part of the Professionals Team. I'd hope that most agree that it's a good way to go about it.
