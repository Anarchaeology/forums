The Buff Begging Guide to MPs
**This guide is meant to be taken with a grain of salt or notum whichever you prefer**

You've read other guides for being a MP but none really tell you what it's like. In this guide I'm going to go over important tactics and skills to raise in being a MP.

Introduction
======================

Unless you're under lvl 15 or you live in a cave with Osama, people are always asking MPs for buffs. I wrote this guide so the new MP player can know how to do deal with this.

Breed Choices
======================
Hands down the best breed choice for the MP is the opifex. Many will disagree but they've probably never played the class to the point where you get infuses. Sure atroxes have lots of health and nano breeds get the better nanos earlier, but you're trying to run away and hide from other players literally sending you tells asking for buffs when your in an IS raid trying to kill a fanatic before he detonates. Hands down Opifex.

IP Allocation
======================
The most important skills for a MP that is constantly being buff begged is as follows:

Concealment

Max this skill. There's nothing more satisfying than being lvl 150 and hiding just when you see a lvl 58 engie whos raking in money tanking Aztur in the temple come up next to you asking for mochams. The 1k board will help you with this too with +140 to concealment. You will see them walk around confused a lil swearing to themselves that they saw you there.

Run Speed

Max this as well. This will allow you to easily run away from other players who have an uncanny habit of tracking you down.

Swimming

When running from players asking for buffs, you'll never know when you need to swim across a lake to get away from them.

Map Navigation

I recommend upping this to get maps and map upgrades in. The people map upgrade will let you see players coming to ask for buffs before they get to, allowing you to hide and they wont know youre there. I dont recommend getting the sides upgrade because people who are even on the opposite side of the conflict will ask for a buff if they need it. Just run from all of the people you see on the map.

Credits

Definitely max this one. Of course, many people will be confused by asking how do I put IP into credits? Well you'll need to figure it out since FC decided to make the most nano dependent and chronically buff begged class in the game to not get a rudimentary humidity extractor. Sure we get soothing spirits but is it really that fun to buff 8 people with comp inf that drains so much nano and to turn around only to find the perk not available. Since nano rechargers at ql 125 are sooooo cheap and nano coils for SL even cheaper it makes our lives that much easier.

Chat/Macro Options
======================
You should have your shortcut bars taken up with all the buffs you can give out. God knows you'll be using them.

Another good macro to have is one to list nano techs in the area so you can buff beg them for HEs so you dont go bankrupt from using nano rechargers while handing out buffs.

I have the following macros on my shortcut bar:

/macro Engie /list engineer
I use this for a warning ahead of time that will tell me what engineers will ask for buffs. Good recon is the first part of war.

/macro NTs /list nano
I use to hunt down engies and buff beg them for HEs so I can save that lil extra bit of money when giving out buffs.

/macro afk /afk
I use this when I go on mishes (mostly solo) and get buff begs while fighting mobs. Good for when you're on raids or mishes and trying to worry about killing that lvl 228 enforcer mob, you don't have people sending tells like "Hey I died in mish. Can you give me MC TS again?"

MPs Role In Teams
======================

MPs play an important role in teams. They debuff mobs init and damage, heal with their heal pet, mez with their mez pet, nuke and hit with their attack pet and use Soothing Spirits. Well players dont give a crap about that! Hell you'll be kicked first in order to fit a doctor, fixer, enforcer, or bureaucrat in the team (even if theres another in the team!).

Composite buffs make our lives even better. Now people will just get a buff that lasts 4 hours and not bother to keep us in a team to complete high lvl mishes. No more reason to have MPs in teams again!

MPs Role In Shadowland
======================

Well I hear MPs are suppossed to pwn in SL and they do. They are really good at getting pwned while their pets try to find their way through the hills of Scheol to save their master's rear.

MPs can also summon crystals for PocketBosses. Yup, go to a CT, lose all your nano, and summon a crystal, then give it to some other player. It's not like youre needed for any part after that so go watch some South Park episodes in the meantime before they want to summon more PBs. And all MPs have their QFT maxed so its like we could do the rest of the process anyway

Since Jobe was founded by Nanotechs and MPs, it made sense that their new innovative clusters for implants would use psychology. I mean when I think of a nanotech or MPs trade skill, the first thing that comes into my mind is psychology. What would we ever do without it?

The Future of the MP Class
======================
MPs are the most stress free class in the game. When you have 4 people waiting for a buff and you get tells saying youre not fast enough makes your experience online a pleasant one. SL opens up a whole new world of us getting pwned as they say and Tier 1 MP armor looks nice but sucks AC wise. Its incredible that our demon can find mobs 9 rooms away that will agro us and pull back an army of mobs with us in SL dungeons.

Ah the fun never ends

If you have any comments/concerns/complaints about this guide, direct them to Mr. Loren Warr who resides in a Newland City, Rubi-Ka.

A SPECIAL THANKS:

To all the engies who have made carb and tier 1 items for me and to bureaucrats who gimme the xp luv and jobe implants.
I do really love u guys!

You forgot to add:

Nano allocation: Don't worry too much about Time and Space, Matter Creation, Biological Metamorphosis, or Matter Metamorphosis -- Having these skills will only make you want to summon pets, the presence of which might alert others as to your profession. Raise these skills only enough to keep up with what an agent might have at your level, which will lead more credence to your claim that you're in FP, and can't give composite infuses. This strategy will work a lot better if you're an Opifex, rather than a Nanomage. Be sure to always cast buffs at full defense, and never raise nano init, as this will reinforce the idea that you're an agent in the mind of the buffee, and will ensure that you're not asked again by that same person if they can avoid it.

Weapons Skills: The best MP weapon hands down is rifle, since this will make others mistake you for an agent more often, and so not ask you for Composite Infuses -- max rifle and aimed shot as often as possible.

Armor Choice: At lower levels, graft armor, organic armor, or any other Strength/Stamina armor is a good choice -- at higher levels, go for CAS, especially if you can equip tank armor with it, since you're less likely to be mistaken for a MP. Under no circumstances should you equip a neleb's robe.

Useful gear -

If you're an Atrox, go get a QL 1 Dull Support Beam.

If you're not, go get a QL 1 Ill-Treated Techtronica Neural Disruptor.

At QL 1, both weapons are pretty easy to equip. Then grab yourself any variety of STR/STA armor - Chilled Plasteel is a good choice. If you're OT, then try the new quest (Mr. Blake's) for an OT helmet; it looks like an Omni-Pol armor helmet.

Finally, grab yourself a coat of some kind. Doesn't matter what; a robe from the temple is ideal, but even a simply trenchcoat will do.

Presto! Now you look like either a lowbie enforcer or a lowbie soldier. The Disruptor looks exactly like a Division 9 (classic soldier weapon) and few people would look twice at an atrox carrying a beam for their nanoskill buffs.

This, incidently, is why I keep a black trenchcoat and a pair of shades handy, or I'd never be able to get a burger at the Ent BB.
