Newbie Packs
You all know how hard it is to get started- you don't know the details of the game, and to top it all of you have a precious few items and no credits at all. Even with the items the game supplies you with, you're very short on the things you need.
I think that all new characters should be treated to a 'Newbie Pack'. This would just be a Small Backpack with:

A set of low-quality armor
5,000 credits
2 nanos
Omni-Champagne and a wine glass as an arrival gift from Omni-Tek
And more...

Post your thoughts on this and any things you would add to the pack...I'd like to hear.

I don't think a newbie pack is needed or even warranted. No other online game gives you a newbie pack, why should AO? You really don't *need* a set of low quality armor to fight the leets and stuff in the newbie yard. You don't really *need* credits for anything at first. I think most professions already start you out with a nano or two.

When you're ready to leave the newbie yard, you can do missions for all your stuff. The only thing you really need to buy are first aid kits and treatment packs. Looks like that will be part of the newbie package come 14.2. So a new character will be all set.
