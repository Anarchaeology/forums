Social armor
Thank you Funcom for providing a wide assortment of clothing we can use to customize the look of our characters, but ...

What is the deal with "social armor"? Currently, this armor can not be worn with any other clothing. This includes armor, non-armor clothing, sunglasses, and bracers. This is very frustrating. I thought that the intent was that this clothing not be wearable with armor. Ok, that makes sense .. does that mean that this is a bug? Why should I not be able to wear my new 250k swimsuit with a visor and boots .. seems odd. This was working, but now its not.

I'd like to propose the following change: allow social armor to be worn with any combination of clothing, just not armor.

Is there any response to Funcom as to whether this is a recognized bug, or if this is working as intended?

- Szentisha.
