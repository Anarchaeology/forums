Guide to understanding
quote:
Originally posted by Benjacrat
Benjacrat looks a little confused and just decided to ignore Templus.
We shouldn't ignore her. I'm sure Templus has some very valid points to make sometimes. You just have to know how to interpret them....get past her OT upbringings. Then you see what a nice person she is.

So other can understand what I'm saying, here is a little guide I've worked up to help us all get to know Templus better.

When Templus says:
And I care because?
She means:
I can't really understand your point of view that well. Could you enlighten me so that we can both converse about it on equal footing?

When Templus says:
If i send anything to anyone...It would be 20 Omni Pol officers to someones place
She means:
I care about others so much, that I would be willing to take 20 OP officers away from their duties to make sure you are alright and well.

When Templus says:
Thyme I dont have a rubber stamp big enough to reject you
She means:
Thyme, I think your efforts are wonderful, and I can't find a big enough stamp to show that I notice your efforts and applaude you for constantly trying to better yourself.

When Templus says:
Im sorry Vixentrox but I will never lower myself to your level
She means:
Vixentrox, I hold you in such high regard, I could never hold myself in comparison to you. We should both work hard to hold ourselves to a higher level.

When Templus says:
Omni Pol protects
She means:
I love everyone on Rubi-Ka!

Hopefully, this will aid in causing Templus to be better understood by everyone, and may help us all in finding her to be the great person she truly is inside.
