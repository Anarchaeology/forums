Reality check part II: the pets are already rock hard, the Engineer is the squishy component in the equation. I've never died because my pet got killed. When I've died it's been because the pet couldn't keep aggro.

So why do Engineers always go on about Mocham's if the pets are so tough? Because better pets do more damage and therefore hold aggro better.

As for AC, there's something known as the Minimum Damage Rule. What that means is that no matter how high your AC is, it can't reduce an attack to below its minimum damage threshold. Mob minimum damage is very high and Engineers have dark blue body dev. The result is that an Engineer can be easily taken out by minimum damage in missions.

In PvP, technically you could reduce your opponent's attacks to 1pt/time, but players have such high maximum damage that no-one has that much AC.
  • Pet HP doesn't need to be increased. It's plenty high enough as thing stand.
  • Nanoprogramming buffs would be nice, but we don't actually need them at the lower levels. Heresy, I know. At the higher levels a small buff would be useful, but +300? Even the MP's only get +140.
  • Something that actually absorbed damage or transferred a big chunk of it to the bot would be a useful emergency line.
