Apartments really should have the following:

1. Several available floorplans, all prefurnished with the standard furniture.

2. Your apartment should reflect your character's level. A Colonel should have a much more elaborate home than a Militia Man, for instance.

3. Ideally, your apartment should reflect your character's profession as well. A Meta-Physicist should have a small shrine in his/her home, for instance. A soldier should have guns on the walls.

4. A nice view. Perhaps looking out onto the city (a prop version of course), with the occasional vehicle flying by.

5. Access to the Trade Channels, the OOC channel, etc. As it stands now, when you enter your apartment, you're cut off from the world.

6. Pets. A reet in a cage... a leet with a leet litter box... something. They don't have to actually DO anything, just be there.
