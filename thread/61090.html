Ninja at Tara, A NINJA'S TALE
Sliker

So I log'd on when I heard the news. Taras up send tell. I run my lvl 10 trader to Avalon. Walk in, Omni and Clan fighting like crazy. Omni have Tara and are trying to take it down. Clan breaking in and finally wipe Omni out. Zmmon notices me and sends me a tell. I send tells to a couple of Omni, "I'll try and ninja Tara if clan get it." Then I send tells to Clan, "I was gonna try and ninja Tara if Omni got it." Needless to say, I was not there to ninja. I stood way off and never approached or came close to Tara. I might of actually gotten some pvp action, another ninja, and dueled another low lvl.

First off, why Funcom, is it possible for a low lvl to even enter that playfield. There is no reason for a lvl 10 to even be there. There's no need for anybody under lvl 100 to be there. I'm most certainly not the only low lvl that has ever gone down there either. Tara is the main object of the high lvl game.

FC needs to:
A) Add loot messages in vicinity.
B) Make the Tara playfield unaccessable to lvl 99 and below.
C) Finally and not least, add more dungeons. There should be more than Tara. Dozens of people crammed into a lag-fest is no more enjoyable than having the dentist drill out a tooth with no novacaine.

Just a thought.
