To answer the question...
Whatever setting gives you 3 'describe item' missions. These will give you 2 items, and hence have 2 chances of generating a battle-suit. Full bad/Full money works for me with nothing else changed except difficulty. Don't know what the lowest QL suit is, which will obviously affect your chances of getting one.

And yes, give clicksaver a try.
