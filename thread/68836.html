The Age of Our Leaders

Im just curious if its possible. One huge question is of Mr. Ross' existentence. Some will tell you he's just a story, while others believe he is dead. Would Omni-Tek tell us if he died? CAN Mr. Ross die? Would Omni-Tek allow it?

Just curious on your thoughts. We dont see Mr. Ross very often these days, and without it we only have the stories to keep us going. Is our leader still around? Is our leader still supporting us? Will we ever hear, see, and communicate with Mr. Ross again?

Legacy...
The legacy of Mr. Ross lies not only in his flesh and blood but within each step of stone and iron that you cross every day on your way through each Omni City. The state of his insurance is no matter since, in a very real way, Omni-Tek itself is insurance to the vision which has propelled so many people toward the service of the one true owner of Rubi-Ka.

Im sure, that much like all of you and I, that one day Mr. Ross will leave this existance to continue his work in some other-wordly fashion. It then falls on you, your children, and I -in an even greater way than it does now- to continue the work which has molded this place from a harsh land to the hospitable place which we have come to call home.

As you know some of us will never know of any other, and lack the ability, biologically, to even try. In a way I feel that Mr. Ross, through his own passion, has also subjected himself to the same situation which is why I, and many other of my breed, feel closer to Mr. Ross than I do to any other Solitus I see around me. So please understand the passion with which I speak about his current state and his future passing.

In time we will all pass...one legacy after another. A fruitful tree, some would say a poison tree, but one that blooms continually with each passing.
