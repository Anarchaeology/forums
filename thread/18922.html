The DJ Triumverate
Well folks, it looks like the inevitable is going to become something so much better than was originally feared. We're teaming up!

There are 3 current official DJ's for Reet's Retreat:

DJ Elektrica - http://www.live365.com/stations/dark77
Elektrica hosts for the lovers of techno/trance and dj mixes! No defined schedule yet, but look forward to that changing very soon!

DJ Rederon - http://www.rederon.com/reet/reethome.htm (station link on page)
Rederon's station specializes in retropop, club, and alternative music! No defined party schedule yet, but the station is up 24/7!

DJ Tarryk - http://www.geocities.com/karmainq/index.html (station link on page)
Tarryk's station is focused on techno-industrial and metal by request, and live voice-over broadcasts. Defined schedule so far: Mondays and Wednesdays @ 9pm EST until 12 or 1am (GMT: 2am until 5 or 6am). New page and URL for station to be announced.

With this team, we pretty much have all popular musical genres covered! Please note that--at least for now--all DJ parties are held at the Reet's Retreat on RK1 only.

The three of us are currently deliberating over different schedules and house rules for DJ nights, and things are looking up! Keep an eye on this forum (Player Events) for future updates and, sooner rather than later, a DJ Triumverate homepage!

h00t h00t Reetheads!
