The grid is simply a teleportation device. Rather than teleporting you from point A to point B, it has several points within it so you can chose your destination provided you have enough computer literacy skill.

There are three levels within the grid. On the first level are Omni-1 Trade, Tir and Newland. In order to use this first level you must have a computer literacy skill of at least 91. All the exits in the grid have their own computer literacy requirement so just because you are able to get into the grid does not necessarily mean you will be able to exit at the point you want.

To even get to the second level of the grid you must have comp lit skill of at least 101.

I don't know the requirement for getting to the third level.

To access the grid, walk up to a grid device and right click on it. If your computer literacy skill is not high enough to use that particular grid entrance it should tell you.

Gridding is the only safe way to get to some places. But even with the grid you will not be able to get everywhere that you want to go. That's where having a yalmaha (a plane) comes in. Yalms combined with gridding gives you freedom to travel the entire planet. (Well at least travel the zones that are active)

The Grid:

A shortcut on Rubi-Ka. A "hyperspace", if you like.
If you have seen Babylon5, you could easily compare it to their jumpgates. If you have not, I will try to explain further.

The grid is actually poorly described in the user manual, and the beginner's guide.

You use the grid to transport yourself from place to place. To do that, you must "enter the grid". Simply locate a gridterminal, and right-click it. What happens then, is that your body becomes digitalized, shaped as a triangle, and placed n a special "room".

From there, there are many exits.

Example:
You are in Omni-trade-area, right-click the grid-terminal.
You'll get a message "changing area, please wait", and find yourself in a strange "room", kinda bluish with three floors. Then you see some Squares, with nametags on them. Perhaps you see "clon****" and decides to got here.

Then you simply move around as normal (with arrow-keys) to that port. Once you're standing on that square, you will be sent the that place (where it's name-tag say it leads).


BUT, to use the grid, you need to up your comp.lit skill (computer literacy) Some terminals require 75 skillpoints, others 100, 150, 200, 250 400 etc... You will get a message if your complit is too low, and how much it needs to be.

Good luck, and happy hunting!
