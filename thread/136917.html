Creation weapons and pet AR rolled into one (or something like that?)
I just had an idea, (as usual at "work").

Don't know if this has been suggested so here it goes.

We have the creation weapons that everybody is unhappy about, right?
And we have the problem with pets having to low Attack rating at the highend of things, right?

1. Well the creation weapons are based 80% on Time & Space and 20% on 1hb, which in turn will give us quite high attack rating (green skill + mochams). Now, nevermind the low damage on these weapons that is not my point.

Stay with me for a while and I'll get there.

We all know that our "beloved" pets inheret our runspeed upon casting, right?

Now we get to the good stuff.

Why not have our attack pets inheret our Attack rating upon casting?

As I see it this would make the creation weapons desirable to wield, and it would give our pets a decent AR.

Now there would be a slight problem with our attack rating being high even with the lowest creation weapon, so adding some sort of tag to it so it would only apply to the appropriate attack pets would need to be implemented.

Wadda you guys think? To complicated?

This is a very rough outline so any downsides, and suggestions are welcome

Nice ideas coming out.

It has been suggested before that wielding the creations could affect pet performance in some way. I've not seen it include inheriting owner's qualities directly.

We need to be a little careful though. My recent testing suggests strongly that meatballs have better NR than an MP of the same level with maxed NR. Since we also cast pets of considerably higher level than ourselves, inheriting the MPs NR would almost certainly be a negative thing. It would also represent a major change in coding for the pets and nanos I'd guess.

Perhaps having creations that added to the existing stats and qualities of the pets based upon the owner's stats. So if you're wielding your creations then the pet gets buffed for a percentage of your attack rating, a percentage of your nano resist, a percentage of your evades, a percentage of you HP etc.

In this way by wielding the creations you would add to the existing stats based upon your own stats at the time. The hard part would be that as your stats changed while wearing the creation the pet buffs would need to change too.... I suspect that this might be rather challenging from a coding perspective.

Good ideas here though - I'm interested to see where the thread leads.
