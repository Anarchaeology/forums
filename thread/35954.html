Party/Fashion Show Thingy at Reet's!
On the night of July 30th I am holding a FASHION SHOW. Instead of rambling on I will list the important details:

Where: The GPS Lounge/Glass Dance Floor at Reet Retreat (If you need directions, /tell Missmaul ingame)
What: Mainly a fashion show. Just pull out your best clothes and come down to Reet's. Of course, we'll also have a dancing contest, and cash prizes for all!
When: July 30th, 6pm PST (9pm EST)
Why: 'Cuz.
Who: Meeee! Oh, and I need someone to help with judging. (But you don't get to be a contestant or model or whatever)

Here's the deal. If you'd like to sign up to be a contestant, send me an email at kalcat@pacbell.net or a Forum PM with:

1. Your character information (name, level, breed, basically anything that is in your character lookup)
2. A list of the clothes you will be wearing
3. An attached picture of you wearing the clothes (A link to a picture will work as well, if you are using PM)

I will respond with either another email or a Forum PM. If I have not responded in a while, and you used email, it may have gotten lost in my colossal daily spam. Try resending.
If you are a contestant, you will need to meet me AT LEAST 15 (preferably 25) minutes in advance in the Lounge. (Fireplace room with the couches) I will go up to the glass dance floor when the show starts, and call for you to come up individually one by one.

If you are going to be a spectator, please sit in one of the booths on the glass dance floor. Please also leave the tables AND center divider clear.

If you'd like to be a judge, follow the contestant instructions above minus #2 and 3. I will /tell you ingame to talk with you.

Anyway, here's how it works. I will call the girls (or guys, for that matter) into the GDF (Glass Dance Floor) room one by one. They will come over to stand on a table, strike a pose, and/or do a little dance modelling their outfit. I'll say a bit about the outfit, and the next person will come in. As for the dance contest, the other judge(s) and I will go into a back room and discuss who we think was the best.

Cash Prizes:

2nd place: 500,000 credits
3rd place: 100,000 credits

---------------------

3rd place: 100,000 credits

Leet Dance Contest
---------------------------

Total: 4,600,000 credits

Have a great time! I hope you show up!

You need to inform me in advance of the second outfit. I'll have everyone come out and do round one, then if anyone has a second (or maybe third, if enough people want to) outfit, we'll go to round 2.
