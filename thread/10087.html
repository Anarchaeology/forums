At level 76 (I'm now 81), I have one rule at the moment for getting cash in missions (and this is easy to solo):

1) Object to find, or mission reward must be a battle suit. (I've never tried for elite armor, battle suits are easy to get as a mission item.) (Missions: 55%-60% difficulty depending on how hard you can go on a mission, full "bad," full money.)

I do not look at how much cash I am going to get for the reward at all. At my level, rewards are between 20K and 40K. Battlesuits of my level (~ql 80) sell for me at around 110K. Therefore, by taking a battlesuit mission I'm going to receive at LEAST 130K per mission (110K for suit, at least 20K for reward). Anything else I receive in the mission is icing on the cake.

Also, you don't have to kill everything in the mission. You can just run in and look for the mission item, forgoing any chance for a token if you just want the cash... and it goes much quicker if you just go directly for the item and then go to the next mission.

Oh, if you are wondering how long I spend at mission terminals to find three battlesuit missions... it's about 4 minutes. How? Well, if you are doing it the regular way-checking each mission individually with tons of needless scrolling- it would take me probably a half an hour or more... but there is an awesome tool out there that I would encourage everyone to use. It's called AOMD (Anarchy Online Mission Display) and it's the coolest helper utility for AO that I've seen. (For those of you that are concerned, it's NOT an exploit. Funcom approved it.) Unfortunately, it's only for the broadband enabled. Sorry modem users. The rest of you can find it at http://muzzleflash.da.ru/

Cloudeh, at level 81, I can easily make 2 million per 6-8 hour day like this... at your level (and 12 hour a day play schedule) I would imagine that you could possibly quadruple that.

At least make sure you are using AOMD.
