The issue here is that there isn't enough content. That's always been the issue. The mission system was supposed to take care of this problem by providing content-on-demand. The shot themselves in the foot by not embracing the mission concept over the last 18 months or so. The single most hated thing in this game is the handling of things like Nugget/Zuwadza, Smuggler's Den, Mercenaries, The One, etc. Single most hated thing. More flames directed at the game company over this stuff than any other general player concern. The only people who didn't bitch were those who were in the huge orgs. Those people were a tiny fraction of the customer base and continue to be so and most likely will always continue to be so.

When a minority of the playerbase spoils the fun for the majority of the playerbase, something has to snap. In this case, it wasn't a mass revolt, but an adaptation.

Once again, the fault is FunCom's. They abandoned their ACG premise to a large degree. If RaidbotX was over in some playfield battling Ian Warr and SuperguildX was in some other group selector of the same playfield battling Ian Warr, this entire fricken thread would not exist. More to the point, we'd have a happier playerbase overall. There'd be HUNDREDS of fewer posts on this forum regarding how much 'FunCom sucks', or 'camping blows', or 'Superguild S is a pestilance on the game'.

So you ask what the purpose of these raidbots (or 'Hunting Clubs' as I like to refer to them are). The purpose is to kill stuff.

The purpose is not to defend towers. The purpose is not to have a group of friends together in one group. The purpose is not to do missions. They are not roleplaying associations.

They kill stuff and get loot for their members.

When I go to the Catskills to hunt turkey or deer, the purpose is to kill things. I go with a club. We aren't necessarily friends. I don't know the political ideals of the people. Hell, I don't even know half the people. I've never had a single member over to my house. That's not what it's about.

Same goes for my Thursday night bowling league. Or the group I go deep sea fishing with 4 times each summer.

None of these are my 'clan', the people I invite over on Memorial Day to have Coronas and Barbeque and to whom I would entrust my life. Many, if not most of those people don't share most of my interests. Some do, most don't.

Sure, it would be great if every one of my close circle of friends enjoyed catching striper or sea bass. But, they don't. So, should I abandon them because I want to go out on a party boat every other Saturday in the summer?
Amen.

Raidbots were....
.. A good thing.

When they first showed up they were a good means to grab enough people to get a certain NPC or to aid in NW wars.

A while back we had the controversy in Camelot. I didnt like the point system then and I dont like it now. The current system gives too much to those who can spend days upon days participating in Raids. I agree that they should be rewarded for their effort, but I find myself helping at all kinds of raids and not having a chance to get enough points to ever get the item I need.

Dont get me wrong, I dont mind helping people, but the current raidbots have too large a population making it extremely hard for any average or casual player to ever get these items.

I play anywhere from 2 to 6-7 hours a day, maybe more on weekends, which I concider quite a bit, but my time is spread on many alts and I have guild responsibilities which may preclude me from running to a raid every chance I get.
I quickly found Camelot a bore back then, and I am definately growing tired of doing mercs and smug now. Those are the only things for lvl 200's to do that might give them something in return...

Raidbots should primarily be run inside a guild, with a few from the outside helping if the guild does not have the needed personell.

Storm is so damn big they can mount a raid anytime so they really dont need a raidbot, therefore we see a LOT of stormers with full notum kits or other goodies. They dont have to share with the entire faction.

Whats the solution?

I dont know.... Should Mayhem mount a kill team and outdamage a raidbot team at Smug, we will be crucified on the forums. Raidbotters have spawn campers sitting there afk just waiting and they claim supreme rights to the kill....

Is there any other way?

I am not so sure there is. Bots are definately needed in AO, since the chat interface is somewhat lacking and we cannot team more than 6 people together. For now I am willing to participate in the raidbots every once in a while, but at the same time I am more or less resigned to the fact that I wont get the items I need.
