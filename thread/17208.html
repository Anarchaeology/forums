Customized Yalm pics
I have no idea how this happened, but all of the sudden my Yalm had a kick ass new paint job. Somehow or other, the skin for the my yalm got switched with the texture skin for something else (not sure what).

A bug, but the coolest looking bug I've ever seen. And also, a great idea funcom could use to give us customized yalms. Just let us (through a special tool, or shop or whatever) buy new skins for our yalms. It would make the scenery more intersting. Instead of dozens of silver planes everywhere, imagine dozens of individual and customized craft.
