Listen to the music of the fight...
Ok, my thread title is a terrible pun, I couldn't resist it .

I love the music to AO, and I think that the music of a game really adds a lot to the immersive quality of the game world. I know a lot of people turn the music off, but I like to keep it in.

However, I hardly ever hear battle music. This is because I'm a nanomage meta, and like to stay out of battle while my pets kill things, when possible. Since I am not directly fighting most of the time, I don't hear much beyond a burst of fight music when the mob switched aggro (this is often swiftly followed by the death music).

Would it be possible to make the battle music play if a anyone in a person's team is being attacked, or if a person's pet is being attacked? I think it'd make the game feel more exciting, especially in missions and so forth.

Any views on this?
