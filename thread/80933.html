For my twin Lola
William Shakespeare, Sonnet nr 10

For shame deny that thou bear'st love to any,
Who for thyself art so unprovident.
Grant, if thou wilt, thou art belov'd of many,
But that thou none lov'st is most evident;
For thou art so possess'd with murderous hate
That 'gainst thyself thou stick'st not to conspire,
Seeking that beauteous roof to ruinate
Which to repair should be thy chief desire.
O, change thy thought, that I may change my mind:
Shall hate be fairer lodg'd than gentle love?
Be, as thy presence is, gracious and kind,
Or to thyself at least kind-hearted prove:
Make thee another self, for love of me,
That beauty still may live in thine or thee.

Horace's Ars Poetica

I long to outstretch my arm to yours, but i dare not; i shall not.
I will always be your evil doppleganger, haunting you in the wee hours of the night. I am destrado to your libido; I am the alpha to your omega; I am the languid lassitude to your I am the corruption to your brilliant perfection

We share such kindred spirits, but we shall never be, must never be togther. As long as you represent the beauty of the world, i shall become anguish, despair, and chaos. As long as you are the clarity of dawn, i shall be the insidious miasma clouding the setting sun. The harmony of your pleasant existence shall be offset by my wanton destruction.

Why must we be the two roads that diverged in the yellow wood?

Fate perhaps; but it is as inexorable as the rising sun, our journey to the ultimate abyss [for me ] or the heavens above [for you ], the triviality of our meek lives.

But ney, it is none of these!

I have Ghandalf. Oh! how i could just luxuriate in his lap, grasping his viral shoulders and groping his smooth round head. My fingers tingle with ectasy at the mere thought
But it is not enough; i am livid with ardor

I must have Skulldawg! The love of my life, the fire of my loins!
Skulldawg; the name resonates through my body. I am at peace; I am at war. It is the subdued tempest, ready to swell up again.

But you Poetica, oh muse of Rubika, you stand in my way

I yearn for perfection, but you hold me back! You have captured his heart with your grace. As long as you live, he shall never be mine!

Oh, cruel world! Why this insanity, this torture? It's splitting me in two.

I beg of you, depart! Your very sight brings me pain.
Exit now, i can no longer go on.......
