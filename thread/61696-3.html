Mobs with pets. Many of the poeple who are replying here are saying from 1-6 it's nearly impossible to do missions but after that it's easier. Well yea it is, until higher lvl, like 40+.

If I see a light orange mob with a red pet, I work my butt off to try and kill that mob as quick as I possibly can, and HOPE the pet stops attacking. Cause if it doesn't, I die. I've died countless times since the 14.6 came to test do to this.

I won't even mention my soldier. He's quite powerful too, 164 nova 110 IEC combo. Luckely he has TMS, but I have to wait for nanoshutdown every room before I advance when I encounter a room with a pet-casting mob.

The pets hit to hard and to fast compared to the mob. Perhaps at 50% the pet-casters aren't as tough as enforcer mobs at the same level, but at 70% plus, they are much tougher.

Team missions at super-high lvl compared to player lvl is over with 14.6, and doing 75%+ solo missions are impossible, accept perhaps for the 'uber' players. My MA and soldier are good, but I'm no twink. I don't have implants that are 70QL's higher then me, I'm not wearing Carbonum and using a ELLTS. But my test characters are good characters, and powerful ones. But mobs that are slightly above there lvl that cast pets, are impossible to fight and win with any ammount of confidence.

On the good side, outdoor mobs have been 'un-nerfed'. Many of the good old hunting grounds such as GTC, Athen Shire, Longest Road, Aegean and VW, are teaming with mobs now . With the 10% increase in xp, thats a very good thing.

The problem with this is many MANY poeple, get a lot of the stuff they use, weapons and armor, through missioning. I spent all day with my MA trying to get a QL75-80ish (15 lvls higher then me) Flower of Life,cause they work now and they rock. Outdoor hunting is back, and extreme powerleveling through team missions will not work anymore. Over all, I'm happy.

First I'll just say I think pet casting MOBs in the backyard are not a great idea. I read here to concentrate on the MOB not the pet, but a newbie coming in wouldn't know that. If they are full aggro and the pet hits them first they will auto-attack it and it can be difficult to un-attack the pet and then attack the MOB. Also the pets do BIG damage for a newbie (10-12 points). If nothing else, at least make the pet casting MOBs in the backyard non-aggro. I think that would make it easier for newbies to learn how to deal with them.

As for the solo missions (lvl 1-5 or so) I've done so far, they haven't been too bad. I ran into one room with two engies. Though I will also repeat that the pet seems to do a lot of damage for the level dungeon they are in. Worst I saw was a very light orange pet on a yellow MOB, though I understand this gets worse as the levels go up.

Also, they'd be easier to deal with if the special attacks weren't bugged! Someone finally explained not to do a special (brawl, fast, etc) while waiting for the Treatment skill to come back. Otherwise you don't get the special back until you zone, or sometime re-equiping the weapon will get it back. Annoying, but at least I know the work around.

I also think the pet should go away after the MOB dies. They don't do anything, as they appear to only be given guard commands and just stand there after the MOB dies. Though it can be kind of funny. Once in the backyard there were several sad robots just standing around waiting for their master to come back, as I just kept whacking the beuro as soon as he came back.

And as long as I'm wasting bandwidth, why no XP or loot on the pets? I tried whacking one after the master died and got nothing. Not even robot junk. At least give us a little credit for taking one down.

I'm done for now. Overall, I'd say it makes things more interesting, just balance it a bit more in 14.6.0.2, OK? Oh, and thanks for all your hard work on this patch. Probably doesn't get said enough.
