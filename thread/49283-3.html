Byom: 13842

(Note, this only helps the Byom) Chuck in a 166 Flurry (+18% CritChance for half the 30s):

Byom: 19016
Rider: 15002
Scissors: 11835
Daito: 13607

Draw your own conclusions, but bear in mind that the Rider and Byom have AR 900 compared with AR 1040 for the others. So you'll probably not achieve the same damage potential due to misses and parries, and as a result their figures should be lower than they are by some amount.
