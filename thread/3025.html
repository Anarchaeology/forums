Pilker
Leetas
The Grid points scattered across the world each have their individual requirements.

The basic ones are as following: in Tir, by the Happy Rebel inn, 80 Comp Lit. In Newland, 90, and Omni-1 Trade - 75.
When you enter any of these grid points, you arrive at the lower tier in the grid, and can access the two others (if you have the skill) or take the elevator up to the main tier (100 CL req).

The Grid is a digital mean of transport, by encoding yourself and entering.

The requirement for each of the exit points show up when you try to use them, by simply moving close. If you have any other questions regarding the matter, do feel free to ask =)
