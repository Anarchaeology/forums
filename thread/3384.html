Does anyone have the link to the 'I Hate Anarchy Online' website?
Hi!

Some time ago there was a link on this forum that led to an 'I hate Anarchy Online' website, in which over a thousand people signed up to say how much they loathed Anarchy Online. It was fantastic fun to read, and was the reason why I quit my Anarchy Online account. I had no idea how many people felt the same way about those little issues that aggravated me about the game.

I guess some of you may have missed the link. In fact, the forum was taken down and replaced, with all the content 'accidentally lost' shortly after the link was put on the forum. If any of you spent ages writing a post onto this forum and wondered why the whole lot was taken down, now you know.

To give you some idea of what was in this link, I would guess about one thousand people signed up to it. Most of the people were boasting about how they quit their accounts before the end of the month so did not pay any more than the initial game cost, whereas others (myself amongst them) was stupid enough to actually pay for an extra month of play. Damn that was stupid of me, and I felt a real fool when people were queueing up to take the p1ss out of me for it! Best of all, people would compare notes on the sh1t bits of the game, like the way you could shoot through walls, or how ball-numbingly boring the missions were. One guy boasted about how he sold off his high-level character for $300 in an auction to someone who was a newbie, and noted that anyone who had played the game for any length of time would not bother wasting their money on it.

Reading through this list of Anarchy Online haters gave me a perverse sense of pleasure that I deeply wish to experience again. I mean, words cannot begin to describe the hatred with which people hold this game. To put it in perspective, I have seen on television inmates of concentration camps who have learned to forgive their Nazi captors, but not one person on the 'I hate Anarchy Online' website has learned to forgive Funcom for the obscenity that is Anarchy Online. They don't just hate it, they rail against its very existence. There was even talk of getting together to hire a 'cleaner' to kill the head of development. There is simply no amount of hatred anywhere in the software industry that matches the sheer, unadulterated venom that people feel towards AO. And rightly so. I mean, think for a moment how it steals hours of your life with the promise that maybe, just maybe, something interesting might happen just once. How many hours have you spent in those £ucking bare rooms on a mission on your own, pressing the Q key and waiting for an eternity while a battle you have no direct control over wastes hours of your life that you will never be able to get back.

Damn, when I think about it I really wish to share the hatred others feel of this game. I want to share the grief with them. Its that feeling of being part of a community of people who have been through hell and back and lived to come out the other side. There was a true sense of catharsis amongst us, and I am glad to announce I am an Anarchy Online hater. We should rejoice in our freedom from this software monstrosity, and spit and curse those that perpetuate this bastarc! obscenity.

And now for a really positive thought: Star Wars Galaxies will p1ss on Anarchy Online from a great height, and drive these people to bankruptcy. But, oh, then the subject of my ire will have passed away, and I will never feel such loathing and intensity of hatred again. It will be like losing a sacred part of me. But damn, it will feel good, like finally scratching an itch.

I hope the person who invented AO will commit suicide and die a horrible, painful death with infested wounds and lacerations about the face and neck. Oh god, the happiness it would give me to know that the lead programmer was brutally murdered by pouring acid down his windpipe while large jagged instruments were driven forcefully into his scrotum. Oh God, the ecstasy of witnessing the bloody crushing of the publishing manager under the wheels of an 18 wheeled lorry, or perhaps beneath the treads of a tank that roll over his feet first before crushing his abdomen in an agony of cruelty. Yes! yES! YES!!


I believe the I hate Anarchy Online website was connected to the new MMRPG called something like Legends of Camelot, or something vaguely Arthurian. Please, please put up the link if you know the one I am talking about. Alternatively, lets share our hatred of this game together. I so love to loathe it!

Dan
