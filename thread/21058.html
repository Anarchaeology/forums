Crit changes in 14.2
Have a look at this one folks:

Quote from Sensei at www.thepunchingbag.net

[Was actually running out the door when I noticed Cosmik had emailed us with some of the goodies going into 14.2. Specifically ...

Martial Artist: There has been a change to the existing MA critical increase buff line. These are now self-only and can only be executed by martial artists (not agents under false profession). 3 new MA critical increase buffs have been added that can be executed on others, but are lower in power than the existing buffs (this new line does not stack with the older versions)]

End of quote, rest of mail is MA specific.


And a quote from Tekkor at www.agentsector.com

[I just received this email a few minutes ago from Cosmik. It gives a sneak peek at some of the changes coming for agents in 14.2.

[SNIP]

- Agents: added two new lines of self-only critical hit increase buffs - Concentration and Sureshot. Check out the boss monsters in team missions, they might be carrying one.]

End of quote, rest of mail is Agent specific.

So, I guess the crit bandwagon is getting a haulover. Anyone else thinking that 14.2 will be the patch of the century? 8P

Personally, I very much look forward to this patch. My main character is an MA and the crit change will perhaps make us wanted in groups primarily as damage dealers, not as portable crit scopes. It will also halt the "insert-insane-crit-damage-gun-here" thingie that everybody is going for atm. This means diversification, which is good.

I always thought that MA's and Agents would have the best crits since we have fewer HP then Soldiers/Enforcers, and I think it is good to bring back some of the "WHOA!" factor back ito crits. Now people rely completely on them for damage output.

It's like:
"Man, whats that crap damage" (normal hit.)
"Here we go, back to normal" (badass crit.)
I find this wrong. Anyone with a shottie and UVC is king. Boring.

Also, on a second note, MA's have never been able to overequip, and will (still) be far more vunerable to debuffs then other professions. Every change in MA skill = change in QL of fist "weapon". All other professions still have 20% leeway, I cannot see how this is unbalancing. For those wanting to start a "nerf MA" whine, lets just wait and see how this patch comes out. Perhaps MA's will now do about the same damage as you do. And please, remember that MA's are supposed to be the best damage dealers over time, rivaled only by NT's in damage output.

I think this patch looks very good, primarily for general game balance. And, I do hope that some diversification will come out of this one, that will do the game a lot of good.
