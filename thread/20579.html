Interesting Thoughts
Although your ideas would remove what I like most about this game, they are good ideas. They probably would be best suited for a new game, rather than this one. Your ideas take the traditional RPG approach, where your lvl determines how much skill you can have, as well as what gear you can use. Typically RPG's will allow certain gear at certain lvls, and then the dmg the weapon will deal is based on your skill, which is limited by your lvl. This type of system would make buffs an effective way to temporarily increase damage.

I like it, yet what I like about AO's current system, is the fact that there are gaps. If you claim you can get to lvl 50+ without armor or implants, that proves that you don't need to be more than a casual gamer to succeed in AO. It'll just take you a lot longer than someone who takes advantage of the over equipping aspect of the game, which in my opinion, is the most fun and rewarding part of AO. It's why I continue to play this game. After all, the story is almost non-existent, the mobs are repetative, the dungeons/missions look the same, and so on. The ability to "twink" and customize your character to be more successful, is all that this game has left.

There are some problems with twinkers that I would agree on. This includes the players who took advantage of a treatment exploit to create an incredible gap in their super twinked character, versus casual players and reasonable twinkers. This also includes the twinkers that used treatment implant stacking to put in ql 200 implants before the 14.0 patch. Since they will be allowed to keep them, the rest of us will never be able to be as powerful as they are at lvl 135, we'll have to wait until 165 or so. Yes, this gives you something to work for, but I can definitly see myself getting very bored with this game by that time. Unless Funcom gives me something else to do other than twink for fun, I can only see myself playing AO for another month or two.

14.2 is a patch that will make or break the game for me. Since I'm a trader, 14.2 will make playing AO different, but also quite decent. I really don't think it will help the game though, I feel sorry for the people who are all for it. They're trying to make AO something it isn't and never will be. The game is fun and seems complex at first, but after a while it's repetative and very boring. OE at least offers some insurance against crappy game bugs killing you, giving you more confidence in your ability to kill a monster (which you've fought about 1000 times).

Your ideas would make a great RPG in a wonderful world, with an involving story line, more interesting gear, more interesting monsters with AI, better missions/quests, and an overall more enjoyable game work well. But for AO, which is basically a text MUD with not all that flashy graphics, lack of diversity in character and monster types, and a non existent story line.......Overequipping is all that this game features that is any fun.
