IC:

It is late. Well after midnight. Garret Silvestrini sits alone under the stars, in the roofless ruins of an old, small house, way out in rhinoman valley.

Now days out in the field, his armour sticks to his sweaty unwashed body. He is taking a few moments out from 'the search', drinking some coffee and browsing the latest set of reports mailed to him in the field, direct to his wristputer.

After reading the reports and some analysis of them, he is struck at the cold irony of how Valaik & DA could possibly feel outraged at the atrocities Ditzie had suffered, at the hands of these hired thugs. The irony of how only hours earlier Valaik himself had along with Lupoh, Trellame and others committed more-or-less the very same acts of mercenary thuggery to someone who had similarly done them no disservice. Something done simply for business.

He wondered how they could feel so aggrieved that someone would do it to one of their own - yet in the same breath carry out the very same deeds themselves.

He pondered...

* Arisuu hires Lehx and Chadul to 'defile' Ditzie, who do so for nothing more than... money?

* Tommyball and Lillemjau hire Valaik, Lupoh, and Trellame to 'defile' Demenzia, who do so for nothing more than... money?

It seems, he thought, hypocrisy truly knows no bounds.

He wondered how long it would take the five of them to discover the two weak-link individuals in their midst.

How long until Tommyball & Lillemjau realise their attempt to remain anonymous at this plan's heart had failed.

How long it would take four of them to realise the fifth - who did not really have the stomach for what they did, and now wished they had not done any of it.

How long it would take them to work out whom the little squealing DA piggy was, the one who could not come running squealing to SPARTA quick enough, ashamed and sorrowful.

He smirked slightly. There is no honour amongst thieves, he thought.

Pondering revenge he smiled coldly. So for Ditzie - DA think they will have their day? Demenzia is worth a thousand Ditzies. Spartans do not allow allow grievous unprovoked attacks to go unreturned.

He recalled the infamous quote regarding Japan's unprovoked sneak attack on America's Pearl Harbour, Earth 1941. Ah yes:
quote:
"I fear that we have awakened a sleeping giant and instilled in him a terrible resolve."
- Admiral Isoruku Yamamoto
He resolved himself. Regardless if they ever recovered Demenzia or not, nor the state she be in, someone involved would pay for this, and pay with terminal finality.
