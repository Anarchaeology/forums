Revelations of Darkness
I was walking past a loading dock in the nastier side of Omni Entertainment (as if one part could possibly any worse than the rest) when I overheard the following conversation:

Time 11/11/02 02:19:00
Location: Alley in Omni-1 Entertainment District

[Thelonis]: My my my
Thelonis cracks a wicked grin as he rests his hands atop a wooden crate.
[Thelonis]: These are just marvelous my friend, well worth the wait.
Thelonis turned to a tall bulky figure dressed in a cloak and laughed under
his breath.
[Thelonis]: Its a pleasure doing business with your sort, I always enjoy
the company of other killers. Well well, many many things to do today, you
best be off.
Thelonis grins wildly and reaches into his coat.

I then heard a huge round of gun fire like a Burst or Full Auto! Either way, it was heavy! The voice sounded familiar to me, so I ran to Baboons to relate to some of the members of Devil's Advocate what I had found.
