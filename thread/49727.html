Barlau and Stormrazer
The softly incandescent glow of the hallway lamp gently lit the suite of rooms they had rented for the night in Rome Blue. Although they were still on their honeymoon, Kiyoko and Edwin had returned to the city to attend a goodbye party for their dear friend, Tuxx. It was late when the party had broken up, so rather than continue on their trip, they opted to spend a night or two at the Historic Rome-Ritz Hotel. They would continue their travels after a good rest.

The only problem was, she couldn't get comfortable. She tossed and turned for awhile, tired, but unable to relax enough to fall asleep. It was her lower back, she had just the faintest, annoying little ache. She finally gave up, and got out of bed. She didn't want to disturb Edwin's rest - at least one of them should get a decent night's sleep.

She padded quietly across the living room, her steps muffled by the plush carpeting, and opened the door of the mini-fridge. Rummaging around, she located a bottle of water, twisting the cap off of it, and drinking deeply. Wide-awake now, she set the bottle down, and stretched for a moment, looking around in the dim light. Such a charming place, but a bit more posh than she really liked. In fact, the longer she stood there, the more confining the room seemed to get. A walk, that's what she needed. She moved quietly back to the bedroom, pulled on her red satin robe, and picked up her matching mules. Stepping back into the living room, she picked up a pen and piece of paper - part of the old world charm of the place, you could hand-write notes here - and wrote a quick note for Edwin, in case he should awaken and worry. "Edwin", she wrote in a neat hand, "I couldn't sleep, so I went for a walk in Rome Park".

She placed the note on the table, where he couldn't miss it, scooped up the room key (another nice Earth-touch, old-fashioned key-cards) and slipped it into her pocket. Then she opened the door and silently slipped out into the cool night.

Her satin mules made a click-click sound against the brick pavement as she walked through the park. The guards pretended not to stare at a wanderer walking around in her bathrobe, and she pretended not to notice their curious looks.

The trees lining the avenues stood silent, ghostly sentinels, their foliage giving off a sweet scent in the cool air. Looking up, she could see so many stars. She could almost understand how ancient humans were able to see pictures and patterns in them. Stars never ceased to fascinate her. On the planet she had emigrated from, Parthia, centuries of pollution had combined with the natural haze to hide all but the brightest stars from view. Before she had met Edwin, she used to spend her nights in this park, finding quiet little corners, where she could fold her arms behind her head, and go to sleep watching the sky.

She smiled, remembering the first time she'd ever seen him. It had been one of her first visits to Omni-1, and she'd had some time to spare between work assignments. Rompa was deserted, so she had wandered up the street to Baboons. Walking toward the back of the club, he was walking towards her, clad in full combat regalia. She looked up as he was passing her, noticed his dark eyes behind the visor of his helm, examining her. She smiled at him. That was all, just a smile. They never paused, each going their separate way. It was already late, and she left a short time later, hoping to get some sleep before Mission Control contacted her. Almost immediately, her comm beeped, and she answered it. She was startled to hear a deep voice. It asked, "Have we already met someplace, or do you make it a habit, to smile at strangers?" She couldn't help grinning, and answered both questions with a simple one-word response. "No", she laughed. She punched a button on her comm, looking up the caller. Stormrazer. Hmmm. He had a delightful voice, it sent little shivers up her spine. The good sort of shivers. But he never said anything else, the call was terminated. And the comm immediately beeped again, Mission Control this time, with another assignment...

Lt. Edwin "Stormrazer" Greble was utterly bored. Rompa was empty, the AF comm was silent. The only thing that distracted him was keeping his parade armor animated.

Of course, when there's no physical form filling the suit, that could be plenty of work in itself.


It had been several months after Stormrazer had found himself a spook, something which even his fellow AF soldiers didn't quite grasp... But then, he couldn't really explain it himself.

Rompa was empty. No clanners to remind about the Tir accord, not even some psychopath just itching to have their insane logic shattered, and Mission Control was being insufferably silent.

So, he went to Baboons. He wasn't sure why he went, possibly just to kill time... And that's when he saw her.

As he walked into the bar, she was on her way out... Just before they passed, she looked right at him. Not at the armor, but at him. Her eyes, for some reason, had the mischevious look of a friend in disguise, as if she knew him even if he didn't know her.

She smiled at him. No greeting, just a look, and a smile.

He switched on the comm and asked her if she knew him from somewhere, or was just in the habit of smiling at strangers.


She laughed, pleasantly, like music almost... as she said no.

Edwin pondered her for a minute, searching for something to say. Something told him there was more to her, he just couldn't figure out what. Nevertheless, he knew he was at a loss for words, and so he shut off the comm.

He was still thinking about her when Mission Control interrupted his thoughts...

She'd run into Stormrazer once or twice after that first time at Rompa, but he didn't give any indication that he recognized her. He seemed to spend a lot of time chasing clanners and criminals out of Rompa. So she'd shrugged it off, busy with the new organization she'd joined, Omni-Reform.

She wasn't really the organization type. She'd said as much to Blitzwrx, who turned around and immediately invited her again. So she joined, to call his bluff, really. To this day, she still didn't know whether his horror was real or feigned, when she accepted.

In any case, the 'busy' part involved another new member, some trader named Dericad. A real basket-case. He'd lost his wife, and - she never really was certain of the details here - had managed to run afoul of the clans, Omni-Pol, and the AF. The Reform members seemed to think he deserved a second chance, though. And she somehow got appointed his babysitter. Not by choice, but she felt sorry for him...

So... one night, Dericad and his buddy decided to get plastered, dress up in some ladies' lingerie - and pretend to hit on the local law enforcement. Kiyoko got wind of this, and reluctantly headed to Rompa, to try and hustle him out of there. Not that he didn't deserve a trip to the psych ward, or incineration by a giant rifle. When she arrived, she found him in the middle of a huge crowd, dancing on a table. Some people were cheering him on, others were shouting obscenties. She managed to get him off the table, and away from the booth, but then he saw a soldier, and pulled away, stepping close to the man, and running his finger over his armor-clad chest. Then Deric said something provocative, and the soldier reached for his gun...

She managed to step between the two men, and, looking up, was startled to see it was Stormrazer. His eyes were steely, he was furious at Dericad's behavior, but seemed to be keeping his temper admirably in check. Trying to diffuse the situation, she laughed up at him, and said, "oh, come now, if you arrested every man just for acting the fool, why, you'd have half the city in custody". He said something about Dericad being a traitor and a murderer, but his words were lost in all the voices around them. She was struck, though, by the weary note in his voice. Storm sounded like a man who was fast running out of patience.

She laughed again over her shoulder at him, then seized Deric by the arm, half dragging him out of the area. "Are you insane?", she hissed at him. "They only want half an excuse to lock you up and throw away the key. This is *not* helping your cause." Deric mumbled something, and passed out. She caught his full weight, but managed to lower him into a booth. She caught the disapproving stare of Storm, and flashed him a sweet smile. How she knew his expression was disapproving, exactly, she couldn't say, since he was still in full combat gear, including helmet.

A few days later, Dericad joined up with the clanners. Not too long after that, Kiyoko left the Reform folks, figuring she'd made her point to Blitz...

Stormrazer spotted Barlau several times in Rompa after she smiled at him... Always, he almost felt like he should say something, but couldn't think of what, so he just studied her out of the corner of his vision. She was always smiling. Always happy.

In his experience, that was usually just a cover for something darker, but what that could be he couldn't see.

Gradually, he began to focus more on other things. Tonight, he'd just finished discussing the Rubi-Ka political situation with two other employees, when an old annoyance/enemy had seen fit to test his patience once more.

Dericad, or as Edwin had renamed him, 'cad, had apparently gotten drunk. Or at least, that's the only imaginable excuse, and had taken to prancing around in women's clothing, and was once again attempting to get to Stormrazer at any cost to his own pride.

It had started when 'cad and some friend of his had hopped onto the couch, and after some brief insulting banner began choking themselves with rulers for some reason.

Stifling a grumble, Edwin had forced his armor to get up, and walked over the the bar. 'cad had hopped up and followed, putting aside his ruler and switching into a frighteningly more revealing set of female clothing.

He would have gritten his teeth, if he could have, as 'cad began murmuring come-ons and tracing his fingers over the chestplate of Stormrazer's armor. He silently cursed the supression field and brought a hand up to longingly tap the handle of his Div. 9.

Just then, Barlau appeared from nowhere.

He watched her step between himself and 'cad as if protecting him, and then noticed they shared a badge. Omni-Reform.

His heart sank a bit for some reason as he began steeling himself for what he expected to be her defense of 'cad.

She jokingly suggested that perhaps Stormrazer was enjoying 'cad's attention. Edwin stopped himself from replying with some acidic witicism. He didn't know why, but something told him he didn't want to make an enemy of her. Instead, he asked her if she knew she was aiding a murderer and a traitor.

She shrugged the comment off, and said something about how he couldn't well arrest every one acting like an idiot.

He just looked at the two of them disapprovingly...

As if to calm him, she added, that she could see how he was having to work to keep his calm, as she steered 'cad away.

He winced, irritated that he'd allowed any annoyance to show through, as he well knew that was just what 'cad was hoping for.

He said he wasn't annoyed. She replyed that she'd have to find out what annoyed for him was like...

He let that hang in the air a moment, then turned away and left the bar.

But she'd had enough. She made her way to the exit, then realized she needed to use the ladies' room. She was washing her hands, when she heard male voices nearby. Three different men, by the sound of it, but one voice in particular sounded familiar. Looking around, she realized the voices were coming through an air vent.

She paused. It wasn't nice to eavesdrop... but... she knew that voice.... They were discussing the strangest things, too..... supernatural beings, cult religions. With a start, she placed that voice. It was Stormrazer's.

Funny, she didn't figure him as a weirdo fanatic type. Oh well...

She was headed to the exit, but hesitated. Really, she should at least let him know that his conversation was easily overheard in other areas of the building. She walked back into the lounge area.

"I'm sorry to interrupt", she said in a low voice to Storm. "But I think you should know, your conversation can be heard over by the restrooms. I think that's the reason why..." She pointed to the airvent near his table.

"I'm not that worried about it", he said, seeming to shrug it - and her- off. She smiled politely, and turned to walk away, when he added, "Thanks for letting me know, though". He smiled, a genuine smile. She could make it out behind his ever-present helmet.

She smiled back. "Well... I wouldn't want to see you get in trouble with the local law. They seem to be very wary of religious weirdos".. her voice trailed off, as she remembered - this man was a member of the Armed Forces.

Feeling rather silly, she smiled again and quickly made her exit.

Kiyoko was bored.

She had been working almost non-stop for days now, doing reconnaissance work for Omni-Tek. It was the psychiatrist at Mission Control who put a halt to any more assignments, citing that the best employees are the well-rested ones.

She wasn't terribly pleased about this. The work kept her busy, and the pay was good. It filled a void, too.

Work kept her from noticing her loneliness.

But now, thanks to some do-good staff doctor, whom she'd never even met, let alone spoken with, she was stuck with several free days on her hands. Empty days with absolutely nothing to do.

She turned her comm over and over in her hands, at a loss. She didn't know many people on Rubi-Ka. Certainly not anyone she might call and talk to. Halyera, perhaps, but Hal wasn't answering her comm. She was probably asleep. It was quite late, after all. Sleep was eluding Kiyoko at the moment, hence her dilemma.

For some reason, she thought of that AF soldier. Stormrazer. She started to punch in a code about half-a-dozen times, but couldn't quite bring herself to do it. He probably thought she was nuts, given their past encounters.

But damn, she couldn't sleep, she couldn't work, and there was absolutely nobody around to talk to. What other options were there? She couldn't think of any...

Besides, the last few times she'd spoken to him, he'd sounded like somebody who needed a break, too. Maybe she'd just invite him to meet her for a drink. No harm in asking.

So she took a deep breath, keyed in a code, and punched the send button. A moment later, she heard Stormrazer's voice.

"Hi there", she said. "Are you busy?"

The comm's beeping jolted Stormrazer out of a boredom-induced trance. Momentarily startled, the suit he was animating threatened to fall apart around him. Not for the first time, he wondered why he was still in his parade armor instead of the battle suit, which being exoskeletal in design required no effort to hold together.

Shaking himself, Edwin opened the triggered his comm and replied.

"'allo?"

"Hi there, are you busy?"

Stormrazer didn't reply immediately, instead taking a moment to try and figure out why she, that strange, inexplicably perplexing woman from the Bar was contacting him. He failed to find an answer. Still, truth be told he was far from busy. His last mission of the day ended several hours ago successfully, and the AF meeting wasn't for another two hours at the least. He would have enjoyed the lull had it not been for the oppressive silence. He hadn't even found a single Clansman in the city to hassle, and no one he'd seen about he cared to speak with.

Except, perhaps, for her... Now that he knew she was around.

"Not at all. Something I can help you with?" he replied cautiously.

He didn't know why, but he was glad to hear her voice even as he was wary of it. Something about her intrigued him.

Maybe he'd get a chance to find out what soon enough.

Edwin awaited her response almost anxiously...
