In love for this game!
I do have posted a lot of messages on this message board and been an active reader. In more then one year I have been playing my soldier Aimabel on RK2. I have been a member of the same guilt the most of the time, it have switched name and merged several times but to day it is called Arcane Circle. Several of the main cores players are still there, but several have just left some weeks after their main hit level 200. It is sad they do so, but so is life. When one leave, there is always a new that take their place in the sun. My motivation to play AO is the chat function and the competition on getting special items. I was not so a much game player before I started to play AO. The role-playing events are not so very active in RK2. When I read the news on anarchy-online.com, I can see that it is mostly created bye the RK1 players and for them. So the players on RK2 is a little forgotten when it comes to that part of the game. But still this game is for me the best alternative. I love the characters I have managed to create, and I do like the other players I have built relations to. Therefore it is very hard when someone u knows very well leave this game. U looses a role-playing friend and a good teammate.

Therefore I would like to ask the AO officials to pay a little more attention on the issues we the players do discuss on this message board. The focus has been too much on PvP-issue and exploiter-issue. The game it self is unique on many ways. Good graphic, good sound, good structure and a very nice world u can move around and explore. And some players will always try to exploit the game. As in real life it is a world with some exploiters around us. We do need a system that takes care of such behaviour, but we must also have a system that takes care of those who not do any illegal actions in this game. I feel that the main issues (PvP and exploit) U has been focused on have putted a break on the progress on the rest of this game development. Therefore u can have the experience that your character can goes from good to bad from one patch to another. And some professions grow in amount of new characters created after a patch. I would not point on any special profession in game. Just point out for u that the balance is disordered to often and too much, it is hard to keep up the good faith in this game. One can use hours and hours to create a character that u like very well, and suddenly after a patch it is almost screwed up. The time u have used to develop the character is useless, unless u are patient and would wait on the patch that fixes the problem u suddenly experience. One should not remove an advantage a profession got, just give strength to the defence other may need. If one does both  remove the advantage and strength the others defence  then the problems seldom is solved. It is just switched from one group to another. And it becomes an unbalance produced from one day to another.

I am not a game maker. And I am sure the world thanks me for that, other has better skills to do such things. I am just a game user that loves to built relations and virtual image thru a soldier called Aimabel some hour a day. This is my main character that I have carefully developed from zero knowledge on gaming up to a fairly good soldier. I remember my first steps around in AO. I did not understand what NCUs and implants was. I had no good weapons at all. An agent friend called Shirna did know about a soldier called Gmaximo. He had a gun I could get for free. We should meet at the road cross near 2Ok. That was my first Nova Flow. My whole game experience did get a push forward after that. After that I did often get for free some armour and guns from other players like Gmaximo and Tindstar. They felt pity for me I guess. I was a low-level soldier and almost useless and ugly equipped. I did rely look horrible at that moment. During my period before that I had learn to know a higher-level doc called Ioalus. He did teach me to use the mission terminals on a better way. So I could get more credits when I requested missions. He did also teach me to blitz missions. Something the soldier almost could do better then the Fixers at that time. He and his RL uncle Fixader were members of the same guilt. And one day I have received 100k credits from a friend after I had lost all my armour during a server crash. No need to tell u that the petition did not help me much. For that credit I did buy me a Vehicle ground. I did not have the skill to use it. Problem solving was done with asking Fixader, and he managed to make me implants. With those I could travel bye car. A huge step, but stupid IP investment J

Then we were suddenly hunting Medusas in EFP. Wow it was good EXP!! I did go from late level 70 up to almost level 120 on no time. We could level 4-8 times in a day. With my car I could travel the dangerous road from 20k to EFP and the Medusas. Sometime I died during my dangerous travel, and other time I did manage to travel safe. The song of the Medusas was very mysterious for me. And they was hard hitting mob that could finish u at no time. U needed a full team, a good doc and a good enforcer to live long enough to get the sweet ding and extra IP. I did buy so much amo and treatment/nano, so I did not need to go back to 20k on days. I was just hanging around the fountains in EFP to seek a team. It was a good AO experience period. Suddenly a patch did remove that possibility to hunt EXP there anymore. We all felt that it was bad they did so to us. But the players do adjust. So after a short vacuum we start to go single missions in BS. We copied keys and teamed up. It was almost the same productive period as the Medusas. We were back on track. Several patches later we all do the same. Team missions in BS with some boss looting. If we feel for it we also do some special mobs around the AO world.

The political situations have changed in AO during the time I have played there. First we were tolerated bye the Omnis on their land. But suddenly we were in a war against the Omnis. So some hunting grounds was removed for the players from the different side in this conflict. And then the Cyborgs become active as one political (none player) group. Changes in that direction could be good or bad. I am not that much active in that discussion. None political player I am, and have just one goal with this game  to be a good team mate and try to be nice to all I meets around this RK2 world.

The motivation for a game like this is complex. My personal analyse on what that gives the players the motivation to play this game goes like this. New items found or bought that you cant use at the moment. These items are of great importance for youre character further development. Therefore u has to become better so u can raise your skills enough to start using the item. Action is to level more so u one day is good enough to equip it or upload it. When u have used that for a time, and u can see the benefit of using a better similar item, then u start to seek for the better one and u tune in your skills to meet the skills required. U also goes active in to seek for that particular item. Either bye getting it youre self or bye raising enough money to get it from another player. To do so u needs to be in a group (guilt) so u can get a channel for communication. Someone in your guilt finds an item that they dont need and they can give it to u, likewise u can do the same for them. Some items are so rare that it cost extremely much. Like the Fixers GA 1-4 (Grid armour) that cost several million credits to buy it from the lucky player that did loot it bye pure luck. The guilt can help u to get that much credit, so u can be the lucky one that manages to buy it.

I can see that the new way of make the game special for each class/profession is to give special items that requires a special profession or level. It is maybe the way to go. But we must not make this game that u have to be level 180 to use this or that, or that u have to be a soldier to use this or that. But of course like the Kaehlers sleeves that are not the best protection so far we talk about AC in general. It gives u ok AC on 3 types of AC. It is the special extra on AR and FA that it is good. One must be a Clansman and u needs extremely much stamina to wear them. That gives the players a possibility to look special and to use special items. Which make the individual player a special look and benefits. Not everything should be so specific, but that there is something in this game that is so special is good. But the last soldier weapon Div9 is not that good for the soldier and does not give the soldier an advantage of equip one. It is approximated the same as a Nova Flow. Does not give u any special advantage in any particular way. It is just a fairly good soldier weapon. The other new soldier one that comes with the new patch is also not so very special I have been told from people on test server. But I would not make this to a soldier issue this time. Just want to mention it, that not all the specials is so good that they could make up for a long period of neglecting.

My hope is that AO manages to keep people staying in game. Each time I see that someone is leaving AO I do feel it is to bad. But the amount of high-level players that is leaving us now is too high. I hope that AO take notice on the situation, and that they will do something about it. Often I feel they have good intentions on the things they do, but often they not meet the players request. And it becomes wrong timing and bad blood between the FC and the players. The forum is the place where we can write our anger and frustration. But the lack of replays from the FC officials does make the forum to a battlefield. Where u can see FC vs. Players all the time. And when one have not a communication, but instead built defences on each side, then u get such situation as the one I can see at this forum now. A profession bites the other professions. A profession bites at the developers and the FC officials. And they bites back in hash words. But this doesnt solve any problem, which should be the main issue, keep as many players u can in game so long as possible.

Did I post a soldier related problem in to the forum now at this moment? I am sure that several other professions would have answered in hash words at once! They would have told me things like - Nahhh u soldiers are way to good already. No need to fix u at all. Look at us instead. We dont have this or that. and so on. And if Cz or Cosmik did read my post and answered it they would have told me such  We have no plans for the soldier at the moment. But we are looking into it. At this moment we cant tell u anything at all. When u read such stuff u gets angry, and u find the sledgehammer and type angry answers back. No need to tell that those words could be hard and might offence some readers. And then we go in circle. No problem solved and a lot of aggression written down for no reason. If I were a first time reader on this forum, I would have believed that I had to write angry words to be heard. As I can see it, the reason for that is because of the lack of reasonable response from U the FC officials in general and the lack of response back to the players in action when a new patch is released. Several times there is official threads on several issues that is supposed to result in to development of this game. A lot of players come up with a huge amount of ideas. But the final result is not online with the players wishes. We go around and wait and wait on the patch day, and when we finally see it. It have often inflicted in trouble and created a step back for one profession or all players in general. So we must go in to a dialog, instead of going in to combat. I hope that u the U the FC officials make the first move in that direction. U did invite one from each profession on both RK1 and RK2 to make a forum for the future AO. It has not delivered a single result. And several of those u elected have left AO. We the players need to have spokesmen to get this game running and to make it attractive for new players and old players. Each account cancelled is a step back for us all still in game. And as U the AO Officials (I hope), I have a dream to see the shadowland released, and to be a player with a role to fill in it.

Other games are no option for me. But try to make my online time more meaningful and less negative. Hope some does read this message. But I have no high hope on any changes.

Aimabel  Soldier RK2

Once bitten... twice shy!
