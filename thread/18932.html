Invitation to clan and neutral parties interested. [IC]
Dear citizens of Rubi-Ka,

The attacks by both Clans and Omni forces on major cities such as Tir, Omni-Entertainment, Athens, Rome have been growing in frequency and scale. My concern for the well being of the citizens grow as the threat of war looms near. At the moment the Clans retain their freedom and Omni-Tek their notum export. If a war breaks out the potential loss of life can be staggering. There are many third parties watching the planet not includeing Sol Banking and the Dust Bridage that have went into hideing. When we are both weak and weary from our own conflict those parties can swoop in and take everything. In that case, we will both lose.

There are rogue elements on both sides that are slowly narrowing our leader's options to war. If you or your organization's leader do not wish to see Omni-Tek and the Clans go to war the time to act is now. I wish to meet with you to disscuss what courses of action we can take to try and prevent it from takeing place. Mutual annhilation is not the only solution and we must let the people know that.

I would like to meet the interested parties in three days(April 10th) at 23:00 GMT at the Happy Rebel in Tir. I will come alone and unarmed. Trust is something that we must establish if we are to work towards peace and I will entrust my safety to you.(Clans)

Sincearly,
Charles "Kithrak" Houston
President of Red Tape

OOC Note: Neutral/Clan parties are also welcome. Omni-Tek organizations interested can contact me in private and we can arrange for a meeting.
