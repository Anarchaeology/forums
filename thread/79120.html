Solution to my problem
I talked to a GM last night. It turns out that the Clan Leaders channel overrides the tower channels. I don't like leaving the Clan Leaders channel open. I had it muted, and when I muted this channel, I no longer recieved tower attack notifications even though those channels were on. Now I am forced to listen to the clan leaders if I want to know when I have come under attack. I hope they fix this problem soon.
