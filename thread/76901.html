I think that Defending towers at all levels is difficult. Sure, the 150 level towers add a significant difficulty with the 150 PvP border.

Now, taking the Title of this Thread to the next level:

Overall, as an org leader, I am wondering what is most fun for my Org. Is it fun to take land, build towers, and then be chained to that parcel for 6 hours every day? Isn't it almost a relief when someone blows it to hell? At least you won't have to defend it tomorrow, lol.

The only way to defend these towers, it seems, is to have people dedicated to protecting them. Sentries, if you will, that will sound the alarm before the attackers make too much headway. Being a Sentry isn't fun. As a leader, trying to Organize people into a Sentry crew isn't fun. People don't want to do it. Also, people don't want to follow 'orders' and be organized as a defense force. They don't want to drill, they don't want to practice. So, Bases get destroyed.

So, I have a choice as an org leader. I'm leaning towards not trying to build and keep bases. We all know that FC will tweak the balance between Attacking and Defending Bases. Until the time comes that Bases are less tedious to maintain, it is doubtful my org will have a bunch of bases. Heck, we may not be bothered to have any!

So, I disagree that Defending Towers is a new PvP requirement. It is a choice. However, if everyone takes my view, very few bases would be built, and the fun of attacking would also go away. I predict it won't be long before this actually starts to happen: when the novelty of Base building wears off. Then FC will have to do something. FC will have to make it more fun and attractive for people to build bases (so people can destroy them!--which, so far, is the REAL fun of NW).

In the meantime:BEWARE OMNI SCUM! If you have a base, we will destroy it! And, you can't get revenge on us, cause we don't have a base for you to destroy. MUHAMUHAMUHAHAHAHAHA
