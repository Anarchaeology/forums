Mission Generated Outdoor Playfields
How about randomly generated outdoor playfields that were accessible using special Mission Keys?

Is it more difficult to maintain an outdoor playfield vs an indoor playfield (i.e. "dungeon")?

This would add a new spin to missions and an enjoyable option for those who hate being stuck indoors all the time.

I see that Funcom wants to enliven outdoor hunting with new spawn camps, but I really like the idea of outdoor mission playfields with no respawn, so you can clear the whole zone without having to compete with other groups for spawn.

I suppose one of FC's worries would be how to display the playfield to players flying nearby, or overlooking it from a convenient landmark. One solution would be to have a default playfield for players who enter without a mission key, and to re-spawn the playfield in a semi-random manner for those players with a mission key. From the outside, display the default playfield to all observers.

This raises the interesting possibility of having "town" missions rather than strictly "indoor" missions. Say I go to the town of Shifting Sands. I have no mission key for that playfield, so I get the default town, complete with guard, shops etc. Anyone else without a mission key ends up in the same playfield, allowing us to interact as we would in any other town. Building interiors are not different playfields, instead they work like rooms in missions, so entering a building isn't zoning.

Later, I come back to Shifting Sands with a mission key. This time, AO spawns a new version of the town. The street plan is largely the same (although temporary bazaar areas etc. would allow some flexibility even here) but the interiors have been randomly generated so that I get a different mission each time. The mission objective could be anywhere, indoors or out. When I complete the mission and leave, the playfield despawns.

There are a lot of possibilities here. Riots are one which could severely change even the basic street plan, as barricades are thrown up and troop carriers land, blocking off entire streets. Roaming mobs of techwreckers and police for you to deal with/avoid etc. Urban renewal is another - your familiar street plan has been changed as work takes place to redevelop part of the town.

One intriguing question: Are suppression levels a property of the playfield, or are they a property of the character that gets reset when you change playfields or areas within playfields? If the latter case is true, then the possibilities for Shifting Sands just got even more intriguing. It would be possible for the town to assign arrivals to a random playfield, selecting from all those which are currently up. When you arrive with your mission key, it spawns a playfield for you, so you always get your mission to yourself with suppression at 75%. Other character arriving in town may get the default playfield, or they may get one of the active mission playfields with suppression set (for them) to 100%. This would mean that you'd never quite know what state the town would be in on arrival, which would make life interesting, but would also mean that you couldn't interfere with the actual mission. However, in that case the de-activated save points could be a pain.
