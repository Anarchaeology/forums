Agent Wish List...
Ok, to the point. I wanted to create a thread where Agents could post some constructive ideas to help make the profession what it should be, spys, assassins, etc. What we need to do this is some of our own unique nanos/items that are made for these tasks. Some say that we have a"huge benifit" from being able to use other profs nanos.(yes I'm quoting you Cosmic ) This is not true anymore, with 2 exceptions, Adv and MP. Mp for the healing pet, and Adv for the leet form, other than that I just ask for ac and health buffs now, like eveyone else, and save myself a WHOLE lot of time.

This is the kind of thing I am talking about, it came to me the other night and may not be possible, but it's the kind of direction Funcom should take, seeing as FP has been such a dissaster..

A spy cam nano/item that would allow the agent to observe an area from a distance. Make it last an hour, and just allow it to pan around a bit and pick up any "vicinity" chat. Make it undetectable to all but the caster so he/she can see how they placed it, and perhaps only useable within the same zone.

Funcom, please, make use usefull info gatherers, make us undetectable from Pc's(but without attack capability) so we can actually spy. Make the cloaking devices work, so that a PC can't "tab" and find you are standing beside him in the corner of that dark seedy tavern, or that the health gains/losses aren't floating away above your head. Even better... make the cloaking devices agil/sense/concealment items and have them only detected by a character with maxed perception. I don't want to be able to attack from this possition(that would be cool though) all I want to do is observe, find a group of guild mates talking about the planned raid on a PVP area, and get that info to my guild. This would make the Agents what I thought they would be.

Agents, I'ld like to see some more ideas.
