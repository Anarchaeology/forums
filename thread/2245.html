OT Survey Team Ambushed by Clan in 2ho
The Order Of Chaos sent a survey team to 2ho yesterday to inspect the facilities there for possible upgrades. As per Directive-108 from Omni-Tek leadership, this activity is necessary to maintain optimal productivity in work areas.

Upon entering the zone from Omni-Entertainment, clanners attacked the unarmed survey team and several members of the team were killed. OOC leadership was contacted and immediately dispatched a multi-guild squad of shock troops to the area.

When the shock troops arrived, the murderous clanners who participated in the attack sought refuge in Omni-Entertainment. Interestingly enough, the lead clan member who initiated the fight and had fresh omni blood on his hands was offered amnesty by the OT guards in the entertainment district.
