*lol* - I think you're right on the misunderstanding part. I can see the context alot better now under the light of L125+, my apologies if I applied them to L25.

I'll draw through some of the points you mention to back them up, and fill in the gaps that I've been researching the last few nights also.

At the moment, I'm doing research into LLTS's/ELLTS to find the optimum QL's that will give the best crit bonus at the least RI cost. The only value that I have found so far is that a QL195 is the first to give a crit bonus of +15, therefore making the 196, 197, 198, 199 and 200 redundant in crit bonus/RI ratio.

A QL200 Yatamutchy will actually increase your range by 50m, which also makes this redundant, so you can probably head back toward a QL120 for a 30m bonus, plus the 10m from HGB, which will take you to your cap of 40m.

In the instance of a QL200 ELLTS, you would also get a bonus of +45m (not sure what the range bonus is on the QL195, but I'd guess it'll be 44m), which again would take you well over the range maximum of 40m alone.

In the long run, it's fairly easy to take the range to 40m with both a Yata and LLTS. Pre-range nerf, you could have had a Nova with:

20m + 50m +45m = 115m

... which is off the sides of the map. The thought of a HGB with 105m range is mouth watering.

Concealment and Perception, like you state, is something for later. Primarily, I've put a soldier in the initial stages as a damage dealer/taker first, and then nice additions, like you mention, are for the higher levels. In many instances, I'm hoping that a soldier that's reached L100 will have at very least a good working knowledge of all the tricks that are being used against him/her at that point.

As for MM, I've been looking at it the same way as yourself, and I do kind of regret the amount of IP I've spent in this just for low reflects. I'll probably downgrade this, or make notes on it's use at low and high level, asit does have associated problems (i.e. TMS won't cast over higher QL low reflects.

Ace's and Real mean's are going to hurt anything. I've seen top slayers getting dropped by them and it's not pretty. But if you're at the point of taking on Ace's, then you should be within the L125+ bracket, and beginning to feel the "joy" of having an IP pool the size of Cuba. Also, if you're L125 and fighting Ace's, then you should expect to get hurt. Master-class's and Veteran's will still stand toe-to-toe with you at L125.

Yeap, Map is a funny one really. If you're a first time player, then you may want to sacrifice some points to this, just to get a grip of the enhancements available. Once you have a good working knowledge of zones and missions, you can avoid this for some time. My lower soldier didn't have any additional maps until L50 at the earliest, as I've got to know pretty much every zone like the back of my hand (except Belail Forest, which I'm going to shortly for a holiday!)

It's very true that variety is the ****e of life, but for a first time player who reads the guide, I'd like to help them get to the point of being able to make a good decision that will not put them at a disadvantage later. I've a gut feeling that many people reading the guide will be first timers, or on rare occasions will be for alts that want the more technical information that will eventually reside in there.

As for Primus/Secondus/Tertius Decus, you are correct that it doesn't appear in the list, but the cross-reference spreadsheet that I used did have this in, and the document does contain information on this armour also. Either way, Primus is still weaker than Flowers for the same skill set.

You have to admit, soldiers that wear tank armour must be wearing it for a reason, rather than wearing a cloak. And to be brutally honest, tank has the advantage of better looks. If people chose armour for it's protection, then everyone prior to the 13.8 patch would have been wearing Flowers, and every person I've discussed this issue with, has gone for OPE because Flowers is pink. Only at high level did OPE come into it's own, as Duskhunter reported that very high level mobs tend not to use Chem damage as much as mobs at lower levels, so later on OPE did become more viable.

As for TMS MkV with tank on... you actually need a Nano Pool of an equivalent level NT to cast it. That's a frightening thought.

Nano pool refilling should be a habit, in much the same way as a Nova user would reload their gun after each fight. But I do play it safe just in case, as I hate suprises.

The 125+'s with OPE will wear OPE for the reason you mention, plus the reason above. It becomes viable at that point, and the implants at that level will cover the weakness more.

You're right that I've kept them separate. Te first reason being that I'm still looking into PvP in ore detail and the weapons available at this time. Also, I want to keep skills separate, just in case FC decide to bring out new weapons that are of value, but have sngle requirements in these skills. Hopefully soldiers will begin to put 2 and 2 together with these skills at an early stage, given most weapons that are any good.

Fling is a funny one, and very dependant upon the style of play, and the player. And you're right, how do I portray all the ways in which people use fling effectively? - I can see that issue becoming a can-o-worms.

As for true damage output, this is something that I want to look at later. The complexities are great in this area, and a slight difference in a requirement or skill can make a lot of difference. My lower level soldier managed to get into a QL131 HGB at level 52. While in BY, I hit leets for about 2k (with 132 wrangle, RE/AR Mast, RE/AR Expert), yet when I stopped the buffs, that value dropped to 1.3k. Now that I've gone up to L57, and I can self-buff AR Mastery, the damage is upto about 1.55k. Damage is hyper dependant primarily on the attack rating and associated skills.

Got to admit tho, 2k damage at L52 was kinda fun, and would have killed me first shot if I'd hit myself - that does raise the issue of a good guideline tho, in you should crit for your HP. Thoughts?

Blitzing is a who new kettle of fish. I tend to call my guildmates if I need something badly (2 engineers with top QL slayers and self-slayer ability), or ask my agents friends to help me out. I'd also add that a fixer buff can be a great help if you're doing blitz.

As for the 30 damage buff, I'd like to think that at L150+, the 25 previous capped levels will have helped to resolve the problem of IP mis-spenditure.

DoT's are a pain, and given the knack for NPC to _still_ nuke spam, it's worth considering. I think it's like you say, something that we both hope will be address in the mystical soldier patch that every soldier dreams of.

Most of the tactics that can be done in PvP against certain classes has been laid down.

Engies-if you're in 2ho, or near a zone, zone when the pet is on you, and you'll neutralize it, then pummel the engie on the way back out.

Docs-Do more damage then they can heal, you're golden.

Traders-Whoever hits first here usually wins.

Enforcers-Tough suckers... absorb shields, Mongo... one thing you can do is use the surroundings to your advantage ie if you're in MMD, when an enforcer mongos, he draws the auto attack of the guards.

Fixers-Stay in range, normal fixer tactic is root or snare, then back up nibble at you while their HoT recovers damage they've taken.

MAs-Strong heals, but long recharge times. Biggest threat here is dimach, Blessed with Thunder and Shen. If you start off with a ranged advantage, you can kill them before they come close.

NTs-Once again, who ever hits first is usually the winner, but have caution. The NT you're facing may have Nullity, which reflects 100% of what you tried to hit him with in your face. One trick is if you are at a ranged disadvantage is that if you jump while moving forward and are rooted in the air, you'll travel the length of your jump still.

MPs-Target the owner and do more damage then the heal pet. Hard to do 70 and under.

Advents-Can be deadly, but not always. Many use Melee, so if you can get some space, feel free to do so.

Crats-Weird combo of Engie and Nt. Haven't seen too many PvP yet, so I can't comment on them. edit:Fought one with my MA. He had a SA Home defender and didn't summon the pet until he was already in the fight. He rooted and let the pet do the work, but his roots kept breaking and my MA dropped him like a sack of wet cement.

Agent-Aimed shot and roots can be a big headache. TMS baby, TMS. If he's dumb enough to be in your face and expects to out last you, he deserves to lose.

Soldiers-Make damn sure your TMS is up when you're fighting a fellow soldier. These fights can last quite a while, or be over real quick.
