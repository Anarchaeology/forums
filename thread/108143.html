Open Memo to OT Employees:
To:Omni-Tek of Rubi-Ka employees
From: Offices of Red Tape, Omni-Admin

Open Memo: Lack of centralized authority in Omni-Tek

To all the valliant Omni-Tek employees risking their lives daily to ensure the security of the company it has come to our department's attention that there has been a general overall decrease in efficiency and cohesion in the responses of the formentioned employees.

Theories of reasons for this efficiency decline is probablly due to a lack of a strong visible centralized source of authority. In the past, the employees have looked to Phillip Ross and other board members for leadership and support. Since last year when the first wave of new colonists arrived on Rubi-Ka those leaders have remained mostly silent and out of public notice.

We at Red Tape wishes to remind all loyal employees that they have not been forgotten and that their efforts are not in vain. Keep up the good work. Together, our combined efforts will build a stronger future for Omni-Tek.

Sincearly,
Administrator Charles "Kithrak" Houston
