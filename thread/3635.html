Hodaka
Phear Leet
That's the point. They *should* be items you find, not the rewards..

Here are just a couple of ideas:
  • Remove worthless junk as mission rewards
  • Add a new, unlocked container that will contain items such as ammo, clothes, common tradeskill items to the missions
  • Put *good* but random items in locked chests, and make them less frequent
  • Remove all the empty chests
  • Make mission rewards more profession specific, along with good *general* items. You can still get handy items for other classes in chests.. only items that are locked to one profession would be considered "profession specific", so everyone would still have plenty of armor and weapon choices as rewards..
  • Maybe an agent under FP would get missions for the profession he/she is currently under..
  • Make a fixer nano line that allows them to cast it on themselves or others before using the terminals to get missions for other professions (just a weird idea anyway, to find and sell those rare rewards.. they could contract out to find items others have problems finding)


Thus, you find a decent reward, you know that a locked chest is worth fighting for, and you can skip unlocked boxes if you're in a hurry and don't want clothes or ammo or the like..

You also would get random junk or good stuff off of mobs you kill..

btw, if you're clan and want omni-med, befriend an omni while there's peace.. They're dirt cheap in omni stores. I got a full suit from a friend..
