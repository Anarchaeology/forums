I made my money for two characters (an MP and a doc) in two different ways.

One was by nanoprogramming work. Granted, if NP is green to you (or perhaps aqua) then this is a viable option. If not, then it might not be worth the IP.

Even so, a lot of requests for NP work aren't for 700+ NP skill, or 500+ NP skill. The majority of requests I've seen are in the 300-400ish range, perhaps because that's about when QL 90 implants come into play. If you can put your skill into that range (by using IP, buffs, implants, etc) then you'll probably find good business. Seek out the Symbol Helper buff for +20 to your NP, or the Advanced Symbol Manipulation buff for +92 to your skill. Both stack with the store-bought +20 NP buff, for a +40 or +112 bonus. The regular Int buff adds a few points on top of that.

Pharm Tech has already been mentioned. I'd like to chip in that monster parts of around QL 80 require about 200 pharm tech (sorry I don't have the exact numbers). If you can reach that skill level (again, consider buffs and implants for this) then you'll probably be able to convert just about anything that players are coming in from Lush Fields and other popular monster hunting grounds.

One last thing. Yalms are available in a huge spread of quality levels, and the higher ones require more Vehicle Air skill and are insanely expensive. Does it truly matter that for many million credits more, your yalm will go twice as fast? Probably not, because you're just looking for the ability to fly. When the time comes to go buy a yalm, check a lot of stores. Consider asking friends/guildmates to go on a search party for low QL yalms with a "bonus" to the person that finds one you can buy. Watch the OOC channels; sometimes a person will say "Hey! QL 35 yalms in the (_____) store!" and that can be your cue. You might not find a yalm you can afford right away, but patience literally pays off.

Hope this helps. Good luck to everyone in earning a yalm!
