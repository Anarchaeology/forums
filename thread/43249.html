Eclipse is Recruiting!
Eclipse is a tighly knit group of people looking for more people out to just have fun. No greifers, no uber l33t. If self gratification is more important then just plain having fun, this Organization isn't for you!

We are looking for some experienced people. The level range with are looking for right now is about 125ish+, that seems to be where the most active of players are. However it is our policy not to turn down any great and fun players. ^^;;

You can see our roster here:

http://www.anarchy-online.com/org/st...1/name/1949697

If you are interested in a laid back fun org that isn't into heavy RP and just into the fun of the game and laughing in guild chat, message me in game (Obtru) and if i'm not online, i'll get back to you when I sign in! If you don't want to wait, you can contact any Advisor in the above roster!

Happy gaming.

Addition: Currently in order to reduce stagnant low levels from joining and never playing a character again, we are not accepting members below the level of 15. We apologize.
