Innate Weapon Formulas
The innate weapon formulas are nano effects that occur randomly while using a weapon (commonly called "proc'ing"). My soldier Exitialis (now retired) used a MTI G90A3 for a long time. I believe the QL of the weapon affects how often it occurs, but do not take that as absolute. I retired my soldier pretty young (around 35th). The first QL of the weapon never proced, the next upgrade I had did it very rarely, and the next higher upgrade I used did it once or twice a fight.

The MTI G90A3 procs a trader nano that drains 30hps from the target. I it supposed to give a small amount back to you (10pts, iirc) but I believe the heal effect was broken. I never recieved the heal message.
