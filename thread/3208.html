Nanomage Breed - Ideas to improve the breed.
I strongly feel that the nanomage breed is the worst breed in AO. Please read through and see why I say this.

When I started this game, I already new the consequences of picking the nanomage breed and that was poor health. What i didn't realize was that the breed doesn't have any clear advantage to make it a favorable class. Since I am a NT I will base my argument mostly within this profession. Correct me if I am wrong, but from what I know the advantages of being a nanormage are:

- Nano Pool
- Superior nano capabilities
- Intelligence
- Psychic

These are what I found to be best about the nanomage. Ok I will show you why none of these matter with making the breed a favorable breed in any profession.

Nano Pool: Our superior nano pool in PvM doesn't give the nanomage any clear advantage playing as a NT. Our nukes soak about about 200-350 nano points on average per medium nuke. The problem is mobs have an insane amount of health. As a nt/nanomage the following two either happens. We run out of health or we run out of nano pool. If a solitus male were in the same situation the same would happen. You see even with high nano pool it doesn't change the outcome of fighting a mob in AO. I can contest that my nano pool has to at least be recharged a few times before I can drop a mob. In PvP, the problem is we can't resist or evade enough to survive a fight. Roots are easly broken and calm has no effect in PvP. Again nano pool plays no role in PvP. Our health is too low to show its true potential.

Superior Nano Capabilities: In the beginning, maybe the nanomage out classes other breeds in this category. Thanks to pillows, mp buffs, unique items and implants all breeds can cast the same types of nukes at around the same levels. MC is the NTs best friend and that can easly be attained by any breed. In PvP, we can easily be debuffed so it doesn't matter what breed you are your mc will be debuffed either way. So the nanomage doesn't show any clear strengths in this category.

Int and Psy: This is basically used for armor. Nano armor to be exact. To my knowledge Int and Psy doesn't play a role in your execution. It does raise nano skills, but not by much. So I will stick to armor. Nano armor is good overall, but nano cloaks are extremely weak. Other types of coats and tanks have better armor rating. Nanomages might be able to equip better nano armor, but the numbers show that it is not that much of a difference maker. Mobs will still hit you hard and in PvP damage is enough to take you out quick. Having high quality nano armor doesn't make up for the nanomages lack of health.

I have listed all the the best abilities of the nanomage breed. As you can see it isn't enough to make it a favorable class especially with the NT profession. You are better off picking a breed with superior health because this is what a NT needs. It buys the profession time and causes less headaches. Nano resistance is not a factor in battles, because even with maxed skills in this "green" category I am stilled debuffed by any mob. The developers need to look hard into this breed and ask themselves what makes this class any useful? It just causes players headaches and trouble. A nanomage doesn't have any useful aspects to it within the NT profession. The only -noticable- aspect of the nanomage breed is poor health. A nts worst enemy

Here are some ideas on what the developers can do to show
-noticable- benefits with the nanomage breed.

- Superior resistance: nanomages know more about nanos then any other breed. So they should know more about countering it better then any other breed. Make them better at avoiding nanos, less likely to be dotted, less likely to be debuffed, less likely to receive full dmg from dd nanos.

- Consistent execution - Less likey to fumble, less likely to to do minimum dmg and more likely to do more max dmg.

I feel these are a few steps in the right direction to making the nanomage a -useful- breed instead of a gimped breed. Feel free to post what you think would make the nanomage breed a favorable class.

Heh, we get a slightly bigger nanopool then the solitus or opi, about the same HP as the opi, and 20% less HP then a solitus (40% less then an atrox IIRC).

The good part of the nanomage is the skillcost. If you don't plan on trianing strength and agility, or only plan on trianing one, our overall skill cost is less then that of a solitus. I don't have as much HP as my solitus/atrox friends though. My nano skills are a few points higher because of my nautrually higher int (but only a few points). However I can easily afford to max int and psy, as well as max sense and stamina, and put a good deal of poitns into agility. Solitus of my level couldn't do that as easily as I could, and atrox definately couldn't. Opi would be roughly as easy, but since I don't train str it'd be a waste for me, as nanomages get a slightly larger nanopool.

Every breed has it's ups and downs, if you think you picked the wrong one (and I mean really picked the wrong one), reroll. But I've yet to see an unworkable combo.
