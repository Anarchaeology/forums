Tarasque and Camelot castle (RK1)
This is just a post to gauge some feeling over an issue which I think would be beneficial to the server as a whole.

As it is we often have to spend either hours and hours of camping the dungeon or waiting for server restarts to organise and conducts raids on Tarasque and Morgan. Reason being is many people log in and out inside the castle thereby delaying the spawn process.. or just walk in for any number of reasons.

FC have given us all 1 mob which drops loot that all of us want and most of the drops are extremely rare. This means that the majority of people will be lucky to get any one item due to the current rarity of Tarasque.

Also and Im sure lots will agree the pvp fights over Tarasque are about the most fun you can have in this game, even people who never usually PVP can enjoy these team efforts with potential reward of killing the dragon afterwards.

FC have also given us the means to do this much more, that is by allowing the server to respawn every approx 40 minutes providing people stay out of the zone. So giving a lot more people alot more chances at getting a nice item and/or a good fight with the opposing side in the conflict.

I propose that we as a whole, both clan and omni's help us all achieve this by the following means:

Have 2 or 3 representatives from either side.. 2 or 3 clan and 2 or 3 omni's who will check on the dragon and keep hold of timer, informing all those outside when the server has respawned.

Also guild leaders can help by requesting to their guild that members do not enter castle or do not log on or off in the castle during times that people sat outside the castle.

This can surely benefit everyone involved if we can establish a kind of protocol where if anyone sitting around outside then people will be patient and not entering to allow us all to have more fun.

Any thoughts, feelings or objections, after all this is very much open to debate.

FC has never played Asheron's call, I'm sure of it. In fact I bet they don't even know what it is.

AO's quest system is a tired old relic of the EQ days. Ultra hard mobs, day long respawns, and a 10% chance of dropping anything, are not fun. What they are is flawed attempts at keeping these rare items from getting very common -- Wrong approach.


The quest system in AC was thus:


{1} Unique items in AC respawned anywhere from 10 seconds to 1 minute. If it was a unique mob, it would spawn every 5-10 minutes.

This ensured that everyone in the group got what they came for, no waiting, no fighting, no camping. They were then out of there and the next group could enjoy the dungeon by themselfs.


{2} All unique items had a personal quest timer on them to stop players from farming them, or picking up more than one. Depending on the item, weither or not it was droppable, and it's value on the market: The personal quest timer could last anywhere from 1 day, to 4 months.

This simple system ensures that no one can repeatedly camp unique stuff and ebay it, or stock up for their guild.


{3} Last, but most importantly; Quest items were never the best you could get. They were nice, and definately worth you time, but the ultra rare uber loot dropped randomly off critters.

Thus, there was no need to limit these quest items because they were not the absolute best, they were just unique.

Quests and Dungeons
The quests should work like normal missions to an extent. You should need to talk to an NPC (and maybe even do a sub-quest for them) to get the quest mission. This would give you a key to a unique instance of the dungeon. This key could be duplicated and given to others, who could then enter that same instance. The quest would have a time limit, just like a normal mission.

The special items in the mission would all be unique and nodrop, and should be the sort of things that you'd happily use, but not overpowered. They might have unusual attributes and powers, but nothing too uber. Each character who completes a quest gets given a special item in a hidden inventory. This simply acts as a flag that they have completed that quest and cannot get it again. This only affects the person who got the quest originally, not any others who just had a key. And just like normal missions, there should be a reward, which should also be a unique, nodrop item. This can be a bit more powerful than the uniques that are just special loot.

The boss mobs in these quests should have a chance to drop stuff off a special 'really rare' loot table (but all such boss mobs should share the same table) aswell as standard boss loot and any uniques specific to that boss.

For a dungeon like Camelot, things aren't as easy. You'd need to ensure there was only a finite number (eg 3) of Camelot Dungeons, and that keys would refer to one of this pool of running instances. These running instances should respawn fully every hour or so, but only respawn mobs that are currently dead.

Most of this can be done without new code, only the hidden inventory would need much work, and even that shouldn't be a major job.
