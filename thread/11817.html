COSMIK: Adventurer mobility in elements
As what the news say about the fixer, he should have great courrier abilities and be very good at it. Then if they beat us at run speed (which I wouldn't bother - we still would be good at it), we should beat them at 'mobility'. You (FC) should emphasize that difference by doing this:
_Create swimming gear requiring swimming skill to go INTO the water. The adv's would be the best at going within the depths of the aquatic elements, finding very special things, meeting odd creations of the nature and maybe some sort of atlantis population; some 'underwater' cities? Also, you should create some climbing difficulty on different surfaces. Even if some ppl could land on a tree or something, at the very moment they do that, they need to make a climb check (adventure skill). The higher you go, the higher the difficulty gets. See the point? You would see some people climb and not go any further because they didn't managed to or "botched", thus fall. That way you could create also some natural climbing sites with secret entrances only people would reach depending on their adventure skill. This would definitely give us our own edge in traveling abilities. Oh and for the swimming ability, you could stay longer into the water holding your breath depending on your stamina and psychic scores; swimming skill would add to that factor.
