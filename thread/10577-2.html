Shoulder Pads
I kept meaning to mention that it is also possible to fill in gaps in your armor coverage by equipping shoulder pads. Shoulder pads provide roughly half as much armor as a matching set of gloves provides against melee, projectile, etc: that is to say, at QL200, +125 per shoulder. However, the most common shoulder pads provide armor against only one armor class:

Calluses (Melee), Deflector (Projectile), Energy Container (Energy), Neutralizer (Chemical), Defrost (Cold) Coolant (Fire), Detox (Poison), Radiation Reduction (Radiation).

Shoulder pads require Stamina to equip. They are not found in stores. They are frequently offered as the "item to find" in find item missions, at all QLs; another advantage of 100% bad, 100% money mission booth camping.

Well, those of you who keep up Parry, might also want to look into deflect shields. I recall seeing in stores only physical shields (melee/ projectile), but you can find shields for other damage types in missions. They fit in utility slots, and they raise respective AC types by quite a bit for about 30 seconds, locking parry for around 5 min. Say, you know that your chem AC is not too good - obtain a chem deflect shield. When you go against a tough chem-mob, activate the shield. These 30 seconds of reduced damage wight be all the difference between life and death.

Basics Still Good, Minor Tuneup Wanted
Basically, almost nothing important changed. The same basic armor types are still in the game, and they still have the same requirements, same availability (other than an odd bug that makes Nadir sleeves available at all QLs), same protection.

14.2 and 14.4 added many things. What they all have in common is that they are only available in team missions, and most of them only available as boss loot. This makes them all but impossible to count on being able to get when you need to upgrade them. Maybe if Shopbot came back, but as it stands, depending on them is tricky.

Web Cloaks: Invisible back-slot armor; the armor (or clothes) underneath show normally. Protection roughly equivalent to a Primus Decus coat. Available in str/stam, agil/str, sens/agil, int/psy, and psy/stam flavors.

Intelligent Shoulderpads: Add-ons to the Titan Plasteel, Kevlar, Obtru, Nano, and Energized armor sets. Provide armor about equivalent to a glove. Protection and requirements match their respective armor sets. Can be worn in both shoulder slots. No change to your character's appearance.

Organic Combat Suits: Martial artist only back slot armor. Looks like the Martial Artist suit in red, light blue, turquoise, or black. Comes in agil/stam (!), agil/sens, str/stam, and psy/stam flavors, each of which buffs different abilities.

Padded Love Armor: Fixer only armor suit. Quite possibly the best looking armor in the game, black with tasteful dark gray detailing. No gloves, no helmet. Based only on Stamina, which ticks off most fixers. Nice built-in buffs. 100% fire/cold, 75% melee/projectile, 63% energy/chemical, 33% poison/radiation - but that understates it a bit, because for only five pieces, it offers pretty heavy protection. Very rare.

Name Brand Mid-Level Armor: Nova Dillon is Sid's Leather Armor with ability buffs (which makes it all but unusable). Sekutek Chilled Plasteel is Chilled Plasteel with ability buffs (which makes it totally worthless). Kirch Kevlar is Kevlar with ability buffs. Nomad Jubilee is Nomad armor with ability buffs.

Servants of Eight: Looks like Primus Decus armor, and is also based on Agil/Str - but also has a level requirement, about 90% of the QL. 100% melee/projectile/energy, 92% fire/cold/poison, 66% chemical, 28% radiation. Each piece buffs about three or four abilities, mostly ones used by martial artists. Unlike Primus Decus, there is no coat.

Head Skinchips: A variety of head-slot armor, with odd combinations of requirements. They replace the helmet, typically raise only one or two armor classes, and typically provide one or two skill buffs or tiny reflect shields. Seem to have been included to give people who don't want to wear helmets a thin skein of an excuse. Note: I have heard it reported, but not confirmed, that these can be worn with Grid Armor.
