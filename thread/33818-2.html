Kuro
Am I here yet?
On some things, most notable the Fixer NCU buffs, yes. Some restriction is necessary to prevent a level 1 player from receiving +195 NCU. Complit buffs were restricted to self only pretty much to prevent overequipping through skill buffs.

But for other things, a level restriction is the absolute worst way to prevent people from using an item. It's too absolute a restriction in games that rarely deserve absolutes. Level restrictions deny rewarding good character development or sacrifice. Level restrictions devalues classes that can enhance skills through buffing, which in turn devalues team gameplay.

If it were desirable to try to ensure most players couldn't use an item before a certain level, there are better ways to discourage attempting their use before that level. For nanoformulas, you could simply take the absolute best possible nanoskills that a nanobreed character of that profession could achieve with implants and set the requirements a little above that. If you don't want people buffing others into using those items, include skills that can't be buffed into the requirements. Add an Adventuring requirement to the Adventurer nanos and now they can't be buffed into those nanos. Add a complit requirement to the Fixer NCU buff nanos and then they can't be buffed into the nanoformulas.

Do anything you can to avoid reminding people they have a stat called 'Level'. The incentive should never be for the players to reach a certain level in a game setup like AO. The incentive should always be for the players to reach attain a certain skill. They'll subconsciously be striving for that level you desire while consciously striving to meet the skill you set.
