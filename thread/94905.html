Sad But True/ *Ephael Prattle*
Ephael awoke with a sudden start. He apartment was silent, too silent, and very dark. The familiar hum of her computer terminal, the various appliances, all missing. She sat up slowly listening to her own soft breath. Lightning crashed outside breifly illuminating the small apartment.

She gasped in terror. She was not alone. She frantically pawed across her nightstand seeking her nightvision goggles and a heavy pistol. In her haste she inadvertently swiped them off the stand. She heard them both scurry across the stand top and clatter to the floor.

She tried to speak to her intruder but all she could manage was a barely audible whisper. The lightning crashed again. The form was closer, near her bed. She sought to quickly rise and gain her feet, however she was merely tangled in her covers to slide to the floor with a gasp and a thud.

The intruder tossed something on the end of her bed, then she heard a sharp click. A green glow stick was dropped near a case there. Dark gloved hands produced a narrow rod from the case, and snapped it into what Ephael now believed to be a gunstock. The intruder was assembling a rifle right in front of her. This took a measure of arrogance that infuriated Ephael.

"Thought you were the only one that would miss, Nargol?" quipped the odd female voice. To hell with the pistol. Ephael heard thunder crash, again the light. She was on her feet.Icy energy poured from Ephael striking her would be assassin. Her throat constricted tight as she saw Xhal's face, then a male face, then back to Xhal's.

She sought to smite the intruder again, and then summon forth her anger servent, but suddenly she was not there. All she could see were images of deep ancient stone corridors before her. Icy tendrils of ink suddenly vomiting forth and consuming her. In a second she was filled, invaded, smothered by shadow essence.

All the intruder saw was her standing mute, a look of horror etched on her face. The intruder chuckled lowly letting go with a few well placed volleys from the high powered rifle. Ephael spun slamming hard into the wall behind her, then slumped to the floor. Darkness was now enveloping her mind as well,she heard the assassin begin to go through her belongings.


*************************************************

She was aware of being very cold.It was as if fingers of ice, lighter than breath were passing through her flesh, raking down across the bone. She was outside. Yes at Harry's Outpost, this was the last place she had insured. The lightning crashed and midnight thunder rolled over her as she was trembling on the ground in ball. The rain began to pour down upon her. She found her voice as a cry of indecipherable rage tore from her throat. She was unsure exactly what had just unfolded, but someone would indeed pay a very heavy price.

*************************************************


When her wits were about her and she could travel, she immediately made her way through Omni trade, then to Entertainment, then back to Rome Blue. She sought Xhal straight away. It took some arguing to get through her attendent, but finally Ephael was successful.

Xhal's bleary eyes rapidly cleared as Ephael related the story to her. "Since you have joined with Reform your apartment has been monitored." Xhal stated reluctantly. "Perhaps we can glean some information." Xhal sat at her personal terminal, typing various access codes, then inserted an encrypted disk. She motioned for Ephael to approach as she scanned through the banks for the evening.

"Here." Xhal slowed the fast scanning image to play. "All your power is indeed dead."
"But it didn't get the camera?" Ephael added.
"They are self sufficient." Xhal sighed. Ephael watched as Xhal slowed the images. She did indeed rake her things to the floor and fall. An odd sense of dread filled Ephael as she recognized there was nothing on the foot of her bed. She saw no dark form with a rifle.
Xhal glanced at her non plussed.
"He was cloaked." She suggested. Ephael's eyes quickly returned to the monitor as she heard her own scream from the terminal. She was writhing in pain.Stumbling to the floor, she located her pistol. She watched in stark horror as she raised the weapon up to her temple...
