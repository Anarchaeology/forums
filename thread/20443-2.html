On the Other Hand
I just did some poking around in AntiGuardians, looking at all of them. My preliminary take:

  • Alsaqri Chemical Rifle: It better be uber, considering it uses the second most expensive ammo in the game and fires it at 1.0 speed. Except that, well, no, it's actually lower damage than even a routine sniper rifle like the Disaffiliation Sniper. Yawn.
  • HSR Explorer 661: Normal damage, poison AC (which will be nice for PvP, but irrelevant for PvM), adequate crits, and a minor hit point buff. Not bad. Except that the BBI Faithful does twice the damage, also buffs (dodge and duck), and is available in the Trader Shop. The BBI'll be more widely available, for cheaper, and at a wider range of QLs. OK for PvP, worthless for PvM.
  • IMI Tellus TT: Arrows? Maxed pistol skill but locked for a soldier? And damage is maybe, maybe just slightly better than a NovaFlow? Anything to be different, I guess.
  • Soft Pepper Pistol: No normal damage to speak of, but poison AC and insane crits. Requires only pistol skill to use, but requires a lot, I mean a lot, of that for the QL. Buffs MattCrea but not SpaceTime, through the roof. Perfect for NanoTechs, dubious for anybody else.
  • Sol Chironis Systems: For the same skill requirements, does less damage than either Mausser. Buffs run speed. Uses energy ammo, does energy AC damage - nice for lower level PvPers, irrelevant for everybody else. Yawn.
  • Summer Burst SMP: Again, for the skills, does less damage than a Mausser. Maussers are widely available in stores, for cheap. Again, anything to be different, I guess. Yawn.
  • Summer Fling SMP: I'm having a hard time figuring out what to compare it to, because pistol/smg/fling is an odd combination of skills. Nor am I sure what profession gets all three to where they could afford to equip this. (Engineers, maybe?) Damage is OK, for a pistol, not that that's saying much.
  • Summer FA SMP: Let me get this straight: Requires very high SMG and full auto, high heavy weapons. OK, it's a soldier weapon, nobody else could afford the IP. So compare it to the Nova Flow - same damage, but 95% lower crits. And the odd skill combination means that if you can't find one in the next QL you need, you're hosed. What a worthless weapon.
  • Summer Shells SMP: Given the high requirement of both SMG and Shotgun, again, only a soldier could afford the IP. Ends up being about as good as an SA Home Defender, a similar dual-wieldable shotgun, with a slightly higher rate of fire. OK, this one's not bad. But I wouldn't overpay for it.
