Bows - could you make them a bit more useful ?
Dear FC. I am now wielding the weapon I wanted since level 1 but never did because of the insane arrow prices. It looks cool, works fine, except for one minor thing - the ammo clip. It's 3. Aimed Shot, Fling, normal shot and I have to reload already. Every 2 shots I skip one because of the reload. After a fling and a normal shot I usually only chain-heal the tank anyway, but still, this weapon is impossible to use for soloing. A pity, it really looks nice.

I don't know where the 3-clip idea is based on, but for playability's sake, please oh please oh please increase that clip size, like on some of the crossbows ?
