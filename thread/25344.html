Equality of the Clans
I have three RL friends in my city I see once or twice a week. We all play AO, and team, trade etc... We all started out clan, and have been playing the game since August, and one of them since beta. Lately though, two of them started making characters as Omni, up to around lvl 90ish, then switch to Clan.

Why would they do this you might ask?? The reason is simple, you can power-level like crazy as an OT, up to level 90ish.

How can you level better as an Omni, then as a Clanner?? The reason is also simple, Omni have lots of great hunting areas for levels all the way up to level 90ish.

Omni lands spawn rates are fast, close to towns, a 5 min jog and you are there, easily excessable for peeps to shout "LFG", and lots of the mobs are non aggro. If you play Omni characters, then you know this to be true. If this were not true, then you would see lots of Omni types hunting in VW (Varmint Woods), it is just as accessable to Omni as Clan, just grid into Newland and go south, and VW comes with an Omni outpost with save terminal. But 99.9% of all the peeps that do hunt in VW are Clan. Why so few Neutrals? Because they go to the Omni areas like 20k or Lush Fields etc....

There are excellent advantages to great hunt areas, it's more fun then mission blitzing, you can't beat the great outdoors for the ability to view the entire battle field, it's more socialble then missions, your pets are much easier to control, and hunts train you for PvP, cuz 99% of all PvP is outdoors.

There is also an even greater benifit to these hunt areas, it builds comradeship, and instills teamwork. Mission blitzing is crowded and confusing at times, sometimes you can't see the other teammates, you get killed by "invizo mobs" your pets get stuck all the time or can't find the target, and missions are boring.

Before patch 12.8 or 12.9, the Clans had some nice hunt areas too, the palce to be in Tir, was the northern Tir OP. You would hit a wall of lag as you got close, because of all the peeps there. The "Clan seeking team" channel was always busy with peeps looking for a team. The OP was a great social place, a place to meet friends, trade and goof around. I made a lot of good friends there, and died a lot hunting at The Temple or the Shades area NW of the OP, Bot Mountain, and The Factory in Avalon. Another good place to hunt was The Tooth in Aegean where the Vulture Squabblers waited to kill everyone as they tried to duck into the mission area there. There were lots of other areas too

After that patch FC "changed spawn rates to some hunt areas", they killed them actually, and nerfeed \agents to death. The Tir OP was, and still is, a ghost town and the "Clan seeking team" channel was silent. I also noticed another thing, after the patch and the weeks rolled on, the attitude of the people in Caln areas changed, they were noticeably less socialble, less approachable and the whole Clan world seemed darker and depressed

All those hunt areas I mentioned above were trashed with fewer mobs and 15 min+ spawn rates. If just one team hunts any of those areas, they will be sitting around bored waiting for something to spawn up again once they kill the few mobs there. I have seen one lone MP own The Factory in Avalon now, it's just pathetic.

As a Clan player, you really can't find anywhere to hunt in clan held areas after level 30ish, so they all do missions til they puke. Only recently with the new team mission has the team channel seen some action, but these team missions are boring also, and people will sometimes request "single missions only".

My entire reason for posting this is to cry out to Funcom to restore our hunting areas and reduce the god aweful respawn times. The difference between Clan and Omni hunting areas is night and day. The lack of hunt areas also affects the attitudes and social climate of the Clan side. I have noticed a big attitude difference between the Clan before and after the patch that killed our hunt areas. Clan people seemed less socialble, more out of touch with eachother. Plus we are not training outdoors for future PvP wars with the OT, we just spalunk the caves and dreary missions day after day.

Either FunCom is completely ignorant of the differnces between Clan and Omni hunt areas, which is possible, they are FC by the way, or the folks at FunCom have some devious reason for killing Clan hunt areas and do not care to make then equal.

I submit that FC knows full well that there is a huge differnce between Clan and Omni hunting areas, and does not care, and has done this purposely. For whatever reason you nerfed our hunt areas, restore them now, to their former selves, and make us equal again.
