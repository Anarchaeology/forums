The Castle Camelot playfield - split?
I have what I consider to be a pretty important issue regarding the camelot castle playfield. At the moment it is all one big playfield with the 25% dungeon with tara in the same zone as the non-pvp/non-mob Castle proper above. This is causing a big headache, because people who like to use the dungeon for pvp and Tara have a monopoly over the use of the ENTIRE castle. Other people not interested in that want to use the castle itself for more role-play orientated things - meetings in the libraries or the dining halls, little events in the 2nd floor, etc... However these people cannot because of the stream of outrage from the pvp'ers below who rant about only 60ppl being allowed in the area, and 'making it the main pf for tara spawn', hence ruining their fun. I don't want to have anyone's fun ruined so I would like to suggest making the dungeon a new zone, so that we can use the castle for other stuff. After all, a lot of effort must have gone into designing the interior, we just want to take advantage of the great setting. Please let me know what you think.
