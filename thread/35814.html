Engineer. Until I break.
We are useless.
I'll say it again:
We are useless.
Lets take a look at what we have, and why what we have doesn't work for us.

What we have: We have ac buffs.
Why it doesn't work for us: AC does absolutely nothing.

What we have: We have reflect.
Why it doesn't work for us: Soldiers reflect is better.

What we have: We have debuff auras.
Why it doesn't work for us: We can't attack while the auras are active or they wear off and they DON'T work on mobs. Also, we have to raise dark blue skills to cast them.

What we have: Weaponsmithing
Why it doesn't work for us: Name a good weapon we can make.

What we have: Armor tradeskills (finally)
Why it doesn't work for us: The parts required are a RARE as nullity sphere.
Why it doesn't work for us #2: We have to camp lvl 60 mobs and missions with our ALTS to get it. Think about that for a second and realize how stupid that sounds.

What we have: Kamikaze bots
Why it doesn't work for us: Nevermind, they were promised but then they suddenly vanished...

What we have: Slayerdroid Transferrence
Why it doesn't work for us: Perhaps the best nano we have...but it forces engineers to be melee to be effective. And it forces us to raise a lot of dark blue skills.

Lets take a look at what we have:
We provide nothing to a group (unless a soldier isn't present)
We have to have very good equipment and go melee and be high level to even consider pvping.
We have tradeskills that are either just plain useless or you need an extremely rare item to make the items.

This has been said and said and said and said again and again and again and again and again! I'm about at the end of my rope....and many of my friends know that.

The armor tradeskills were a step forward! Then I find out how rare the PPE (the item you need to make the armor) is and now it is a huge step backward. What are you thinking? I sure as hell don't know and am close to giving up. Call this a whine or what have you, but it needs to be said until something is done. I'm a known and respected engineer...I think I know what I'm talking about.

-Fotik

Engi reflect caccons are a good example of a wanted buff. Specialy if there is not soldier in the group to cast his reflect sheild.
