Misc. Wear tab
I think we need a wear tab for miscellaneous(sp?) things. Items that just shouldn't be unusable or make your character ugly, whatever, just for AC bonuses for example. This is my idea: A new wear tab that includes slots for various social armors and certain AC booster armors.

Slot lists:

1. Left/Right arm tattoo (Yes, a different tattoo for each arm)
2. Front/Back tattoo (Same as left and right, the ability for a tattoo on the front and back. Wearing a vest and not seeing the tattoo with it on is not right I tell ya!)
3. Web cloak (less AC with my decus and look cool or more AC yet look ugly with conflicting armor gfx? Just let me use both!)
4. Social robe (Now I can cover up that silly tank armor with a leet lookin robe)
5. Social clothing (Ok, to be fair, my armors in the Wear tab are redded out to 0% effectiveness, but at least I don't have to unequip about a dozen things to wear a swimsuit nor rebuff to requip them, not to mention the inventory hassle)
6. Face (Glasses!!!)
7. Hood (I think having a cool lookin hood over that ugly cyber helm would do me good. Anyone agree?)
8. Vest (Well? SHouldn't I be able to wear a vest under my armor?)

Maybe the web cloak and vest parts might not work out, but the rest should! Comon, FC, give us a Social tab at least! =D
