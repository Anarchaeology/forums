Player Created Missions
I realize these were removed because of possible exploits involving trading no-drop items.

These would help add a bit more ****e to the (poorly run, thought, created GM "events" that are boring, dumb, and has an already determined outcome) every day mission.

This could bring the RP factor up quite a few notches, actually rewarding people who come up with some sort of "event" or mission, helping high levels entertain each other or lower levels.

Imagine you've just started, and have painfully gotten yourself to level 50. You finally can remember where WarTorn valley is, and you remember how to travel to this "bot mountain" people speak of near Tir.
A high level player comes up to you and asks for assisstance, stating they will pay a handsom amount of money to help them complete a mission. Since you've been trying to raise money for one of those damn yalmaha's you see everywhere, you would gladly do this.
The high level player offers you 200k to find someone in Tir named "whatever" and retrieve an special item needed. Upon finding them, you receive another mission, because "whatever" tells you a special component was stolen and taken to a static dungeon in "some place". You get the item from "some place", return it to "whatever" and receive his mission reward in 300k cash as well as the original mission item to return to this high level player for additional 200k.

well anyway, i'm tired but sooooo many possibilities! unless this is one of those "expansion pack specials of things we should have had in launch but wanted to charge extra for"!!!
