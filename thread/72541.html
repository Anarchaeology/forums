Yes. They've really borked up PvP with the last patch.

Most people might like the fact that it's harder for people to team up and kill you at arena or whatever. But you can't tell me it wasn't exciting to be in a full team, gank someone of the opposing side, only to find out they're in a full team, too. One person aggroing another turns into a 6-on-6 battle. That's the excitement -- and danger -- of the arena. And I miss that.

The inability to use specials/hostile nanos unless the other person is aggro'd on you is just ridiculous. This needs to be fixed right away, 'cause NT's are especially hurt by this.

Example scenario:
Trader drains NT.
NT auto-attacks trader.
Trader steps out of 0%/25%.
NT can't nuke or use specials, and is relying on base damage from whatever weapon he/she's using.
Trader plunders/divests the NT and turns their MC nano skill to ass.
NT is, essentially, screwed, 'cause they never even had a chance to get one nuke off.

Needs fixing. And fast, IMO.
