If not for titles?
Without being too redundant, i want to explain why to pvp if not for titles.

Its fun.

Hope thats clear enough. Granted this is coming from a trader who feels that he is SUPPOSED to win every time, not because im good but because of the tools at my disposal. Consequently i dont really get a kick out of just killing some random person, i even feel bad (for a sec) when i 3shot some poor little whinner in the omni arena. The good fights for me are the long ones, and i tend to lose a lot more long fights than short ones - at least since i havent been getting alpha'd to death post 150.

Course not everyone will pvp for the "experience" or in search of an epic 5 minute fight. For the underpowered among us, i'd understand going out and killing, and appreciating, whatever i could. But for the really big/strong out there, how impressive is it if you're lvl 200 and a novice? Quite frankly i'm not quivering in awe.... Pvp is hugely a matter of
A: choosing the right class
B: spending time dying stupid deaths learning what not to do
C: Lvling to a point where you're damn near invincible to anyone not already doing the exact same thing you are.

So anyway, if you're an adventurer, fixer, nt, crat.... wear that title with pride. if you're a trader or soldier? then title = time spent in pvp zones, whoopdeedooo. Hope all that time spent was worthwhile and fulfilling enough in itself
