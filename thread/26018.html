I have had the fortune of participateing in a few apparently discontinued side plots in the story back in the beginning of the year.

The Vandelmir Consortium(spelling might be a bit off) had great potential but it ended with a whimper and left many loose ends.

More recently a few weeks ago there was an OTSOD meeting called for by a GM. My hopes were up, it was off to a tension filled start. At the end the GM/Character said she will be around for a few days and we can contact her if the need arise.

Status update: The GM/Character has been missing for over 2-3 weeks without any sightings or comm traffic. Have you seen her?


It is very fraustrateing to see all the potential there is that is unused. Unfortunately they always abondon these side plots once they get off to a great start or in the case of the recently revived Dust Brigade, sporadic activity are the only remote signs of life.
