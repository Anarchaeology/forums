Truth on Dual Weilding (repost)
Basically I'll try to summarize. (and I have tested this in game and it appears to be accurate)

This will basically give you the attack benefits of dual weilding. Damage benefits will depend on what QL of each weapon you are able to weild. In the dual weild skill description it states that dual weilding is not as "accurate" as single weilding, so this may account for slighly lower damage outputs from equal weapons when dual weilded.


Multi-weild rules.

1) Only one can attack at a time
2) Recharge happens if no weapon is attacking
3) Both weapons recharge at same time
4) recharge will be pre-empted if a weapon is ABLE to attack

Example:

2 Pistols. Pistol A 2.5/2.5 Pistol B 2.5/2.5

Attack sequence:
Attack-A, Attack-B, Recharge Both ... repeat

So doing the math ... in 15sec ..
Single Weild: 3 attacks / 3 recharges
Dual Weild: 4 attacks / 2 recharges


2 Pistols Pistol A 1/1 Pistol B 1/2

Attack Sequence:
Attack-A, Attack-B, recharge 1sec, Attack-A, Recharge 1sec, Attack-A, Attack-B ... Repeat

I hope this makes sense.
Dual weilding weapons with different att/rech times gets complicated and it requires calculation on a per weapon basis to determine whether it is an advantage or not.

Dual weilding matched weapons with equal att/rech times should yield somewhere near 33% more damage output OVER TIME.

Dual weilding's biggest advantage over single is from matched weapons with a high recharge time.

i.e. hypothetical weapons with the follow times: 1s/3s

Single Weilded:

Att(1s), rech(3s) , Att(1s), rech(3s), Att(1s), rech(3s) , Att(1s), rech(3s), Att(1s), rech(3s) (20s total)

5 attacks 5 recharges

Dual Weild:
att-a(1s), att-b(1s), rech-both(3s),att-a(1s), att-b(1s), rech-both(3s),att-a(1s), att-b(1s), rech-both(3s),att-a(1s), att-b(1s), rech-both(3s) (20s total)

8 attacks 4 recharges

This gives you 60% advantage over a single weild. If you lower the attack time with init, this advantage will increase.

Side note: dual weilding is an even greater advantage with high crit weapons.
