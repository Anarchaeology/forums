a little extra point
Firstly, thanks for the positive comments and the bumps guyz

Just a couple of comments in Funcom/business terms to make it seem more appealing for them to do.

Most games as we know - background music is pretty important - not so much as graphics, but at least up there.
Standard games last - what... maybe a month ot two until you complete it or get bored.
Multiplayer games - well the majority are action, action, action - so the sound effects and speed of play are the main factors here.
(excluding the graphics of course)

This is my first MMPORPG and the idea is to keep the player going over a long time (hey Funcom even would like us for 4 years !) - they have the excellent graphics already, they update the game with new ideas, places, events etc to keep the game interesting. (OK - I know there are problems - please don't flame over this point)

However, music takes a back seat here. Why ? well a couple of reasons - The music is good already (IMHO I think they have done a good job), there are always newbies joining who are new to it, why spend money, resources, download time in patches to produce a new score here when there are for more important other factors to consider ?
BUT - for players who start hitting the month - 2 month barrier when 'normal' games usually get shelved, the background music gets less appealing and more tedious.

So give us the chance to work on it ourselves, let US keep the music interesting etc. Let US do the main work here.
I don't want to take away Funcom resources from the main issues - bugs, gameplay etc. but as we all know (or should know) there are different project teams working on the different aspects of the game. So for what I think is a small outlay in intial coding (when they can fit it in) - Funcom might never have to worry about this aspect of the game into the future - one thing we are good at - is listening/choosing our personal choice of music

Regarding Winamp - yeah sure that is one solution - but we all know from playing the game that AO has diiferent scores for diferent areas/modes of the game - I like that Just give us the directory structure to match and I'm a happy man ( rather than us doing the equivalent - which we most probably couldn't be bothered doing - loading up a different play list into winamp each time we zone or start a fight *lol*)

Anyway - just another two pennies worth.
Hope you spot this thread Funcom/Cosmik - even a feasabilty comment would be nice

Regards all,
