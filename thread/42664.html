An Old Man
My name is Shirley Miyashiro, yes I know, Shirley sound like girl name, my honorable father always wanted girl. My friends call me Windkata.

Yesterday I was severely insulted, stupid person called me old man. This is an insult. My hair may be gray, and I may be somewhat bald, but I am merely 105 years old, and still agile and graceful as panther. "Hello young one" I answered.
"Do you know where Ententainment arena is." He asked me.
I did not know. And did not want to know. I did not need to know.
He insult me even more, telling me I die there. "Hah" I say. I have no time for this sir silly person. So I go away.

Next time, I perhaps go there, and show him age does not matter. If silly person ask next time I shall agree. Then I show him why I called Windkata.

/Windkata, Martial Artist.

Note: Windkata was very young when he came to Rubi-Ka, his family looking for a better life. This they did not find. Instead they got stuck between the Clanners and the Omni. Shirleys parents died in meeting a group of shades. As they were to poor to afford insurance, they died. Windkata managed to barely escape, and promised he would avenge his parents death. He swore to become the greatest martial artist of the known universe, and to rid Rubi-ka of all the shades. The petty fighting between omni and the clans mean little or nothing to him. However, he will probably choose sides in the future, as he will uncover something about the shades that will change his mind.
