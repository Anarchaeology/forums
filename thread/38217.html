There are 18 chapters altogether, 331 pages in the hardcover. I'm currently on chapter 8. So far, I give a billion thumbs up.

I've heard people complain that it skips around too much, but I think that was done to keep the short-attention mentality that the book is really geared towards. I've been following it fine so far, just gotta pay attention to the dates at the top of each sub-chapter and compare them to earlier ones you've read.

The storyline is progressing awesomely, and I'm quite hooked into it. Very believable characters as well.

If only I had more time to read it.
