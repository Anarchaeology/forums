PLEASE take the huge monsters out of tiny missions
Recently I've been getting tons of missions with Wastelayer Beetles in them. These things are so large they don't even fit in one room. I attack one and five come running from other rooms, presumably because they are so enormous they all think they are overlapping into that room too. I attack one and run away and all sorts of monster warping, through-the-wall hitting, insta-death without seeing any hits, and other lame stuff starts to happen because they don't fit through the doors.

Stuff like this happens all the time with those giant spiders and with snakes and every other large monster that doesn't fit through doors. Please, make us some kind of LARGE mission with HUGE doors if we must fight these things. And what the heck are wastelayer beetles doing in houses in the city of home?
