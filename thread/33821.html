Attacking guards?
I have no real idea why people do this. I have never attacked the guards in any mmorpg I have ever played. But in all the mmorpg's I have played I have always seen people do it. I don't have anything against it though.
What I am against is the fact that you can use attacks against the guards but not healing or buffs. When I watch a group of 150+ quickly dispatch a guard I only wish I could help defend. In my opinion these people are obviously not true Omni material.

Heres an idea. Allow people to attack guards and get loot and xp. If the person kills a guard or of the same alignment (Omni,Clan,neutral) they loose a token/+. The loss would depend on the level of the victim. Every 10th level of the victim increases the token penalty by one.

Example: An Omni employee kills a level 200 Omni guard. The employee gets xp, loot and - 20 tokens from his/her personal token board.

However if the employee doesn't have 20 tokens their employment is terminated with the company and their token board deletes and they have to join a side again.

Considering the attitude towards killing ones own guards, I don't think this idea is harsh. For those who enjoy it and don't want to loose tokens I would say "take that aggression to the enemies city and take it over, We've already conquered this town we don't need to do it again."

Rebuttal?

OK, lets sum up the ideas...
OK, lets sum up the ideas.


1. Allow people to attack guards/citizens and get loot and xp. If the person kills a guard/citizen of the same alignment (Omni,Clan,neutral) they loose a token/+. The loss would depend on the level of the victim. 1 token + 1 token per 10 levels of the victim.

2. Disallow sales to the offender in the town that the act was made. Disallowed sales for 1 week + 1 week per 10 levels of the victim.

3. An offender gets put on a temporary long term hate list for the town the act was commited. Banishment for 1 week + 1 week per 10 levels of the victim.

4. Title change/alteration (traitor, coward, untrustable). The change will stay as per the same formula. 1 week + 1 week per 10 levels of the victim.

#1 would not apply to neutrals but Omni and Clan should be accountable for 1, 2, 3 and 4. Neutrals only 2, 3 and 4.


Example: An Omni employee kills a level 200 Omni guard. The employee gets xp, loot and - 21 tokens from his/her personal token board. 21 weeks of disallowed sales and 21 weeks of banishment in the city the act was commited. However if the employee doesn't have 20 tokens their employment is terminated with the company and their token board deletes and they have to join a side again. The employee gets branded with a title change for 21 weeks.

As for killing opposing aligned guards/citizens. There should be an increase chance to receive a token of bravery. This chance based on the victims level. Omni vs Clan only. The person killing the opposing guard/citizen will have a title change(hero, valiant)
as per the same formula, 1 week + 1 week per 10 levels of the victim.

And to top it off, if people in a 100% suppression gas area can attack guards we should be able to buff and heal the guards.

These are our ideas to date.

Under these circumstances a bored person is more likely to go to another town to kill the oppositions guards and keep with good roleplay. And if that happens the other players can help defend by helping their own guards. Now you have Clan and Omni conflicts that will cause talking that will lead to more roleplay.
