Random Idea 1: Rechargable Treatment Labs
I think it would be great if we could recharge our treatment labs etc with a higher QL one.

E.g. suppose I have a QL 50 Treatment Lab. Then Shift-Right Clicking a QL 60 Lab on it would add the QL 60 charges to the QL 50 Lab.

It seems logical to me since this is basically "underclocking" nanobots to work less efficiently. Adding lower QL nanobots to a higer QL Lab should not work, of course.

Hmm, perhaps some fixers/engineers/nanotechs should be able to "overclock" nanobots to some extent.
