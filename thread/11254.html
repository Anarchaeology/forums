If it would be possible for a lot of small guilds to buy land together, and divide the castle/house/bunker into parts, I think the money problem would be lessened a bit.


An example:

Guild A (mostly engineers and traders), B (mostly soldiers and enforcers) and C (mostly adventurers and fixers) all wants the same spot for their guild houses, so they determines to buy it together.

They build 3 small appartment 'pieces' instead of one large, so that each guild has a sepparate area. These can be used for whatever the individual guild wants.

Along with this, they build a garden or similar which is open for everyone in the 3 guilds. In this guarden is a generic guildbank, where you can donate money and items to the common cause of the guilds.

They also build a store, where guild A and C sells things. Since guild B is mostly a militaric clan, they don't see the need for a vendor. However, they might get a discount from things they buy here. Everyone else can come here and buy things too.

Guild B maintains the guards. There are often players nearby that can help, but they need some NPCs to help them out. These NPCs helps guild A+B+C.


Thus: Everyone helps everyone, and in the end cooperating small guilds will get the same chance as a lone large guild.
