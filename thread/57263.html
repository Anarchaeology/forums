Newbie Fixer Guild!
I've seen most guild have requirements on a minimum level to join. Not so here! I love my new fixer character, do you? Let's get together, regardless of level, on Rubi-Ka 2. I'm currently in Omni-1 Entertainment doing missions for money. I'm also in the process of making a webpage for the guild, so feel free to submit your ideas for a name. Please be serious. I foresee us learning from each other, helping other newbies as we progress, and being a source for information and aid specific to our profession. I am Omni-Tec, but I think the guild should be profession-specific rather than affiliation.

I am in the game most evenings and Sundays, Mountain Standard Time (GMT-7) as Tainiste, currently level 8 Omni-Tec Fixer. PM me and join on up!
