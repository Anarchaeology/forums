Clan attacked Omni Trade!
Today 3/5 around afternoon in Pacific time. I was gathering equipments and supply in Omni Trade and saw severval Clans attack Omni guards. Since Trade is absolute no PvP, we as Omni citizens couldn't do anything to stop them, but standing there to see our guards got killed. Sadly, I didn't draw their names down for future action.

However I believe FunCom should change the rule of PvP in these cities that don't allow PvP. For example, in this case: if Clan opens fire to any citizen of Omni, then he or she would be attacked by any of Omni employees (and the other way around, if Omni attacks Clan guard then (s)he would put him/herself into PvP in the cities).

I understand some people might say that people with lower level will not be safe anymore in the cities, but that's why we need to take action to prevent such crime happens again.
