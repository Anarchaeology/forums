OFFICIAL WHISPERS EDGE PRESS RELEASE


Omni-Pol Presence in West Athen

By Shawnta Viksentrix Clancy  Saturday 25 Nov 29477

Growing Crime Against Omni-Tek Or Something Else?

Clan members, Omni-Tek employees, and Neutrals,

Hello loyal readers. Been a while since the writing muse has been upon me. The Notum Wars has provided plenty of material a war journalist could use. I have covered many battles in my day and have witnessed atrocities on both sides of the conflict, Clan victories, and their defeats and have gotten weary of battle reporting. Frankly, I am very surprised at the lack of coverage of the current war. One must wonder if the recent shutdown of the Voice of Freedom and its Omni counterpart was a result of pressure from both factions to limit the amount of war news that gets out. Perhaps it is the ICC that ordered their shut-down? It does not bode well for the population when free press is restricted. Anyway, I didnt put pen to paper to discuss the war or the recent closings of the major news organs. No, today I would like to talk about Omni-Pol and their increased presence in West Athen.

In the past few weeks the residents of West Athen have noticed a marked increase in the presence of Omn-Pol officers. General Lida Templus Pierce of Omni-Pol has made regular visits to our little town. Often she comes alone, sometimes accompanied by a squad of troopers, and on at least one occasion, accompanied by Garrett Bonefish Silvestrini, Director of Sparta (OTSEC). Rumor has it that Omni-Pol has at least one if not several different operations laid on in our little town. Several people have been dragged off the street and subjected to Omni-Pols brand ofquestioning. Omni-Pol troopers have been seen investigating The Cup, Speedys and have taken an extreme interest in Back yard Four.

Omni-Pol has been very tight lipped about exactly WHY they are here. One can guess why they are investigating Speedys and the Mockers. Their reputations as being one step ahead of the law are no secret. But what have they been doing in The Cup and why has Back-yard Four been of such interest? Jynne of Unity of the Rose had this to say, West Athen grid terminal is unguarded. The proximity of The Cup and Back-yard Four to this unguarded terminal may be why Omni-Pol concentrates in these areas. She went on to say, Back-yard Four is also a gathering point for several Clan guilds. This is very true. In my brief interview with Jynne, I spotted two different guild leaders and members of at least five clans. Anyone or all of these guilds may be the target of their investigations. Vixentrox, leader of Whispers Edge, has reported that there is a new skilled agent that has been added under Templus command who seems to be stalking her.

Perhaps the most disturbing thing is they are allowed to operate with near impunity in this little town. Little can be done to stop their incursions although the occasional guard gets lucky and sends one of them to reclaim. I encourage all Clan members to report Omni-Pols movements and doings to their respective guild leaders and also to Unified Intelligence Service (UNITS). Long live the Clans!

*the fall of 29475*

The suns were rising behind the junkyard and hunting parties were organising in Freedom Outpost. It looked like it would be just like any other day when suddenly across the malfunctioning and extremely inadequate comm channels the call came. Omni-pol was assaulting Old Athen!

Shouts rang out across the valley of Athen Shire and before we knew it there were in excess of thirty clanners forming up to go push back the oppressors. Mostly adventurers weilding pairs of Freedom Arms pistols, but there was a scattering of soldiers, martial artists and Nanotechnicians. I was the only Fixer there, so I set to helping the adventurers increase the speed of the party. The gathering set out down the road and was luckily only stopped once by a virulent minibull.

We stormed into the western gates of Old Athen just as the suns were reaching their zenith and the city had an eerie look to it. We spread out and started looking for them, but the streets were strangely empty. I noticed a body take shape on the resurrection pad and I ran over to the soldier sitting there.

"Hey you there", I shouted at him as he stared blankly at the reclaim terminal.

He looked over at me with a wild look in his eyes, "He's a madman... Ninja killed me.... never even saw it coming.. Nnninnjaa."

Shaking him by the soldiers I tried to get some information from him, "Snap out of it man! Who's a madman, and where are they?"

His eyes focused and the soldier seemed to see me for the first time as he replied slowly, "It's Meister, and he's at the East gate, the guards can't do anything to stop him."

I looked up and saw a squad of adventurers heading south so I yelled for them to head to the east gate. We got there just in time to see a black trenchcoat running out the gate, the echoes of a maniacal laugh sounding off the walls.

The adventurers stormed out of the gate, but the vision of that soldier so shaken up at reclaim made me take a more cautious stance as I moved out to meet the enemy. Too my right, three omni-pol shocktroopers were gunning down the guard and the adventurers were swarming towards them. Off to the left I saw it, the Ninja. In glistening black steel, it was single handedly taking down another guard.

Then I saw Meister, standing off to the south just a bit. He was alone and his Ninja was pretty far from him. I moved around to get a better vantage point, particularly so that the ninja would be farther from me. I got in range of Meister and I realised that Omni-tek had treated him well. He moved like a well trained machine and had the look of death in his eyes. I knew I would be no match for him.

I hesitated for a moment as I knew that if he realised I was this close to him I would surely end up resurrecting. Then a little voice in my head said, "If he isn't stopped here what will keep him from Athen Shire?" It was all I needed to resolve myself that I was willing to die for the clans.

Levelling my Mausser Particle Streamer at him, he turned and I saw eye to eye the enemy. A gleam of amusement flashed in his eyes as he knew as well as I did that I was no match for him. I squeezed the trigger and said "get away from our town, you murderer."

The cacophony of explosions that marks the burst of my MPS would have been so reasurring at that point, but there was none. Again I pulled the trigger, suspecting the weapon to be jammed. Nothing still, I looked down at the ammunition indicator to see that it was full. I squeezed the trigger again as Meister smiled and said dryly, "or what? you'll shoot me?"

In all the excitement to come help defend my city, I had neglected to sniff the air. It was definitely there though, the dry, pungent, stale smell of suppression gas. Glancing over to the other clanners that had come to help, I saw that they were coming to the same realisation as they stared at their freedom arms that wouldn't fire at the troops killing our guards.

Meister had turned his back on me and was directing his Ninja to attack another guard that had ran in from the south. I pointed my gun at the Ninja and squeezed the trigger again in disbelief. Still nothing. I leaned over on the rock I had been hiding behind and looked down at my gun again.

We sat there as the suns slowly began to set and the slaughter continued. Others came and went, but I just sat there in the dust of the Aegean desert wondering why our guards should be subjected to this while we could only watch impotently.

Eventually darkness fell and the shock troopers were slowly taken out one at a time by the guards somehow. Or maybe they just got bored and went home, I was in too much of a daze to pay enough attention. I looked up in time to realise that the Ninja was gone as well, and saw a guard plant a bullet right between Meister's eyes. His head rolled to face me as he fell to the ground, I couldn't help but notice that he still had that smirk on his face.

I silently began the long run back to freedom outpost, wondering when Omni-tek would stop pumping the infernal suppression gas into the clan territories.
