Uniformity
Ok, here goes.

A little thought, what about having an easy way to facilitate a guild uniform without the hassles of having to take off armor, and then having to get yourself buffed back into it, possibly even have to swap implants.

How about a little item like a guild uniform. It wont have ANY ac attributes or other modifiers associated to it at all, just an item that covers the existing armor you are wearing.

Guild Uniform

The guild leader, who will associate items to the uniform, can only edit this item, not exactly sure how programming wise this would be implemented. However something like an /org uniform command.

E.g.
/org uniform edit

At which there will be a message in the chat box that says

Drag item to chat box that you would like to associate with the uniform,then press {enter}, hit {escape} to finish.

Leader then takes the clothing item he wants to associate drags and drops it into the chat box and the Slot that that item would be associated with would be filled.

If you need to start from scratch then you could.

/org uniform clear

There are many possibilities that could come with such an item. You could even have different uniforms associated to the rank that each person holds within the guild

/org uniform rank1 edit
/org uniform rank2 edit
/org uniform rank3 edit
and so on..

I just had a little idea, and thought I would post it here before submitting it anywhere. To see what the general consensus would be about an item like this. I am proud to be in my guild and I would like to be able to easily spot another member of my guild in the heat of battle. I would also like to see something like this to help unify us as members and make stronger bonds.
