Yeah, absolutely correct. I don't know how many times I've had to leave a shop and go to my bank to either check an item, pick an item up, or get a med suit etc.

You need to have banks in the places where their contents are going to be used.

You do a lot of work in shops: buying new equipment, getting implants and clusters, adding NCU and belts, etc etc. All these things, involve an interaction between what you have on hand and what you need to get to finish what you want to do. That means you need access to your bank in shops.
