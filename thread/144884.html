Unity of the Rose releases Rpchat
Unity of the Rose is pleased to introduce a new public-access chatbot to Rubi-Ka, RPCHAT.

Intended as a method for Roleplayers of all factions to communicate OOCly in support of and coordination for roleplaying events, any player on Rubi-Ka 1 can join the channel with the command /tell rpchat chat for an invitation to the fun. Several Roses and selected other well-known roleplayers have been trusted with administration access to stop disruptive behavior, ensuring a positive environment.

Rpchat is maintained and hosted by Unity of the Rose as a service to the community, and has no ties whatsoever to Funcom or ARK-Events staff. Use of the bot is a privledge, not a right: be sure to read the Rules via /tell rpchat rules.
