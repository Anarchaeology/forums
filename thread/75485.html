Help Wanted (clan RK1)
I am looking for some people to help me with my clan guild Masked Crusaders. I am in need of people to hold seats in my council so I can get this guild on a roll. I have a lot of things planned that would make this guild fun and introduce some new Role-play ideas to AO, but I can not do this on my own. I need interested people to help me out. Here is some basic info on the guild...

1. We ultimately want peace. Will work towards peaceful solutions but will use military action if necessary(council approved).

2. Protect and serve the people.

3. Entertain the people of RK. This is done through many different events such as a Lottery system, plays, parties, global events, and role-play events.

4. Every voice is listened to.

If you are remotely interested, please tell me in game (/tell Chizad) or email me at mcrkd1@yahoo.com with any questions you may have and I will gladly answer them. I am looking forward to working with you all to have fun and make RK a more fun place to play!
