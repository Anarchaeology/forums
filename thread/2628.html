Expansion
I know some people will probably loath and hate this idea and resent the idea of paying FC more money, and that others may agree and think my idea is valid.....


BUT...


What i think AO needs more badly than anything else is an expansion pak/CD they can put on the market to in one big go add lots of stuff and sort out a lot of the bugs and problems within the game...


This would enable them to give us much more new data and updated stuff including content and new areas than would be viable in a patch, or, which IN patches would probably take a good few months to put out to us all, and would also create bugs as it would be put up in sections and not just one big go.


An expansion pak could solve so much, add so much and if they priced it productively (say £15.99, i cant do monetary conversions but that'd probably be around £9) then they could give the game a new lease of life and make their playerbase a lot lot happier.


Its a thought, of which i hope FC considers......
