The differences between the breeds right now are the base value, cost and maximum on the six main abilities (Strength to Psychic), and the amount of health and nano points gained per level and per rank in their respective skills.

We are considering several ways of making the breeds stand more apart, especially the nano breed, but won't divulge any details on that yet. You'll have to wait on the edge of your seat.

Megabio...Psychic does work. Psychic affects the nano pool through the Nano Pool skill. This skill is linked 70% to Psychic, and so gets most of its 'ability bonus' from that ability. The effect is more noticeable at lower levels, but is often overseen since you can only increase Psychic three points per level, which does not always give an extra bonus. Perhaps this is why you feel it is broken?
