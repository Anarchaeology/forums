IPR
While Cz has made it clear they aren't allowing IPR points or resets in the newest patch, I think this is a big mistake. There should be some viable way to make changes to a character short of rerolling it. Equipment changes, and mistakes are made. Funcom needs to come up with some sustainable way of getting IPR or reset points, including possibly:

Losing some amount of levels per title level per reset point.
Losing some amount of tokens per reset point.
Paying an exponential amount of money per reset point.
Going on a quest or mission to get a reset point.
Some combination of the above.

The first ideas complicated. The player would have to have enough IPs available to lose during his level loss, or he must choose a skill from which the IPs will be removed.

The other ideas are easier to implement. I really think Funcom needs to implement this. Casual players like myself do not have the time or tolerance to reroll a character to try something else. Instead, we'll just quit (like I did before when my fixer was unplayable). Don't give people a reason to leave.
