Come to be Enlightened
We Bear the Burden of Guardianship!
We Enlighten the Ignorant!
We Toil for our own Enlightenment!

We, the Enlightened of Rubi-Ka, seek those out who wish to be enlightened and will bring enlightenment to our ranks. What type of enlightenment do I speak of? The enlightenment that the world about us, the blessed Rubi-Ka, is a living divine entity. But there is far more to us than just that...

While we share a singular unified belief we do diverge in how that belief is expressed. Within our devoted are those who believe that Rubi-Ka is the manifestation of Bantz the spirit of retribution, there is the belief that Rubi-Ka is the Great Mother - that all of Rubi-ka is living, and that Rubi-Ka is a living enigma of reflection of a divine being that is itself a reflection of Rubi-Ka. These are the beliefs that we began with!

Most recently a neophyte, Mistress Rapture, has brought her off-world belief that Rubi-ka, the moons, and the suns are part of a religious celestial cosmology known as the Sisters of Mercy where females are dominant and males are submissive.

But this only scratches the surface of who we are! We are a structured religion open with the potential of a neophyte who can rise and join the leadership as a Prophet... a prophet being one who has risen above individual beliefs to embrace all as having equal truth... that the truth is more than a singular belief but something above and beyond what we as mortals can only toil to grasp only a glimpse of before our bodies join the dust of the world we worship!

Furthermore, one does not have to belong to the Enlightened to be a worshipper... anyone may believe, regardless of their personal affiliations... we are cultivating a congregation of true believers who have no membership within the Enlightened. The Enlightened are just those who have devoted themselves, utterly and completly to the Divine in its many masks!

DarqOmen,
Prophet and Vicar of Spiritual Well Being

ooc: We are a clan-based Roleplaying guild on RK1 with a different twist from that of the typical guild; we do not envision ourselves growing large nor do we wish to do so at this point... the current prophets all believe strongly in moderated growth; allowing entry only to those members that are a best fit to the Enlightened. We do not just accept anyone, nor do we randomly invite everyone! Every member goes through a period of learning about the guild and its diverse structure as a candidate member (known to us as Neophytes) where membership is not guaranteed. We look upon this period as a time for the candidate to determine if we are a good fit for them and vice versa.

We were founded in September and officially formed with the release of the guild patch (heh, I cannot recall the path number). We are planning and will have our own events that are more than just a hunt or party at one of the local watering holes. Furthermore, we have a formal guild structure that is open to those who just wish to roleplay, rise to leadership of the guild, or to roleplay the award/earning of titles such as Minister or Celestial Guardian.

Our intent is not to wait for Funcom to provide us with Roleplaying but to create our own!

For further details reference TERK at www.guildportal.com and from there visit our charter.

Additional Details
I pointed out one belief that was brought to us by Neophyte MistRapture. This was done to point out that our structure is such that one with ambition and conviction of belief is more than just a drone. You can make a difference!

For clarification, our three primary Sects are...

Sect of the Great Mother where the devoted view the world of Rubi-ka as one single, living, divine entity. That notum is the divine essence of the mother. Typical believers in the Great mother act as the mediators, explorers, and diplomats of the Enlightened. Many notum users are drawn to this sect.

Sect of the Enigma of Shadows where the devoted view the world as a physical manifestation on our plane of the divinity and our world casts back upon the divinity. That all truths and realities are wreathed in shadows and all shadows hide truths and realities. Typical believers in the Enigma of Shadows act as the eyes & ears as agents, spys, and information brokers of the Enlightened. Many of the science oriented are drawn to this sect.

Sect of Bantz where the devoted believe the world of Rubi-ka is the physical manifestation of an ancient spirit of vengence and retribution known as Bantz. Typical believers in Bantz act as the the sword of the Enlightened in both battle and guardianship. Many of those who glory in the use of their skills in battle are drawn to this sect.
