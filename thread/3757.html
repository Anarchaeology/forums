How Funcom should fix the range and other exploits
Why is an exploit bad for the game? Exploits become a problem when a player can get an unfair advantage over a mob, then repeat it over and over again to rack up experience with out any danger to themselves. So far Funcom has been trying to attack the problem by removing the first part. They have been trying to remove the unfair advantage. What they should be doing is eliminating the second part. If a player is unable to repeatedly use that advantage to benefit themselves, then it is not an exploit.

Make it so that a player will get reduced experience as they continue to attack the same mob type. In a way it even makes role-playing sense. You already know how to beat that mob type, so killing it again and again will teach you less. It can be made a sliding scale. As you kill a type of mob, your experience gained will go down. As you kill other types your experience penalty will start to be reduced.

Now if an NT wants to blow up a stationary mob from a safe distance let them. It is one of the perks of their class after all. However, they will only be able to get so much experience doing it. If experience were what they are after, it would be much better for them to team up in a group and hunt multiple mob types.

A nice side benefit of the new rule is that team hunting would be less repetitive. You could no longer go into a camp and only hunt one mob type. Teams would have to switch up or move around to find better hunting spots. Balancing mobs against players becomes a whole lot easier when you can allow for a particular class to have an extra advantage. The up side for players is that now there is less need to nerf and you can keep you special abilities.

You can extend this technique to handle the sneaking through missions problem. If an agent want to sneak through a mission at 100% difficulty, let them. Put a limit on the number of sneakable missions they can get in a time period. If they sneak through a few missions in a row, then give them missions that are hard to sneak through. Require them to fight a mob at the end, or put more locked doors in it. You get the idea. Once again it make role-playing sense. There are only a limited number of missions out there. As you get done with the ones that are easy for you, youll have to start taking the harder ones.

The end result is that characters get to keep their uniqueness and the gameplay get more variety. To sum up, Funcom please look first to removing repeatability of exploits first before nerfing abilities.

Part II of range nerfing fix.
The extra range of NT was a problem with mobile mobs too. Apparently, the mobs max aggro radius was at about 40m, so NTs could blast them too without fear of retaliation. Upping every mobs agro radius to handle this problem is not a viable solution. Just think of all the adds you would get then. A much better solution is to up the agro radius of a mob that is under attack. After all, if you were being shot at wouldnt you be on high alert looking for the culprit.

Also improve the mobs AI so that if they cant find and return fire against an attacker, they run away. (That last idea came from someone else but its pretty good.)
