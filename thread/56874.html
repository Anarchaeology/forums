Kat took a draw from her cigarette, and tossed it to the ground. She ground it into the cement with the heel of her boot, allowing the smoke to reach her nose.

People are so stupid

They could at least wait until someone had hacked my insurance away to kill me.

Shed get the Locknane brat this timeand possibly a few of her little friends too

Kat fingered a small metallic device in her hand and turned it over, admiring the fine work. At least some people did know what they were doingbut as far as she was concerned, ninety percent of the world was useless

And maybe it was.

She stalked slowly through the grass out in Borealis, making her way up the mountain near the mining outposts. She stopped at the top of the sandy path, and took a seat, letting the cold night air blow her pigtails into her face. A faint smell of dampness rose from the many lakes, and a million tiny pinpricks of light shone in the sky. It was a clear night, and a large round moon was visible.

Kat allowed herself to smile a little.

Im coming for you Locknane, because

You are a puppet

I can make you do whatever I wish on my stage, for I have your weakness now

Because sociopaths dont die.

And she smiled

-----------------------------------------------------------------

MM woke up from her nap. Struggling to realize her surroundings, she came to the conclusion that shed been gridded back to her apartment after a de-leeting. She smiled, and got to her feet to test the fact her legs and arms still worked.

Giggling with joy, she ran to her apartment balcony and admired the suns-set. Reets were fluttering about the sky and twittering their song, as the twin suns played a beautiful melody of color across the deep blue sky. Countless trees dotted the ground around vast, beautiful lakes that reflected the orange, pink, and purple wash of color coming from the sky. Smiling, MM checked her watch: 3:00.

Just in time for the party.

She turned and walked across her apartment, when suddenly she felt a small shock.

Crossing to her bed, she realized she was starting to remember something

There was a great wall of pulsating blue in various shades and tints. MM felt like she were floating in the mass of blue, on a sticky platform of some sort and almost had to shield her eyes. From the wall of blue an unearthly song played, so beautiful it could stop any living creature in its tracks. It seemed to be a beautiful flute melody encased in a hum, and entirely indescribable. Not believing her eyes, MM sank to her knees in the mystical place and listened to this song

And suddenly the music faded. She felt almostempty, and a little sad. She was drawn to the wall, and began to walk towards it

It blinked out of existence.

Frightened, MM continued walking blindly forward with her arms outstretched, trying to reactivate the wall in some way, until she hit the end of the platform, and began to fall forever-

Im back! Look what I got!! Her apartment door banged open and Cristin came running in, her arms full of bags. MM sat up quickly.

Just a dream.

Her cousin began to talk quickly, pulling things out of the bags and placing them on the table. But MM didnt seem to hear it, and stared at nothing a little moreit wasnt possible..

Just a dream.

Moving in slow motion she sat up and talked to Cristin as she showed her the things shed bought, but MM didnt feel quiteall there.

Realizing she needed to get BoyStation together and start moving towards the party, she hugged Cristin goodbye and left her with the gridvision remote, and began to head for Omni-Entertainment, beginning to come back down to the ground.

Just a dream

it isnt fair.

its okay.

Well if youre sure, then lets have some fun, shall we? The seconds ticked by as Kats hand moved towards the button on her commlink, deathly slowly, shattering her existence with every finger movement until-

The button moved-

Deafening silence.

Whomessed with?!

Nono, this is better. No, come outyes, this is rich

Stomach long since distentigrated into nothing, MM slowly edged back into the clearing, where Kat had holstered her mausser. No, she was holding a small, beaten-up solar-powered pistol.

Since you had to go and have someone wreck the bombyou do it.

no. I wont.

I cant

Hmmno, this is even better. Kat threw aside her mausser, which made a dent in the hard dirt besides her. She gave a sickeningly sweet smile that would sour milk. Go on She stared MM down nonchalantly, an innocent, forgiving mask laced with the same evil smile shed worn so many times before.

Well, I oughta take this seriously.

If I can keep her grounded

Itll explode.

It was a deep, hollow BANG, and air rushed from the epicenter, knocking MM over into the lake, as well as mowing down a nice crop of trees. When the hell-rain had more or less ceased, she ran straight through the living gates of flames into the forest.
