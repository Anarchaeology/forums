General Quests
Now I havn't read the patch notes too well, and I'm not too up on the forums, but I had some ideas that I'm sure have been mentioned before.

From what I understand a lot of these NPCs walking around will now give quests, well what about the "Male High Commander"s? Or the "Shadow Mutant Blot"s? Will I be able to go upto a High Commander or a Watch Leader and get a mission to kill 20 leets?
Will I be able to make friends with ownz and go buy him a Burger or something?

I think having quests like this could add a lot to the game play, and ofcourse you would add general quests of all levels. This could be a good way to eventualy develop a friendship rating between yourself and a group of mobs, or guards.

I personaly don't mind missions but I would like to go outside more, and although this upcoming patch sounds very orientated for outside hunting, I think quests to guide you like this would add a lot more fun to it.
