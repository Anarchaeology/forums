A long night...
The rains fell hard against the concrete steps of the SPARTA HQ building in Rome Blue. The dark thunderous sky rolled the clouds across the moon. Inside Cordell sat at his new terminal reviewing the latest reports from field operatives across Rubi-Ka. His investigation into the murders of Irvin Scola and Mona Scales turned up nothing. He closed the case file making sure to save it to the mainframe computer. Cordell sat there a moment thinking. Slowly he began to fall asleep.

Lightning struck the courtyard in front of the Admin building jolting Cordell out of his light slumber. He shook the sleep from his eyes and looked back at his monitor. Cordell noted a small link blinking in red at him. He activated it. The silent alarm had been tripped. The link brought up a live tranmission via survalience camera. The intruder was sneaking through to the top levels...near the director's office. Cordell picked up his pistol and holder while bolting out his office door. Running up the four flights of stairs he tried to raise someone on his comlink with no success. Three flights. Two flights. One flight. The door.

Cordell drew his pistol from the holster and pressed his back up against the wall. He took hold of the door handle and slowly opened it. He slipped through the crack in the door and walked silently through the hallway. His heart was beating inside his chest so hard he thought the intruder would definatly hear his approach. He slowed his breathing as he was taught by his mentor Sezmra. He approached the end of the hallway. It split in two directions. Left and Right. Left led to Mr. Caris's office. Right to the Directors. He slowed and peaked his way around the corner, peering towards the Director's door. It had been opened.
Cordell checked his comlink again. Still no signatures were on. He was alone. He raised his pistol to heads level as he advanced towards the office door. As he did so he heard what sounded like rumaging. It was time. Again raising his pistol to bear he kicked open the door. Shocked the intruder looked up wide-eyed and immediatly dove behind the desk. Cordell let off two shots, one of them going out the window. He ran across the dark room and jumped on top of the Director's desk in time to see the intruder grid out. He looked at the window inspecting the newly placed hole. He could just barly feel the rain coming through. Good thing the Director was on leave. He looked back across the courtyard. An object caught his eye as the lightning flashed again. He stared at it, wondering what it was. Then it hit him. He ran.

The rain hit him coming out the doors as he sprinted across the courtyard. Standing at the edge of the platform he looked desperatly to reaquire the object. There!. Lighting flashed again and he saw the man walking quickly towards it. Cordell yelled to the guards to stop the man, who now at the sound of Cordell's voice, starting sprinting towards the object. Cordell was right behind him. As they both approached Cordell kicked his foot out infront of the fixer's right foot as it came back down to meet the pavement. The fixer, unanticipating, tried at the last second to break his fall by twisting his shoulder to take on the impact. Cordell past by him picking up the object and drawing his pistol. The fixer came back halfway to his feet and rushed Cordell, slamming his back into the wall. Cordell kneed him in the chest and smacked the butt of his pistol across the back of the fixers head. He went limp. The guards, catching up, drew up their rifles. Cordell showed the Corpral in charge of that nights watch his SPARTA identification card and badge.

"Check his com for identification" Cordell ordered.
"Yes, sir" the Corpral quickly snapped. The soldiers rolled the body over revealing the fixers pale opifex complection.
"Sir, I'm not getting anything" the Corpral reported.
"Hmmm" Cordell thought a moment. He nudged his foot against the mans torso. "Wake up........Wake up!" Cordell ordered.

The fixer groaned back into conciousness.
"Who are you?" Cordell asked.
The fixer only smiled and slowly started to reach towards his coat.
"My ID is in my coat pocket here".
"Slowly" Cordell responded.
Cordell aimed his pistol toward the fixer as he reached into his coat.
The fixer kicked the rifle out of one of the guards hand and tried to roll. Cordell fired a single fatal round to the forehead. The fixer was dead.

Cordell walked slowly back to the Admin building as the guards cleaned up the mess behind him. The rain clearly soaked his body. His clothes hung tightly around his body and rain dripped off the ends of his hair and nose. He continued walking as he studied the object which had been taken. Apparently he had shot it out of the fixers hand, but the material hadn't been damaged. The object was the Director's internal memory drive from his terminal. He walked up the steps of the building. "Damn" he thought, " I have to write a freakin report on this".
