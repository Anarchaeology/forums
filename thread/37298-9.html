Level Reqs
Okay I hope to consider myself a more balanced voice here because I'm one of the many ingame who isn't threatening to cancel their account over this issue (at least not right now).

I can understand level reqs with some of the nano skill buffs (mochams, wrangles) and in combination with the new fixer NCU buffs. Someone could use them together in such a way as to destroy AO's skill system that is rather universally loved in my opinion.

That said I think the WAY it was done was all wrong. I don't think a level req should ever be so low that you can't self-buff into the nano. I don't care what is happening to the game system in 6 months or 1 week, it shouldn't be in there NOW because as it is now I hate it. That was the first bad idea.

Second the skill levels they're set at are all wrong at least for early levels. What to give us something to look forward to? Well then don't set level reqs the same as skill caps - 15, 50, 100, 150 level reqs should immediately be thrown out for obvious reasons. By the time we overcome the skill cap hurdle, we'll no longer appreciate those nanos we could have used because we'll be looking forward to the next nanos that we can use suddenly with the new skill caps. It is has been said that you won't Nerf anyone again, well you're nerfing all of us by removing the importance of the skill system. Level reqs should only prevent exploits from power buffing, not make you wait for a nano till you no longer care about it.

This game started out with a vision, a vision which attracted me to the game and got me into it. I don't want you to lose it because it is what makes the game fun, but many things being thrown into some of these patches are making me think that the game developers have lost their vision.

Print these out and post them over the developers and managers desks please:

Anarchy Online has an evolving player driven 5 year STORY!
Anarchy Online has a SKILL based system
Anarchy Online has a SCIENCE FICTION based setting
AO has a two side conflict and each side has its unique Advantages

Then go through and read all the descriptions of the breeds and professions and make sure that each one is living up to it. When making changes to a profession, make sure it fits their description. The professions I'm glad to say feel like they're fitting their descriptions more with the last couple patches.

Cosmik and CZ, I know with your jobs you've got to play Devil's advocate to both the devs and AO community. Thanks, and I hope you can make the developers see how your customers want the game to be (Simply as promised).

I'm kind of sick of the self-buff mentality as well. And it goes for weapons and armor as well as nanos. My only point is that 'level' requirements should never, ever, come in the way of a player who really, really, wants to execute a nano and has the wherewithall and resources to do it. As long as he's not exploiting or otherwise playing outside the rules, then then let him have at it.

I would always bear in mind as a nano designer 3 key stages of a nano's use:

- Level they can execute with Mocham and Wrangle
- Level they can execute with Mocham
- Level they can (possibly) execute self-buffed.

Look at the nano and it's duration and effects. Combat nanos and heals can be more lenient with respect to the Wrangle. Long term buffs less so.

Nice example would be essence of Behemoth. Requires 700 BioMet and Matter Creation. Take off 132+140+20=192 right off the bat for common and easy to obtain buffs. So 508 BM and MC.

Then assume a Level 110 NanoMage Enforcer is implanting for Essence and has qlvl150 implants in. Disregard conflicts with other clusters since we're looking for 'worst case'. Gives +80 in BioMet and +160 in MattCrea if you favor MattCrea in the Shining Slot. That makes BioMet the gating skill for setting a level requirement. Take 160 off the 508 and we got 348 as the remaining skill needed to execute the nano. When can we do this? Find out and set the level requirement.

As an aside: yer gonna need around 200 NCU just for the Mocham's and Wrangle and Expertises. (This is where Buff-OE'ing was supposed to be 'balanced' but when the new game designers were hired after the original team was let go, they must not have read the original design document. So much for that idea with Carbonum and the new Fixer buffs). At lvl 110 our test case could easily have well over 300 NCU with Cyborg Armor and a moderate Fixer buff.

Back to our Enforcer searching for the Holy Grail of bodily enlargement.

At level 110 A NanoMage Enforcer with qlvl 150 implants favoring MattCrea maxing BM and MC and taking advantage of his teammate's Mocham's and Wrangles will have 701 BioMet and 781 MattCrea assuming he blows all his IP's on NanoSkills. If you don't believe it use the skill emulator yourself.

110 would be the level requirement for Essence of Behemoth (+996 Hitpoints, +27 STR/STA). It is completely and without too much difficulty quite possible to have the skill to execute that buff at lvl110. Earlier with bonuses from armor and NanoMage special items, but we're close enough.

Does anybody think this is unbalancing? I hope not. The poor fool jumped through hoops and probably gimped his character to be able to do this one trick. And for all the effort, I would hope we congratulate the skinny pasty faced nanomage on getting him some blood in his bones.

What would FunCom do with respect to level requirements on this particular Nano? Judging from what we see with the other new nanoformulas and the Servants of Eight armor, it would have a 'level' 195 requirement for some reason.

And there's the rub. We'd have a character on Rubi'Ka who did not cheat exploit or otherwise break the rules, who would be able to have the skill to execute a nano for 85 levels and would be prevented from doing so. This does not foster a sense of enjoyment in the game as you level for no other reason than to level.

This is the reason we think level requirements suck in the form they are currently in the game. They make a mockery of the skills system. They make it impossible to do things that you clearly have the skills to do as shown when you hit the 'U" button. 'Hmm .... this littly thingy sez it takes 700 BioMet. Well, I got 701. Must be something broke with this one.'
