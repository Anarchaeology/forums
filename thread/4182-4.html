My 2 Cents
For what it's worth here's my 2 cents.
(Whatever happened to the 'cents' symbol on keyboards?)

I think the IP machine that Snarf came up with is a great idea!

Here is the major change to the idea that I would make:
(Sorry Snarf, you know how it is, everyone wants to get in on the act.)

Instead of making the machine run off of a credit amount that is charged against you and limiting it to every 100 hours or so, what if we made it run on tokens.

You'ld only be able to recieve these tokens as a reward for a mission or as loot, they would not (as the TV commercial goes) be available in stores.

The tokens have a quality level, just like everything else, which limits the amount of IP you can reasign. For every QL you get to reasign 100 IPs. QL5=500 IPs, QL100=10,000 IPs and so on.

The tokens have specific skill names, limiting the TYPE of skill you can pull the IPs from. This could either target a specific skill, like navigation, or target a class of skills, such as with Nano & Aiding.

This would keep people from doing blanket reasignment of IPs, so it could be something you could work towards.

This does two things in my mind:

a) It alows the reasigning of IPs that I find alot of people have on there wish list. Now whether these IPs were spent incorrectly in the first place or were spent in areas that FUNCOM changed in some way that made it less attractive to the player is of little concern to me. What matters is that this might add new life to those characters that have been put on the shelf for one reason or another.

and

b) It adds to the wealth of the mission items that can be obtained. I have only played for a couple of weeks now and have a lvl 24 Nano-Tech and am getting kind of tired of the same reward crossing my path time after time.

I truelly enjoy this game and will probably play for a long time to come, even though I've never been into these MMPOLG (or whatever you call them) before, but I have NO interest in rerolling a new character using the same proffesion that I am now, if I am going to start a new character it's going to be of a different proffesion. So a system along these lines would alow me to work towards correcting mistakes that I made when I was but a n00b.

Just my 2 cents,
