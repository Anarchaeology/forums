An inside look into the dev's inside jokes
The dev's are Norwegian, and we can read a lot into what they put into a game like this one - if we know where to look.

So here's a few hints:

Rhompa Bar:
Obviously named after "Rompa Bar" (lit. Bare Ass), the old prime hangout of the Oslo MC club "Rabies", who later became a leading part of the Norway Hell's Angels chapter...

Stolt Jensenberg:
A rewrite of Jens Stoltenberg, former prime minister and current leader of Norway's Labour party (they're not too impressed with this guy, I think - just ask Stolt about atrox appendages, and u'll get the idea...)

Yuttos:
One of the songs they sing is called "Mil etter mil" (lit. Mile after mile), a song entered by singer Jahn Teigen in the infamous annual Eurovision Song Contest, which won Norway the first of many glorious zero point scores...

Be 2 Bar:
This 0 % bar in Wine is another plain rewrite, "Bar 2 Be", situated in a cellar in downtown Oslo, was known as a rather seedy place in its heyday in the late 80's and early 90's...

That's a few, there's got to be more. Come on, gutta, let's educate our international friends =)
