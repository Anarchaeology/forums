Go see the picture at AO-Basher.

Seems people are a little confused about all this.

This whole thing is little more than 'capture the flag'.

It's not guild housing. You aren't setting up a base. The towers are essentially 'MOB's' that you can fight and fight back. Some others might buff you like friendly MOB"s might do.

Think of them as those plant monsters. Direseeds, Parspalum's, etc. that your guild can put on the ground. While they sit there, they give bonuses to your guild members. Enemies can attack them for a couple hours every day.

I personally think this is PvP done right. As long as you don't have to put up a tower to remain competitive, this simply offers a way to signal to other people that your guild is open to the idea of PvP. They can now make the majority of the planet 75% gas. If you want to PvP, look for a tower that has dropped to 0% gas and has a group of people at the ready. Ganking becomes a thing of the past.

But, don't ever confuse this with anything more than Team Fortress Classic brought into AO.

Guild housing is later. I'd prefer it to be first as it would keep more people in the game than the towers will. But, the dev's got a hard on for PvP. And I'll admit, they might have got it right with this. Probably not as right as SW: G with their spawned 'battlefields'. But, it seems to be a very good concept.

They just have to ensure that the 'uber guilds' don't destroy everybody else's fun with all this. The number of people in your guild and the average level, as well as aggregate PvP points absolutely must be taken into account when deciding which guilds can attack which other guild's tower.

At a minimum, defense should be so friggin' boosted that it is a rarity to see someone's tower fall as long as a reasonable number of guildies are there to defend it. It should be the battle that is fun. Not knocking down someone's tower and pissing off 20, 30 or more people or causing guilds to dissolve over the political squabblings falling out of whose fault it was that the guild lost their tower.

These things could spell the end of many fine organizations if FunCom allows them to fall too easily or lets the super guilds wipe out all the small close knit groups of friends.
