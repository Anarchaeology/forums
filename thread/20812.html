Make IQ Rings and MA Attacks NOT Unique!
I find a nice IQ Ring in a mission I'm in. It is not as nice as the one I wear, though. So I pick it up and give it to a friend or sell it on the black market, right?

WRONG! It is unique and therefore I can't pick it up.

An MA goes on a mission for a new MA attack. Unfortunately he forgets to delete his MA attack before attacking the assassination target. Ooops, you already have the unique item and therefore you don't get your mission reward. And he might not have been able to complete the mission without the attack anyway.

Funcom, you threw this nerf through without including it in the patch notes and continue to ignore the dozen or so threads that talked about it. When you finally admitted in the next patch that it was in, every single person that responded to your thread hated the idea. Not only did the change stay but you didn't even both to lower yourself to respond to your customers! Change this or at least tell us why you can't!
