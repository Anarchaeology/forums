Attack of the Snake is nice if you aren't increasing any secondary MA skills (specifically parry and dimach). It runs off pure MA skill, and it recent was boosted so that it did more than 1 damage per hit. It also recharges fairly fast, you can occasionally get off two on the same mob in a fast team. This is a great low-level attack, and/or a great attack for the IP conscious. If you're using MA at all, you get this attack for no addition IP expense.

Bird of Prey requires that you raise a secondary skill or two, though the secondary requirement is low. I think it's Dimach but I can't seem to remember off-hand; it might be Brawl. Either way, it's also been boosted recently, and recharges quickly enough to be used at least once per fight in a fast team. If it uses brawl as the secondary, consider it an IP freebie, since you'd want to raise brawl anyway. Probably the best attack to be using in a fast team.

Blessed With Thunder requires the absolutely useless IP-hole named Parry. However, in terms of damage, especially damage over time, this is the best MA attack out there. After an initial damage, it also DoTs the target for 6 pulses, 10 seconds apart. It will stack with your nano DoTs, as well, and furthermore gives you a Melee damage shield. This is a great attack for soloing or for slow teams, and for bosses when you can land it.

Shen is now useless. Just don't bother.

Flower of Life is often overlooked, especially by us because of our heals. But it can be a great panic self-heal, especially in PvP, and it has an awesome animation. The downside is that it requires high Parry skill, which as I've said is a black hole of IPs.

New MA attacks are being added with MA-only tags. So things like Upon A Summer Wave and the upcoming 14.7.8 Tree of Enlightenment attacks we might as well forget about too.

You can only use one MA attack at a time.

You must wait for it to recycle to cast another attack (of whatever type)

I typically get off 2 BWT in a fight. The last one is a finisher, ussually, just one pulse.
