Roleplayers of Southern RubiKa
The South RubiKa Society is for you!

For people that love to Roleplay, we are a guild that involves you as much or as little as you want.

You can be as much of your own group or as individual in your roleplaying as you wish...


We do RP (Roleplaying) Missions for ours and other guilds which involes us setting out a story and a guild playing it out in any manner they see fit (and also involves an actualy mission area if wanted) among lots of other things


The Leet Fighting Debate is part of Savoy's excellent efforts (along with Tatjaana's of the FriendLeet Foundation)

and our own Oshovah got his own spiritual beliefs aired by the assassin Aberic in his trial (the planet being as a living entity)

We have Father Chagidiel and his ever expanding Church of Omni-Tek who has become very influential and a part of the Rubi-Ka political scene


and we have Rubi-Ka's most famous Tailor, the fashion of armour people need at the lowest prices, the one and only Tappert.


We also have many other people in the guild, like Ddraig, the shadowy information broker and Heksa the assassin, Sadie Flange the tart (her words, not mine)
Shallag, the popular Omni-Ent socialite,
and me, Lev Rivers, professional assassin, at your service.


So join today and experience Rubi-Ka's most enthusiastic and encouraging Roleplaying Guild
