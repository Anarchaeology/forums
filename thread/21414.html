Give Traders A Pet!
Okey, I think FunCom has done a good job on traders now, making them quite good. They got good guns, calm and root, heals, debuffs, wrangles, and warp to grid nano, but I feel something is missing.

Give traders a pet!

It should be called "Traders Best Buddy", and have some of the following abilities:

It should be an excellent fighter, just like the trader wielding a shotgun. It should have better heals then the trader (it's a pet, of course it should), and it should be able to both heal itself and its owner at the same time. It should also be constantly area-mezzing every hostile NPC or player inside the same zone as itself, and have 1000k HP. It should also have a yalmaha, plus the ability to fly over obstacles in missions so it doesnt get stuck. And when it dies, it should explode like a kamikaze, insta-killing every living object inside the 3 closest zones.

I agree that I might be a bit modest in my suggestion, but I feel that we shouldn't get to overpowered cause that would be unfair to the other classes.

BTW, this pet should be ql 1 so that all traders can enjoy it.

And when I am at it, give the traders a nano to summon special mobs that have not spawned... F.ex. so they can go to Clon**** and spawn Nuggets by casting the nano at any time.








FUNCOM, TRADERS ARE OVERPOWERED! DO SOMETHING!

But don't nerf them! I am an agent and know how that feels! Make the other professions better! In the description of a trader on the website from BETA-days, you (FunCom) said that traders would not be any good at fighting, but "could defend theirselves if necessery". Well if that is your definition of not being any good at fighting, I would very much like to see how you envisioned f.ex. soldiers.
