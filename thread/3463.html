Why a Nano Pool buff is a bad idea; Why Humidity Extractors are the key (Poll)
I posted this as a reply to another thread but the issue is very heated right now. I thought it deserved a thread of it's own so that comments on it could be directed solely at the NP Buff vs Increasing HE usefulness. Sorry for the repeat but I believe it is warranted.


Having double nano is not the same as having a quickly refreshed and highly valuable NF cast in your NCU such as a Humidity Exctractor. Here is why.

-----

Double Nano Hypothetical:

 Longer time before you need to use rechargers yet longer down time while waiting to use them. The only advantage is that you can go longer in 1 fight without recharging, and then it still doesnt help because you spend more time sitting and waiting for your nano to recharge. Your down time is greatly increased.

 More rechargers! This solution is still highly dependant on rechargers and will eventually cost you more (not the same) in rechargers in the long run.

 Nullify Humidity Extractors! That's right. At my current level Humidity Extractors are ALMOST a waste of time because they don't give enough nano and don't work fast enough. It is always the second NF to go if I need the space (first is range increaser especially in missions and groups). If there was a nano pool buff you might as well never cast HE because instead of it refilling 3% of your total nano every 15 seconds, it will refill 1% of your total nano every 15 seconds. Do you want to wait 25 minutes for your nano to fill?

 Takes up VALUABLE NCU space. I don't have any NCU because 2 of my deck slots are filled with non-NCU essentials! That leaves me 4 slots to put rare as hell NCU chips into. As of now, I have to cut back all the time to make room for new buffs. I can't even upgrade certain things because of NCU space.

-----

Increased HE Speed and Nano per Tick Hypothetical.

 MUCH longer time before you need to use a recharger. You have a constant flow nano coming at you now. It's not quit enugh to give you infinite nano because you burn it much faster then it comes, but it definatly increases your total pool at a steady rate.

 MUCH Less Recharging! Save money! You will always need rechargers, especially while fighting reds in teams but at least you can save yourself DOWN TIME and MONEY if you opt to sit for a minute and let your nano recharge. While somebody else is pulling you are regaining your nano every tick the tank is away. No more stopping EVERYBODY's exping because you burned 500 nano chargers in 45 minutes.

 Humidity Extractors they way they are supposed to be! They are *barely* worth the NCU now, if they changed em they would be beyond worth it. Everything would have to go before I killed my HE.

 NO extra NCU. It will be the same as always.

-----

NANO is an NTs blood. Forget HP we have none. Forget AC we have none. NANO is what keeps us alive and it is the most important factor in an NTs life. We use it more then any other class in the game. While an Pool buff fixes some of the problems some of the time, a revamp of how HEs work the way i describe would fix all problems all of the time. Nobody has been able to prove otherwise.

I agree with Lucid Flow. In general what he has said is a good idea. It is much better to have a better HE than a nano pool buff.

Let me give a rough example to try and explain.

I am currently fighting red mobs with at least 6000hp, probably alot more. But for this example I will say 6000.
I am using Ol'faithful currently with roots and calms to recharge which I often have to do 3 or 4 times per fight.

ok so leaving roots aside I need at least 11 Ol'faithful's to kill this red

I am of course going into the fight fully charge with nano.

594 * 11 = 6534 damage ( I mostly get min damage)
at 197 per use thats a total of 2167 nano

on top of this I would also have to root and bewilder so the total nano I use is a lot more especially as bewilder takes about 3 attempts to work.

the total time to use those 11 nukes say 5 seconds each (4 attack 2.89 recharge) is 55 seconds.

my current nano pool is only 1700.

so even without roots I can cast about 8 nukes. Of course in a fight you cant cast till empty or you dont have that 300 to use for upto 3 calms.

Ok so supposing I am being reckless and cast till empty
8 ol'faithfuls will take me 40 secs.
with the current HE (i use 51 point) I get 100 nano back. wow really useful!!!

ok now if that HE gave me 51 points every 5 seconds I get 408 nano back.
So with that 408 nano I can now cast another 2 Ol'faithfuls another 10 seconds worth. That is now a total of 10 ol'faithfuls for exactly the same nano pool. and in those 10 seconds I will get another 100 nano back. Whislt not enough for another major nuke, it is good enough for a root.

So by just increaseing the effect of HE I can now almost take out the mob with no recharging during the fight at all.

Now if we try the same with a nano pool buff we get to follow.

I start the fight with my normal nano. I then see this red mob. I use my nano pool buff to increase my nano pool by 500 points (or whatever).
Ok now I have to sit and recharge. That is one extra recharge.
I now have around 2200 nano 500 points more. But wait didnt I just get 500 extra nano in the previous example with increased HE. Yes I did and for that I did not have to sit down and recharge. So it must be obvious to everyone that I have just used 1 extra recharge. That cost me money.
When I run out of nano I now have to recharge 2200 points, to stay fully topped up, That means using more nano rechargers, that means more money.
With increased HE my nano pool stays the same and yet I still get the equivilant of a 2200 nano pool and that costs me nothing extra at all. it is FREE.

The end effect is the same. But one costs me money and one doesnt. I know which one I would rather use.

Now that was just a small example. Just how much and how fast they make the HE will obviously be tricky. To fast and you will never have to use nano rechargers, to slow and it will be no use at all.
