Actually, I think the pathfinding code works fine... as long as you're right next to the server. Pet pathfinding is dependent on lag and the speed at which you run at. Right now, your client updates the server only every so often. More if you zigging and zagging, less if you're moving straight.

The pet (at least my experience with an engie bot) tries to trace over your steps. If you move fast enough though, it'll lose that lock and figure it's own path. Do it even faster, and your next update could actually put you well out of the pet's path range and it'll go into roam mode.

Even if the pets effectively travel at the same speed as you, certain conditions can make your position updates far between and put the pet into roam. It's a logic problem in their code and sadly one that doesn't show up if they're testing it locally. I hope someone from FC can pass this along to the dev team as people here aren't hallucinating, and no, pathfinding isn't totally working - at least when you take into account network concerns.


With that said, there is an interm solution for those having difficulties with their bots. First, you can try strafing left and right (keys z and c?). Tapping your movement keys instead of constantly holding them down also works. Both of these methods update the server with your position. Note that this isn't 100% guaranteed since it's ultimately up to the client code to determine when it wants to send the position packets, but you can try to nudge it along.

I'm not totally sure if it'll work at the high speeds you're travelling at Wenzell and I'd appreciate any feedback on whether it does. In missions, you can also try walking with short straight sprints, as ironically, that keeps you from 'confusing' the pet.


I hope that helps and I hope it gets fixed soon so we don't have to rely on these tricks.
