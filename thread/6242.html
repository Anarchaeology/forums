An IP reset, and why some of us NEED it.
I don't think it's much of a secret to Funcom why there is a large population of it's players that NEED an IP reset at this point.

I'm not talking a gamewide IP reset, I'm talking a simply command that allows you to reset it once.

Take my character for example.


Level 105 Advent, I created him on day 2 of release. I've stuck with this character through EVERYTHING.

1. I picked pistols, because yes .. they started me with them.

2. Freedom Arms where the best at one point, then they where removed.

3. When FA's where removed, they also made the Multi-Range skill really cheap.


How does that screw a character up you might ask?


Well, First off.. they upped all the multi-range req's on ALL pistols.


Secondly, by removing the FA's .. pistol users had to turn to other models. Rendering Ranged Energy (dark blue skill) useless.

There goes a ton of IP.

Multi range turning cheap? ... Another huge chunk of IP gone, that we'll never see again.


Now to train a decent pistol, I had to start using Desert Reets. Which use burst.

Burst is dark blue for Advents, so there's another IP sink.


This has left my character, and COUNTLESS other characters severely broken. We have problems keeping up with people 5 levels below ours.

Now if I had been an idiot and simply spent this points unwisely, I would be ok with it .. and live with it.

This was not the case.


Another fine example is concealment. My character currently has almost 850 concealment buffed, yet I can't sneak an even lvl mission.

How can we make money?


I'm *just* speaking for my class, how many other class's in the game would benefit from this? .. How much EASYER would it be to balance a game when all the characters at least had a fair shot to become equal if not better than the characters around them?


I didn't start playing this game early to have a gimped character.



Please address this Funcom, and please do something about it.

I noticed you removed your statement that you where looking at it again.

Do your original chracters a favor, and cut us a little slack here.

1. Gimped original characters, people play these characters thinking they might be able to stick to one profession/character for their duration in this game. And NOT have to reroll. These characters have lost THOUSANDS of points of IP to nerfs and adjustments that should have taken place pre-release. These characters can barely function if even at all.

2. Tweaked original characters - These characters have been screwed out of all the same IP as the type given in example 1. These people have basicly tweaked out their character despite missing THOUSANDS of points in IP. If extreamly lucky these people can become AVERAGE. You will not see any Advents created in the first week doing well for a shining example.

3. Characters created post-nerf - These characters are probably anywhere from 5 to 10 times as powerful as either 1 or 2. They can effectivly solo missions, and do many things examples 1 and 2 only WISH they could do.



So funcom, how are you going to balance ANYTHING when you have that kind of a system in place?

How can you say anything is good for the population of the game, when your own stats must be vibrently incorrect?


The answer?


A one-time IP reset. Something that would allow us to regain that LOST ip. I'm happy with all the placement of the ip I've placed myself.

I'm not happy with what you've done to my weapons (Pistol) And I'm also not happy with the result of your "fix".

Give us a one-time IP reset so we can fix YOUR MISTAKES.

It isn't quite THAT simple... an IP reset would be a major, a HUGE step. I had a doc who dualed FAs. Suddenly, I was in the same boat. I ended up giving up on my main char and rerolling a new one. I've finally gotten back to above my previous level, asnd it is really a much better char.

The problem with an IP reset is that it would be abused to no end. Every character has misallocated IP; the worst is that you lose a few levels, which isn't crippling, considering that people have been known to max out chars fairly quickly.

Yea, I really wish that I could reallocate a bunch of IP on my character, but the drawbacks from IP reallocation would outweight the benefits.

On the plus side, look at it this way; eventually, something else will be nerfed, everyone will have wasted IP, and you might even be able to use something new that you wouldn't otherwise be able to. :-)

I tend to agree with kalris, enforce a server wide IP wipe, give people notice, have them remove everything (armor, weapons, token boards, implants, etc) and assign a time where the wipe will happen (or better yet, for those people who happen to be away from the game and don't get the notice in time, have an auto-announce message pop up when they log in that they have 1 hour to get everything out of their wear window and the IP reset will commence automatically). Of course to stop a massive uproar, funcom will have to explain a way for non-removable ring (i.e. IQ rings) to safely remove those items without destroying them.

Then, as an incentive to promote long term play (funcom marketing types listen up), I would offer a 1-time only IP reset at title lvl 4 (lvl 100), put in something in the level up message where it says your title has changed, so that the player can think about it and activate the reset anytime during level 100 but if the player decides to proceed to the next level he has waived his option for a wipe. This will give people incentive to stick with a character that they may have screwed up experimenting with different routes during the developemental stages of their career and with a server wide IP reset those already over lvl 100 will in effect already have had their chance to reallocate their IP points so its not unfair to them either, and future new players that will be critical to this game's financial success will have reason to put more than an initial month in. And most important of all, make this feature KNOWN, so that people who are working through it can look forward to it and not just stumble upon it. Information is critical.
