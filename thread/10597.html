Together
The fighting I saw at Stret West may have seemed typical. There were there were clansmen. There were neutral. There were Omnis. This may seem like a typical scene, Omni , neutrals , and clans in Stret, fighting, however, there was a difference. That difference was not THAT they were fighting, it was WHO they were fighting. Not each other, but the Dust Brigade. The scum of RubiKa. The single biggest impediment to peace. Yes, even more so than OmniReform and extremist Omnis, the Dust Brigade. They must be removed from RubiKa and, from what I saw tonight, it the first combined effort to protect the future of RubiKa since Sol Banking and TruSpace invaded and OmniTek, or for that matter, the clans couldn't defend RK alone. They had to cooperate. They had to cooperate tonight and they did. Together, we fought terrorism. Together, we fought evil. Together, we fought for the right of our children, on ALL sides, to live. Together, we fought. If we can do this, why are we still fighting each other? Why are my children not safe as long as OmniTek has as much power as it currently has? It is because of that power. No one was fighting for power tonight. They were fighting evil. There was no intent in the warriors hearts tonight other than eradicating evil. The only reason we fight, is because our purpose is other than eradicating evil. Our purpose, most of us, is to gain power. There seem to be few who think as I do. Who think as Breogan do. There are few who believe that there can one day be a democratic RubiKa that includes OmniTek as a company, not a government. Please, if you are one of those Omnis who try to bring me the argument that democracy is not safe and will bring down the ICC, do not come to this discussion. What you say is not true. While a company may be more efficient, a democratic government would be more beneficial. Sure it would have its bureacracies and its corrupt political figures, you can never rid yourself of those, but we CAN make it so that they are the minority, not like we currently are, where they are the majority. We can make RubiKa democratic and include OmniTek. If we can fight together against evil, we can work together against evil. We can turn our intents to destroy each other onto destroying tyranny. We can set an example. If we can fight together, we can work together. We can build a glorious democratic example, a beacon to the rest of the universe. We can be a great nation, united and strong. We could turn our work to tame the countryside and truly make peace on RK. We can consolidate our forces to protect ourselves from other corporate tyranny, such as Sol Banking and TruSpace. Perhaps, if OmniTek could do this on all its planets, they could change the universe. They could make the universe a better place for billions of people. OmniTek could bring peace to millions of planets by simply relinquishing some power. After they do this, they will be insured there rights to produce and provide whatever they need to do to thrive. The democracies that could be and OmniTek could work together. They could fight the extremists, the warmongers, the power hungry daemons on ALL sides. This could happen. As I said before, if we can fight together, we can work together.
