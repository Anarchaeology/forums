What is the official stance on bugged nanos?
Even though its possible to come up with a flimsy explanation for their existance (prototype, etc) its obvious these are bugs. So technically, taking advantage of a bug is an exploit. But they have been around forever. One of the following must be true:

* FC doesn't know about them

Very unlikely

* FC knows about them and plans to fix them

Who knows, this is why I'm asking.

* FC knows about them and won't fix them

Again, who knows. If this is the case, they should come out and say so.

Lets face it, everyone uses bugged nanos. I use them, you use them, there is no real stigma attached to using them. The problem is after level 100, all people do is play "find the item mission" for uber high level crap, so there are tons of bugged crystals floating around. I don't think its realistic to expect players to simply resist the temptation of using them. "Psst, wanna upload this crystal instead of booth camping for 2 hours and dieing in Belial Forsest three times?" "Ok!"
