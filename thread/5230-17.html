fixers operate the black market, we are the black market.
I must say I disagree somewhat with you on this. From my knowledge of the Fixer profession beyond AO, from CP 2020 to be more specific, the Fixer was the guy with the connections.
He could get you what you wanted through his contacts on and beyond the blackmarket, but the market itself consisted of anything from petty criminals to white collar ones. Even sales agents from obscure and even well known arms manufacturers working for the companies "less known" department.
The "middle man" was what I pictured the fixer to be in AO as well.

As for the agents role on the black market, consider the fact that the black market is also a place for trading information as well as merchandise. And we are, after all spies, informants.

What I see the blackmarket as, is basically a collection of contacts. Yes, there are probably none fixer people involved, but they would all have their fixer contact to "plug them in". The fixers are the middle men, the link between the suppliers and the customers. Each fixer would probably have his on suppliers, and would also have contacts with other fixers, so if he couldnt get hold of somthing, he would "know a man who can". For an Agent to infiltrate this, it would require them to both pursade the established fixers that he was genuine, and to have his own collection of suppliers and contacts (if he doesnt have this, he is just a customer, and should buy something off the fixer).
