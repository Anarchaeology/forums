Hint to FC
I've been thinking (rare thing) that to enhance the experience of a story going on in the game it self FC could use the short clips of movies they let us see as clues of things to come, and do. The news as well for that matter.

If it where to be mentioned a place, or a person, or an item that would make people team up in large groups and head out in the wilderness to explore, and obtain, it would be very positive.

And if this again would inflict on the storyline, (maby giving new ideas of things to do?) players would think themselves more a part of the storyline going on around them.

AO is after all a sosial game I think, and this would bring players together in another way than a regular mission, hunt or attack would. It would be a "new dimension" to the game.

Anyone else got any ideas on how to get the storyline more a part of the game ?
