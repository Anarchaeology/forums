Noer seems to be the only one getting the point.

Everyone seems to automatically assume the scopes are overpowered when in fact they are not. The reason i'm opposed to the nerf is not because my godhood is taken away, but the fact that using my favourite gun (the ithaca) will no longer be viable.

To take an example: If I were to fight a mob selfbuffed with my ithaca and scope, I would be hitting for crap damage the most of the hits, but every once in a while a crit would come along and make up for all the crappy hits. Now if I changed to a HD and possibly a TIM scope I would do much better regular damage and not be so reliant on the crits, and the crits I do get would be higher than with the ithaca. The bottom line is it all evens out, heck I'm sure the HD/TIM scope combination would come out a bit better even.

Throwing in crit buffs would increase the crit for both scenarios, so that doesn't make a difference.

I do however realize that in some combinations the crit scopes may be perceived as overpowered, but nerfing the scopes all over the line will only hurt those people not using them in a such a fashion, and as Noer states will make a whole line of guns not viable.

I also agree that it sucks that they don't drop anymore making them hard to get. A better solution I think would be to make them drop again and adjust who can use them and in what combinations, but that I guess would be too much work for FC and nerfing them entirely is just so much easier.
