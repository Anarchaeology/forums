Benjacrat boards the shuttle outbound from Rubi-Ka.
Benjacrat activates his PoliticProtector Bot.

Hi Hubert.

Hello, Master.

Hubert, the time has come my Mechanical misfit friend.

I'm sorry Master, I dont understand.

Its ok Hubert. I just wanted to say goodbye...

Master?

Yes, Hubert?

Does this Goodbye mean forever?

I dont know Hubert. It seems that I am not welcome on this planet anymore.

I see, Master.

Unfortunately I cant take you off planet without taking some Notum with me, and I cant afford to buy any. So you will stay stored in the NanoCrystal until I find a way to bring you out.

Yes, Master.

You have been a great help Hubert. I hope this is not the last time I see your Physical form.

Me too, Master.

Goodbye Hubert.

Goodbye Master.

Hubert disappears in a swirl of white nanobots, not to be seen or heard from for a long time...

What a day this has been...

Benjacrat looks at the UT on his NCU interface and sees that it is almost time for his shuttle to depart.

Well...Lets get going...

Benjacrat grids to Omni-1 and boards an outbound shuttle to return when the planet has calmed...
