Missions, Missions, Missions
OK, so we all know that in the next patch you will be getting more tokens based upon your title level (hopefully). I realize that the people responding here are probably not the people who would enjoy this feature as much as others but still here it goes.

I think there should be a few more slider bars on the mission selection menu. There should be one for length/tokens. So that if you only have 30 minutes to play, you can still do a mission, as opposed to getting 1/6 of the way through a 3 hour-long mission. It could be as simple as:
Longer |---------+-----------| Shorter
or
"x" Tokens |--------------+-------------| 1 token where x is based upon your title level.


And there should be another slider for how far away the mission is. We all know that at higher levels its not hard to get to your missions cause u grid/whoompa/fly. But what about 75% of the user-base who DON'T have yalms? They gotta run. (as do I ... ) Running 2 missions sux. So does driving. I think there should still be in-city missions over lvl 50, and yes they shouldn't be multi-token missions, as they are short & close by. But this would help the average person who can't sit down & play AO for 3 hours a day just 2 do a mission. The slider would still be easy to program & implment, something like:


Far Away |----------------+-----------------| Same Zone



Thats just my 2 cents. Bump if you agree or flame me, whatever. I don't care, so long as I can get some-other input.
