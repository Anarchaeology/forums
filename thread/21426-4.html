Actually the way concealment/perception checks work in PvP makes perception much more powerful than concealment. If you have enough perception to have any chance at all of detecting someone in sneak mode then you will. In order for anyone to successfully enter sneak mode against another player or mob that has enough perception to detect him, then the player wanting to enter sneak mode must enter sneak mode outside of that player's/mob's passive search radius. If you enter sneak mode when there is another player that can see you on the map or tab target you then that other player gets a perception check on your concealment skill. If you fail the perception/concealment check then you aren't concealed against that player. If you succeed then you are concealed against that player and the other player can only do another perception/concealment check on you when you enter that player's detection radius. Once in that player's detection radius that player has a chance of detecting you every 5 seconds.

In short anyone can become invisible to anyone else if you go into sneak mode outside of everyone's visual range (tab target range on the map also known as your passive serch radius). Once this is done you will be invisible to that player until you enter that player's search radius and that player does a search for you. The larger your (active search) detection radius is, then the better the chance you will be able to detect someone concealed will be.

Now for the agent side of things when attempting to do an aimed shot in mission areas. Passive search goes through walls and the perception of mobs in missions are maxed or better in comparison to what a player would actually raise their perception to at that level. In general if I see a mob in the mission area on the map (using people/monster map upgrade) then I will be unable to become concealed against that mob. Once I enter the (active search) detection radius of that mob then the mob will attack me. Luckily for me active search doesn't appear to go through walls between one room and the next room and it also appears to be linked to line of sight as well to some degree. Further testing on our chance of successfully entering sneak mode in a mission area is needed.

The concealment/perception check outside of a mob's detection radius needs to have its percent chance to detect reduced. After all concealed players outside of a mob's detection radius should be much harder to detect...especially through walls. Passive perception is the main reason why we can't use aimed shot in mission areas most of the time.
