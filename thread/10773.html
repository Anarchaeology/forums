A question about 'cars'.
Anyone else drive a land vehicle here? Anyone else wanna know who replaced your engine with that wound up rubber band under the hood?

Here's the skinny... I have 130 in my run speed, my car gives me 245 bonus run speed when equipped for a total of 375 run speed. My friend has a run speed of 126. When in my car and running along side of him I barely (and I mean BARELY) creep past him in speed. Now seems to me that with more than three times the run speed, I should be going about three times faster than him in a dead run.

If that weren't enough... Here's another little kicker to think about. My friend also has a Yalmaha, it gives him 242 bonus to his run speed. When he equips his car, and I equip mine I have a total run speed of 375 and he has a total run speed of 368. If he and I start out even at a dead run he is literally out of my sight in open ground in less than 3 full seconds. So the kicker is, even though I have higher movement speed than he does I will still get left in the dust in a flat race.

Synopsis... Here are a few lines of thought I'd suggest if your nearing the level to purchase or start finding vehicles in missions. 1. Never spend your IP on Ground Vehicle skills, those points will increase your speed M U C H more if you put them into the Run Speed skill.
2. Never spend your money on a Ground Vehicle, you would be simply wasting your hard earned cash seeing as you'd need to be saving it up for a vehicle that will actually ADD to your speed.
3. Saving and/or spending the IP on your Run Speed may actually be better in the long run (seeing as how Ground Vehicles move incredibly slow) since that run speed may actually add to air vehicles.
4. Either Run Speed does not add to your total speed when driving a Ground Vehicle, Ground Vehicle's listed speeds is incorrect, or Ground Vehicles are bugged as a whole and SHOULD be going as fast as they are listed.

Please feel free to copy and paste this in any forum you all think this might get a bit of attention, I'd be quite happy if this were given attention by the Dev team or any players were able to avoid the loss of cash and or IP due to Ground Vehicles.

A little more testing.
We did a little more testing, this time with another friend of ours who has a MID (Since that vehicle sucked so outrageously bad we'll not even include it's performance here). Roads seemed to make absolutely no diffference in move speed at all, we checked this by visual trial (running side by side on and off the road 10 or 11 times), and by timing how long it took us to travel from a stone near the road to a building on a straight stretch of road. Each timed drive got us to the building in 23 seconds on the road, and 23 seconds off the road (we actuall went a good distance off of the road to allow for any kind of terrain/texture type crossover that could have affected the results).

We also tested the 'curvature of the land' theory in Newland desert just last night. We found a stretch of land that took us over two moderate inclines, this test was quite a bit harder because we had to find an equal distanced travelling point that would not take us over hills. Even though the measurements on this test may be off by an estimated foot to three foot at most, we couldn't find that travelling over hills caused any significant difference in movement either. Both series of tests got us from our starting point to the skull totem in 31 seconds.

We are currently levelling a character for the sole purpose of getting a natural runspeed to match the current improved runspeed of our Kodiak. Hopefully this will let us know whether or not a pure run speed can match or beat an improved vehicular run speed.

Overall the testing isn't so much to dispute whether or not vehicles are useful, but whether or not you are getting the most bang for your buck (and IP). I personally can even say that due to the speed attributed me by my Kodiak I've been able to escape death by vultures no less than three times. On the issue of Air Vehicles I would tend to agree with the posters that stated there may be a hidden or unspecified 'bonus' to movement. Not to mention the sheer usefulness of being able to fly over terrain and buildings instead of having to go around them.
