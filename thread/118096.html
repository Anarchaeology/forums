General Nano to scale back Twink PvP
New players hate twinked characters running amok in low level PvP and a number of older/experienced players don't like it either. Fact is that twinked tower-destroying character really harm the enjoyment of a lot of lower level players.

However, we don't want to destroy twinking, as it is something that can be fun. Rather I think most of us agree that we should try to limit how much damage a twink character can do. My suggestion is to strike at the buffs running, particularly the ones that consume large chunks of NCU.

New General Nano Idea (castable by anyone)

NCU Overload
Nano Cost: 25
Range: 25m
Attack: 1s
Recharge: 0.5s
Nano Resist: 5%
Use: Sensory Improvement >= 31
Psychological Modification >= 31

Effects:
If target level =< 10 removes all buffs with NCU >= 15
If target level =< 25 removes all buffs with NCU >= 20
If target level =< 50 removes all buffs with NCU >= 25
If target level =< 75 removes all buffs with NCU >= 30
If target level =< 99 removes all buffs with NCU >= 40


Characters level 100 or more would be immune. Some of the smaller buffs such as fixer HoTs will be generally missed, but RRFE, Behemoth, GSF will be hit. Also, I can't think of any contemporary buffs that would be hit (are there any 15 NCU+ buffs a level 8-15 can reasonably cast?).
