Summary of the Pains of Pat.

Uber pistol. For engs and advs and crats these are to die for.

While wrangled and buffed and implants it burst upto 4.3k with just over 1070 attack rating. Crits for over 3k. Normals shots from 700-1.2k

I been playing with it since pistol conflicts with my MC and I would have to sacrifice a MC implant to ensure I dont OE. With the worse case which I been testing just a moment ago with 75% OE......I do from 400-1k normal(like a V3) Crits for 1.9k(simliar to a v3) and bursts so far up to 2.3k. My impression at 75% OE is that its just a little better than a V3 in normal damage.....but with burst and fling.

I have to play with it more and what my guild bonuses etc I can get to work the best combo but I may go back to 100% and just lose out on a MC implant and get a mochams.
