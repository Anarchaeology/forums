Fixer Discussions/Week 1 - Burst
I'm going to start running a weekly discussion starting every Monday on a Fixer function, be that a hack or something related to weapons.

Since some people are talking about Burst this week, we can start with that.

So here we go.

Burst: Most common fixer special attack.

Higher skill = Higher average burst, as well as max burst.

Burst is the primary special attack for mostly all smg's. The exception of this would be (example) a gun like the Suppressor, which does not have a strong burst.

Strong bursting weapons come in 3 basic styles, depending on the purpose for which you will use them.

Style 1: A strong bursting weapon will have very high non critical damage. Example, 1-750(100). This gun would have a MASSIVE burst potential because of the incredibly high average damage. I think you will find, the more average damage a gun has, the slower it is, so be careful about selling yourself on a slower gun too early.

Style 2: Another example of a strong bursting weapon would be one which the burst recycle is very fast, such as the Mausser Chemical Streamer.

Style 3: Finally, any gun that has a reasonable burst potential, a good (yet not uber such as the manex catastrophe line) average damage and has elemental damage. An example of this would be the OT Kerans line.

Suggestions:

Don't limit yourself or "lock" yourself to a single school of bursting weapon. Sometimes, you will find that doing other damage types can be very beneficial to you.

Overly fast guns, with very low minimum damage and extremely high burst recharge are pretty much worthless in practical application, no matter how good they look on paper.

If you use burst as a primary attack for your fixer, implant it where ever possible, without giving up mg/smg skill. Most fixers use the bright cluster for burst and forget the other two.

Just for reminders:

[Time to recharge Burst] =
[Recharge Time of the gun] * 20 + [Burst Delay]/100 - Burst / 25

The Recharge Time is the "original" recharge time : not modified by Ranged Init.
The Burst Delay is proper to each gun. You can find it in Jayde's db.
The Burst Delay is far from being proportional to the Recharge Time.
