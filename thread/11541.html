Things are starting to get boring
I live in a time zone that when I play would be for most the early hours of the morning. I am now on my 4th character that is almost level 80 now.

I logon and LFG for about an hour. If I cannot find a group I logoff and try again the next day. Now that the amnesty is over the spots I can hunt at (at least the ones I know about) do not have many people LFG at anymore at least not when I am on.

Now most would say, "If you cannot find a group do a mission". I have done too many missions since I have been playing AO. Faced with a choice of either doing another mission or say defrosting the fridge, I would defrost the fridge.
I am so sick of the doing the same missions over and over and over and over again. If you want a decent reward doing a mission then you need to group although if I had a group the last thing I would do is a mission.

What do I think could be done to fix this?

(1) Make it worthwhile to hunt solo. Increase the xp gained if you are hunting alone; give the greens the same sort of xp you would get from a yellow.

(2) Healers - My 2nd character is a Doctor I figured that if I were a doc then getting a group would not be a problem. True getting a group was not a problem but making money and staying alive was a big problem. I couldn’t solo a gray mob and if I healed someone I would get aggroed and most likely die. When I would go group hunting I would go though 70k worth of nano packs and bring home 20k worth of loot.
Make it so docs can afford to hunt, maybe give them HE or something so they do not burn so much money in nano packs.
My doc got nerfed big time as it was using dual wield FA's, so many ip points wasted :P

(3) Missions. Why would I want to join another 5 players doing a mission for no reward? As the missions take so long to do there is no way we would stick together long enough so all 6 of us can get a mission done.

All I really want is to logon play for a few hours and have some thing to show for it rather that just sitting at 20k saying "79 trader lfg" every 5 mins for 2 hours.
