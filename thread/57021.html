Nt Nano Program
Had a thought today. A new NT nano.

This nano is to used in conjunction with Whompa technology.

Its called "One way trip". The NT targets the whompa doors of a specific whompa that does not have a destination. Once the doors are targeted the NT runs the program and a window pops up. The NT types in the county and coords, chooses yes and has 5 secs for the NT and/or team members to step into the whompa. Anyone else that steps through at that time will also end up zoning into the same area.
No zoning into dungeons or major cities is allowed. Towns that you don't need to zone into are fair game to zone into. Harry's and Galway castle for example would be fair game.

This gives a little more travel ability to the NT. And lets the NT have an area of expertise that some of the other classes have not saturated. It really seems like NT's should be able to get around more.
