Complete (?) MA Bug List.
(An updated version of this buglist can be found on page 2 in this thread. I didn't want to edit this one, since people commented on the original post.)


Well then, heres my attempt to put together the complete MA Bug List. Please fill in on what Ive missed.
(Ill fill in my personal opinions and some explaining within paranthesis below)


MA Special Attacks:
Attack of the Snake (AoS): Does not work over QL100.
Bird of Prey (BoP): Requisites seem to be the same whether its a QL20 or a QL200. So is the damage.
Blessed with Thunder (BwT): Stated by Funcom not to be working correctly, as the DoT part is doing too much damage, and the reflect shiled seems to be a bit over the top sometimes.
Angel of Night (AoN): Requisites seem to be the same whether its a QL20 or a QL200. So is the damage.
Flower of Life (FoL): Hurts the MA using it, is believed to be a lifetap attack.
Bright, Blue, Cloudless Sky (BBCS): Well, the damage seems a bit low for the duration of locking MA skill, no?
Shen: No personal experience here, but I assume it to be the big brother of BwT, therefore facing the same fate.

(The damage output on these fellas was fine and dandy to about lvl 50ish. Over that, its kinda embarrassing to hear teammembers ask you what was that 1 point nanobot damage? Hm. Anyway from MA specials we move on to the famed: )


Book of Knowledge:
Currently doesnt work.

(Its rumoured that it would be a special little bag for us MAs, so we could use MA specials from the book instead of having them in our inventory. Therefore (at least I do) we have 3 or 4 items in the inventory taking up the much needed space. Add to this the hints of more MA special attacks, and we have one full inventory indeed.)


Weapon wielding:
If an MA zones with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA buffs his MA skill with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA runs out of an MA buff with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA opens the skill menu with a weapon wielded, his fists cease to function.
If an MA is debuffed by a trader with a weapon wielded, his fists sometimes cease to function.

(This has been the case since launch of AO. Thats quite a long time.)


MA Melee Weapons:
Handblades have tough reqs on Sneak and fast attack compared to their damage output.
G-staffs have tough reqs on Sneak and fast attack compared to their damage output.
Taichi/Wallblades/Parry Sticks have a very long equipping time, making them unviable in conjunction with MA specials.
Burning Crescents do not have Brawl/Dimach req.
Click stabbers do not have Brawl/Dimach req.

Wielding MA weapons (with the possible exception of dualwielding Acroblades) brings your damage output down at lower lvls.

(If you compare the requisites vs. the damage output against most non MAweapons out there, MA weapons fall short in all aspects. Add to this the utter unability to overequip them for more then 2.5 minutes, it doesnt quite cut it. And, please consider the damage output of the Handblades. At higher QL they add to melee init and MA skill, but frankly, making [8-32(43)] at QL 200 with a 1HE req of 993 is simply strange.)


MA Distance Weapons
Sharp Objects have never been in the game.
Bows use a massive amount of dark blue skills (fling, aimed shot etc)
Bow Special attack causes you MA to wave goodbye at the wasted arrow instead of causing any damage.

( Its nice and all that we were given some pulling ability, but unequipping the bow doesnt exactly go fast.)


Minimum Damage:
This does not apply specifically to MAs, but to all others (are there any?) wielding fast weapons with a low top damage. Whats believed to be the cause is that the Targets AC reduces the weapon damage to the minimum level. Though not as common now as a few patches ago, I personally run into it at least once per mission.

To recreate; Create a 55-60% percent mission and find a few Adventurer or Soldier MOBs in there. Wait a while so they can buff a bit. Hit q. Enjoy the show. Or simply go watch an MA fight a borg in Mort.

(I have personally (lvl91 with QL130ish implants) run into yellow unbuffed MOBs I can only hit with min damage. My QL99 Acroblade does min damage of 123 all the time. Against any MOB except grey. But hey, its better then making 97 damage all the time, eh? Its some variation at least?)

Please fill in the parts that Ive missed and what might be incorrect.
