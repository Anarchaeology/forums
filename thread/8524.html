Not Mentioned in 13.6 Patch Notes: New MOB Behavior
My curiousity just got the better of me, so I finally set up a test server login. On my first night, I noticed something on the test server that isn't in the patch notes, nor has anyone else mentioned it here yet:

Under 13.62, mobs in missions are free to move from room to room and random, and do so.

This adds a nice note of realism - and makes missions a lot more dangerous. Remember how you could easily predict that the second room in would either have 0 mobs, or 3, but never any other number (except in the static missions)? Remember how small rooms always had 0 or 1 mobs, and large rooms 0 or 2? Also, remember duking (or nuking) it out with a mob while being watched (peacefully) by a mob one room and a certain distance away? Forget all that, even at low levels.

I've run into small rooms with as many as four mobs in them; by freak chance, mobs had wandered in from all of the adjacent rooms. I've been cheerfully beating on the third and last mob in the second room - only to have another one jump me from behind, having crossed a fair distance before stumbling into me.

Running 75% difficulty and above missions just got more unpredictable, and therefore a lot more dangerous.
