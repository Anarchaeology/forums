Rhinomen

The Rhinomen would only be a closed file in Omni-Med, if not for the clan of the Eco Warriors. Rhinomen were originally bred from human and rhinoceros genes. Omni-Med had decided to terminate this experiment, when the primitive breed was let loose in the wild after an attack on Omni-Med installations by the Eco Warriors. The animal instincts of the rhinomen are usually stronger than their human rationality. The northern side of the big rivers, close to clan land, is the area they usually roam. The Rhinomen sympathize with the clans, but they are not able to recognize an ally if they are the slightest bit provoked.
