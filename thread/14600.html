Not always...
I just wanted to add a few experiences on this topic.

Chain casting still happens in 13.91.

Not all MOBS chain cast, even of the same type.

Chain casting seems to be per mission. If a mission sports a chaincaster, then all the monsters will have a faster then ususal cast time.

Chain casting is not dependent on mission sliders. Same setting might or might not produce a chain casting mission. You might want to check some randomly assigned variables here FC! Off course if there are a lot of nano dependent mobs, you will notice i much better...

Chain casting is not always negative. After the latest patch, mobs no longer do damage while throwing nanos (unless they cast very fast, then they also manage to squize in a shot or two). This means that they die faster, and a mob that dies faster is a good mob

Casting through walls? If they had to abort their nanos when you "accidentaly" dove for cover, it would be a situation very easy to exploit. mobs are not very intelligent (to put it mildly...), so I think we should let them keep that little advantage...
