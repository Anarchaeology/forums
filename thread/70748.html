My memory is kinda fuzzy on this, but if I recall correctly Funcom used to recommend that people start on Rubi 2. As a result, I think that's were I made my first char: a very confused nanomage MP, btw, who died almost immediately because he couldn't figure out how to get his pet to do anything but coo in his ear.

Anyway, embarassing personal history aside, many people I've talked to feel that Rubi 1 and Rubi 2 have different personalities. Rubi 1 is considered to be more social, and more role playing takes place there. Rubi 2, on the other hand, has a reputation for power levelers, and intense game players. One reason given is that Rubi 2 has less lag.

I have chars on both servers, and while I can't say I've noticed a big difference in lag, Rubi 2 certainly has less people. On Rubi 2 there is a noticable reduction in the volume on the selling channels, and the number of people who are around to team.

The population on Rubi 1 makes a big difference, and all my main chars are there. As a result, I'd certainly recommend that newbies start on Rubi 1. Also, I think it be a problem if Funcom started to take an active hand in 'balancing' the server populations, because of the different personalities the two servers have. It perhaps would even out over time, but initially it be a significant culture shock to find yourself on Rubi 2 one day, when you expect to be on Rubi 1. Or vice versa.
