Tradeskill Weapons Idea
I have amused myself from time to time Building low level weapons and dropping them on low level Charaters. (Most Look confused and say Thanks and wonder what they did to deserve this mana from heaven.)

But in Building the weapons I wonder if why can't you Upgrade weapons you already have. In example.

Take a Pitol consruction Kit.
add a Weapon Upgrade kit to it. (this is a New item to allow the Upgrade.)
Add various parts to upgrade for the weapon. (They can change the Range, Ammo Amount, Damage Min & Max, Crit chance and also the Damage type. From Projectile to Chemical though this can change its ammo type.)
When done the upgrade is used on the weapon. Upgrading it. and Making it a NoDrop.
It cannot change the Requirements of the gun or its Special Attacks. (i.e. Fling Shot, Burst, etc. etc.)

The Upgrades will have a QL and you can only apply it to weapons of that QL or higher to prevent a QL 50 weapon having a 200 QL Upgrade. It'd be cool but unfair.

I'm still thinking on it and will listen to replies and post responses.

Been thinking more on this Idea.

Modifications to Weapon. Parts to make can be Bought at any store but must be of QL or Higher of weapon being Modified.
Also You must Choose what systems to modify.

Range or Ammo Capacity.
Faster Weapon Speed or better chance of Critial hits
Alter Damge type or Increase Base Damage

You cannot do all six you must choose which of the 3 pairs you will modify and the weapon can be Modified only once.

Nolonger Is the weapon a Nodrop.

New Ideas along the same lines

Weapon Quality Level Mods (found as Loot Only)

These Improve the QL of the weapon they are applied to allowing a weapon you no been able to find a better one of to increase it's use to you. Also if used the Weapon so changed cannot be Modified as discussed above.

Special Attack Mods (found as Boss Loot Only)

These Rarest of Mods give a Weapon a special attack it didn't have before. Available mods are Fling Shot, Burst, Full Auto and Aimed shot. If a weapons is so modified it will gain the applicable Skill need to use the special attack. Also the weapon cannot be Modified by either mods discussed above.

This Idea will make Customization of Items more fun for Players. however I have no idea how much difficulty it would be to Implement.
