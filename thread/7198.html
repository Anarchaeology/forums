OK, since you were nice enough to delete the post and apologize, I'll try to take it slowly here.

The chat interface in AO is based on Internet Relay Chat. That's why you can chat to the in-game people from the web client. They probably have some authentication features built in to prevent people from spamming the game with bot's and otherwise trying to cause harm. But that's about it.

FunCom has a group (or maybe only one guy, what does it matter) that work(s) on the chat functions and nothing but chat. These people have been working nothing but that little box at the bottom of your screen for quite some now. They recently asked us what type of improvements we'd like to the chat interface. Things such as filters and multiple detachable windows were discussed (I'd like a window just for /tells to me). You see, this group was probably just finishing up work on adding the Japanese character support to the chat routines and were looking for their next assignments.

These people have nothing else to work on other than chat. Once the Japanese support is run through it's paces on Test server, they are off to add some of the nifty features discussed over the last couple weeks. That's what they do: chat.

The chat functions more than likely run in their own thread within the client. They open up a communication line over a particular port and their job is to get text from the server and stick it in a window where you can see it. Likewise, it sends your text input back to the server. These functions have nothing to do with class balance, dungeons, graphics performance, or anything. They're their own little subset of the client worked on by the three guys who got lucky and got the cubicles by the window.

You can't expect FunCom to tell these guys to stop working on the chat interface and help the guy who is making colored backpacks. They probably aren't artists or database experts. You can't ask them to fix the 'Please wait ...' bug because they might not even have heard of it. You can't expect your plumber to put in an electrical outlet in your bedroom.

Does nobody read these posts or do they just like complaining? Everytime something nice goes in, somebody starts whining.

This particular change is important. If they can increase the playerbase, they increase revenues. That means more content for everyone. The Japanese $12.95 is just as valuable as my American $12.95
