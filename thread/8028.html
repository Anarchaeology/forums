Just became aware of this (pertaining to one of the visual bugs)
Not sure if this was common knowledge or not... of course everyone's aware of the new (or old/fixed prior but back again) visual bugs that came with the 13.4/13.5 patch. But I didn't notice the specifics of this until someone pointed it out to me recently.

There have been quite a few people going about lately, wearing no armor. Or they look like they have no armor. Some have a helmet visible. Others are wearing a trenchcoat with the unarmored-clothing color sceme. Okay, I thought... new visual bug. Maybe something they're carrying, or something they had equipped. My armor was visible enough, so I didn't dwell on it at length.

...until I realized that other people couldn't seem my armor. Only I could see it, unless I physically equip or unequip anything that changes my appearance. Only then can others see what I'm wearing.

One other thing... this seems to be exclusive to the black & asian solitus templates (I chose a black solitus template for my Doc). Every unarmored character I see (who *should* be armored, i.e. not a lowbe) & every 'nude trenchcoat' I see, happens to be either a black or asian solitus.

Please tell me this will be fixed next patch...? *crosses fingers*
