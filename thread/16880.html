The State of Engineers
I'm doing this because I am bored. I am just an average nice joe, trying to tell you exactly how I imagine my profession. I hope that after you read this, you will feel like you know alot about engineers.

I am a lvl 128 ngi, and I see the prof pretty clearly from this point. BTW, I'm atrox, so anyways...

The Engineer profession is both a simple and powerful one.
Here is a list of all we have:

Bot - A pet with a massive amount of life. seems equal in life to a mob of it's same ql, which is like 15k hp for a lvl 100 bot (guess. I don't know how to find out). Also, as u get higher lvl bots, their combat defensiveness seems to go up exponentially because of their AC/hp. A ql 185 slayer lasts more than 5 times as long in a fight as a ql 129 warbot.

AC buffs - We got the singular buff that at lvl 200 adds 650 ac I think, and we have the area buff that adds about the same at the same lvl, but is rare. These use the Matter Meta skill, which at lvl 128 I don't raise because I don't care too much about AC, and have only a few precious IPs

Reflects - At lvl 200, about a total of 90 reflect damage. This is quite low compared to what a lvl 200 bot hits for, which is around 1k. It spams the chat, so I don't even use them.

beacon warps - warps a team member to a person's location. lvl 90 nano for singular persons, lvl 159 for whole team, but twice as long.

Trimmers - These things are vary mysterious because it's like (do this and ur bot will be better at hittin ). I have upped Electrical Engineering for the positive agressive because it lasts forever, but I do not do the divert energy to offense because most use a seperate skill, quantum ft, and need to be reapplied frequently. I heard my friend after starting to use the positive agressive-defensive trimmer for the first time saying that the bot critted more (which is hit more, don't see many hits out of the ordinary).
There is a agression trimmer that keeps mobs aggroed on the pets more, which is a must have.

Oh ya, and nice heals. a ql 84 heal heals for 500-900 with a 2.5 attack speed I think. I use my ql125 nano packs as fast as possible.

I personally don't use a weapon because I want the mobs to stay aggroed on my pet, and because I at times heal full time.

Summary of team play: Basically, Engineer pets are, if treated correctly, an unkillable force that leads the fights into battle and are cherished by teams because the people in the team are in alot less danger.

Summary of Solo play: Bots can solo much higher mobs with buffs and lotza heals, but they hit for just a little. If it weren't for agressiveness trimmers, I don't think that the bots would be attacked for too long in a group. My ql 185 slayer in a ql 185 mission hits for 550-900. I rarely see him hit for more or less in those missions.

Summary of PVP: I personally don't PVP because I think I'm always gonna die. I have 3k health, and a bot that hits for 500, while there is a Enforcer that hits me for 800, and has 6k health with heals. Engineers are simple. what am I to do? I am proud to not PVP, because PVP as it is, is full of uber ppl beating down unuber people.

Summary of buffs: So as u can see, The engineers are both good and bad. There is alot of time spent just standing around looking at ur bot fight for a while. And about buffs: here are all I know

Personally, I can build a slayer now without external buffs besides mochams, which is nice. Thank you for reading this. I hope you don't regret it BTW, I'm all for the 20% patch, because it won't effect me now, and when I was low lvl and didn't know much, I disliked seeing lvl 35 people with lvl 80 pets.
