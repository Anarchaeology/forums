Minimum Dmg done by NPC's?????
Ok, I have just had an unsolved discussion with a friend of mine, who also plays AO alot. The thing is this :

Once awhile I take missions below my level, mostly greys and some greens. In some of these missions I have met A-500 Soldiers, where some have been grey to me, others green. These have ben QL35-40 Missions. On other occasions, I have taken missions my own level, 50-55. Here is the point and source of the debate :

In the lower lvl missions, these NPC hit me for a minimum of 65, sometimes a crit hit of 91. Then in the higher missions, they hit for the same.

This lead to a discussion :
Is it so that these NPC's have the same weapon on different QL's of the NPC's? Beacuse this means that a QL / lvl 35 NPC can hit the same crit as a QL / lvl 200 in theory?

I just find this somewhat confusing. Becasue this leads me and my friend to wonder about how damage output is calculated for NPC's. I mean why is so that a lvl / ql 35 NPC has the same minimum damage as a lvl / ql 50 NPC unless they have the same weapon and if they don't then how does it work?

If you equipp a lvl 10 char with a QL 30 Support Beam and the same Support beam on a lvl 50 char, they will in theroy do the same ammount of minimum damage, when looking away from modifiers.. Otherwise the higher lvl char would do somewhat higher minimum damage. One should think they same rule applied for NPC's as well. That a higher lvl NPC with the same weapon as a lower, should have modifiers that increased the minimum damage, but this is not the case.

Anyone has a explanation to this? I will do some more "testing" and see.. but I find it strange.

NPCs does not use weapons like players do. They have a set damage, and it doesn't really fluctuate much. Mobs with pistols hits for generally the same, even though players with pistols can be rather variated.

You might have noticed a bigger difference if the mobs wouldn't hit for minimum damage, since it's usually quite small. The maximum damage (and thus, technically, the average damage they deal) might increase, but your armour negates it.
