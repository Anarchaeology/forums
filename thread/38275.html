Party and Contest for Rubi-Ka's Sinners
Pride, Envy, Greed, Sloth, Wrath, Gluttony and Lust. The Seven Deadly Sins. Look at the words, think about them, roll them around on your tongue and experience the taste of sin.

On this Friday night, the 19th of July, these words come to life, as Devils Advocate hosts Fiesta del Pecado, the Sinners Ball.

When: Friday the 19th at 8pm EST
Where: Avalon at the Fiery lake east of Camelot
Who: Devils Advocate
Why: To party and have a chance to win the grand prize - 20 million credit

Thats right, folks, someone is going home 20 million credits richer. But were not just giving it away. No. Youre going to have to sell your soul to the Devils Advocate.

As the party gets underway, Devils Advocate will be signing in contestants for one hell of a contest. At 9 PM EST, the contest begins. The Best Display of the 7 Deadly Sins Each contestant will have 10 minutes to show the judges his/her skills in heading to hell, and the winner walks away with the whole pot. Team effort (2 to 6 members), or solo, contestants will use their creativity, originality, and a whole lot of scripts, to impress the panel of judges. A word of warning though: Be creative  not banned. Vulgarities and offensive displays will not be acceptable. So, who wants to get rich for sinning?

A live broadcast will be on site, and booze and food will be in ample supply. To help set the tone, we encourage guest to dress in red or black. Transportation will be provided for Omni Citizens, via Beacon Warps from Baboons.

Check back for more details on this Devilish Bash!!


Often imitated, never duplicated! Thats us! Devils Advocate !
