Team missions...
I really like the team missions.

The reason? Lots of extra mobs charging in from all sides.
--With a decent group, you can take it slow and not really get too swarmed, or (as I prefer) wade in and find yourself constantly fighting to stay alive.

I normally only group with 1-2 friends (1 now since the second quit), but since I am a crat (and just got my next charm) we can do missions that will give us some xp (mostly green/yellow) while having a blast keeping each other alive.

We may actually start trying to find groups to travel with now when we have long stretches of time to play. Do we have to? Nope, but it sure is a good incentive without requiring grouping like some other game(s).

I can see why some might think they are bad if the goal is xp/levels rather than a challenge/test of teamwork.

As a 31 crat, I can go out soloing reds that let me hit the 10% xp cap as long as I am on my toes and see when my charmed mobs are getting antsy.

However, I much prefer to have a good time fighting for my life against greens and yellows with a couple of friends. Not nearly the xp (450-600/mob as opposed to 3k/mob), but a much more entertaining use of time.

Anywise, just my 2 cents.

(and no, normally I can not go after such mean mobs, but having just hit a new charm, I can do way better than normal for a couple of levels)
