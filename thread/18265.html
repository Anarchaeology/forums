My solution to static dungeon pop camping.
Each static dungeon should have a type of key distributer, whether its a npc or a machine i dont care, npc would be more propper as far as role playin is concerned though i spose.

The key is just like any other mission key, it accesses the location of the static dungeon and can be duped to share with team members, each key pops a totally seperate dungeon so there is 1 dungeon per key.

This way entire organizations can dungeon raid or smaller teams would have the ability to have thier own dungeon experience without having to compete for the pops with other players.

Granted it makes items that are "rare" more accessable but the thing is every player is paying funcom the same and should have the same benifits in preportion to what they put into the game, meaning anyone who takes the time to get a group together with the power to go kill the tarasque should at least have a chance of fighting it, not to be turned away because someone else has been forced into hogging the pop for thier own gear.

So what if it makes certain items downright common and everyone is walking around in the same gear like clones.... if thats not allready happening i must have something wrong with my vision.

The only way to fix the problem of cloned characters is to add additional items that have the same basic stats just with different models, partical effects, and skin jobs. Maybe even different damage types heck at high lvls most AC is good anyway so alt damage isnt really anything too special alot of the time, that and it makes different weapons better in different pvp cercumstances ****ing the game up a bit. This would make it so a player can choose how they want to look without having to sacrifice a better weapon.

Really nice gear that should be extra rare should be from static missions recieved from npcs, similar to the EQ epic weapon quests, just make the related monsters and NPCs randomly pop in different zones and incorporate dungeons that need specific quested keys to get into so its a mater of pure luck not a matter of how long you can sit in one spot waiting on something to pop.
