Lush fields. It's to the west of omni-1, you can go there by griding to lush hills or harry's.

Then, you go to the outpost (OP), by following the road going to the west from harry's or to the north from lush hills. You can find people to build a team there, or try to join a team on the hunting spot.
There's several places around to hunt at various levels, just ask the people at outpost.
