Jubbal has left the building...
(haven't used these forums much but gonna do it one more time.)

Malcolm "JUBBAL" Harshaw just got his ticket outta here.. ICC officials confirmed his ticket off world and he is now currently sitting in ICC:Andromeda spaceport awaiting his trip back to mother earth. Two and almost a half years have taken a toil on Him but he seems happy and excited to see what is waiting for him on our blessed home world...

Jubbal's Statement: "I wish all clan's the most success possible against OMNI-TEK. The world of RubiKa is ours due to the blood, sweat , and tears we and our forefathers shed in terraforming this world into the "paradise" it is now.... I am sad that I can not continue to be a weapon in our epic struggle but I am forced towards other concerns.. My heart and thoughts go out to you all..I hope you all prevail and in the Shadowlands you find the means for RubiKa's salvation."

So many peeps and memories... Thanks to the community for making AO a positive experience... God knows the game and Funcom (even though I forgive them ) didnt make it easy sometimes.

One thing I will say... Go Lost Chapter!! My heart and Virtual allegiance belongs to you forever
