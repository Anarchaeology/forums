quote:
The XP has fling quite often the fling will hit as hard as a crit
Well, that's kinda true... A Fling is just a normal attack. It can crit, just like a normal attack as well, but it doesn't have any higher chance at critting than any normal attack either...

A lot of people swear by the QL 200 River XP, but I wasn't impressed with it. I use a V3 and a QL 155 River XP combo at level 136. I tried the main-hand XP, but prefered the better crits on the V3. I use a +4 crit scope (low-end, QL 153...best deal out there...hehe...4 crit for -190ish init), combined with Grinning Hunter gives me at least 1 crit per battle, usually 2...and often 3. The V3 crits for about 2500 with 895 attack rating, offhand XP crits for about 1300.

I find this combo works very, very well... Especially if you're lucky enough to get a crat or MA crit buff to get crit % in the 8% range.

It's up to personal opinion, of course...but I much prefer the V3 in my on-hand right now.

EITHER WAY...get that Custom Reet outta your offhand and replace it with a River XP between QL 160 and 170. You'll be glad you did. =) Don't bother with a Boomer or a Kaen, they both suck compared to River XPs for offhanders.

quote:
Originally posted by Emiko
Which hand is my main hand? What identifies a hand as MAIN or OFF?
Generally, the left is considered your 'offhand', but for all practical purposes, it makes no difference.

People say you only have to meet the multi req of the weapon in your offhand but it's more accurate to say that you just have to meet the lowest multi req of the 2 weapons you wield.

If you were to wield a single pitsol, it makes no difference if it is in your right or left hand.
