self only buffs and OE
On the topic of should adventurers have any self-only buffs....

Adventurers have nothing to make them special as it is now, all their buffs can be casted on other profs. What to be a dragon and not be an adventurer? That can be done! So complaining about a few buffs that will be self only seems very lame. Simple buffing others only, plays one part in helping a team, afterward fighting the monsters/npcs is also a way to help your team. Which is more important? I would guess both are.

Now the adventurer also needs some self only buffs that make the class important in battle. They would become important because these buffs cannot be casted on other profs and that makes having you *there fighting* beside them useful in itself.

Here is an example for those that think you must only cast buffs on other players to be useful to a group...

It's like asking, should the enforcer have rage, mongo and challenger if they can't be casted on other profs? Is he still useful to the team if they can't be casted on other profs? If the enforcer casts Mongo and pulls the 3 mobs going after the doc onto him, saving the doc, did it help the team even though he can't cast mongo on other profs?

If anyone thinks adventurers should have *no* self only buffs at all, I would like to know why, and if other profs should lose their self only buffs as well.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

As for OEing...

Ok first off, Funcom said players will be able to OE by 20%, meaning that OEing is *not* going anywhere."

Second, OEing is something that did need to be reduced. It will allow more people to compete in the battle between Omni and the Clans (PvP). Some players just didn't have the money, time, patience or friends to OE extreme enough to PvP. Meaning less PvPing because they couldnt (or wouldnt) become a OEing-uber-noleveling--pvptitled-ganking-character. In short, it gives more players the ability to compete, though the players able to be the best now, are going to be the best after patch 14.2 anyway IMO. At least more players can take part

Third, OEing for monsters is also a bad thing. Why should a player have to take forever to OE to a god-like-status, just to lose it as they level? More important is, why should they have too? The best thing for funcom to do is reduce OE (which they are in patch 14.2) then decrease the monsters/NPCs hps as needed.
