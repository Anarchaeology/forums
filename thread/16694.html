Social Skills
Funcom why don't you guys throw in some social skills in the skill menu, and make a seperate lesser social skill pool (the point of this being so you don't draw from people's already tight pool of combat related skills) that gets a handful of points each time you level, then you can put points into dancing or something like that(only social points), and at certain skill levels you can unlock new emotes commands. These skills shouldn't be as hard to raise as similar combat skills, of course I think certain types would be more appropriate for various professions.

Social Skill suggestions:

Dancing

Taunts

Etiquette (shaking hands, oriental style bowing, things appropriate for formal meetings between people)

Service (actions appropriate for people roleplaying things like say, pouring a drink, cleaning a table)

Seduction (not outright adult porn here, but something for those enthralled with romace to focus on, something to ****e up the love life rpers)

I would also perhaps recommend that some of these levels permenantly change the demeanor of your character's day to day normal actions. Most obvious example(please don't kill me ) is say a female character with a high Seduction skill would have her normal walk changed to something a bit more enticing.

And of course for higher level people they would not miss out in that they would recieve retroactively all the points not yet recieved or used
