My Book.....
OOC: Alright, in my time at work, and on various occasions when I was utterly bored, or just really did not want to do anything, I started to jot down little notes here and there, after a while I noticed they all fit together quite nice, so I started forming them into a coherent manuscript, eventually this little manuscript grew into twenty pages, then forty pages and so on, now I am on the last leg of my book, and for obvious reasons once I am done I will have to rewrite as to not copyright infringe upon Funcom, but before I do rewrite I wanted to share the Prologue with you all, and see what you thought, this is strictly OOC, so please give me honest OOC answers, no sugar coating, if you hate it say so, and tell me why, if you love it I could use the feedback also, so here is the beginning of my boredom.

The Chaos and The Shadow


PROLOGUE

*

The mind can be ones own personal hellor it can be ones personal haveneverything is based on the chaos that is Self.
Gabriel Cayll Baptist

Amusing the memories one can draw forth from a sound, a taste, or a scent, amazing how the senses are so defined that the aroma of a young woman can make ones mind go back to days far gone, or in cases such as mine, how a scent can bring one pleasure.no do not misunderstand me, I do not speak of this as a perversion, though I am sure many would, but I speak of a delicious scent, one that is unrivaled, the scent of deathonce again my mind wanders, keeping focus becomes harder these days, my mind takes hiatus into itself and my body runs on animal instinct alonesometimes it is quite exhilirating, but in cases such as these it is quite bothersome, I came here with a purpose..the question is, what was this said purpose..ah yes to end my business with the kind Doctor.

***

I entered the unremarkable Omni-Med building as I had done a hundred times before, the scent of bleach, and cleaning chemicals was strong, I quickly headed to the lift, and up to the 202nd floor, I slowly made my way down the familiar hallway to the large grey door at the end, unusualI never noticed before, he had a plaque on his door, amazing what one learns when you have a new purpose in mind, ah well time to tell all my deep, dark secrets

***

The scent of cheap cologne was thick in the air, why must these suit types adorn themselves with such repulsive chemicals, it would not be as bad if they had perhaps chosen it by scent and not price, but these are harsh times even for the filthy rich.this man disgusts me, his appearance is more of an old library keeper than a doctor, a short stocky man, probably in his early sixties, with his carefully parted white hair, and those annoying steel brimmed glasses perched atop of that beak like nose, the thick moustache underneath was somewhat amusingwhy does he find the need to wear the glasses, vision correction was perfected a millenia ago.

***

OH! Mr. Baptist.I did not hear you enter, please sit down, we have much to discuss. Hardly, he reads his news feeds while I sit there in silence, not much of a discussion, this was supposed to help me remember, by rehashing the past and all the memories I could, I was supposed to remember who I am..but this fool obviously finds me far too worthless to bless with his attention. I could feel a slight heat coming over me as I spoke, my voice calm and unbreaking  Doctor, my dear man, I do believe your services will no longer be required, I have found a much more capable physician, she has offered to care for me mentally and physically. The expression on his face was almost amusing enough to make me smile, but I held it in, I knew he could not keep quiet, he had to respond..Mr. Baptist, I do not know what to say, we were making such breakthroughs this made me grin, breakthroughs, now that is the best idea you have had in the last three months old man, I knew the look on my face brought great fear to him, I could smell it, taste his sweat in the air, his lips slightly quivering, how I do so enjoy this, I dominate this pathetic worm in every manner of the word, I am his mental and physical superior, he knows this, as do I, but the greed in me requires me to drag this out further; Doctor, you are shaking, surely you do not think I am going to kill you..well who are we kidding here, you must of known the day I entered this office that once you had served your purpose, or lack of for that matter, I would discard you like the filth you are. HA! I thought he was going to break into tearsugh, I let out a sigh as I caught wind of his bowels and bladder relieving themselves down his leg..true at my seven feet of stature and my muscular build I am an intimidating man, but it does so disgust me when they cannot control themselves, at least live with some dignity, after death the body will relieve them for you, anyway too much thought as usual, it only took me two steps to clear the room and hoist this being off the ground by his throat, my gloved handle holding him firmly off the ground but just gentle enough to not crush the life out of himshould I say morenay, I tossed him into the glass bookcase across the room, and delighted in his scream as he crashed through the shelves, slicing him across his back and arms, amazing that his head took no damage, he actually managed to cover it, perhaps I underestimate you old man, I was there in an instant as he tries to stand, this time I hoisted him by the scruff of the neck, I watched as the blood dripped freely from the lacerations on his back, thick blood..I should of known with the garbage this man ate, I am surprised he had not ate himself to death, but those were the benefits of working at Omni-Med, I notice the clock on his desk registering the time as 22:04it was getting late, I do not have time for this, and to be quite honest he bores me..but what to do, what to do.then I spotted it, the Psychiatric Award on his desk, the shard of Notum in the shape of a tear drop with the point reaching for the skies, I quickly flicked my thumb and heard the sensual snap of his spine, he let out a simple whimper as I placed his mouth around the pinnacle of the award, then with little effort I drove the make shift spike through his skull, the sound of his brain being forced out of his head almost made me giggle with delight, the smell, the delicious smell of his life essence leaving, no cell restructuring for this one, as my Associate had removed his Insurance earlier in the day, I prefer my hobbies to stay dead, I closed my eyes and listening to the rythmic beat of his blood dripping from the desk into a pool on the metal floor, one could sleep to such a beautiful sound, I could not contain myself any longer I had to look into the pool, I stared at it with the glee of a child opening a gift, I tried to read the reflection, the ancient tribes of Earth swore that one could deem their future by using the blood of a freshly killed vicitm to see into the afterlife, but once again I saw nothing, I wiped my glove off on his pant leg and slowly made my way to the door smiling to myself the whole way.this was a good evening, I am glad we had this talk Doctorvery gladyour final session helped to remove a dark cloud of brooding and place me into a good mood, and your sacrifice will not be forgotten.HA! Cayll the Comedianof course you will be forgotten, to tell you the truth I am not even sure what your name was, but you served your purpose, may you burn in pain and anguish for all eternity.
