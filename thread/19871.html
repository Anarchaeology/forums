Titles for 1 on 1 kills was basically what ruined PvP for this game no questions asked. It's probably the root of everything that emits a stench coming out of the corpse of the PvP system still rotting in the alleys of Meetmedere since July of 2001.

With proper team-based PvP you can keep the 'f*cking crit situation' as it is and still have enjoyable interactive player combat. Our friend the 'crat has a crit-reducing aura. You'll probably have a doctor in you group to help you out. Likewise you'll probably have your own MA in your team. Almost all the horse manure Nerfs that FunCom has dropped on us have stemmed from single players whining that they were killed by another solo player. A properly distributed group of six fighting another properly distributed group of six should come down to more of how your teammates handle things and can make decisions than any selfish whining individual profession issues. Likewise a group of six uber-twinked characters who can't work together should have a hell of a time with six players who have their act together but might not be super twinked.

Cooperation between the professions has never been encouraged and has in fact been systematically DIS-couraged by providing vanity plates to those who decide to forgo fighting for their faction or team and instead go solo hunting on weak solo targets to pump their precious ill-conceived 'titles'.

No title points should EVER be given when the victim is not in a team. Title points should go to every member of a team that makes a kill of a member in another team. Title points should be lost for every member in a team who lets one of their men die.

Title points should be like tokens and be Faction based. You get points for kills you make while fighting for each faction. No title points should be given for mixed Clan/Omni teams. Let them team up and fight in mayhem zones. But, neither organization will condone or approve of the situation.

Neutrals should be able to fight with either side and not change the faction of the team to 'missing'. 5 Omnis and a Neutral is an Omni team. The Neutral on that team should earn 'Omni' title points. If he earns enough Omni title points then his title will show to all who care to look where his allegiances are weighted.

Joe 'FenceSitter' Semen may be a Neutral but his OrderKeeper title will show who he teams with regularly and who he kills. No more of this ganker copout bull crap 'if you aren't with me you are against me.' If he's against you, you'll now know.

Arena fighting and 'dueling' is for fun and practice. It should be considered 'sparring'. Solo people going around killing other is not 'combat', it's Assassination. Not something worth admiring.

My personal selfish wish is for them to scrap the bogus meaningless PvP titles they have now and implement proper rewards for team based combat.

Going PvP'ing with your team should be just as much an option as going into another mind-numbing mission.
