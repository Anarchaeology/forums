Profession population stats
I was looking for a couple of buffs in Omni-e last nite and noticed that 7 out of 10 player at the BB were metas. If that is anywhere near a representative number, there may be great opportunities for new players in other professions.

It would be a useful and interesting addition to the web site to see statistics on the distribution of players by profession. I realize that most players have more than one character and that many characters are not played often but I still believe the data would be relevent for certain purposes.

Something like:
Profession %Total %New<30 %Play<30 %New<60 %Play<60
------------- --------- ------------- ------------- ------------- ------------

Where "%New<x" would represent the number of new characters of that profession created within the last x (30,60,90) days. "%Play<x" represents the % of total with less than x hours of play time. I'm not sure what the appropriate numbers are to represent time played - my example is for illustrative purposes only.

This would be useful in helping new players and those existing players contemplating a re-roll see where the gaps are.

Thanks!
