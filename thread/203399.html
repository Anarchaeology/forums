Science Symposium Sat 22nd at Reets (RK1)
Just a reminder that the Isaac Asimov Science Symposium will be taking place this Saturday at Reets fireplace room at 20:00 GMT ( 3 PM EST) on Atlantean

We have quite a line up of interesting speakers including:

Omni-Mining who will talk to us about the myth about notum depletion,

Bogosorter who will tell us more about the effects of Leets on Solitus females,

Missmaul who wishes to talk about something related to toothpicks,

Verson who will make a speech on 'dissociative notum recombination',

Ariekel who is keeping her topic hush-hush,

Kronocide who will describe his invention called the AO Market and

Askel tell us a little about the Xan, our funny looking cousins from the Shadowlands.

Of course, this being Rubi-Ka, we can expect any number of last minute changes, but I think there will be enough here to keep everyone entertained.

See you there!
Dabblez
