Really, it depends on what your intentions are within the game. Its very good for some things and blatantly useless for others.

For mass pvp id say its a no-no due to the potentially large amount of dmg from multiple specials but for duels it is variable from extremely good to very poor depending upon what class you are up against-(as stated above, not good vs nanotechnicians since the advent of the gridspace nukes and same vs traders as they can have it overequipped in the blink of an eye).

But you may never wish to pvp and instead stick to missions/uniques or dynaboss hunting.

For a long time i was in awe of a friend of mine who was equipped with GA4 and the way he could happily solo lvl 190+ dynabosses with little or no serious damage, maybe many other classes are capable of this, I couldnt say for sure tho i do know that playing as a 170+ nt at the time I damn well couldnt.

As to you hearing that it will be nerfed in shadowlands, I put it to you that this is speculation-what has already been stated is that grid armor wont work IN the shadowlands but I wouldnt write it off just yet on the basis that it will still, so far as I know, be fully functional in the game proper.
