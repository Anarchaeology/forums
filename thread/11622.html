(Erp. This is a reply to Agentq232. Someone posted right before me.)

I know. I thought the same thing at first, but here's what apparently happened: Funcom moved a lot of the old stomping grounds to different areas in order to promote exploration and adventure. Before, clanners could play to level 50 and beyond without ever leaving Tir (don't know about Omni).

So the Temple is gone, along with a few other choice hotspots. I went to the Outcrops recently to see whether there were any robots left there, since that used to be a popular hunting area. There were, but evidently not as many. I didn't want to go too deep, though, as my current char is still low level and everything was conning red to me. Anyway, the days of the Northern Outpost rave parties are definitely over, which is in one way sad but in another way...well, I'm glad to see them go. I've had a lot of fun hunting areas up there that used to be impossible due to the crowds (I'm a soloer).

As for where everthing got moved to, I don't know. Maybe someone who was around at the time can answer that.
