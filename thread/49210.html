Tara Loot..The Fair Way!
First of all before I start I would like to give credit and thanks to Javaschick and all she does for the clan community at Tara. Java we appreciate you and all you do so please dont think that this is directed towards you in anyway. Now... for some reason I have somehow became the person everyone comes to in regards to fairness at Tara Raids. I have thought long and hard on this situation and feel i have came up with a viable solution. I dont know whether Funcom is aware of this but.. Tara Raids cause cause many jealousy and trust issues..Why?? Example: 1.Kill teams 1 and 2 go down to Tara and kill her. 2. Kill team 1 outdamages kill team 2. 3. The leader of kill team 1 loots tara. 4. Tara drops 4 items..Padded Coat, Dragon Flesh,Globe of Clarity and Poker.. 5. Leader takes Globe of Clarity and Dragon Flesh. 6. Leader tells everyone who has invested time and hardwork that Tara dropped 2 items. Now...Im not saying this happens all the time or even happens at all, however it does raise much su****ion and people begin to develop hard feelings toward each other..I know! I recieve tells from people asking me about the fairness all the time..most people I do not even know or have not even heard of. Here is what I propose. I fell this is a simple solution for you (Funcom) to implement and it would keep peace and harmony amongst not only clan but omni as well. Proposal: Give us a tara loot command..it would go something like this. ./tell helpbot pf 973 taraloot: helpbot would then in turn return back a list of all that tara dropped in that pf for that kill.. I feel this would make things much more fair. It would also make people feel like they have a real chance at obtaining Tara loot whether they are in a kill team or in box room. Please donot blow this issue off. It is very important and causes problems on a day to day basis. Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

Morrizon 197 Doc
Advisor of RK-Mafia
