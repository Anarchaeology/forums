This is a tough one for me.....


I chose to play on RK2 because I heard all of the expoiters were on RK1 (No offense) Just a friendly tip someone gave me....

Now if they join the two servers i keep wondering what happens.... First of all,

-= The Economy=-

It is very simple actually.... RK1 is the home of all the "big" guys... chars that have reached lvl 200 and a huge load of players getting there long before we get one over at RK2.

What this means is, people on RK1 have a lot (!) more money than us at RK2.. therefore, we have other prices on things.
It is easy to go on ebay and buy oneself 100 million credits ingame, but (for now anyway) only on RK1 there are some for RK 2 as well but noone is selling big amounts like 500 million .. (fact)

So I think this is a huge reason they should not (!) open the doors between the servers....

BUT!

on to problem nr 2.

-=Roleplaying and Storyline=-

This gives me the feeling that we get left out... and I value Storyline long before any kind of economy, so I guess I would have to settle for the economy being changed for a better fell of the Storyline.
