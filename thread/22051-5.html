Using places like MMD to label all PvP/PvP zones is misleading. It's extremely easy to setup an ambush in MMD because the places where a character might be going are limited and extremely well known. Complaining about that setup is like complaining about getting ganked in the arena.

I don't PvP unless someone jumps me. But I do travel PvP zones and take missions in them. The only place I have ever been jumped is in Meetmedere. In my numerous visits to 4 Holes with my Omni characters, I have only seen ONE other person. And he was Omni as well.

I think a lot of people want to be able to camp the spawn points in PvP zones and not be vulnerable to getting killed by a player. That's the only reasoning I can see for a PvP switch: They want the cake and eat it too. If such a switch was made, though, I'd hope FC reduced the rewards in PvP zones accordingly. Part of the reward is based on the risk of getting killed out there. Although, that risk is trully infinitesimal.
