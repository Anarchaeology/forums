Economics of RK
OK, Im going to start this off with a few things.

1) I do know about economics IRL and I think it would be an improvement to all players if we can improve them ingame

2) Can we please keep this as a serious thread, no flaming etc etc etc

OK, people, lets be honest we have a problem in RK. We have a situation of hyper-inflation, let me explain.

Inflation - prices going up
Deflation - prices going down
Hyper-inflation - very rapid, very large increase in the price level

Real world examples

1922 Germany 5,000%
1985 Bolivia >10,000%
1989 Argentina 3,100%
1990 Peru 7,500%
1993 Brazil 2,100%
1993 Ukraine 5,000%

Now, fortunately there is NOT hyper-inflation accross the entire economy, just select and rare items. As we would expect rare items WILL and SHOULD cost more, however recently the prices have spiralled out of control and lead to a much more barter economy where rares are traded for rares.

This has a number of effects.

1) The haves will continue to have. The have nots will have a very hard time getting anywhere into these items.

2) Money is becoming worthless.

We need to work out a fix. What we need to do is remove money from the economy, to slow it down, to bring things back into line with a normal 'stable' level of inflation, that is normally accepted as 4% max in the real world, probably more like 10% ingame. However the removal of this money must give a level of 'economic satisfaction' to the players otherwis your just going to get lots of unhappy people.

Here are two ideas, one comes from a friend who says a fixer (I belive) suggested it a while ago, but I can't say I know the link.

a) Advance Face graft options. The ability to use a terminal to choose faces that are NOT avaliable at character creation. This would also need a change to the Agent nano so that they get another benefit from it, perhaps the ability to CHOOSE size values (not random as most agents want to be small)

b) Renting. Have a number of shops in the game that will hire out items. These are slightly better than the 'normal' ones in game (not much just slightly) but have a reasonable level of hire chrage. This keeps coming out of the account every game day, so being offline with one of these will hurt, in fact there should be a 'after i have spent so much return' option. If the rent cant be paid, then the item auto disappears.

c) Tweaking. Have some mobs that can, for a short period, change items. Make a machine gun cold damage, make a piece of armor better etc etc

These are just some ideas to start the ball rolling. Hope to hear from you soon

Just some random ideas...

There are all sorts of things that could and probably should have been done.

Firstly a hard limit on the amount of creds any player can have (far lower than the current one) would flatten all prices, including rare items.

A state taxation scheme - we live in an advanced society but dont get taxed according to our income? daft. A sliding scale of taxation where a percentage is taken from each transaction based on the total creds you would have after the transaction completes. Non existant at low levels, minor at medium levels, heavy and hard at high levels.

Find things for guilds to spend money on - I favour advertising. Imagine how much big guilds would pay to be able to put up a nice clear graphic sign boasting how much better they are than their competition. You could rent signage space throughout RK, prime spots/small adds are cheap a big billboard by omni ent could cost many millions per month to rent. Smaller guilds could advertise for members for lesser amounts of money. Yes there are technical issues but if games like Quake and CS had spray logos years ago I'm sure something could be done. It would sure make RK feel more lived in too.

Gambling. Hooo yeah! Lets get some casino tables in the bigger clubs and you'd find far more people in the clubs.

Random items > QL125 available in special shops/terminals at high prices. You *can* buy the item you want if you are prepared to spend a lot of time searching and spend a *premium* price on it.

High cost searches of shop inventories planetwide. Special orders of items at an exceptional cost.

Different vehicles, different skins for vehicles. Custom skins for vehicles.

More clothes.

Name/Sex/Face changes at a *high* price.

Upgrades - spend the cash and have your weapon upgraded by n levels or your implant. Would cost a *lot* though.

Increased cost for rolling missions.

Transportation charges (grid/whompa)

The ability to name things after yourself or your guild (streets, buildings, shops etc).
