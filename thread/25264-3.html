Unfortunately, for those of us who WENT to E3, Funcom was a huge disappointment. They were not in any of the main Exhibit areas with anything on display for the public. When I found their 6'x8' cubicle with 3 posters and 2 bragsheets about the company with release dates for their Special Edition and Expansion Pack, I was told I was not allowed in and that any discussions with Funcom at E3 were "behind closed doors only." I'm assuming it was only for investors and the media.

This was very disappointing, since Funcom was the biggest reason I went to E3 in the first place. I expected them to at LEAST let the public see screenshots, or look at Concept art for the Expansion pack, or even TALK to Funcom employees. I expected at the *VERY LEAST* to be pushing their Special Edition of AO which was to be released just one week after E3. None of that, not so much as even a computer or a copy of the game in sight (for those who were able to get into the special cubicle).

On the plus side, before I even PARKED at the LA Convention Center for E3, I was being overwhelmed by people handing out copies of AO to people in their cars and exhibition attendees. It's the old copy, not the new Special Edition (which makes sense, since the old copies are about to be useless), but nonetheless, it made my day to see my favorite game being handed out to hopefully acquire a larger player base! The people handing out the games were constantly opening new crates of boxes to pass more copies of the game out. They said they were giving away upwards of 10,000 copies of the game! Way to go Funcom!

In the end: Am I disappointed with Funcom's role at E3? Yea, pretty much. From what I read on the AO website before E3 began, I was fully expecting AO to have previews of Shadowlands on public display -- But for them to tell the public (their playerbase!!!) they weren't allowed in to see anything, that hurt.

Even Sony and ID Software were being overly cautious about letting people see their new games. Sony had "By appointment only" previews of Starwars Galaxies and Everquest 2, and ID Software was (correct me if I'm wrong) selling tickets to get into the Doom III preview (there was a sign on the door saying "Sold out," so I can only assume..).

Ahh well, I still had a blast trying out various games and seeing previews for games such as AC2 and Age of Mythology. Hopefully next year Funcom will have a much larger role at E3! Don't let me down again next year Funcom, please!
