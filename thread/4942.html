Bliss has been around since the beginning. Very nice town, if you don't mind the scorps. Has everything you need once you have graduated to the meds sold in Advanced shops. Highly recommended for characters 30-60. I got my first missions there when I hit level 21 and was not yet equipped to handle the scorps.

Best way to get there if you are lower than level 55 is to cross over just north of the road, stay in the woods until you can see Bliss to your Northwest and then cut through the desert on a straight line. Once you get there there is an insurance terminal, bank, a few stores (not general), and mission booths.
