Council of Testers
Things I'd like to see in ToRK...

1) Social clothing tab on the ragdoll and a timed chat command to switch from your armour/weapons to the social wear and vice-verca. In social mode, you would lose all benefits from any item in any of the other tabs except belt/NCU. This tab would have the same slots as the armour tab, but also have left and right hand slots. Social items like clothing, food etc would then be identified as going into the social slots rather than the armour tab ones. This also resolves many of the silly social armour restrictions.

2) More clothing, hand-held social items, emotes, rp items etc.

3) Terminals to generate indoor areas. These would work just like ACG missions, except the zone would (mostly) be of static layout depending on where the terminal is and on the type of area selected. Typical areas could be meeting halls with real seats, theaters with a stage and backstage, empty single-floor ACG 'mission' areas etc. Using the terminal would generate the area and a duplicatable key and an 'area' icon in a special section of the mission window, and last up to 24 hours or until the area icon is deleted. A mechanism similar to the one for team missions would allow updating, but you'd only have to target someone to use it (and they'd be asked if they want to accept).

4) Houses/Appartments with actual furniture and stuff...
