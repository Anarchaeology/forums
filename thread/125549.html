Not quite true, but close enough. There are items wearable with social armor, but they are few and far between.

The short list:

- Corroded rings from Foreman's
- Merlin bracer from Tarasque
- Head Skinchips (boss loot, but another type drops from the ace camp that is also wearable with GA)
- Sunglasses (all types AFAIK)
- Visors (including the Truspace Visor)
