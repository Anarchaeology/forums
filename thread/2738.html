Well.....
MAs are a lot of fun. Equip them in high lvl armour, use evade close combat and you will hardly ever be hit, and when u do you will only take minimum dmg if you kept up the ac. Use subconcious guidance to increase your citical hits, which means you will kick big ass. Max out brawl and use dirty fighter for more big dmg.

I am kinda undecided if you need the fists of shocking touch line of nanos or the meditate on an autumn leaf line or the restore essence line. Now, i used dimach so i could use a high lvl bird of prey. I loved it, but i had a huge ip struggle meaning my char ended up being jack of all trades....

Maybe someone else made an ma without these and can say whether there is any use for them, if you are a team player. MAs are very fast, very damaging chars. Although mine is only low level on the scale of things.....

On the flip side, if you are asking 'What profession to make your next char' i would hole heartedly offer up an MP (Meta Physicist). Check out my thread here : http://aoforums.funcom.com/showthread.php?threadid=2487
