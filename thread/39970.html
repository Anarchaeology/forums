Social Issues
I rarely waste my time posting, for as most people know, it is fuitile to try to make any point whatsoever. Nonetheless, I will take the time to do so simply because I have the time, and nothing better to do.

AO is nothing more than a mirror of the real world, whether you look at it from the standpoint of RP, or you consider it from an OOC perspective, simply because it involves people. It is those people who make it good, and those people who ruin it, plain and simple. This goes for both players, and GM staff.

I play this game because I enjoy it, and because I meet new friends all the time. However, I also meet a great deal of people I would just as soon have no contact with. It is easy enough to leave a hunting party or any team, and it is just as easy to ignore a single player that is getting on your nerves, however this is not the case when people start shouting in social settings.

When I go to a place like Rompa's, I go there to socialize and relax, to meet new people. However, when I am forced to turn of my vicinity chat because people are being rude and obnoxious, bickering back and forth, THAT is a problem. Certainly, I can just leave, but why should I have to...after all, it isn't me that is causing the problem, I'm just keeping to myself.

When people get upset, immediately I hear them bash AO and how they are leaving for SWG (or cancelling their account). Great! please do so quietly, as I plan on trying to enjoy AO without those type of people. Sometimes I wonder if things would improve if they put an age limit on the game, but that would be unfair, because I have seen older players be just as rude and immature. Sometimes I wonder if such individuals act that way in real life...because if they did I am sure they would get the "stuffing" beat out of them on a regular basis.

This post isn't about one incident, but about a variety of incidents that I have seen in some of the socialization spots. I, myself, have been personally hassled by people trying to "sit" on me, and then following me around when I move. When I politely ask them to cease, they do not stating that "It is only a game, and they pay for it and can do what they want." Clearly that additude is from someone whose "Mommy" pays for the game. Then again, why don't I keep that in mind when you are in the upper levels and I can kill you over and over in any PVP zone I find, that would be "REAL fun." In reality, I would never bother doing that, but it would be an example of the same type of mentality.

There are people you will like, and people you will not, however, it is still just a matter consideration just to try to let people enjoy themselves without being rude or offensive to everyone else. If you have an issue with someone, or even multiple people, use team chat or tells to work it out (or even just to bicker for that matter). Whether I am OOC or RP, I always try to be considerate. From an RP perspective, my character is a "ladies man" and loves to flirt, still I try not be be obscene about it. I always try to keep in mind there is a REAL person on the other end of that machine, a person that deserves my respect.

Thus is the sum of my post, I could have posted it anywhere, but a friend referred me here, and it seemed as good a place as any. I hope some of you have found worth therein. One last thing...Sezmra, Trayla, and Vashti (in alphabetical order), you are beautiful ladies and I love ya! Stay beautiful and save a dance (and a kiss) for me!

Some potential solutions...
I agree with you that common courtesy and respect for the players behind the character would solve the social problems we have seen mentioned in various posts on these forums.

It comes down to the fact that the people causing these problems do not believe in courtesy and dont appear to respect anyone, including themselves.

Most of us (I like to think) buy this game to entertain ourselves and others, and make an effort not to infringe on someone elses enjoyment of the game. However other people, slightly twisted in some way, seem to enjoy the fact that in an online avatar game they can actively ruin someone elses enjoyment of the game and get away with it.

Potential Solutions:

1) An RP server

That was one thing DAOC did right in my opinion. The world itself might not have had the social facilities that AO has to support RPing, but they laid rules down on the RP severs which did reduce the numbers of idiots. It wasnt 100% perfect, but people knew that if they were blatently OOC or socially disruptive they would be dealt with. I never heard of the kind of idiot spamming and rude posturing that seems to have gone on in Rompas.

Downside of this idea is that Funcom are unlikely to invest in an RP server, and even if they made a new one, people would have to chose between keeping established characters on a chaos server, or making new ones on a social server.

2) Defensive RPing

I tend to do this. I will find quiet out of the way bars and back yards for pleasant social chats and plot advancements. The idiots dont know where to find me, and so they dont ruin my fun. One time I went to have an IC chat in Rompas, I found I was hasselled by some inconsiderate person RPing a drunk who insisted on vomiting on our table, even after being asked not to (IC) and followed my character and her friend to Baboons when we left to get some peace. Not being confrontational people we gave up on the idea of RPing in a public bar and found a quiet back yard to chat in.

3) A more complete ignore

I would like people I ignore to become invisible to me; I dont want to hear them; I dont want to see their emotes and I dont want them to be able to interact with my character in any way.

4) Moderated Bars

This could be special form of guild housing, and perhaps the most ideal situation for the folks at Rompas. If Vixen Entertainment as an Org wanted a guild house that was in the form of a bar or night club then they should be able to get one. They should then be able to have a guest list AND a BANNED list. Officers in the org should be able to ban anyone they like from the bar. Once banned a character should get teleported from the bar and then find themselves unable to return.

Some or all of these things could help solve many of our social problems.
