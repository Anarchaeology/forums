I recently had the misfortune of seeing an NT with tank armor on and I was extremely disappointed that such a thing should happen. I'm not talking in terms of stats and the fact that tank armor has now ceased to become exclusive to the atrox professions, most notably the Enforcer, but more regarding the role-playing aspects of the game.

With respect to profession development, an NT or an MP with tank armor on or with a gun seems a huge contradiction. With particular reference to the Nanotechnician, although Funcom have strived to develop professions outside of the norm, there is no doubting that the aforementioned is what we would classically call a Mage. A mage casts spells and their reliance on these spells is apparent in their inability to use weapons or take mass amounts of damage. Therefore to see Nanotechnicians wandering around with Tank Armor and Weapons says a great deal about Funcom's failure to properly define the profession in the game.
