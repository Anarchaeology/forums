The background of dartelosolus
A long time ago in the year of 28973 a man named Ulrath a scientis of omni tek laboratories created another breed for rubi-ka Tha breed was called a nanomage. The professor made many nanomages probably over a thousund, But then a tragedy happened his wife and children got killed in a horrible vessel accident near omni forest. The professor got very lonely and decided to make his own nanomage for himself. He called the nanomage Dartelosolus and made him his personal slave. The professor made this nanomage very special and gave it special powers that allowed him to move thing with his mind. So dartelosolus serverd the professor over 40 years. But then the professor had become a old and angry man, So he started to boss dartelosolus around with anger and curse words. This made dartelosolus very angry so he killed the professor while he slept took all his credits and ran off to the city of rome. He lived there in the sewers for over 200 hundred years. The reason he lived in the sewers was because we was highly wanted by omni pol and other omni tek departments. But then one day dartelosolus climbed out of the sewers to hunt for food. When he reached the forest near rome he noticed a small reet, he shot it with a gun he stole from a omni pol guard. The reet dropped dead but when dartelosolus went to cut off the meat on the reet a large vesse appeared above him and landed near him. Omni pol officers came out of the vessel and arrested Dartelosolus, He was brought to omni HQ for analyzing. But then while in the laboratory chambers beneath omni HQ a large explosion took place and the building above the laboratory chambers collapsed, Killing most of the builidng guards, Then minutes later large numbers of caln soldiers entered the laboratory and killed all the guards and freeing all the prisoners. Dartelosolus was taken aboard a caln vessel but minutes after boarding the vessel, Large numbers of omni pol soldiers came to the area and whiped out all the clanners in the building. The clan vessel that carried dartelosolus got away and flew to the city of tir. Dartelosolus was given his clan ID and became a proud citizen of Tir.



Dartelosolus lived a happy live in Tir for over 50 years. One day dartelosolus went to get his usual breakfest at the Reet retreat bar, But when he got there he noticed a lot of omni tek employees were inside the bar. Dartelosolus then just walked up to the bar and ordered his usual food order. When a large omni tek atrox walked up to him and said "Your in my chair" Dartelosolus then moved to another chair on the same table, Then the atrox got angry and said"Your still at my table clan scum!" Those words made Dartelosolus very angry so he stood up pointed his finger at the atrox and the atrox flew to the other side in the room. A large number of omni employees then attacked dartelosolus but he got so angry that a strog force field appeared around him preventing the employees from hurting him. Dartelosolus then walked easy steps out of the bar. That same night dartelosolus had horrible nightmares about Ulrath his maker, And when he woke up his only words were "Omni Tek will be destroyed".
