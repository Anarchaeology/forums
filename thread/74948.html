Hullo
Greetings

My name is Mr L.Bleeder. I am posting here because i have recently been the victim of an awful lot of prejudice and have lost a member of my family, allow me to explain.

I live in a cave at lush fields, i generally tend to keep myself to myself. Me and my father (Mr C.Minion) used to work at the local mine along with the mutants, we feel that its nice to help out even if the mutants can seem a little stupid at times.

But one day i woke up and found that my dad was missing, i searched all over my cave and found nothing. When i asked mother (Mrs F.Stealer) where father had gone she said that he had probabaly just gone to work early and i was to go and help him. So off i went to help my father.

When i reached the mine i began to shout for him but i had no reply. Normally we dont go deep into the mine because its too dangerous so i started to get scared. I hovered down to the mine as fast as i could and found nothing but a lot of dangerous equipment and someone who kept saying
"ffs damn FC, WTF is an LB doing in this mission"
This scared me a little so i decided to hover away, but i still hadnt found my father. I decided to ask one of the mutants if they knew anything, i was rather worried because normally dad talks to the mutants and hes never in a good mood when he returns.

"hello Mr S.hade i was wondering if you could tell me where i could find my father"
He seemed to think for a moment before saying
"Step into the shadow"
This seemed an odd thing to say seeing as the nearest shadow was about 10 feet away but i went along anyway. After we had reached the shadow i asked the question again
"Step into the shadow"
this was getting rather odd..i was clearly in the shadow.
"Excuse me, i am already in the shadow, look, no sun"
"Step into the shadow"
i was starting to understand why dad was always unhappy after chatting to them.
"look i am already in the shadow so could you please just tell me if you have seen my father , yes or no?"
"Step into the shadow"
I realised that this chap was clearly insane and decided to just hover away slowly, he just stood in the same place playing around with his fingers.

After talking to a couple more mutants and getting similar replies i had lost hope, i decided to head back to the cave and tell mum that dad was gone. Then i saw something which i had never seen before, it was a group of people running around killing things. After talking to the mutants i could understand why they might do this so i decided to go and ask them if they had seen my father. This is where the prejudice starts.

"Excuse me i was wondering if you have seen my father around, he looks similar to me but is a little taller"

"Quick an LB run"
"i thought theyd nerfed em"
"no, they just nerfed the chaos minions"
"ahh, i wish the lbs would die too, they are just annoying"
As you may have guessed i was extremly hurt by this comment and i wanted to know what they meant about my father being "nerfed".
"Wait, come back i need to talk to you, i dont want to hurt anyone"

Sadly i was too late and they had already gone, all i heard was somone saying something along the lines of "High level alt".

I decided to go sit down and think about what they had said, what could "nerf" mean and was my father ok. Prehaps it meant he had been promoted or had gone on holidays. So i decided to stop worrying and start thinking of what i was going to do while dad was away, i could finally kill that annoying lizard in the cave without my dad complaining about every animal being equal, or i could ask mum to buy me the new game i wanted. Whatever i was going to do i had to do it now before my dad was back from nerf, so i got up and started hovering to the cave.

When i was on my way back i ran into the group again, i figured they had thought about their actions and come back to apologise to me, this made me feel even better. One of their members seemed to have got a lot bigger though and he had a rather menacing looking beam in his hand, i just thought he was going to work a the mine. When i got close they started to shout again

"There it is , by the tree, gogogogo athx"
Suddenly the big chap with the beam was running towards me, i panicked and ran straight into a tree, as i was recovering i suddenly felt an intense pain in my chest. I turned round to see the big guy waving his beam around, although he wasnt actually hitting me at all it was still extremly painfull and i could feel my life leaving me. Just as he was about to finish me off i dived for the nearest hill and let it absorb me into it, as i sat and healed my wounds i heard the team shouting abuse at me and "FC" because they could not loot me. I think i was finally beggining to see what was happening......



[Edit] I later recieved an e-mail from my father saying he was alive and well in pleasant meadows and i was not to try and contact him again [Edit].


The end :0
