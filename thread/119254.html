Symphony of Sin (TRA)
The TRA (Territorial Redistribution Alliance) was formed by the one time leader of Name Withheld, Xaeberfix and expanded to include his new org, Symphony of Sin as well as Devils Advocate, St. Michaels Order, Black Sun and Name Withheld. The goal of the alliance is to help organisations looking to own land aquire it for a price. Holding up the highest ideal of the Omni-Tek corporation, profit above all else, the TRA seeks to redistribute lands currently held by the clans to the Omni-Tek and neutral buyers to use for their own purposes.

In conclusion to the alliance, Symphony of Sin would like to open it's doors to any new recruit who is interested in contributing to this cause. Please contact Brittain, Xaeberfix or Hammerstrike to schedule a meeting.

Thank you and we hope to do business with you soon.

Brittain
Symphony of Sin
