Wow! It all makes sense now!

Engineers only *build* the robots. So, with that in mind, I would like my trader to be able to control slayer-droids.

Let's look at the big picture

*Traders are fair in combat (PvE) - bear in mind that even under OE, DOCs and Engineers wield QL 200 shotties. Their wrangles don't benefit the traders, they benefit others (unless of course you're delusional enough to think that money is worth anything to most players after they have their implants in and yalm in stock).
*Traders have the best trade skills. (Trade-Trader? hmmm). The trader will need to build and install implants, use tutoring devices, wield tradeskill enhancing items, or ask for buffs to reach the same NP potential of NTs and MPs or the same chemistry potential of engineers.

*Engineers, alone, are fair in combat (PvE) - They get the exact same QL 200 shottie that the trader gets (of course, they needed the trader to wield it - but they have the same weapon none-the-less).
*Engineers have the same potential for trade skills, but no buffs to enhance them. The engineer will need to build and install implants, use tutoring devices, wield tradeskill enhancing items, or ask for buffs to reach the same potential as a trader in some trade skills (total of 125 difference in the 5 skills buffed skills if the engineer is too anti-social to ask for a buff).
* Engineers get a slayer-droid. I have watched CL 120s soloing aces. Woops! Did we forget this 'advantage' of the engineering class?

There are other differences, of course, but many things remain the same. For example, traders get team heals while Engies get bot heals, traders get mezz while engies have trimmers, and traders get "charm" (ARGH!) while engies get shields.

You see, it's all pretty even to me - someone who has played both an engie and trader to 100+.

So, with that in mind, I would like my trader to be able to control slayer-droids. You get your buffs, we get the bot, and everyone is equal.

Sound good?

Argh. Trade = exchange of goods and services. Trader = merchant who facilitates said exchange, and earns his money in this way. Engineer = man who makes the things traders sell. The trade in Trade skill is actually an archaic usage, harkens back to when hand worker/ crafter professions were referred to as "trades." It's a different context, and I'll wage that "trade skill" crept its way into MMOG vernacular thanks to goofy fantasy games where people feel compelled to use archaic speech. An AO trader is a merchant, not a craftsman, ergo he should not have skills and skill buffs that are pertinent to technical ends.
