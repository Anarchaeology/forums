Someone should get a res...
Now, I may be wrong here, but I don't think any class has a res nano. I don't see why there is no way to bring someone back with like 50% of their xp since their last save or just 50% of what they died with. Now, I play a doc which I started the day the game came out and just started playing again two weeks ago after about four months off. So I may be wrong and there may be someone with a res. But there should definitly be one a doc or some other class can use by lvl 60-70. Now, I have two reasons for this.

1. Insurance terminals are awsome for your first forty levels or so but now whenever I die I lose up to 200k which can take hours to get up. Plus I'm a complete grouping doc who sticks nothing in offense so I can have better heals for my guild when we adventure. I mean I'm glad some of you have made docs your happy with that solo but in large groups at later levels you just can't really. So since everything attacks me everytime I heal for over 300 (and docs who group know this is still true) I die a lot unless someone is competent enough to attack the mob. Which never happens in just random groups. So I'd like to salvage some xp as I think everyone would.

2. If I'm in clon**** at some mission in the middle of ****ing nowhere I would like to bring whoever just died back to life so I don't have to wait forty minutes for them to get there.

There is really nothing gamebalance wise that would be hurt by the ability to bring people in your group back to life. And if you made it something that could be cast on your body or your resurected body then it would be one more thing docs could make money off of. Which they need badly.

Now, I'm quiting my doc because I'm ready to play something else. But when I hit sixty with soldier or whatever else I play I would like to know that I could get a doc to res me if I needed it.

Anyways, if it's a dumb idea I don't care, but me and my buddies have been talking about it while we wait for player x to get his ass back to the shades in 20k. It's sure as hell makes more sense than docs having a grid nano that takes forever to cast.

An alternative
Check my post regarding Fixers being able to activate those reclaim terminals that are out in the middle of nowhere with no insurance terminals associated with them.

If a particularly suited profession was given the ability to hack these terminals, it would give most of the benefits of the 'rez' ability, but still do it in a uniquely 'Anarchy Online' way.

Imagine hunting in Deep Artery Valley and stumbling across an abandoned remote outpost with a reclaim terminal. You know, a couple empty shacks and a picnic table. Well, it's of no use now. But ... if you had a Fixer with the right skills, he could hack the terminal to transfer your teams insurance data to that location. See where this is leading?

Your NT overburns his nuke and draws agro. Of course, he dies. But, now instead of ressing in 20K (three zones away), he materializes just over the hill at the outpost.

If the Bureacrat in the party managed to cover him with the soon-to-come experience saving nano just before he croaked, then everyone has done their job.

Of course, if you happen to have an Engineer in the party, you can simpley Beacon Warp him back from 20K. But what if the Engineer dies?

Variety is the ****e of life and this simple change might make exploration and riskier hunting in far off lands more attractive to those groups with the Fixer who can hack the terminals and the 'Crat who can prevent experience loss.
