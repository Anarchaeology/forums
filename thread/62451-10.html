Do we all see the irony here?

It seems to me that after battling to gain control of tara there was a group of players that basically got along with each other regardless of guild and things went very smoothly. This group of players was for the most part also very generous and fair. Additionally, because of the level of trust among these players, little effort was put into organizing kill teams to control the looting of Tara. With the exception of level limits and clan alignment no restrictions were put on participation in Tara raids and loot rolls. They did this despite the fact that they could have easily organized the best clan tank/dmg/heal teams on RK2 and split the loot only among themselves.

As a result, numerous people started showing up and participating in Tara raids. The more people that showed up the more complaining that occurred. Funny thing is most of the complaining was done by people who took no part in the battles to regain Tara and who contributed the least to the killing of Tara or organizing raids.

Then a self fulfilling prophecy began. Complaints about level limits resulted in an ever changing level requirement. This displeased the longer time regulars many of whom had yet to win a roll at Tara. At the same time accusations of loot swapping and ninja'd items (mostly by people who only recently joined in the raids) reached greater and greater levels, certain people, apparently motivated by these accusations, decided that they would ninja and loot swap. This ultimately led to this weeks actions (right or wrong, it doesn't matter).

These actions have resulted in bickering among the group of players who worked the hardest to gain control of Tara and put the most effort into organizing Tara raids. This is same group of players who could have rightfully owned all the T loot for the past couple of months, but were more generous and fair than to do so. Unfortunately this generousity and fairness seems to have created the environment in which many people who have put the least effort into gaining control of Tara or organizing raids do little but show up at raids complain and, if they are lucky, walk away with loot.

Obiviously, this is just my perception of the situation and the events leading up to it.
