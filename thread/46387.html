Whiney whiney whine whine
So i try to move my river6 up in my inventory and my finger slips. The gun is gone. My first impulse is to send a petition, then i just laugh at myself.

This may not be most appropriate in the pvp boards but i dont really bother with the rest so you're stuck with it. I just wanted to say that every time there's a blatant FC mistake that they blatantly dont care about i get less and less mad. Pretty soon i'm not going to care at all and that's when ill leave.

This certainly isnt a threat, there's no reason to believe that any of us are important enough or obnoxious enough to make FC care about something like this. I'm tired of being thoroughly disheartened with this game though. The honeymoon's over FC and you're going to be in for some real tough times soon as a few new games come out.

There's plenty of other things that have driven me mad lately but the individual instances are really not the issue. What is proved again and again is that it is a policy of Funcom to ignore their mistakes. Move on, maybe next patch will fix it maybe the one after that. I cant even imagine how complex this game is to run, but complexity does not provide them an excuse to ignore their players.

So consider this the last manifestation of my good faith, my last piece of advise to FC: at least do us the favor of PRETENDING you care. I wish the best for the employees of FC and i hope for a miracle for the players. The economy, high lvl "content", the pvp system, the inability to track or return items, the inability to reset players to a position before a zone crash.... get that into a patch and you'll have a hundred check in the mail, screw the land takeover.
