Done for now...
I'm done! At last! Stick a fork in me please!

I've played almost every spare moment for the last three months, but I have to take a break now. The game has degraded to the point that there is no fun left. Every patch seems to either create new problems and/or make the game even harder to play.

My typical session consists of :

1) spend 30 minutes finding a mission I'm willing to take. Not one that contains any useable items - the 50% missions (all I can complete) reward you with trivia that's well below the level you need to succeed, but perhaps I can sell it or give it to a friend or guildmate.

2) spend 20-30 minutes running to the site (this is not a complaint about mission distance though - it's a big planet! However, bugs with run speed etc make this worse than it has to be.)

3) spend 3-4 hours completing the mission itself. This consists of running for the exit 20+ times because the mission is stacked with uber MoBs, multiple Mobs in the same room, etc as has been mentioned many times.

I'm used to this. I'm also used to, but finally worn out on, dieing and losing my hard won exp due to bugs. Most frequent one, as tonight, was showing plenty of health, getting no damage msgs, but suddenly dieing. Mind you - not trying to exploit - I had a Manteze in the room with me and knew I was getting hit, but was healing and making good progress on taking him out - can't help it if his hits weren't showing up - it's a game bug that happens all the time. Then, all of a sudden with over 60% health left showing, I get the dreaded "your items will be made available.." msg. Losing, three plus hours of experience and wasting three of my valuable hours of personal time. I suspect that there was an extra "invisible" Manteze in the room that I had pulled from the back part of the mission area but couldn't see.

So, I'm finally done. Taking a few weeks off from the game. I'll come back then and see if anything's improved. I love this game, but it has turned into nothing but work, labor and frustration. I have enough of that in RL - I'm looking for fun!!!!

You need to quit striving so hard to make the game difficult! It is supposed to be fun! I think you are so scared of people maxing out too quickly that you just make it continuously harder for everyone else. I recommend that before you make any change you ask "Does this make the game less fun or more frustrating?"! If so, don't make the change!!!

Anyway, I could add a number of creative comments on gameplay improvements, but they've all been said before, and there is no evidence that anything is being done to remove the frustration. You fix some bugs, yes, but you tweak the gameplay and make it harder every time.

I love this game, but it is too hard and no longer fun! Please don't let it die like this! I'll be back in a couple of weeks. I only hope it's not to write an obituary!
