Dream weapons...ideas anyone?
I speak specifically for the doc. I realize there are a lot of docs out there that use Vektor's for weaps. But docs specifically have to sacrifice weapon skills and those associated skills for nano skills to heal better and other skills if they want to do cool things like team grid. Team grid you say? I didn't know that docs could team grid??!! Wellm neither did I until the other day. But, I sacrificed my shotgun skill, ranged init and strength (all necessary for vektor equip) to do so.

So, here's my proposal: Why not create weapons that use skills like Matt Met. or Bio Met or Pharm? How about a Nanogun Departiclizer [QL 60 does 20-300(300) uses energy ammo and does energy damage] that utilizes NP and Pharm to equip? Or a Bio-Pathogen Projector [QL 60 does 3-127(200) uses energy ammo and does chemical damage and poisons the target 42 points at intervals?] that utilizes Pharm and Bio met?

I'm sure there are other professions that have to sacrifice ip's as well to obtain combat skills. I created a pure doc...no combat skills. Great healer/team addition...but forget PvP and solo missions and the like.

Please submit your ideas for new weapons based on that same idea here. I have little experience with the other professions besides doc/ma/adv. So comment as you will...I'm sure a lot of you have creative ideas to add.

prof weapons
I like the idea of profession-specific weapons for people that don't mind outputting a little less damage in exchange for profession-specific "procs" (pardon the EQ term )

For example, the Doc gun that would hurt enemy inits (traquilizer gun?) or infect them with a short-term DoT (such as a poison or disease) but with a low normal damage output and long refire time. In exchange, the requirments for these weapons should either be switched to non-combatant skills (such as nano skills) or just made much lower, yet with perhaps a level and class req to avoid serious twinking.

Playing as an engineer I'd look forward to weapons such as an EMP grenade launcher (procs a stun on mechanical creatures, resistable,) a nanocloud interference shotgun (procs nano drain or inturruption of executing programs, or perhaps some type of "dispell" on enemy buffs) or other proccing weopons that would effect mechanical creatures/items/nanobots. something useful, not overpowered, and tailored to our specific classes. I'd hope for more of these types of weapons for the support classes that arn't going to be doing a ton of regular damage anyway.


just an idea...
