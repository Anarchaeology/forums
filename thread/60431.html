As previously posted, the "best" prof in one situation is not the "best" in another.
An enforcer/ma is devastating in the arena's close quarters, while a soldier has an advantage in mass wars, due to TMS/ranged alpha with a bite. :P

Personally, I would hold enforcers high on the list, as their massive hp allows them time to think through their actions (well, mostly ;p), and they pack a good punch when up toe to toe with their foes. Downside is, of course, they're only good at melee. :P

Ma's would be comparable, with high evades and a heal (which heals 700ish every 3 heals :|), and kickass critting.

The reason I put enforcers ahead of ma's, is their NR, which gives them the edge over debuffing professions (well, traders/mp's anyways, since doc debuffs and crown goes in almost every time :P)
