If you need help go to the beginners corner. If you want to change human nature, forget it.

Funcom has two distinct issues:

1) Gameplay bugs- still rampant, totally inappropiate for a non beta game. This aspect drives paying players crazy and should.

2) Balancing professions- this is a process in evolution. There is no way Funcom could have looked at all the thousands of variables pre-release and produced a perfectly balanced game. Some oversights were glaring and player feedback and test servers is helping this, albiet slowly.

The problem is that some gripes are legit and many are whines. Everyone wants to be uber. There is no way, based on human nature, that his game will EVER be balanced as perceived by all players. Funcom has the unenviable task of sorting through the thousands of complains and separating food from fodder. That is why the process is so slow.

My character, an adventurer, needs some help. So I quit him and am happily an enforcer now, until my adventurer is tweaked. So learn to develop multiple characters.


MY POINT: People will always complain. Ignore them and play your own game. I am not a newbie, but if I ever need gameplay advice I post in the beginners corner. People will answer you there and there is not much whining. Just play your own game and don't worry about the twinkers, exploiters and whiners. That only matters in PvP anyway and you can mostly avoid that.
