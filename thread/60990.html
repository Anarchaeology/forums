Trash King? 20min spawn again, plz!
Of all my experience in AO Trash King is amoung the worst. I liked it when the quest was just starting and Trash King was spawning every 20 or so minutes. Now he has been completely nerfed to spawn anywhere from 20min-4 hours (and I have never seen him spawn any shorter than 3 hours :/).

I for the most part do not know why Funcom decided to nerf his spawn, I understand he had some phat loot and teams were camping for that stuff and not the rack but you removed the loot AND nerfed his spawn. I think that changes his spawn time was a bad move because over the summer more and more ppl have joined Ao and there are a lot more higher lvls that can camp him now.

Im not the kind that is like "F**k camping", I like camping on some mobs who drop rare items like the Shades of Lucubration, but cmon, Trash King is a QUEST mob! Quest mobs should not have to be camped for 4 hours with a team of 5 lvl200 Fixers just to win the loot.

Im not in a giant guild and most of the ppl in my guild dont want to stand around for 4 hours waiting for TK to spawn just to get a "quest" item.

I think that TK should be returned to his original spawn time of 20min. I think this will help lessen the amount of camps there at a time and ppl can obtain the lvl50 quest item. If FC doesnt want to change TK then they should put the 4 hour spawn on all the other mobs such as Greasy, Nut n Bolts, etc. Why is TK the only one with 4 hour? :/


Just my thoughts..
