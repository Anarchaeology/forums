On missions where they do not give you the zone name, right click and upload the mission. Then hit "P" to bring up your overland map. There will be a flag on the map where the mission is. This will give you a general idea of what zone it is, depending on how well you know where zones are laid out, or what map you are using (some skins have better maps with them).
