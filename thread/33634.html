The Joint was Jumping: Exclusive! (Aktrez: Frantic, Faithful, Fabulous)
Ehrynn was ushered efficiently into Aktrezs secluded studio dressing room by Teirrie, one of the new Vixens. The entertainer was pacing and wringing her hands, something wasnt right. As the door closed behind her, Aktrez motioned for Ehrynn to come and sit down.

Im so glad you could come by to see me on such short notice, Ehrynn. She paused, Can I have the staff bring you something?
Ehrynns interest was peaked, Im fine just now, Aktrez, why dont you tell me what this is all about.

Aktrez went back to pacing. She smiled weakly, I just finished producing my virtual video for my Platinum Single Faithful.
I know but thats not why you asked me to come by is it, Aktez?

The entertainer stopped pacing and sat down across from Ehrynn. My temper got the best of me and
Ehrynn finished the statement, you shot your mouth off without thinking. Aktrez nodded. Youve made a terrible mistake and want to set the record straight, not so? Aktrez nodded again. Okay, lets have it, what happened?

Aktrez let out a little sigh, I was in a meeting with Throll and Garrisen, about Rompa. The topic came up about Clanners at the club. Ehrynn looked up briefly, and then went back to making notes. Garrisen wanted all the Clanners out of Rompa. I could only think about GridStream Productions, they are for all intent and purposes neutral, and wellI said anyone was welcomed at Rompa including Clanners. Gawd, Ehrynn I didnt mean it the way it came out. I hate this war.

The entertainer leaned back and closed her eyes; Ehrynn noticed a tear trace its way down Aktrezs cheek. She brushed another one away and took a deep breath.
Ehrynn wanted to lighten the mood, So what have you learned, Dorothy?

Aktrez stared for a moment at Ehrynn and then smiled, Ive learned that Ive been blind to the problems at Rompa, but that is all going to change. I will enforce the rules and Vixen Entertainment wont serve Clanners. Its not like I can keep them out, you know that from your encounter with Lev Rivers. But, Ill adhere to the rules.

Ehrynn nodded. One question Aktrez, are you a loyal Omni-Tek employee?
Without a moments hesitation, the entertainer said, Yes, absolutely! I just wish there could be some accommodation for GridStream Productions but thats a separate issue.

Ehrynn interrupted, I know you read my column. I thought I made it clear about Throlls interest in loyal Omni-Tek employees. Aktrez nodded. Dont forget that point or you and your girls will wind up asking Harry, over in Lush Fields, for permission to run his advance store lounge.

The entertainer frowned slightly, Im a loyal Omni-Tek Employee, a little of the old familiar fire had crept back into Aktrezs voice, Print that, Ehrynn.

Ehrynn grinned, All right, dear, now tell me what are your future plans for the club?

Aktrez beamed, Oh, Ive got a full slate of new shows and events lined up. Im working out the details and the dates right now.
Ehrynn winked at Aktrez, Dont forget, The Joint was Jumping, gets the exclusive.
No problem, JWJ, will get the exclusive, I promise.

Getting to her feet Ehrynn smiled, Ill show myself out, get some rest, you look spent. On the way back downtown, Ehrynn decided she really liked her new job.
