Tutoring... how does it work?
Now, I've had it on good authority that Tutoring does, in fact, work. I've heard of people tutoring in the realm of 400 Comp Lit to others. But... I haven't been able to get it to work myself.

With a skill of 500ish (from backpack, implants, +20 buff and skill from attributes), using a Q100 tutoring device, I hit somebody with it. I lost 5 points of skill, and theirs didn't go up. Now... I've heard that the buff is in fact invisible, but is there. But they certainly didn't gain 200 points of Comp Lit. I tried hitting them with it until I had lost 200 points (took a while). Still no luck.

Now I know that some skill is being transferred, as I managed to get my NP tutored and assemble an implant 5 points over my displayed skill (with a Q50 device used). But, I wasn't able to assemble one 50 points over my level... even after the tutoring person had lost over 50 points of his NP skill. So it was transferring something between 5 and 50 points, for a device that required 100 NP and 250 Tutoring to use.

DOes anyone have any information on this skill? Before I start sinking IP into it, I want to be sure if and how it works
