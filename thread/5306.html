Trade skills not an option.
I play a doc on r1 and was excited to hear when we could finally make treatment kits, and stims. This sounded like a nice break from kill kill er.... well heal heal in my case.
So all excited I went and made my first treatment kit. Started small, just to check out how it worked, and made a ql 19 treatment kit. Nice stats, almost 500 heal only need 101 skill, and to make it better comes with 75 charges. Now what was not so great. The price to make it was crazy. Even at this low of ql, all items obtained from a basic store, it still cost me well over 10k. There is no way this trade skill will be an alternative to a doc to make money in its current state. First off no one will buy these things at a price where you can make a profit. They might if it healing wasnt so needed for every class because of the need for implants. With everyones treatment so high, why spend 15 or 20k on a treatment kit no matter how good it is, when they can buy a ql70 from shops for alot less.
I also made a ql27 treatment kit and awsome stats again. This one cost more then DOUBLE the ql 19. Im not saying change the skill any, that seems fine, how about lowering the prices to a level where it becomes a useful skill. A level 19ish kit should never have to be sold to consumers for 10k, thus the parts needed to make them should be much less.
Oh, and the stims are even worse.
