Clan Leaders Respond to the Return of Radiman
Friday night I (Skaat) contacted numerous Clan Leaders to obtain thoughts and comments on two questions concerning 'The Return of Radiman': 1) Does this mean more 'official' leadership is immanent? and, 2) Are you glad Radiman is back?

Following are the responses in approximate order received.

I would of course hope that Mr. Radiman is back to lead, I think with his recent hiatus, and all but abandoning the clans and the COT without notice, that my trust in him has wavered. However, he did respond to our Rally in Tir. Our stance has been all along for Mr. Radiman and the CoT to lead us and unite the clans. Clanners working together for one purpose is the only way we will win our total freedom from those who oppress us. Now that he is back I would hope that is what he intends, I for one will be watching him....

hmmmm.. Am I glad Mr. Radiman is back.. That is a hard question to answer. First thoughts are yes, then doubts seep in. He left us once and failed to lead and give direction and because of that we are floundering in the wind all Clans going and doing their own thing without concern of the out come. Mr. Radiman's absence has done great harm to the peace efforts and to the morale of the clans. So, am I glad he is back.... I can not fully answer that question at this time... Myself and Opposing Force will be watching him. If he decides to lead us to victory we will support him... if he chooses to be silent and do nothing we will replace him.

Rorie
President of Opposing Force
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the time that Radiman was gone, hatred and distrust have ruled the day. It is time for him to unite the clans under the banner of peace once and for all. He can't do this from inside a cave, or behind a desk. He has to be among the people.

- Szentasha, leader of Unity of the Rose.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Question 1. Radiman has been lacking leadership for the clans for some time now. His lack of responsibility to just up and leave to think over things is not acceptable. He left the clans when they probably needed him most, but now that he has been gone I feel he has lost his power. I also feel that Radiman will not be able to rally the clans as he thinks he can. Radiman will be replaced!

Question 2. To me it makes no difference if Radiman is back, myself and other clanners will carry on our plans for things in the future that are already set in stone.

Redruum
President of Clan Anarchist Syndicate CoA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Answer to 1. I hope so, even if I don't agree with the Council, something is better than nothing.

Answer to 2. Yes, but the circumstances I am not happy about, it had to come to the eve of a coup for him to say anything. I wouldn't be surprised if it was nothing more than a little piss on the fire that is raging.

Rederon
President of Knights of Sacrifice -{Order of Virtue}-
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

With Radimans return I wouldnt get my hopes up too high about any immediate actions by the council. Radiman has led the council into a coma and its a long way to recovery.

However, this pilgrimage of which he speaks, we can only hope that he has walked among the people of Rubi-Ka; felt their pain and awoken. My hope is still there that Radiman is still a clanner in heart and see the deperation the indetermination of the Council has caused.

Yours truly,
Phelsior
president of Ancarim Iron Legion

1) InI am certainly happy that Mr Radiman have decided to return. We need someone, now more then ever, that can unite the Clans in the war against Omni-Tek. The Council of Truth is by far the largest and most respected of all Clan alliances and without any official guidance from them the last months, InI am under the impression that many of the Clans have been more scattered then ever before, only following their own path and without unity with eachother.

2) InI am by this very happy Radiman is back and hope he see what many of us already seen. That the war against Omni-Tek cant be avoided at this point and all Clans must unite in arms to face the evil!

Jah bless!
