Psychic scores of various MOBs?
Normally, the last thing that we would care about is what a particular attribute score of a particular MOB would be, but the Psyche check nerf on charm/fear makes this a very important question.

Let us take human MOBs for instance. Many classes have stopped spending IP in Psychic well before level 50, perhaps as early as level 25 or 30. Is this taken into account when the computer computes Psychic scores? Or does a level 60 MP have the same score as a level 60 Fighter? Is race taken into consideration, since it is much more likely for a nanomage NPC to have maxed out Psychic than an Atrox.

And do non-humans have good Psychic scores also? I'm talking about animals and robots, the kind that don't have any nanos.
