Very good post York
Very good post York - I really enjoyed the SWG synopsis. I'd like to touch on a few of the notable flaws of AO that aren't truly bug ridden but have been voiced in the past by many.

First is the apartments. Many people have said this before and I'll say it here to. Right now I think its 10 items you can have and after that they start self deleting. There aren't any true furniture items like couches, chairs, beds, etc. and people can steal things from your apartment like an entire painting or plant when you let them in. If this feature were fixed, it would provide a giant RP approach and I know some people say they don't do it because of children but the EULA specifies you have to have be an adult to have an account considering you have to have a major credit card or paypal to use the game. Yes, there will always be sex of some sort in any MMORPG game - it happens and its part of the lure for mature players who are adults.

Secondly - faction. AO does not have a faction system and therefore you can kill your own sided guards over and over and never be hunted. People tend to disrupt the game a lot by killing their own guards and it would make for interesting results if they did have a finely crafted faction system in place. Not having one detracts from RP.

Thirdly - Storyline. Many of the top influencing people that worked on the storyline and enhanced it were canned (fired) by Funcom. I found this out while a Professional and I knew a few of them. CZ says that the remaining storyline personnel are still good but I have yet to see any major plot present in the game recently and if it is present - it's not being seen. The sad fact is that only guild leaders who have access to the leaders channel see the introduction of smaller storyline encounters and most turn it off (due to spam) or never tell their guild. If you are guildless you don't even have a chance with the storyline because the event will be closed or over before you get there. That is of course, if there is an event.

Fourth - Bugs. Yes there are many bugs still current in the game that were there from beta and have yet to be fixed. The worst "BUG" of them all is that AO does not clear memory when changing zones and instead loads it through paging which grows and grows and grows and never stops until you quit AO and you see the closing bar which releases the memory that was being used. So this causes a large performance issue later on as you play the game unless you have a super computer which can still have problems.

Pathing - Pets still do not path correctly. Monsters / People in missions can go invisible if they move too fast into a few rooms ahead and you have to move out of map range and back in to see them. The same thing can be said for mobs that go invisible and are still able to shoot through walls. (something that was not fixed since beta).

Zoning - Zoning can crash you when doing many different things and zones themselves go down quite a bit do to proxy failures. Even when dying in a large populated zone (by PvP even) you can find yourself still standing around and not die only to be killed a second time by a person and then finally zone.

I could go on and on with other major bugs but it's pointless. The problem is Funcom does not have the money nor the resources to keep the game to above par standards. The reason why other games do well and why SWG will do even better is because funcom can't do what SWG can.
