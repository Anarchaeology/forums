<<ooc>> Role Players "Club"
I have found recently, that the members of this game are split into three groups

Role Players: Those who stick to their character at LEAST 75% of their time in game. And who create storylines to drive a specific character.. or group of characters.

PvPers/Griefers: Normally in the same group unfortunately. These are the people who enjoy killing anything they can lay their hands on. Especially other players

Undecided: The people who know they like to hunt/level.. but also like to party in the bars and socialize when they have a chance.


On some games, there are specific servers for each of these types. But, here on Rubi-Ka we all mingle into one.

I for one am a hardcore role player. I level characters on my other account and use all my own characters to roleplay. I am very rarely out of character in the game (even though it may seem very real to you... most of it is playing) And I work very hard to set up story lines and plots for my guild and my character.

Now... How do we seperate the PvP/Griefers from the ROleplayers? Well.. we can't really.. as we all pay our $11 to play.

<<</me whips out her soapbox>> I would ask though, that you try to respect the roleplayers. I have heard SO many people getting almost offended that someone has the imagination to come up with a storyline. If you dislike what you see.. please just keep it to yourself. <</me steps off her soapbox>>

BUT... I do have an idea that might help us, the roleplayers, out.

I am calling forward all of the roleplayers.. individual or in a guild.. that are dedicated to making the storylines in this game Real!! And Large! I need people who would like to plan and run storylines throughout the game.

I am proposing that we develop a SL "union" persay. This will encompass all regions. We will have monthly meetings <<ooc.. in a private, neutral area>> To discuss current storylines and how we can all become a part. We can also plan ahead and bring ideas to the members to create a new story each month - all running together. And when Funcom has their events, we can meld right in using our own storylines as a beginner. Doing this might also give the folks at Funcom a little extra boost, if needed, to help us out with the stories.

If you are interested in this union. Please post your name, Org (if guilded) and faction on this thread. We will then organize a meeting time and date to have our first meeting and discuss storylines for August and September.

If anyone is interested.. I am currently working on one called "A warning" If you would like to read about this current storyline.. and how it is unfolding, Please see the post by Ghostofrizzior.

I do hope that we get a large group together to form this union. I think it will not only be fun to create.. but, will also be fun to act out.

Thank you for your time.

Thanks for the press, Savoy

Aye, The Neon Zebra was created for just this sort of thing. To give small and large groups looking a space to plan, coordinate and socialize a place that they can do all that without worrying so much about the effects of harrasement by griefers.

And, by griefer, I do not mean the non-rper or the PvPer. Many of these players respect and give room to their fellow rp-players. The Neon Zebra is a place for anyone who respects the way others play, who can give space to their fellow players and can even encourage them by simply supporting their right to their own play style.

The Neon Zebra is not for rude, distruptive or childish players. Posts that demean others for the way they play, posts that bash the game or Funcom, posts that attack the player directly are not welcomed at the there.

Being sociable and rping is all about cooperation. Working with each other for big and small stories. Working with each other to allow all of us to enjoy these moments. We all have a stroy, or a character that we want to share. It's up to each of us to help, or atleast accommodate, others around us. I enjoy watching the unfolding of someone else's hard work as much as sharing my own.

The Neon Zebra is in no way meant to replace the official forums. Rather, it is there as another tool for rpers and socializers to take advantage of. I hope that the Zebra will become a place that people can go once they are ready to involve themselves with the individual and group events that are constantly happening in our world. Once a player has had a chance to dip their toe into the social and rp community, so to speak, they can come to the Zebra to really start to involve themselves and to find plots to join, people to meet and events to jump into.

Well, this is getting bigger than I planned . Basically, The Neon Zebra is there for you guys, the players - rpers and socializers. There are lots of ways to use it and I'm willing and able (as much as my html skills will let me) to help make it even more useful for you all.

I like this idea...
OOC:

I think both Savoy and Aktrez are right.

I think all RPers should be communicating about their plots and ideas. I like the idea of an IC meeting but I think that with more than half a dozen people it is likely to get out of hand. Lots of words from lots of different people with a hard time making progress unless people are prepared to sit for hours keeping their own chat to a minumum and reading that of the 'main speaker'.

Even big IRC conferences/discussions get out of hand and they are normally heavily moderated with more windows for private dialog and ways of preventing people from speaking until its their 'turn'.

I think the best way to communicate plot basics and to overcome timezone differences is to use the web/forum to give out initial information. That means anyone who is interested reads up on as much as they want to.

If people running plots can put up an IC and OOC summary and maybe some idea of plot hooks for bringing in a few new characters, and their idea of how 'random passers by' or 'extras' could get involved in their plot.

If there was a face to face set of meetings it might be best run more like the conferences where there are a number of talks going on in one timeslot and you go to the one that interests you the most. The Plot Director/Team for each plot then has an hour..90 minutes...what ever to talk about their story and answer questions on it...if that's what people like.

It maybe that such discussions can be done in forums. If each plot had a private forum with access controlled by the Plot Director then quite a bit could go on.

Stories could could evolve...people would add write ups of what happened to them....PDs ( Plot Directors...made this up because GM alraedy has another meaning in AO) could add new plots hooks, request extras for particular dates and timezones....

I know that some work has gone on and is going on for websites that could support this structure, but I will leave it to the creators to inform and volunteer their creations as and when they wish.
(Domu has already volunteered her site since I started writing this post.)

Savoy is right for me in that the small personal RPing is the most satisfying. Many of these kinds of plots are hard to open up, and sometimes they cover topics and emotions people are only happy RPing with people they know.

However if people are happy to provide write ups that give other RPers an OOC view of their character and their motivations, then adhoc meetings in bars or at parties become that much more meaningful because you know what IC things to talk to them about to have some small talk about. You can make a judgement about what you are likely to have heard OOC and use it to make conversation.

It may be necessary to write in big letters "This story is OOC info only" and have a section for each story which lists Public Information.

For example the adventures of H&H are there to give players an OOC view of the characters, so other players get an idea of their likes and RP background. From reading that story I would say that someone could realistically decide to have heard that Hirunda is recently from off-world, and that Heket is the strange blind nano-human who grew up in Athen. But perhaps not even that...H&H are small fry who have barely interacted with the world yet; your average person shouldn't have heard of them. On the other hand someone could decide to RP being a local from Athen who had heard of Heket because they grew up their as well. Erm sorry...went off topic.

Attempt at Summary:

I want a place where I can go read about all the 'open' plots that other RPers are prepared to write about. I would expect to just read the story summaries and news of most of them and enjoy watching all the stories unfold. I would occasionally be inspired by a write up, suggested plot hook or an extras advert to involve myself in someone else's story. This would give me the big picture so I felt I was part of a living world.

The knowledge I would gain from reading about the bigger picture would give me the confidence and ideas for interacting with other RP characters in game on a more adhoc and spontaneous basis, giving me more personal RP.

As well I would still be part of smaller more interesting plots that I was accepted into or part of co-writing. Just because a plot was written up, wouldn't mean it was open to everyone to join in with, and people shouldn't get offended if they can't get involved if the PD only wants to run for a small core of people they want to know. By sharing the existance of a plot and asking for occasional extras the creators have already added to the life of our world.

Actual Summary:

1) Yes lets get the pro-active RPers talking to each other and sharing ideas.

2) I think we need the right website to communicate effectively for this task.

3) Large scale communication should encourage large events for those who love them, and support and inspire a myriad of smaller personal plots and fluff
