All that glitters is not gold - 100 million credits for kicking Clanner butt!
Fact: Buffer Funcom Story Dev term; little story 'drops' that have nothing to do with anything, but are placed in to appease the players.

I want to believe that 100 million credits for kicking Clanner butt! is the beginning of another Golden Age for the storyline, where instead of the usual one piece of story-news per three months, it will in-fact be the first piece in a long series of news lasting the next three months-plus without interruption or break.

Like the Halloween '01 to mid-Feb '02 period I keep banging on about.

However, as an insider-friend just said to me, It is a "bone" thrown to a barking dog, but the bone has very little meat on it, and is pretty rank with decay. And that pretty neatly sums up how it feels.

Imagine... Oh god, they're still moaning about the storyline. We let them keep their forums, what more do they want?! Quick, we need a buffer. Make up something that has nothing to do with anything, something safe and more-or-less meaningless to the storyline, and post it ASAP. Oh, and we better use Ross, we've not had him say anything in ages, hopefully that'll quiet the moaning Omni players.

Funcom... please... prove me wrong; I want to be wrong... making this piece of story-news the first in a long run of months and months of frequent updates?

I tank j00 in adwance...

The article may be a bone. But, it has a certain degree of fresh meat on it.

The Sentinels did their thing in Tir with respect to murdering Neutrals. Several Neutrals responded by killing Fisk several times.

The Tir Accord was annulled owing to some players deciding to abandon the Council of Truth. Certain of the most visible Clans lost their focus towards spreading virtue and the good things in Man's inner being and took up the mantel worn so well by those basest elements in the dark soul of Omni-Tek.

Notum Mining was subsequently deregulated. People were free to stake their claims.

Several Neutral organizations put down a half dozen Notum Mines spilling no blood in the process.

Certain Clan organizations took it upon themselves to destroy these facilities and kill their owners. When the claimants attempt to regain their land, an entire faction takes this as an agressive act against themselves and proceed on a witch hunt against all Neutrals. Basically making life miserable for the true independant and free peoples.

The highest profile and most powerful Clan guild takes it upon themselves to repeatedly harvest Ian Warr's extended family in East Foul Plains.

All this is fact.

Rhis Lee approaches the leader of a well-known guild and wishes to discuss their 'clan problem'.

Discussions and negotiations ensue. The 100 million credit number is put forth by Mr. Lee. 3 days are given.

Much soul searching and debate goes on and it's decided to at least give this a shot as nothing else seems to be working with regard to the bloodlust of many high visibility clans. Word is leaking in that the other dimension has basically fallen prey to the worst elements of feudalism thinly veiled in the guise of freedom fighters.

The money is raised through non-stop missioning, generous donations from sympathizers, and sacrificing the guild bank.

Mr. Lee is contacted and informed 'we have the money'. His response is a little shocked and apparent disbelief that one organization would sacrifice so much to gain redress for the pain inflicted on them. His response is to request a meeting of 'like thinking organizations' to ensure that one organization does not bear the brunt of any repurcussions to come.

A meeting is held in Borealis Backyard 3. Representatives from any Neutral guild who has ever indicated they wish to have an impact on the world are invited. Even Independant Rubi'Kan's were present though it was clear they would stick to their charter of non-involvement. Not all were leaders as not all leaders were on. The word was put forth. Debate was held. Support was obviously granted for the agreement to the contract by the members of the organizations more likely to be involved in political events.

Key points of the meeting were that this contract was mainly to be taken for non-offensive reasons. Raised were the issues of disrespect for Neutrals and what they have achieved in erecting Newland, the Tir situation and the Clans inability to oust the thug from their fine city, the destruction of Neutral mining interests that had been claimed without a drop of blood being shed.

There was without doubt a 'consensus amongst like-minded organizations'. Not amongst all Neutral organizations of course. Nobody expected that.

There must have been a little birdy situated on the canopy at this meeting because in the morning, we all saw the TAG article.

This turn of events stemmed from real actions by real people. This turn of events has history. The money is real. The dialogue with Mr. Lee happened. The meeting in BY 3 happened. The TAG article would not have happened if enough organizations didn't show support as I'm certain there was a 'bug' somewhere in that backyard.

I lay it at the feet of the powers that be to ensure that for better or worse, Mr. Warr's people show up to put a Yin to the Yang that Valentine's day is sure to bring. We're all excited over something happening in our haven by the lake and hope that the follow through that what must have taken more than a few man-hours of credit collection to achieve deserves is implemented.

My only hope is that we aren't treated to another 'dead end' with respect to the goings on around our planet.
