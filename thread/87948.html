Change in tokens? What about us Neutrals that don't get tokens?

Putting a Quest in game for an RP would be a waste as this would bottle neck everyone on the server, and need such a high requirement to make sure it was for high level players...

I don't know at each exact level we recieve RPs... so far I know its 1 at 15, 3 at 20, and another 3 at 50... I would assume its another 3 at 100, and 3 at 150, and 3 at 200... for a total16 RPs...

Now through the life of a character, by level 100 you should have a pretty good idea of where your headed or at least be set in such a way options are limited so very little room to mess up a character. So all RPs after 100 should be gravy...

However I believe at lvl 200 all leveling should be converted to an RP scale... meaning that the first RP is X amount of experience... and the second is twice the first point worth of experience. This would mean that as you gained RPs the amount of experience to get the next one would grow larger... meaning if you have 5 RPs it would take X amount of experience times 6 to get the 6th one... the only alternative to this would be that if you spent the first RP you gained the scale would reset and you would only have to gain that back... so you could go after a lot of points or just one at a time... or simply over time they would accumalate.

However I don't believe RPs should be granted to lower levels as they will gather a pool of their own on the way to lvl 200...

The only other alternative is to try and come up with a PvP scale much like DAoC's Realm Points... as rewards for killing foes defending towers etc... however this system would cost a lot of development and serious retooling of the current PvP structure...
