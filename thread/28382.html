The Mythical Trader (rumors dispelled)
Mists swirl on a barren plane, and in the distance trickles of lava bubble up through the cracked earth. Music swells, a mighty bass roar of drums and trumpeting horns, and a shadowy figure appears, backed by the light of a distant sun, a large gun slung accross his shoulders. He stands straight, and proud, a mighty god on a new-formed planet. He can do anything. He can be anything. He is a Trader.

The mighty, mythical trader (Mercator Mythicus). He is reflected in each of us, every trader of RubiKa. His image haunts us all, evoked in whispers and accusations. When the common citizen looks at a common trader (Mercator Vulgaris) he sees the mythical trader, replete with godlike abilities, rather than the actual and limited mortal struggling against innumerable frustrations.

The purpose of this post is not to claim traders are miserable helpless wretches incapable of even loading a shotgun without assistance, but instead to address some of the popular myths about traders... oh and to amuse myself while i finish my morning coffee.

Myth : Traders heal better than docs/Trader healing rivals doctor healing/Traders, like Aesclepius, can bring the dead back to life.

Reality : trader heals have higher skill requirements, higher nanocosts, and do damage to the caster. Like all healers, due to client/server sync issues, a trader heal can sometimes save someone who has already died, but just as often, a trader heal will land to heal a corpse that cannot be revived.

Myth : Traders outdamage soldiers, enforcers, NTs and all other professions

Reality : Shotguns are high-crit weapons. Any profession that uses a shotgun and has a high enough attack rating will see the same damage numbers as traders using shotguns. Additionally, shotguns, aside from their crits, do not do very reliable high average damage. A soldier with a novaflow will do more damage over time than a trader with a same-quality shotgun, provided they have similar attack ratings (that statement does not take into account crits/special attacks, but i'm pretty sure it would hold true even with crits and specials)

Myth : Traders are the gods of pvp, and will be even more effective after 14.2

Reality : PVP traders, created solely for pvp, are highly effective in pvp situations, but are still not as powerful as doctors. Additionally, a trader can only win a fight if a) a divest/plunder sticks and b) their opponent can be kept at a distance. With the removal of crit buffs in pvp, traders will lose their one real advantage. Shotguns, with limited or no special attacks, did most of their damage through crits. This will no longer be possible. Trader heals can in fact finish off a trader during a fight, unless the trader has anoutside-buffed absorption shield.

Myth : Traders have everything, can do everything. They calm better than Crats or NTs

Reality : Trader calms have a higher resist, higher nanocost, lower range and higher skill requirement for shorter calm-duration than Crat calms. NT calms have a higher resist but lower skill req. The only reason trader calms may appear more effective is that, due to the higher skill requirement, traders often have a higher casting skill, thus a lower chance of being resisted.

Myth : Trader nanodrains make effective HE's, rivalling those of NTs

Reality : Nanodrains tick 3 times, returning just about the amountof nano that it costs to cast them. They have no visible effect on their targets outside of pvp.

I've forgotten the other myths i've heard lately... feel free to add to this though

"Truth Nobody overequips better than an AGENT who can FP for Trader Divest/Plunders Along with Boost there main attack skills by +150 by themselves and LLT which can be boosted on for Aimshot buff, In fact its possible at level 41 to have 551 Aimshot the amount needed for a Renee. With enough NCU a Agent can buff his main weapon skills by 578(Divest/Plunder Advanced + Unexpeted Attack + General)"

Fact: Agents have the most expensive Comp. Lit. Agents don't have Comp. Lit. buffs. Therefore, when one accounts for the amount of nano skills it takes to FP Trader and have enough left over to cast Divest/Plunder after the penalty, and expensive Comp. Lit. without a buff this is basically an inpropable situation. Without tons of NCU, there's no way you can stair-step Divest/Plunder well (because of the stacking), and an Agent would also need Proficiencies/Masteries due to the -25 nano skill penalty.

Some quick math...

At level 41 an Agent can have 195 Bio Met/Psy Mod/Sense Imp. This gets knocked down to 175 after FP.

He can then cast Deprive Skills (Lesser) for + 29. Now his skills are at 204. So now comes Ransack Skills (Lesser) for + 34. Now he's at 238. So now comes Deprive Skills for + 40 (+11) to move it to 249. Ransack Skills for +47 (+13) makes it 262.

Now, we've hit a wall. Can cast Expertise's and go up to 282. Deprive Skills (Major) makes it +55 (+15) and 297

Ransack Skills (Major) is at 324. So that's out of reach without masteries. Let's not consider masteries for now since we're talking self buff.

So, basically we now have running Deprive Skills (Major) and Ransack Skills for a total of +55/+47 = 102 buff.

We can get Sniper's Bliss on for +50 rifle/+15 Aimed Shot. That stacks with the general buffs for +70 rifle/+35 Aimed Shot. We cannot use Unexpected Attack. That uses 628 nano skills. A waaaay off. (Not to mention 42 NCU.)

That means a buff for 172 Rifle and 35 Aimed Shot. This process would require at least 66 NCU by my estimation. To go any higher, one would need to have enough room for masteries (~30) and 96 NCU just for holding. This doesn't count the addition increasing reqs of Ransack skills, etc... So one would really need more in the 100-120 NCU range to go much higher.

Now, at level 41 an Agent can have 195 comp lit. (Although quite unlikely.) This is 215 with the general buff and good enough for 4-slot belt. It would be very difficult to get that much NCU. Would need 4 NCU +27's or greater. Not sure on the scaling of NCU units on memory, but that would be a tough order for 215 comp lit.

Let's settle on +172 rifle and +32 AS. An Agent can already have 228 Rifle/Aimed Shot. So, we're seeing a 57% over-equip on Rifle, 12% on Aimed Shot. 400 rifle and 260 Aimed Shot will get you...

Maybe a QL 85 Tsakachumi PTO-HV3a Counter-Sniper Rifle at level 41. Which, incidentally, will lower dramatically in power when your rifle drops to 228 and the power is based 100% of Rifle.

This is a far cry from a +578 buff that you're claiming there. And we still can't up Aimed Shot by much. The Aimed Shot limit will keep us below QL 90 no matter what.

One last detail being that every nano we cast cost 150% normal. We also have a -2000 nano init penalty, and a 75% up in our nano interrupt chance. Range is reduced by 50% on the trader skills, too...

Also, this assumes we live in 14.2 where Agents can cast their own nanos while in FP. As it stands now, we would need even MORE NCU than above, because we would have to cast Sniper's Bliss ahead of time. That puts him at 80 NCU JUST to do the initial buff I mentioned. This makes doing the mastery/next level of Ransack impossible, since there's no way a level 41 Agent could get 120-135 NCU.

(Note that I have both an Agent and a Trader. So I know all about this. *L*)
