FC responds about Engies in Groups
As you may or may not know, the professionals has a rotating q/a with the devs where each profession gets a question answered every month. In this way we attempt to get an answer about one of the more prominent issues that the profession has.

The first week for engies I chose Engies in groups - the answer from FC follows:

"Engineers in teams is something we're looking at, but we're very interested in seeing the full effect of the improved pet pathing (and the Parry tweaking) first. It takes time for the added efficiency to sink into the mindset of people, and maybe that alone will give Engineers some more popularity in teams.

We know Engineers don't have too much to contribute directly to their teammates, apart from their auras. We don't have anything shiny to throw at you right now, but note that making AC buffs mean a lot more is something we aim at. Building on their pets (and trade skills, definately) is more what we'd prefer to do though, as they are very focused on that."

My commentary on the answer:

I think that the answer is fair considering we have had a major change to our effectiveness in the last patch and even the AR patch isn't that long ago. Also, with the new solar powered pistol stuff coming in next patch (and maybe new engie pistols) engies have some good stuff coming in anyhow. If AC becomes more important, we wouldn't even need a new line of nanos to be more effective - I'm not sure if that is on the horizon but it would be an interesting way to make engies better without altering our nanos.
