Engineer Gadgets (From engy forum)
I don't really know if anyone from FC reads the engy forum, but in any case I wanted to post these ideas here for further discussion as well.

In the spirit of giving engineers an identity so that we will no longer be a class best defined as a 'Gimped MP'. I offer the follup up to my 'What we're told at character generation' thread.

In that thread I pointed out that the game *itself* defines engineers via gadgets and robot helpers. That we're supposed to be a 'powerful ally, or opponent' due to our gadgets and robot helpers. We are not the masters of trade skills, that belongs to the trader for some freakish unholy reason. However, we are the masters of robot helpers and... GADGETS. Of course there is an extremely limited number of gadgets in the game and nothing particular to engineers.

So I submit to you, some gadget ideas! Too often I think players focus on damage output as the only defining measure of a class. This need not be so. Forget OE (it won't kill the engineers) forget about how our bots are (sniff) sub-par to an MP pet... (sniff, sniff) I mean...

Sorry had to pull myself together for a moment.

Engineers do not need more 'nanos' let the MPs and NT have extensive and wierd nanos. We (and I speak for every one that started this thread, i.e. me) need extensive and weird gadgets!

In all seriousness these are some ideas, please feel free to add more, but not nanos, just gadgets! All gadgets would have limited charges before needing to be rebuilt, and last some length of time while locking a skill (similar to trimmers).

Homing Beacon: This gadget allows members of the engineers team to detect the location of the engineer. A way point (similar to a mission way point) appears on the team member's compass indicating the direction of the engineer.

Noise Generator: The ultrahigh frequency harmonics of this device disrupt nanobot coherency. Frequencies emitted are not sympathetic to the engineer's nanobots, or those of his team. As a result, enemy nano castings gain a flat chance to fail (based on the QL of the device) and existing hostile nanos can be disrupted.

Light Refraction Imager: Engineers can use this device to make the 'images' of him and his comrades appear out of phase to their actual locations by a few inches. While not effective at preventing opponents from 'hitting' their targets, it does reduce their chances of a critical hit. This reduction is based on the QL of the device.

Notum Destruction Field Generator: This complex devices emits a powerful waveform that destroys all notum in the immediate area of the engineer. As no nano programs can be cast (by either friend or foe) and all currently active nanobots are destroyed. These effects remain in place for the duration of the generator's function.

As I hope you can see, I'm trying to urge a move away from absolute dependence on the bot. Afterall, if all we have is the bot then we really are nothing than a gimped MP. I hope that FunCom will implement gadgets that do NOT necessarily

1) make our bots more powerful
B) increase our damage output
Pi) Take something from another profession

Instead with a little creativity there are thousands of things you can do with gadgets using rules already existing in the game. Gadgets can have simple but extraordinarily useful effects (Homing Beacon), or can be powerful yet two edged (NDFG above). They can function as weird little debuffs (Light Refractor). Essentially, take any effect in the game create an opposite effect and you could make a gadget for it.

Again I hope to see some more great ideas from my fellow engineers.

I think Dr. Oppenheimer said it best at the Trinity Test Site...

"Now THAT is a big boom!"

or something like that I'm sure.
