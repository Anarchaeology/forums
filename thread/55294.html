Hehe.... Remember how mission terms work? Clicksaver does not collect 10 searches for you. But searches trough ten times for some special equipment you have asked it to search for. So you still have one mission set at the time as always with missions.

If you chose "Give me missions" (or what that button is called 8) ) you get a NEW set of 5 missions always. You cannot push that button 10 times and get 50.... And that's what clicksaver does, pushes that button 10 times. But if it gets a mission that conforms to your search criteria, it stops and displays those 5 missions (of wich one is the one you want).

But if you have NO search criteria, it just zooms trough 10 times and showes you the last 5 missions.

Hope that helped
