Ashes to Ashes, Delusions to Dust....
"Why is the Sky Blue, Pvt. Cratty?"
"Sir, Ah don know, Sir!"
"Cuase GOD loves the Infantry, Private!"


=5:43=
That was the readout on Private Malcom Cratty's comm unit as he stood there, Nova Flow in hand, sights set. He checked the charge on the clip to his weapon for what was probably the fifteenth time in the past twenty minutes.
A thousand emotions rushed through his heart as a thousand memories rushed his mind. He tried to place the flood of images and thoughts into the right order, forgettiing the steady breathing excercises hed been taught, so as to not pull him of his sight picture.
The ranch. That was probably the right place to begin......maybe, maybe. So frustrating, given his current surroundings. Being deep inside enemy lines will do that to you, Cratty mused to himself. The feeling that something was crawling on the back of his neck, the urge to chsck his rifle constantly, the need to remind himself that yes, yes did wear all his armor he was issued.
The ranch. Focus. Breath. Yes that day on the Bronto ranch, when he told his Uncle Cort he was heading out to the Big City of Tir.
"Malcom, ya shure son? Ya know yer welcome fer as long as ya like."
"Shucks Unc, ah know, ah know, its jus...."
"Jus wut?", Uncle Cort pressed, not willing to let the subject go so easily, especially since Malcom hadn't given him a strait answer yet.
"Ahm a man now. Ah gotta make muh stand," Malcom answered finally giving in, "RubiKa's gone ta Hell, Ah aim ta make a differnce."
Cort just laughed. " So yer gonna go an save tha wurld?"
THats when Mal blew up again, that infamous temper taking over once more.
"Ya can staiy here an shuvel Bronto crap fer tha rest ah yer life if ya wont. Ahm goin ta Tir ole man. Don member muh name no more. Ya don exist muh eyes no more."
Crackling static shook him from his memory.
===SIT REP, ALL UNITS====
It was General USMC Devildog yelling over the team comm frequency. The current order was to inform all units to report on their current situation."
====Red One, standing by====
====Blue One, Standing by===
====Green One, Standing by===
about ten seconds passed, then Devildog shouted back over the com...
====Christ sakes, Black One, you gonna report or do you want be to come pull it out your frikkin throught====
"Uhh, Ahm standin by, Sir!"
====You screw this up, Cowboy, Ill perma-kill you myself=====
"Sir, yes, Sir!" Cratty yelled into his comm.
===2:40=====
Devildog, that was probably where it all went wrong, he thought. The day young Malcom Cratty, EternalRebel, visionary, savior of RubiKa, met DevilDog, he was awe inspired. Here was a hard as nails vet soldier looking for "brothers" to help HIM save RubiKa. How could Malcom say no?
That was before he knew what Dust Brigade was all about.
A hard nodd shook him back to his reality as it stood in the moment. His Nova Flow in hand, his sights set. One more clip check. He hadn't the time to think on it any further. He looked at his com once more.
====0:23===
A few more seconds. no more time to think. All that remainded was to fall back into the training Dust Brigade hammered into him, over and over. He was just a soldier. Just a gun to be pointed in the right direction. After that, it was all automatic.
====0:02====
====0:01====
====0:00====
a huge flash was followed by an immeadiate boom that rocked the ground on which Malcom stood. Malcom reset his stance, waiting, waiting for a clear target to immerge from the debris which clouded the entire area.
Then it came, a a stumbling outline, still not totally visable, but definately there. It looked too wide to be solitus, maybe Atrox, maybe....
General Devildog came back over the comm link
===All units, all units fall back to Rally Point!===
Cratty nervously thumbed his comm, keeping the target insight, "Sir, ah got a mark in muh cross-hairs!"
===Pull the God!##@ trigger then, just get outta there====
Cratty still couldn't make out the exact shape of the outline, but all his training told him it didnt matter anyway. He swallowed hard as he fired a three round burst into the center of the shuffling target.
Then, a second too late, a strong breeze cleared his target picture, and young Malcoms world changed forever. Two young solitus children, dirty, crying, clinging to a fallen mother that could protect them no longer.
All he ever thought he knew, dissapeared in the time lapse of a three round burst.
====Black One, Black One, WITHDRAW, WITHDRAW!!!====
