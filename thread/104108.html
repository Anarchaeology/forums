Bureaucrat Top 3
The top 3 issues submitted to the professionals for the bureaucrat. The top issues were compiled from various threads within the bureaucrat messageboard. In order of urgency.

The new professionals layout is arranged so that each week a certain number of professions get their top issue answered. To get more information on how it works please go here.

----------------------------------
New Top 3 as of 6-15-03
----------------------------------

Bureaucrat unique abilities in pvp - Their functioning crowd control abilities(mez/snare/root) are not unique in pvp. Worse, there are other professions that can do the same things as a bureaucrat and more. Mez's de-init is of questionable usefulness in pvp at due to it breaking instantly. In pvp the prevailant use of reflect shields, area root/snares, and any other area attacks that cause damage means that the lifespan of any "mez" is quickily broken very quickily, almost instantly in some cases.

Bureaucrat profession report (Part3) (Latter half)

Summoning Mobs. - Any mob that summons the bureaucrat will instantly break any charmed pets they have. Is this intended? In addition, the mobs that can summon are immune to crowd control nanos that can redirect aggro such as Mez and Fear. Roots and snares do not stop the mobs from continously summoning the person(s) till death. For the bureaucrat, crowd control is impossible as they are completely unable to break aggro on the summoners.

Tradeskills - Bureaucrat needs a tradeskill. There have been good suggestions for paperwork.

Related threads -
Bureaucrat profession report (Part3) (Latter half)
Official description of "Strong fields" include some tradeskills
Paperwork tradeskills

Definition of the Bureaucrat's role. - Our role has been in a word, grey. We are not quite the team leaders as the official description would suggest, nor are our crowd control abilities consistently reliable. The bureaucrat's core skillset work on a black and white nature, meaning they either work or they don't. No other profession has this handicap on their core skillset. If the core skill set has been delibrately crippled in this way due to balancing reasons, are there any ways to allievate that by giving the bureaucrat an ability type to fall back on that isn't second or third rate? (ie. 3rd rate pets, 2nd rate nukes/snares, poor general weapon affinity.)

Of note is that it was said that existing mobs that are immune will not even be considered for "fixing." The official reply to crowd control immunity in PvE is only consideration that root and snares might work on new mobs. Yet, nano resistance will be toned down because the NT's core skill set(nukes) work extremely poorly on those mobs in question. The bureaucrat's core skill set has not recieved any such consideration and continue to be unusuable due to total immunities or psychic checks.

Related threads -
Bureaucrats, the ignored profession.

Lack of profession specific content for the bureaucrat. - The bureaucrat profession has recieved very few new items in terms of content since launch. The only bureaucrat specific items currently as of 14.7 is the bureaucrat suit and Nelly Johnson's dress(female). The upcoming suitcase has been long awaited and for some, the only new item to happen to the bureaucrat in recent memory. It will bring the total up to 3. Lack of improvements to problematic nano lines (Mez,Charm/Fear, useless nanos) combined with the lack of new things for the profession, is dissappionting.

Old Issues - Answered, but not necessarily fixed.
------------------------------------------
Psychic checks - The black and white nature of the psychic checks leave large level gaps where the bureaucrat's main abilities(charms and fears) don't work. For charms the stable and unstable are treated as the same line. This created situations where the bureaucrat is forced to use the unstable and far risker charm. The origional intention was that the unstable charm was to be a voluntary tradeoff of risk versus an extra pet. The fear sneaking terror is bugged and a downgrade from the previous fear. - Slightly misunderstood answer.

mezz
NTs are fortunate in getting to be in the first pile of professions to submit issues under this new system (along with traders and soldiers). Issue #1 on our list is mezzes and the various flaws with them.

It would probably be valuable to our submission and to everyone who uses mezzes if you all took the opportunity to point out any additional issues that you know of with these lines. While there are a few considerations that are profession specific (dispersion over level range, attk/def rating, etc), most of what we have listed are issues that affect everyone and I doubt anyone is going to complain if we get these things looked at.

Here's what we have at the moment:

Calms have the following problems:
  • a. Mobs calmed that have not yet aggroed will aggro after calm has been successfully executed on them and you will receive an attack message up to x amount of rooms away
  • b. Mobs get extra attacks in once calm is successful - this may be a sync problem
  • c. Mobs chain counter calms more than any other nano.
  • d. Stun nukes and Calms run on the same line so you are not able to calm them. This is a problem if multiple NTs are present and you need to calm to get aggro off of you.
  • e. Multiple NTs casting calms will bug the calm and the mob will appear to be calmed but instead is just rooted and can continue to attack. - Note: this also happens when used with other calm lines like those of Crats, etc.
  • f. If a mob has been calmed by a 'higher quality' calm than the one you use and is in that half-state where it is still rooted and glowing but is fighting, you cannot calm it at all without first nuking it (or shooting it) to break the calm. once these buggers start shooting at people, that running calm needs to go away.
  • g. Nullity sphere breaking calm. Nullity up....calm mob and before Calm drops the mob breaks calm and proceeds to walk around room.

Single Mez Inconsistency
Wandering Mind QL 156
Defense: nano resist 100% level 80%

Contemplation QL 189
Defense: nano resist 103% level 5%

It was said all mez nanos were given a -10% to nano resist to compensate for the added level%. The origional resists for crat single mez was 110% with the exception of the last one Contemplation with 103%. Now after the added level% all other single mezzes got 110% - 10% = 100%. But the 103% resist remained as is on the final mez, the correct base nano resistance should have been 103% - 10% = 93%

Speech descriptions
Currently all crat speeches tell "what" it is they affect but none of them tell "how much" they buff or debuff. It would be useful to know ahead of time that Assassin's Focus gives a +7 critical chance for example as opposed to guessing.
