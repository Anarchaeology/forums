The CoG
I am interested in how the different OT faction leaders would feel about forming a congress. A Congress of Guilds (CoG). This organization would allow all OT guilds on Rubi-Ka to work out thier differnces. It would not make decisions on OT policy ... only decisions on how to carry out OT policy.

What is happening now is unhealthy. The bickering will only hurt us in the future. Once war comes... and it's only a matter of time now... It will benifit us greatly if we react with a united front.

The congress could be called something else and the rules that guide it should be made by thoose who are in it. It should be welcome to all OT guilds. The member guilds though should be bound to accept the majority decisions. I think this would have helped us greatly in dealing with things that have already confronted us.

Lets leave the anarchy to the clans. Omni-Tek should organize and prepair.
