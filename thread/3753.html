PvP (Lets fix it!)
Last night I almost broke down into tears. Im a Agent, I havent really been pking since 13.0 and someone informed me that a doc and fixer were raiding 2ho. Im like, ohh yes, this is going to be a cakewalk, ill nail that doc in a AS and 4 normal hits. Then beat the living daylights out of the fixer. Pre-13 I only lost one fight, I never oneshot anyone with my AS, but I could see how some agents could do it.

So it begins...

I creap up, spot the doc circling the fixer while he is fixing someone else healing him. AIMSHOT 679 damage, okay I think to myself, that must mean hes got 1500 hitpoints, not bad for a doc. My rifle begins its normal shots, 159, 150, 200, 57, 150, i notice the doc dropping a heal *full life*, I think to myself, this is gonna be a long fight... I root and back off, then I get hit by his melee attacks finally... 359, 240... i quickly move back and before i get out of the range, wham his DOT nails, im now ticking for 129 damage. I heal, my heal kit basically does only 130 heal. I havent really raised it because there is always a doc to heal me. I despretly try to distance myself, but the doc has on a speed buff from the fixer and he runs me down, wham, i shoot him again, hardly any damage done, and two more shots from his uber-shotgun kills me.

I went back 4 more times, and died 3 more times to the doc, and once to the fixer. The fixer who did less damage than the doc, more or less did his mini-alpha strike to kill me. He was burst/flinging me to death.

The point to all of this:

PvP changes were not well thought out, and rather stupid. The old powerful classes Agents and Soldiers were atleast realistic to be good at PvP, after all its their job to kill. Who rules now? Traders and Doctors. I just want to cry sometimes. This fix solved absolutely nothing. This was a blatant attack against pretty much these two classes, then they more or less neutered NTs to the point that i never see them. If i see a NT in a political or mayham zone, im just like, if you really want to fight ill give you a handycap on me, if you dont, just leave i wont attack.

Quick Fix: Remove the cap on specials and just keep the 50% reduce damage.

Good Fix: Hire me, I cost a meager $50,000 a year, id move to Oslo, im fun at the workplace, and id do nothing there but handle the PvP scene
