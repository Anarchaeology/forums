FC: Supplemental Quest/Plot Content From Subscribers
Hello Funcom,

There's a method of generating player/subscriber content submitted by said users, and i'm not just speaking about the current tilting that the playerbase is having on the storyline. This content will come in 4 forms where you as Funcom will just need to create some additional applications to supplement the game AO. For example with the: quest/mission generator parameters; random generator of editing replay event sessions by guilds/individual users(submitted by their choice & permission); a random generator of synthesized audio/text dialogue; screening software by guaging parameters of player combat, interaction, keyword statements in text of objectives/scenes players partake in sigular/plural activities (Using your numerical rule system).

To spell out what all of this means is a non-linear movie created by the players/users themselves. Where the subscribers can toggle a scene recorder on through their chase camera. Thus the camera records their actions as individuals/teams as a replay event like in Real Life sports. Then the applications i've named above process these recordings for private viewing by the authored subscriber. Dependent on how much hard drive space they've selected for storage via a slider scale. Where anything in older archive asks the subscriber if it can be purged for new recordings. The recordings are relayed to the subscribers using streaming technology.

That's on the user end of things, on your end Funcom when a individual/team/guild submits their recording by a EULA permission to you as the developers. It becomes your property to break down for editing and/or content development. Like i mentioned for parameters to your quest/mission generator by adding more random events into the catalog. Secondary content for you Funcom is those high pitched action/adventure recordings you receive and process can be added to the AO Lore & History books. Good way for the subscribers to leave a legacy of the event. Maybe even guilds can create their own archive of recordings/movies for entertainment and/or strategy development of gameplay tactics.

Thanks for reading this suggestion.
