Although this is not the right board for these questions, I'll answer them:

1-Full xp missions giving more cash than xp

This is the reality until very high levels as i hear. The thing is not that full xp missions give you more xp, its that full cash gives you 3-5 times more cash and little/no xp (its as if full xp missions were the "neutral" setting)

2-Sliders
Off the top of my head
GOOD-BAD (does nothing except change the mission type, not logical, full BAD full MONEY nets you item pickup missions, while full GOOD full MONEY nets you assassinate missions, heh)

MYST-PHYS (affects the type of mission slightly and the typr of mobs, MYST gives more nano users, PHYS gives enforcer and soldier types)

HEAD ON-STEALTH (affects mission type slightly, also affects the chances of locked doors, locked chests, trapped chests)

OPEN-CLOSED (see HEAD ON-STEALTH, also rumored to generate more cave missions if set to closed, base missions if set to open, unconfirmed)

MONEY-XP (self explanatory)

3- Take it easy....

Not much i can tell you. "Take it easy" comes and goes, usually a burst fixes it. Other fixes are:

- Letting an aggro enemy attack you (to initiate autoattacl)
- Deequipping the weapon and using brawl/dimach

Not much more i can tell you
