Personally...
I hope that they don't lower HP and ACs too much once they've seen the effects of the changes, but I do hope that they lower the damage that mobs/monsters can do and the (very high) skills levels that some mobs have. Why?

1) It's not supposed to be easy! It's supposed to be a challenge. By lowering damage/skills a fair bit and HP/AC only a little, we'll be able to take some more time killing a mob. Debuffs on skills will make a major difference and add to the strategy of teaming. Right now many teams rely on killing the mob as quickly as possible instead of relying on clever play and tactical play. For me at least, a little more emphasis on teamwork and clever play and a little less emphasis on 'bang bang you're dead' would be very cool.

2) Right now at various levels (but mostly at higher ones), teams can choose to do missions and hunting at low levels that allow them to kill mobs in a couple of hits and get good XP for it. If they choose higher dificulty hunting, the XP isn't good enough to make it worth their while, because the risk of dying is so great and they actually have to work and take time to kill the mobs. In terms of XP over time, the easy missions that offer no challenge are the easiest option.

The OE and crit buff changes make it harder for them to eat through the easy missions like they used to (sometimes literally killing mobs in a second or two). By lowering the Damage on mobs, the higher level missions would have reduced risk of dying somewhat and the more interesting play that those missions would bring would make them more attractive. At the same time with the lower crit chances on the lower level missions, they would find them less rewarding. As an example, right now, in the range from about level 110 to level 200 nearly everybody is doing QL 185 to QL 195 missions. The harder QL200 - 230 missions are rarely used at all.

(it'd also be nice to see the XP rewards for the higher QL missions get raised some to make them more lucrative)

3) It would bring the focus back to damage over time better. Lower damage from the mobs would allow us to actually use our damage over time strengths without dying too much more and leaving the HP/AC around where they are will extend fight time, giving the damage over time professions (which is the majority of professions) the time to make the diffference that they can make.

Professions that use Nukes, Dots, Pets, MA skills and Debuffs would all benefit from longer encounters with mobs that would come if HP/AC stayed largely where they are. Right now, many debuffs and dots give relatively little benefit because mobs often die too quickly for their effect to be appreciable. Also, those professions like NTs, MAs, Crats, Engineers, MPs etc that have always had good damage over time rather than really big damage can only contribute so much at higher levels.

I look forward to 14.2 in so many ways! It's a great patch.

The problem with mob damage and hp is that it's calculated using a formula that scales with higher levels. The biggest problem is that it is exponential.

Funcom pretty obviously wanted to slow down leveling at the lower levels, but because they started the mobs off tougher, they have become simply ridiculous at the high levels. The reason why it isn't so easy to just reduce the mob hp, is because if they do that with the current system, they would lower mob hp for the lower levels too. They certainly don't want to change the formula because a new formula would be so incredibly unpredictable in terms of what the results might be.

I would reccommend to Funcom that they just tack a bandaid onto it. One way would be to make new mob items which reduce hp and AMS. Make it so that it only ranges from QL 150 and up or something. That way, only mobs of level 150 and up would get this thing equipped, and thus only mobs of that level and up would be affected.
