Suggestions based off the online manual.
I intend to spread this out over a few days as I think over every statement I make here at least twice. Today I speak only on the body section of the manual concerning Martial Arts.

Martial Arts
Martial Arts is the main unarmed combat skill. Training this skill will allow you to not only hit more often, but increase the damage done on each hit. It depends 50% on agility, 20% on strength, and 30% on psychic.

What this says and what it really means is two totally diffrent things. The first two sentances only relate to you form of combat (unarmed is not actually true anymore as there are martial artist weapons out there) and what it's primary intention was. It is NOT actually based on 50% of your agility score, 20 off your strength, and 30 on psychic. This is what its BASE score is. The base score of it is actually all those scores taken into that percentage divided by another # which I don't remember right now and can't find it online.

Martial Arts is very general. Attacks like attack of the snake classify as a Martial Arts attack. Why are these items not equipable in hand slots? It would be much more appreciated by me if I could equip my martial arts attack forms (such as attack of the snake) if when I enter combat I actually have the movements of it.

If you like the above statement please don't just post for the bump of it. Read, make your own decision on if it's a good idea, and agree or disagree with this statement. I will return once a day to check replys and to further the list of skills. Next up is Brawling and should arrive sometime within 24 hours.

Untill then.

Daeem

Time for Brawl.
Brawling
Brawling is a special attack that can be used in conjunction with other close combat skills. It is very effective, and on high levels can do quite a chunk of damage if applied correctly. Many non-specialists train this as it is quite cheap to learn, though not as effective as martial arts. Dependencies are 60% strength and 40% stamina.

The dirty fighting tactics of the known world. Everything from a kick to the knee to a bodyslam or a tackle. Every melee weapon, martial artist attack, and some ranged weapons should have this. Assualt rifles should allow a player to brawl but only if the opponent is within range. Current military weaponry allows this type of attack when within range. Although it would probably be difficult I suggest putting this option in. Set brawl as a limited range special attack useable by anyone.

The only weapons I can see having no brawling capabilitys are SMG's. While terrific at spraying lead they don't do so well with being bashed on an opponent's skull. An assualt rifle however has the lead spraying capability while being a bit more cumbersome. Anything one handed can be used with the hilt to deal a minor ammount of damage.
