Well, Tara isn't really a quest, so I'm not sure what to put in a walk-through. First, you need to find out when Tara will spawn. Since you're an RK2 omni, you can try asking the RK2 omnis that post in the Tara threads here. Not sure who'd be a good point of contact these days, though. A potential good source of info is the Tarainfo bot. Try [/i]/tell Tarainfo spawn[/i] to get the spawn time. You can also try the chat group with [i]/tell Tarainfo invite[i].

Next, you'll need to get to Tara. Doing this from memory. I'm sure people can correct me if I screw it up.
1. Go to Camelot (duh). Expect to get attack by guards.
2. Look for castle (it's the big castle shaped thing). Run/fly across drawbridge to zone in. If flying, you need to be at low altitude.
3. Go up stairs to entry room. This part is a 100% zone.
4. At top of stairs is a room with NPC guards (still 100%). Go through door to your left.
5. That's a very small room with another door in front of you. Go through it.
6. You'll be in a hall with columns, a fireplace and more guards. Go to the far end, and there's a door on your left. Still 100% here.
7. Go through the door to the stairway down. This part and all the rest are 25%
8. Go to bottom of stairs and then keep going straight through a room, a door, and down a fair sized hallway.
9. Where the hallway ends, there's a door on the left. Go through it.
10. You'll find a series of stairs. Go all the way down. Various aggro mobs from here on.
11. The room at bottom of stairs makes a U-turn. Follow it.
12. Now you enter a cave area. Go down a lengthy hall until it ends. There's a door on your left, don't use it. Back up a bit and take the door that would have been on your right as you passed it.
13. Go down another long hall. There'll be a large cavern. Go all the way to the other side of it and through a door.
14. Go down another long hall until it ends at a doorway
15. Go through doorway into the box room. This is a huge chamber. Tara spawns in the middle.

If you want a list of Tara loot, there's one here.
