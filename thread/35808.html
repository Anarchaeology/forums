This is a Professionals thread designed to help us decide what to suggest to The Community Team (Cosmik/Cz) on this reflect issue.

We have read several good suggestions, which are very productive and make good points, but they are spread throughout the message board.

We would like to bring all of those suggestions / bug reports related to the bracer / reflect issue into one thread so its more organized and without "flames" and other such unnecessary commentary.

Why the bracers?

As I had stated above it is not the damage or healing that is the problem it is the reflect that is widening the gap between the two causing heals to be too powerful.



________
Example1)

Lets assume that Doctor A has no reflect and is equally matched with Soldier A to the point where the doctor can heal exactly as much damage as the soldier can dish out. They would be evenly matched in a stalemate (actually the doctor could attack during the recharge of the heals which make the doctor win.)

Now if you were to give the Doctor 72% reflect the doctor could take on that original soldier AS WELL AS 3 other soldiers exactly like the first for a total of 4 soldiers (actually 3.57 soldiers in total, but you get the point.
_______




THAT is the general unbalance that is ruining PvP atm (aside from there be no fair PvP place to PvP for either side). It does not matter what year this is or what profession you are. NO profession should be able to take on 4 others who are each equally matched to the person taking on the 4.


The extreme reflects are pretty much crippling and class who can not heal in PvP and therefore must use pure damage to win rather then some damage and healing to back it up.

It is pretty much agreed that a high level soldier is probably the best at causing the most damage in an instant amount of time. Now if they have 8k health and 30% reflect from RRF plus two 21% bracers and I just happen to get very lucky and have my record alpha I'll only damage them by about 2546pts of damage or 31.8% of their health.

72% reflect under 50% PvP is 14% effectiveness. ( 100%-72%)*50% = 14%

Why not something else instead of bracers?

1. Nerfing the reflect portion of the bracers in make the playing ground more even. Although it is still skewed towards healing and not damage, its just not as muchas what is currently ruining pvp. If you just had a max of 30% reflect from RRF the results would have been different in the above two Healing and damage examples

Example 1) With 30% reflect. The doctor could take on an additional 0.7 soldiers and still be evenly matched. This is a little bit more fair as no profession should have the previous kind of an advantage.

Example 2) Total damage of that lucky power alpha would have caused 3202 pts damage (Target with 8k is down to 59.997% health. Even with the best alpha that is still not even close to an insta-kill. The most powerful player attack should actually be something that takes a bit to recover from and not laughed at. Using the average results I had posted before when I shot up Kiantirsa the results come out to an average alpha of 2287pts damage (target at 71.4% thanks to the max 40% rule)

Its just no longer that 1k heal can totally heal up 7142 pts of damage after it goes through reflects.
