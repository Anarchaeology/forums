Uncle Omni wants You!
Yohon bribes a guard at Omni HQ with Bacon, and enters into the Control room, He is pressed for time as the Sub Zero operating temperature of this area will freeze him solid in a matter of seconds, He finds the button he is looking for "In case of Emergency" He press's it and barely makes it out crawling the last few feet.

A loud low freqency resinating sound is heard from the statue in the square, apparently it is more then just an old statue... As the reets on the statue fly away startled, the message is well on its way... the soundwave eventually reaches its destination... another statue, of a man, whos name noone even remembers, all we know is that he stares at Rubi-Ka's redundant Sun.

Suddenly, the Eyes of the Man Glow Red, as the data is passed towards the Star, or is it a Star?

Almost instantly every Omni-Tek employee stops a moment and mumbles "Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow" then resumes activity as if nothing happened. Strange though... We feel somehow compelled now to spend less time with internal operations and personal gain, Stealing, Policing, Reconditioning and Infrastructure, A strong feeling of self preservation is among us.
