This is what I did during beta when I was a n00b:

Upped my tutoring skill in hope that my NT could teach my friends MA some neat things.. well we lucked out..

Missioned like crazy to afford a Light Torch at a whopping 450 credits. just to realize that the light it emitted was as good as lighting a lamp in the same room as my comp.

Actually thinking that NTs would become uber and blast things into tiny pieces.

getting the impression that the NT profession was one of the few professions that used nano programs.. oh I was wrong..

Creating an engie just to find out that they didnt have any pets (yet)

Being happy to figgure out that I could use my shift key to lock a key combo, like running forward.. just to realize later that I could use the numpad 0 for the same.

Upped my adventuring and Swimming skills to atleast 100 at all my chars incase of water or mountains.
Later I realized that they both where a hoax as I never had to swim nor climbing mountains. and backpacks where bags that you couldnt equip.

Never upped my Duck explosion as I asumed that those would be evades from bombs and other things like that, that had yet to be implemented. untill I met someone with an shotty that blasted me senseless.

Telling myself to never use implants as the bonus gained was so low, it wasnt worth the effort.. that was untill I realized they went to ql 200.
