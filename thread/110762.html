Someone Wicked This Way Comes...
My codename is Kamelot, my real name is unimportant. This is an open message to all Militant Clans on Rimor looking for a skilled Spy/Assassin.

I have been fighitng Omni forces for several years and was involved in several bombings and assassinations. Nearly a year ago I joined the West Athen Militia and became a General/ lead assassin. 6 Months ago, our leader was kidnapped after making a public speech calling for armed resistance. He has never been found, and is assumed dead. The WAM disbanded soon after, and I laid low for awhile.
