The end for Electronix4
To all my friends on Rubi Ka - and most of all my dear former colleagues of The Healing Hands:

Today, march 16th 2003, I found myself in an unpleasant sticky situation where I hurt a lot of feelings, and decided to throw in the towel and end the legacy of Electronix4.

My entire time together with all of you has been heavily reliant upon trust and friendship, and having failed to be true to the ones who have meant the most to me, I was left with no choice but to send Electronix4 to his grave.

So I have finally after two long years closed my account, leaving a vivid and bittersweet world behind me - taking only the fond memories with me into the future.

Each and every one of you have all made an impact on my real life, and regardless of good and bad times - I will always be grateful for the kindness and respect you have all shown me. Especially during my very long reign as Chief of Staff in this gem of a guild - The Healing Hands.

I know you are dying to know exactly what I did that was so terrible that it made me leave, but some things are best left unsaid and unknown. I hope this can be a secret kept between me and the people whose feelings I have hurt so bad.

In the end, I just want to let you all know that I'm going to miss you a lot, and it is going to take me a long time getting used to not having AO at my fingertips. I have no idea where I will go from here, but I hope you won't forget about me, and lend me a fond thought inbetween. And I beg forgiveness of those wonderful people whose feelings I found myself hurting. I am truly sorry - I wish it never happened.

With love and respect,

Bjoern Sandvik
Formerly: Electronix4 <electronix4@one2access.com>
