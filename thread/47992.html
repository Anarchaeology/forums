Apartments, again
I got my first and main char an apartment before he even left the BY. Emptied everything that looked like it could be "fat lewt" on the floor and ran off to kill some more furry things.

I later lost my key to this apartment, but I never bothered to get a new one. Why? Because my apartment couldnt offer me anything I needed.

So here's what I'd like to see:

1. I want to be able to buy an apt anywhere. Tree Pine, Sabulum, 10-3 OP, Frisko, anywhere. I don't need an apt in Borealis anymore and I haven't for quite a while. That town is perfectly safe and there's been periods of time when I didn't have a reason to go there at all.

2. I want to be able to buy a Scanner for my apartment, and not just some personal mini-scanner. I want one that can be used by anyone (of the same alignment) that enters my apartment.

3. Bank access from inside the apartment, or even better, being able to use funiture to store items.

I want to be able to use my apartment as my base of operations, but it's pointless as long as it's in a zone I rarely visit, and all it can be used for is "private" RP stuff and storing 10 extra items.


(Yeah, it's all been said before, just had to vent.)
