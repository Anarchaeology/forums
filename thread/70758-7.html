My last post
Thank you so much everyone for your posts and kindness. I can't begin to tell you how uplifting it is for me. I am truly touched by this, it is the best farewell gift I could receive.

I am doing well (although going through major withdrawl)... I've been going to therapy and been taking medication for the last 12 days which seems to be doing what it is supposed to as I seem to be normalizing a bit. I'm still feeling like me, just suddenly everything seems to be getting a lot easier somehow. Definately a good thing :)

I took some time off work and i've been getting out a lot, going to the beach, jogging, exercising, and trying to work out my RL relationships, and generally staying away from the computer for the most part.

I miss you all like crazy, and you are in my thoughts.

My best to you all, lots of love,

-Dovve
