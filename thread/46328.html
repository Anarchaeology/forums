**The Perfect PVP System.**
Before I start I would like to say that the following concept is the result of hours on careful research into the function and form of PVP in other game titles. This concept is sort of like a massive sampler of features and ideas taken and modified from other sources to ultimately form what it is now. It is its own, true blue, concept. So if you recognize things that are similar to other games please do not flame me about it.

_,.-==-.,__,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_


The Perfect PVP System for Anarchy Online

By Tieku Moranora

_,.-==-.,__,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_

(Aggression Terminals)

The whole idea behind these Aggression Terminals is that a character could approach one of these and then use it to change his Aggression from passive [PVM only] to aggressive [PVP/PVM]. This new aggressive state of being would last for 2 hours and this 2 hour period will renew itself if the player kills someone. After the 2 hours have passed the player could then change back to passive if he or she chooses. This process could be thought of as signing up for active duty for your side. I suppose you have already discovered the implications of this. Fighting in the streets, death and destruction, and overall anarchy [As long as your party to the mayhem via the use of these terminals]. And real war will finally be possible, just think about it.

But now down to the nitty-gritty. I am going to go over the exact dynamics of the system and how it would fit into the pre-existing elements of the game world.

_,.-==-.,__,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_

(Aggression Terminal Shoppe)

When a player chooses to become Aggressive he or she would then travel to the local Aggression Terminal Shoppe. These shops would be located in side only cities, [As neutral could not partake in PVP unless they travel to an arena. This will be explained later.] and would be protected by the meanest of side guards, and plenty of them. This would prevent shop camping which I foresaw in the development of this concept.

_,.-==-.,__,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_

(Stepping out of the Shop)

Once you step out of the Terminal Shop you are in fact a different kind of player. Many aspects change for you and I will explain those now

All zone gas for an Aggressive player becomes either 25% or 0%. Nothing is sacred at this point. Keep in mind however combat between players on the same side will not be allowed except in 0% zones. Neutral cities will play a very important roll. All neutral cities and areas will be at 100% suppression for obvious reasons. Arenas too will have a roll. People who do not choose to become Aggressive can fight each other in Arena zones and other 0% zones.

Aggressive players can not be buffed by non-aggressive players. This would have to be done to avoid an obvious advantage of bringing a non-aggressive doctor with you on an Omni/Clan hunt. The doctor would be invulnerable to attack and still be able to heal his or her comrade. This would be very, very bad.

Aggressive players would only be able to be seen as Aggressive [By means of special radar symbols and selection colors] by other Aggressive players. This would have to be done to avoid God Scouting. Sending a non-aggressive player to scout a location for Aggressive enemies, and then report back to friendly Aggressive players. To scout an area with little risk you would have to send an Aggressive Agent. You can see where Im going with this.

Guards would still attack members of the opposite side regardless if youre Aggressive or not.

Whether or not Aggressive players could be teamed with non-aggressive players is a sensitive area to lay down the law. I know how I would prefer it but Ill list two options and leave it to you to decide.

1. Aggressive players CAN team with non-Aggressive players. But remember non-Aggressive players cannot participate in PVP combat unless within a 0% zone of some kind. So if youre running through a 75% zone [Which is actually 25% to your Aggressive friend] and an Aggressive enemy happens by and attacks your friend. You would be powerless to help him. Also teams containing a mix of Aggressive and non-Aggressive players would not get the benefits of Team levels. Only pure teams would get this advantage. This would eliminate any kind of exploiting and give Aggressive players a good reason to team up.

2. Aggressive players CAN NOT team with non-Aggressive players. Simple as that, yet would get complicated. Say if a non-Aggressive team was looking for 1 more to join and they happen across you [an Aggressive player] and you really want to join, you could not. Nor could the non-Aggressive players tell your Aggressive in the first place. So players would run into constant Sorry but I am Aggressive. as they looked for team mates.

**One way to remedy this is to make it so only members of the opposite side of the non-aggressive player would be indiscernible as Aggressive or non-Aggressive to the non-Aggressive player. Example: a CLAN non-Aggressive could not see if an OMNI were Aggressive or not. This idea in combination with option 1 might be the best course of action, but its up to you. **

Remember 0% is 0%. You can fight or be fought by anyone regardless if he or she is Aggressive or not.

_,.-==-.,__,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_

Ok now onto a particularly sensitive area. Should there rewards for killing people in PVP? Personally I say yes, and heres how I would make it work. This is the perpetual gem in the crown of this concept so please take the time to read it carefully and make sure you understand.

Right now in AO you get Titles as reward in PVP. This is a fine idea and by no means should be taken out. But what people really want is something that would make PVP combat worthwhile. So not only would useful PVP reward be more fun, but they would encourage people to go PVP so this whole story line for AO works out.

My proposal would involve a reworking of the XP Pinky items which are already in game.

First off XP Pinkies would only drop as the result of combat between two Aggressive players and pinkies would be no-drop of course. Pinkies will only be dropped if you kill a player that is at an equal or higher level than you. A player could not have more than 5 Pinkies in his or her inventory at one time. Pinkies will not be bankable.

Now what do I do with these pinkies? you ask.

Well in steps another item that would have to be added to the game world.

During missions or outdoor hunting, mobs close to the level of the player will drop items called Essence Extractors at a reasonable rate. When these are dropped by mobs they will be dropped IN ADDITION TO their normal loot, not as loot itself. Essence Extractors will be no-drop and you can only hold 5 of them in your inventory at one time and they will not be bankable.

You can then right-click an Essence Extractor onto a XP Pinky. This will destroy both the XP Pinky and the Essence Extractor, but will yield a Charged Essence Extractor. This Charged Essence Extractor can then be right-clicked to give the player an amount of XP bases upon the level of the player that the pinky came from. Example: A ql 50 Pinky from a level 50 player, who is being used by the level 49 player would killed him would yield exactly the same XP the player would get from killing a Boss mob of the same level. This would make PVP worth the time but not as effective as leveling in team via outdoor hunting or in missions. Everyone is happy.

Now I feel that the current setup of level ranges in PVP is not a very good system, so here is my take on it.

Level ranges will still be in effect, but as I mentioned before you get nothing for killing someone lower than you. But to couple with this, you will also get something taken away from you [That is if youre the one who started combat with the lower player. If a lower player based on the existing scale, comes after you Then you will still get nothing, but you can kill him penalty free.

I bet your asking, WHAT PENALTY!? Well here it goes

When you kill someone lower than you, you will loose a proportional amount of XP [If the player your killing is only a couple levels lower than you, then of course youll loose very little XP. But the wider the level gap, the more youre going to loose]. If you just leveled up and have no XP to loose, you will begin to form a XP Dept. This Dept will be infinite and could put a serious damper on the leveling process if you continue to kill newbs. Now when u get past level 75 and are free to PVP with anyone 75+ you could consider yourself safe from the very highest level because if a level 170 or the like were to come after your and kill you they would receive a crippling XP loss and it would defiantly teach them not to do it again.

**Now I know some of you are growing brains out there. I bet at least a few of you have thought of this tactic Attack a newb then hit Q to stop fighting, then let them hit you again and let your auto attack handle it. Thus getting you a penalty free newb kill. Sorry to rain on your parade, but when you attack a newb, a timer will be set. Any further combat with this newb in the next 15 minutes will result in penalty. You thought you were so smart. **

_,.-==-.,__,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_,.-==-.,_

Well thats about the size of it. If you like it let me know. If you dont like it let me know why. I would hope someone from Funcom would read it and like it but I know such a good system would never get implemented. Mainly because this system would require some massive changes and would probably be the whole content of a single patch.

But lets face facts here Funcom. The way PVP is AO works now is pathetic. You know it, we all know it.

I think Ill close with that statement. I cannot be anymore blunt.

**EDIT: I realized that a level 200 who cannot level anymore would hardly care if he had a massive XP dept. So a special PVP level range would have to kick in at level 200. Making it impossible for them to fight or be fought by anyone not in the range. **

**EDIT 2: Thank you Qui, I forgot to add this to original post. You can only get a pinky from a single person once. Once you get that Pinky a 24 hour timer is set. If you kill him again in 24 hours, no Pinky will result. You would have to have some serious patients to exploit with that in effect lol. **
