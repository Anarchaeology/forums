Reflect Bracers, PvP, and Ninja-Looting.
This is not a nerf cry. Hehe. (I have most of the bracers that I need anyway.)

Reflect bracers. Those beutiful things that deflects 21% of damage.

First let's do some math here. To start off, they are *extremely* hard to find nowadays. As far as I can tell, they no longer drop off of Unique mobs like they used to, and therefore 'ninjalooting' is the only way to effectivley get Reflect Bracers.

2x 20% Reflect Bracers and a 30% Soldier Reflect Shield allows 70% reflect. (72% is maximum, but a full set of ql 200 bracers are rare, and usually it will average out to 20% each)

In a PvP Situation:
2000 normal damage is delt. So, 1000 damage from halved PvP damage. Then, 70% from the reflect bracers... And you only get hit for 300 damage. (NormalDamage x .5 x .30)
A doctor can easily outheal a lot of damage. For example, an entire 10k alpha-strike pvp damage is reduced to 3000 damage, which a doc can Complete Heal to get rid of. I am not putting the pressure on docs, though. In other words, Reflect Bracers are very powerful.

Now, since they removed bracers from uniques (as far as I know), the only way to get them is from chests. The average drop of a Reflect Bracer seems to be about 1 in every 500 chests, as far as my expierence notes.

I am a 'ninja-looter', but I don't like to be. Many other people are as well, and none of them like it. It is also a psychological game. Every chest you don't get, running through your mind is that "Oh... there was a bracer in there..." Well, maybe that's just me, but anyway, it gets you rather stressed out in missions.

Anyway, there are a few solutions here.

For one, you could completely removed the bracers from the game. However, many people have worked a long time to get them - so this is not suggested.

Option A) Make Reflect Shields self-only. This gives Soldiers and Engies a unique advantage in Player versus Player.

Option B) In PvP, reduce the reflect qualities of bracers or all reflect in half.

Option C) Keep reflect bracers the way they are, and halve healing.

Option D) Keep reflect bracers the way they are and take Complete Heal out of PvP. (Not Suggested.)

For 'Ninja-Looting':

Option A) Make it so Bracers drop on unique mobs again. (not suggested - camp fest)

Option B) Make them more common in missions as loot.

Option C) Place bracers on Team Mission Boss Mobs (A 50% chance would be nice.)

Anyway, that's just my two cents.
