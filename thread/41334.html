beggers
personally, I don't like beggers (or is it spelled beggars? too lazy to look it up anywhere right now. I think I'll go with beggars from now on).

But one strange thing about them, they seem to make profit out of their "work". Otherwise they would have given up after a while, right?

A norwegian jurnalist once tried himself as a beggar one day. Just to see how is was.

He made about 10k kroner which is about $1250 (not accurat, but the 10k isn't either ), which around half a month salary for many norwegians. In one day!

So it is clearly profit from it. But it is degrading. You need to admit to yourself that you are unable to make money in a normal fashion, and thus the only option you have left, is living off other people's god will. I know I wouldn't be able to do that. Not in RL, not in a game. If all else fails, perhaps I would concider it. But it would be a big loss in selfrespect for me.

I suppose other don't have the same kind of selfrespect.

BUT: one of the things the jurnalist mentioned, is that the best way of getting profit from begging, was to not run around, following people, asking for money. It was when he sat down, with a sad face and a tin-can or something in front of him for money, that was when he got the most cash.

But that's in RL.
In AO, or any other game, money is usally easily obtainable. You kill something, you get cash. You do a mission, you get cash. You kill some grey monsters for no risk at all, and collect monsterparts, get them converted (either convert yourself or get someone else to do it), you get money . There is really no magic to it. Run around, get a team, do some teammissions. No big deal. The loot alone from some missions (team or single) is enough to get some decent amount of money. The nice thing about beeing in a team, is that you can do higher ql mission, and get higher ql loot/reward and sel it for more cash.

Oh, that brings us to the inflation-trouble many see today, but that's a completely different story.
