Better targeting!
I think the taget-cycle (pressing TAB) utterly sucks. I don't know how many times enemies come charging against me and when I press TAB it targets some enemy 200 yards from me instead of the attacking one.

Here's what I want:

The targeting should be distance/activity based. If enemies move or attack, they should be the first targets in cycle (if several attacks you/your team, the closes one should be targeted first). If there is no enemy activity (attacking, moving and so on) then the distance should decide the cycle.

Several times I've stood right in front of an enemy and pressing TAB, just to see the target being another enemy. I use the mouse for moving (holding down RMB) so I can't just click on the target.

If you fix this I'd be VERY happy!
