The "10 kills gets you freshman" thing is not true. It is variable, depending upon the level and title of the people you are killing or dying to.

The way it works is, you get a certain number of points towards your next pvp title with each kill you make, and you lose a certain number of points with each death to another player. It takes a certain number of points to get the next title, nobody knows how many.

You get more points for killing higher level players and higher titled players, less points for killing lower level players and lower titled players. You lose more points for dying to low level low title players than you do when you lose to high level high titled players.

And that's as much as anyone knows about how the pvp title system works. It's possible that some of the above is wrong, but this is the current theory.
