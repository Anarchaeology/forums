QLs for guns
ZenBeck your gun is WAY to low for you. I have a 39 Agent using a 51 rifle. No twinks or buffs needed. I do have some QL30 implants (one faded and one bright).

I "heard" that heavy damage is better than quick weak damage. I am not positive on this but it seems to work for me. Agents below lvl 50 have a wide choice of rifles. The Gripo generally is a strong choice because it shoots fast. I chose other brands becasue their low damage is higher even though they shoot slower.

Also, and way more important, I have raised my evades. Implants and IPs. I set my agg/def bar to about 60%. I had to play with this a little and 60 ended up being optimome for strong MOBs.

I have stood and slugged it out with heavy yellows to the tune of six 1st aides! I also have my 1st aide as high as I can and buff it. I do retreat once in awhile but not often. I am NOT uber by any means. I buf my rifle with a nano that adds 4pts per hit and I use "lethargy" on every MOB- this means they can't dodge me as much. My minimum is low and hits more often than I like but with the extra 4 pts. it is usually enough.

Remember now, no bursts. Single shot only and no aimed shots. My order of preference is:
weapon,
armor,
implants,
skills (attack and evade equal) then
armor.

Wish you well. I know how frustrating it can get.
