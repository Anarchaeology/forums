There's no excuse for rudeness. That's for sure. I'd use my Monofilament Whip on his butt, if I were his father.

I think we got a problem with a lot of these huge guilds. People are in them for the wrong reasons. They aren't there for friendships or common ways of thinking, yet they are there to get phat lewt or benefit from the guild's towers .... all the while not knowing half the people in the guild or having any intention on being a PvP'er most every time they log on.

If I were in a guild that had towers, and tower battles continued to be the thing that they are now, I would be in a quandary. I'd log on and have the option of doing something I found enjoyable, perhaps meeting new people, perhaps killing some monster I hadn't killed before, perhaps getting some new item to tweak my guy, or getting some item to tweak a friend .... or going to the cesspool that is a tower battle, what would I do. I personally would reluctantly go and help my guildies, because if they are my guildies, they are by definition my friends. But, I wouldn't enjoy it and I might think twice before logging in again during 25% hours.

Now, my guild might feel bad because they enlisted me and I came and helped with something that, to be frank, I'd rather be watching some TV or organizing my sock drawer than doing. They are forced into this situation because they need all bodies possible for a defense or attack for fear of losing the almighty PvM org bonuses.

Nobody is happy.

Now, I have an opportunity perhaps to check a raid channel and even though my friends in my guild are out tower bashing, I can still go and have fun.

What is missing, as was pointed out above, is an analogue to the raid bot that is just as popular but designed for PvP. This way, everyone who would rather PvP than kill AI-controlled monsters could get together and do what they like.

Then when the day is over, you get together with everyone in your guild and swap stories.

What I don't like are the comments by a few people above who seem to think that 40 people who are all in the same organization have some sort of god-given right versus 40 people of varying organizations who band together to achieve a goal. Just because you are in a guild means nothing in this context. You are just a bunch of people. Being in a guild gives you no special rights other than a common chat channel. I get alerts from certain raid bots and sometimes I help. I read the comments by those in certain supposedly 'powerful' guilds and think to myself:

'Now, what the heck are they complaining about? They have every right to implement a capable and fair raid tool that rewards participation and not just ass licking just like this. Hell, the source is freely available. If they want to have these activities with only their members and friends, then they have the right to do exactly what I just saw'.

What's the name of the guilds up there that are complaining? 'Legion', I think is one. There's nothing from stopping you from creating LegioCom and working just as hard as these other people to ensure that it is a success.
