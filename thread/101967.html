Pricing for tradeskills
FIRST: These numbers are good basic numbers for tradeskills. If you get good fast service you can/should consider tipping over the charge if you can well afford it.



Implants: 125-200 1K per QL per piece. Minimum charge is 2K per the level of the person making it.**

Nano Creation: 30-100: 3K Per QL. Minimum charge 3K per level of the person making it.**
Nano Creation: 101-180: 4K: per QL. Min charge 4K per level of the person making it.**
Nano creation: 181-200: A good tip would be 1-2M
Nano creation: 201-215: A good tip would be 2-3M

Implant Disassembly Units/Treatment Libraries: 30-180 3K Per QL. Minimum charge is 3K per lvl of the person making it**
Implant Disassembly Units/Treatment Libraries: 180-200 3K Per QL. Min charge 500K. A good tip would be 750-1M.

Guns: 30-180: 2K Per QL. Min charge 2K per lvl of the person making it**
Guns: 180-200: 3K Per QL. Min charge 4K per lvl of the person making it**

Stims/Emergency Treatment Labs: 125-170 4K Per QL lvl + cost of items.

Agression Enhancer: 3K Per QL.

Virral Triumvirate Egg : 2M Minimum.

Augmented Bow Tie : 250K Minimum. Good tip 500-1M.

Overtuning Tank Armors: 2K Per lvl of the person making it. Good tip: 3K per lvl of the person making it. They provide the tool**

CAS/Barter Armor takes the same skill so consider I consider the following to be for both.

CAS Symbiotic Armor 30-180: 3K per QL Minimum charge 3K per lvl of the person making it.**
CAS Symbiotic Armor 181-200: 4K Per QL minimum charge 1M.**

Bronto Hide Armor: 30-180 3K per QL minimum charge 3K per lvl of the person making it.**
Bronto Hide Armor: 181-200 4K per QL minimum charge 1M.**

Metallic Mantis Armor: 30-180K 3K Per QL minimum charge 3K per the level of the person making it**
Metallic Mantis Armor: 181-200 4K Per QL minimum charge 1M.**

Flesh Hoods : 30-180: 3K Per QL minimum charge 3K per the lvl of the person making it.**
Flesh Hoods : 181-200: 4K Per QL minimum charge 1M.**

Carbonum Plate Armor: 30-180: 2K Per QL minimum charge 2K per the level of the person making it.**
Carbonum Plate Armor: 181-200: 2K Per QL. Minimum charge 500K. Making a Full set with 30% discount. **

Rollerrat Carbonum Helmet: 1M for the first piece 500K for each additional.

Junk Metal : QL125-150 : 250K Min or 100K a Piece whichever is higher.
Junk Metal : QL151-190 : 1M Min or 500K a Piece whichever is higher.
Junk Metal : QL191-200 : 3M Min or 1M a Piece whichever is higher.
Note: Do not bother bringing a piece that is QL210+ robot part to a tradeskills person unless you check with them first. It requires 6XQL in ME to create so the requirement is very high.


Implants requiring 1250 in QFT : 5M EACH! They should thank you for such a low price too.

Implants requiring 1250 in Psychology 10M " " " " " "

Implants requiring 1200+ in WS : 5M " " " " " "

Implants requiring 1100+ in Pharm: 5M " " " " "


Dragonflesh Armor: 1M per piece.

Coffee Machines: 500K.

Custom Towers: QL50-125: 4K per lvl of the person making it.**
Custom Towers: 126-175: 500-1M each or 4K per QL lvl.**
Custom Towers: 176-190: 2M Each.
Custom Towers: 191-200: 5M Each.
Custom Towers: 201-217: 6M Each.
Custom Towers: 217-222: 8M Each.
Custom Towers: 223-230: 10M Each
^^ You supply the nanobots


Custom Laser Towers: 160-255: 2.5M Each


Other considerations: The items value: If your making a GAIV you should tip heavy because of the trust involved hence the price for the VTE.



**= Minimum charge should be whatever is the greater in the case of multiple pieces.
Reasoning: Chemistry/Pharm Tech should cost people more. No one uses it for ANYTHING useful except for tradeskills.
Nano creation: Requires 5 skills when creating that needs to be maintained at a decent level.
Bronto/Flesh/Mantis/Dragon/Carb: Price of the PPPE and skills involved in creation.

Adjustments will be made as market conditions change
