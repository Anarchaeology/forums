Right now, I believe that xp awarded for the kill depends a lot on mob's bar color. A lvl 100 player killing a lvl 80 mob will receive quite a bit less xp than a lvl 80 player killing that same mob. When soloing, most players go for green to lower orange, while teams can go for solid reds - who give a whopping lot more exp.
At lvl 122, I get about 10k exp for a yellow kill, but taking out a lvl 155-160 red gives me 40-43k. A lvl 155 is not 4 times tougher than lvl 122, but I get 4 times more xp because he's so red. Thas's the main source of team xp advantage - they can make better use of the tough mob bonus. And of course, a team can usually take things down much faster, and far more efficiently.

Soloing can be made a bit more viable if they increase the exp awarded by mobs, while at the same time trimming down the team bonus. But as I pointed before, team bonus is not what makes the difference.
