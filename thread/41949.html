Will Sparta be in Shadowlands?
Back in beta, when I was still trying to learn the basics of the game, I likened Tir to be the Washington D.C. of the clans, a place where they made laws but didn't really do much else, a place of corruption, if you will (Tir was supposed to be the most wishy-washy city of the clans, home of the pacifists and the tree-hugging CoT). Athen was somewhat like Los Angeles, a big city with their fancy buildings and their ladies' men and their fast cars. Likewise, Athen was described as somewhat of a show town, not really holding much importance, but more of a "Hey, look what we can do" to the Omnis. And then there was Sparta, which I compared to New York City (maybe in particular the Bronx), a place where all the hardened badasses lived and where alcohol and cigarettes were never in short supply, and where stuff really happened. It was supposedly the headquarters for the militant clans to plan and train, far the the wastelands of the northwest, safely away from the Omnis. Yet Sparta has vanished.

FC, you have mentioned Jobe in what little insight into Shadowlands you have given us, what about Sparta? Yeah, I'm Omni, and I'll probably never be there, but it is an important city in the overall scheme of things, probably more important than Jobe.
