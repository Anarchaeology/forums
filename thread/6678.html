Nanomages: Their advantage?
Exactly what distinct advantage do Nanomages have over other breeds?

Back in Beta when Breed effected skills of the professions, though it was changed (I believe - not 100% sure, but I've heard about this) then I am sure it was not a bad profession for nano oriented classes.

However, you have to ask why not to play other breeds instead of nanomages?

Solitus, for example. All in all, they will have more HPs, easier to raise their skills (No need to raise psychic - it's broken.. does not increase nanopool, so that's a skill you don't need at all). You can raise all abilities 3 points per level, so there is no advantage/disadvantage there. Max intelligence and stamina for the average NT, and then maybe strength for Str/Stam armor, because Str also raises Time and Space, which makes it perfect for a pet class. Max Int/Str/Sta and you have all the needed skills, and it is cheaper too.

So, where is the distinct advantage for a nanomage? They have green Int/Psy and that's it, and psy is more or less a useless skill...
