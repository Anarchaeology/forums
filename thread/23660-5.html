Bump

long...sorry..

but I don't think we need anymore healing, OMHH may/may not suck when you are able to cast it, but Soldiers really shouldn't be good healers. We just should be able to take things down faster.

As some others have said...we SHOULD be masters of Ranged Combat though.

- improve our ability to increase our Attack Rating with our guns
- Maybe give us a Ranged Crit buff (self or not)
- Extend our Damage Amplifier Nano, stopping at +10 is a bit silly really
- Increase our AC Nano line

- a Nano that reduces our reload time?? I read somewhere someones idea of a 0 reload time, the Nano would act like an ammo belt directly to the ammo in the Soldier backpack. NB if nothing else, then surely a reload button isnt too much to ask.

- something like a Full Auto Nano(different from the Full Auto special), that allowed us to fire constantly with like 0 sec or tiny recharge rate for 20 seconds(scaled by level) or until the current clip is empty(wouldnt stack with the above idea if this option was used, of course).

I dont think we need our HP Nanos to match the Enfs, but we should have good if not the best shielding/armour available. Were soldiers ffs! You know, the big heavily armoured, gun toting dude at the front line. We shouldnt necessarily be able to take more damage, we should be able to STOP more damage getting to us. TMS and Deflection does a damn fine job of that, but our AC line needs a steeper improvement curve.

- Make some good damage dealing weapons use the Heavy Weapons skill. I don't think Prof specific items is a very good idea, as that would lead to carbon copies of peeps.

When I picked a Soldier, I normally play mage type chars in games, AO is a game based in the future. I wanted to utilise the future aspect as best I could, and to me that meant excelling in GUNS. I do get a bit downhearted when I see other professions wielding guns just as well as us. Which to me means they get our cool ability to wield guns, AND they get their cool ability to <insert prof specfic ability here>

- I read someones ideas of some cool Nano ideas
- Buffing Vehicle Ground or Vehicle Air would be damn fun and not too Uber. Maybe +25, +50, +100 (or whatever stepping) to Vehicle Ground, which also adds +15, +30, +60 (or whatever) to Top Speed in the vehicle.

- Coding it so Grenades could be thrown without unwielding your gun with a 'grenade' button. Give soldiers something else to do than wait for the Fling Shot special to come back.


Basically I like my Soldier, I do get asked into some groups, Lots of Hit points, good of Damage. But if there is a Trader or an MA around, then I get skipped.

...And not necessarily to do specifically with Soldiers, BUTwhy on Rubi-Ka are there only 3-4ish types of vehicles?? This is the F-U-T-U-R-E! With a few weeks/months/years of the invention of transport, there were literally hundreds of variations and themes of modes of transport, from flying/driving/riding/hovering/ on water, off, etc. Why dont we have a few more styles of vehicles!!?!! It would save theWhere are you?Im in the Yalm.Which friggin one???

And here is the Soldier bit, maybe we could get a Troop Transport vehicle, an armoured vehicle able to transport the whole team somewhere, together, safely. I know there is the Gunship, and maybe it isnt possible, but getting a team of peeps to a hot spot is a main aspect of current warfare. Everyone arriving at the scene of a battle 1 by 1, leads to lots of deaths1 by 1.

I dont want to be Uber I just want to be the best I can be

Sorry for rambling.

Some things..
I have mixed feelings about soldiers being able to alphastrike anyone to death instantly. To me this would pretty much reduce 1on1 pvp to worthlessness. And team PVP.. well one thinks of EQ where the useless class (Ranger typically) was sent forward to absorb harm touch (instant death) and then hope someone resurrects him before the fight's over. I wonder who'd be sent ahead to absorb the alphastrikes? Fixers? Adventurers?

Seriously it would be great and appropriate in my mind if a soldier could burst me to death instantly. Or hell, really, it doesn't sit well in my mind that anyone should be able to absorb 3+ shots from any weapon easily. But that's how it goes, I think you'll have to live with the non-instant-killing in PVP, because it's so much worse if you don't.

Burst could be better though.

Full Auto should be an area effect. Whether this is better or worse is irrelevant. You're unloading a clip, it should affect an area. I know they can do it, just look at the Fixer cluster bullets thing.


Or failing that, give Soldiers a special attack. I'm happy fixers got that cluster bullets nonsense. But by definition Soldiers should have something like that as well. Maybe an area effect stun, like having a concussion grenade launcher hooked on your novaflow..


By making existing weapons Soldier only, they'd just anger everyone else.. but were they to, say, add some sort of soldier only gatling cannon (requiring heavy weapons/ranged energy/assault rifle perhaps) that was soldier only and had rather immense stats I wouldn't be opposed to that. Since adequate representation exists in the Burst/FullAuto weapons markets, I'd be interested in seeing a gun that has a high rate of fire AND high damage, but no specials except maybe Flingshot. Picture a waist mounted gun that fires every second or so (if you've got good ranged init), but can take out structures and vehicles.. well ok maybe that's not what most people have in mind. When I think professional soldier, I think heavy hardware. Any schmuck can carry around a tiny little Novaflow.


I think it would be an appropriate direction for soldier weapons to take to begin utilizing with more frequency the Heavy Weapons/Ranged Energy/maybe even Grenade skills. We all know Grenades are busted, and most people have ceased to care. Funcom can decide at any time to turn that around, and after 14.2 and IPR we will be able to adapt. They can give Soldiers big HW and Grenade buffs..

I remember sometime, long long ago, seeing some kind of shoulder-mounted cannons. I think it was during an E3 years ago. Soldiers need some auxiliary damage? I can see a shoulder mounted cannon being very cool. And Soldier only. Or maybe a defensive cannon that shoots down incoming projectiles. Personal defense grid? Oh yes. Think like Parry works, well, how it works for NPCs anyway.

Nano lines.. I have real, and horrible issues with the many skill requirements put on non-'caster' profession nano programs. The soldier health buffs are a worthless drain of IP.

Really, too many profs have to use too many nano skills that have bad skill curves. Soldiers, how many are used? 4? 5? 6? Depends on which nano lines you abandoned... Also, Soldier T&S is linked to one of their most important nano lines, and they have the worst IP curve in it.

Example good: Almost all Doc nanos use 2-3 skills, with a few not-entirely-necessary nanos that use other skills. Nanotechs, Engineers, and a few others are in this same boat. Appropriate skills for said professions are also green. They are casters after all.

Example bad: Soldiers, Enforcers, Adventurers.. don't have extensive personal experience with all of these, but I'm aware that they have pretty bad skill curves and most people choose to ignore half the nano programs their profession gets because they just can't raise those skills. No matter what. I suppose maybe you could say 'to hell with my stats, evades, weapon skills, treatment/firstaid/spying/vehicle skills, I want to raise my nanos'

Example worst: Fixer. Got to feel for the little fellas sometimes. All 6 skills are used, all 6 have the worst possible curve, and all 6 are arguably attached to useful nano lines (except mattcrea/grid armor, which you have about a 1:1000000 chance of ever getting anyway so you might not need to raise that.)

Ok running off on a tangent put aside for now, I am suspecting if you take an average of the IP Soldiers have in Biomet (or Mattmet for that matter) right now, then take the same sample again after IPR, it's going to be very significantly closer to 0 after. There are few reasons I can see to keep the health buff line, or the AC shield line. Either way you're wasting precious IP you need for T&S and Mattcrea to mirror, since you have to build your entire career around that nano for lack of anything else useful.

(on a side note, I -guess- if you were an atrox soldier you might leave points in biomet to use Da Taunter.. I guess..)

We know, after the adventurer multi ranged incident, that they're never going to change a skill color again.

That doesn't mean that they can't change the requirements on the programs though. I hate how many profs are locked out of using half their nanos due to skill requirements, even if the nanos in question need some serious work (such as the soldier's.)

So you say, by definition, that body buffs -have- to be biomet? That's just fine. I am saying that they will cease to exist.. as they pretty much have already. IPR will just be the final stroke that slays them forever.

I've already typed far more than anyone cares to read, and am just sort of ranting at this point anyway.. summary:

Add some soldier only heavy weaponry. Make these use soldier-specific skills if you don't make them flat out soldier only. Make Heavy and Grenade useful skills somehow with this.

Add soldier shoulder mounted cannons.

Aggregate some skill reqs on prof-specific nanos if possible.

Leave PVP/Alphastrikes alone. Complaints about it are a symptom of the problem, changing it won't cure the real issue.

Give soldiers some kind of special attack, maybe like the Fixer got, maybe something different.

Burst needs a looking at. I watched its damage go down, which hurt my fixer at the time, and I watched it never go back up. Burst should consist of 3-5 hits, each of which can be a critical. Might beef up alphastrikes, sure, but it'd leave a factor of randomness in that would not necessarily mean instant death.

FullAuto should come under consideration for possible area effect. This is not always a beneficial thing, given that there can be guards, calmed/mesmerized mobs nearby, etc.. but you ARE unloading a clip of ammo in the target's general direction, assuming there's any recoil it should be area effect.

I could type more, but everyone has stopped reading by now.
