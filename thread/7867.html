Tanks, and other War Machines, why can't we get them?
By War Machines, I am refering to tanks, armored troop transports...
In the air, I see fighters protecting the bombers, and dogfights between the fighters...
In the water (what water there is), I see nuclear powered submarines and battleships, aircraft carriers with scores of planes aboard.

If we are to wage a war, we need the tools of war. We need to start production on war vehicles, and train our ranks in the proper use of them.

Simple one man transportation devices are great, but there is a distinct difference in mere transportation and putting holes in your enemies with your tank.
In fact, I am all for letting people with high mechanical engineering and electrical engineering be allowed to install guns on your transportation vehicle so you can defend yourself while you are in your car moving around.

Thanks for your attention, bump me if you agree!
