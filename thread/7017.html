Shopping (trade) channels
Everyone who has tried selling something on the Shopping Channel knows its a pig of a thing...
You try to sell something on there, only to have it spammed out of the way in a second by other things being sold.

This, must, annoy both seller and buyer - it is impossible to pick out a certain item from this huge list and thus, not much is sold.

I don't know how the AO engine works, I don't know if this could be done - But, how about the idea of a "Filtering" system on the Shopping Channel. A option which allows people to filter all messages out except ones regarding a certain product (e.g. "Practiced Health Haggler" and any messages which contain "Practiced Health Haggler" will then be displayed)

Imagine how much easier it would be - for both customer and merchant - a customer can simply turn on the shopping channel type a filter and go about killing mobs until someone is selling the product, a /tell message can then be typed and everyones happy.


any thoughts/comments?
