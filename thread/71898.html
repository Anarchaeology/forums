Creating a new breed is a big task, especially something as unique as a Rhinoman. So the first two questions that come to mind are What role do Rhinomen play in within AO?, and How do you select a Rhinoman as your char?

One possible role for Rhinomen would be as defenders of the natural state of Rubi Ka. So their main focus would be to remove all non-native people and constructions. This would fit in well with the Notum War stuff because a key Rhinoman role would be exactly to eliminate Notum Towers and their defenders, independent of owner.

To enhance the PvP role, they wouldn't be affected by the supression gas, but rather their strength (physical and magical) would vary depending on the degree of naturalness of the location, and the amount of Notum present. So in cities they'd be very weak, but in the desert, and around the Notum Towers (which mine Notum so there'd be lots present) they'd be very strong. This would make them fierce opponents in the Notum Wars and strong defenders of their home, but not so strong as to be able to successfully attack large cities. Clearly, destroying a Notum Tower would increase the degree of naturalness of the locaction, so they'd derive concrete benefits from doing so.

Getting Rhinomen into the game would have to involve some special transition because the reception area is set up for colonists, not natives. You could probably work around that without a huge amount of difficulty, but I think it be fun to have Rhinomen be the product of some sort of mystical transition of normal characters.

This would allow a higher degree of intelligence than the usual Rhinoman you meet as well. As a key aspect of Rhinomen is their attunement to Notum, it seems appropriate that the transition would require an initial attunement that is also reasonably strong. Perhaps then only Nanomage breed characters could make the transition to Rhinomen. The prof they become as Rhinomen would depend on the prof they initially chose in the normal character selection process.

The mysical transition to Rhinoman would involve some sort of trial, at the end of which you become a full-fledged Rhinoman. This could involve a quest, but I think a special cave or other locale that had a set of trials would be more interesting, and easier to keep track of. Something like a static dungeon. As you progress though this trial you begin to lose your normal char appearance and looking increasginly more like a Rhinoman. When you emerge triumphant, you have proven your worth and can begin your life as a mighty Rhinoman.

Anyway, just some thoughts.
