I am very happy to see that FC now takes an offical stand against thieves. I do a few comments on the rule though.

quote:
Ninja looting of corpses is not allowed. Ninja looting includes looting an item from a corpse boss monster when the players who killed it are still discussing who should get the item and looting an item from a monster then logging out/leaving the team to avoid other players.
First about the first rule: "looting an item from a corpse boss monster when the players who killed it are still discussing who should get the item"
A lot of times the item doesn't get looted during the discussion, but when the lootwindow has to be transferred to the winner of the itemroll. The lootholder has to close the window, giving the thief an opportunity to try to quickly hijack the lootwindow and steal the loot. So in other words. The discussion was over, but after it it was still ninjaed.

A better wording would perhaps be:
"looting an item from a boss monsters corpse before the players who killed it have divided the loot"


About the 2nd rule: "looting an item from a monster then logging out/leaving the team to avoid other players."

First of all, it seems this sentence is not complete, it looks like something else was supposed to come after it. This rule seems very confusing to me right now. I'm not sure what situations do and what don't fall under this one.

I hope the following situations is also supposed to be addressed by this rule:
Take for example the Guardian of Tomorrow who drops the rare Tank. It's a nodrop item so whoever takes it has it forever. What if the tank drops, and is rolled for within the team as it should. Tim wins the role, but Tom having his eye on the tank for so long, takes it anyway. Leaving Tim empty handed.

I hope this situation is also to be found under the 2nd rule. Please clarify that sentence a bit, so it's clear what it does apply too.


My last point is about this policy:
quote:
Originally posted by Lucien

The item is deleted, it is not returned to anyone since the original owner is extremely difficult to determine and it can end up with one persons word against the other.
I do not agree to this, because it still leaves the robbed player empty handed. You might have catched the thief, but the player stolen from also is still punished.

You say it's extremely difficult to determine who the intended owner is. I don't agree, take the 2 situations I mentioned above. First take a roll at a big mob like tara or mercs where someone wins the role, but the loot is hijacked when the loot window is transferred. Since the item has been rolled, and won by someone it seems very clear to me who the intended owner would be.
Also in the 2nd situation of Tim and Tom, again it seems clear that Tim was the intended owner.

I suggest to have the policy to be to return the item if the owner is clear, and only if there is doubt to not return the item. I like to remember that with killing mercs there are often well over 40 players helping (highest count was 89 we had) so if all those 40 say Tim won, it really isn't one word against the other right?
If it is 1 word against the other, deleting the item seems more fair, but when there is no doubt at all, it doesn't seem fair to me, and the item should be given to the player who won the roll.
