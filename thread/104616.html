ooc - A tribute to Mr Rogers
Ok this isn't roleplaying (I know!) But I needed to post something about this somewhere, so here it is..

I grew up watching and loving Mr Rogers.. I think everyone has for the past 50 some odd years, but unfortunatly today, Mr Rogers has passed away.

He had a huge heart and great love for children, and children loved him.. Please all find it in your hearts to pray for his family, friends, co-workers and all the children who, in a way, has lost a best friend.

Here's to a great man.
