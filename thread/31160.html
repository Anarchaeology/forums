Introducing the Professionals
The Community Team is proud to announce the creation of a new player program, nicknamed the Professionals. This group is made up of 24 players - 2 from each of the 12 professions - that will work closely with the Community Team to identify and highlight profession specific problems, issues and suggestions from the player community. The aim of the Professionals is to ensure each profession has an equal voice in such a large community by taking discussions from the bulletin board and presenting them to the Community Team for further discussions with the designers. The Professionals will also be a channel for profession specific information from Funcom, such as changes, fixes and improvements. Essentially, the role of the Professionals is to make sure all professions are well represented and all professions are well heard and addressed.

I see it as just not practical to have anyone pour through that much information and input. Something valid *will* be overlooked, at least in my experience.

I manage a major internal system for a large organization, I am the chief 'user'. Since systems exist at and for the pleasure and purpose of the users, I determine the priorities, what will and will not be done, and when.

I have 18,000 other users, all of whom want, and deserve satisfaction. There is just no way I can go through input from that many sources and make timely and correct decisions.

So, I did exactly what FC has done - I appointed a working group of users. It was the only functional and positive way I came up with for allowing all to have a voice and still have a manageable amount of data to sort through and digest.

Maybe you have a better, but still practical idea. A free for all doesn't really qualify, though, at least I don't think so. Because a free for all is highly unlikely to yield anything of lasting value. But an organized, sincere attempt at self prioritization is just the opposite - it is far more likely to offer rich and constructive suggestions.

I do not see how it fails to offer anyone anything they don't have today. If you do not want to participate in this, you can continue to send your feedback directly to Funcom. Nor do I think it is incumbent on the two people representing each profession to necessarily personally agree with every thought and suggestion made to them. I trust them to be capable of having a personal disagreement with an idea, but still to be able to put it forth in a sensible manner.

These folks aren't here to obstruct or simply put forth two personal agendas per profession. Their purpose is to be a conduit.
