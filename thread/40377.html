Bionitrous
Supa Leet
The way Atrox is hosed compared to Solitus and Opifex is not even funny. I don't begrudge the others their mental superiority. That's not what this is about. It's about the total lack of a clear advantage to offset the deficiencies.

I got 200 more hitpoints than a Solitus. Big friggin' deal considering I got a nano pool that's 3/4 the size and my less refined brain can't execute the same nanos.

It's supposed to be about balance. What one breed can dish out in enhanced nano damage, another can absorb with brute force, and another can evade or run away to come back.

Point is Solitus kicks Atrox and NanoMage rear. The extra Strength and Stamina of the Atrox are worthless. Pointless. A waste. They do absolutely nothing for him. They have no ingame value. There might be one armor in the game that only an Atrox can get the skills to wear.

Intelligence has value. It's clear that 80 more intelligence for a Solitus versus an Atrox is about +10 to each and every nanoskill. Now add in the bumps from Psychic and Sense. Huge advantage there. Tangible difference in-game. What does Str and Sta buy you? +12 to 2HB or 2HE? Great. An extra 1 damage.

And please, I beg to to God, don't start putting in friggen 'items'. I'm sick of the Diablo II 'item, item, item' mentality. The simple answer (at least for Atrox) is one more HP per point of BodyDev. That might give him 1000 HP more than Solitus instead of 200 at breed caps. If you ask me that sounds just about right at the end-game.

Please, no balancing with items. We're sick of it already. Just ask the poor NanoMages who are forced into the hell that is Prof. Jone's Vest and the hated Shades of Lucubration. We don't ever want to see that fiasco again. It's what you consider, hmmm .... not fun.

An even more elegent solution that could be slipped into 14.6 is simply to double or triple the HP regeneration rate of the Atrox. You know that piddly '12' that floats over yer head every now and then as you naturally heal. Well if it were a '24' or a '36' it would give a sense of identity to the breed while not affecting the core of the current game mechanics very much if at all. It's been suggested many times that an increased HealDelta would be a nice 'babystep' start on the road towards proper balance. The other option is to have the current HealDelta remain the same but triple the rate.

Think of yer Atrox as 'Wolverine' the kind of guy you slash his arm and it's sorta healed by the next day.

Fiddle with what you want, just note that most Atrox are not asking to be smarter. Just to have the increased size and metabolism be tweaked enough to offset the downsides.

And once again: no 'items' plz.
