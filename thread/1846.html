For Those of Us Who DON'T PvP...
It's an important issue, and as most nerfs and breaks have been in response to complaints about PvP, I think it's THE MOST IMPORTANT issue in the game. OK, top 5... :^)

I don't like PvP. I'm here for an RPG story, and since we can't yet attack enemy outposts, take them over, occupy them, etc., PvP is little more than a Quake game popping up in the middle of my Cyberpunk EverQuest.

I don't grudge anyone PvP. I DO grudge them sucker-punching me in a political zone when I'm just trying to buy meds or walk to my mission. And since 100% supression zones DON'T WORK when you flee to them, we non-combattants have to suffer the pain in the ass of duking it out and hoping for the best. When you win, the other guy whines that you're a cheater, exploiter, uberyada, whatever. When you lose,...well, it's a long walk back to where you were.

I have no interest in PvP, especially since it's the primary cause of breaking my expensive game. I don't like PK's or ambushers. I just want to grow my character, hang out with cool friends, and enjoy the story. When the story BECOMES PvP, I'll deal, but right now, it's adolescent bullies in a schoolyard rolling unsuspecting innocents for lunch money. Who cares.

My idea is to create a generic nano called Supression Gas. 25-30th level minimum to use (appropriate stats). Lasts 20-30 minutes. Can't be used in combat (to prevent exploits). Result? You can't PvP, and no one can PvP you. Cost? Monsters can still kill you. OK, call it "Neutrality" or "Cease Fire" or whatever. I don't care. Just give me a shot at buying a damn backpack at MMD without some punk blindsiding me to make himself feel better about his small...weapon.

End of tirade.

Anyone else hate PvP and want an alternative?
