The Gridgrid report: Rhinomen attack, reets dive, and brontos walk
Welcome to this week's edition of the newly-titled Gridgrid report, seeking to bring you news of the more interesting side of Rubi-Ka life, hopefully on a weekly basis. We start today's report with another hopefully weekly event: a profile of one of the more interesting Rubi-kan personalities.

An Enterprising Adventurer
Meet Mike300, an adventurer with a different spin on earning money. Tired of taking missions for credits and with few other options at the level of 22, Mike300 earns his living in a far more dangerous way: as a daredevil stuntman.

On the days he's at work, you can find him in Andromeda by the whompas, advertising. "Daredevil reet will jump for credits!" he shouts from the rooftop, greeted by cheers and applause from the onlookers below. A few begin to chant "Jump jump jump" and then, he does. In a flurry of feathers, he plummets to the ground, aiming at the onlooker who offered the most credits.

I stopped Mike300 after one display to ask a few questions. He says that he has jumped in leet form as well and eventually aims to perform his daredevil dive as both a playful cub and a pit lizard. The exhibitions seem to be rather lucrative as well. I asked how much he had earned and his number was well in the hundreds of thousands of credits. While we spoke, he grinned and pointed at a passing Yalmahala, saying that his goal was to one day have one of those to fly in. Perhaps people are more likely to donate money to an enterprising youth than a sedentary one.

A bronto in peril
Running through Tir county on my way to a mission, I came upon a very sad sight indeed. Groaning and moaning quite loudly, a minibronto lay helpless upon the ground. I checked to see if it would move, but to no avail. Poking and prodding did nothing for the poor creature.

Just before I was about to put it out of its misery, I raised my arms to the sky begging for aid, which was quickly granted. The poor beast struggled to his feet and began walking around!

One may only guess why the poor creature was in such a state. I would assume it has to do with some of the toxic green liquid found in caves in the area. A young bronto may not be able to handle the high levels of toxins left by omni-tek.

Impending Attack!
Could it be that the rhinomen are planning an attack? This reporter firmly believes so.

All of the rhinomen authorities refused to comment other than saying "Me strong, me crush bones" or, more foreboding, "Must break bones". Even the underlings had been trained to give no information. However, after I had asked questions for some time, they grew wary of my presence. After a chase around the village, I was shortly sent back to reclaim.

I advise strong caution when dealing with rhinomen in the future, especially if you happen to be an opifex female by the name of Gridgrid.

And the last tidbit...
It seems that Omni-tek has installed decoys in some of their mine caves. From a distance, they seem to be regular employees, but on a closer examination, it is obvious that they are not what they seem. Perhaps the legions of clan members sweeping into omni offices and sending all workers to reclaim has finally manifested itself with such a cost that distractors such as these are necessary.
