Suggestion: Re-vamp the mission terminals
Apparently with the advent of 14.2, a lot of folks using the AOMD/ClickSaver app will be unhappy. While it is FunCom's right to determine policy regarding 3rd party apps, it is the responsibility of both sides to come to some sort of compromise with concessions by both parties.

I suggest an overhaul to the mission terminals be made:
  1. Instead of the incessant clicking/scrolling/sifting fest, maybe a setting could be set for a brief or verbose mode for the manner in which mission information is presented.

    VERBOSE
    *shiftleftclick*
    *one window pop*
    Mission story/background...
    .
    .
    .
    .
    .
    .
    .
    <item image>
    Item Description

    BRIEF
    *shiftleftclick*
    *onewindowpop*
    Omni Forest 123,-123 Kill/Get/Fix/Watch Soandso.
    <item image>
    Item Description

    (Note that with the present map system coordinates are superfluous anyway.)

    This way, both the players who like description/storyline/ambience/fluff can have it and those who what to get straight to the goods can as well.
  2. Add an option to save the mission preferences client-side maybe?
  3. Add some sort of shortcut to present new mission lists without having to cancel and re-rightclick on the terminal (there is a way around this already actually but nothing hard coded).
  4. Please re-look at the algorithm/formula/hamster hitting a random button that is determining the mission rewards...either it is too random, way off, or something. Maybe tweak the database that it accesses to not present so much junk. Now I understand that this may be some calculated way to diffuse the amount of leveling/equipment release in to the world, but I think as it stands it is very tedious to sift and sift and sift and my mind starts to wander and think about why the hell I sit here and do this just to get some dang Vito's sleeves! Source of frustration for a lot of folks. Is there any way to hook the profession of the requester so that missions are better suited? Keep some of the lotto action, but for the love of Pete, there is too much junk sometimes.


Anyway, I just tossed some ideas out there. Hopefully between the customer base and FunCom we can come to some sort of solution.
