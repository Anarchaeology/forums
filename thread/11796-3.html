Well I am an agent. And I don't want access to the black market. I am happy for the fixer class, they're finally getting a nice little bonus to help tide things over till they get fixed properly. Both of our classes IMHO are a bit too similar...too similar at times which often causes conflicts between our two classes. At times I think that both the agent and the fixer might be better off if the two classes were combined into just one class. But because of this both of our classes suffer an identity crisis. The idea of what makes an agent an agent or a fixer a fixer is being redesigned by funcom...at the cost of our ip. So I am truly happy for the fixer class for the addition of a fixer only shop...you'll finally be getting some much needed class defining love.

Right now it seems Funcom is refocusing the fixer profession into a more tradeskill oriented class like a trader but without the heavy reliance on nanoskills. This is evident in the recent need for extremely high levels of breaking and entering skill in order to modify weapons. I suppose in Funcom's mind a Fixer is more and more becoming a "fixer" of weapons. This approach might be good since it would definitely give your profession an ability that an agent for example couldn't touch...and that is important in seperating your class defining abilities from other classes(more specifically agents). But they still have to worry about determining a way for you to be able to contribute to a team while hunting. Making your HOTs better with lower nanoskill requirements would be a good start...the high nanopool cost already prevents most agents from using them before you can as is.

Both of our professions have been needing some love for a while. I'm actually looking forward to the overequiping fix patch because with that comes the toning down of mobs. Mob health doesn't need to be changed as much as mob AC and mob initiatives which currently are set at ungodly. With less mob AC fast firing SMGs and rifles might actually become useful again. But who knows what will happen I'll believe it when I see it.
