I heard an explanation some time ago that seemed quite accurate.

The team level will produce three effects:

1) It will raise your innate crit chance by 1% per team level

2) It will raise your AMS by 10x team level (more AMS = more damage and better chance to hit)

3) It will raise your DMS by 10x team level (more DMS = more chance to dodge/evade/duck an attack)

This seems somewhat consistant, at high team levels things just suddenly seem to go better, and crits become more common.

Don't ask me for hard evidence to back it up tho. Hard to know anything "for sure" about hidden variables...

From On Team Play - Part Two by Trond Ivar Hansen (aka Jim Salabim)
quote:

The "Team Level"

When you enter a team, take notice that there is a field in the "Team" panel called "Team Level." It always starts at 1, and has the possibility of reaching 5. The longer the team sticks together, the higher this number gets. Some of the areas where you receive bonuses with higher Team Level are:

  • Chance of landing a successful hit with a nano-program;
  • Bonuses to AMS/DMS (chance of hitting with weapons and chance to avoid hits); and
  • Chance of successfully picking a door or disarming a bomb.
Check also On Team Play - Part One

And Primer: Team Mission Combat Tactics by Brad, STL/MO
