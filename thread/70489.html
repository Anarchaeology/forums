What buildings/structures would you like to have to improve RP?
Hello all,

today I had a visit from a Dev in AO asking me about what buildings or structures we as roleplayers would want/need to improve the roleplaying in AO.

Since i had just recently read about a/war command, i instantly brought that up and he said he would have to ask the coders and that it would be difficult to change the PvP rules of AO, but we will see what happens there.

Now.. i also said, that we would need something to have more realistic law enforcement to at least be able to punish criminals in some way. A prison-like building with an interrogation room and a jail cell, where the interrogation room has no suppression gas, would be good i suppose. Just hope that something like this would not be abused by the non-RPing players to have some PvP (seeing how nanotechnology would not work in this building, it would make a good hideout to ambush people, which is not wanted).

I also asked for a large assembly hall for Omni-Tek, such as the CoT building. This hall should be open for people to have political debates, organizations like OTSEC or other political bodies of Omni-Tek, to assemble and have civilized discussions ingame about roleplaying topics or to have a place for the roleplaying conventions and such.

Since that is all i came up with for now, i need ideas from you to bring to the world designers for consideration. If you have an idea (a serious one!) what we need in AO for the roleplayers to improve roleplaying (structures and buildings, no special game mechanics or something too exotic!) - this can be a dungeon, a special building, whatever you think is needed. Post your ideas here and I will see that it gets forwarded to the right people once a nice list of ideas has been posted.

Thank you and let the posts come in!

PS: I forgot to add that this call is for all RPers in AO: Omni, Neutral and Clan!

Some additional ideas
**OOC post to discuss OOC about RP**

Very good ideas!

Here's a couple from me for fun:

Some office space. Omni-Tek, Clan, Neutral. Think of the office-like rooms that are in Borealis (the ones that often require jumping through walls to get to), only not. Bureucrats (and others) need offices, and using an apartment as an office just doesn't have the same flair.

For me, I'd *love* it if the 'official' buildings in Omni-1 and Rome (-admin, -med, etc.) had these kinds of interiors. Communal would probably be okay as a starter - think of it as a fill-in before we get proper guild housing.

And preferably not requiring walking through walls or taking odd routes through town.

Along that vein:
Secret laboratories with secret-lab-type interiors and NO hostiles. Some in or near cities that are 100% or 75%, some furthur out that are 25% and in very isolated positions (great for those guild v. guild RP events), such as out in the boonies in Stret Valley.

I'd go as far as to put the interrogation/jail things in a similar boat. Some in towns with non-pvp suppression, some out with 25% or even 0%, but enough of them and in isolated enough spots that the PvP crowd won't want to trek out there on the off-chance that someone's in it at the time.

And (because no forum is complete without *some* disagreement) I'll cast my vote for not having a lower supression in interrogation rooms. Interrogations can be carried out quite effectively without it, and terminal interrogations can be handled by a well-placed /terminate.

As an aside, one thing that would be nice would be being able to fire weapons or execute nanos at people in areas of high supression, where doing so has no discernable effect on the target aside from the visuals. This would be a great aide to RP without having to worry about PvP.

Oh, and hello again everyone! It's been a while!
