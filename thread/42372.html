Brad, STL/MO
Phear Leet
Real Estate Advice
I can't tell from your notice just exactly what you're looking for, but let me bounce a couple of suggestions off of you, give you the plusses a minuses.

Tir 5: Where the crowds are. Tir City Apartments #5, located conveniently on Tir Gridpoint Plaza, is where nearly everybody above level 50 hangs out, and where most of them live. Its Courtyard is the easiest place to find doctors to help you update your implants. Its courtyard and entertainment park are the easiest places to find traders for wrangles. It's on the same plaza with George's Armory (Advanced Quality Armorer) and the Happy Rebel. As you can tell from the name of the plaza, it's the closest apartment building in the game to a grid terminal. It practically has its own mission terminals. It's a short walk to Hi-Tech (Special/Advanced Supermarket) and just around the corner from Fair Trade (Ordinary/Basic Supermarket, Bank, Tir whompas). Of course, the downside to all of this is the same as the upside, it's very crowded.

West Athen: I agree with you, West Athen is just about the prettiest city on the planet. It also has the only coffeehouse on the planet. The shopping is convenient, if not as extensive as the "black iron prison" across the Stret. (In particular, you really feel the lack of Special Supermarkets.) On the other hand, spending any amount of time in West Athen or Old Athen means putting up with the worst weather on the planet. (To me, it's worth it. Even though I work in Tir, I maintain an apartment in West Athen.)

Tir #1 & West Athen #3: Both get steady traffic, because these are the apartments where new colonists are being dropped off at the moment, with West Athen #3 apparently getting the lion's share of them. West Athen #3 also has the advantage of being on main street, close to the city gate, and if you're a low level character, the further advantage of being right next door to the Ordinary/Basic Supermarket.

Tir WestGate Apartments: I forget the building number, but there's an apartment building facing the west gate of Tir City. This gives you your choice of two mission terminals (one next door, and an even better one just outside the gate). It's also very conveniently placed to the Hardware Shop (Ordinary/Advanced Supermarket), and even closer to Fair Trade and the whompas than Tir Gridpoint Apartments.

Old Athen - no: Old Athen combines the ugly weather of the Athen area with the "black iron prison" architecture of Omni-1. No social clubs. No grid point. I can't recommend it. The Old Athen Mall is a great place to shop, but I would never want to live there.
