Fix Nano Refresh Sync Bug
This is not a new bug, but it is a lot more apparant while hunting the outdoor bosses. The animations of the huge melee bosses cause a sync bug that does not allow nanos to refresh when they are attacking you. It does not happen if the mob is far away(root and nuke works) but does occur even if the view is changed. It does not seem to happen with humanoid bosses. If youre ever been hit by one of the Trash King's many lackeys you wouldve noticed the sync bug on casting nanos because the mobs with area nukes cause this also.

Being a nano reliant profession, this bug is most noticable with us for soloing the outdoor bosses. This is one of the most frustrating and annoying bugs in the game. Please make bosses small until they fix this.

At first i thought this was the blackhole bug everyone was talking about but the blackhole bug is soemthing else entirely and is rare. This bug happens 100% of the time u get hit by one of these big monster bosses no matter what nano casting profession you are.

Has Funcom even acknowledged this bug and will it ever be fixed?
