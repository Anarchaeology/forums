Sizes
As a member of the medical profession, it tends to be nice for the people who rely on me for survival to be able to see me. It isn't often that I need to sneak around.

And now, not only does Enlarge not enlarge you, it makes you TINY, so NO ONE can see you AT ALL.

You guys are really crimping style points here. I look like every MA on the planet.
