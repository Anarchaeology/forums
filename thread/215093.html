Breaking News - Former NLC Secretary Speaks out on "Council of Fear"

In a report broadcast moments ago from the Independent Reporters of Rubi-Ka, more light was shed on the plot revolving around the Newland City Council (NLC) and hired protector Lauren Warr. The full text of the report follows:

"This is a Council of Fear!" exclaimed Amahnda Dwemse, a former secretary of the Newland City Council. "Let's be real here, the instant we invited these thugs into our city, we lost control. We're living at the mercy of hired killers who care nothing for us, just our credits!"

Ms. Dwemse was a senior secretary for one of the Councilmen. She became agitated with the Council a number of months ago when the current guards moved in. "They're hoodlums, all of them." She said to our John Zorg recently when he interviewed her. "From the moment they arrived they've done nothing but harass and intimidate us. Is that protection? Being held at gunpoint in your own home town?"

(part of the interview)

Zorg: So how is the Council handling the Replacement Acts consideration?

Dwemse: They might be compelled to change, but the reality is they're afraid of Warr and her thugs. They're afraid that if they dismiss them, we'd be a city held hostage.

Zorg: So the Council is not willing to ratify the proposition?

Dwemse: I think they'd like to. But you never know. Warr is a side effect of something large, people like her are never the root of evil, just it's manifestation. Whatever the root, I'm sure that scares the Council far more.

Zorg: Has the Council asked the ICC for assistance in this issue, military, or investigative?

Dwemse: The Council has little faith in the ICC, that's why they were replaced to start with. I don't know what they're going to do. But I'm fed up with living in fear.

After the interview was broadcast, the Council dismissed Ms. Dwemse from their service. The IRRK will continue to follow this situation.

Naturally, Omni-InternOps applauds the actions by the Newland City Council in this matter, specifically its choice to terminate an evidently rogue employee with such a tremendously skewed vision of the current situation in Newland City. Furthermore, the breach of applicable policies regarding confidentiality of Newland City Council proceedings (which is evident by the manner in which it conducts itself) is reprehensible and Omni-InternOps wishes to extend its public support for legal action to be taken against Ms. Dwemse in this regard.
