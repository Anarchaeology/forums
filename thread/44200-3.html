The answer to this is simple: nerf camping!

How to nerf camping (being the expert nerfers you are FunCom, you shouldn't even need to read this):

All regular mobs (i.e. leets, claws, etc..) should be limited to a playfield (not just an area of that playfield). All unqiue/uber mobs should simply be able to spawn anywhere.

As for the existing rare items. Update existing items to make them comparable to the current load of unique items in the game. Tradeskill made items should be better than dropped ones anyway...
