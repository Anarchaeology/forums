That is correct, Whites.

Unfortunately, what allows me to do what I do is the Crat N-Ball, which doesn't drop anymore it seems.

http://auno.org/ao/db.php?cmd=view&id=152266&t=ag

Though, getting back into a q180-ish V2 is doable without the N-ball and is still a very good weapon.

The whole process used to be a little bothersome. At 193 I used a total of 5 buffing pistols and neleb's robe.

Here they are:

MTI SW500 Geyser
http://auno.org/ao/db.php?cmd=view&id=123818&t=ag

When I'm not wearing my Bright Pistol implant, I use this to get back into the V3. The N-ball alone doesn't give me enough pistol bonus. An excellent weapon skill buffer weapon, it also allows me to squeeze into weapons with higher mult range reqs.

BigBurger Inc. Chapman
http://auno.org/ao/db.php?cmd=view&id=123883&t=ag

This is store bought and you will need it to buff into the Khan pistol at level 192 or so.

HSR Arms N.M.
http://auno.org/ao/db.php?cmd=view&id=123890&t=ag

Store bought also. Newland's superior store has them frequently. Ranged energy req is easily reached by maxing ability stats, wearing a faded energy weapon knowledge implant and using LR expertise, and maybe a tiny bit of IP. Gives +25 to Stamina. I had to use these in my off-hand with a BigBurger in the main hand in order to equip a Galahad Khan. The Stamina boost will give you those last few points of Full Auto. I use 2 of these and the Shades of Luc to hotswap my Neleb's Robe with Psy/Stam armor cloaks (Biomech, Lokas Web, Marcus Divaad). They allow me to not wear Stamina implants in favor of evasion implants.

And of course I use the Khan and the Druid which everyone already knows about.

At 193 the whole process of self casting ninja droid and heroic measures went something like this.

Equip BigBurger. Equip HSR Arms in Offhand. Hotswap my Psy/Stam cloak with Neleb's Robe. Buff up with expertise and boosts. Hotswap Galahad Khan into main hand. Manually take off HSR Arms from offhand. Manually place Druid Pistol in offhand. Cast Droid/bot whatever. Manually take off Druid pistol. Manually equip HSR Arms back into offhand. Hotswap 2nd HSR arms into Main hand. Hotswap Neleb's Robe for a Psy/Stam cloak. Take off the offhand HSR Arms. Hotswap a Geyser into the mainhand. Hotswap a V3 into main hand.

Was lots of bother...only saving grace to it all was that the Bot and Aura both only need to be cast infrequently.

At level 198 the process is a bit simpler. Equip BigBurger, then equip Khan. Then equip Geyser, then V3. Not having to hotswap Neleb's Robe with an armor cloak saves lots of trouble.
