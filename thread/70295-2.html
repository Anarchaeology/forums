My biggest problem with "let's make it harder" is how thin the gray line is between easy and dead.

Ex:
Team of 5 is mowing through a QL 180 mission. 1 MOB vs 5 well equipped PCs = dead MOB with little to no worry (team has RRFE, MA for small heals, and HOTs from the Fixer)...

2 rooms later the team gets an add! now the offense is split and the healer is aggroed and working his target. It isn't a huge problem, the solider might of had to TMS to keep from getting killed while waiting for heals. Everyone is fine and just needed a quick Treatment Kit use and some nano.

2 rooms later we get a bad pull and get 4 adds! MA drops in 8 seconds FLAT from a slayer droid. Soldier throws up TMS, Fixer does a root-all and runs for the first room, Agent is dead, soldier runs for front door but will not make it...wtf went wrong?

For me, the line between what 1 add can do compared to 2 or 3 is HUGE. Every team doesn't have a DOC and having a DOC doesn't make it simple either. The DOC is almost always #2 on the aggro list.

Teams can go from "mopping the floor" to "rez terminal" in 10 seconds flat with just 1 add.

My question: Why is the team dynamic so weak? Even the "perfect team" has a limit as well. You can only Calm so many, root so many, tank 1 or 2, etc. Granted that having a calmer in the team makes a TON of difference.

So I ask again, why are so many "mixed non-perfect" teams so weak when it comes to the logarithmic impact adds have to the chances a team has to survive a fight? Also, why do adds that don't add to the TANK cripple a team so often? "3 adds on the DOC!"..."Don't worry, I died 5 seconds before you typed that!"

Remember when a team of 3 still had to deal with 3 MOBs plus the boss in the last room? They changed it so there would only be 1 or 2 now. Why? So the team had a chance to win.

I'm all for make things "more fun" but I think it starts with better AI, MOB variation, and having to alter tactics to counter an attack. (ex: What if see a MOB winding up Last Word was the "trigger" to going FULL Defense so you would most likely resist it? The MOB is fiddling with his weapon..he's going to burst! Stun him!..call me silly but these would make combat a little more fun).
