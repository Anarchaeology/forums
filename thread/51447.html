Fixers and PvP, the Final Truth
I have never in my life been witness to a greater misconception than that which 90% of the AO population holds about fixers. Fixers are not in any way, shape, or form, uber in PvP. It is without a doubt, the most pathetically unfounded pile of horsecrap I have ever heard. In order to set the record straight and attempt to educate some of you knee-jerk ignorants out there I am posting the following information. This is not opinion, this is fact.

Let me first state a few precepts, or fundamental scenario that will help define my PvP evaluations.

1. Gridding away from a fight is not winning. It does not make anyone "uber" and quite simply, sucks if that is all a Fixer can do. If gridding away is considered being "good" at PvP, so are all the whompa and bunker huggers in 2h0.

2. All PvP scenarios are taken against professions of equal level to that of the Fixer in question.

3. All PvP scenarios are taken to be between opponents over level 120 or more. Results may also hold true for those below 120, but as I am not that low, I cannot say withou absolute authority.

Ok, now that we've set these "rules", let's continue.


There are 3 categories of opponents when fighting Fixers in PvP.

Professions that will always beat a fixer:
Trader
NT
Agent (in FP Trader)
Doctor
MP

Professions that have approximately equal chance:
Enforcer
MA

Professions that cannot beat a Fixer:
Soldier
Engineer

I do not know where to place 'Crats, as I've never fought one in higher levels. I have a feeling they'd go in 50%, but I'm not certain.


Now to support my positioning of these professions. In the first category we start with Traders. I am sure this needs almost no explanation since Traders are the most unbalanced PvP class around. However, I will provide reasons anyway. Traders not only destroy the ATK rating and nano capabilities of the Fixer thus crippling him (yes, they do this to every class they fight), but they also destroy any protection afforded the Fixer using Grid Armor. At higher levels, being able to reduce nano skills by 400 points is a ridiculously easy way to kill a fixer in GA. An contrary to the whiney damn Traders, they have no problem landing drains even on a max NR fixer of the same level. All they need to do is land one.

With NTs, since they don't debuff, its all about who does more damage first. Fixers do not do much offensive damage. They never have. Even with a CHS equipped, I've yet to see an NT that could be killed by a Fling, Burst, FA by a fixer. That being said, all the NT needs to do is sit back and drop 100% resist nukes on the Fixer who will not evade those unless fighting at 100% defensive. Everyone who knows anything will tell you that fighting at 100% defensive is not an option for Fixers. Healing stims would overpower our damage at that rate.

Agents are traders when it comes to PvP with a Fixer unless they get caught unaware, in which case it is simple for anyone to run away.

Doctors. Its been said that while Fixers cannot kill docs, a doc cannot kill a fixer. That, I am here to tell you is a load of crap. If a doc lands Both DoTs on a fixer of the same level, the Fixer's hoTs will NOT keep up with that damage and the weapon damage of the Doctor as well. Then, thare those wonderful Doc nanos, Rapid Palsy and Epsilon Purge. A fixer can not kill a doctor of equal level.

MPs. Soon as the MP gets Nano Shutdown, its a no contest. And again, contrary to those MPs that whine that its easy to resist, no it is not. Even at 100% Defense (and like we said, you can't fight at 100% Def) a fixer will only evade that debuff SOMETIMES. A Fixer cannot beat an MP of equal level at the higher end of the spectrum.


So, that takes care of the first category, lets review the next. Enforcers at higher levels are more than evenly matched for a Fixer. In the arena they have better than a 50% chance of winning. In the open, they are equal. While running rage, the enforcer is just as fast as the Fixer. Enforcers are damn near impossible to root if they've paid any attention to NR at all and even then, rage will break it or a simple general debuff. If the fixer is at anything other than 100% defense, an enforcer will hit right through GA, especially those using those alloy sticks. If the fixer is lucky enough to keep the Enforcer rooted, and doesn't run out of nano doing so, the fixer will win. If not, the Enforcer will win.

Martial Artists are like Enforcers in that, if fighting in the arena, their chances are slightly better. MAs, like enforcers, will stay rooted for all of 3 seconds at a time given the crap with general debuffs. Also, with the popularity of MAs using crossbows and Rivers with their ridiculous crit buffs, roots don't matter anyhow. When the MA DOES get up close, their insane damage will go right through GA. Add to this their pretty decent healing and you have a class that is definitely able to kill a Fixer. Its much harder for them in the open, but I've been killed multiple times by MAs of equal level.


So, there you have it. FIVE of the 11 classes in AO are unbeatable for a fixer at higher levels. TWO are equal. Soldiers and Engineers? What can I say here. A fixer should really never lose to a Soldier or Engineer in PvP. But the funny thing is, how many times do you see a Soldier stand their ground against a Fixer without help? Hmm....never?


Now that we've discussed specific profession match ups, lets again talk a little bit about the reality of PvP and situations in AO. 1 on 1 PvP takes place in two places for the most part. The Arena and MMD. You cannot say 1 on 1 takes place in 2h0 because if someone is losing, they for the most part always run away and zone. I have met very few people that are not cowards and will stand their ground in 1 on 1 if they're losing in 2h0

So, in the Arena, we have 1 on 1. But guess what? You generally get to choose who you fight there (pansy ass gankers not withstanding). Where does that leave the Fixer? Standing there like an idiot. The only profs that will ever duel a fixer are the 5 that know they'll always win and sometimes an Enforcer or an MA, both of which should have a slightly better than 50% chance of winning. Soldiers and Engineers will never duel a Fixer. So, we get to fight 2 profs at the arena....sometimes. Most of the time players of Enforcers or MAs are either too cowardly or too ignorant of the fact that they can indeed kill a fixer and they won't duel either.

MMD then eh? This is the only place that a Fixer might get a fair one on one chance to kill someone (because again, 2h0 is full of zoners). But remember, Fixers again can kill 4 profs (2 fairly, 2 unfairly). Yes, I said unfairly. It is no more fair that Fixers can kill soldiers or engineers with impunity than it is for Traders and the rest to kill Fixers the same way. FIXERS SHOULD NEVER BE ABLE TO HAVE A 100% CHANCE TO KILL ANY PROFESSION, JUST LIKE NO PROFESSION SHOULD HAVE A 100% CHANCE TO KILL A FIXER.

One thing I'm sure I'll hear is "look at all the fixers with titles" Ok, do that. First look at the higher level fixers, then look how high those titles are. Most are rookie or apprentice at most. Then ask that fixer when the last time they increased their title was. Fixers got most of their titles right at the 14.4 change when no one knew how to deal with them and people stopped refusing duels and starting running from us. I have never gotten a single point towards PvP title in 2 months and as a matter of fact, I've lost my apprentice title since then from standing my ground against the only profs that will fight me.


So, there you have it people. Pull your heads out and learn the truth about how "uber" fixers are. They are NOT. Unless you count being and "uber escape artist", which sucks.


Funcom, get off your damn ass and balance PvP. Its the only goddamn thing to do in this lousy game at higher levels anyhow. If you don't deliver there, you're gonna have more and more folks quit. Like I am.


Bye
