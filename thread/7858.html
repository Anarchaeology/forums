Restriction of Skills
The point of this post is about have skills or items that are restricted to certain classes instead of the free for all we have now. At present I am free to pick and choose what skills and items I can use. While is freedom is nice and offers everyone one the same chance to develop there chars it has lead to every class becoming the same.

People tend to choose only the skills which provide the most profit as for example so many classes and using the monster parts to blood skills to create money. But from a roleplaying point would it not be better to restrict this just to doctors whom you would understand have to under go some training to perform skills. Also true of other classes and skills such as gem making and weapon making.

The idea behind this is create classes which feel they have a real purpose in the game. It would be nice to have to find a engineer to create a weapon for you from the parts you find. Or to find a Doc to convert them monster parts. It would help to foster a bigger sense of community and enter a trading part of the game.

With this I would like to see an increase in the requirements to use our skills. I dont feel they have much purpose right now, most people dont have a real reason to create weps or gems. Doing so requires an investment in time and often more cost than buying them from the shops.

So this end we should be able to create unique items, or at least items which are better than we can buy in the shops. It would also be nice to be able to custom build items to make us look unique. I am rather sick or seeing everyone running around in the black nano or omni pol armour. We could for example be able to create our own armour which we could select a colour for. This I feel would help to give people and guilds some much needed indenty in the game.

I dont like to speak for everyone else but from the posts I have seen I get the feeling we need a break from the missions or hunting in groups. Being able to use our skills, to be forced on mini adventures or quests to do so would be a welcome break for many of us.
