Assembly of theoretical research

Let me introduce myself Im Rax Devenport,
I usually spend my time in my observatory on the Morning Star,
my grand passion in life is the existential questions of life, religion
and persona.

Due to recent discoveries of shadowlands, my field of work has gratefully thousand folded.
An associate hired the mercenaries of Black Sun for an infiltration job, and came over
some unexpected documents and logs from something called The AOTR - Assembly of theoretical research. As u might imagine I was stunned and amazed that such a assembly existed.
Intrigued to see evidence of the extent they must been using to keep it a secret, as the documents predate my arrival here on Rubi-Ka.

From the logs I can gather that not even the participants of some meetings knew each other,
as all seems to be ranked with member numbers besides the referee and chairmen.

I was overwhelmed to see that more are investigating the issues of essence of the shadowlands. As it seems there are 3 posible theorys now in discussion on AOTR.

1) future, past and present
As they gather Ergo is manmade and refer us as there masters, they seem
to form a theory around the fabric of time, that shadowlands is acctualy
the contented future of Rubi-ka, and its source is giving us a warning !
A warning to change our way of life and leave Rubi-ka.

2) the paradox of the mind
that shadowlands is a extended effect of the notum field itself and
enhances the current state of mind from Rubi-ka, as there is war
and destruction then as so to in shadowlands. I unexpected side effect.

3) the loop
A theory that seem to be based on a mix of physics/chemical and mathematical
calculations, that Rubi-Ka and we are non existent and trapped in a loop spiraling downward
to destruction. A major experiment of gigantically proportions gone wrong,
that they desperately trying to safe.
