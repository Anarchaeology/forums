It's a *very* low level quest item, used to complete the missions that Tirbo Ratcatcher gives out. He's outside the gates of West Athen and only gives out missions to extremely low level characters. My level 14 Enforcer could get the missions, my level 21 Nt couldn't. In order, the missions give:
  • Cyber armour helmet
  • Another piece of Cyber, gloves IIRC
  • Full set of Metaplast
  • Flak sleeves
  • Flak boots

After that you get the same response he gives to any higher level characters who try to speak to him - Tirbo actually shouts abuse at you in vicinity...

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EDIT: There's some variation in the mission rewards, it seems. The full metaplast set still drops, but might take four tries instead of three. The other armour pices are random, by the looks of things. Still a very good way to start off your character's life though.
