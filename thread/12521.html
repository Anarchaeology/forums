Attn: COSMIK (Fixer idea you might find interesting)
Here is a way that that the fixer could be “Fixed”, a cool ability added, while keeping in the fixer character. Introduce several "Fixer Kits" that operate on ranged weapons.
This will help to “Fix the fixer” while not limiting the fixers to pure MG/SMG weapons like the current weapon upgrades will. These can be sold in the new black markets, of varying quality levels. The quality of the kit should be equal or greater to that of the quality of the ranged weapon it is used on. “Illegal” weapons should cause the mobs on the “City Guard” AI to agro on the player, (Clan, Omni and Neutral) if the character has the weapon equipped while in range of the guard.


1. "Lighten Trigger" (Q 1-200)

Base Price: 1500/Quality Level

Description “This kit contains custom tools and worker nanobots programmed to increase the sensitivity and optomize the feedback/response of the triggering mechanism to that of the user. Such hair trigger weapons are considered too dangerous and unstable and are banned in the civilian population.


Skills Required:
Breaking and Entering 4 points /Quality
Weapon Smithing: 3 Points/Quality

Effects:
Flag weapon as “NODROP” and “ILLEGAL”
Decrease the Delay of the weapon by 1% (Q1) - 20%(Q200)
Add the letters “LT” to the end of the name of the weapon.
Add the following to the description “This weapon has had its trigger customized.”


2. “Widen Ejector Ports”

Base Price: 1500/Quality Level

Description: “This kit contains custom tools and worker nanobots programmed to modify the ejector ports on a ranged weapon. This will increase the cycling speed and reduce weapon jams, however this increased cycle speed is gained at the cost of some loss of compression, which can lower muzzle velocities.

Skills Required:
Breaking and Entering 4 points /Quality
Mechanical Engineering: 3 Points/Quality

Effects:
Flag weapon as “NODROP”
Raise the quality of the weapon by 1(Q1) – 4(Q200)
Decrease the Recharge of the weapon by 1% (Q1) - 10%(Q200)
Decrease the Range of the weapon by 1%(Q1) to 20%(Q200)

Add the letters “EP” to the end of the name of the weapon.
Add the following to the description “The ejector ports of this weapon have been widened for faster cycling times.”


3. “Re-Chambered Wildcat”

Base Price: 2000/Quality Level

Description: “This kit contains custom tools and worker nanobots programmed to modify the chamber of a ranged weapon. This will allow the weapon to use larger “Wildcat” rounds which produce more total energy output. However they take up significantly more room in the magazine. The wildcat rounds however are banned and considered highly illegal.


Skills Required:
Breaking and Entering 4 points /Quality
Mechanical Engineering: 2 Points/Quality
Chemical Engineering: 2 Points/Quality
Weapon Smithing: 2 Points/Quality

Effects:
Flag weapon as “NODROP” and “ILLEGAL”
Increase the critical damage of the weapon by 1% (Q1) - 15%(Q200)
Increase the maximum damage of the weapon by 1%(q1) to 20% (Q200)
Decrease the Ammo Capacity 5%(Q1) to 30%(Q200)

Add the letters “WC” to the end of the name of the weapon.
Add the following to the description “This weapon has been re-chambered to fire “Wildcat” rounds.”


4. “Muzzel Break”

Base Price: 2000/Quality Level

Description: “This kit contains custom tools and worker nanobots programmed to modify the barrel of a ranged weapon, adding muzzle break to offset the force of the kickback of the weapon. This allows for much greater accuracy in sustained firing situations, and reduces the time it takes to re-acquire a target. Unfortunately it accomplishes this by diverting some of the energy from the round, reducing its penetration slightly.

Skills Required:
Breaking and Entering 4 points /Quality
Weapon Smithing: 4 Points/Quality
Mechanical Engineering: 3 Points/Quality

Effects:
Flag weapon as “NODROP”
Increase the minimum damage of the weapon by 1% (Q1) - 20%(Q200)
Decrease the recharge by 1%(Q1) to 10% (Q200)
Decrease the Maximum damage by 1%(Q1) to 5% (Q200)

Add the letters “MB” to the end of the name of the weapon.
Add the following to the description “This weapon has a custom muzzle break to reduce recoil drift.”





5. “Cyborg Bio-graft”

Base Price: 3000/Quality Level

Description: “This kit contains custom tools and worker nanobots programmed to modify a weapon to use a direct neural interface with its user. This enables the user to also control and utilize nano-clusters implanted in the weapon. Weapons with this modification will require surgical supervision to equip, and once equipped are extremely difficult to remove without permanently damaging the weapon. This is a modified cyborg interface for the weapon and as such is banned in almost all systems.

Skills Required:
Breaking and Entering 4.5 points /Quality
Weapon Smithing: 4 Points/Quality
Nano Programming: 3 Points/Quality
Pharma-Tech 1.5 Points/Quality

Effects:
Flag weapon as “ILLEGAL”
(Possably NODROP, but would be a cool skill to have for fixers if it was not )
Add “Requires Surgery supervision to equip” flag
Add Ability requirement based on the clusters used to equip.
Add treatment requirement (same as the implants would be) to Equip.
Enable one shining, one bright and one faded nano cluster to be placed in weapon
(Same quality restrictions as for implants, Requires same NP skill as for an implant)
Add Required level to remove, as Quality of Weapon minus 10

Add the letters “BG” to the end of the name of the weapon.
Add the following to the description “This weapon has been equipped with a cybernetic interface.”



Analysis: This would give the fixers reason to use those green trade skills, that gives a significant in game advantage. At quality 200 the effects would be

Delay -20%
Recharge –20%
Range –20%
Critical Damage +15%
Minimum Damage +20%
Maximum Damage +15%
Ammo Capacity -30%

Also allowing 3 nano implants but the weapon becomes nodrop, cant be unequipped till level 190 and is flagged as illegal (with the no –unequip becomes a problem.)

This would be a significant increase in total firepower, it would vary with wich weapon was converted, but would probably be about 35-40% increase in total damage output. but would also take a lot of IP to make, requiring the following

Break and Enter: 900
Weapon Smithing: 800
Mechanical Engineering: 600
Nano Programming : 600
Chemical Engineering: 400
Pharma Tech: 300

The cost would be around 2Mil for a q200 fully customized ranged weapon, not including the nano cluster cost, or the initial cost of the weapon.

The question becomes, does that put fixers above soldiers in damage output. As you have stated that "Fixers should not be able to out damage soldiers"

Sounds reasonable right? Soldiers = Good tank with heavy weapons.

However when you REALY think about it... A soldier is able to stand and take damage for MUCH longer than a fixer.

That means against the same mob, a fixer has less time in which to kill the mob, before the fixer dies. So either the fixer has to do MORE damage than the soldier, or the maximum mob the fixer can take is far less than that of the soldier.


THIS is the fundamental problem. Other classes can take damage better, AND do more damage. That is the fundamental reason a fixer is broken. Until that changes fixers will not be "fixed".

What does that mean for a group situation. In a group situation, where the group has a healer,(doctor), crowd control, and a good tank, enforcer, they are looking for one more to add. THIS should be the place where they want a fixer, hey lets get the high damage. This is the place where they grab the soldier instead, because the damage output is better, AND they can tank if they have to.


The role of the fixer should be to be able to put out the fast damage with our mg/smg. We aren't setup to take the damage, that means we either have to deal it BETTER than those that can take it, or we are useless.

This will make the aggro enhancers for soldiers/enforcers actually something that they NEED/WANT to use. Right now there is no need of them because other than keeping the mobs off the doctors, NO one is going to out aggro them because no one out damages them (Well except maybe a careless/over nuking NT)

This suggestion, I believe would enhance the fixer to the point of being almost on par with soldier damage output, although not quite. With the additions of a few new weapons this might be enough to push up the fixer to where it needs to be. Again at a huge IP cost, but probably well worth while.
