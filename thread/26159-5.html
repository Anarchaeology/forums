Heh, it seems like nobody likes their own class... I've only been playing for about a week an a half, and I love my melee adventurer... I'm only level 40 right now, but I'm having a lot of fun playing him. My only real problem is IP distribution... it seems like I never have enough IP. I've gotta put points into 1h edged, mult melee, brawl, and fast attack... then there's dimach, which I sink some IP into just to meet some wield requirements. Then of course, the melee initiative, evades, treatment, first aid. Can't forget nanos! Biomet and mattmet for heals, mattcreation for damage shields, sensory improvement for morphs...

Oh man, my NCU is low! comp lit, comp lit, comp lit. I managed to get my comp lit high enough to wear a 3 slot belt and got buffed so I could put in 3 NCU chips totaling 56 ncu as a whole... which is more space than I need for now, but it's definitely nice.

Aside from all of the IP distribution though, I have a lot of fun playing an adventurer.

I, on the other hand, can't stand playing my agent. Slow firing, slow reloading... Aimshotting is hella hard, because it ALWAYS seems like the mob has noticed you. I usually just get impatient and attack them normally, with my incredibly slow rifle (I max ranged init every level by the way).


While I'm at it, I'll talk about my MA... I got my MA from level 1 to 29 in about five hours, no joke. This was after I made my agent and got extremely impatient... I decided to make a melee class, and what better than martial artist? I mean, I watch martial arts movies. Scratch that, I *worship* martial arts movies. I really enjoyed my MA at first, while I was mowing down mobs left and right in the newbie area... I was setting mission difficulty all the way to the right, and just breezing through them. It eventually got harder, and I had to move the slider left more and more as I progressed, but it was still pretty easy. I just wasn't really satisfied with the class though. My MA was excellent in combat, and could heal and deal damage and all of that stuff, but there wasn't really a lot of extra stuff, just combat/healing stuff.

So, I made my adventurer, and haven't gone back to any of my other chars. I haven't even logged on my MA since I created my adventurer.

The order in which I made my characters were: Agent was my first, then MA, then Adventurer. I can definitely say that agent isn't really a newbie friendly class. On the other hand, MA is a very very newbie friendly class, at least starting out.

Before all of you level 200's jump on me and tell me I don't know what I'm talking about, please be aware of this: I am fully aware that I don't know what I'm talking about. Like I said at the beginning of this post, I've only been playing for about a week and a half, and this is just MY impression of the game in the time that I've played it. I am fully aware that a class doesn't fully develop until higher levels, but first impressions are what really matters. These are just a few opinions, from a real newbie's point of view.
