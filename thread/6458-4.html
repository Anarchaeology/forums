My philosohy is this:

As long as it is in the game mechanics to do so, overequipping is the natural and expected course of action for any power conscious player.

Duh of course. So anyway, I've got my MA and he sucks rocks even though it's my favorite class. In the meantime, I've levelled up a FIXER who kicks the holy crap out of everyone. Yes that's right a fixer. Now, before I draw flames from the rightfully angry fixers out there, this character is TOTALLY BS from square one. He has insane AC, a ridiculous vector (yeah right, mausser? pfffft!) and is naturally very fast. He's, in a word, obnoxious. I get great fun out of killing all those classes that once dominated my MA and then when I whined said things like "dude you just don't know how to play." Well, I have the resources, I have the game knowledge, and I have the skill to kill these people now. Now everytime I drop someone new(well 1 in 3) the first thing out of their mouth is "sploiter!" Well, I don't exploit, but a newb or many classes even twinked just don't have a chance. I explain to them calmly that they can get a GM anytime and send them my way, and usually they're cool after I drop them a few pointers.

I won't care if this nerf goes through.

I won't care if it doesn't.

There will always be imbalances of power. There will always be people who maximize their power under any ruleset. These are mmorpg facts.

What bothers me is when they "adjust" something that one has invested IP into without compensation. THAT is time they're costing me. Time is golden. I cherish it way more than money. THAT will kill my subscription. For example, if suddenly my MA with maxed MA skill and about 450 2HE is given the choice between sub-par 2HE weapons which would REQUIRE me to spend IP in 2HE to get my 2HE up to snuff with my level, or to keep my now useless 450 skill in 2HE while using fists alone. That would do it for me. I wouldnt mind these changes IF they unlocked said wasted skill and allowed points only in those areas to be adjusted back into the unused pool. Not all skills, just those effected. This would also allow NT's and other classes with multi-weild from pillows to get those points back, retroactively.
