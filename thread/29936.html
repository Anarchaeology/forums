I don't know what you're talking about. Thin Bernice is an NPC just standing there in Sabulum. If you can get to Sabulum without getting killed (no easy task w/o a yalm or the fixer grid) then you can safely talk to Thin Bernice.

Right click on her to bring up a trade window and buy the armor you want. I found the prices to be very reasonable. Far less than $2 million for a Battle Suit.

Keep in mind that some desert monsters walk around Sabulum so watch out. They were red to me (I'm L70). Thank God I have a yalm.
