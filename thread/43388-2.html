First off... every 12 points in intelligence is three or four points in the nano skill. Apparently someone forgot how to multiply percentages. Here's how it works. Intelligence is 80% of every nano skill, seconday ability is the remaining 20%..

80%+20%=100%=1 point in the nano skill.

FOUR fifths + one fifth = five fifths = 1 point

It comes out to 3 or 4 depending on how your secondary stat rounds off at the end.

So thus the troxes are getting jipped out of not 14 nano skill, but 24. 24 is a big difference. It's whether or not an atrox engie can self-cast the highest slayer. It's whether or not an atrox MA can self-cast UVC without tons of uber rare items. So lay off them.
