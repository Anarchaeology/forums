Deathmatch in Wine SATURDAY OCT 12, 7 PM EST
Thanks for showing up last Saturday, the 1st place prize was 81.5 million credits, the highest ever. Grats to Abid/Morrizon and Cudus/Fyrestarter for second. I've gotten some requests to "mix it up a little" so today I'm going to try a different format for the Deathmatch: 2 ppl team FFA. I'm going to hold it in a building in Wine, instead of Hope. Inside there are 2 levels, stairs, rooms you can hide in, and corners you can use to avoid shots. Basically you walk go inside with a team of two. Everyone spreads out, and after count down, its FFA. The deathmatch ends when all but one team is eliminated.


TOURNEY INFO:


SERVER: RK1
PLACE: Meet outside Bar in Wine. CLAN: Whompa from Tir->Varmint Woods->Wine OMNI: Rome->Broken Shores->Wine (note: there's a clan guard near the whompa, so be careful zoning in)
TIME: October 12, Saturday 7PM EST

600k credits entry fee for lvls 150 and below
2 mil creds entry fee lvl 151-200 (not firm, post below)
money added
winner takes all

No level restriction
Profession restriction: You cannot have two of the same profs on one team (e.g. 2 docs, 2 traders, 2 mas, etc.)
Outside buffs are allowed (ask for buffs outside the bar)
Bring a partner; if you can't find one, come anyway, there's gonna be other ppl there that needs a partner

Zoning will be counted as death.
30 minutes time limit. If more than 1 team remains after time runs out, the match will be INVALIDATED. However, requests for extension will be considered, depending on situation.
We'll hold a calcutta (team auction) before the tourney starts. Starting bid is 500k. Anyone (even if you're just watching) is allowed to make bids. The winner of last week's calcutta won 46 mil.


See you guys there
