Supa Leet
That's only because of the rounding. Lets take that the skill increase is 105, now a QL 199 should give 104.4475, but it gets rounded to 105. If you have a QL 198 it will give 103.95 that gets rounded to 104.

Now if you take a shining max life implant that gives +405 HP at QL 200...a QL 199 will give 402.975 HP, which gets rounded to 403 HP. So the answer is yes and no, depending on what the implant contains.
