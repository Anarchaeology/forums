Envisioned: Mountains
One thing that I really miss when traveling through Rubi-Ka, is large mountain ranges. Granted, I've not been everywhere yet, but I've yet to come across huge snow-capped mountains. And I'm not talking about the odd peak here and there... I mean entire zones of ragged mountains with winding paths and little streams and glaciers and all that (yes, yes, I know... lots of deserts in RK, no snow... If you make 'em high enough, there will be snow, right )

Perhaps the adventuring skill could be used here. You know, that glacier can only be climbed if your skill is high enough.

Oh well....
