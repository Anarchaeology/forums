Putting some "Fun" in Anarchy Online
Ok Funcom here are some simple ways to ****e up AO. They all involve creating a new type of item.

Mission item
  • Item that when carried, prevents the wearer from equipping a vehicle, gridding or taking a wampa.
    In short, if you have the item, you are traveling on foot. Now make missions/quests or just plain trade routes that involve transporting the items to another part of the world. These mission/quests can range from a very low level one which just involves couriering the item within a city to a multi-stop trek through high level or PvP zones. If you die, you have to go back to the location, retrieve the item and continue on.

    You can mix up the missions. Some can have the pick-up and drop-off occur in mini missions or the pickup point could just be a general location and the player would have to search for the item. You could have to fight a mob to get the item or only have to double click it. The item could decay over time to give the mission a sense of urgency. The greater the difficulty however the greater your reward would be for delivering it.

    People have been looking for a reason to explore the planet and this could give them one. It is also an outdoor mission, which is something else that has been greatly desired.


Guild event item
  • Item that wont disappear when placed on the ground for an extended period of time. These items could be used for guild events such as an egg hunt.


PvP item
  • Item that can be used as a ball in a PvP sport.
    When it is carried, the player will be highlighted in some way. If the person dies while carrying it, the item will automatically appear on the ground. The item can be passed to another player in your team by simply highlighting them and pressing a hotkey.
  • Item similar to the ball above but gives bonuses to stats or damage. This item cant leave a PvP zone or area. If the person carrying it logs out or leaves, the item will be transferred to another team member. If the person is not in a group, the item will return to a one of a few preset locations in the zone.
  • Another ball item that gives bonuses and can leave a PvP zone but the wearer will always be vulnerable to attack


Now get to work Funcom, I want to see this in the next patch. Ok maybe the patch after that, but that is it
