basically anything that does 'add all def' adds that exact number to all your defensive rolls, just like add all off adds to all your offensive rolls(atk rating is what most people know it as). SO if you are fighting someone with an ithaca, the defense skill is duck. The game uses in its calculations your duck + the add all def. The easiest way to think of it is as a boost to all your evades, except it doesnt show.

I use a 7% exp ring when with a team but for soloing take it off and use two damage rings, fist buff and attack nano.
example:
Chem Fist buff Toad: +16
2 ring of physics +32 Title 5
Waiting Panda + 69
Total extra damage +120 per hit

add a BTW and the damage is further increased
Naturally I have all evades maxed and RI running, plus good armor and Titanium skin so that I can use the added damage rings without getting beat up too much.

Tip: do not use the same attack type that the mob is hitting you with. If you are getting hit with chem damage, kill the toad buff and use polar star (fire). It seems that the defense skill if the MOB is connected the offense.

Same with sheilds. If you see a red shimmering shield on a boss, that is a fire sheild. Kill fire fist and switch to energy or chem.
Blue is Energy, Green is Chem, etc...

Mob ac
Switching dmg type corresponding to the mobs lowest ac type may increase your damage by lots of points! After doing millions (ok, maybe hundreds) of missions, I think these are the damage types which work best on each mob:

MA mobs: melee or fire
Enf mobs: fire
NT: melee
Soldier: chemical
Adv: chemical
Trader: melee
Fixer: melee
Agent: melee or chem
Doc: either
Eng: same as docs
MP: melee
Crats: melee

I think it could be useful being aware of this, especially when soloing high level missions. Eg. when fighting Veteran Enforcers, Ill crit for appr. 2300 points with melee damage, and 2800-2900 with fire damage. Same goes for NT mobs (altho in the opposite direction), 2500ish with fire damage, 2900 with melee.
