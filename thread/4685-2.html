Have you even fought the new medusas Zarch? The aggressiveness is not the problem at all. Aggressiveness makes mobs easier to kill not harder. You can pull aggressive mobs solo every time with very little difficulty.

The difficulty with the medusas is that they hit for an average of 500+ damage now. This isn't a really the problem at all. They are level 200 monsters so they are supposed to hit hard. Other level 200 monsters hit hard too. Medusas were too easy for the level and needed to be made harder. The problem is that this reveals the substantial lack of xp that the monsters you are SUPPOSED to kill at my level give.

I have fought at the medusas. With a level 118 doctor healing for like 400-1000 damage or something like that and me (Trader level 112) team healing for 600 average (with 500 nanoc init) non-stop my team was unable to heal fast enough to deal with the damage dealing capability of the new medusas. Half our group died so I distracted the medusa and we ran away.

I have fought at cyborgs in Mort too. Myself and the 118 doctor were not able to keep up with the damage output of the highest cyborgs although we were still managing to kill them before our tank reached 0 health. We added an adventurer with some healing ability to increase the safety margin which was previously on the level of "going to die very soon" up to the level of "lets save every 500k, this is too dangerous". The chance of an average group on RK2 at my level having 3 healers and 5 of 6 group members over level 112 on RK2 is pretty slim, so this can pretty much be discounted as an option in the normal course of play at level 110ish. These do still give somehwat reasonable xp at 30-55k, if your group is uber. Most groups are not. We managed to barely keep everyone alive though a few people in the party went below 10% life several times and we gained about 1.7 million xp in about two and a half hours or so. I need about 2.7 million total to level so it would have been about 4 hours total to gain a level.

Now while we were standing there at the cyborgs, a mantis wandered up to us. It was red. We killed it. It wasn't too terribly hard. We could do it easily enough with one good and one weak healer, maybe just one good healer even if we could avoid adds. This is about what your average thrown together group could expect to kill at my level but you'd have to find a spot where adds could be kept to a low level. The mantis was worth 12k xp. A decent group at an average spot could expect to kill one of these per minute I would guess if you are being careful to avoid adds. It'd take an endless stream of about 240 kills, but you could get a level in about 4 hours.

I went to level 133 mission with two buddies, a level 113 enforcer and a level 102 fixer. The monsters ranged from red to light green. The light green monsters were worth 5k xp. Yellows were worth about 7k. The red ones that took us 5 minutes to kill were worth about 15k. We fought for about an hour and gained 350k xp of the 2.7 million I need to level. If we had continued at this rate it would have taken another 7 hours to gain a single level. We got a bad add and all died and lost 100% of the xp we had gained.

Now I played EverQuest for a year and a half. It had 50 levels for the first year I played it and 60 levels total later on only after they had added a massive amount of content to the game. They haven't added the content to this game yet, so I'll compare to the 50 levels of EverQuest. Each of the 200 levels in Anarchy Online would be equivalent to 4 levels in EverQuest. My level 112 character here would be equivalent to my level 29 one in EQ. My 4 hours to gain a level would be equivalent to 16 hours for a single level in EQ. It never took me anywhere near that long at that level in EQ. I could make a bubble of xp in 2 hours if I was doing pure xp up to at least level 35-40. Even as I approached level 50 it took a MAXIMUM of 3-4 hours for one bubble of xp. That's 15-20 hours for a single level. I'm going at nearly the same pace here and I'm only half way!

In EverQuest there is a LOT more interesting stuff to do at high level than in this game and frankly I almost couldn't stomach reaching level 50 in that game. If it takes longer than that to get to level 200 in this game which has very little in the way of new things to do at high level, then why would I ever try for level 200 in this game? It seems that extending the high level game will have opposite of the desired effect and just cause me to quit sooner rather than stick around longer once I discard reaching level 200 as a goal.
