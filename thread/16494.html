Storm is recruiting.

Requirements for admission
1. Respect the other guild members.
2. Always be trustworthy!
3. Level 100

Advantages of being a member

One of the premier guilds of Rubi-Ka. Nothing ingame is out of our reach thus far. Plenty of high level grouping. Plenty of Unique hunts. Plenty of in depth info on the game.

We are a group of players that are very self-reliant. So unlike other smaller guilds you wont constantly be getting tells for your help. You will also have many people to help you when you do need it. The kind of help high level players really need.

If this sounds like what you need in a guild give a /tell NikkiDial ingame and ask me any questions you would like. Hopefully we can come to count on each other in the near future.
