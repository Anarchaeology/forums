Night at the Red Tape offices.
Mrs. Hallas was afraid. It was well into the twilight hours at the darkened offices of Red Tape. She rummages through a fileing cabinet at a corner cubicle with a flashlight in one hand, hopeing that the cubicle walls will shield the glow. If a security guard found her here in a restricied area after dark, she will have a lot of explaining to do. Her eyes dart from folder to folder with headings like "4th Quater Profit Reports", "CoA's Favorite Color" and other useless mundane tidbits.

Finally after five minutes of flipping through the cabinet she finally found a biosealed folder with the heading "E.M.S." At the center of the folder was the circular logo of a winged sword. She pulled the folder out and sighed in silent relief that Administrator Houston didn't trust the computer systems completely and liked to keep a hardcopy of everything. Mrs. Hallas stopped breathing as she heard footsteps approach her. She clicked off her flashlight and shrank further into the cubicle as a security guard shone a flashlight above her position. Her hand went to the OT-120 pistol strapped to her thigh and waited.

The guard waved the flashlight over her cubicle a few times and seeing nothing out of the ordinary continued on his rounds. Mrs. Hallas mentally counted to thirty before dareing to peek around her position to see the guard turning a corner and move out of sight. She let go of her pistol and started setting the cloaking device's timer to ten minutes. She wasn't familar enough with the device to deactivate it by touch alone and she didn't relish the thought of fumbling with it when both her and the controls are cloaked.

Mrs. Hallas stepped out of her hideing place and started to stalk her way out of Red Tape's offices. Her destination was to a Yalm parked outside the office where she can fly out of range and safely escape. It only took five minutes for her to sneak to the back door leading outside. She used her hacking device on the slot for the keycard and the door quietly slid open. Mrs. Hallas paused and wondered briefly why it had been so easy. Didn't Administrator Houston know that pratically anyone can simply use a cloaking device and elude the guards and cameras without trouble? She walked out into the night and looked around to see there was noone there. She was about to mentally cheer for success when she started to feel a slight burning sensation on the hand holding the folder. The folder was covered with a glowing blueish goo, a nanovirus and it was spreading rapidly while eating through her glove. Her mind screamed. "Stupid! You should have known better than to think it was that easy."

It was about then that the perimeter alarms went off and she heard the sound of a Slayeroid powering up behind her.
