Omni-Tek Encrpyted Personal File No. 37
Public Name: Irvin Caldon
Given Project Name: Zero37
Height: Normal
Weight: Normal
Build: Normal
Status: Active
Danger Lvl: 55%

Bio:
Started 10 years after Omni1 was built a small group of bio-cybernetics scientists began the "Zero Projects". The project was supposed to be a way for the scientists to see if a organism could live if it was fully implanted from birth. The project was a complete failure as the infant's bodies would violenty reject the implants due to poor quality, and that is where Omni-Tek stepped in. Omni-Tek sponsored the project secretly with superb quality implants, which seemed to make the infants live longer though after a few monthes as their bodies matured they wuld again react and reject the implants.
This would be the case until a scientist by the name of *CLASSIFIED INFORMATION* developed implants that seemed to grow with the organism they were impanted in. When the time came and they were added to the infant it was a success and baby Irivin Caldon (the first name of the scientist that created the implants and the last name of the lead scientist of the project) was born. The scientists raised and studied Irvin till he was 18 years old, and again Omni-Tek stepped in but this time did not help the the project instead they dissolved it and put there own scientists in place of the formers. Meanwhile young Irvin was sent to a Omni-Tek taining facility.
For the past few years Irvin has been in training till a few weeks ago he was released to the world as Zero37 (the 37th subject to be created). He is still young and not to his full potential yet but information gathered senses he will be immensly powerful when at full maturiaty. As for now he is a Omni-Tek assassin taking only missions that require a spy killed, a mutant den destroyed, or a hideout cleansed. He has always be seen wereing a pair of sunglasses and black trenchcoat which cover his body armor underneath. He is lethal in all forms of combat ranging from hand to hand to melee weapons. He will be watched over for future devlopments.
