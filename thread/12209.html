Hit points: The real killer
The one fault I think FunCom has concentrated on too much is Hit Points. If you are not sure what I am comming from think about this. Would you rather a game based mainly on hit points and weapon or a game based on mainly skill.. Well most think The skills should count for most. As in ya... Atrox have a lot of hit points what does that leave a say.. MA..... well it should leave em dodging skills.

Instead FC has gone HP crazy. Not sure what they can really do about this. But it starts to get boring a repitative(sp?) with thousand apon thousands of hitpoints. What really works is if you actually hit something.. that should be the main thing not the HP factor.

Like I said not sure if they can rebalance the whole system to be more inline with skills. A hit by a beam should be a killer but dodging it should not be that hard. But that all depends on the skill of the player has in say... dodge?

I am just rambling on really just getting tired of HPs being doubled tripled... or whatever to balance the classes. hit hit hit hit... yae.. unloading a full clip into someone's head.. yae.. damn futurestic weapons .
