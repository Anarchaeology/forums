Oh no, roleplaying is not dead. That is a nasty rumour that has going around since day one of AO.

But its not plain sailing either, never has been. Keeping an active roleplaying life in AO takes as much dedication as any other area of AO, be it power-levelling, successfull PvP (unsuccessful PvP is remarkably easy) or hunting uniques.

The rp orgs list Lille point you too is a pretty good starting point. Follow the links there as most orgs will list their website as part of the org info on the database page. There is quite a range of orgs out there, especially if you are not fussed about sticking to a particular server or faction.

You are welcome to check out RUR's website, but warned RUR is something of an acquired taste :-)
