Learning to Recognize Sore Ch0de Syndrome:

1. After a defeat, the loser posts hate directed at the players that beat them. This can be a series of personal attacks that skirt the topic of the defeat, but go on to point out the flaws of the players, and imply that something the winning players did to win is something that "normal" people do not do.

2. Make excuses for why the loss occoured. "We weren't ready", "We were all off-line" and of course the combos of point 1 above, "You knew we weren't ready, and that is the only reason you won."

3. The loser comes here and posts why the tactics the victor used were low down and dirty, and the only reason they won was because they used some sort of pudly tactics. These are often mis-read as personal hate posts but they often include many players and their choice of tactics in general.

4. Appeal to bugs: you only lost because of game bugs/CC working in the favor of the winner and if those bugs/CC issues didn't exist the outcome would have been reversed. Note the difference between pointing out legitimate game play issues that need work in a neutral context, v.s. using them as an excuse as to why you lost.
