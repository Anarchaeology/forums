Why, oh why ?!
Ok, today I picked up AO and after patching to 13.2 I decide to get me another of those 3-mission-trips that my everyday life so much consists of...

After having checked out the new wompah from Borealis to Stret West, which was nice, I decide to get my missions from the booth in Borealis, which has always been among my favourites.
After some picking and deleting I manage to find 3 missions in The Longest Road. "Lucky day", I think, "from the coords I can see that they are not so much apart from eachother". However, with 3 missions in my pocket, I decide to press to request button one more time, to maybe get an even better mission. "Unable to generate mission: mission limit exceeded".

Now WHY oh WHY? We used to get that message only when ACCEPTING a mission, not before REQUESTING. This means you can no longer request a 4th mission when you have already 3, alternatively DELETING one of your old ones before accepting the new, should you like it better.

Ok, this was really the "dream" situation, I already had a good pack of missions there so I really didn't need another one but...
...most of the time I will pick and delete and pick and delete like that for 25 minutes before I finally have 3 missions in SOMEWHAT the same area, and that includes running from city to city, trying all booths, picking and deleting, etc etc...

Ok, I know everyone doesn't try to play efficient in this game and might consider this a minor issue, but; WHY oh WHY do they have to do EVERYTHING they can to ruin the playability in this game ?!

Funcom has always been doing what they can to make it impossible to get more than 1 mission in the same place. The "pick-and-delete" routine I mentioned is just the way that will sometimes make the booths' locaction "cycle" rotate, thus tricking them into giving you a new mission in an area you already have.

Funcom, why dont you just go all the way with your *so incredibly annoying* line of thought? - I suggest you do this : make it so that when you pick a mission, you don't know what it is, what the rewards are, but foremost: you don't know where it is! No coords or compass or anything! That way players will have to explore the entire planet of Rubi-Ka by foot, trying every single cave and hole to see if that's the one containing their mission.

NOW WOULDNT THAT BE FUN ?
