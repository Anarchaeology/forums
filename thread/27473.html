Hello and welcome to Rub-Ka,

We, the South Rubi-Ka Society are a dedicated roleplaying guild. We run pen & paper style adventures for our members as well a variety of other in-character events. We are Omni, Rubi-Ka 1 and our player base is almost entirely European.

The guild itself is based on the concept of a gentleman's club. We are not as an organisation directly involved in the clan/Omni conflict (though some individual members are). As such the adventures we run tend to be a little more personal, tailored to the individual player characters, rather than aimed at afftecting the official AO storyline.

I'd like to invite you to visist our website at http://rubika.no-ip.org/srs/ to get a better idea of who we are and the things we do.

You can also check the following thread for a list of roleplaying-friendly guilds
