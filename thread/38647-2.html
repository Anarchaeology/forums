Half the people on the ten most wanted list either stopped playing the game, or barely play much anymore at it is. This system would only encourage more of those players to stop. There might be anywhere from zero to five people out of the 20 on all lists that would keep doing PvP because of this change. Anything that causes more people to leave the game is a bad thing. Having all these people not around anymore would make it even more difficult for any new persons to break into the list.

Overall it is a very interesting idea, but it has way too many downsides to even be considered a realistic possibility.

The knife would not be able to contain the persons name, as that would require Funcom to update the item database every time the top ten lists changed. They could introduce knifes with ranks, and make it up to you to find out which one to kill, but then you could just buy all ten knifes which would be too easy. Either way is a bad method for implementing it.

They could make it so that you have to buy a special most wanted knife from an outdoor vendor in a 25% zone. The new knifes could cost 50M credits, and expire after 48hours. The vendor should be very hard to kill, but not impossible, and should be on an 18 hour spawn time. That way people on the 10 most wanted list could kill the vendor, but would also put themselves in risk of being PKd while doing it (or just get their friends to do it). It would also put the spawns throughout the day, making it an inconvenience to kill him every time. The huge downtime would also make it nearly impossible to obtain one of the knifes, which is how it should be.

Either way, I dont think the knifes should perma-kill, or cause them to lose all their stuff. Possibly another penalty that isnt quite so severe. One that wouldnt cause people to quit playing, but would make the people on the top ten list want the vendor dead.

Anyway, just some ideas. Since this idea will NEVER come to pass, and is one of the more horrible ideas Ive ever seen, I thought it would be fun to throw out some alternatives.
