PSA: Stream Still Up! (Everything else down temporarily)
This is an important Public Service Announcement for the listeners of GridStream Productions.

Here's a hefty little FYI for everyone.

It has apparently seemed to many that the stream for GridStream Productions is down. It is not.

What has happened is an issue with the "mydomain" redirecting service. We do not know what this issue is, nor how long it will last, since the mydomain main site is down as well (these must be some SERIOUS issues!)

So for those who still wish to tune into the stream, we are no longer accessible through stream.gridstream.com or live.gridstream.com, but rather directly from the live365 stream itself.

To tune into GridStream Productions at any point, please play location with winamp, realplayer, or a later version of windows media player to this location:

http://www.live365.com/play/gridstream

Or you can access and listen to us through the live365 webplayer simply by browsing to the following location:

http://www.live365.com/stations/gridstream

We apologize for any inconvenience, and if the current domain directing issue is not solved in a timely fashion, we will be searching for other means to redirect the domains for our stream, our forums, and our main site at www.gridstream.com.

Thanks for your patience, and a HUGE thank you for listening! h00t!

P.S. I'll check back here periodically, please post if you have any questions, comments, suggestions, rants, raves, bullets-with-my-name, or bagels in your fridge.
