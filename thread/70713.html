I presume you mean a bot that lets you grab team missions to solo ? Well. I'd not expect FC to make one of these, as I suspect they didnt intend team missions to be run solo

But a teambot that helps assembling teams would be very cool indeed.

Perhaps a team leader could do this :

/tell Teambot form 30-40

Then a player who is, say, level 35 could do :

/tell Teambot lfg 35

And get a list of team leaders looking for people his level.

The problem would be the chat traffic to and from this bot. The chat system does not deal well with 1000+ tells per second.
