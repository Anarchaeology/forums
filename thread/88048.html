I dunno... seems like bandwidth is rarely a problem for me. In REALLY huge fights my ping will jump to 600-800 and every action I take will lag a second or two. The killer is the initial load time when I arrive at the scene of the fight. It's loading up info on every single person in the area very suddenly and this causes my comp to tank. A few days ago at the battle at our ql200 base in Belial, I arrived, scouted a bit (it's a VERY huge base) and eventually stumbled on the attacking force of 25-30 people. When I did, my comp immediately tanked to .1 fps, and when it stopped 20 seconds later (I guess after loading every person there) I was dead and my screen was filled with red damage text.

The trick seems to be surviving that initial hit as you come across a big group of people. After they're loaded, my system works pretty smoothly and lag is rarely an issue.
