Apartments (and things of that sort)
Well, for me, what makes or breaks me playing a game is housing. Yeah, it sounds weird, but that's what i like, a place to live. So here are my suggestions on how to make the housing better.

1) More items to allow in your apartment. Up the limit from 10 so you can actually have a populated apartment that doesn't look so empty. Maybe have a system where it costs people money to upgrade how many items they can have.

2) Have a way that the owner can duplicate keys for others to enter. Or...have it set so they can add "allowances" for ther apartment, allowing their friends to enter while not in a group.

3) Have all types of furniture available. Beds, couches, tv's, tables, etc...this may be already implimented, but I'm not sure, so I added it anyway. Just so it'll look like an actual apartment.

4) Possibly have lighting control where you can adjust the lighting in the room and in the closet.

5) Just a minor thing, but possibly have a command like the "sit" command that will let you lay down. So if you have a bed, you can lay down on it (makes it look more realistic).

6) Maybe make it so you can have an aquarium in your apartment. At the furniture shops, buy different fish to put in it so people can have a variety of fish swimming around. (Just thought I'd be neat.)

7) Have missions randomly (possibly rarely) tack on to missions for furniture that is only available through a mission, ,meaing you can't buy it at a store normally. This, in theory, would promote teaming up to take on missions if you added it to only the team missions.

Hmm...er...some of that went way out there, but I think it'd be neat. If I think of more, I'll tack on. But for now, at least take it into consideration if you will :o)

Take care!

Additions
Last night I went looking at other peoples posts and saw their ideas, so I figured I would list them here as well since I agree with them.

NOTE: I do not claim all of these to be my original ideas. They have come from others, and I list them here to further emphesis that the players want them. If I offend you by listing them, I apologise profusely.

1) Guild halls. How great are they? It would be nice to have buildings set up especially for them. And if you want to limit it, have the owner pay for it, possibly through tithing of the guild members.

2) Villas someone where with a nice scenery, robot maids, already furnished so no decorating is done, but costs (rent) to live there. And this could be in addition to having an apartment, since you're paying for this one.

3) Player run stores. They could pay an upkeep to keep it going, but have a robot (through making themselves with the trade skills) or having an NPC selling them. I'd say an easy way to do it is have a room (or just a chest) that they put the things they want to sell and they give them a price. That way things can be isolated to one "slot" and easier to implement.

4) Actual housing. Different types of houses to buy (or rent) in various locations. That way they could live somewhere else instead of just in the city (also serves as a purpose for roleplaying. Some people, even if it's rare, role play characters who hate the city).

Now, I recognize to the makers of AO that this does take memory on the servers. The only way I can see to changing that is possibly an overhaul on the "loading" system. I do not know how it works now, but maybe a reference to an item, then a location of it through a hex, binary, or even decimal file. Someone mentioned storing them on the client side machine, but that would allow hacking, so that will not happen.

Now, let's see what people think, shall we? :o)
