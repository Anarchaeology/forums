Lost IP mystery solved!
Sorry folks but I've solved the lost IP mystery.

It's not a bug but a devious plot of thievery and deceit by some less than honest Funcom employees, in association with corrupt high-ranking Omni-Tech officials, running an IP Black-market Store in Lush Fields.

I got this mission from one of the disgruntled Funcom employees that thought his fellow conspirators where not giving him his fair share of credits for the risk he was taking. Seems the "risk vs. reward" ratio is a bit off even amongst thieves.

Anyways, the Funcom Employee, lets call him Sven, hacked into the mission terminal and gave me what he described as a "get even mission". This didnt surprise me a lot since I had my good-bad mission bar set all the way to bad, but I also had my money-experience bar all the way to money and the xp reward was showing as much as you an get away with.

As much as you can get away with? This intrigued me like no mission before. And as I read through the mission description it all became clear that this IP disappearance was intentional. Sven went on and on (kinda like me sometimes) about how he programmed the game engine to randomly steal IPs when the characters briefly entered the Shadow World between zoning out of one area and zoning into another. He also had the game pick off a few IPs when a person traveled through the Shadow World on the way to the reclaim terminal. Seems there is a lot of stuff they do us when were in this Shadow World of theirs.

Anyways, I accepted the mission of course and worked my way deep into the hills in northern Lush Fields. I was surprised to discover this mission was a tongue mission and I had to enter through the cheek instead of the mouth of the cave.

Funny thing about this mission too, all the walls where lined with what looked like gold and the floor was lined with dollar bills, almost 13 laid end to end from wall to wall.

After fighting some unique mobs (anyone ever see a Nerfbat or a Server Disconnect Bot before?) I finally came to a door at the end of the green tongue. Now this was an unusual door too. No locks, hinges, or even frame was evident. All that I saw was a little window about chest high in the center of the door. No amount of picking could open that door and that really upset me because I always keep my Breaking and Entering skill maxed at each level. Finally, out of frustration, I said out loud  Open the #%$@!%& door or Im canceling this mission! Well, Im not sure if it was what I said or the way I said it, but I heard this click and the door opened a crack.

After making sure I was fully buffed, I entered the room. Man, let me tell ya, I was ready for anything or so I thought. As I quietly entered the room and the door closed behind me, I couldnt believe my eyes. This room was full of IPs, they were everywhere. There were so many I could even begin to count them all. And sitting in the corner was a little scrawny mob totally oblivious to me. He was sitting in front of this huge console filled with monitors with writing in a foreign language on little plaques below them.
Unfortunately I couldnt make out any of the writing since Im an American, but I could tell that it was watching a monitor showing some fat Atrox dancing in Baboons while munching on potato (or is it potatoe?) chips and slurping on a can of Jolt Cola. Not only that, but fuzzy pizza slices and overflowing trash cans where everywhere, except where the IPs where of course.

Soon my amazement wore off and I moved into a position to end this poor souls existence in Rubi-Yipie-Kai-Yay. But as soon as I hit him the first time, he jumped up and started running into a wall. It was then that I noticed that he was a Novice Adventurer and knew the fight was all but won for me. Sure enough a few whacks later he slumped to the floor a mass of oozing slime.

Now the good part I grabbed so many IPs I maxed all of my skills. Greens skills, blue skills, dark blue skills, it didnt matter. There where so many IPs that my IP counter over flowed. Man what should I do? I figured this would probably be considered an exploit since I was getting rewarded for ripping off the people who where ripping us off. So I thought Id just take as many IPs as Im allowed and leave all my skills maxed. Maybe then Id be able to stay under their radar and not get banned. Then I destroyed all the remaining IPs using my newly maxed out dark blue engineering skills.

As I left the cave I was a happy Omni-Tech employee, maybe my raid would show them crime doesnt pay if you get caught.

Little did I know how far the corruption went though. Because during the time that I zoned out of the cave and the time I zoned into Lush Fields, can you guess what happened? Yep, they not only took all my extra IPs back and reset all my skills ten IPs less than they were at the beginning of the mission, they nerfed me into a thong wearing Atrox Adventurer.

Oh the humiliation.
