Okay, here's the story behind the nokkens and fossegrims:
They are sort of like tree/animal hybrids that were created by omnitek. You can find the fossegrim's little brother, the nokken in stret east. Another rare creature, the primordal (sp?) chirop lives there. unique to say the least!

As for what they drop, the fossegrim is around the right level to dorp grid armor 4 and the nokken may drop (i think) grid armor 2 or 3
Oh yeah, almost forgot. a martial artist, enforcer or metaphysicist over lvl 125 or so can wipe the floor with one of those beasties

Yay glad to see some people interested in the wilderness/wildlife instead of most of the drivel on this forum recently

Dont mean to sound nasty but Nokkens are actually the bigger brothers of Fossegrims. ((Just slightly higher level - 10 or so i think)).

Also Nokkens are a lot rarer than Fossegrims. There is only one small valley that holds the entire Nokken population of Rubi Ka in Upper Stret East. Whereas Fossegrims are quite common in areas like Belial Forest and SAV.

Another thing to add, as someone said, is that they are a lot tougher than their level suggests. Very nasty in regards to health and how hard they hit. A mission-mob of the same level would be nowhere near as tough. Even Enfs

Hope this helps. And keep exploring
