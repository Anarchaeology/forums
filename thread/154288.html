Download.
"Ah, hell. This is a lot less comfortable than I remembered it being."

Yeah. That's an insurance terminal in front of me. Guess that means I lost. Oh well. Arms, check, legs, check, head, check... yeah, definitely back on Rubi-Ka.

Guess that means that the version of me that went digital is back to good old analog.

Where did I leave my... armor... cash... no, screw that. Where are my *guns*?

Oh, that's right. I left them with a 'friend'.

We'll see if he still has them. If he doesn't, I guess he wasn't such a good friend after all.

"K-D...? I thought you were, you know, gone... in the grid?"

"I thought so too. I'm back. Now, do you still have my Manex, or did you sell it like the sleazy backstabbing clanner scum I know you are the second my corporeal remains dropped to the ground?"

"Uhm... I thought you were go-"

"You can shut up now. You sold my gun. I'll take that one. The one in your hands. And whatever you have in your pockets. For my pain and suffering, right?. And don't come asking me for any favors, any time soon. Just be glad I'm not making you remember what insurance reclaims feel like."

I'm glad I didn't have to do more than talk. 6 months in the grid, I can barely remember how to walk and talk. Shooting is going to take some time. That's okay though, I suspect I'll be able to find plenty of people to practice on.

I was really getting to enjoy it in there, too. And then *it* showed up. Hell if I know what - who? - it was, but it definitely did not want me in its territory. And, unfortunately, it knows a lot more grid-tricks than I do.

That's OK, though. A bit of time, a few bribes, and I'll know where it stores its data. A bit more time, a bit of explosive, and we'll see who's pulling grid tricks.

I see some old friends. They don't exactly look happy to see me, either. I wonder what they did to screw me while I was away.

Still, you have to laugh.
