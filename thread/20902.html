The Praetorian Regulars on a 'cruiting drive!
The Praetorian Regulars are on a recruiting drive! If you are not a complete cabbage, on RK1, and like to enjoy playing the game then we are after U!

We are an Omni guild (are there any other kind that matters?) and are quite happy to take on all comers - assuming that you are reasonably capable and keen to play the game.

We are not going to dish out trillions of creds to n00bs, nor give you all the **** you need to lvl another 40 lvls in one night, as that is not what we are about. We play the game for FUN - not for levelling contests or ubertwinking.

Currently we are mainly Kiwis or Aussies, with a smattering of US based players - so these are the time-zones to look for

So - if you are keen to join up to a friendly guild who likes to go and cap clan asses, and you've got a bit of a sense of humour, send Weta13, Richomuffin or Shaydefist a tell in game, and we can look at hooking you up
