I think the IP cost is current Ability level x 3.0 for dark blue, 2.0 for light blue and 1.0 for green abilities.

So an Opifex begins with 15 Agi, to raise 1 pt = 15 IPs, Stamina is dark blue but begins at 6 so 1 pt = 18 IPs.

BTW, this is for breed abilities only.
