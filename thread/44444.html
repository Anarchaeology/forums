booster packs...
So does anyone else see the announced booster packs for AO as a ploy to suck even more money out of us for half baked product? First i was disapointed to learn that funcom had cut its other projects to focus on AO (clearly they have money issues) Then they announced the expansion pack (while the game isnt really even in a state where the origonal game world is complete) Now they annouce that the street for Shadow Lands has been pushed back but some of the features they would have included in SL will be available in smaller/lower cost "booster" packs.

The lowest cost you could expect these booster packs to come out at in the US is around $9.99, though i would expect 14.99 based on other similer small booster type things (mechwarrior4 mech packs for instance) Now at the store i work at a regular expansion pack for a game like this usualy goes at 19.99, maybe 29.99 if its really packed but not so often. So basically your paying half of the regular expansion cost but getting far less than what a normal expansion offers.

"We hated the idea of holding stuff back from our players until everything for Shadowlands was done. So, we came up with the idea of booster packs." - Gaute Godager

Tha wonderful quote from GG translated: We cant aford to wait for Shadow Lands to street because we are failing horribly and need to pump more money out of our player base by making them pay for ideas that where origionaly in the envisioned section for regular patches and game updates.

I would rather wait and pay 19.99 once for a completed expansion pack than pay an extra 15 bucks just to get something a bit earlier then end up paying 19.99 for an expansion pack with less behind it. Especialy when funcom is neglecting to use its patching system. I dont believe that all of these updates are too big for downloading especially when you break them up into patches over a period of time. Funcom is simply trying to squeeze us for as much as they possibly can.

Funcom really does make Verant look better every day. This game was obviously streeted before it was ready because funcom ran out of money, its expansion annouced prematurely to try and save whast left, and boosters to try and pump even more cash out of us, funcom is really makin it easy on its up and comming competitors.
