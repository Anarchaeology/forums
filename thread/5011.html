New overequipping changes giving Casters an advantage and putting Agents in the hole
The recent overequipping changes are going to cripple agents and give casters an advantage. Why?

Well.. the overequipping cap applys to weapon skills. I didn't hear them say anything about the Nano programs.

I remember in a thread when an NT asked for Funcom to remove the feature were if you don't have the skills even if the nano is uploaded ou cant use it. Funcom said "maybe".

So if Funcom makes it so casters can equip nanos and they will still work if they dont have the skills (sort of like Rifles). Rifles will only be able to be 20% overequipped.. and then Casters will be were Agents were before 12.8... VERY overequipped.

Lets think about this. a lvl 110 NT has about 698 MC (I know one of them) buffed with expertise. Add a +132 and a +50 to that. Roughly 780 MC skill... and this is just with QL 125 implants. Imagine if he got QL 200 (I dunno QL 140-200)... he would be able to cast very strong nukes. As 10 lvls = roughly a +50 if the skill is maxed. So a lvl 120 NT has about 830 MC. I think thats enough to casts Izgimmers little nuke.

What Funcom is doing basically right now is making Casters happy all the while nerfing Agents to nearly nothing. Cz tries to be polite but what he's really trying to say is "Sorry guys... the NT boards have reached a total post of 2000 almost.. and we gotta nerf you so we dont lose half of our player base". He's saying it in a nice way such as "How would you feel if we annihilated your ability to overequiped?" "How would you feel if we removed your ability to Summon pets?".

In my opinion the AGENT is in its medium state. It needs boosts to fill its role and to make some of its main abilities more effective. But overall we are a fun proffesion. It really looks like the overequipping changes will be implemented. It will really hurt alot of proffesions and even though I oppose the patch I am not really sure what its effects will be.

The reason us Agents have to overequip rifles is because Funcom nerfed Rifle damage. My QL 150 dissafiliation sniper rifle at lvl 60 Barely gets the job done. Funcom has basically created in enviorment that is impossible to strive in without overequipping. If Funcom removes overequipping.. the last redeeming feature.. always trying to GET BETTER.. IMPROVE.. if this is taken away from us the game will be very boring.

Seeing an ever expanding oppurtunity in this game to overequip. You find out what your proffesion and breed is all about then you try to get the best guns, armor etc. for it.

If Funcom puts a 20% cap on this they will be putting a 20% cap on the oppurtunities of thier game. Seeing the possibility to improve in this game is half the fun. The ever expanding ways to get better rifles, gear is very fun. But if it stops at 20%...... then its just not fun.

Ok , a QL 150 Rifle is to the equivelant to a QL 101 Nova flow (which it is.. ive done tests).

So what Funcom is actually doing is making it so we can no longer overequip our nerfed rifles. Overequipped nerfed rifles = Rifles equivelant to our lvl.. just nto 20% because if we used 20% we would be so ineffective that im pretty sure Adventurers and NTs would kill us in PvP.

I'll be the firsts to say a lvl 20 equipping a QL 170 is very unbalanced. But to nerf it to the extent of 20% is an outrage.
