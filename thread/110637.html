Okay, I'm a third-generation conspiracy theorist... but in this case, I genuinely believe this flu isn't a government-released agent. Truly.

I used to work at a hospital, and for awhile was an admin assistant to the Infection Control director. There was a really terrific publication, Morbidity and Mortality Weekly, that was always running collected data on pretty much anything that makes you very sick and/or kills you. Viruses, bacteria, static-generated gasoline fires that resulted when someone with a plastic pickup-truck liner fills his gas tank in cold weather (YES, this was an actual article), ephedrine-related deaths, you name it, they've probably published an article on it.

Anyway, let me get to the point - Singapore/Hong Kong/that whole area of Asia, seems to periodically have some really nasty strain of influenza that will devastate a (usually domestic) animal population. Remember the "Asian Swine Flu"? or when Singapore had to destroy almost all their chickens when that bird form of flu went through? One of the primary reasons for doing that was concern from the World Health Org that it might mutate into a human form of flu.

Okay, okay, I will also freely admit, I'm totally speculating here, I need to read up more on it. But I kinda sorta have a feeling this is another one of those nasty little adaptive viruses that probably started out on someone's cattle ranch where maybe the manure didn't get shovelled out as often as it should have. Or maybe it started up in a rural area that doesn't test and maintain a baseline standard for safe drinking water. 30 or 40 years ago, people living on houseboats would dump their wastes into the water - the same water they fished out of. Mmm-mm!

But I think, if nothing else, this does make a good case for why biological weapons are EVERYONE's concern (wow, I sound like Bonefish). You don't have to show symptoms to be a carrier (hell, I can recall, when I first went full-time at the hospital, with regular patient contact, my roommate had the flu three times in about two months). Anyone can look healthy and get on a plane. Anyone can cough into their hand, pick up a pen, sign a check, and then leave that pen sitting on a store counter for someone else to pick up. Nobody is out of reach.

LOL, is everybody all freaked out and scared yet? Then don't watch The Stand...
