Urbanmouse
A small, dirty Opifex girl sitting on a medical bed talks to a Omni Doctor as he listens to her whilst performing tests.

"Well, my story really began a few days after I arrived on this planet, "the "shipment" as they called us was attacked on the way here and I was told by someone at a medical place that it was the Third attack on ships, but not frequent enough for the military to do anything" she said.

"I was told that my predecessor was changed by the clans, just like the rest that were found from the crash site and helped smuggle items, can I smuggle items too?" the girl said to the Doctor with a blank expression on her face.

The Doctor replied "That's up to you, I'm only here to find out if the damage to your stasis pod caused you more damage". "More damage?" the girl queried, "Yes, the attack on the cargo ship caused much damage that only 58 percent of of the pods survived." he said and continued "From your initial medical logs, you barely survived, but at a cost I'm afraid."

"Cost? I didn't pay anything" she said, still showing the same expression before, the Doctor laughed a little, knowing it was going to be hard to explain to someone that they have lost the ability to care and feel, smile, cry and understand how to survive as what they have been created for.

He sat down next to her and asked "When you're in the City, what do you see?". She paused for a moment and said "Buildings that disappear into the Sky, Cars, Terminals, Sky at day and night, Droids that clean, Droids that watch, People talk to each other", the Doctor, still smiling thought to himself as she continued "just like a Computer reading a list" and then said "that's very good, you can stop now".

"Okay, these nano chips should help you "Interact" better with other people" he said as he injected her with a pale blue substance. "Remeber you have to come back in Thirty cycles time so we can see how your're doing, try to talk to people, okay?" he said as he lead the girl to the door.

"Oh! one more thing, what's you're name?" he said, the girl looked around and said "I thought you knew my name, it's Urbanmouse, was my predecessor called Urbanmouse?". The Doctor quickly said "Just checking" as he closed the door before she could ask more questions, then walked over to his desk to finish his report. "Continue report" he said.

"The subject has little or no comprehension of feelings, this will also effect judgements in situations and will appear "cold" to others around her."

"Also, I believe from the state of subject arrive in, she is having difficulty with most of the basic things others don't think about from a day to day basis. The nano chips implanted will keep track where she is and record her progress...

... Records show that the original Urbanmouse was terminated by Omni-Pol on the outskirts of Rome only 50 cycles before her replacement was to arrive.

"End report" the Doctor finally said and pressed a button.

"How was that Ms. Pourais?" He said looking up at a tinted section of the ceiling. "Perfect, Dr. Border" a female voice replied "She will make an exceptional addition to Omni-Tek's forces against the clans, and please, call me Eva".

"Okay, Ms. P.. Eva., I'll sent the report to Omni-Pol shall I?" he said, still looking up at the dark window. "No" Replied Eva, "I'll deal with this One. Personally".
