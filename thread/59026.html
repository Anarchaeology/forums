Nano execution and aggdef bar:

0% = +1s atk time
1-99% = +-0s atk time
100% = -1s atk time

Rch time is never affected, by ini or by aggdef bar

Weapon and aggdef bar (AFAIK):
87.5% = listed atk/rch times
each 1/8s step = + (or - for 100%) 0.25s/0.25s
(so it is one step up to -0.25/-0.25, or max 7 steps down to +1.75/+1.75)

Not 100% certain whether there are more steps than eight or not. But that is the general outlining for weapon & aggdef bar.

Is Initiative applied before or after defagg modifier?

IE: Have a 2.5/2.5 weapon to consider.

Case 1: If initiative is applied after defagg modifier
Wtih defagg on 100% defensive, this should make it 4.25/4.25.
With 900 in weapon initiative, this becomes 2.65/1.25.

Case 2: If initiative is applied before defagg modifier
Initiative of 900 makes weapon 1/1.
Defagg on 100% defensive makes it 2.75/2.75.

Also, is 1/1 weapon cap applied after all calculations, or it applies to each individual calculation?
2nd case looks different if 1/1 comes in the end of all calculations:
Initiative 900 makes weapon 1/-0.5
Defagg on 100% defensive makes it 2.75/1.25
