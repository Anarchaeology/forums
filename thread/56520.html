Multiclassing by the numbers -

First off, when starting a new character from scratch only create MPs and NTs. The reason for this is GREEN NANO SKILLS. All 6 of them. That way when you get to level 200 and multiclass you can move immediately into casting the best nanos available for your 2nd, 3rd, and 4th professions. Sure you are going to drop alot of IP into your weapon skills, but when you won't be spending as much IP as you wold if you be if you maxed all the Nano skills.

If you went MP first for your second class I recommend Fixer. Green Nano Skills sure will make your life alot easier. Can anyone say GA MKIV and NCU buffs? I knew you could! To top it off your Treatment is now a green skill. You can get Ql200+ Implants in easier.

If you went NT first, I suggest MP as your second class. Before you know it you will be casting Mochams on yourself and Nuking like a Pro. Add in the dubious benefit of a Belemorte, and better Nano Cost Reducing nanos, and you'll be nuking like a pro in no time.

After you have your NT/MP/Fixer up you have a hard choice ahead of you. Adventurer for the temporary +7k HP buff, heals, and HoTs? Doctor for the very delicious heals? Or MA for that oh so nice Crit buff and the heals? I think in the end the only choice that one could possibly make is MA. The addition of the Crit buff is just too much. You would become an unstoppable force of nature.

Enjoy your Multiclassing!

Honestly folks, the only ways I see that FC could pull this off are to have Level Reqs on every single Nano, new and old. We all know how well that would be received. The other way would be to have to respend IP in every single Skill you need. Only this time you start off with maxed base abilities. The interface to handle different skills for different professions is enough to give me a headache just thinking about it. The IP for the second and following professions would have to be reduced due to not having to spend anything at all on base abilities. It would be a coding nightmare.
