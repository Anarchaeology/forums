If you get so tired of leveling and PvP; go dancing, exploring, meet up with some friends and talk...role-play. I've told many people many times that if all they have in AO is XP and PvP they shouldn't bother. It leads only to what you have all been saying, boredom. Take some time to enjoy things while you can. So many rush to be high level only to realize it didn't get them much. You need to have something else. Be it friends you can just goof around with, a guild you love, or a character with personality and depth that you like to role-play. Some even turn to tradeskills.

There are plenty of options, but most of them take more thought and effort on the part of the player. I understand that many do not wish to put that in and that's fine, but don't say there is nothing else. Rubi-Ka is not so bleak an environment that you can't give yourself other things to do that aren't entirely combat/leveling reliant. There are tons of people out there for you to interact with, I don't mean team, and become friends with. The game need not be all numbers and loot. Breathe some life into. Funcom is not responsible for every bit of your enjoyment in this game. They give you the medium and they give you activities. If those aren't enough make your own.
