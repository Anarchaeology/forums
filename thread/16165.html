Keep asking
Keep asking about this and bringing it up and FunCom is going to re-implement encumbrance. They almost did this two patches ago and it's probably still on their minds. For those of you who are new, next time you log on, take notice of the encumbrance rating which currently reads 'normal' no matter how many pieces of tank armor or thousands of bullets you have in your backpack.

Veterans, remember when ammo and other items in your inventory had weight and too much would make you run like you had a snare on you? Don't be surprised to see that make a come back. Then that 5000 ammo will make you think you have a _root_ on you.

Why do you think there's a nanoline for 'summon ammo'? It's for the inevitable time when they get their acts together and figure out how to fix encumbrance. Then your friendly Fixer will be able to 'aquire' several hundred rounds for you without anybody having to run back to town.

So, just 'shhhh' about getting Carpal Tunnel Syndrome and suck it up when buying ammo. FunCom's 'fix' may not be what you like.
