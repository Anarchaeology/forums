THE AGREEMENTS

Upon cursory examination, all three agreements are copies of documents originally printed on ICC letterhead, but this amounts to little. Given how miserably easy it is to procure illicit ICC items, such as the famed ICC cloaks, it is relatively obvious that letterhead would not be too difficult to obtain. Furthermore, as these documents are reproductions, they lack the noticeable security measures (such as watermarks, etc.) generally found on official documents.

Counterparties

The agreements variously list the following counterparties as signatories, as noted:
(i) Sol Banking Corporation (SBC)  all agreements;
(ii) TruSpace Networks (TS) - all agreements;
(iii) The Council of Truth (the CoT) - all agreements; and
(iv) the Interstellar Confederation of Corporations (the ICC)  agreements I and III.

I was initially taken back by the absolute absurdity of these unlikely partnerships, for a number of reasons. For example, the Clans assisted OT in battling SBC after SBC landed troops on May 19, 29418, as well as the open discord between the ICC and SBC following the ICCs delaying the arrival of peacekeepers on Rubi-Ka.

Furthermore, it is extremely unlikely that SBC and TS would contract with the Council of Truth as well as the ICC, in light of the Clans Rights Treaty dated as of July 4, 29223, which provides a limited lease on certain of the lands of Rubi-Ka from OT. It is quite unlikely that the Council would have trusted the opportunism of SBC and TS to honor this treaty, given their obvious distrust for OT (and indeed, all corporate entities).

Couple this with the fact that agreement II details a plan for the overthrow of OT on Rubi-Ka, titled The Dawn of Sol, and the two corporations sincerity, (especially SBCs) is cast into tremendous doubt. This is reinforced by the fact that CoT member clans New Dawn, Terra Firma, Gaia, and particularly and especially the Unionists, would never in their rightful minds contract either for the purposes outlined in these agreements, let alone acquiesce to assisting other corporations. More on my reasoning can be found here.

What is interesting to note within these documents (specifically, agreement II), is that, as consideration on its part, the CoT agrees to supply manpower for the battle against OT  in the form of the Dust Brigade. The audacity of this contractual term virtually cancels any su****ions of the legitimacy of these agreements, as I quite frankly do not believe that Clans New Dawn and Gaia would support the violent actions of the Dust Brigade, given their peaceful, pacifist nature. The Dust Brigade has claimed the lives (permanently, I might add) of almost as many clan-affiliated citizens as OT employees, and, notwithstanding the actions of two secretaries within the CoT, most are quite eager to see the Dust Brigade punished severely for its actions.

ICC INVOLVEMENT?

Perhaps the most damning evidence against the agreements legitimacy lies in the fact that the drafters failed to take account of the structure of the ICC and the manner in which it operates.

The ICC is composed of a total of 263 member-corporations, of which only 7 are classified as Hyper-Corporations. This is indeed a large number with vastly different interests among them. All corporations, regardless of size, post delegations to the ICCs General Assembly, which is the principal and sole decision-making body of the ICC in ordinary times. The secretary-general is merely its figurehead and principal, and lacks the personal authority to bind the full 263 corporations to any decision she makes herself.

The only way the secretary-general can exercise exclusive, binding powers over the ICC is by invocation of the Emergency Powers Act, which was used during the Corporate Wars of 29389, and more recently in August of last year as a result of the destabilizing political situation on Rubi-Ka.

Furthermore, the ICC does not itself engage in any business; this would be a gross conflict of interest on the ICCs part. As such, it lacks resources of its own. The ICCs operations are supported by member-contributions (the allocation and structure of which is currently unknown), and as OT is the largest and most profitable corporation in the known universe, it is logical to assume that a great number of said resources were donated by OT.

Given that the Isidra Derousse lacked any emergency power at the time of these contracts execution, if she is indeed party to any of them (I note that a reproduction of her signature appears on the second agreement as witness thereto), she acted on her own, and was not in a position to bind the ICC.

(Side note: even when the Emergency Powers Act has been invoked, the ICC cant take unilateral action that is not supported by member corporations. This is evidenced by the fact that the ICC was still compelled to put a verdict to vote in the general assembly, despite the operation of the Emergency Powers Act.)

It is thus reasonable to conclude that the ICC as a whole and as a legal entity is in no manner involved herewith.

Preliminary Conclusions

Although I have yet to completely set the second part of this analysis in writing, it is worthy to note that I can no longer support the actions of Mr. Damon Demonantous Fadri, and my involvement in this matter is at an end.
