Returning
I quit 1 month after pay for play.

I have since returned and the game feels the same except it is easier to get around. I am looking forward to messing with the tradeskill stuff as that is something I have always liked Before the game was released I was pictureing myself as a enginer that has next to no tools and was makeing gizmos for my clan buddies.

Not much else has really changed that I have noticed. Well I don't get those blue question mark things anymore. The game plays basically the same way it did before it is just less buggy.

As much as I like these improvments the game still seems empty to me. I know it has to be lack of RP. The game right now is just running around and blasting anything that upsets you. It is difficult to engage anyone in conversation as most people are running tho their next mission or refuse to talk to anyone outside their team. The game is alot less social than it was when it first started. I have ready and watched the storyline released by funcom and I am impressed. MY problem is I don't see how we are supposed to effect the outcome of anything. All missions that I have seen are randomly generated. I can't just go up to a important NPC and kill them because I don't believe this will have an effect on the story (If it is even possible to find them)

Without RP I cannot enjoy this game. This was my problem with UO. RP was very rare. It is hard to imerse yourself in a game when people are talking about their computers and their RL jobs.

I am curious as to where the RPers are. I am not a hardcore RPer by any means. I enjoy going out and shooting things to a extent. I just want to be involved in the world and I see no way to really do it. My omnitek character might benifit from funcom opening the departments like Omni-pol.
