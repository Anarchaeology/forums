Soldier Improvement Ideas:
I don't play a soldier, so for one, I'm not heavily going into the
reflect shields or whatever stacking with TMS. (I actually think
they should, only because the reflect aspect of TMS was nerfed.)

To begin, I've noticed the following problems with soldiers:

1)ATK rating hurts when you're forced to use a 33/66 weapons. It
is one of the main reasons I hated Ranee's and combos, as an
agent, aside from the fact that the ranee had 5 round clips.

So, we'll address that. Simple solutions.

2)No healing. (Join the club, guys!) This can be improved, but
honestly, your defenses are very good, with shields and such.
Armor with small evasive mods, and further shielding could help.
But I *will* add a few ideas on additional healing for you.

3)FA is nerfed. (Hey, a fix for that is beyond my comprehension.)
I see my lv136 team-mate/guild-mate hit for 250-580 Fa's, and
wonder just what the heck happened to that stat. Of course,
when it actually crits, it's damn good, but as you know, we cannot
rely on crits anymore, and it's all about DoT and reliable damage.

4)Soldier HP's suffer. Now, with this, I cannot understand. Who
ever it was that said this is mistaken. The fact is you have a 5-pt.
modifer for this, right? Same as other tanking classes. What
seperates you from Ma's is healing. And you from ENF's is the
massive HP buffs that they get. Instead of going either way in an
extreme way, you should find more soldier like ways to improve
yourselves, and balance out your unfair recovery shortcomings.

Hell, even mongo acts as a heal. (Continuing on...)

5)Minimum damage is a bit low. (Same with AGT's.) Why is an
assault rifle having a minimum damage of 1? These are devestating
pieces of weaponry. Your regular attacks should amount to more.
Excellent base damage is a tank trademark. You ARE tanks, but
you're admitably lacking some of the atributes of one.

Anyway, I'm going to prepose some fixes, and item ideas for you..

Just remember, though! I'm an agent/MA by trade. I have no
soldier characters, but I'd like to try an add any useful input that
I can for you guys. You need help, and I hope one day you'll
return the favor by looking out for me, one day when the time comes.

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Regarding ATK rating, this is simple. Fixed in two ways, each
equally important to the other, therefore they should co-exist.

1)Pure Assault Rifle guns.
2)Pure Ranged Energy guns.

3)Mid/High end Assault Rifle buffs.
4)Mid/High end Ranged Energy buffs.

Now, to stop here.

Depending on the new pure weapons stat types, each weapon
buff should include a special attack buff. Like fixers have buffs
for SMG/Burst. Well, Assault Rifle, in my mind, is more designed
for Full Auto. Ranged Energy is more for flingshot. Riot for burst.

Oh, yes. And a Low/Mid level burst buff. Riot Control is up there.
We need to have one lower accessible by lv45-50, and another
that can be used around 100, to spread things out.

I'm going to "sketch' you new soldier weapon types. By balancing
speeds and recharges, range and clip size. And special, too, I
hope these are pleasing solutions for your weapon needs. And try
and remember, these are only 1 of each. You need a good 5
solutions in each field, to actually diversify your weapon pools.

New assault rifles, and ranged energy cannons should look like:

(R-E.)
Morthotech Heated-Particle Ejector:

QL200:

Requirements:

Ranged Energy - 975
Flingshot - 605
Burst - 590

Attack: 3.2
Recharge: 2.0
Range: 24m
Clip: 48 Flamethrower Ammo.
Damage type: Fire.

Damage: 10-502(435)

Now, this gives a great recharge on your specials, which are the
two fastest cycling ranged ones, while mainaining a fair amount
of attack speed. Great clip for a soldier gun. This gun also follows
FC's trend of staying below 500 with the crit mod, and increasing
the actual regular damage. Now, many non-soldiers would say,
why the hell is it fire damage? 10 is still low base damage. This
will not help crits in any way, but it WILL improve the base DMG
a soldier does on a mob. It'll also improve regular damage, too.

A gun like this doesn't NEED 600 regular damage, when it does
FIRE damage. It'll naturally hit for a bit more, due to weak Fire AC.

To move on, here's the Pure Assault Rifle.

Morthotech PUR-4200a Auto-Cannon:

QL200:

Requirements:

Assault Rifle - 980
Full Auto - 640
Burst - 580

Attack: 2.15
Recharge: 2.65
Range: 30m
Clip: 30 Energy
Damage type: Energy.

Damage: 25-618(265)

Now. Fast gun, pounding out the quick but HARD hitting energy.
A 'balancing' recharge on the specials, awesome clip considering
it'd be a HUGE waste of ammo with a big BIG clip. Good range,
and not unholy FA reqs, assuming the new Ar/Fa buffs come in.

Okay, both designs do many things for you:

1) -(With buffs)- Aid in your ATK rating issues, by being made a
PURE weapon, on both sides of the ball. Not dropping one or the
other, we opt to have two choice routes, each beneficial.

2)Helps out BIG with base damage issues. A soldier is trained in
hitting and making the most out of that hit. This is how it should
be considering they ARE tanks by design.

Okay, weapons and buffs taken care of.

(I realize I left out the buffs, but mid/low level on burst, and high
down to mid(100 or so) on AR/RE should go with the 70-ish mid,
and 132-ish high end trends. It's that simple...)

Healing:

My idea is a HUD item, that rocks. You use it like an item, much like
agent repressors, and it hits you with a cool lil' HoT. It'll require
First Aid, so new soldier armors (Can be issued, if requested.)
will have to add a nice little First Aid mod. Maybe a soldiers very
own first aid buff, that also buffs a small heal delta. (Like 10.)

The item will go by QL, but is not found in every QL like repressors.
It will be found like TMS nanos, in that it'll have a new one for
certain stages of a soldier's life. Each jump in QL, of course, extends
it's duration, and lowers it's recharge/lock skill times. Capice?

QL10 Soldier Field Rations MK1. (Tentatively titled, heh..) (15)
QL25 Soldier Field Rations MK2. (25)

QL180 Soldier Field Rations MK9. (250)
QL200 Soldier Field Rations MK10. (325)
(Post-QL200 content, included for high end game.)
QL225 Soldier Field Rations MK-XI (375)
QL250 Soldier Field Rations MK-XII (450)

Use the item, it locks first aid for 5 minutes. The actual HoT will
only last for 1 to 2.5 minutes, depending on the QL. For balance
sake, it should also lock nano skills for 1 minute, but this is a ricky
idea. If it has to be, then so be it, if it's not unbalancing, forget it.

There you go. Healing, but not to a whine-fest fueling extreme.

...Okaaaay, well, we'll have to see what happens there. What we
CAN work on is HP's. I think they're fine. With the previously
mentioned HoT item, you don't need an MA's healing, or a ENF's
huge HP's. You HAVE good HP's, fair evades, you just need a small
bit of recovery *ON TOP* of your defensive shielding.. good?

Speaking of: Reflect should stack with Shielding. Simple as that.
No new kinds of reflects or new shields, they're great, as is. They
just don't maximize your ability, unless used together.

The rest of it for you guys is a content problem. No special mission
or any special soldier gear. It's content. I think what I suggested
is about all a soldier could ask for in a love patch, as this is much
better than any other profession has faired, for the most part.
