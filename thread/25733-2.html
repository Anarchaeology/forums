Well, I came, saw (for 7 days), and started paying.

I am a rpg fanatic, and I have taken a lot of time to choose a mmorpg I would start playing. Diablo 2 didn't quite meet the requirements anymore. Actually, it didn't meet them for quite a while, but I just continued playing coz it became a habbit to log in, meet with ppl I knew, and start doing some levels.

I was really amazed when I found out how many skills and abilities AO had, and how linked they are with each other. Not to mention the amount of professions that are available.

However, I already noticed that there does seem to be a lack of motives to continue playing. (Main problem in D2) Sure, finding weapons and improoving skills is a motiv, but it is not role playing. There must be more to it than selecting a target, and eliminating it - with or without team. But then again, I started many, many characters, and therefore didn't make it to a high (or medium) level yet. From what I've heard, there is little more to expect aside from items, and stats. Okay, exploring new areas is an interesting option - but that won't last.

From what I've seen so far, leveling and getting the best buffs to wear the best items is all people tend to think about.
That can't be it if this is an rpg game. Maybe I expect too much (probably), or maybe you start playing a role with a 3 digit level.
Either way, I am far from being a high level, so I hope I havent seen the best of the game yet.

Diablo 2 may be the most boring game around now, but I doubt I will get the thrill of rpg from AO like I did with Diablo 2 the first time I played through the story. Everything else is way better in AO, of course. Especially the love for detail...

PS: This probably sounds like I dislike the game, but I'm just thinking out loud AO is a long term game, so it will take time, which I have already paid for.

As for Bronto_Cowboy: I want a story too, but If you didn't find it, and stopped looking for it..... Then what R U doing here? Play Diablo 2! It has a good story. If you visualize AO without all of the pretty graphics and just think about the code behind it, it's probably one of the least impressive games ever made.
