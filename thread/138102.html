The End
Tikon stands in his apartment looking out to see the early morning sun rise, Two Omni-Med medics are standing in the background quietly chatting.

Tikon slowly and happily smiles as the sun rises over the valley
"Im ready now" he says softly.

Tikon walks over to his bed that overlooks the lush fields valley's, he slowly lais down so that he can see the beuty of the morning one last time.

"Okay Tikon, are you ready"? the medic says gently
"Yes" says Tikon whispering

With that Tikon lies there staring at the sun with a smile on his face, the medic begins to inject a lethal serum into his vains.

"Goodbye Tikon" the medic says quietly and softly

Tikon turns to look at the photographs that he has on his side table, there are pictures there of him with Omni-Pol all standing there smiling, He slowly closes his eyes with his head facing the picture.

One last soft breath is drawn, he slowly drifts away..............................
