A Valentine
The other Day i founf this Extremely old Poem well Protected in a layer of plastic, its about a Valentine and since its the day i shall share it with you.

A Valentine
For Her this rhymr is penned, whoes Luminous eyes,
Brightly expressive as the twins of Læda
Shall find her own sweet name, that, nestling lies
Upon the page, enwrapped from every reader.
Seach narrowly the lines!-The hold a Treasure
Divine-a talisman-an amulet
That Must be worn at heat. Seach well the measure-
The words-the syllables! Do not forget
The trivialest point, or you may lose your labor!
And yet there is no Gordian knot
Wich one might not undo without sabre,
If one could merely compreheld the plot.
Enwritten upon the leaf when now are peering
Eyes' scintillating soul, There lie Perdus
Tree eloquent words oft uttered in the hearing
Of poets, By poets-as the name is a poet's too.
Its letters, although naturally lying
Like the Knight Pinto-Mendez Fedinando-
Still form a synonym of Truth.-Cease trying!
you will not read the riddle, though you do the best you can do.

There is a Riddle in this strange peom, if any of you can see the logic please tell me what it is, so i might rest in peace agian.
