Red Tiger's Beach Party!
If you want a break from pounding mobs in missions. Or relax after another trip to reclaim. Or maybe you want to see old "friends" whose towers you've demolished. Then look no further! We have just the thing for you.

The Red Tigers are throwing a beach party! Thats right! What better way to cool off during this heatwave that has washed over Rubi-Ka, then to take a dip in the cool waters in Wine. The fun will start around 19:00 GMT (2pm EST) on Saturday August 9th. Since this is outdoors, you need only wompah there. Everyone is invited. Clan, Neutral, and Omni. All proffs! Come one, come all!

We ask that you please wear social armor. Might I suggest a bathing suit... or some light colored clothing. Anyhow, if you wear armor, how will you swim? Imagine all the rust spots if you do wear armor in the water! Sunblock is probably a good idea too. And please bring your own towels. The monogramed Guid towels are for Red Tigers only.

We are hoping to get GSP to DJ this event once again. Katieblue did an awesome job last party! I shall announce who the DJ will be as soon as I know myself.

See you all there!

P.S. We are on RK-1 (Atlantean)
