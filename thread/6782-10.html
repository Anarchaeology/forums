Ok, this is what I've read on these forums from other people that have experimented with weapons. Whether you're dual wielding or using weapons with fists, only one weapon cat attack at a time.

By this I mean if you're punching and using a Taichi, the taichi will only attack while the fist recharges and vice versa. So if you get your physical init to 150 and melee init to 400, then on full aggr, you will punch (1 sec). Then while the punch is recharging, the taichi will hit attack (1 sec).
