Tir Moat Swim
Tired of the same old dance, DJ and fashion contests? Come to the First Annual Tir Moat swim! Tell your friends! Tell your family! Heck, you can even tell complete strangers!

Display your swimming prowess by paddling around the moat within the city of Tir in protest of the constant dumping of toxic substances into that fair city's water table. This swim race will take place next Saturday, the 12th of January, 2002, at 22:00 GMT (17:00EST, 14:00PST, 23:00CET).

The race will consist of three brackets, the first for those of title level one, second of title levels two and three, and third for title levels four and above. The race will start on the ramp just north of the southern gate of Tir, with the competitors swimming forward around the central island. The first race will have only two laps, the second bracket three laps, and the third four laps. If needed, swimmers may swim around the Mongol Meat island after each lap to get a free heal, in case the moat's toxicity is too overpowering for some.

The only rules are No vehicles and No going on land. If you are caught walking on land or using a vehicle during the race, you will be disqualified. Anyone may compete in the race, but to be eligible for a prize you must register for your bracket - /tell Scrub or Razishlyat ingame, or catch us in #ao-basher IRC chat to register. You are only eligible to win a prize in your title level bracket.

Prizes are to be announced, but include hanshi coats, nano cloaks, luck rings, IQ rings, and cold hard cash! The first place winner in each bracket will have the first choice of the three prizes for that bracket, the second place winner will choose between whatever the first place winner didn't select, and the third place winner will get what's left. The awards ceremony will take place atop the big fan on the west side of the central island - Even if you're not competing, you can just hang out on the railing watching the lowbies dying! *snicker*

There will also be a prize of 50,000 credits awarded to the best looking swimmer! So wear your hottest bikinis, ladies(though its fabric will probably be dissolved in the toxic moat-water )!

BTW, it's on RK1.
