Here is a list of clan and neutral shops. Found it not too long ago somewhere on these boards; I think the author was probably Brad STL/MO.

There are different ways to classify shops:

1.By the "quality" of the products sold inside. For this classification, the grades are Basic, Advanced and Superior. For the highest QLs, go to a Superior shop. Be aware that very little (if any) gear above QL125 is sold in shops.

2.By the type of products sold inside. Here, shop refers to a "zone". So there are supermarkets, which contain many machines selling loads of different products, as well as shops having only a single human merchant, as well as special shops. Special shops sell vehicles, furniture and I believe there is a trader only terminal at these as well. In the list I linked to, this is what is meant by a shop (rather than a specific merchant).

3.If you think of a shop as a specific merchant you do business with, then they can be classified as machines and humans. Typically you will find multiple merchant machines close to each other (supermarkets, special shops) though occasionally you will find a single machine in an outdoors area. Machines base their prices on your Computer Literacy skill. Human merchants are normally on their own, not too close to other human merchants (at most two or three within sight). They base their prices on your Psychology skill (I think). It may also be that humans can only deal with one customer at a time.

Some nano programs are not available from shops. If you're looking for a doctor nano your best bet is to check the supermarket of the appropriate level. If it's not there, you'll have to find it yourself (probably in a mission) or buy it from another player.
