The crashed ship in front of Omni-1 has been left purposely, as a reminder to all Employees of the atrocities the rogue Clans are guilty of. Look to it as a point of motivation when you are faced with cowardly Clan terrorist tactics. Think of those who died when that ship was shot down. Their needless deaths must not be in vain, nor should they go unavenged.

As for your search for knowledge Yonoko, I would suggest waiting until Omni-Pol can conduct an official investigation into the matter. There is no way as of yet to know what is out there, and it may prove quite dangerous to our beloved Employees. However, it is common knowledge that the ship predates our terraforming of the planet. How and when it crashed here we do not know at this time. HOWEVER, be rest assured that there will be a full investigation conducted immediately, and we will uncover what, if any, evidence that will lead us to a conclusion as to what exactly this ship is, what it contained, and when it crashed here.
