Swap option: your pvp title for your Title-level title.
This thought struck me today, but when I hit TL6 (soon hopefully) I want to walk around and have Saint Meurgen floating over my head.

I would love an option to have either your PvP title showing, or your title level title. I personally could care less about whichever pvp title I have above my head, and those folks who are actually proud of Neophyte or something similar could indeed show it off. Anyone who doesn't want to could have their Title level name as a prefix to their real name.
