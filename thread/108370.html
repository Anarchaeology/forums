Hi everybody,

We'd like to know how you all feel about people having numbers in their names in Anarchy Online, e.g. Czeffyr666, Atrox12 and Omnih8.

Is this acceptable names to you? Should it be allowed, or stopped? Let us know your stand on this, and whether we should change it. Note than any change is very unlikely to mess with existing character names.
