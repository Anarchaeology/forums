I favor hunting the 'borgs in PW. I haven't tried Mort yet.

Since the advent of NW, I stay away from the town where they roam around. Instead, I run down the road, and there are many at various intervals.

They're usually far enough apart that you don't have to worry about adds.

What I like to do is to inch forward until I'm just in range, to help minimize the adds. If possible, I draw them back to the road. This way, if I do get an add, I kite them back and forth, up and down the road. I'll area snare them, then just backpedal, stopping occasionally to shoot. The constant stopping and starting seems to help minimize, or prevent, them from shooting.

The thing is to do the kiting in a controlled area. Don't run a long way up and down the road, just where you know there aren't any others to join in.

I'm loathe to fight mobs, especially "non-human" mobs that melee. I find they attack too fast . Human mobs with weapons, like the cyborgs, tend to attack at a rate manageable by our HoTs.

I primarily fight these because I want the CDS armor. I don't go after anything that's deep into the red. Orange to red-orange. I'm not in it for the XP.

For anyone of the level where the Avalon Cyborg camp would be a good draw, know that it was frequently camped (when I first decided to look for CDS) by level 150-200 clanners, usually from (you guessed it) Storm. They don't feel the inclination to share. Also, since the mobs there are in a smaller, enclosed area, you (or the puller for the group) should pull them out onto that flat ridge that runs up to the entrance to the camp. You should not attack a mob to pull it here, unless it is very well isolated from all the other 'borgs. You will get 4 or 5 if you do. In order to get as few as possible, you should run in close enough to the one you want to draw, and wait for it to notice you, then draw it out. If your auto-attack is on, you might fire back and get the aforementioned 4 or 5. It's a fun place to practice pulling, and you might get some CDS as a reward.

Learn the vagaries of LOS and mobs will almost never warp on you. Well, as long as you're fighting on anything resembling flat terrain. Also, don't fight huge mobs with large combat ranges. Small melee mobs best; anuns, mantises, the like. Kiting pit lizard bosses = bad.

Don't wear your reflection bracers if you're trying to use root crowd-control. In fact, it might be best to cancel your reflection soldier buffs if you have any, but only if you foresee root CC being more important than losing that utility in a one-on-one fight.

Dynaboss mobs can be good xp in the title 4 range, if you just run around doing a cycle of them in PW or something. They're great right up till you run into one of the HP-Booster ones; the bastards that take 45+ minutes to kill. They're usually not worth the time; I only take them out occasionally to reset their spawn or just to make sure I still can.

Best way to solo at high level is to get your best snare. Spin Nanoweb and cast it on mobs, once they all aggro you, hide somewhere so you can go out of sight and they will try to reach you but their run speed is low that they are almost rooted. After hiding for a few seconds, come out and start shooting. The mob priority is to reach that area where you were hidden once they reach it they will shoot at you but use that time to get some free shots. A high level fixer can solo ranged mob without even losing a single HP when he use that technique. Its easy and it works very well. You can also do it in the boss room if theres a place to go out of sight.
