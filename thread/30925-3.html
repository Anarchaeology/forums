I reached 1000 tokens at the same time that my main character turned Level 97.
Not one of those tokens was collected due to an exploit/cheat or illegal trade.
Every single one of those was collected from completing a mission.
I had my 400 token board whilst each token was worth one point, not up to 7 like they are now.

The people that cannot always find teams or do not always have the time to play in teams do a lot of missions.
If is how we make our credits, it is how we get items, it is how we play a lot of the game.
However a few patches ago us mission players were royally screwed.
Variable token rewards were added along with one of the best lines I've ever heard from funcom:
You should not consider this a devaluation of current tokens
Ummm, yer, right, unfortunately I did and lets be honest, it was.

The reason that change was added?
Well a few people, actually quite a lot decided to play the "First one to Level 200 gets to quit the game first" competition and that was their choice.
They decided that they wanted to play the game in that style, level as quickly as possible and have an advantage over us lower level characters.
We mission players had a slight advantage though because our missions tended to be smaller and slightly easier.
It was all nice and fair, either rush through your levels and find it hard to get your token board or play missions, get your tokens but always be a lot of levels behind.

Then the whiners came out of the woodwork with "But we want the best of both worlds" and low and behold the changes were made.

Now, let us take out of the equation those that exploited/cheated/traded/farmed 1000 tokens.
I think when we consider the whole player base these are actually few and far between.
However as somebody else has already said they will have got their 1000 tokens and that was it, they probably haven't collected another single token since.
Factor in that by normal game play you are almost guaranteed to have 1000 tokens by around Level 100, lets consider any extension of the current token system a Level 100+ feature.
I am not saying only allow players Level 100+ to participate, I'm saying that cheats & exploiters aside this is when it will come into play.

We have a bunch of people who do not want the addition of new token boards and I'm sure they have their good reasons for this and please post them.
However what does not makea good "against" argument is the following:

1. I will find it difficult to get any higher boards
A. Tough, you chose your style. Please understand that just because you will find it difficult should not mean that others can give it a go. Lastly please understand you do NOT have to won every single item in the game.

2. Why should people who solo missions have an advantage over me, a team player?
A. Then compaign and ask Funcom to add something for you team hunters.
Don't attempt to take potentially something away from us because your play styple doesn't fit in with ours.

3. They shouldn't be "ubber" boards because that would be unfair.
A. No it would not, everybody has got the chance to get as many tokens as they like. Even if you are Level 200 you can still gain tokens, it's just you'll find it a little more difficult.
However even if a mission takes you two hours you'll still get 7 tokens at the end of it, you are still on a level playing field.
If you really want it you'll get it.

So, how good should these new boards be?
Well there were some nice suggestions about new boards being cosmetic only, shows you've collected x amount of tokens but doesn't really add any new features over the 1000 board.
I'm sorry but I don't believe in this approach.
That takes us back to the crowd complaining about "ubber" items that they find difficult to get.
I feel that the 1000 board is our base board (which it will be for the majority of 100+ players) and we can work on that.
Start increasing other skills with higher boards, maybe add to NCU's, add to AC that kind of thing.

I am not going to farm for more tokens, I'm currently Level 119 with 1250 tokens and all of these have been gained through normal gameplay.
However I would like to see my mission efforts rewarded.
Maybe after you've uploaded 1000 tokens to your board you can then only upload "special tokens" which do come from missions but have different requirements to gaining, having to do the mission without dying, having to explore 100% of the mission, having to open all locked doors/chests etc.
That idea would have to be explored.

Alternatively Funcom go to the other extreme, make it so that when we try to upload any tokens past 1000 we get the message "You cannot upload any further tokens to this board" and then force us to delete them.
Why?
Because if these additional to 1000 tokens are not going to be of any use to use then stop "baiting us" and "dangling the carrot" in front of our eyes.
At least then we would know it is time to totally change our playstyles to that of other players.
