Feeling detached?
Just wanted to comment on the storyline so far in AO. Is anyone else feeling rather detached from it? I mean when you look at the animated series (which is done very well I might add)) you get a good sense of character and story and environment, even trucks that have names (corky) however in-game, things seem to fall flat, the storyline is supposed to be centered around the players, but it seems to me that players have little to no effect on things. I kill cyborgs all day in mort, and the storyline comes up and wow lookie here, need to kill more cyborgs in area X (just for example)
not my idea of fun. Rubi-ka is supposed to be a place of the future, but oddly enough devoid of any vehicle traffic or even buildings with windows. I know I know, having vehicles zipping through the streets will make lag even worse. Aside from that however, why not give the players something to actually do? Could be anything, hold elections for certain offices, giving the select players who win these certain powers and what not. assign people to play as the NPCs we hear so much about (Phillip Ross having a drink at rompa?) surrounded by warbots or something, anything to make it interesting.


I'm sure other players will agree that things so far are very limited as far as player interaction is concerned. If FC plans to make this game last for the next 3 and a half years, give us something to do besides power level and twiddle our thumbs.
