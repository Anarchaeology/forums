Well, no more waiting.

The pvp-formular is the same used widely in chess and backgammon also known as the ELO-system. If you want it in details you can read more here: http://www.vogclub.com/ratings/elodetails.phtml


I am not gonna explain in details what this mean, but I can explain a bit.

The more pvp-points you have the less you get from killing a person with low or none pvp-points. Actually you can loose points from killing a non-titled (mind that I don't know if Funcom implemented the system that strict), and that might explain some of the weird stuff happening sometimes with people loosing novice after killing a person.

The higher title you get the harder it gets for you to advance in titles, until there are more with same title or higher to kill. You can advance by killing lower titled, but the lower the less you gain.


I am pretty sure this is how the system works. It seems logic and explains some of the weird things happening now and then with titles. A lot of the titles even follow the chess-system. And I think you can be quite sure that the highest pvp-title is grandmaster. And the title after Neophyte is either amateur or experienced.

There you have it
Mind that this is all unconfirmed info I found myself. :P
