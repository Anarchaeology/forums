Hello,

AO has improved a lot since launch, I should know since I've been playing since then

1. Yes, both dimensions are located in the US

2. Lag, some problems...mostly in the major cities (trade, ent and some of the clan cities). Outside of these major zones it's fine really, at least for me on a 64k (ISDN) connection from Europe.

Zoning issues are 99% gone; I haven't had a problem zoning in..hmm..a month or two? And I used to crash to desktop everytime I zoned before, now it's just peachy

3. Don't know much about Martial Artists, but there was a problem with people hitting for minimum damage all the time on mobs - this was fixed. All I know is that an MA is one of the best professions to play right now, based on the reports of my MA guildmates.

There are still issues with the MA profession, but nothing gamebreaking.

4. New ideas
Every profession is getting a major upgrade right now, starting with Metaphysicians (they really rock now, at least compared to before) and I think everyone's going to be happy with the new changes.

They have added two static dungeons to the game, and one more is coming this week (probably) with patch 13.4. I've seen screenshots from the new dungeon and it looks really good.

Static missions are coming in 13.4 (aka quests), but I don't know much about them yet.

Organizations are finally starting to work as planned, we are still missing tax, salary and upkeep, but they are coming soon.

There's a lot more coming, and I suggest taking a look at the in progress and envisioned parts of the official site. Some of the most awaited features right now are team missions (with rewards to everyone in the team) and of course the profession upgrades.
