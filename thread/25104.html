Traders are a lot more difficult to play than people imagine, mostly because of the fact that people start them as alts or twinks.

A trader has to keep nanoformulas a class above the ones they can execute with their base skills to remain adequate. This means 1 of 3 options: 1) Taking missions with teams above the level they can reasonably survive solo, 2) Buying nanos priced for the mission rewards above their level 3) Depending on the higher level players in their orgs or their higher level alts. When I rerolled into my current trader I kept 2 things: my Pioneer backpack and my tradeskill tools (was rerolling from an Adventurer). Nothing else and it was painful.

Traders using shotguns are highly dependant on critical damage, with the Vektor being one of the few shotguns that do adequate damage without relying on crits. Post 14.2, don't expect to see crazy crit damage from the Trader in your midst without having a MA along. I learned to play my trader "straight", without outside buffs (including LLTS cause I hated the nightvision effect) before doing anything. This forced me to use every nano I could (health haggles, nano drains, and AC drains as well) to improve survivability.

Trader nano duration is either the results of pure genious or the results of a demented mind like no other. If a trader depends on both Ransack and Deprive, he's guarranteed to go at least 7 seconds before he can rebuff to maximum power. And nanoresist works extremely well the Plunder line. So the Divest is always running out before the Plunder, the time depending on the Divest 6 second recharge + however executions you wasted on resists.
