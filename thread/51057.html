Simple solution.
The usage of a simple calculator comes to mind...

Simply, take the AC value of the QL200 armor. Let's say this is 1000, for simplicity's sake. You want to find out what the QL140 version is like?

1000 * (140/200) = 700

The AC of the QL140 piece of armor is, in other words, 700.

Repeat this with the other armor stats/types to your heart's content.
