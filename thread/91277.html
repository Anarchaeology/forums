FC wanted longer fights and it is working GREAT!

1. CC (The fight starts)

3. You have to yalm back (if you can't grid. Professions who cant grid enjoy the mass pvp more, because they spend LOTS of time because of this.)

4. When you arrive, you LD. And while they are at it, take down atleast 10 of the attackers. That way the towers cant be taken down quickly. If the leading side isdefending, they LD the defending side so that the attacking side can get more exp%

5. You come back from LD after trying to log in for 5 mins. YAY - It makes the action last longer!! It takes about 1min before you can move and then what happens? Oh yes, check next point.

6. You get killed by the enemy!! Now you are at reclaim, waiting quite some minutes to get in shape. This can be done while running/yalming/gridding to the tower. To make it more fun, you get a quick LD when entering grid too.

7. When you finally are buffed and ready to go the zone goes 75%.

(For extra stimulation: When you ld, you can go check forums(as I do) while trying to log on. That way you have the time to put in some words about how much you love NW )
