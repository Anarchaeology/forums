What's up? (August 22nd 2003)
Friday already? Well, well, well. What do we have this week?

15.0.1 on test-live
The pre-Shadowlands update (15.0.1) has been moved to test-live, with all game changes for Shadowlands that will affect everybody. I leaked some of the content two weeks ago, but there's quite a bit more there to be seen, and tested.

We also added a new 15.0.1 forum under the Test Server forum, to keep the feedback and bug reports on this specific update apart from other discussions.

Ripped databases
Of course, the sites displaying ripped databases have the 15.0.1 database up and running by now, and "everybody" have seen that it contains the current version of Shadowlands items and nano programs. This of course starts a lot of speculations and discussions.

While discussion is good, please understand that this is not the final versions of everything, and you're also not seeing the full picture in those databases. The testers are still bound by an NDA, and thus can't really correct people who jump to the wrong conclusions. So try keep a cool head, and don't go off on a rant. If you want to participate in beta discussions, join the beta.

---

That's really all I have this week. Have a nice weekend!
