first off, i'd like to state that i like the overall idea of balancing the game, but i would warn at the same time not to look past the identities of the professions while doing so. and yes, by this I mainly mean MA's (i'm biased, so sue me ) MA's were the original crit machines, and now in my view (along with agents and their new crit nanos), they are the only ones "proficient" in critting for dmg. that means they have a higher chance than everyone else correct? well, under this new proposed system, no, they dont. simple illustration of maximum long duration crit mods -

MA - UVC (24) + TTS (4) = 28%

ranged prof other than MA = ql200 llts (15) + MoP (7) + TTS (4) = 26%

melee prof other than MA = MoP (7) + TTS (4) = 11%

this is assuming that all scopes (vision enhancers too) will not work with melee users, but that is not certain tho. anyway, as you see, the difference is negligible in the first two, and my goodness look at those poor other melee users! this change accomplishes nothing IMO, you still have a large chunk of the population that can achieve high crit amounts (FC does not want this correct?), and the other melee classes are put at an unfair disadvantage.

now let's try and get to something that works. it seems that FC is anti-crit, so i will assume that a lesser maximum crit chance by a majority of the population is ultimately what is desired. here are all the points thusfar i like to make this possible :

1) UVC line, MoP line, and FoB melee only, with bows added for the MA archer possiblity. this all makes sense, MA's know how to crit due to their intense training in the various martial arts disiplines, so this knowledge should only work for those sorts of attacks, and the knowledge we impart (MoP line) should be also. FoB is self explainatory - it's "blows," not bullets

2) Scopes only for ranged users (I'd say all of them). Where exactly on your bare fist are you supposed to place a scope anyway?

3) LLTS needs to be reduced in crit chance in the higher ql. Sorry, but 15% from an item is way too much. I'd say they must match the vision enhancer amounts of right now (7-8% tops). Not only is this a reasonable number as far as FC's "quest" to lower crit chances, but also it will be fair in comparison to the non-MA melee users.

4) Make TTS agent-only if you think it adds to the problem of high lvl crit amounts. Not only will it reduce it, but it will give the agents a chunk of their identity back as well (both crit and AS).

I know this is not what ppl want to hear, but its the only way i can see FC keeping the crit profs the way they are meant to be while balancing everyone else and lowering the overall crit chance. If they think that a higher crit amount is ok, then just tweak the numbers a bit, just make sure not to approach the UVC amount too closely, or you risk ruining the whole MA crit identity (such that it is today )
