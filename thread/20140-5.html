As some of my studies includes sound design and engineering, i've looked into the soundfiles and sounddessign og AO with great interest. Making a guess i think 90%+ of the sound files is used.

The diffeculty with sound is if you add to much of it, it can very soon gets tiresome or be make it all cartoonish.

The design and use of the soundfiles in AO is used very nicely IMO, but there is several designs that you very fast hear is looped. In combat i agree with others here that more effects could be added.

Myself i play with the sound off mostly, listening to radio or music instead, but i have the sound in earphones on the table so i can hear when shots are fired.

The only times i usually have the sound on, is when i solo missions as MP with pets. Pet pathing we all know is somewhat broken, so i found out as long as my healpet is in same room as me i can hear its slow breathing. Thus using sound i know the pet is there and dont have to turn aroound and look up. This is really good and we need more of it, useful information to the player in the sounddesign.

About the yottos song, there is several norwegian phrases in it. The 'dumdeda..' is just singing of sounds, but some of those 'ett to ett to' sounds is norwegian for 'one two one two'
Song_05 is ended with 'left shoe right shoe'
song_06 is actually the most funny as the lyric 'mil etter mil', english 'mile after mile' is taken from a norwegian singer, Jan Teigen, who is maybe best known for getting 0 point in the Europeen Melody Grand Prix, for those familar with that event. He usually dont suck that much and have actually done some very fun stuff too
