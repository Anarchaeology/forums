The forecast says no more blitzing. Now what of the doctors?
It's all too obvious that blitzing is on its way out. From the way the community reps talk, I can tell blitzing missions will soon (if not already) no longer be a viable option. With fixers chain rooting from 8 rooms away I find myself wondering, what is to become of the non-combat profession? Doctors, at current, are dependent on blitzing missions for income. I know we could get a lot of cash from selling plasma, but more and more combat classes are mooching monster parts from parties. I rarely walk away from a good hunt with more than 8 monster parts. That said, will there be a provision made for us non-combat docs to maintain a suitable income? And even if we can get an income, what of gear? Were it not for blitzing missions I'd be wearing Cyber armor still. I know there have been talks of trade-related missions, but those are so far on the back burner, my fog settings keep it from view.

I've also heard /whispers of each class getting forms of roots and / or tames. While it strikes me as a cute idea, it does me no good if I'm having to tame the fixers who are chain rooting me.

I implore you, don't make it any harder for the %100 dependant profession to live on Rubi Ka. We've lost far too many doctors to the meta phys pets. Lets end that procession right now.
