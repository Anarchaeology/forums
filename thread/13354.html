lockpicks are idiotically time consuming
If I want to open a locked chest (and let's say for the sake of argument that my B&E skills are a bit low for the chest I'm trying to open), why do I have to reselect and use the damn lockpick EVERYTIME I TRY THE CHEST. This is not only extremely annoying but it is a huge waste of my time and wrist dexterity. Oh yes, I'm sure that the damn chests in AO will give me carpal tunnel syndrome.

Since I don't just want to point and say, "Hey, this sucks. Do something about it" I'm going to suggest another approach. How about putting lockpicks in a utility slot and then when you try and open a locked chest, the lockpick is automatically used. I don't mind having to click on the chest 20 times as long as I don't have to pick up the lockpick EVERY TIME.

Or how about this? When I click on something to use the lockpick, how about not putting it away automatically? Let me continue to use it until I put the lockpick back in the inventory???

There, how about that. Two ideas to make gameplay both more realistic (why would I put my lockpick back in the inventory if I wasn't done with it?) and easier on my patience and wrist.

Picking a lock is like attacking a MOB. That's the way the code goes. Your B&E skill is the Attack versus the difficulty of the lock's Defense. The

Just like a level 1 character has a chance to hit a lvl 200 MOB, a player with only 1 in B&E actually has a chance to pick a 'lvl 200' lock. The chance might be so slim that it takes a million attempts and therefore is for all practical purposes not worth your time in front of the computer to attempt.

The issue of this thread is that for those locks that take a couple dozen times before someone with marginal B&E is able to jimmy it open, the physical procedure in front of your computer tends to produce carpal-tunnel syndrom meanwhile not adding anything to gametime enjoyment.

I can't see having a dice roll resulting in a countdown timer to success being an improvement to the process. I'd just bring my lvl1 character into a lvl200 mission hope for no monsters in the first room and an accessable chest. I'd click on the chest and see I have 10 hours to go before success. Pour a cup of coffee go to work and come home to my goodies.

Likewise I can't see a success/failure one-time check. What is to stop me from trying again? The game won't let me try again? Perhaps if there was a chance to break your pick on failure and you could try again with a new pick I could endorse this.

As it stands, all we want is a more convenient and less wrist-breaking way to do the clicking. It's just not fun.

As an aside, I never understood by there wasn't a physical way to 'force' a chest open. I would expect a 'brawl' or 'dimach' to have a small chance to either:

- open the chest and destroy the first item.
- destroy the chest completely
- fail totally

I wouldn't mind seeing other skills come into play with this. Why not another type of chest that sometimes appears that can optionally be hacked with a 'Handheld Programming Device'? You can Pick it, Smash it, OR connect your handheld. Depending on you CompLit and the difficulty of the chest you have a 1 to 100 digit code with an appropriate number of digits filled in. Someone with a CompLit of 10 might have to fill in 99 of those 100 digits on a lvl200 chest before getting it open. The guys with a comp lit of 800 might only have to fill in two.

I'm all for options and varied gameplay.
