In all honesty... majority (can't say all) of the ARKs out there do it because they actually want to help others. Now if you want to go volunteer your personal game time to go listen to abusive players (on the majority of petitions), please feel free and show them how it is done.

If you DO have a valid concern or complaint about an ARK, you can always email support@anarchy-online.com and mention the ARK you had a problem with. However, most of the time in my experience people who complain about ARK fall under one or many of the following categories:

1) A player who waited for a long period of time to get a response and didn't get the response they wanted and want to complain and gather solace from any support he/she may receive from the boards.

2) A player who started being abusive with the ARK and is insulted when the ARK didn't sit around to take the abuse.

3) The player got an ARK who was having a bad day and/or was just downright rude. This case, that ARK should be reported since I doubt ARK directors wouldn't stand for that.

As for GM's being a better source... I doubt it. It would be more or less the same.. if you get a GM who is in a good mood, you might get a more favorable response than if the GM was in a bad mood.

My ONLY complaint about ARK, is that they don't get the support that they deserve and need from Funcom. As it stands now, they are just a filtration system for Funcom so that they don't have to staff more full time customer service employees.

So, if you want to bad mouth ARK... go do it somewhere else. Of course if you want to bad mouth a certain two faced OPS director.... well... go for it. But really, if you think you can do better and deal with the trouble of upset players time after time... then go visit the ARK website to see how to apply.
