Open letter to all Omni-Tek departments concerning BORPAC
My esteemed colleagues,

Once again we find our lawful mining operations under threat from terrorist actions. The creation of the Borialis Pact (BORPAC) between the Nanomage Liberation Front and the Desert Winds has, in it's declaration of intent, declared a state of open war between OT-RK and BORPAC members. Any incursions on Omni-Tek held territory by any BORPAC member should, and will, be dealt with using extreme prejudice.

The interdepartmental strife which has been, as of late, prejudicial to the development of Omni-Tek operations on Rubi-Ka should be put aside in our endeavour to obliterate the BORPAC threat to our notum mining operations. In addition, some Directors may find it to their benefit to have their departments cooperate with clan forces in the defense of installations or coordinated assaults on the BORPAC terrorists, however they are advised to remain vigilant as always when dealing with clan organizations.


-Shuldrich

One man's terrorist...
According to he latest edition of the Omni-Tek standard dictionary the term "terrorist" is defined as "One who uses violence or other illegal means to further a political or social agenda" Which is what it seems, to me at least, what BORPAC seems to be doing. The destruction of mining complexes, the threat of continued destruction of Omni-Tek property, and further disruptions of OT-RK mining operations to further your socio-political agenda will not be accepted, nor will it go unnoticed by Omni-Tek employees. One could then argue that we are all terrorists to someone else as we all take part in the Notum Wars and attempt to destroy installations that belong to another faction, however, remember that one of the qualifications of being a terrorist is illegality, which brings me to my second point.
The Omni-Tek Corporation has a legal lease to process and ship the notum of Rubi-Ka, and due to the recent deregulations on notum mining OT-RK departments also have a right to conduct mining operations on the planet's surface. Your actions interfere with our legally sanctioned rights of operation in that not only do you interfere with our notum mining operations, but by decimating clan and neutral installations you cut down on notum production in general, affecting our legally sanctioned monopoly on processing and shipping.
Omni-Tek has a lease on Rubi-Ka for one reason, and one reason alone, notum. We are here to make a profit and I don't see why we should be apologetic about it. I'm sorry for te plight of the nanomage people, but that is not the primary concern of OT-RK, our primary concern is keeping notum prodction at, or as close to 100% as possible. We have to answer to a board of directors on Omni Prime who in turn have to answer to shareholders, if we do not meet our obligations to them what will be the impact on our livelihoods?

-Shuldrich

Omni-Tek cannot be concerned with matters of ethical and moral consideration, as a corporation our job is to first and foremost turn a profit, all other matters are of lesser concern to our management. While these veiws may be seen as callous and heartless by some it's the only way for Omni-Tek to function efficiently.
A further clarification on the issue of defining BORPAC as a terrorist alliance: BORPAC destroys mining facilities without replacing them, by doing this they artificially manipulate the supply of notum flowing to OT-RK for processing and shipping. Through this manipulation they interfere with the legally sanctioned right of OT-RK to process and ship the notum which is mined by all. While OT-RK would prefer to get the notum supplied by Omni-Tek departments we have no qualms about buying it from the clansor neutrals as per the new ICC deregulation acts. Thusly, if a clan faction destroys an Omni-Tek installation and replaces it with an installation of their own they do not manipulate the flow of notum to OT-RK shipping/refining installations. However, when BORPAC obliterates a facility, be it Clan, Omni-Tek, or Neutral affiliated and does not replace it they interfere with OT-RK's legally sanctioned rights to process and ship thought the premeditated artificial manipulation of the notum supply.

-Shuldrich
