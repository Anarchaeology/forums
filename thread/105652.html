Namewitheld, a step to far.
It was a cold frosty morning.
I was standing in my office which towers above Omni Trade, watching the morning sun splice through the cold morning sky.

Standing in front of the huge window, I look out over the vast expanse of the city. Freight ships coming in and out of orbit in the distance, collecting their hourly cargo of notum, in front of me is a forest of highrises clouded and obscured by the cold morning mist.

I slowly close my eyes as the warm morning sun hits my face, whilst taking a sip of the steamy hot sweet coffee, there is no noise just quiet. The office is so quiet I can hear my own heart beat, slowly beating.
I gaze over our great city holding the warmth of the of cup in both hands, and i wonder to myself "whats in store today?".

As i stand there enjoying the morning view, my mind is blank and i am at peace, as I slowly take in a breath my comm unit starts to beep on my desk.

"Oh, cant a guy get some peace for five minutes".

I look at the comm screen, it reads "Emergency Transmission".
I place my mug on my glass table and I hit the view button, Sergeant Onris's face appears,
"Good Morning Sir" he says,
"Good Morning Sergeant, and what may I do for you that is so Urgent".
"Sir we have received information that the group of terrorists Namewitheld are planning something very serious".
"Understood Sergeant, so what is it that they are planning that is so urgent" I ask.
"Sir this channel may not be secure, im sending you a copy of the documents obtained now, via a secure link".
"Very well Sergeant".

I look at the screen as I see Onris punching in the buttons to transmit the file "all sent Sir".
"Thankyou Sergeant" I reply, "is there anything else that I can do for you?".
"No Sir this is currently top priority".
"Understood Sergeant, Tikon out....".

I close the comm unit and I sit down in my big leather chair, I tap in my ID code into the Data Unit so I can retrieve the information sent to me by Onris.
I slip in a little clear data plate disc into the data unit, and I hit the download button.
The data unit responds "Transfer Complete".

I slowly slip the data plate disc into my computers drive unit, and I lean back in my chair, swiveling round to face out of the window.
"Computer Online".
The computer loads up and responds with the prominent voice of a woman " Good Morning Lieutenant".
"Access File ID 100alpha3",
" Accessing file now, please standby" the computer responds.

As I swivel around to view the information on my screen, the headline of the document catches my eye, what i see is not pleasing but disturbing.

I sit poised in my chair focused on the document, "Namewitheld Planning Attacks" the headline reads. I sit back in my big leather chair which is hand crafted from bronto hide.
I kick my big black Omni-Pol issue boots up onto the desk and i quietly say to myself "This time they have gone a step to far".
