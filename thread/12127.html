As with many things... it depends.

Personally, I like being self-sufficient, so I invested a sizable amount of points into Nano Programming. I knew I was going to take a hit in other areas, but I figured that later on, when the cost of raising the skills became too much to justify paying for them, I'd hand my implant-making issues over to a friendly neighborhood Engineer or Trader. Thusfar, I've managed to, with the help of specialized implants and the Nano Prog. Expertise buff, finagle myself into QL50 implants. At this point, it remains to be seen whether I'll continue to invest points in this skill, as I'm currently concentrating on working up my other skills, and don't need to upgrade just yet.

The Pros:

1) You're self-sufficient.
2) It's a handy way to make a little spare cash from donations and such.
3) While minor in comparison to hunting/missions, you get XP from it. (Making a full set of QL 30 implants for a friend's character netted me a little less than 6k in experiance for about 10 minutes of work).

The Cons:

1) The skill is not used for anything other than putting clusters into implants.
2) The points you spend in Nano Programming are points you're not spending on other skills.
3) Shopping for all the right clusters can be a hassle, not to mention expensive. Of course, if you're making clusters for someone else, then they should foot the bill anyways.

In any case... I've been happy with my decision thusfar. As with many things, of course, Your Mileage May Vary.

Oh, one more thing:

You're right... it *is* something else to do. Especially when you need to take a break from missions/hunting.

In any case, a couple of tips:

1) Download Nano Nanny if you haven't already. You can find it at http://www.timezulu.com.au/NanoNanny. It speeds up planning out your implants SO much that you'll wonder how you got along without it.

2) Use the Nano Programming Expertise general Buff. This adds +20 to your Nano Programming skill.

3) It's minor, but since Nano Programming is dependant on Intelligence... use the Intelligence Boost general buff to get an extra point or two in your Programming.

4) If you don't mind 'stretching' the boundaries a bit... make yourself a set of Nano Programming Implants at the highest QL you can manage (That's three clusters, one in each of the following implants: Head, Eye, and Right Hand). You'll have to swap out your usual implants in those locations, of course, but you'll boost your Nano Programming skill enough to make your programming easier... and I'm of the personal opinion that, not taken to unreal extremes (IE "Twinking"), this is a excellent use of the tools at your disposal.

Hope that helps...

[EDIT: Included the bonus that the Expertise nano gives, for reference. Also, I should point out that, to my knowledge (if FC's made an official statement on this, I don't know about it) this is not over-equipping... provided it's not taken to ridiculous extremes. I, personally, only have a set of NanoProg-specific implants that are designed to help me program my next level of implants... once I actually have that stats to wear them. If this is a non-no, I'd like to hear about it.]
