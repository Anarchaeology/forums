In the hands of anybody with dark blue skills even the biggest guns are crap without critbuffs.

Against any high level mob you need crits, either naturally or with the help of crit buffs. Without a crit your barely going to scratch the mob enough for it to worry it.

Alot of complaints about shotguns are through PvP because most players AMS is far higher than their DMS, coupled with crit buffs results in ALOT of crits. Traders are especially feared due to the fact that with deprive/ransacks they can push their AMS well over 1000, resulting in alot of crits, a crit buff on them just makes them even bigger killing machines.

This is true of ANY weapon a trader desides to use.
