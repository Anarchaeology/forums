Well I am an Engineer, and I am very happy with my profession, even before the patches I loved being an engineer, I chose the Nano Breed, mainly because I liked the way they looked I have an Adventurer friend who is Solitus, she seems to enjoy being an Adventurer also.

I'd say the easiest way tomake money is to get a mission, they
are challenging and fun at first, although they can get repeditive.
(Funcom is soon going to add new team mission, and other fun things.) If you don't enjoy missions, then you can make some money killing monsters, but I wouldn't get that money from the training grounds, go outside your city. If you get items, use the shopping channel to sell them, things usually go for good cash. I charge 1000 credits for every 10 quality lvls. This should get you started out, hope you enjoy Rubi-Ka as much as I do!

If you play on Rubi-Ka 2 I'd be happy to help you out, send "Sepentura" a message.
