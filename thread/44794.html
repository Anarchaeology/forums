Most likely fiction.

A lot of online-ordering places will do this: take an upcoming product, put a fake box (or no photo) up on their site with a best guess price in order to get lots of pre-orders for it.

When the real price comes out, they simply update it and tell you.

Don't believe anything anyone says, until FC says it. And then, think hard about it, because FC might be wrong about it too (that'd be a shock.)
