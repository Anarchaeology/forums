>> New sounds <<
I'd like to see new sounds for :

- Mobs dying. Human mobs have all the same sound for their agony.

- Riding a ground / air vehicle. No sound ? Damn my Yalm is worth a nice low frequency engine purrrr.

- Weapons : doh my Hammer or Support Beam make the smae whympy splating noise as a Blackjack. Give me something more ... Atrox-like. A big loud solid THUD or nice metalic BLAM would be cool.

- Nanos : same noise too. Some improvements could be done here too, a sparkling damage shield should twingle with metallic noises, while the Humidity Extractor should slowly buzz around.

- Cities : more specific ambiant musics or crowd buzz should be done to singularize better each city. Omni cities seems to have a better specific music.

- Wilderness : sea-side and rivers should have watery noises. Harbors should have specific noises from boats and birds too. Lava pools in Avalon, etc.

Maybe some social moves could have specific noises linked to them too.
