Mastermind
"Do you know why your here?" The large atrox had an voice of authority and determination. "No officer, I do not, but I can only guess by the treatment of your troopers that it is pretty serious. Am I under arrest?" Cain asked calmly. "No, mr Tapanes, you are not under arrest. We just had a few questions." The Atrox answered with an tone as much demanding as it was polite.

Cain looked around the room, glancing slightly at the two Omni-Pol supremes guarding the only exit from the room that was clearly intended for interigation. He felt a bit unnerved by the large atrox in the black Omni-Tek officer outfit but remained calm. "Wont you please have an seat, Mr Tapanes?" The atrox said and gestured at the seat on the oposite side of the table. Cain sat down quietly. The reason they did take his sidearm from him during the pickup disturbed him, but he decided to cooperate never the less. Even though he didnt quite enjoy the unexpected visit of the Omni-Pol supreme guards during his work time, he had nothing to hide from Omni-Tek and decided to make no attempt to make it seem as he did. "So are you going to tell me what this is all about yet, or do I have to guess it?" Cain asked in an voice he tried to make as professionally polite as possible.

"We wanted to ask you about project Mastermind, mr Tapanes." The atrox obviusly didnt enjoy trying to be polite and made little effort to hide his military background. Cain's eyes grew cold and his voice as well, "What's your clearance?" he replied. "A-1" The atrox replied. "With ICC's arrival planetside, Ive been assigned to investigating the project to make sure we know all we need to know about it." Cain averted his eyes from the atrox for a moment as if remembering something painfull then looked back up at him. "You've got clearance, you can read about it in the files." Cain replied cold. "No mr. Tapanes." he replied "Im afraid those records have been deleted after the projects termination by source unknwon. We've just recently digged up an hardcopy of the files but they where to vauge in details. It does however have your name on it among a few others under the classification: "Released."

"Let us begin then, shall we? " The atrox said, leaned forward and press an button on the console in front of him.

"Investigation concerning project Mastermind, classified under clearance A-1." The atrox said out loud and only now had Cain discovered the camera positioned overlooking the interigation chamber. "Questioning, Cain "Centurion3" Tapanes. Omni-Tek employee." Cain glanced at the guards at the door again then slowly slipped on his shades. "So where shall we begin mr Tapanes?" The atrox said and leaned back on his chair, "Tell us about your involvement in project Mastermind."

"If I must." Cain looked up at the camera and then back at the officer in front of him. "As you well know, Nanomages can reproduce naturally, unlike atroxes..." Cain didnt try to hide his dismay for atroxes in general, and only exchanged an small glance at the officer before continueing. "but I was not the result of such. I was engineerd. The only home I knew was the bright white walls of laboritories and resting halls. It may sound strange to you but I didnt see the sun untill I was 20 years old. Everything happend underground, they never let us out because of fear of contamination of nano viruses or fear of us escaping." Cain sighed. "Who's they?" The atrox interupted. "I didnt know then and I still dont know... all I knew was that they where scientists backed by Omni-Tek. The guards wore the same black outfit as Omni-Tek, but to an white lab and white labcoats, they where an welcome change to the monotomy." Cain sighed again, "The world I knew then might have been cold and cruel to you, but it was the only world I knew at the time and I adapted. I made friends with some of the guards, some of the scientists and some of the other..." Cain paused, searching for an word that would actually fit what they where. "... experiments."

"We didnt know why we where there, and we didnt know any other world beyond the lab. We had no reason to belive there was more to this world then what we saw. And no one would teach us otherwise." Cain takes off his shades again and closes his eyes and dream away for a moment. "The people with me there, my brothers and sisters, because that is what we where... since we grew up together, most of us where Nanomages as you call us, but there was also some solitus, opifex and even atroxes. Even though we where always under watch from the guards and scientists, we where the only ones we felt we could trust... sure there was some guards that took liking to us, felt pity perhaps, but those guards never seemd to last. They where always exchanged by some hardcore military man who refused to even look at us. It was quite a life for a child."

"When we reached an certain age, mostly around the middle of our teens, the guards would begin to take some of us away. I wish I didnt know now what I didnt know then." Cain paused. "We where born and raised as experiments, our lifes forfeit... do you want to know what project Mastermind is?" Cain's face turned grim. "I'll tell you, it was a slaughter, it was hundreds of people's life's thrown away for an manic dream..." Cain paused and calmed down alittle before continueing. "Project Mastermind was an attempt to chain-link the nanobots in the atmosphere to create one enormous net, chain, link, all over the planet and use this net for inteligence gathering and surveilance. This was to be the first step in an long row of experiments planned." Cain glanced up at the camera in the corner of the room again.

"Go on." The atrox said in an tone alot less demanding and more focused and interested. "Well," Cain answered. "No computer is powerfull enough to process the amount of information gatherd by the billions of nanobots in the atmosphere, so the scientists sollution to this problem was to connect one man to an device and have all the information channeld by the nanobots directly into the nervous system of this person. We where supposed to be able to controll what the computers could not. The first stage was surveilance, the second would be to actually controll the nanobots." Cain paused and his voice turned to that of an scientist, cold and systemical. "The results? About 95% of all experiments had massive nervous damage and died instantly. The other 5% where left insane... paranoid, dellusionary..." Cain glanced up at the atrox across the room who where beginning to look alittle more respectfull but nervous.

"The nanomages where the obvius choice to clone for this task. They raised us for years while trying to teach us how to master nanobots. The other races where just to test the effect. Atroxes for their superior stamina might have an better chance for survival, opifex and solitus where more balanced in their buildt and was not relying as much on nanobots and therefor decreasing the strain on them. At least that was the plan behind it all, The result was always the same though, death or massive strain to your brain, leaving you insane or paralysed within seconds." Cain paused for an long time as if quelching the memories of his lost brothers and sisters.

The guards at the door who all this time had been staying in military discipline where now looking blankly at Cain and forgetting their training for a moment. When Cain looked up at them they quickly snapped back into position. "But you are still alive mr. Tapanes" the large atrox said to break the silence in the room. "How can that be?"

"Well, I was lucky, nothing more to it... Omni-Tek cut its founding to the project before I was being taken to the surveilance controlls. I suppose they finally understood that it couldnt be done. However with the training programs they had been giving the experiments, we where to valuable to simply terminate and we where released into service of Omni-Tek. Even after finding out all that ive told you now, I still dont blame Omni-Tek of what happend. If project Mastermind had been possible, then it would be in far better hands in Omni-Tek then it would have been in the hands of the Clans."

The large atrox across the table nodded slowly, "Great sacrifices has to be made for the safety and survival of us all, especially at these times. Its what differs an soldier from an individual..." The atrox nodded at the guards beside him and they took an step towards Cain. "We've modified your insurance pattern to make sure you have no recollection of what we've talked about here today. With the latest technology available and the new threat of the clans these times will require the best of us all im sure you understand. Omni-Command has decided to reinstate project Mastermind to make sure we regain the cutting edge in this war. We may need you again mr Tapanes." he said calmly.

Cain sprung to his feet. "No, you cant do that. Its not going to work!" he shouted. The guards opend fire on him without question and he fell back to the chair dead.

Cain woke up at reclaim with memory of the last couple of days hazy and with an vision swirling around memories from an long time ago untill they faded and the fog cleared from his eyes. "Huh? What happend? Hmmm... I dont remember..." He got up slowly and slipped his shades back on.

"Well I do have work to do today."
