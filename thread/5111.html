Guild Buildings
From what I understand.. Funcom originally wanted to encourage cooperation between different classes so that we could hunt in groups and be more stronger other than relying on ourselves and our weaknesses. We could enforcers aided by docs, and meta physicists would stay behind while their ..things did all the work. Soldiers would auxillirate the enforcers etc.

Funcom even implemented the concept of a guild, so that people could run around and have an organized guild dedicatd to a particular aspect of AO. Like.. Docs guild or whatever example you might have.

But we don't even have guild buildings. I mean, Im in a guild, but, I havent even met half of my members in game except through the guild channel. That's kinda disheartening. I mean, you might argue that well why dun you go meet them. set up a rendezvous?

Well ya. But what about, being in an area to congregate so that we can plan out things ? It's heard to plan things when your running in Omni Ent, and another guy is in Omni Greater Forest. And another guy is in Harry's and another guy is in Andromeda.

Funcom. you could do much more by implenenting guild buildings. It would sooo add to the idea of a guild. Not only that, but a guild bank and a guild armory. Cause right now there is one guy who is holding our all cash and our spare gear. But we haven't seen him in awhile. I think he got arrested for drug trafficking. Just kidding.

But please... =\

Anyone else agree ?

Oh and I know this probably isn't a new idea.. but, I just thought I would post it in case funcom forgot. ^^
