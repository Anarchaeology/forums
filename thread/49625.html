PvP: Debuff duration needs to be changed?
Firstly, let me start by explaining the problem. This got ABSOLUTELY nothing to do with any debuffs on PvM situation, so leave comments regarding PvM issues out plz.

Well we all know that most PvP battles are over in a less then 1 minute. However, there are 3 professions currently that can pretty much disable other professions for a duration of 3 minutes or there about.

The problem with these long duration debuffs has been there for a long time while they are relatively easy to land on your opponent. Doctors and Traders for example can pretty much disable their opponents Defence and Offence for a total duration of 3 minutes.

Example:

I am an NT:

MP can totally make my nanos useless for duration of 3 minutes which will result that I can no longer use any of my defensive or offensive nanos...this nano has only 100% resist and does not take an MP many tries to land it on me even with a maxed and implanted nano resist.

Traders can totally wipe out my offence and defence for a duration of 3 minutes while gaining a huge boost to their skills for same period. Divest is easy to land, and albeit it doesn't shut me completely down the duration does stay for 3 minutes...and I will be on huge disadvante during this period. If trader manages to land a Plunder me as well, I can safely type /terminate.

Docs, same thing, the init debuffs shut me down nearly completely for a long duration and again has 100% nano resist...and is child play to land on the opponent even wiht a maxed nano resist and on full defence.

Suggestion:

In order to balance the issue, I would suggest the following:

All debuff nanos should not have longer then 30 seconds duration on the TARGET in PvP situation. However, traders for example should have their skill boosts from successful drains for the full 3 minute duration, as they are currently.

SO, I suggests that FC caps the duration on all debuffs that affect the TARGET to be 30 seconds MAX in Player versus Player situations. This way none of the nanos would not have to be changed themselves

This would still give an advantege for those professions that have these debuffs, but would not make other professions completely useless.

Please keep an open mind on this post.
