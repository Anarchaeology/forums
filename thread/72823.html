The Mission: On the Job
It was late. The moons of Rubi-Ka were shining brightly in the sky, though the lights of Omni-1 were brighter still. Even with all this sparkling light, there were plenty of shadows and hiding spots for those with the right skills and experience. Someone who could find a shadow the same way a rat can find cheese. These few shadows were havens to Jasziak. She had spent the last couple of hours traveling across the city. Moving from one hiding spot to the next, avoiding the guards so as not to be recognized, she was finally at her destination, an Omni-Pol facility. Suddenly, she heard a telltale noise and slunk back into the shadow. Two officers stepped out of the door in front of her.

damn Mockers, always pulling me out of my bed at the worst times. I have to get some rest tonight!

Sorry sir. Were all just following orders. What was that? The guards stopped and leveled their guns at the shadow next to Jas hiding spot. The commander said something under his breath that she could not catch. She considered her options and prepared to Recklessly Digitize.

Its nothing. Lets go. He locked the door and straightened his uniform. The patrol moved out of hearing and Jasziak finally took a breath. Instinctively reaching for her com-link to warn her comrades, she stopped and realized how vulnerable she was.

Later. She said to herself, looking around before slipping out of the shadow. Kneeling down by the door, she examined the locking mechanism. Not seeing any traps, she pulled out her Personalized Lock Pick. Piece of cake. The door opened silently and Jas disappeared inside like a shadow in the darkness.

The corridor was empty and well lit, just what Jasziak did not like, but it was expected. Voices lingered from down the hall, and Jasziak quickly consulted her map. Moving with enhanced speed to the second door on the right, she stepped into the commanders field office. There were a few random files on the desk, but she ignored them, knowing what she was looking for would not be out in the open. Taking out her hacking interface, she sat down at the desk and began her work on the computer, all the time listening with sharp ears for the sounds she dreaded.

Where is that file? Wait, this looks good ok now here. Ah! she smiled to herself as she hacked her way through the encryption, and found the reason she had come. She began the download into her NCU hacker interface. Just a few more seconds and then Ill digitize out of this stink hole.

The door began to open and suddenly those sounds she had heard registered in her distracted mind. Dropping to the ground, she prepared her escape. They must have known what she would do, because she felt the nanoprogram take over and strip away the skill she needed to convert her body into digital information.

Welcome, little Mocker. That voice! Looking over, she saw the two guards she had encountered earlier; the two she should have known had seen her. She cursed Omni-Tek as the shackles closed on her wrists.

They took her to a typical interrogation room, the kind with the single bulb and straight-backed chair. After an examination of her interface, the commander sighed loudly.

Who are you working for? This was very foolish of you to try. You mockers arent usually foolish unless there is a lot of money at stake. Jasziak just shook her head, her mind racing along with her pulse. Come now, just tell us and the punishment will be reduced. You know how these things work.

Jasziak raised her head and gazed into the commanders face. Come now, you know how these things work, too. Im not going to give away any information, and you arent going to lower the penalty even if I did. Jasziak hoped she sounded braver than she felt. But it wasnt this stupid commander she was worried about. Her employer, she was sure, was going to have words with her when he found out.

The commander sighed again. Jas could see the fatigue in his face, and smiled inwardly. She almost had the shackles off, and knew he would soon leave her to his goons.

Normally I would leave you to my goons, but you dont know what kind of information you have been paid for, my dear. Then he did the last thing on Rubi-Ka she expected.

Youre going to kill me? The gun leveled at her head was one she had never seen, but it looked powerful. Where will that get you? After all, they hadnt had time to mess with her insurance, and she would wake up in Tir.

Ill be keeping this little device, you dont mind of course.

She saw him pull the trigger, and still could not grasp why
