Proposal for The Council of Truth [FUNCOM]
What follows hence is an official proposal for The Council of Truth for new rules and regulations from the Mercury Dragons Clan. [This document is written In-Character, the [] notation will be used to denote portions that are stated out of character]. Without further ado, I give you the proposal, which has been put forth to and ratified by both the Command Staff and Commander Windguard.

Disturbances during important events


There have been quite a number of reports on clan and omni citizens interfering during important negotiations between Omni-Tek and Clan factions, specially at times of truce to talk or meet at a location. We believe these individuals should not be allowed to disrupt the balance of such delicate situations and should be dealt with quickly.

We believe if the individual (clan or omni) who causes a disturbance (verbally or through force) should be immediately gridded to the nearest prison and held there for at least 1 hour in a secure cell, and released afterwards.

[The disruptive player gets gridded out to a cell in a Prison somewhere, in his cell there will be the some sort of explanation as to why he was sent there, perhaps a sign which reads You are here for disturbing the peace, etc and he would have to be IN the game for 1 hour (not log out and come back in 1 hour) before he is released automatically and gridded out in front of a prison (doesnt matter which clan town really). Now a smart player would /terminate and get himself out of the place, but if once he is gridded he is automatically saved in the cell as if he has used an insurance terminal inside his cell then he cant get out until he is automatically released. Also the player wont have access to the chat server to avoid him sending a bunch of petitions or try to get people to petition for him, even thought out of game he could send an e-mail or instant message to another player, the player may realize the serious situation in which he is not even allowed to contact someone in the game until he is out. This would reduce the amount of players who ruin roleplaying events since nobody really wants to stay in the game sitting in a cell and forced to do nothing and have no contact with anyone in-game, and it would also give the role-players the feeling that the prisons ARE part of the storyline and not just empty square buildings. This of course applies to clan, neutral and omni.]

CoT Ranks that are unrecognized or forgotten

More than a few clan citizens have earned the titles of Blade, Guard, Officer or some other rank/title granted by a CoT Official or clan military/political authority. And most are wondering, I got a title, great, what are my duties? CoT Officials have granted most of these titles, yet they leave their proven loyal men in the dark about their role and duties to uphold. We believe the CoT should make an announcement to call all those individuals with such ranks and titles to the CoT Building in Tir and inform them of their duties and assignment and if it doesnt apply to them to remove the title.

[Since last year, so many unfinished events made by Funcom has left a bunch of people proud of having a title, and then disappointed to see its worth NOTHING. Hey man, youre an Officer, great! What do you do? Nothing? Bummer. is this what a player wants to hear, no. We think perhaps a small group of ARKs can run a schedule of SMALL events for those who hold titles, by contacting then and performing small tasks. To avoid the crazy lag which usually an event creates (when enough players move to one playfield, and then crashing it), the player should be asked confidentially in-character ONLY to bring another few people, maybe to form a team for a mission or something like that. FC should encourage active role-players, be it through political/military ranks/titles as change of first name. Not everyone wants to be a Commander or a Blade, there are more than a few who would love to be a Bartender, Clown (or Comedian depending on what kind of comedy), Reporter, Dancer, Waitress, Singer, Bouncer, etc. Player with titles should be summoned through a message in the AO Launcher, in-game by the announcement through a clan/omni official ARK/GM and at the AO Web Site and AO Official Forums to get all those people with titles to attend to a meeting. Players with titles should be asked directly by an ARKs or GMs during the meeting if they wish to hold their title/rank (and asked through a private tell if they are willing to role-play their roles, if the answer is no they should be explained that the role requires roleplaying and that is the reason why they will lose the title). This of course applies to clan, neutral and omni.]

CoT Show of Strength

I think it is time for the CoT to show their strength through a yearly parade, an event that would show the clans military strength to our people and would certainly give the Omnis something to think about. Council of Truth Clans and Clans who support the CoT would be in uniform and armed (in case of an omni attack) parading in Tir or Athen. The day would be one of celebration, we think July 24th would be appropriate in memory of that day in the year 29206 because of its historical significance (Reaching a critical mass in numbers - and taking advantage of the escalating war between Omni-Tek and Sol Banking Corporation - the clans finally rise up against the authorities. Killing thirty-nine OT guards, sabotaging security droids and other military hardware, the clans seize control of four notum mines and announce their plans for a democratic Rubi-Ka; run by the people, for the people.)

[This one day would require every ARK and GM available (it is only once a year, so I think its an acceptable situation) to take the role of Guards, Watchers, Blades, Officers, Lieutenants, Captains, Commanders, etc to show a REAL military force for the clan gaming community. This can also be applied to the Omni side, to balance the scale. The Neutrals wont have such event, however I think they should have a yearly event as well.]

Clan Apartments

We clanners live a difficult life, and our one place to relax privately or with friends without interruption is our apartment. Im yet to see any shop sell a BED on which we can lay down and go to sleep. I believe it would be good for our economy to make other kinds of furniture, like Vid Screens [like a TV], Desks, Clocks [not working ones, just for show], neon signs of your favorite place or trademark (like Happy Rebel, The Cup, Bronto Burger, Mirr, etc), bigger sound systems, framed posters of famous clanners (Im sick and tired of finding Boss Ross portraits in missions), framed diplomas [Like Graduate from the Tir Nano-Technology Institute or Acquired Class C level of Public Relations from Athens Diplomatic and Human Resources Society, etc), more statues, and the ability to order different colors of the same items, we dont want every apartment to look the same or too similar after all, we arent clones.

[I think a few new EMOTES should be created to lay down on the bed to sleep or just relax. The first would still need someone to jump on the middle of the bed and type /sleeptime and the character would lay down, unfortunally it wont make the character close its eyes since the face its a texture which you cant change for that one emote, but it is nice to sleep in-character. A second emote would require someone to jump on the middle of the bed and type /relaxtime and he will be laying down on the bed relaxing in a position in which he obviously is not asleep, but relaxing, like having their hands behind their heads and legs crossed, like watching TV. These two new emotes in combination to the items we suggest would help greatly in roleplaying WITHIN an apartment, roleplaying is not limited to talking to a CoT or Omni official or hunting a criminal, many clans have developed in-house events which starts or end in an apartment to have a controlled environment for certain part or parts of the event.]

Unfair Trade of Goods

We are yet to understand how armor we buy at a shop can cost 100,000 credits and when we try to sell it back even 5 minutes after (sometimes we do purchase the wrong items) the selling value decreases to a number which is a joke!, the economy would be better if we could sell back things at a more fair rate, I believe a percentage should be proposed to the merchants between 60 and 75% of selling value to the items we purchase from them. Otherwise well have to start trading between ourselves even more (the shopping channel is active day and night) and go to the shops less, something Im sure they would like to avoid.

[This is more of a convenience than anything else, some players dont do that many missions to acquire an item, they go out with friends to hunt and role-play and when they have a bit of credits sometimes they buy the wrong thing and when selling backtheir funds are down drastically. This is more for the casual player, who, unless he has generous friends, wont have the funds to upgrade the weapons and armor he needs, or the equipment to make items, etc. Something Funcom should address, there are quite a few casual players, and this should not be ignored (not that FC is ignoring casual players, just this specific situation may not have been in their list).

CoT Monthly Meetings

Yes, there should be a meeting at least once a month between CoT officials and Clan Leaders and high-ranking Clan officers (Generals, Advisors, etc) to discuss the political and military problems the clans face and to hear any requests/ideas/suggestions from clans representatives. Only Clan Leaders will be able to speak the CoT Officials, the rest will be there as advisors to their leader. This is something we NEED if CoT is to show the people their presence; were not happy of just looking at the CoT building in Tir and wonder Is there anyone working in there? These Meetings should be SCHEDULED ahead of time of course.

[FC has done only a handful of these, and without notice most of the time, not something which players who are Clan Leaders are happy about. FC only needs 4 ARKs to run this scenario. 1 CoT political representative, 1 CoT military representative and 2 of the biggest Atrox with the meanest looking weapons and level 300 as guards. The CoT building inside is set to have at least 100 players seated, and I think the intention was to have Clan Leaders to sit to listen to the CoT or have CoT listen to the audience (or both), come on! the first floor has FOUR galleries for people to sit and face the center where CoT officials usually speak from. And I believe ARKs should use more EMOTES (heck a few macros would fix that) since almost every meeting/event has the ARK just standing like a statue, why add EMOTES to the game and the people who should encourage the use of them. not use it? If someone gives a speech, there is a /speech EMOTE for gods sake. Plan ahead FC, and have ARKs be 100% prepared to play their role as realistic as possible. Also the meetings should be announced at least a week ahead of time (preferably two weeks) so players may make the proper arrangements to attend or have a representative attend. This would make a BIG difference in the game, as it would SHOW players there is an active storyline (in which CoT is a big part of).]

CCL and Alliances

The CCL (Council of Clan Leaders, meaning every Clan Leader is automatically a part of it) needs a place to gather for meetings on a weekly basis, and we think asking for a place to do so its not out of the question. Will CoT be able to provide such a place, for our Clan Leaders own safety? We hope so, since the meetings focus on many important issues and it opens the lines of communication between clan organizations. The same would apply to Alliances, be it political or military who need a safe place to meet. CoT should consider seriously, since Dust Brigade and even Omnis have not only interrupted important meeting but have attacked and slaughtered Clan Leaders.

[Tir and Athen would not be a choice, crowded as it is. We think either the Bliss outpost in Longest Road or the clan city of Sabulum in Perpetual Wastelands Zone would do with another building set up to 100% suppression field inside to avoid any unwanted fights, also it would be a nice touch if the door CHECKS if the person trying to enter is clan side, if not he enters an empty building (different from the one the clanners enter), while the clan side players enter the same building, or Omnis and Neutrals just cant enter, period. This would help the players who role-play the role of Clan Leaders, and it would also encourage the travel to in-game zones, which are rarely used.]

We hope this proposal reaches the desk of CoT Clan Leaders and they consider it seriously.

Sincerely

Windguard
Commander
Mercury Dragons Clan
