Most of the shops have been junk for some time now. Please fix the armor, computer, etc terminals to at least "occasionally" having something useful in them. (The weapons terminals would be greatly helped by having many of the junk weapons deleted from the database entirely)

Please take out half the outdoor basic shops and replace them with something useful or interesting. For example:

What percentage of clanners in Lush Fields are going to use basic terminals? (Of course it would be nice if this beautiful castle had something of interest in it, period)
