dynamic load suggestion
I was just reading a request for you to move the gridpoint in Tir, and it reminded me of something.

Can you put in something to change rendering selections, on the fly, according to conditions?

Something like this. Open up a text editor, and type out the following, (or better yet, a template to modify.)

100%
Liquid_Animations = 1
Client_Wildlife = 1
Realistic_Sunrays = 1
Realistic_Clouds = 1
Simple_Clouds = 1

Show_Others_Effects = 1
Show_All_Names = 1
Texture_Resolutions = 2 (Full)
Texture_Quality = 16/32
Use_continuing_level_of_detail = 0
CLOD_full_detail_distance = 100
View_distance = 75
Character_view_distance = 70m
Ground_quality = 130
Full_ground_quality_radius = 7

75%

Basically, let us "script" different resolution settings for different levels of lag. In an open, and empty, plain, we can have all the extras turned on, but have the client shut them down incrementally as client-side lag builds up due to crowding.

Of course, to do this, certain things would have to be doable on the fly. Things like Texure resolution and texture quality, which you can adjust in the client, but which the client warns you won't take effect until you log back in.

BTW, I like the grid being there, but you might put in an alternate exit for high-load hours. Back on the island where the exit used to be, (before the terminal was there) would be about right. (Anyone else remember having to run to Newland to get into the grid? Remember AVOIDING exiting in Newland because you'd fall to your death?)
