Originally posted by Vydas
What I think both you and Kuroshio are saying is that, with the exception of the last iteration, the DoT, in a way, is being 'cheated' as it's not allowed to extract its entire rightful penalty. Maybe so. I question, then, why any nano doesn't work in this same way, and stack upon itself.
Traders appear to be rather unique in the implementation of the DoT mechanics. Other professions have DoTs and HoTs, but it is accepted that having them stack would be unbalancing and are restricted by game mechanics from having them stack. Imagine if a Fixer's HoTs stack, either in terms queuing subsequent execution or stacking the effects during the HoT duration. Or a DoT against a mob (or worse, a player). In the case of HoTs, the fixer could queue up a stack of HoTs that lasted hours or days. Same with a DoT against a player or mob. And if the effects stacked for the duration of either, so a single tick applied the effects of all running DoTs/HoTs, chaincasting HoTs could make players nearly impossible to kill. Chain casting DoTs, could kill the target just as easily.

However the DoTs on DHP are tied to an ability that cannot be refused for any significant amount of time and remain worthwhile.
