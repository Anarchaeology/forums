Allies
There are numerous organizations out there that have bound themselves with other organizations with like goals. Organiztions of guilds such as the CoA, EG and numerous others.

What I propose is individual chat channels and other perks (perhaps a ranking system of some sort) for guilds that belong to one of these organizations.

This is especially important now that Notum Wars is upon us. Organizations would assist each other in the defense of towers and the sort.

This could also be the "clan advantage" everyone has been so desperatly crying for. As omni tek cannot have various leaders as they all fall under the corperation.
