Crystal Line presents .. The Rubi-Ka Lottery and more.
Greetings Employees

Crystal Line , an Omni-Tek guild is now starting up its activitys within the world of Rubi-Ka.

Our guild is Primarly an Entertainment guild ,bringing quality ,frequent and well planned entertainment to the residents of Rubi-Ka.

Amongst our current projects we will be bringing to you Rubi-Ka's first ever lottery, a popular game , based on matching 5 numbers from a selection of 1 - 25 , We are expecting this game to be available to all residents within 2 weeks,probably sooner.

This event will be a continous event running every week or every 2 weeks from our guild hang out , The Relax Bar in Omni-Forest

Our set rules for this event are already available for you to view.

The Rubi-Ka Lottery

Another of our planned projects is a Rubi-Ka Team Fighting League where teams of 3 contestants fight head to head over 3 rounds , last man standing after each round is declared the winner.

We will be having strict level rules in reguards to the league tables and contestants with special events ,like team knockout tournaments , our current plan to have this event hosted in the Omni-Entertainment Arena , but we are also hoping to be able to help arrange clan teams to participate in the league and tournaments also and for them to use the Tir arena.

Of course we will be having fundraisers to raise prize money for the winning teams who provide this high level of quality entertainment.

Stay posted for more news reguarding The Rubi-Ka Team Fighting League.

On top of the events listed above .. we are also in the process of starting regular DJing at the Relax Bar in Omni-Forest and will soon have a time table for these in house DJs for your listening pleasure.

We are currently seeking members to help us provide our events to the public as best we can , we have no recruitment requirements as in what level you are etc as this is unimportant to our line of work .. we are however looking for mature and responsible members who are able to role play and behave in a respectable manner.

If you would like to become a member of the Crystal Line guild and feel you meet the requirements above please feel free to contact me via Email at Yorkforce@hotmail.com and i shall arrange an interview.

We will , once up and running , be offering a competative salary aswell as performance bonus's and also include uniforms, not to mention in guild events just for Crystal Line members.
