Honestly, you should drop the MTI...it isn't too good.

At your level, the Archy Kaen Kolts are very nice pistols. They are fast, have a fling, and do decent average damage. They are adventurer only, and must be missioned for.

When you get to level 41, get a bunch of friends (higher level if you can) and visit the static dungeon in The Longest Road called the Foreman's Office.

At the end of the dungeon is a guy named the Lab Director (i think). He holds a Custom Desert Reet which is very good. I'm level 100 and still using them. Although, I'm about to upgrade my main hand.

The custom reets have a level requirement of level 41, but they take bullets as ammo, and have a fling/burst combo. the minimum damage is outrageously high and it does fire damage. also, there is a special attack to do an extra fire damage attack at random times when you use them.

I think the custom reets are an adventurer's bestest friend between levels 41-110ish. Also, they are crazy good pvp pistols between levels 41-70...then they taper off because off the lower max damage and lower crits.

Drop the MTI....get a Kaen Kolt. Then go for the custom reets. You won't be sorry.

Look for, but don't use, BBI Faithfuls as well, especially if you can't get a Custom Reet or can only get one (they make GREAT offhand weapons and I bet they work offhand even longer than Borr's noted a combo would). Like Kaens, they're missionable (and I think you can buy 'em from Specialist Trader stores, or you originally could). Like Riders (the melee weapons, y'know?) they do tons of damage along a rather wide scale with basically no minimum, but good average damage.

Unlike Kaens, and like Riders, they use Melee Initiative for some reason. This means Ranged Initiative won't speed them up, that bullets can be parried, etc. It's a real mess, and it's a shame, because the BBI probably outdamages nearly everything but the Mantis Scissor or MTI Grey (which I don't believe anyone has ever found) before crits are applied. It's a bug and will be fixed, but your guess as to when is as good as mine. A real pity.

But yeah, if you have ANY way to get Custom Reets, go for it.
