Here's a thought...
Why not make those 'useless' weapons have something 'useful' to them? E.G., certain weapons out there have, as we all know, REALLY bad damage curves. Why not take those weapons (E.G. Breathing Space), and give them some kind of special? I'll use Breathing Space for this one:

Breathing Space: 90% chance the person on the recieveing end of the damage's nanos are interrupted, 75% chance they become 'stunned,' or rather, runspeed is lowered, 25% the target is paralyzed, or rooted.

Flamethrowers: Chance the target becomes, 'ignited on fire,' where they are DoT'd for fire damage every few seconds.

Bioguns: Chance of infection, or poison the target.

1he weapons: "Ignore Armor" hits. A rare hit that bypasses all AC, or perhaps, "Open wounds" or "Bleeding" effect, meaning, the target begins to bleed and loose health like a DoT.

Just a few ideas for weapon changes, rather than making more weapons.
