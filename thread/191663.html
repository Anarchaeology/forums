--Whispers in the dark--
(MysteryWolfs encounters part1)

The sun had just gone down behind the mountains of greather Tir county, one hot spring night. Mysterwolf, or Myst as his friends call him, had just been out hunting with some friends and fellowrebels, not so far away from the OP in the valley of the tree winds, a scary enough place for a poor Martial artist as himself.. Hi did not feel comfortable walking from there and in to the city all by himself. His friends had gone to the temple to try to steel some tresures, but Myst had better things to do than steels some poor loot from some dead cults. He was on a mission, a mission he did not understand, a mission he needed answers to...

Anyways, Myst started on his way back to Tir city, the city he was born and raised in, the city he had worked as an erranboy for the brontoburgershop in, the city he loved, the city he hated, he`s home. But after running just a cuple of minutes through the woods trying to find the highway, he encountered a bunch of OT-hired guns, stacked with weapons, and armed to the neck. Oh my good Myst said to himself, what the hell do i do now. Theres 4 of them agains me, i better try to sneak past them. But not long after the shots rained agains poor Myst, his sneaking had failed, the OT guns had seen him, he was doomed.. With his poor Martial art skills, and some good brawls he manged to knock one of them out. But he was badly wounded. He had taken several shots to the body and where bleeding badly. Not long after Myst fell to the grownd, with 3 huge atrox OTs standing aiming at his scull.... They whispeard to each other some words he did not understand, but it was clearly what they where talking about when one of them pushed his barrel agains mysts backhead... They where going to take him out.....

The shot came...But Myst was not dead,... Another shot came, and he heard a "tump" sound besides him, that was one of the atroxes who hit the ground.. Myst Turned and looked at the other atroxes that stood there. Bang, out of nowhere 2 new shots flew over myst and in to the 2 Ots that where left... They where dead before they hit the ground... Myst collected up his last strenghts and got up on his feet. He looked around in the dark, bus saw nothing.....

Minutes went, and Myst still did not either hear or see anyone or anything in the woods where he had been jumped, but the sound of crasshoppers had returned, the idyllic sound of the forrest where back, nature was in harmony again... But suddenly a voice from nowhere said: Follow me, follow my voice........

[edit no.2] And here goes again, PART 2!

Our dear Myst was frighten, very frighten now. His heart pounded like a train in totw, it hurt. But he came out of his litle tranze, and followed the voice. The voice was not a mean voice, but a soft light voice....A womans voice.... The voice took him out of the woods and on the highway, then it stopped, it was deadly silent again, just the light sounds of crasshoppers and the small sounds from the brontos on the other side of the road. Noone was to see on the road at this hour. Myst had lost track of the time some hours ago now, and was a bit sleepy, but then again to stressed to sleep.

He wondered who the silent helper was, the woman who had saved his life, and from the pain of beeing killed and resurrected again. He had been killed sometimes before, and the pain form beeing resurrecting was worse than beeing killed in the first place. But sudddenly a voice broke the silence that surrounded him.. It was the same kind and warm voice. Are you hurt the voice said. Myst was shocked again, and stuttered... yes. And right infront of him a person appaired. He could tell from this persons shape that it was infact a woman. She took off here helmet. Myst looked right into here eyes at once, like if he was under a spell or something. He got totally paralized. It was the most beutyfull eyes he had ever seen, and so kind and mysterius at the same time. -Stand still the woman said, this is gonna hurt. She held here hand over Mysts mounth, and put something over he`s bulletwounds. Myst said nothing, but u could see in his eyes that it hurt like hell. There u go, that should stop the bleeding she said, and smiled at him. Myst smiled back. Thank you miss....? And before he had finished his sentens, the woman was gone, disapered into thin air. It was silent again. - We will meet again Mysterywolf, we will meet again, he heard in his head.........

Back in the town, noone belived his story, none of his friends,and certanly not the boss at brontorburgers belived him.. They all said, stop drinking Myst,go to bed and sleep it off.... Sad but true he did go to bed, after a litle visit to the doctors office that is.And just before he closed his eyes that night, he swore he would find her someday,that kind and warm woman.....that...that ....Omni Agent!! He rembered the marks on here shoulder... Oh my god he thought, she had blue marks on here shoulder.. She was an Omni Agent.... And she saved my life...... why did she do that,why? With his head now filled whit questions,he passed out.... But still in hes dreams he heard: - Someday MysteryWolf, we will meet again.........

If there is one thing I could advise to any writer here, though, it is to 'cut' their texts at the proper parts.
