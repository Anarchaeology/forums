Due to the design of the different professions some are very favored (Agent, MP and Trader, Enforcer, Soldier) in overequipping while others are not (Adv, NT's, Crats, Docs, ...). And I am talking mainly about self buffing possibilities. Simply, some professions have better self buffs to enhance their primary skills (= damage output, the only important factor). And to my view and experience this is not yet overcome by compensatory mechanisms. So, as we knew, that skills are more or less meaningless (until a certain level), the one with the better equipment rules. This sort of imbalance I would like to see reduced.
Typical argument at this time point: Go find a trader that wrangles you or a doc that buffs you +80 treatment to get your higher implants in. Alright, but again, this favors guild/group members and that is severe imbalance, because it means, org members are generally favored. Although this is a MMORPG, not all players can and want to join an org to get such benefits, and, FC stated, you can get up there (lvl200) 'mostly' on your own. There are very few profession who actually can do that and I would bet some money on the fact, that all lvl200 that we have by now, didn't solo for the most part of their leveling (might be wrong).
So by setting clear rules and leveling the playfield to a certain degree (still very debatable), it will help a lot of non powerplayers. Most of all, I support the idea, that someone, that hasn't the skill to use an item properly, should be reduced in his effectivness. Of course the gun shoots on it's own, but the better the man behind, the more often he will hit the target. Again, open to discuss this.
