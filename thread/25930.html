2 new wompa destinations have been planned for next patch. These 2 destinations are Bliss and wailing wastes.
You will notice that these 2 locations are very clan-specific.

Why did they put them in? I'll let you figure it out, but i will mention that each of my clan characters has switched to omni from levels 35 until lvl 120. Suffice it to say that armor and money is not Omni's only advantage. I am able to level 3 times is fast in omni-territory, and that's no joke.

Ironically, even neutrals find it difficult to get to 20k 'the supreme leveling spot'. Partially this may be because omni and clan guards occasionally take a shot at neutrals on general principle.

Note that to us the relative leveling speeds may seem like conjecture. To a server op it is somewhat different. He can look at the database and see that Omni levels nX as fast between the levels of x and x. With the understanding that omni and clan will average to the same playing skill it will become apparent which side finds leveling easier - but this only becomes evident after the end of the amnesty.

Aside from all this travelling through omni trade is possible at any level for clan, but it can take a long time to figure out how. I have a route i was taking at lvl 20 with an alt - and she made it across 75% of the time with no deaths. After a while i'd get tired of doing this and just switch the alt to omni until lvl 120.
