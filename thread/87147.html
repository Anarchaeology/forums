Liberators (Clan) Are Now Recruiting!
The Liberators have existed for about 4 months now and are a clan org looking to expand our numbers to help defend any towers that we may place. Our level range is from about 9-125 and all lvl characters are welcome.

We are not heavy on role playing but do expect good behaviour and a fun attitude. Our playerbase includes members from Europe, North America, Australia and Asia so there should always be someone on.

Visit: www.anarchy.tokyopcgamers.com for more info or give Teniko a /tell in game.
