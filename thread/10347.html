Dust Brigade and their affiliation
As most of you probably know by now, Omni-1 and several other Omni-tek areas has been attacked by terrorist commandoes from the Dust Brigade two days in a row now.

What is shocking is not so much that these attacks have taken place, nor even that these commandoes are obviously well trained and equipped, but the fact that at least some were neutrally aligned.

Does this mean that we have terrorists operating from bases in neutral territory? Is Newland city a stageing ground for their attacks on our territory?
The possibility must be investigated.
I respect the neutrals and their determination to stay out of this conflict. I do not wish to violate their territory, but neither can Omni-tek allow murderers to go unpunished due to political reasons. Perhaps it is time Omni-tek conducted a sweep of neutral areas to determine if indeed the Dust Brigade is hiding there, and if so, take action accordingly.
