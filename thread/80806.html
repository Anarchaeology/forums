An official Statement from Elite Operations
Elite Operations issues the following official statement in response to Caryn Chini Sherills insinuations regarding mining operations from her recent article An Explanation of Failed Neutrality.

The notion presented by Ms. Sherill is a complete fallacy. At the time that Elite Operations acquired the land in question at The Longest Road it was both unowned an unoccupied by any guild. Therefore EO was the first guild to state claim to the said land. Thusly, we did not engage in violent behavior to obtain it.

As a result we consider ourselves the legitimate owner of the land disputed by Ms. Sherill and we shall act accordingly to defend it.

Teri "Ladykali" Kibler
Morale Officer of Elite Operations
