An Exchange terminal in ICC.
I dont know if this suggestion has come up before but it seems so simple that I wonder why I havent seen it.

Ok there is a Market forum. People want to sell and trade items. But the players are from all around the world. So some sales are quite difficult or even impossible. So heres my idea.

An Exchange terminal in ICC.

You put in your item, you enter the name of the character who is picking up the item and the credit amount that needs to be deposited buy that player before he/she can pick it up. Of course no unique-nodrop items. And of course the spelling would be critical when exchanging items. (item for item)

You can even pass stuff to your alts this way. Just put zero for the credit amount. Or your Alt puts in a QL 1 monster part but needs 1mill credits before your Main can pick it up.

Once the second player or character does their part the first player has to come back and pick up the cash or item.

There would need to be a check pending item in the Exchange menu because you might have more than one transaction going at the same time.

This terminal would only live in ICC. This would make the ICC more of a hub of activity that I think it should be any way. Nice easy whompa access for all levels.
