Disagree almost completely. But what else is new? Somebody needs to explain to me why an Opifex can use a particular pistol and a Solitus with identical skills cannot.

Likewise, my Martial Artist has 600 Pistol skill. Tell me why he can't even put a Kaen Archibald Kolt of qlvl1 in his hand.

I hate that crap.

I agree with the diversity thing. But turning the game into EQ is not how I would personally prefer it.

Breed should have always effected skills. If you tell me that an Opifex gets +1 to Pistol every other level simply by leveling, I could completely understand that at lvl200 they'd have 100 more Pistol skill than any other breed. Then drop in the Pistols that require 1200 skill to equip and I'd agree that it was basically an 'opifex-only' pistol.

But, that brings us into probably an even worse situation. If you wanted to use Pistol, you wouldn't choose any other breed. That sucks just as bad as breeds not mattering.

The only time I can accept this arbitrary nonsense like the Adv-only pistols is when there is some logic behind it. The fact that a Support Beam probably weighs 600 kilograms helps me to understand how only a 3/4 ton beast like an Atrox could wield it without tipping over.

Likewise, I don't like tell the Engineer that he 'should' got Martial Arts or Pistol. Why? Because FunCom sez so? Somebody explain to me why an Engineer of all people would be better at shooting a pistol than firing a rifle or pulling the trigger on a crossbow. There's no sense behind that. So, somebody is gonna ask for an Engineer-only Pistol that blows all others away and now every damn Engineer on the planet if forced into Pistol wielding?

That sounds quite boring and very EQ.

Work with the breeds. Let Atrox get an innate increase in Heavy Weapons or let them dual-wield a 1HB with one 2HB weapon. Give the Opifex natural multi-melee and multi-ranged increases. Maybe some day we'll see one with a machette in one hand and a pistol in the other hand.

Just please don't let me see more items like a Solitus-only shoulder item adding to Brawl. How stupid can you get? Like Solitus are the only breed that has shoulders?
