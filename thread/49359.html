A Problem
FC I hope you hear this. This has been bugging me for weeks.

You change the names of many players, without even discussing it with the player. My most famous example is Superslag, who got changed to Superslang because someone out there thinks "Slag" is offensive. Fine, but slag is also an innocent term for a pile of molten metal. Nothing wrong with that.

Now, here's the kicker. Check your random name generator databse please. You will find the last name "Semen" shows up there very often. This may indeed be a harmless name for a vital biological fluid, but a lotta people could construe this as offensive and/or not a topic for little children to be seeing (and if you think you don't have small kids playing you are sadly wrong)

My point is there's a double standard. You jusge some names wrong, yet others right, in ridiculous cases. I saw a player out there the other day called "lickmypanties". THAT's offensive to a lot more people then "Slag" I bet. Just please, think a little bit about the standards on names.
