High end game is pretty bad guys
And it's just getting worse patch by patch.

I'll have to admit, AO is damn fun up until level 75 or so. Developing your character is a blast, and the levels come at a decent pace. But most importantly, there are good places to hunt, and missions are still doable.

Between 75-100, it gets a little more monotonous. Most people just try and race to 100 for the extra IP. Missions start to suck for solo'ing, but group missions are passable and the camps get you levels too.

Boy, once you break 100 all the fun bleeds out of the game like air out of a leaky tire. There's nothing magical about 100, but by 110 or so you are extremely limited in places to hunt and things to do. Missions are damn impossible to solo unless you want to blow 6 hours and die countless times to yellows. And now you're not even guaranteed of the reward even if you clear and complete the mission.

To put it plainly, the game sucks when you're up there. With few exceptions, you are forced to group. But now with 13.0, groups are markedly less effective cause docs get aggro'd all the time. MOBs are so nasty that once the doc dies, odds are everyone dies. And it can happen in the blink of an eye.

Funcom, the game is too hard at the high levels. There's no reason that an orange mob should hit me for 1000 damage thru decent armor. No reason for it. Those little server hiccups that let mobs hit you for 2 or 3 times instantly are deadly as hell when they hit that hard.

You gotta make the game more playable, and ultimately more enjoyable past 100. My character is now stuck at 113 with no hope of hitting 114 (13.0 was the kiss of death). There is no way to efficiently gain experience, and nothing else to do. Quite frankly, there's really no reason to log in anymore. Should I sell my character and move to DAoC like the everyone else?
