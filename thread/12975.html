Sitting on that building is nothing new, and most certainly NOT limited to Omni.

Zones where there are multiple levels of supression gas are always going to be prone to these kind of "tactics". Look at MMD. Look at 2ho, okay, that's not the same thing, but the whompa so close like that makes it effectively the same for omni (i.e. someplace to run). Clan use the shops, so they are not absolved from this either.

It's a game mechanics issue. As discussed before many times though, making all cities 25%, etc, etc would scare away too much of the player base. It's a catch22 -- the game would be really fun for you for about 2 weeks, until FC changed it back or went under because of mass cancellations. I mean, look at all the stink when Mort was changed. It's all about making most of the people happy most of the time.
