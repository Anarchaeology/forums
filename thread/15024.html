The King isn't dead....
I had recieved a mission to go spy on someone. Like most jobs, it was a blur of where, by when, and for how much.

Good pay, and a chinese suit shirt to boot. I could use something a bit more than my ratty trenchcoat! The only drawback: it was in territory unknown to me and I'd have to travel by foot overland to get to the insertion point.

But I had a decent idea where it was, so I took the Whompa from Tir to Newland and from there to Athen. I knew it was in Athen Shire after consulting my map, so Athen was a good starting point. I slipped off my coat, slipped into my battle gear, unslung my weapon and left the city and headed roughly west, passing through a light forest and a small outpost while trudging through the pouring rain. The clouds were so heavy with rain that it was almost dark enough for me to have to use my shoulder lamp. At least there was no lightning like there had been back in Tir. There was some light chatter over the comms, but this place was fairly deserted. Only fools like me were out running in the rain.

After passing out of the woods, the rain stopped and I ran under the leaden sky out into the desert. It was desolate, even for a desert. Hardly any wildlife to be seen. Those few living things that I saw were not worth the ammo to shoot. But still I was nervous about being out alone. It's easy to wander into very dangerous territory out in the wilderness.

At first, the desert was thin, with a few ravines and high dunes. It started to deepen and flatten out into rolling dunes as I approached what I knew must be my insertion point. I saw the town from a long way off. It was squat, dark grey and foreboding. I thought of stories I had read of shadowy ruins buried deep in the desert hiding blasphemous horrors, and I got a real bad feeling about this, but the pay was high enough that I pressed on.

As I approached, I saw that this town was not a town so much as a ruined outppost. Some major battle had totally devastated this place. The buildings and walls I had seen from a distance were all broken and blackened from battle and what must have been a firestorm in the aftermath.

I slowed my pace. If bad things lived in this desert, surely this was their lair. I entered inside the shattered remnants of the outer wall, alert for danger. I saw the rust-coloured skeletal remains of a man, an Atrox, judging from the size of the ribcage and length of it's limbs. Probably a fallen soldier lost in the battle that ruined this place.

I looked up from the dead soldier, and my heart caught in my chest as I saw a dark figure standing on a building top, observing me. I quickly targetted it and saw to my profound dismay that it registered as deep red. It was far stronger than I. I was a deadman.

But it didn't move to attack. It just stood there, and I realized it wasn't a monster, it was a person. Was he on a mission here as well? Or perhaps he was recruiting for an organization by waiting for seasoned professionals to reach this place? I half-jokingly shouted out that he had scared the crap out of me...

But no, something was still wrong. No time for relief. I saw now that he was from Omni-tek. My enemy. A very high level enemy. He shouted back a query as to why I was afraid. I quickly checked the area I was in and saw that the suppression level was high enough that we couldn't attack each other. Out of dumb luck, I was safe.

I told him this place had given me the creeps, and that him just appearing out of thin air like that had startled me. I also remarked he was the first member of Omni I had ever seen. I noted my luck about the zone we were in. He said he would have defended himself, but not attacked. I'm not so sure. I would be easy prey for him...

By this time, he had descended from the roof and walked over to a mostly intact outbuilding. We no longer had to shout to each other. I began to relax again. He had begun to tell me he was on some kind of quest when a Buckethead scavenger strolled past us. I did a quick target and saw it was harmless, but my new "companion" reacted as if he had been stung. "The King is back, I have to get out of here!" he said.

The King? What could he be talking about? I pondered this and started to pose the question to him as he pounded the Buckethead to oblivion in a lightning-fast combo of martial arts moves.

I turned around to face him and the remains of the techno-scavenger and was about to ask again what the hell he meant by "The King" when I saw it.

I have seen engineers and their droids before. I have seen the kind that looks like an emaciated black metal ape, lean and powerful, with sharp claws and an unblinking photoreceptor mounted in a head that no ape has ever had. They have large drum-shaped protuberances on their shoulders, giving the impression of great power in their arms. What I saw now was that same basic machine, only many times larger. It stood as tall as the largest building roof. I knew what it's name was before I targetted it: The King.

This thing must rule a band of techno-scavengers, who were appearing all through the rubble now. I moved out of it's field of view and asked "Cobrastouch," the Omni, if he could take it out. "No way," was his response, but even as I ducked behind a wall out of sight of the thing, he moved to engage it.

I quickly asked if a temporary team was even possible for us, but he had already begun combat and was far too busy to answer. I moved from cover and was going to do my best to keep us both alive. Not only had he caught the attention of The King, but a Buzzsaw scavenger as well. I moved closer and the Buzzsaw spotted me. It broke off from Cobratouch and began moving towards me, leaving Cobrastouch and The King locked in mortal combat.

I was under attack! I heard the circular saw that served it as a right hand spin rustily to life as it swung it high in the air over it's head and brought it down against me, glancing off my armour. My targetting scan showed it wasn't too tough. Tough enough, but I had fought creatures as bad and survived before...but could I finish it in time to be able to try and help Cobrastouch kill The King to save us both?

I opened with a burst from my Mauser SMG, and continued to lay firepower into it as I activated a healing-over-time nano. It would keep healing me by small amounts for the next few minutes. Hopefully enough time for me to kill this bizarre thing, which resembled a man fashioned from spare parts, swathed in rags and with a multitude of mismatched lenses in it's unfinished head.

But it was slashing through me too fast! I used a first aid stim and it brought me back a bit, but I couldn't heal fast enough to keep up with the incredible damage it was inflicting upon me. I kept firing and used an instant heal nano, but it was still not enough, as it's next cutting blow did as much damage as that and more! Even with my nano-enhanced skills, my shots weren't finding their target often enough, and when they did, they seemed to be pitifully ineffective against it's metal hide.

This was a lost cause. The Omni was insane for leaping against something of that magnitude. I wasn't about to let his rash actions kill us both! I ran. I broke off and ran for the nearest dunes, hoping that once it lost sight of me it would also lose interest. They could dine on Omni flesh, but they wouldn't have a Clansman for dessert! "This mission is over! They can keep their money and their lousy shirt!" I thought to myself.

It was fast. We fixers are notoriously fast, but this rusting wreck with it's whining buzzsaw was keeping up with me, slashing as it came, bringing me perilously close to death. I fumbled for my speed-enhancing nanos, and activated one hoping for a burst away from this hellhole. But to my horror, I realized my computer belt was full! I had no more memory left to run another nano-program!

Another blow fell, and I started to stagger, slowing me. I knew it was over, then. I struggled in vain to deactivate one of my other programs to make room to activate the speed booster. But the final blow fell and the sandy desert floor came up to meet me.

I woke up in Tir, where I had my last cell scan done. I reminded myself to never complain about the rising cost of insurance! I checked the comms and put out a signal via private channel to Cobrastouch. He had fallen, as well.

Perhaps I'll see him again, but probably not. If we do meet, it will most likely be when Omni and the Clans clash in open warfare. I hope I'm stronger by then. And before then, I'll be back for The King...but this time with friends.

Jarekloss, Fixer.
