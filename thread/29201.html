Our oregin is realy the memory from a drunk and bitter Engi.

Some time ago my boss had promised me to improve some issues in the corporation,but as time went by i found out that nothing was being done,and probably never would either.

I pored up another glass of "Hit the floor Jack", and drank it all in one go. I was starting to get realy drunk, and by the way the bartender looked at me i must have been a mess.

Suddenly a person comes running into the room,desperat and shouting, "I NEED A MEDICAL SUIT,PLEEEASE". A freindly person shouted back that there was a Vender selling them not far from the bar.
The person looking nervous arround the bar, and shouted.
"The damn vender whont serve me, i'm a clanner". People looked up from there drinks, and stopped talking.
The bartender looked and him and firmly told him to leave since he was not welcome there.
The desperat clanner now had a sad look on his face,"Calm down,I'm leaving,I just wanted a damn Medical suit".
Still standing there he souted one last time, " Please, i beg of you, i need a Medical suit, Ill pay with everything i have on me,please help me!.
I looked at him and tried to tell him that i would buy him one, but was to drunk to speak the words.

I remember 2 members of omni-pol draging the almost crying man out of the bar.
The room started to spin in all directions,not just around and around as it did earlier, and i tried to grab hold of the Bar, but fell of the chair and landed on my back.
The next thing i remember was the face of a BIG Atrox bending down over me and saying "Are you ok? I think its a good idea if you go home to get some sleep!"

I woke up the next morning with the worst "hang over" i have ever had. The bright light riped threw my eyeleds as i tried to close 'em and fall asleep again.

I got up and walked over to the couch stumbeling over my own feet,I fell and landed in the middle of my living room.

As i was laying there i started to think of the man last night, about how my work was a pain and how the relations between omni and clan resulted in people with no access to a number of things,including Medical suits.

Suddenly a loud squeeking noise broke the silence!

"Why are u not at work?"
"I.. I..."

"Yes, i ca..."
"Why arent you at work? You're suppose to be an engineer! Do you realy think you can just stay home when ever u feel like it? Get your ass down here,pronto! Are you listening? Hello......"

I droped the intercom on the floor, and sat down in my favorit chair.

I contacted my boss and told him that his best engi ever had found something better to do,and that he could take my job and...... Well,you know.
