Very RP Suggestion
Something that would be cool for those of us who like parties in the clubs but are tired of the repetetive music.

DJ booths. Only one player would be able to use a booth in a club at any given time. Once it is used, the user would be connected as the feed to a shoutcast type streaming mp3 server at funcom's end (since I doubt many have the bandwidth to serve a whole party from their computer while playing ao). If the music quality was streamed at maybe 24/32kbps, mono it would take a very small amount of bandwidth per partygoer.

Or some variant thereof.

Basically just a way for the parties to get some real music to hang out in rather than the few rapidly repeaded beatsamples that are in there now. It could make parties a lot more fun.

Once you can invite others into your apartment, you could use the same technology for the clock radio or some such and everyone could listen to the music in the apartment.

Just something I think would be neat.
