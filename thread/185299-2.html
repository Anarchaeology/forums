THANK YOU ARK & GSP!
Today on Rimor, ARK and GSP got together to spin a MARVELLOUS party to celebrate the 100th generation of ARKs. I was fortunate to be able to attend. The atmosphere was more festive than I have ever seen it on Rimor and the turnout was so overwhelming that we managed to crash Reets.

You want my reaction? On behalf of myself and those members of Fur n Feathers who were able to log in and attend, our reaction to the event is: AWESOME! Please, sir, may we have some more?

Lightshows- ARK has outdone itself in their lighting special effects, lending a downright disco atmosphere to the normally overly-bright Reets Glass Dancefloor

Giant Dancers- They so stole the show while they were on the floor. Cooler than the invention of Leet Piss (vodka & gingerale, thanks Tarryk!)

Multi-DJ musical show- GSP, you rock. But then, you already know that. Riakoo, even your technical difficulties are fun. Nothing quite like partying down to fixer-sped leet-sung punk tunes.

Contests: Hey, who can help but love contests that have such keen door prizes as teddybears? I didn't win, but I was still having a ball. No. Really. I won't kill all the white manz just to get one. That was just the usual Reet-like silliness.

Greenie Engagement: It's so ROMANTIC! Greenie luv. Good luck, you two!

Thank you all for the best party I've attended on either server in my entire time gaming with AO.
