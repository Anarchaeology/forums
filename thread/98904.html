Tuesday, Feb 11th 2003
From the desk of Gollar
quote:

Greetings you Testers!

As you all have been informed, the Player-Wipe will take place on Wednesday the 12th Feb 2003.
We would all like to invite you for a few events to take place on the Tuesday 11th Feb prior to the wipe on Test-Server.
Gollar has a bunch of fun entertainment ligned up for you all, so make sure you get that time marked "dark red" on your calendar.

Events will take place from 3pm EST till 11pm EST (21GMT till 05GMT).

Here is a short brief of intended happenings:
- Costume-Handout for the complete events (as far as possible)
- "Pin the tail" on Bug Hunters (not ubered, but rather like being a lvl-1 player)
- Dance-Contest (gimme "Saturday-Night-Fever")
- BH will try to compete to your dance-skills
- Lunch at some Burger-Bar (beware of some unforseen surprises)
- Vehicle racings...

So much more to come... we might just come up with some more surprises (perhaps a chat with some FC-Employees... who knows).

So, be sure to attend and bring lots of booz and friends. Let them know what fun we can have on Test, not only prior to Wipe but also post-wipe.
