Envisioned - Ability to capture towns/territory
Ok funcom, where do neutrals stand in this impending disaster? I think the ability to capture towns and territory is great, it will add a real flair to a game that should seem like a war. However, you have taken away from us (us being neutrals) the ability to shop in clan or omni towns, the ability to use clan or omni insurance terminals, the ability to use clan or omni mission terminals. If our towns up and disappear to one of the larger factions and the affiliations for the machines changes with it, where will we shop/save/get missions from? Where will new neutrals start in? Don't tell us we'll have to take a town back before we get access again, as I can practically count the number of lvl 100+ neutrals on both hands if it even needs more than one. I can guarantee you that within one day of this being implement all neutral territory will cease to exist and various omni/clan organizations will set up feifdoms of the various towns. This is a serious concern for neutrals, please give us a response
