Freshly Brewed Fun
Conquistador Coffee, the premier importer of quality beverage to Rubi-Ka is always seeking new people.

We are not 'Hardcore' or 'n00b' we are a social bunch of people who like to hang out together and have fun.

We like friendly orgs and people with a sense of humor. We dislike d00ds and greedy ninja looting campers. We range from a teeny level 4 up to level 140+ with all aspects covered.

We have a good rep and some nice allies. We expect our members to be forthright, honest and great to team with.

So if you think you have something to offer and want to see what CCI can do for you, get in touch with some of our most famous salespeople!

Femtosecond, Lunashade, Chinarain, Filigree, Gharn, Sysangel, Bonniefoo, Karatenyllet.

If you have money and want to bribe us then you'd better speak to the boss, Mr Vandamar. He handles the sleazy stuff!
