Heh, you asked for it...

From my sister Orgela, her Angel Moon Anthology, the first of these are old and posted long ago. There are some more recent ones at the end however.

This series of haiku (or haiku-like poetry for you purists out there) was written over a period of a month after my blinding love-at-first-sight of Angelynne settled into a never-ending fascination and longing ache for her lovely Opifex butt. Angelynne and I are both Metaphysicists on RK1, our hearts and souls forever entwined.

Chasing breathlessly.
Angel Moons Anthology
Laughing in my eyes.

My Angel, woven
From inexplicable Void.
My heart, Moon-caught, Sings.

Gale-shaken leaves torn
From complacent wandering.
Angel-kissed new Dawn.

An Angel Moonrise!
Sensually framed between
Long legs, arching back.

Summer Moon-gazing.
Wide-eyed imaginings of
A still further Shore

(chasing Agelynne, wearing much-too-short shorts, across Stret West Bank)

My eyes sigh, then chase
The steamy-locomotive
Of my Angel's butt.

My Angelic Love
Sensibilities undone
Scent of Rainy-Moon

Sleepy-eye's view
Past the rise and fall of an
Angel's morning-Moon

Pooled summer rain in
Hollows of an Angel Moon.
Fingers wandering.

Across Angel's Moon
Glistening delectably;
Freshly-squeezed peach.

Two ripe pears peeking
From under a fallen leaf.
The thrill of tasting.

(running toward Bliss, at night, from the LR outpost...Rubika's two moons hung heavy over Bliss as I ran behind Angelynne as I like to do)

Moons-framed in Bliss, an
Angel Moon rises tonight
Against both. I gasp.

(upon me leaving Independent Rubikans to form Celestial Emanations with Angelynne)

Bright Stars in my Heaven.
My Angel's Moon-lit path calls,
Yet I roam not far.

(Angelynne in Yalmaha...stuck in a tree)

Gloomy gnarled tree,
Snatching at my Angel's Moon.
Scratchy fingers.

Moon-gazing through leaves
Fingers caressing it's Orb
Shining white Angel

Angel's Moon rising.
Drenched in another day,
My sleepy eyes wake.

Moon-captivated!
Unaware of Angel's eyes
Over-shoulder smile.

Head raised, gasping air.
Falling still into night-Moon
On lips: Angel-rain.

(The following four I call the Mission Haiku Quatrain)

Chasing vapor trails.
My Angel's metallic-ass
Across Rubika

Impetuous Moon!
Angel dancing close with Death.
Nostrils flare with thrill.

Suddenly alone,
Lamenting-not brave-death.
Fallen Angel Moon.

Reclaim tenderness,
Gazing into Angel-eyes.
Two Hearts: Ecstasy.

(outside Angelynnes Borealis apartment waiting for her to arrive)

Scurry little roach!
Only the Moon is patient.
Stomp! No Angel home.

(her inability to reach Rubi-ka, and those frustrations)

Narcoleptic Moon!
I cry the seasons of my
Angels frustrations.

Watching empty skies.
Waiting for Angels Moonrise
To brighten my eyes.

Moon-imagining,
Since that is all I can do.
Angel out of reach.

Puddle in my palm
Angels scent in every breath
Moon-imagining

Waiting for Moonrise
Cycles of Seasons pass by
Sweet Angel of Memory

Scent and sight unmade
I surrender to her Moon
Embracing Angel-dreams

(on joining Desert Winds after disbanding Celestial Emanations)
(this first was written for Naxilite when Angel joined DW)

On Summers goodbye
Caught! My Rainy Angels Moon
I laugh your demise.

Gentle austral breeze.
My head dizzy with the scent
Of an Angel Moon.

Plundering my eyes:
The hard-rain of Angels Moon
Night-dream falls to wake.

(on leaving Desert Winds  our obligations left unfulfilled)

Silently Twin Moons,
Drop behind winter storm clouds
The Desert grows still.

Sleep little Angel.
Curled tight with winter Moon
They doze together.
