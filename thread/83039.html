As I have found out recently, RK 2 is alive and well with RP, and in fact I will go out on a limb and say the role players of all factions have a much better respect for each other as players than the RP'ers on RK 1. On Rk 1 all I see groups that seem to be unable to agree with others what RP should be. When I play on RK 2 I see groups, do thier very best to let everyone and anyone RP with them

Sure there are still jerks who like to make things difficult for them, that will happen anywhere, but they seem to have been much stronger as players to handle it.

I also have to respect the the majority of the RP'ers from Rk 2 don't waste time with posting thier dirty laundry and public ego trips, which I have (not saying always or trying to point fingers) seen happen a lot on RK 1

I believe both servers have great role players on them But I think Rk 2 is much more supportive of the community as a whole.

Anyway...Just my opinion from playing there for the last few months
