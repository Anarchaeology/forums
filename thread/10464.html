I believe the in game community IS finally starting to come to life
I was reading various posts here and on other boards on the web from people saying that AO does not have the sense of community that say Everquest has....and I did agree, at first.

However, I think people forgot that that community did not evolve over night in UO , Everquest, OR AC.

The other day, prior to patch day, Avalon and Camelot was invaded by Omni Slayerdroids and Juggarnaut. I have to say how impressed I was to see the Clan of all levels, pull together and really get involved in the battle, even though it was unorganised, and communication was poor, (it WAS rather chaotic )(okay at times it was a huge clusterfkjld ) people pulled out of miissions, (summoned by word of mouth) guilds came forth and alot of us had a heck of a good time!

After 5 deaths , I had to log off and never did see what happend, but I tell you I made more new freinds that day than I did in all the months since AO went live , hope that this is just the beginning of this sort of thing. I do wish they would have dropped the suppression gas in Camolot so the Omni players who were hanging out there rooting for their droids could have taken part in it , instead of just running from the guards... heck when the juggarnaut attacked , even the players in their teens were running around taking shots just to join in, ROFL you should have seen the stacks of corpses.

I really think though the population of this game will never be huge, maybe in the end those left will form a tight friendly community , and heck I would stick around dispite all the bugs , if that were to happen We've got alot of good people playing this game , Thanks for a great time !

<Toast to all those who participated and died in Avalon that day >
<thinks we really need to get used to taking orders though, something we clanners may learn from Omni IMHO , LOL >
