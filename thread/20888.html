It all depends on what armour the other player uses. My char, for example, has very good protection against cold damage, so you wouldn't do well if you use that. Chemical weapons would work very nicely, though... It all depends. Each armour set has it's weaknesses.

As for mobs, the general rule is that Cold/Heat/Chemical/Radiation works better than Melee/Projectile/Energy. It depends on which monster you are fighting here too, but you are basically always better of with a cold based weapon than an energy based one. Cold ones are much more rare though.
