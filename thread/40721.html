to many Nano programs have same icons
I tend to play an NT, and this is were i noticed it - a lot of my nukes have the same, or "sameish' looking icons.

Created a doc to check them out, and thier heals are the same way. Sameish icons.


Is thier some way you can alter this? Put the QL lvl on the icon, put the initals on the icon. Icons are ment to be a quick visual key as to what your dealing with. But if the icons are all the same, it can become confusing.
