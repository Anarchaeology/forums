However, that sounds like EQ to me - and all they had was an abuse of tradeskill mules. Don't ask me - I don't have an answer.

Be all that as it may, the bottom line is this: great ideas, wrong game. Go play a MUSH/MUX - roleplay 100% with 0 true 'levels' - especially none that are visible. I can even reccomend some I've roleplayed. However, AO isn't a true RP game. The 'RPG' in MMORPG doesn't mean roleplaying game in a true sense - it means it in the D&D sense - dice, character stats, etc. You understand the dilemma.

As opposed to trying to change a great game (yes, it has problems. Yes, one of them is in my sig. However, despite the work it needs, I'm playing it, and nothing else right now - right now ) (What a tangent), try to find a game that suits your needs. Its that simple. You won't change AO into the game you want. So, find something that suits your needs. And then come back to play AO with us once in awhile

Despite that - this is a bump. Enjoy.
