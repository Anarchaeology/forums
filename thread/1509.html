RK1-RK2, The Underground!
In an attempt to stabalize communications between the shadier side of Rubika1 & 2, AN Egroup has been setup to provide reliable mass communication to all those within the underground who choose to take advantage of it.


OOC - AO-fixers forums are up and down and never really work well, so i've come up with, what i think is a good alternative since funcom isn't moving fast enough to provide the communication we need in game, at least it will now be available out of game.

The Egroup is not limited to fixers however, I realize that many classes of people have deep connections with the underground element spanning both dimensions, and so it is open to all who feel that the Fourth Faction on RK1 & RK2 is whats right for them.


Fourth, being the Underworld of course.

And possibly the biggest faction, as it infests the clans, neuts, and OT all together.
