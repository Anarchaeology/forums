New PvP /duel command
Id love to add a duel command to the game that will allow players to practice PvP even in non-PvP areas. While the arena serves a purpose, they dont allow for practice under the conditions that are likely to exist in normal PvP situations. There is also the potential for outsiders to interfere in a match.

/dual
Sends a request to the targeted player to request a duel. The targeted player will receive a dialog box for confirmation that will display the request along with any modifiers. If the request is sent from one team leader to another, the request will be for the two teams to duel each other. If the targeted player is currently in a fight or has the sender ignored, the command will not go through.

When the dual is accepted all participants in the dual will see a 15 second countdown. At the conclusion of the countdown all PvP gas protection from the opposing players will be removed. During the PvP match, the participants will not be able to zone or equip a vehicle.

One or more of the following modifiers can follow the dual command:
  • nodeath
    Player will only go unconscious when they go below 1 hitpoint. The unconscious player can still /terminate if they want to. The unconscious player can be simulated by a 1 hour hostile mezz that cannot be broken by damage. The /duel over command by the opponent is the only way other than terminate to remove its effects.

    If the nodeath option is specified, then no experience points can be gained for killing mobs while dueling. An unconscious player will still have aggro and can be attacked and killed by mobs. A conscious player will always draw aggro before and unconscious one. An unconscious player under attack by a mob will not take damage, but will have a 5% per attack of being killed.
  • nobuff
    All buffs will be removed from the participants at the start of the dual
  • handicap x%
    Opponent will have all their damage modified by x%. The percent can be positive or negative.
  • timelimit x
    The PvP battle will last at most x minutes long. After which all unconscious players will wake up and PvP restrictions will return to normal.
  • over
    Causes any unconscious opponents to wake up. This is the way to signify the end of a duel if the nodeath option was chosen. When the player wakes up they will have 1 hit point and 1 nano point. An unconscious player cannot use the /duel over command.
