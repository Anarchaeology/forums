Social Clothing : Why no armor?
Is there something unbalancing about wearing social clothing and armor at the same time?

I was surprised to find that when I purchased a black martial arts suit from the clothing vendor that even though it is back slot like my green cloak, I could not equip it without removing all of my armor.

I see that it is good to have an idea of what an opponent is wearing, but since we can already wear cloaks and trenchcoats with armor (that cover up everything except boots and gloves), why not let us wear whatever other clothing we want that will fit?

I still think the suggestion of a clothing tab (seperate from the armor tab) would be great, but at the very least, why not make back slot clothes fit with normal armor?

Another thing that would help for the pvp side is to make different clothing flags (i.e. Loose, Normal, Tight) that would be wearable only with certain types of armor (Loose will go over anything - cloaks, trenchcoats, etc; Normal would fit over most armor - excepting heavy armors; Tight would only fit over the lightest types of armors).

It would help remedy the fact that most high levels all seem to look very similiar And besides...I want my kung fu suit =)
