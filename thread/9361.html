My impression is that it is ok to play ma at lower levels. The real problem starts around lvl 80-100, when they start hitting the min dmg barrier. I have given my ma a hammer to overcome this problem, but I'm already starting having trouble dealing with yellow mobs (lvl 63). I don't exactly feel like a powerhouse. Currently using ~lvl 70 implants. It's a bit sad that you have to twink your character to be able to do 50% missions, and there definately is something wrong with the balancing between fast, low dmg weapons and slow, high dmg weapons at higher lvls / AC. The low dmg weapons (like ma fists) will eventually end up doing min dmg agains high ac mobs, resulting in reflect shields returning more dmg than the wielder can deal. I think the only real solution to this problem is to reevaluate the whole system with armor and ac. I suggest that insted of a fixed amount of 1/10 of the ac being subtracted from the dmg, a certain percentage of the dmg is subtracted. Of course the max percentage should be no higher than, lets say, 80 %. If the current ac system is to be kept, the dmg of fast weapons would have to be upped conciderably at higher lvls.
