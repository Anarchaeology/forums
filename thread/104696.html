Yohon, here's the thing. There is a group of terrorists known as the Neo-Luddite Movement. They advocate a complete ban on thinking machines and robots specifically.

Using a flaw in a standard component found in variety of different models and makes of robots, they have discovered a way to subvert ordinary robot's programming and turn your humble domestic bot into a relentless killing machines.

The NLM believe if they can engender enough fear in the general population against robots, they will succeed in getting bots banned. This most not be allowed to happen, if for no other reason that my company, Rubi-Ka Universal Robots, would soon go out of business and I would end up working in my dad's fake-Italian restaurant in Borealis.
