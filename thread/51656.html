Rubi-Ka-Unique
Since rk1/rk2 is already full of level 200s and any new content added will be quickly sucked up. I think a new planet should be created.. Rubi-Ka-Unique. On this planet, all outdoor camps will give twice as much XP, all unique mobs have a 20% chance of spawning every 20 minutes (kinda like how TK will be), have about 10 camelot castles, one placed in each popular city.. just totally overwhelm RK-Unique with Uniques. Tir, Omni-Trade, Omni-Ent, Newland and West Athens will all have a 50% Suppression Field (no fighting 'til after level XX).. or maybe even 25%.

The point in this is to give these bored-as-hell level 200s something to do. I personally would jump at the chance to start a noob character out. A planet full of noob characters enjoying the world *again*.

Sure, after 5 or 6 months, it'll have a ton of high level (power-leveled) campers, but the whole idea is to get our money for a few more months, and not have us quit due to lack of high level content, right Funcom?

I think you're misunderstanding Nedian's post. Creating such a server wouldn't mean "every 1 would have everything". It would mean everyone would be able to get everything - EVENTUALLY. They would still have to start from scratch, levelling up, killing the mobs. The difference would be that along the way, those elusive uniques that they wouldn't ever have a chance to acquire on the regular servers would be available. And while those items would lose some of their appeal because of their improved availability, gameplay would be more interesting, because there would be more items to customize ones character with.

If, by some miraculous twist of fate (), this proposal would be implemented, I think the new server should only be available to those who already have very high level characters, say 190+. Otherwise, no one would play on RK1 and RK2 anymore. Setting this restriction would add a goal for players, perhaps making the journey up a bit more bearable.
