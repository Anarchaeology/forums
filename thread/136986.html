This is yet another plea to fix the Agent Disguise Kits.

SUGGESTION ONE: LIFO QUE THE KITS

This very SIMPLE fix would be to make a flag adjustment that let one kit "overwrite" another. Right now, you must let one kit wear off before another can be used. If you could use another immediately, agents could switch sides at will. (As in, "let's see... we're about to enter Clan territory, so I should use the passport for....").

If you think rapid flip-flops are "too powerful," remember that agents will be constantly messing up their org membership, with major chat channel hassles, as well as losing their neckboards whenever they are "unnaturally" aligned.

Also bear in mind that high level players can pretty much travel at will anyway. At full defense, in a yalm, you can enter any whompas or grid term you want and survive, even with the latest anti-yalm logic. Given that reality, kits are just a nice sublty that lends color to the agent profession, and gives youngsters the ability to travel more widely, like a senior player.

The ability to flip-flop like this becomes especially meaningful in Shadowlands, since it would allow the agent to adjust his alignment to redeemed/unredeemed to enter the garden of his choice, and to help his entire team's alignment, which may their rate or ability to collect faction points.



SUGGESTION TWO: CHANGE THE DURATION

If you're afraid that the LIFO QUE idea will make agents "too powerful," here's an equally simple adjustment that would at least restore them to quasi-usefulness:

Make all disguise kits, regardless of level, have a duration of 4 hours. Simple change to the item data. That's it.

This is enough to prevent rapid flip-flops, covers about one evening's activities, and won't bleed over to numerous days and weeks into the future.

Note that kits of all QLs are still needed, since as an agent advances, low-level kits can no longer be used, and a higher level kit is still required.

I know it's tempting to tie duration to QL, but in this case, it just HURTS gameplay rather than enriching it.
