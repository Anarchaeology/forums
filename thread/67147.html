Community Update - Nov 8
Patch 14.6.0 out, server side patch yesterday, and 14.6.1 going out now. What can I say? I've been swamped the last few days, and will probably spend some of the weekend simply catching up.

I've gotten a lot of e-mail, and only had time so far to reply to some. I can't guarrantee I will reply to all I got either, and I hope people understand that.

Professionals and profession issues
We're closing in on an agreement in our discussions, and it's looking good. I would like to stall a little bit on profession issues until I know how we will handle them from now on. Better to see the hand I'm playing with before playing the cards.

Patches and patch activity
I'm sorry to say I doubt I will be able to keep an equal information flow with each patch as I had with this one. The information will come, but probably not as frequent. This because I can't just put all other work aside for several days (and I need food and sleep too ).

The Notum Wars
Today we sent out more invitations to the beta, and more will come next week. We're very happy with the participants so far, and it's all just looking better and better.

---

Apart from that, not much to tell right now. Might come up with more later.
