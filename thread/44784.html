NANOMAGE LIBERATION FRONT Looking for Dedicated MAges and Atrox!
The NLF is pleased to announce the opening of its new feeder guild, the NLF Training Camp. Here we will help to ease the transition of creating a neutral character and guide you toward level thirty. Then it is off to the NLF, where you will become a warrior in the struggle to free the nanomage from impending extinction. You have probably heard of us through news, roleplay, or events on rubi ka. We are looking for any and all neutral nanomage and atrox players who pine to fight for the noble cause! We are especially looking for bold Nanomages who wish to break the mold and choose the less typical nanomage professions, particularly doctors, traders, and solsiers at the moment. Know this: You will have fun, you WILL roleplay, and you will be well taken care of. Also seeking Bold Atrox who dare to step up and be part of the queens ROYAL ATROX BODYGUARD. Send headmaster Bremner a tell, or any of his teachers at the training camp: Junestar, Zanzi, NLFHelper.
