GridStream Productions Returns
After (as well as during) tonight's edition of Talk Deviant, GridStream Productions is proud to announce that we're back.

No need to beat around the bush with silly PR-value banter, let's just get right down to it, shall we?

The new site, twice as azzkicking as the original, thanks to Xtrophic: http://www.gridstream.com

The GridBot, an incredible tool designed and maintained by Gridfan: "/tell gridbot help" in-game

The new stream address link, just a tad bit easier to remember: stream.gridstream.com with winamp or realplayer (no, you don't have to change from www.live365.com/play/gridstream they both point to the same location).

New DJ's. GridStream welcomes the awesome DJ Tristalyn back into it's ranks, and we have several more DJ's on the way (as of yet we cannot announce who, but you'll see them soon enough!). Welcome aboard, Tristalyn! Also, our director Wyldthing has become quite the kickass DJ herself! h00t!

New Organization. Check out our site to register on the listener forums, and to access the automatic scheduler! Schedule GSP to help liven up your RK1 party!

(okay, so that last bit was useless PR banter...I apologize, sometimes I lose myself to the hands of proper business)
