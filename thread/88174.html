Starting a new group
I'll be starting a new character soon (Neutral), and am looking to start a guild that will embed itself into the storyline of Rubi-ka.

My primary idea will sit as:

Diplomatic agency that seeks to bring peaceful resolutions to potentially large conflicts.

Simple, and not fully worked out yet. This is where you come on..those of you who are interested in joining, anyway.

I'm looking for the following:

No limit on level. 1-200 is great.

No tax. Tax is silly, dedicated guildies will put into the bank if they feel the desire

Roleplaying will be important but not ENFORCED. Guild chat is just that..chat. vicinity chat will be prefered In-character.

Mainly this guild will be for friends to start stories, mission/hunt together, help one another, etc.

I would prefer to avoid highly twinked characters (low level character with high level alts to funnel funds from), but I certainly don't care as long as you are a roleplayer. There are no strict guidelines to the RP, either. It's free-form.

Any of you who might be interested, send an email to thebull@awpti.org

I'll try to get back to you via email or via /tell in-game.
