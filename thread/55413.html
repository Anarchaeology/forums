Frustration King
Why is the Trash King not on a 10 minute timer like his lackeys ? Right now it's random, we have no way of knowing when he will be back, so when we do the quest the only thing we can do is sit. And wait. And wait. And wait some more. Then you log out and log in an alt so you can actually do something. A while later you log back in and yup, there he is. At 1% health and about to go down. So, you wait. And wait. You get angry, you get frustrated, wondering what could possibly the reason for his slow spawn.

It's not like the reward for the quests is a good reason for a slow spawn. He doesn't drop any loot either, and the xp isn't worth it.
Why is he on a slow spawn ? When Dodga or Alvin sends me on a quest to tag Trash King, he should be there.
The ability to complete a quest should NEVER depend on luck.
