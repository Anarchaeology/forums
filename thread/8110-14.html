Some thoughts
Gaute

As a EQ player of 2 years, I have some ideas for you. Yes I'm sure the dev team probably played AC, EQ, UO just like we did when they were thinking about stuff for AO.

This game lacks a few things that EQ had:

1. Fear - no not the plane the feeling. The feeling that as you traveled somewhere you could get cut down even at lvl 60 if you werent carefull. Fear made EQ fun. It was the same with dungeons, if the whole group died the corpse run was usually tense. Now I don't want to see corpse runs implemented casue frankly I hated them, big waste of precious time.

2. Accomplishment - That feeling that you and your friends really triumphed over something. You pooled your collective skills, followed a plan and totally kicked butt.

3. Large group encounters - this sorta goes with accomplishments. I found it fun to get 4 or more teams of 6 together to take something out. Raids brought guilds together in EQ and made people proud of their affiliations. Nice boon to socialization. Good raid leaders were revered in the community for their strategic abilities and managment skills.

4. Class Specific Armor - Everyone in AO wears a Primus or Nano coat past lvl 150 it seems. EQ had it's problems here to to some degree but there was variance due to rarity. But it would still be nice to see a opi MA, Trader, Fixer and Agent and sorta be able to tell them apart. We now have either people wearing omnipol and a primus coat or flowers and a primus coat.

5. Classes were a bit more defined - This is kind of a sticky point cause I agree and disagree with this. No one really contended that Warriors weren't the best tanks in EQ or that clerics weren't the best healers. Sure there was cross over in the classes but you could still see a defining point to each class. Currently in AO; 70% of the classes cary a shotgun, 10% assault rifles, 10% sniper rifles 2% nothing, 3% melee weapons. So yes there is some difference but we have a nasty trend starting where classes are becomming vanilla. As a MA the only defining point to my class is buffs cause everyone can choose to use MA attacks and everyone can choose to use dimach/brawl and everyone can use MA weapons if their skill is high enough.

6. High Level Content - Yeah I understand that you guys planned on a 4 year story and probably didn't anticipate the zeal of your player base in both the sheer power leveling and exploiting to get to higher levels fast. But at 164 there isn't much to do accept grind EXP everytime I log in. Sure better places to hunt would be nice but I would like to see missions where we have to go assasinate a super mob behind enemy lines and it's so hard that the exp equals a mission amd maybe it takes more then one group and maybe it drops MA armor that is really cool. Maybe we could get missions to raid bases or something.

In EQ I couldn't wait to get to the high levels cause there was stuff I just couldn't touch as a lvl 34 Warrior. I had to get 46 to go to the plane of Hate. I had to get lvl 50 to be effective in Hate. Stuff like that. At high levels in EQ the game changed and it envolved knowing the right people in game, planning strategy and in alot of ways was a different type of excitement from the sub 45 game.

There is alot of good in this game don't get me wrong but I'm watching alot of my friends who I started the game with leave now cause they either get tired of getting stomped in pvp by the new class of the moment or they get bored by the exp grind. You guys have so much potential here and I hope that potential is realized.

Oh and please give us 2HE MA weapons please.
