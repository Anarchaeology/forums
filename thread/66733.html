Cratto, Squadron Leader:

Omni-Tek has called in an squad of corperate mercenaries called the ORDER OF THE SHADOW. Upon arrival to Rubi-Ka and to transport to Rome, our group was ambushed and several of our new recruits were KIA.

To fill our missing ranks, the Order of the Shadow is now available for open enrollment. We are taking applicants on a very easy to gain membership basis.

Applicant Status:
New Recruit, Below Level 20 or less than a week of service.

Member Status:
Above Level 20 AND at least a week of service.

Veteran Status:
Level 40 Rank OR Extraordinary performance within organization

Advisor Status:
Finding 5 new recruits for the organization or Outstanding acts of heroism and trust in the eyes of the Order.

We Want You! All low level residents of Rubi-Ka are welcome to join us, as i will personally hand over a 2k Signing bonus to help you get on your feet.
