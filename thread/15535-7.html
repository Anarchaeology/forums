I agree, and strongly support nurfing crits, if that means making MA crit buffs self/melee only and making LLTs ranged only that would be a nice way to reduce their frequency by 50%. The ranges of funbombs weapons are silly too. If they don't do this they should just reduce the frequency of crits by 50% across the board.

Shotguns have 20-25M range and most useful and fast assault rifles have 10-15m range. I don't really get that at all. I know range is about game balance, not reality, but it seems sort of counter intuitive to me.

Funcom obviously had no vision about how frequent crits would be in both PVM and PVP. If they did, they wouldn't have had to make high level mobs have so many HPs about 10 patches ago as an emergency fix to how easy they were to kill because of crit frequency.

I also would like to see nano resist be more effective and at the same time, nano resist lowered on mobs to reasonable levels. Seeing ACE soldiers chain resist when the skill should be hard for them to rase is just silly. It's part of funcoms very divergent PVP/PVM combat systems. One of the main reasons leveling is so BORING is because mobs are so predictable. Funcom wants this, but I think they should consider making MOB combat a bit more dynamic and AI a lot better.

Fixing nano resist and crits would go a long way to balance classes.

I would also like to see only one of the 2 trader debuffs lines effect only nano skills, and the other effect combat skills, but this may not be necessary if crits and nano resist are fixed.

Finally, I'd like to see 100% damage for normal attacks again with either 40% or 30% caps.

All these changes would do a lot to balance PVP out, and dont require any class based nurfs (except for the debuff one that isnt really necessary). Furthermore, some classes should get some real love, Agents, Fixers, Advents and Nano Techs. I cant understand why it takes Funcom so long to make class adjustments, its like they only have 1 programmer working part-time only, or maybe they are just a bunch of college interns that they don't have to pay.
