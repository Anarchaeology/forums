Hey Medibird,

Good question about buying heals. You'll find that it's a total waste of money to purchase every nano in the shop. The difference from one heal to the next is quite small, so it's just not worth the expense. We get so many single target heals that you can easily skip 3 or 4 and no one will notice. The only nano that I do suggest you stay up to date with is the team health buffs. People always want that buff.

A few brief tips:
1. Get used to looking at the team window. You'll be seeing it a lot. You don't usually need to know what the hp status of the mob is, and often don't even care what it is. You need to know who needs to be healed.
2. Be very careful about chatting in combat. Your teammates can get killed that way.
3. It's useful to keep an eye out for adds. This is especially true if you don't have a crowd controller in the group. Knowing how many mobs are in the fight helps you to know what to watch for in the team window.
4. Do not be a silly doc like I was and think that health and evades are for tanks and not docs. You may not need them as much as the tank does, but you certainly need them.
5. You will get aggro. No way to avoid it. If you're not capable of doing damage, get used to moving around to limit the damage you take while the team kills the mob for you.

Being the doc can be very rewarding. Everyone will tell you how great you are. Don't get a big head over it, but have fun and enjoy it.
