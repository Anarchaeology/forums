What PvP means to me?
I am by far a PvP expert. Hell I so low in the PvP ranks I havent hit n00bie status yet. I don't have a title probably never will.

I go to Tir Arena mostly to watch while I take a break from missions and eat me a late night gamers snack. Every now and then someone I know from online will ask for a friendly duel. Most of the time we just quit at 50% sometimes (most of the time) if we go past that I will die. I have killed a few but not many. But to me that isnt what "I" think about PvP. That is dueling to see which of the two competitors is the better.

"I" think PvP is like deer hunting. 2h0 with 886 concealment waiting for the person of appropriate lvl to come in. Jump and attack sometimes they run, sometimes they die and sometimes they fight back and kick my arse ( most of the time the last one happens ). But if I die, no biggie I am not out for the title. If I wanted a title I could just pick on the green and greys. But that isn't fun. Also group vs group combat. This is the one I like the most. More strategy involved.

This is just how "I" look at PvP. Everyone is entitled to an opinion and this one is mine. So I am not saying your ideas are wrong so please flame not.
