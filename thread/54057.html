The Warrant...
0100 Rubi-Ka standard time.
Lab of Cole “DocQuest” Sanders

The acrid smell of formaldehyde permeates the air and a lone spotlight shines on an examining table sporting the remains of a life-bleeder. A figure emerges from the shadows, a large scalpel in one hand and forceps ready in the other. He smiles as he looks over the corpse and the fine condition it is in. “Mostly blunt trauma, probably a Martial Artist or one of those fool Atroxs wielding their steel support beams or hammers.” He quietly says to himself.

“The subject is a life-bleeder,” the doctor says into the air, the recording device in the room picks up every syllable and every ambient sound. “Killed near the NorthWest mines in the vicinity of the Omni-Pol outpost. Subject is approximately 220lbs and approximately 7ft in length from head to base. Beginning Y incision now.” The doctor slowly leans over the corpse and begins cutting into the cadaver.

BANG! BANG! BANG!

A loud booming voice yells, “Open up, Dr. Sanders! This is Omni-Pol! We have a search warrant and we wish to ask you some questions! If you refuse to open the door we are authorized for forced entry!” The door to Cole’s lab is then kicked in and a group of Omni-Pol elite shock troops pour into the room. One grabs Dr. Sanders and shoves him to floor. “Dr. Sanders,” the officer begins, “you are being investigated for your part in the assassination of one Jessica Kay, a high-ranking Investor of Omni-Tek. This search warrant is fully comprehensive and allows us to search and seize anything we deem pertinent to the case! Do you understand the warrant as it is presented to you?” the officer throws a pamphlet into the lap of Dr. Sanders, Cole picks it up and looks at it nonchalantly and drops it on the floor. “I understand what you are saying, but would it really matter if I didn’t?” Dr. Sanders replies. “It wouldn’t matter in the least.” The officer says as he gives the command for his men to start their search.

The officer and his soldiers begin to tear the place apart. Dr. Sanders, seemingly uninterested in the destruction of his lab, only stares at the specimen lying on his examining table. “I was there, I watched as my brothers and sisters stole the life out of Jessica Kay; but she had it coming.” Dr. Sanders says to himself. “What they did to the Intercessor…. Lane, was unthinkable. To take a man and strip his past and his dignity and to turn him into a THING! We did what was right!”

The soldiers continue to tear apart the lab, but Dr. Sanders’ eyes are fixed on the examination table. “Looks like I am going to have to pay my delivery people less next time. I prefer my specimens dead.” Dr. Sanders thinks to himself as he notices movement from the table…
