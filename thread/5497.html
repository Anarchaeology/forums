Varmint Woods
You can find them in Varmint Woods. The farther South you go (assuming you start in Newland and go south to get there), the higher their lvl. From what I have seen, they stay close to water. You may see a few as you cross from any of the Newland Flats areas to the next subzone because of the water in that area.

A few subzones in, you will find more water and more Breiflabbs in the area.

After lvl 50, I pretty much stopped seeing anything deep red in Varmint Woods.
