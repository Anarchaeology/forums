<< New Recruits Needed Immediately >>
Visions, a clan based military organization, is in need of a few more members to help get this fledgling guild off the ground. If helping a relatively new guild grow, read on!

We're currently looking primarily for people around level 50 to 70 (though this not a scrict limit, and will expand in the near future as we get established) and of any profession, who are interested in a helping a new guild grow into its full potential. We want people who are mature, familiar with the game, and willing be part of a team dedicated to working together. We have a lot of plans and ideas for our future, and a great website to coordinate the guild with, and now we need the people. A few to help get us started, and then we expect to grow larger once we have things more solidly established.

Most of us play in the evenings in US timezones (from around 4pm to 11pm PST (7pm to 2am EST) or so on weekdays, and often all day on weekends).

We aim to be a prefossional, mature organization dedicated to teamwork and taking our place in the AO story-line. We plan to have a wide variety of events and activities, and highly encourage participation.

Please visit our website at:

http://www10.brinkster.com/aovisions

for more information. Be sure to visit our Charter section, and fill out an application in the Recruitment section if you are interesting in joining. We'll also keep an eye on this topic as well.
