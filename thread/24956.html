People who don't find PvP fun don't PvP.

People who don't PvP are no longer possible opponents in PvP zones.

Eventually, when nobody on one side or the other PvPs, what is there to do? Go into Omni-Trade/Tir, kill all the guards and say 'I own you all!'?

Now, people might say that the idea isn't to give enjoyment to the other side when you kill them. But... if people die because they attacked a superior force, or got beaten by superior tactics, they get angry at themselves for being beaten/not thinking... and perhaps resolve to adapt and improve. But if people die because they see the other side using dubious tactics and abuses of game mechanics they get angry at PvP in general.


It may be a War, and by stopping one side PvPing (or "shattering their morale") you would effectively be winning... whether or not the game world changes to this... but where would the fun be if one side was the only force on the battlefield? Peace (or even 'occupation', such as France in the middle of World War II) is far less fun than conflict.
