The Dark Carnival is banished
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Despite tortuitous statements and constant mocking, Truwind, Aberic and his "representative" Machiavelli failed.

Truwind was found guilty of not only ignoring (and going against) orders (implied or real) as for his actions during the trial (constant disrespect and mocking of those present) This was found in a vote of 10 to 0.

He was fined 25million credits, his guild was banned from events for two months, and must write an apology to Radiman to be posted in the news.

Aberic was found guilty of attempting to kill Radiman. This was found in a vote of 9 to 1 (one person said he was insane, and should not be held accountable)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Okay, now here comes the somewhat OOC analysis of what happened.

Truwind avoided most every question, responding with things equivilant to "why should I answer this?" and "my guild is better than yours". Was insulting, and luckily Derva and Bulletr saw it and tacked it onto his charges.

Aberic remained in character, and I really can't say much about him. Acted crazy, and that was fine.

The problem I feel (as do many other people who I spoke with) was due to some rather problem people at the event. Most being from Dark Carnival (you'll see who I'm referring to once logs are posted) who couldn't refrain from swearing and insulting people in very OOC ways. About 4 or 5 DC members were booted from the event for harassment of people speaking or those who were expressing a disdain for what their guild has done.

The punishment was light, and needed to be re-addressed after the harassment that the members of DC extended from RP to OOC. I was personally called many names following the verdict (like I said, check the logs. Last 15minutes was spam from 5-6 DC people saying how I "suck" or some other less savory things.

RP wise, they threatened my character and my guild and said things such as "you better not hunt in DAV if you know what's good for you". So uh, we give them light punishment for being traitors and they go back to being traitors (and breaking EULA by harassing people) right in front of the judge?

Hopefully they enforce this ban, and it forces them to realize they can't go around crapping on everyone with insults and their self-imagined elitism.

I hope that this did not set a precident for future "rulings".

DC as a guild was not punished (much) because Truwind whined about cancelling AO. "I already know what the judgement is, and I have to say tonight is my last in AO" then he logged out before the ruling came. Very cowardly.

Overall, this could have been an amazing event (well, if you can forget about the idiocy from DC it was great) but there was little done to punish those who were blatantly breaking the EULA and harassing other players.

Hopefully this is addressed, or futher actions will be taken by people loyal to Omni-Tek and against immature insulting done due to feeling inferior to those who are able to make a point without swearing or harassing.

It is a dark day for loyal Omni-Tek employees, but thankfully it's even darker for Dark Carnival.
