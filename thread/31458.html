XP balance
If you sit back and think about it, caster-type mobs give greater XP then shooters, and shooters more then melee mobs. Why is this? Technically, the casters would be more dangerous, due to the fact that they can nuke/root/whatever from range. Weather or not they actually =do= these things is irrelevent to the XP model that Funcom seems to have built into the game. Same thing goes for shooters: whats more dangerous, a guy with a rifle, or a guy with a lead pipe? Anywhere but AO, the rifleman wins, hands-down.

Personaly, I'd like to see the 'order of precedence' reversed. But not untill after the important things like the horde of bugs we still have lurking about are cleared up.
