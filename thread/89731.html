Problems with dynacamps
This has probably all been stated before. However, the sitation with dynacamps is nothing short of an absolute bloody mess right now. There are three issues that I can think of off the top of my head.
  • Bosses warping you, or warping to you - this is not working as intended, according to old traffic from Cz. Bosses "summon" you to them (thus defeating the object of runspeed buffs, root-and-run tactics etc.) but worse than that, being warped pops you out of combat mode. If you had someone other than your fighting target selected when you were warped, this is going to cost you.

    If it's just the boss warping to you, it isn't so bad. However that still eliminates root-and-run as an option.
  • Problems casting nanos - this affects everyone. Pretty much all the time, nanos will refuse to cast properly in the presence of a camp boss. The first attempt doesn't work, the second gives you a "Cancelling current execution, please wait" message and the third might actually cast the nano. Or it might not, depending on how the gods of chaos are feeling today.

    This is bad if you're trying to cast an emergency nano, such as a blind, grid evac nano, heal, Mongo etc. It must be an absolute nightmare for NT's, who are almost totally reliant on their nukes.
  • The pet-only nanobot attack - we've not had an explanation of this one yet. It only affects pets and seems to be custom designed to prevent pet professions from hunting at the dynacamps. You can use your pet to tank, but only if you heal him constantly, which is almost impossible given the potential for adds and the nano casting problems mentioned above. It doesn't affect non-pet tanking professions in the slightest.
There's a reason no-one hunts at these places. Only the solo professions would have any real interest in hunting at the camps in the first place, and they've been systematically excluded from doing so.
