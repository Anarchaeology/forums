Several approaches
- The save approach
As you have 70 in Comp Lit, grid is open to you - well almost. You know the grid terminal in Newland outside West Gate - good. Just go to a central place in Newland (Mission Terminal, Bank) and ask (shout) for some generic Comp Lit buff (Expertise)- every higher lvl player will (should) have it and you should get it really fast. The Grid terminal in Newland needs 90, the one in Omni-1 Trade is 75. When you enter the grid (in Newland) you usually are headed towards the Omni-1 Trade exit, just head straight forward. It is not that big, you can't get lost. (don't bother about second tier by now)

- The risky approach
MAs are fast, aren't they (green run speed). Ask a Fixer and/or Adventurer for a run speed buff, Whompa to Borealis, head for Holes in the Wall (BB) and follow the road south (attention 25% areas). When/if you reach the Stret, head left (east) until you reach the bridge. Now you enter 4Holes! (mostly 25% zone). follow the road until you reach the southmost hole - there is a Whompa to Omni-1 Ent. Turn Omni when you are in 4 Holes or you won't survive the wandering NPCs in the holes.

- The brutal approach (not advised, no guarantee)
Buy two application forms (Omni and clan). Whompa to Tir, change to clan, 'save' in Tir (or any other clan side), change to Omni and say 'Hi' to the clan guards. You should be resurrected in Omni-1 (at least the guy I spoke to yesterday told me so). Well, please don't rely on this way, I am pretty much sure, this is not 'intended' to be used in that manner.

Anyway, you are aware of the fact, that once you make a decision for one of the two faction, there is no way back to Neutrality - the one and only way of live.

So long,
