Enforcers from NT PoV
just posted something on AG, thought it should be here as well. i know a lot of high lv Enforcers, and read a few posts on their current situation. some points (not including weapon/weaponskill) that i happen to agree on and should receive some notice. Remember, this is from an NT's perspective. God knows how many times i've teamed up with an enforcer. my best friend IRL has a Trox Enforcer main.

1. Essence of Behemoth should be self only. (just like RRFE)

2. Enforcers need another HP buff stacking with Essence+Mongo (even if RRFE becomes self only, Enforcers are only 60-70% the tanks Soldiers can be with TMS, current battles do not last long enough)

3. Enforcers need a decent heal delta buff. (Just to have one really, they definitely fit in more as the 'berserker' type from RPGs as opposed to the generic warrior/fighter, they should be able to regenerate)

4. they need a GOOD taunt line. one that does 'some' dmg and gives 'some' debuffing ability. (something like NT's Gravitational Anomaly or Coronet of Frost, but definitely much less dmg. towards how much dmg Da Taunter deals) NT + Doc still pull the most agro. ql200 Da Taunter is a joke.

5. Better yet, give Enforcers an AE taunt, something that taunts much stronger than Mongo, but with very little additional use, perhaps even with negative bonuses for 10-15 seconds.

6. find them something to do, seeing a total of around 10 nanos in their superior shop is just pathetic. no matter how little they view of nano-technology or whatever your story wants, the game is practically 100% dependent on nanos, and enforcers need their share as much as NTs, Soldiers, and everyone else.

this gives them a much better edge to perform their necessary tasks as solo and in a team, and have a little fun as well.

(not to take it away from us NTs. woo! we don't wield Blackholes, we have it tattoed in glowing gothic lettering on our foreheads)
