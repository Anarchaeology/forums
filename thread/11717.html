Dual Wield Hack! All Pistol Adventurers Read This!
I found a way to dual-wield an offhand gun with a QL200 gun which DOESN'T have a dual-wield requirement. As you know, if one of your two guns fails to have a dual-wield requirement (i.e. QL140+ guns have a >1000 multi-ranged requirement, so they cannot be dual-wielded), you can't dual-wield it. This is what many "higher" level adventurers have been facing -- all the wasted IP in multi-ranged, and the fact that all higher-QL guns don't support multi-ranged.

Well -- here's how to get around it!

1) Get yourself two identical guns -- what I did, is I went and got those modified Desert Reets from the Longest Road dungeon.
2) Dual-wield the guns.
3) Pull out your QL200 (or non-dual-wieldable gun for that matter).
4) Simply RIGHT-CLICK on the QL200 gun in your inventory. It will equip in your left hand, and WILL NOT un-equip your right hand!

I tried this many times, but ONLY with those Modified Desert Reets, and it worked every time. I even zoned and did it again, same. It stays dual-wielded when you zone.

Let me know how it goes for you guys, but this is great! Even though I'm going shotgun this level, it's still neat
