Are mobs harder now?
From Avalon I took a mission in Aegean, and on the way I met a Hammer Bull blocking the entrance to the mission area. I thought ok, I have been dealing with these things solo 5 levels ago (I am lvl 59, so they are light green / green to me). I start buffing up with 9 buffs, one being for example the critical buff of 8% being a Martial Artist. I strap on everything I got that could help me give a little more health and help me other ways (merit board and pioneer backpack).

I run up to the bull, starts the fight with a nice crit brawl of 668 points, and my not so high leveled Blessed with Thunder that only does 140 (+ 140 DoT). I hit in the speed I used to against green type of mobs (maxed out Psy Int), but only to notice that this green bull did hit me even faster than I did with the Def - Agg bar all the way over to Agg. I thought to myself, ok this calls for Dimach, but no, I failed to hit with it so I started healing as fast as I could with my little healing nano formula only to find out it didn't help much. I died of course, only to get the bull halfway down it's own health.

I also had a mission with Strike Mambas that I managed to kill with no problem in the mission, but when I found one outside the mission it started going crazy like that hammer bull and hitting much faster and with high damage.

So, have anyone else noticed this?
