"Ninja Looting"
About a month ago I had never even heard the expression "Ninja loot".
When i first read some threads about it on the boards i tought it was a way of looting a corps from someone that had died - as ppl kept shouting that this and that guy had ninja looted this and that item from them.
Then I tought it was a way to loot a corpse even when you didnt have the loot right (as in the ingame function) to do it as i saw a number of posts about guys ninja looting Tarasque and mission bosses.
After a while though I understood that this was an expression used about players that looted something, and kept it hidden from the others. Like looting a chest and not telling what was in it, or saying it was ammo or something useless.

This thing is realy realy getting stupid though, seems like it is trendy to call everybody a Ninja Looter theese days. I have had players in my team shouting "ninja looter!!!" at other players who were looting remains when there was no "alpha loot" on in the team.

Is this a highlevel problem? Or a wave hitting AO nowdays, since I had absolutely no problem with it like a month ago, and it is roughly the same people I am teaming with now, that I teamed with then.

My point in this thread is that I feel this is getting ridicolous, when people quit teams because a guy opens a chest it is just getting way out of hand.

Tanks and looting
Speaking as one who usually Tanks during team missions, nothing ticks me off more than Ninja Looting. As an Atrox enforcer I can hold my own against most MOBs, but I've been in missions where I've gotten 3(!) chests out of an entire team mission because I'm soaking up damage while some Fixer or Agent is looting the chests. If your tank is fighting you should NOT be looting! Help the poor 'trox out and take a shot at the MOB he's fighting. Tanks have zero chance of getting loot if theres combat going on, please bear this in mind when teaming.

Another of my peeves is the "I'm doing this for all of us" ploy. This happens when someone has a bazillion points in B&E, and so in the interest of speed they loot the chests and say that they'll divide up the loot after the boss has been killed. In my opinion this is like opening up your birthday presents for you because you're too slow. Plus, the dividing up at the end NEVER happens! In fairness, the chest loot people I've been with do display the loot at the end but the group is too "polite" to ask for 2 or 3 random items, so the looter walks off with all the loot. I've asked for a couple items to sell in situations like that and the room falls silent as though I just committed a big faux pas.

I think that chest looting should be alpha just like mob looting. It would solve so many problems!
