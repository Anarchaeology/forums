I've been thinking about why I'm still in AO and why I'm still having fun - especially when I tried the demo of EnB and was about to leave for it. I began 2 weeks after the US-release when it came to Sweden, and I played Beta4 *shiver, shake*.

I'm still having fun in AO because of the great people in my guild, the wonderful persons I've met in this game, the one I've gotten to know through AO and become friends with outside of AO. I've never played to have the best loot or be the highest leveller or the fastest runner or the best PvPer, infact I hate PvP but I do it if I have to, to help my guild or Omni - I suck at it and is easy prey and usually stop after a few deaths. I play to meet people, to help others, to chat and to do some minor roleplaying. To have fun, in my sense of the word.

Recently I began exploring AO - I realized I had never been to DAV and really got a look at the place, nor Avalon, nor the northern zones - except for going on raids. I've been Omni from the start, with one exception - Diamondroad, who went Omni after 7 whole levels, and I still can't find my way around Omni1 Ent I think the clanside is an experience I won't stay long enough to see - I prefer the hightech cities of Omni.

I've lost count of how many chars I've made, but a few months ago I counted the levels of them all in the javachat and came up with just over 1000 levels combined.

I spent 4 years in UO, 3 of them with the same character, I think I went through every profession-template with him, mage, swordsman, archer, woodchuck, fisherman, bard - but I settled on armor and weapons-smith, and didn't leave Britain for months in a row, and still had the same amount of fun. Because I felt I could help others best as one. Repairs, making armor and weapons - and Grandmaster made items.. *my* name of a weapon someone used! WOW! Making money was fun too, I was so happy when I had over 80K gold as I had to make a new stack of gold in the bank =) Anyways, I always longed for a futuristic MMORPG, and that's why I left UO.


Theres my rant, I'm still having fun in AO and I don't foresee myself leaving anytime soon.
