Hey, give the girl a break. I just fired up my MP again after about 3 months and tried a mission. I only died twice because my heal pet fell asleep (right beside me), only had to recreate my full set of pets about four times because they got irretrievably lost or stuck, and only got chased out once because my attack pet went AWOL at a critical attack point.

AND I completed the mission. Yes sir, I think they've got this pet problem licked.

So, now that's out of the way, yeah I want to talk about transitioning pets from meatball to demon, because I think this is an interesting idea.

The MP pets are supposed to be quasi-physical creations of the MPs' will formed from his/her anger. As it stands right now, all that happens - up to demon - is that the MP's anger becomes more intense. But the form stays the same, implying that his/her will (and creative ability) does not improve significantly.

The demon, on the other hand, is a radically different sort of creation, and reflects and improved willing ability. Interestingly, however, although the MP has 3 pets, only one, the attack pet, changes in form at the demon level. So this by itself is an inconsistency.

I suggest, that the discontinuity at demon is also an inconsistency. What should happen instead is that along with the increase in contolled anger, the MP should also be improving in will and thus creative ability. Hence, the MP should be creating more complex pets all along the way. Exactly as Antimosity has suggested.

There are already existing forms that could be modified somewhat to fit this transition. I'm thinking specifically of the Avian Wingman line and the smaller Pixie version. So what you could do would be to start out with a meatball (amorphous blob), transition to Pixie, then grow into Wingman, and ultimately end up with Demon. That would add a fair of interest to the attack pet line.

You'd also want to do something for the other two pet types. The Pixie to Demon transition is not appropriate for these guys because they have different termperament. A healing Demon is just not believable. A healing angel though would be ok. So maybe you could have a tinkerbell sort of pixie for healing, rather than the little demon like pixie we have for the attack pet, and transition it to angel.

The mezz pet is more trouble conceptually, but is usually handled in fantansy with a sort of a scarey apparition. So one option would be to transition from the blob to say, Wraith
