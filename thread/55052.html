There is nothing more stupid for than some one killing a guard of his own side

To make a reputation system shoul not be too difficult, there is example on how it could work :

- every pc has a "bad reputation"
- every one begin with 0
- each time you kill a npc of your own side, you get 100 points in "bad reputation"
- each time you kill a npc of the other side (clan for a omni, omni for a clan, and omni or clan for a neutral), your "bad reputation" drop by 1
- if you have more than 500 point, people of your side are angry and will not sell you or speak to you (for quest)
- If you have more than 1000 point, you are warning (can buy item for booth)
- If you have more than 1500 point, you are wanted (attacked by guards...)
- The "bad reputation" is betwen 0 and 3000
- The "bad reputation" can works for EVERY side (someone with a every bad reputation will be attack everywhere...). The system can have a change for the neutral position : the limite can be 1000/2000/3000 for a omni/neutral or clan/neutral situation (i mean by that a omni in a neutral city or a neutral in a omni city, ...).
- When you change side, the "bas reputation" will not be reset. If you have a "bad reputation" over 1500, you will have a repuration of 500 else 1000. (for agent, i can be allways 500)


This system is not perfect but it is better than nothing.
