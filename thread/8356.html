Mission Difficulty
I did a 70% mission yesterday and every mob I came across was Red. I was running it (no chance of fighting) and I made it about 40% of the way through the map before I was killed (I have max run and Free Movement).
It seems that moving the slider more than 10% either way of 50% results in either all greys or all reds..actually I know that 60% can often not have a red but it's so unpredictable which is really what I'm getting at. With that in mind why not implement a very simple scale to the percentages:

>0-10% all grey.
>11-24% mainly grey with a few easy greens.
>25-40% easy greens with some tougher ones (sick green colour).
>41-55% Yellows with some greens
>56-70% Yellows and Oranges
>71-85% Oranges and Browns
>85-94% Mostly red with a few deep browns
>95-100% Really hard Reds.

That way you can take a mission that you believe equates to your skill...right now it's almost pot luck.

On your level mission (that would be the 50%) highest level mobs you can some accross are usually light orange (which I believe means one level higher than you). If you go lower you start getting greys and if you go higher you can get reds too. One way to see the level of the mission is to take a look at the reward items, they are the level mission is (nanos being an exception). Higher level you are the wider the level range of the missions gets - if my level 77 soldier moves the slider 4 small clicks to the red side I will get a mission I absolutely can not do alone (but soldiers are pretty crappy and I can barely kill the hardest of the yellow npcs even though I wear good amour, have a good gun and use the mirror shield) where as my some of my lower level characters can "easily" solo 60-70% missions.

In 70% mission you really should have expected to see a lot of reds.
