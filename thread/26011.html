Questions for the community
Hey there, everybody. Having recently decided PvP is the place to be, for better or worse, I have some questions for the PK community. The thrill of fighting against another player, as opposed to going on _Yet_Another_Mission_ is what gives the game it's replay value...especially when RP that can tie one along with the developing storyline seems to be virtually nonexistant. Anyways, the Questions--

1-As far as I can tell, Ganking is essentually 'jumping' somebody, Using surprise and/or numbers to secure a kill. Forgive me in advance if I somehow mangled the definition within the context of your posts, But how is this a bad thing, exactly? Having come from a text-based Mud(Waterdeep:City of Splendors and Abandoned Realms, specifically) I'm used to having to stay aware and alert at all times. Why are guerilla tactics so heavily frowned upon? Is it because you don't wish to stay alert in PK zones, or because you start viewing things as being 'unfair' from the get-go? Self-defeating attitude? You probably won't beat that six-on-one, but Aren't there defensive/Evasive tactics one can employ beyond b!tc#!ng on the forums immeadiately afterwards? Is the 'motif' of the community supposed to be something of an 'Honor amongst Thieves' or more of a Cutthroat 'Everyone for themselves' thing?

2-I know this isn't the smartest thing in the world to do, But my primary character is a fixer. Having just breached level 15, This is the character that I used for my PK excursions. Decent evades, Green primary attack, Rooting, Good first aid...I couldn't figure out why everyone said these guys were the lowest of the low, Beyond maybe the ip problems that the nanoskills cause(Fix? Please? Thanky.). In any event, I was wondering how big of a difference the points gained through something like grid jump(+3 evades, big ol' boost to runspeed, -3 damage) makes in PK, along with the general buffs/debuffs (ie-SMG expertise vs. Dodge Ranged Incompetance). Does that swing in stats make a noticable difference? Trying to use this 'in the field' has been pretty inconclusive for me...I die too damn quickly anyways when getting beat on for triple-digit crits. :/

3-This question isn't really directed to everyone and anyone here, Instead it's to the ones with questionable practices in order to get their PK titles:Why? As far as I've been told, they don't have any real value in terms of mechanics. They don't make you any scarier, save to perhaps beginning players who don't know any better. They don't make you physically tougher, Being freshman or Neophyte or Senator won't keep you from getting beat every time by a superior, unranked opponent. What's the point of arranging for guild-killings to pad the titles, or whatever else?

4-I couldn't help but notice that Fixers had a relatively cheap Shotgun ip cost in comparison to a lot of the other classes that use them. Is it worth having a lower attack rating and the increased ip cost to purchase shotguns instead of using SMGs(Go, Mausser Go!)? I understand why it's done in the case of classes such as the Agent(hooray for the short-range pea-shooting rifle?) but for me and those of my ilk?

5-'Twinking'. While it makes it impossible for starting players to even have a chance in PvP, I'm forced to wonder wheather or not it's actually a bad thing in concept, especially after reading some of the more thought-out arguments against that part of the 14.2 patch. Equipping higher-level(within reason) than usual weapons and armor via implants, buffs, and whatever else....I initially assumed FunCom simply designed the game to allow for different classes of character intentionally, from the rich who can afford the best of the best implants and service(read-characters who can do the proper and highest level buffs) down to the people who need to work _hard_ in order to just have the money for an implant of their own ql or less.

As a side note to 5, Doesn't 14.2 Just make Traders even more imbalanced(Some here would call it uber733+, or whatever it is you people type), as they can now work their deprives/ransacks/etc to even _Greater_ Effect? Was this the intended effect? If so, I'm forced to scratch my head in confusion on that one.

Oh, and while I'm taking up the space in memory, I'd like to give a big thanks-again to Ciola and 'The Family'. The little time you put into seeing me off meant the world to a newb like myself

Thanks for listening to the ramble. I'll try to keep it shorter next time.



Sascha/Stlythe(level 15 non-twinked Fixer)
