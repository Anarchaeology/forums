The meaning of life (in AO, that is)
I'm a veteran UO player and left it for AO because of the underlying story. AO is a great game, wonderful graphics, a nice audience, plenty of strange gadgets, scenic places and a truckload of details to immerse yourself in. Yes, there are bugs, but theyre fewer than these forums would suggest.

BUT to my disappointment what lacks in AO - as in all other MMORPGs I know of - is a STORY. Where is it? Yes, there are some report of obscure attacks, and once in a while war is declared, so it gets harder to buy your Omni-Med suits. But thats just about it. Even UO had more story, at least potentially.
On the Felucca facet people had the opportunity to join the -> FACTIONS <-.

To outline the concept of Factions shortly for those unfamiliar with UO, there are 4 factions. All of those are in a state of constant war with the other factions and their members are open to attacks (importantly: without any penalties for the attacker!) from other factions at any time. Additionally, each faction has its own gear (like Faction Warhorses), craftsmen from a faction can create special things (like Faction Traps), all factions has their own strongholds, and each faction has its own friends and enemies among the wildlife.
Most importantly, however, is the ability to conquer territory. A number of cities each have a place where a so-called Town Sigil is placed. If a thief from a faction manages to steal the Sigil and bring it to the faction stronghold, and if the faction can keep it safe there for a certain number of hours (for corrupting it), the entire town switches side to that faction. The faction then has the ability to place (read: buy) guards, vendors and so of their own.
The concept is not without problems, of course. One of the most unpopular things is the now-changed server-up-raids, where a large group from one faction gathers at around 6 am where the UO servers go back up from their daily down-for-maintenance period and makes a raid at another stronghold to seize the sigils. And how many people from other factions are at guard duty and 6am? Go figure out the outcome

Now, AO instead has 3 large factions where everybody has to belong to one of them (unlike UO where it was voluntarily). Even better, in AO there actually should be a reason to be in one of them because of the background story.
The sad, embarrassing, cheap, thrice-damned, sorry fact is that it really doesnt matter. If youre OT you have one part of the map, cheaper saves and better armor. If youre clan, you have another part, and you might at some point have access to certain benefits from your clan (and a clean conscience, of course). And thats about it. The major events that reach the headlines are a swimming competition and a Brontos rights demonstration. Yawn!

The forums are flooded with complains because Agents are better at 1-on-1 PvP than Nanotechs or vice versa, and  staring in disbelief  I see that there are many, many complaints about people who have friends help them in PvP in the 25% zone. Eh? I thought the noble clansmen were SUPPOSED to help each other out against the evil Omni-Tek, just as the scoundrels from the great hegemony were supposed to join forces against the brave freedom fighters???

Now, Funcom, these complaints from PvPers really sound to me as a sign of warning. You make a game where the story is THE virtue, and all the PvPers seem to think about is whether they make it to Freshman or Rookie
Why? Because nobody cares how many brain washed Omni-Tek slaves you kill since it has absolutely 0 consequences for the story and for your AO life. I was looking forward to PvP in the beginning, but frankly I dont see the fun in just seeing whos got the most over-equipped char

Fortunately, this may all change. Funcom has announced that some day (!) there might be territory you could gain. Wohooo. The sooner the better!
If for some reason they should show interest in suggestions from players, I think we should all send in some.



Mines here:

Make a large territory 25% zone all over. Place the saving terminals outside the zone so that staying alive is actually important, and make it impossible to log out within that zone. Also, make some grid points close to the borders of the area. Perhaps it could be made QW- and Yalm-free zone as well to put all fairly equal? And dont place ordinary mission targets inside. Special missions are another story (see below).
In the middle of that territory, place a town  not huge but not too small either. Equip it with vendor terminals, banks, guards (hard-hitting ones) and perhaps a physical installation like a big cannon, machine-guns or the like. But still no saving terminals.
Then make a certain point (call it Command Station or whatever) that has to be held for a certain time (lets say that the hackers of another alignment need that much time to change the software of the citys mainframe). Say, two hours. If at any time in that period a player of another affiliation manages to stay at the point for, say 30 seconds, the clock resets.
After those 2 hours, the entire city changes side  including guards, defensive machinery etc.

Put simply: the city is yours as long as the other side cant manage to hold the Command Point for 2 hours straight without interruption.
This would allow for some large-scale battles between Clan and OT  and with a purpose.
AND it would be more fun for the game developers to try to balance the sides instead of just fine-tuning 1-Soldier-lvl88-vs-1-Trader-lvl85-battles.

Imagine this
Through whatever source, the defending side has gotten word that a large attack is on its way. They have no idea where the attacking forces are gathering, so they must just keep an eye out.
Yalms (and perhaps QWs) are not allowed in the playing field, so constantly shape-changed adventurers, cloaked agents and run-buffed fixers from the defending side are scouting the area for possible attackers  the enemy may even pull out a small attack to lure defenders away from the main attack.
When the attack is on, the NTs supply artillery, the docs heal wounded comrades, and the enforcers and soldiers smash their way through the defense, helped by the buffs from traders, and assisted by robots and Meta pets.
I dont know about you, but Id really love to be in such an attack (or defense). Everyone can help  also the low level players, if nothing else, by supplying buffs, heals etc.

That may not be enough purpose for some, though, so the really, really cool thing to do would be to include missions in this. And even better: missions whose outcome has consequences for ALL OT, Neutral and Clanners.

Lets make a script (loosely inspired by the WW2 assassination of the German Reichsprotektor Heydrich in Prague)
*edit* The man I'm referring to is Reinhard Tristan Heydrich*edit*

The cowards from Omni-Tek have managed to conquer the city of Frey from the noble Clans in an unexpected night-attack (this would be possible if you could have only chars from on affiliation on your account  spies, traitors and scouts may actually become useful, then).
They have held the city in a firm grasp for several days, and the clanners are desperate. If that was not bad enough, words then come out that the OT has sent their most feared uber-bureaucrat, Ricardo Hernandez, from Omni-One to Frey. He has a thing with the clans (he cant really forgive a quite violent assassination attempt), and he intends to impose a new tax on all clansmen and neutrals.
To show the power of Omni-Tek he plans to make a symbolic travel through the political zone around Frey, and when he ends his trip in the city, hell use its powerful mainframe to change the insurance computers of all of Rubi-Ka.
He will be going slowly through the territory at a given time point by an unknown route and end his trip in Frey, and if he manages to enter the city, ALL Clanners and ALL Neutrals will have 20% added to their saving fees for 4 full weeks.
Everybody.
For real.
Also the newbies.
It is therefore vitally important that hes thoroughly killed this time, but hes protected both by the defenders who can get to the spot where hes attacked and by a heavy escorte. To avoid the new tax, the neutrals have to join forces with the clanners in the attack  and trust theyre not gunned down by their new comrades
If the Clanners/Neutrals manage to take down the infidel, the tax will not be imposed.

Now THIS could be fun in my eyes.

Smaller missions related to conquered territory could be for instance retrieving an object inside the enemys city. A large clan/faction/group could probably not conquer the city and then hold it out against the upcoming counter-attack, but they may be able to make a quick strike, kill the nearby defenders and guards, get the item and get out. Of course, if they fail the first attempt, it will be way harder next time, when the defenders are prepared. The reward could be a clan benefit not obtainable from stores. AND you could also make a penalty; say 5 to all attacks from members of that faction for missions not completed

Now doesnt all this sound fun?

Send your comments / own suggestions. Who knows: FunCom may actually be listening
