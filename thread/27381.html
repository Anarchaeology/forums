Some basic statistics for y'all:

I use a 170 Westinghouse and a 178 River Series 6. Around 650-660 attack rating with either.

Both weapons crit for around 2.5k. Fling does around 1-1200 non-crit, burst does around 500-3500, and FA is, of course, all over the place. I've done a 255 FA and in the week or so that I've been using the Westinghouse I've gotten 3 10K FAs (how many MAs can say they've done 10K damage? ;-p ). Can't say much about aimed shot because I've only used it in Camelot where seeing how much damage I do is the last thing on my mind as I get creamed by overwhelming clan forces

I wouldn't necessarily recommend using a neural disruptor (although I haven't tested it) because you're only using it for one special. Even though the equip time is 0.25 seconds weapons don't actually equip that fast due to lag and animations and whatnot. It's simpler to just use the AS on the river series.

EDIT: Oh and it's really up to you if you wanna use Flashpoint or Westinghouse. The only difference is that I implanted a faded heavy weapons cluster in my right hand for an extra 14 attack rating. If you use flashpoint you can have a burst cluster in there, or treatment if needed. The advantage of using Flashpoint is that soldiers can buff ranged energy, but I don't wear MKII and wouldn't waste ncu space on something like that anyways.
