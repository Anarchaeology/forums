Me and cless had a chat about pvp the other day and he came up with this nice idea. He say's he never posts on the boards so i'll do it for him

A fast solution for now might be to put in special rewards when you get a higher title. Not uber items that gives you +1000 runspeed etc, cause that would make the flaming here even worse, but visual items that makes you look uber cool and really makes you stand out from the crowd. Like a pink yalmaha or something. Superman suit anyone? :] Stuff like this shouldnt be too hard to implement, and if the rewards are really funky, it might lure more people to the battlefields...
