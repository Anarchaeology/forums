Character and organization info on the community site
This was a topic (well, the character part at least) that we started on the 'old' forum about a week ago, and we'd like to get some more feedback from you on it.

First of all, the people working on this web feature are not game programmers, so this will not affect the progress on the game itself. Just wanted to make the clear.

We have a small list of what we'd like to implement as a first step in making this information available, to start off the discussion. So tell us what you think, and feel free to bring up new suggestions.

Character info
The only way to get info about a character is to know his or her name. The info given will be...
- Full name
- Photo (character face)
- Affiliation
- Gender
- Breed
- Profession
- Level
- Dimension

We would also like to show what organization the character is a member of, if any, and link to the organization info (below).

Organization info
Getting info about an organization works the same way, but you can also follow a link from a character within the guild. Info listed will be...
- Name of the organization
- Affiliation
- Organization description (from in-game)
- Number of members

Additional options
Later we would like to give players more detailed info about their own characters, and full member lists for organization leaders. This will have to wait a little while though.

So, what are your thoughts on all this?

Oh, and the old thread can be found here: http://archive.aoforums.funcom.com/s...threadid=64096 (old forum login)
