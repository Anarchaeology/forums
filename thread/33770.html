Customisable Info ( Legend )
Please add a custmomisable info ( a short legend ), others can see ingame,
so everyone can put there whatever one see as important,
and don't want to explain to everyone over and over and over again.
Sure, you can use macros for it, but often it's already too late,
and ppl. feel uncomfortable, on both sides.

So, before you approach someone, before you ask something,
you look at info, and see not just level/class/guild,
but also this short legend written by this player.

There are so many unpleasant situations, you could avoid,
and it could help you to find friends as well
( read legends, and talk to someone, whos legend you like,
for example while teaming up: i would rather pick up a nice person,
who is weaker, then the one who is stronger, but i dont like at all )

Things which could be put there:

1. Services: what i CAN do, what i'm WILLING to do ( right now ? ),
what is the price ( may be with adjustments, if someone help newbies for
free or whatever else )

2. Roleplay:
a) not everyone, who play female character want male characters trying to flirt with her;
b) if you plan to build a mixed omni-clan team, there would be an easy way to save trouble
not picking up the "hardliners", also if you have already a 100% one side team,
and there is a hardliner in team, you avoid the trouble without asking
every time someone joins.

3. Language / behavior in general
Well... everyone have some ppl. he/she likes, and others he/she dislikes.
For example usually i dont need to write the whole post
to know do i like this person or not;
it's even more about how it's written, then what
( while "what" is sure important too )
It's not just for you, to make sure you ask only people you like,
but often it could even help you to be NOT contacted by these,
who you dont like ( for example i often just ignore tells written in "leet speak",
but at the same time i also feel bad for ignoring them...
if he have read my legend and still ask this way,
or even dont bother to take a look at the legend -
then he really deserves to be ignored

Sure basicly you can put there everything you want,
explain your roleplay for example...

I think it would make the game so much more fun and less frustrating...
want it even more then Shadowlands...
