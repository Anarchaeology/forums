Lawsuit filed against Mythic dealing with right to sell items on websites like EBAY
Actually this does have something to do with AO as it does with any MMORPG on the market. There is an article posted on Anarchy Lore regarding a lawsuit filed against Mythic, the company that developed Dark Ages of Camelot.

It seems that a group has set up a website listing items and accounts for sale in DAoC and have listed it on EBAY. Mythic sent them an order to cease and desist whereupon the group is now suing Mythic on the grounds that (they claim) players are selling their game time and Mythic has no right to own that.

Go to Anarchy Lore for the full article (http://www.anarchylore.com/default.asp#3) It should be
interesting to keep an eye on this case. We have had quite an argument here on the message boards about the legality of people selling items and accounts on EBAY. Now it looks like that legality is going to be determined once and for all.
