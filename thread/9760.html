Some suggestions and ideas... mostly ideas. ;)
Here are a few ideas that you guys at Funcom can toss around:

- Jet Packs: could be pretty cool, for those who cant afford a yalmaha and dont want to travel by land...of course they should be slower than yalmahas and could use the Meta-Physicist flying animation with a mounted item (the jetpack) on your back. Or if you want it to be really fun, make it a hoverboard! (People would love skating around)

- Claws for martial artists: not like other claws on other MMORPG's but are a part of the martial artist (takes off the MA's hand and puts a steel claw in place)... could make it nifty and use some sort of light-saber type claw blades.

- Able to mine? (not sure if u can already): if u could actually mine in the 'mines' of Rubi-Ka that would rule, gives a little depth and an extra way to make money other than doing mission after mission after mission.
How i think mining should work: Talk to a miner in omni-tek or clan area and then he/she will (after you click yes to enter the mines [or make the whole 'person' idea a machine]) teleport you in and u get a miner suit and start drilling (or digging)!

- Mini-Games: like capture the flag or futuristic soccer, some people dont like to kill, kill, kill, or kill as an alternative to hunting and going on missions. Or use my hoverboard idea and just make a little place to skate around, or have a race track for yalmahas, mids, or the 'jetpacks'. Combining soccer with hoverboards would be fun too.

If any other brainstormers want to add their own ideas, they can go right ahead... nice to see and read some fun brainstorming. I'll probably add more ideas tomorrow (its 12:30am here and im tired!). Thanks for reading.
