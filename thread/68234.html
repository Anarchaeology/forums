OTSEC Demand Council of Ares Surrender Military-Grade Weapons
OTSEC Press Release
13:30 November 11, 29476 RST

ROME (OTSEC/PR)  The Omni-Tek Security Council (OTSEC) sat in session last night, debating at length the recent acts of unprovoked aggression committed by the Council of Ares (CoA) against the Omni-Tek Corporation of Rubi-Ka. Amongst many others, the widely reported attacks on the Omni-Pol outpost in Avalon, the corporations facility at 2HO, and the mining community at 4 Holes have forced the council members to pass a unanimous resolution to disarm the CoA clans; by force if necessary.

The Council of Ares very existence represents a seriously destabilising element in the north, whose continual acts of open unprovoked aggression not only causes harm to corporation assets and personnel, something that can no longer be allowed to go unpunished, but also puts in harms way the lives of innocent clan civilians by bringing the threat of open warfare to an otherwise generally peaceful area.

The Security Council encourages all peaceful clan civilians to unite and rise up, to remove the oppressive Council of Ares from their midst. This unstable, militant regime serves no peaceful nor reasonable mandate, only bringing the harsh reality of war to your doorstep.

Terrorism will not be tolerated. Talking, diplomacy, and caution did not get us the needed results, - CEO Philip Ross, October 15, 29476 RST.

Omni-Tek Security Council Resolution OTSEC/R-001[list=1][*]All Council of Ares clans will immediately surrender all military-grade weaponry. These are defined as assault class weapons, support weapons, heavy and vehicle mounted weapons, weapons that serve an obvious offensive purpose, and all weapons of mass destruction.[*]Small arms class weapons such as rifles and submachine guns are permissible for local defence purposes, upon registration with the Security Councils investigative teams.[*]All Council of Ares clans will immediately stand down from military readiness and desist in all offensive operations against the corporation.[*]All Council of Ares clans will immediately prepare to accept Security Council investigative teams to ensure full compliance with military-grade weaponry disarmament is taking place, and is being adhered to on an ongoing basis.[*]All Council of Ares clans will afford full and unimpeded access to these investigative teams.[*]These resolutions are unconditional and demand full and immediate compliance. Failure to do so will give the Security Council no reasonable option other than to disarm those Council of Ares clans forcefully, and without further warning.[/list=1]This resolution would normally have been entirely avoidable, if not for the flagrantly aggressive position adopted by the Council of Ares, and their continued unprovoked strikes against the assets and personnel of the corporation.

The time for talk is now over. The time for clan commanders such as Redruum and Windguaerd to act responsibly and have their forces stand down is at hand. Only Council of Ares commanders such as these can halt the escalation in violence that at least we want to avoid.


Omni-Tek protects.

Strength through unity.


Garret Bonefish Silvestrini
Director - SPARTA
Chairman of the Omni-Tek Security Council

OTSEC - Barzillai, Bonefish, Chartan, Drlog, Kithrak, Meister, Simukil, Ukblizzard.

The Omni-Tek Security Council is dedicated to ensuring the companys security and interests against all threats, coordinating any action deemed necessary to protect corporate assets and personnel.
