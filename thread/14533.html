Hi there.

I have been avoiding leveling up because of lack of high level content, and also am afraid of reaching level 200, and becoming bored with the game.

There has been ideas of expanding the game to level 300 going around, but i dont see that as a good solution. What if instead of leveling up even more, at level 200, you could just get more IP.
