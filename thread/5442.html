Small bugs noticed
As a lvl 79 soldier, here is a brief list of a few bugs that bug my soldier has run into from time to time:

1) While doing missions and attacking a monster, often my soldier will stop dealing damage. He still fires normally, is not reloading, but does not seem to hit the monster for about 4-5 rounds. I believe this is an animation bug, because if I jump around or switch between first and third person view, it goes away sometimes and my soldier will deal 2-3 shots in a row.

2) Special attacks, such as the fligshot and burst, are wasted if used while reloading. The trouble is that there is no way to tell when one is beginning to reload, since the character simply stops firing for a few seconds. If the reloading message would come up before a player finished reloading, many troubles would be solved.

3) The dependance my soldier has on nova flow. Its a great gun and I enjoy it, but some variety would be nice and is needed.
