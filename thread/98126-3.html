OOC:

A note about spying - it would be one thing if a character pretended to defect from one org to join another then was spying from there. Placing alts in another guild as spies is not roleplaying, it's just placing alts. The problem with placing alts is that there is no way to detect the spy since it is an unknown. It is possible to discover if a defector is spying or at least have an inkling that the person might be a spy. In other words, placing alts as spies is a form of RP godmoding.
