The Sentinals
The Sentinals

We are located on Rubi-ka2 on Anarchy Online. We are a clan guild of people who help each other and other clanners. We are players who are interested in having fun. We hold events like Help a newbie Day, parties (in clubs and at the pool or on a beach), Dungeon crawls (both static and missions) and anything else somebody thinks of.

Philosophy

The Sentinals believe that the tyranny of Omni-tek corporation is responsive for the problems of Rubi-ka. The Sentinals know that in the end, lasting peace will only comes from a political agreement. Sentinals will support the clans in the struggle, however they hope for peace.

Rules and principles

1 Sentinals help each other
2 Sentinals never charge or accept donations for buffs from a clanner (they can refuse to buff a non-sentinal).
3 Sentinals never attack clanners unless both sides agree to fight.
4 Attacking Neutrals including npcs is not in the spirit of the Sentinals.
5 Sentinals are expected to help newbies.
6 Any non-immediate decisions will be voted on at meeting of general membership.
7 Applicant can vote at meeting but a majority of members and higher is sufficient to carry a vote
8 A Sentinal member or higher can only be voted out by a majority of the active members.
9 An applicant can be kicked by an advisor or higher.
10 Prejudice of any kind is not permitted.
11 Roleplaying is encouraged
12 PvP is not required
13 Begging is not allowed if you want something look in the guild
14 Exploits are to be reported to Funcom and the guild

Organization

The Sentinals are a democracy.

-A president (elected)
-Advisors (elected)
-Veterans (any person who has been in The Sentinals for 3month and been made a member. Or any member who demonstrates that deserving the rank and who has been a member at least a month)
-Members (An applicant will be promoted by an Advisor. Veterans and members are encouraged to remind Advisors that an applicant is worthy of full membership when that person has show that he is capable of membership. After a month a applicant should be a member or kicked out)
