A little more verbose explaination:

1-50(117). A normal attack does between 1 to 50 points of damage. If a critical hit is scored, then (117) points of damage are added to the total. In addition comes the extra damage from damage-buffs such as 'Weapon Enhancement' which gives 5 extra points.

Also, your Attack Rating (based on your attack skills, like Pistol, for instance) affects the damage given, multiplied one time extra for every 400 pts of attack rating, if I remember correctly.

If you had an attack rating (AR) of 400, then you would do two times the damage. With an AR of 800, three times the damage, and so on.

Subtracted from the damage is 10% of the mob's AC rating in the class your weapon uses for damage, down to the weapon's minimum damage, which in the example above would be 1 point.
