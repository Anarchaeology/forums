Revenant Pictures & Research Info !
Today myself and another MP have played around some with the new lvl 60 "Praeritorian Legionaire Pet". I took alot of pictures during the process, and those of you who have not seen it yet will probably enjoy these ! :

http://www.geocities.com/balderac2003/Revenant/

We took the pet with us to fight monsters outside Home in Broken Shores, and it did very well against "Shade Y44 Runaways". It did only cold damage.

The pet does not make any sounds, except when it dies. Then it makes the same "moan" as any human mob.

After that we took it to the arena. On Arena I summoned a

Greater Wrath Incarnation

wich is a lvl 93 pet, yes 23 levles higher than the "Praetorian Legionaire", but it has only a bit higher requirements to cast.

This pet made mince meat out of the poor Legionaire.. It stood no chance whatsoever.

Then I summoned a

Lesser Wrath Incarnation

This pet is level 76 and is easyer to cast requirement vice than the Legionaire. This pet also made mince meat out of the little Legionaire. Worth to mention is that now the Legionaire was buffed, but the meatball not ! Still it kills it easy...

Conclusion: Meatballs of the same level rule the lvl 60 legionaire. (*cry*)

The Legionaire hit for 1 hit at a time, no double hits.

After that we tried to send the Legionaire on me, to see if it did some "special attacks". It hit me for a long time, but I could not see it do any special effects.

He hit me for 40 damage constantly when it was able to hit me, wich was quite often. When the other MP cast the next highest Evocation on it damage went up to 170, I think.

Quite a jump.

Anyway, it has very OK Attack Rating for a level 60 pet. I have maxed evades, Add Def implant, some evades on my rubber pants, OK AC, and it hit me quite often.

After a while I tried to CoC it.. To my big surprise, i could not !

I tried over, and over, but it resisted. I nuked it, and that went fine, but I COULD NOT CoC it. I started to believe that it was immune to this nano only, but soon found out that I was lucky with my nukes. Cause in the end I tried to nuke it with Mind Quake, and it resisted 7 out of 8 tries. THIS PET HAS NANORESIST !!

I cant imagine how the higher ones will be.. This level SIXTY pet resists me ALOT. Nukes, CoC, whatever....

If so.. what about the level 202 version? Will it resist EVERYTHING?

One last thing: The pet is very small.. as you can see from the pictures, its a midget next to the demon. Is the higher ones bigger?

P.S. The pet cannot do emotes.

Enjoy the screenies !
