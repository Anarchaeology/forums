Aktrez says goodbye
<<IC>>

It is with Deepest regret that I announce my departure as Director of Vixen Entertainment. VE has been my family, my love, my life for the past 4 months and has given me worlds of joy!

Unfortunately, the constant stress of not having control over things within the bar, and our manager, Throll, being uncounted for has brought me to this conclusion. I need to take an extended break, not only from Vixen, but from Rubi-Ka.

I have learned recently that my mother, who was pressumed dead, is alive.. and that I may be in danger. Many of you even witnessed the 2 kidnappings that took place from Rompas this week. I cannot handle this pressure. I need to go to a place where I can wake up and feel safe.

With that. I bid you farewell.

<<OOC>>
It is seriously the LAST thing I had wanted to do!! You ALL know how much this game means to me.. and how much I enjoy putting my time and energy into playing with you guys and creating this world that we all inhabit. It has just become too much stress for me to handle recently.

Between impersonators, People mocking my ideas, People trying to destroy what we have worked for, Friends who turn into enemies... IT's all just too much! This is a game.. and the word game is suppose to equal enjoyment. It's not fun to be told you are a ***** and that your real life boyfriend should leave you. It's not fun when someone creates a character called "Aktrezsdad" to screw up your storyline, It's not fun to see a character named "Akktrez" who looks like you (only 50lbs heavier) running around telling people they work Rompas now.
These things just make me shake my head and turn away from gaming.

I can't stand igorance in real life.. and to have to put up with it on a game that I play to step out of real life for a few hours.. is not my idea of fun.


I have gotten loads of e-mails and IM's from people I have known for a very long time.. people who I have just met.. and people I have never even heard of begging me to come back. I would love nothing more than to honor your request. But, until I know that I can come back and be able to enjoy playing this game.. I will have to decline.

I feel there is FAR too much Funcom bashing going on. I do not blame FUNCOM for a single part of this! You do not come out of hte womb knowning how to program the perfect game... it's not something they teach you in school. It's something that needs to be learned through Trial and error. Funcom did it once, it didn't worked.. they fixed it. And many of us came back to a wonderful new world! yes, there are still glitches.. Yes.. there is much to fix. But damn it! Give the guys and girls of Funcom some credit! I would like to see all of YOU try to create a game as unique and imaginative as this one! The simple fact that you are all here reading this is proof enough. I just wanted to add that.. so I don't have to read any replies saying "Yeah Funcom.. look what you did!!"

I do however think this game needs more control. We need to either have players who have control in this game, or more green names to run certain areas. The storyline does need tweaking.. but, I don't see why we, as players, can't do this! It seems that anytime a player tries to start their own storyline to liven things up... it gets shot down and called stupid. (I received about 5 tells last night during my story event of this nature. This is seriously what pushed me over the edge. I'm not going to name names..but, those people know who they are.)

So.. with that.. I tip my sunglasses to all of you. I retire my black heels and Miir suit shirt.. and walk out the ever so familiar door of Rompas to board my turbo jet back to earth. Until I know that things will be better... I feel this is my only option.

Vixen Entertainment: I love and respect you all!! You have been my family and have been some of the BEST Rpers I have had the chance to work with!

Saletor/Gencop and the rest of OP: Thank you ever so much for not only being our protectors, but our friends. I will miss seeing Gencop hitting on a different girl each night. And Sal.. you are a dance god.

GSP: Wyld, Metalynx: I am SOOO sorry for ever doing anything to hurt you guys! Again, if we had a way to share our storylines with each other.. then none of that would have ever happend! I was stupid.. and i"m sorry. Tarryk: You made Aktrez a star!! You are a great guy and an amazing DJ! I want to thank you for coming up with the best thing that happened to AO! And for the others. Good work

Thyme, Nevver and Devil's Advocate: I know we have gone through some VERY rough times! But, you really have always been my friends! Nevver, you and Ditzie were with me from Belle Rouge and you were my right hand through that and my dear friend through Phoenix. I wish you two all the luck in the world!!

Doncarnage and the NL girls: You guys all rock! Keep being unique and original!!

Ehrynn: You DO need to keep writing JwJ!! You are a brilliant writer and a wonderful person!!
And for all the people who I have missed and who have entered my little world at Rompas.. I want to thank you all for being there for me! Being great friends and making Rubi-Ka a place that I could not WAIT to come to!

And Sakuraz: My strength... and seriously the wind beneath my wings! You have been my #1 fan and supporter! I am so sorry to have to leave you with this burden. I will always be around to support you and give you any advice that you need. I love you and will miss you greatly! I want all your dreams to come true!

I may be back.. only time will tell. I just know things need to change. Until then, I bid you all a fond farewell! YOu will be in my hearts always

/me waves as she wipes a tear from her eye
I really had hoped this would never end. But.. I fear that it is for the best.
/me picks up her bags, blows a kiss to the crowd, and heads out the front door of Rompas....

* Aktrez *

Unfortunately there is a strange kind of irony at work here.

Your success can be measured by how many people try to bring you down, and what lengths they will go to in order to achieve this.

People will be people and unfortunately this means that there will be those that are envious of what you do and how popular you are. Bringing you down makes them feel beter about themselves since they 'beat' you (its a replacement feeling for the fact that they know they cant do any better, or even as good)

Unfortunately by becoming a 'public figure' you (the royal 'you' not you specifically) set yourself up for this kind of treatment.

If you walk away now they win. Not that this will (or should) matter to anyone other than 'them', but once they feel they have beaten you they will do the same thing to others to feed their 'fun', this is bullying pure and simple, and the characteristics are no different here than from RL (except its much easier to get away with)

However to continue you would either need to accept that this is likely to continue, tone your role down for a time, or play a less-public alt. You could try to 'fight' the griefers with whats available but you are correct that the current setup of AO allows this behaviour to go unpunished so easily (and any action by GM's or ARK's is likely to result in more hassle in one form or another since people will believe you are being given 'special treatment' by FC)

Its no easy decision, but above all you should do what makes you happy, not what makes other people happy.
