Soldiers and minimum damage
I have read some discussions in the soldier thread about the necessity for higher minimum damage on our guns. As a level 79 soldier I am obviously biased and agree with such an idea, but I offer some valid reasons and a solution to the trouble.

First, the problem: 90% of the guns have damage output 1-???. For instance, my nova flow does 1-174 or something like that. Its a sad day when someone higher than level 50 goes about shooting for less then 10 on occasion, but it happens. I had a burst the other day that only did 25 damage. Normally I do over 100 damage, usually around 200, but...

The reason soldiers want higher minimum damage is because at high levels a player needs to hit a monster consistantly over 100 points of damage. If we are soloing or doing a mission and get three attacks in a row with damage say: 39, 43, 57- even to a green or grey monster- then its time to put up the mirror shield and run, because that's a battle we're going to lose. Soldiers are supposed to be tanks, but we cannot stand around if we are sometimes unable to lower a monster's health significantly. Say a soldier only gets low damage output 1 out of every 25 monsters. That does not sound too bad, except when one realizes that it takes hundreds of monsters to level and that one out of 25 can mean death. No experienced is gained if a player dies every now and then.

The solution: put the minimum damage a soldier can do in comparison to the level that they are on. Example: I am level 79, so my minimum damage would 79. A person on level 50 would have a minimum damage of 50. This raising of minimum damage should not and does not need to effect maximum damage output. This would simply make it so the dreaded: "You hit leet for 17 points of energy damage", never appears again when I am level 18++!
