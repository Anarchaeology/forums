Bad Luck
The fixer flew through the grid searching intently. Not going anywhere in particular, not looking for anything special, just good old fashioned Grid Slamming.
Information. THat was his addiction, we wanted to know needed to know as much as he could download about anyone or anything. Just keep the data flowing.
Seldom did he get to "Slam" anymore. Much of his time had to be spent outside the grid now. His time had now come and all the data he had been gathering all these years must now be analysize, processed into Intell.....
And observation. Grim was one of the few fixers who knew you COULDN"T find everything in the grid. Sometimes you had to use your eyes and ears.
His job as a reporter offered him some comprimise. He did get to collect data from people. Usually it was in no way relivent to what his mission was, nor was it anything as rapid as the Grid, yet it was still enjoyable.
So lost in this train of thought was he, that he nearly missed her. It was the woman that guy kept talking about. What was her name? Jenae....MARKARIAN!
"Prometheus, access Markarian, Jenae"
A data stream fed into a hardwire leading up into his cerreblum...
" Female, Homo Solitus, Fixer, Omni-Tek Employee..."
" Close data"
What was she up to......
She was a good one, she was hacking something alright. He couldnt tell what, but something. And important or not, it called to him. He wanted to know, but how
"Prometheus, trace scan, this area, flag unusual data transmissions"
"No unusual data..
"Trace scan, this area, flag-OT-SEC encryption protocol transmissions"
"No OT-SEC en.....
"Trace scan, this area, flag-failed encryption transmissions"
"Trace detected"
"Locate source"
"Source located"
THe fixer left as quickly as possible to a remote node, where he began relaying to a network of other nodes before finally claiming his prize. Couldn't be too safe, anything this important might be very well gaured. It could even be......
"A DAIRY!?!" he yelled in anguish
"Prometheus, confirm location."
"Location confirmed"
Impossible. All this excitement for a diary? how many tetra-gigs of data could he have had by now in the like it took him to find....THIS.
Perhaps he would look at it later, perhaps not. Daires just didn't do much for him. But seeing how much effort it cost him, he may "peruse" it later just so his lost time wouldn't a total waste.
Now, however, he had to get back to Borealis. There was a man there waiting for him. Maybe he would have something for him
