Combine RK1 & RK2?
I started playing AO when it was released. I endured the first months of Cities-Of-Lag (TM) and twice-daily patches. When they started charging for the game I personally felt it wasn't finished and ready to pay a monthly fee, so I sadly took a break. It wasn't because I didn't like the game, I just didn't feel it was worth the monthly fee.

However, after 1500 hours of DAoC, I regret almost every hour of it, I have returned to find the game is about 1000x better then I remembered it. One thing however is I have noticed the world is far from as populated with PCs as it was back then. Infact I would say RK2 is very underpopulated. The amount of people online doesn't seem to reflect the RPG elements since some cities are ghost towns.

What would people think of moving all the Anarchy Online players onto a single server? Most likely moving all of the RK2 characters to RK1. Granted there are a lot of technical issues involved (ie characters with the same name on both of the servers, character limits, etc.) I, personally, don't feel like I am qualified to state my opinion since I just recently came back to the game. (Although if I did state it I think it would be a good idea for the dwindling populations.) What would all of you that have been playing to the high levels think? I think this could both enhance playability on the low end, giving characters more possible people to group with, and on the high end it might make the PVP content a little more battle centered rather then the 1v1 encounters.

Please don't flame me for making this suggestion, I'm just throwing out ideas. If you have a negative opinion on it just state it politely without any flamable content.
