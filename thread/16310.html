Theres an exception - crat.

A crat is unlike a NT. 99% NTs dont use the highest 2 nano as main nukes. So it really doesnt make a difference for a NT to have the extra 20-30 MC. A crat (who wishes to keep max pet implants) however needs as high skills as possible

Damage wise, a high lvl nanomage crat with special nanomage items is a lot better than a solitus crat. A nanomage crat can have
1) at least 2 steps higher pet (15-30 more damage per hit),
2) 1 step higher charm (charmed mob with 20 more damage per hit, 2x charmed pets so there are 40 more damage),
3) probably 1 step higher nuke (100-400 more damage every 3.x sec).

Sum em up, it makes a difference.

In terms of survivability, they are the same at high lvl, pets do the tanking, and crat has the most ultimate crowd control weapon - calm, fear etc. Its never a problem pulling away agro in non-cave mission.

Its the breed-only-ITEM taht makes the major difference. If you are a nanomage, you should go for the BEST nanomage items ie vest, shades... to maximize your nano abilities
