Relocalisation of the Will to Fight PvP dungeon
This place is such an awesome play ground for PvP players. The area is very good, perfect for group PvP and also very well made. Main problem is that no one go there at all. Probly because theres no loot but since its supposed to be a full PvP dungeon, people shouldnt go there to worry about loot, we all know the bs that happen in camelot but most hardcore pvp players went there to get some good fights but the main reason is because the the dungeon is too far away.

So heres my suggesstion. I think that FC should change the location of Will to Fight dungeon so it can be easily accessed by any PvP players. You could place an entrance next to the Arenas and one in Boreailis near the grid for the neutrals. Or maybe an entrance that you can reach through the Grid.

Another good thing would be to rework the Crystal dropped from the unique mob in the dungeon so that whenever you use it, the possitive effect will always work instead of a 30% chance.
