Wow, I'm not the only one who took the time to think about a childhood for their character! YIPEE!

I have heard many different 'ideas' about how we all got to Rubi ka, the most common being that we were constructed in labs and 'let loose' as adults. I could believe that one to be true to a certain point, prehaps some do enter the world that way. But how long has Rubi-ka existed? How long has it been populated? You can't tell me that people haven't gotten together in all the time and made a few babies. (=

So with that in mind I set out to create in my head what a child's life would be like on Rubi-ka. It was for my own backgrounds at the time, but since there seems to be an interested I will share it. Keep in mind this is my opinion, not stated fact or anything.

Children in Omni territories would have a stricter life. Perhaps begin attending school as early as 4 years old, where the edicts of Omni Tek would be repeated over and over again. Since Omni Tek is primarily a corperation and out for money, children would be screened for various positions early in life. If they showed the ability and talent for the 'good work', they would head that way in training around the age of ten, if they were average, they most likely would end up working in the mines, or lower level jobs.

Although many believe the clans to be completely unorganized, I honestly believe that with cities like Tir and Athen's, they are in fact more organized then many believe. Their schools would be more like your present day schools in America. Free thinking teachers trying to broaden and expand the mine of the children through education. I also believe that the clan children would be screened for best abilities, but offered the choice of which direction they wanted to go in life. The beliefs of most Clanners would be expressed in school as well, the sense of freedom for all, and the opression of Omni Tek.

Neutral children I don't see as having much schooling at all. Their cities are more like places of gathering then actual cities. Education for them would come from parents, and entire families would be fighting for survival in a harsh land. At least this is the impression I have been given. Parents would be trying to keep their children out of the war between Omni Tek and the Clans by telling them that both sides are evil, or something along those lines.

OOC.

I would think that in Omni-Tek territory, children would be screened at an early age for aptitude and then either educated, or not, depending on the test results (and of course, the children of the rich and the important would always screen well, heh heh).

Ie, if someone's screened to become a manual laborer, OT would most likely give them a basic academic education and a decent vocational education, with lots of propaganda because of the known morale problems of menial workers. But there's no profit in educating laborers, so after they learn readin' and writin' and 'rithmatic, they probably wouldn't get any more academic education. Figure maybe a 5th or 6th grade level of academic education, followed by vocational training... and propaganda/indoctrination their whole lives. With the right indoctrination and psychological conditioning, they could probably put their labor force to work full-time by age 14, and the laborers would like it.

People screened for 'better things' would be better-educated, probably close to the ways people are educated now. Ie, different complexity and subject matter for different ages. Technology would certainly be a good teaching aid and there's probably a well-developed standard education pattern.

I think the various Clans are very different from each other. The militant Clans, like the Sentinels and maybe the Knights, probably run military-academy style schools with heavy doses of indoctrination into their ideology. There are certainly organized schools in the major cities, but enrollment is probably voluntary - certainly once someone has achieved a basic, functional level of education, it's up to their parents if they continue or not. This isn't because the Clans are ignorant, but rather because the adolescents might be needed by their families to work. More bohemian Clans will probably encourage everyone to learn as much as they can, others might want the kids back to herd their brontos or something. Clans like the Pilgrims might encourage home-schooling. Higher education is available, but probably on a paid basis - somewhat like the current university system, you probably would pay to attend.

That's what I think education might look like on Rubi-Ka... just a guess though.
