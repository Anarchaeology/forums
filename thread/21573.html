Agents... enough with them
Not too long ago I was teaming up doing a team mission and I, being a fixer, was with an enforcer, an adventurer, a metaphysicist, a martial artist and an agent. Before we even start our little adventurer, the Agent low and behold goes and complains about NOT having ANY healing nano's. Now wait a minute... last I remembered, there are only 3 classes that have any decent healing, the doctor(obviously), the agent, and the martial artist. So, naturally being a fixer(means I'm already grumpy enough), and being the passive aggressive I am, I knew there was an enforcer in the group and promptly said, "Enforcers don't have any heals either." The Enforcer agreed. But, what did the agent reply? He deftly dodged both our comments, and kept on complaining. I felt like shooting him.
Now, why do I complain if I'm a fixer, and I have my Heal over Times? At my level, 54 I'm using a nano called Insurance Hack... it's a quality 47 nano. Sounds good, right? I'm a Nanomage who has invested every trickle of IP into my nanoskills. One of my guild mates who is level 63 asked the other day, 'What's the next HoT in line after medical claim?" Which is a level 30 or so nano. This isn't an example, this is a common occurance.
So, what's my point? I have really good reasons to complain. I have really good reasons to complain when agents have 7... new features... already added on to what they can already do.
Oh... and that ability to hide from other players, who is THAT going to help? Agents definetly, fixers sorta, anyone else? Nope, have fun pressing the search button everyone. Don't get me wrong, it's a really cool feature, but who are you kidding? It's more meat for agents to swallow whole.

One last thing to say... I do NOT play a class so that I can feel lousy because someone else playing an agent can do everything I do and MORE!

You're destroying the essence of being individual, special, worthwhile. Basically, you're destroying everything you gain pleasure from when you play a game because it feels like the other side is cheating, and NOONE likes cheating. Might as well open the floodgates for exploits.

In reality, you need to spread that over 25 or more levels in order to keep other skills at minimal amounts.

Out of a total of ~1400k IP earned by that level. That's 70% of our IP spent. Then there's Stats, SMG/Burst, Comp Lit, Evades, Init, Run Speed, B&E, Treatment/First Aid, Vehicle, Body Dev, and Nano Pool to spend the rest on.

Even if we could AFFORD that massive IP dump without skimping elsewhere, we'd still be a bit on the gimp side because at 3points per level, we would STILL be lagging behind the QL of our Nanos by a good bit.
