Blade Corp Omni + Neutral Divisions (RK1)
Blade Corp has positions available.

Blade Corp are looking for a few good men/women/atrox.
We do not accept just anyone, yes we wish to increase our numbers but not at the expense of the quality of person we are looking for.

Blade Corp is a mercenary company, though the widespread use of supression gas on Rubi Ka has hampered this course of action. Therefore the Corp has evolved into a more multi-tasking organisation, bodyguarding, manufacture, etc as well as the more traditional "mercenary" work.

Anybody interested in this offer please visit our information site at http://www.bladecorp.com . Here you will find further information on the Corp and it's members in addition you will find our online application form.

You may be the person we want, so apply and find out.


//////OOC//////

Blade Corp is a tight knit, friendly group of players. If we can help one of our own in some way we will. We're not looking for selfish or just plain obnoxious players.

Just applying doesn't guarantee acceptance. If your application is promising we will set up an INITIAL interview.

The purpose of this interview is for us to gauge the type of person you are and for you to ask questions.

If the interview proves successful you will be admitted to Blade Corp as an 'Applicant'. This is to allow you to get to know us and us to know you. If you are what we are looking for and you like what you find, a department head or our President will promote you to Unit Member at some point.

This may seem drawn out but as I've said before "We do not accept just anyone". Anyone who joins must live up to what we've fought hard to earn...namely...the respect of the omni and neutral communities.

We strive to keep good relations with the Omni and Neutral Orgs out there and one or two of the clans as well Usually a group hunt, static dungeon or event is held once a week, all are welcome and we usually end up with a mix of all factions.

Active, helpful, friendly, loyal, and like roleplaying?? If you answered yes to these questions you might be just what we're looking for.

So if you want some fun, meet helpful players and (hopefully) get involved in events take a look.

/////////OOC Ends//////////
