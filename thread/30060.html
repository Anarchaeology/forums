Some good ideas on making the mission system more appealing and effective
Yes the mission system needs a revamp now.

[Solo Missioning]
Either the missions are too easy or too hard. At no time in a 50% mission should you get 2 aggro linked mobs over yellow. However, I see two oranges all the time. This leaves the mission impossible, not merely difficult, to finish.

Why
1) Monster difficulty does not scale properly considering player advantages. Yellow monsters are infinitely harder than green ones, etc for a soloer. Having several orders of monster difficulty in a solo mission is detremental to the gaming experience. We do not have the tools to do that kind of mission. At most the player should only see 2 color ranges in a given solo missions.

2) There is too much variance in picking a mission. Players should be able to pick missions that 100% have what color mobs they like to fight. If all you can handle is greens you should be able to get a 100% green mission. Or if you well equipped and a soloing prof you can get a 100% orange mission. No double or triple mob rooms.

[Solution]
Make the mission slider have demarcated points on the positive side to indicate a 100% green, yellow, orange, or red mission. Randomness is great for those looking for an adventure as opposed to players that just want to be productive and efficient. The space in between the demarcations can be mixed mob mission combining the color range to both the positive and negative side of that point.

Past the demarcated area it gets tougher for the players that want more challenge and it goes back to highly mixed mob placement.

Don't forget though, that demarcated mission settings (4 of them) need to have no statically linked aggro of any kind. Only singles. The areas between the demarcations can have up to 2 mobs in a room. Past the demarcation point there may be up to 3 mobs in a room.

Any criticism or other ideas?
