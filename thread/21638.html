All Trolls: Reunite :/ (:D)
Now that the bridge seems to be growing (especially with those zippy fixers.. they are like *bing* *bang* to one place and then another.. too bad they can't do beans for work)

Well, anyway, to honor the reintroduction of Troll Corp��, we would like to reintroduce the story of the "Attack on Funcom", made at the end of 12.6 finasco. [If you wish to proceed to the "Troll Ranking System", scroll down to the big red "END" sign. Thank you.)

-------
Code named 'AoF', this is the story of the attack that destroyed Funcom.

----------------

9:00 AM
"Alright, men. This is it," I said, from my seat under the now massive bridge. "The final invasion against the evil known as Funcom shall begin. People from all breeds, Solitus, Atrox, Nanomages, and Opifex.. People from all jobs, from Enforcers to Nano-techs.. From Clans to Omnis, this attack will be joint. The evil known as Funcom will not exist."
"Yes!" cried the thousands of trolls. Thank goodness Trolls got special 500 Gigahertz machines with 1 petrabyte of RAM, or the lag would be horrible. Luckily, the zone was also supported by it's own server allowing it to hold 99% of the online population.
The great Troll Army began to depart, as FunCom HQ was dead ahead.
"Men, we need to attack from both the Grid and Whompa points. We don't want the Funcom zone to crash!" I said above the rumble of Trolls. "Arm your weapons, don't forget to get nerfed, either!" I directed the main forces to move into the Grid, where it quickly crashed. After everyone relogged and the Grid was stable, I quickly directed the rest of the forces to the Whompa-zone in. The few remaining stragglers got into their Yalms and began flying to the zone.
It was finally transpireing. The Attack was under way! I gave the order for the people to whompa and Grid in! The zone immediatly crashed.
10:00 AM
Everyone quickly relogged, and a nice time warp brought us all back to the Grid/Whompa locations. Once again I gave the order. The zone promptly crashed.

10:30 AM

In an angered voice, I once again gave the order. For a spice of change, the zone lasted two seconds before everyone was staring at a beautiful desktop.

10:45 AM

After everyone relogged, we went back into the grid/whompa locations. "This could be a problem, men. Their servers can't take us all at once! We have no choice but to go to plan B!"
The 100 Trolls who were heading to the funcom zone via air cars quickly stationed themselves on the roofs of the buildings. They killed the 10 guards, giving us the go ahead to zone in.
After dividing up the troops to zone in 10 at a time, I gave the order! "Group A, zone! Group B zone! Groub C! Zone!... Group A1 Zone! Group B1 Zone!... Group A941, zone! Group B941, Zone!... Dang I need a macro for this! *Cries and sucks thumbs*"

11:45 AM
Finally, everyone was zoned in. The zone promptly crashed.

12:00 PM

Everyone relogged, and luckily we were still in the Funcom zone. All was quiet. The Roof-Exploiters jumped from the tops of the roofs and joined the masses. We tried to enter a door. "Playshift error.."We tried another. "Playshift error.."

5000 trolls typing /me mutters isn't pretty. The Chat Server promptly crashed.

12:30 PM

Using sign language and out-of-game Instant Messages, we continued to coordinate the attack. Funcom's error defenses made this attack nearly impossible. However, we had come far to long to be destroyed now.
We finally found a door that worked! The NTs began to nuke the door to open it. "Funcom Door has countered your Nano." "Funcom Door has countered your nano."
The gun classes began hitting the door. No damage was done. Finally, they did their specials, and the door finally broke open.
The group was met by 50 level 200 Healing MOBs. We outnumbered them 500 to 1. We would win? Right?

6 hours later, we finally killed all the MOBs.

7:00 PM

We proceeded inside the door. The entire server promptly crashed.

8:30 PM

After 'emergancy matenance' was completed, the server was opened again. A Wacko GM began dropping Knight Armour all around the place. We shrugged it off. Nothing new. We killed the GM and continued.

8:45 PM

Tensions were running high, as this was the first 3D Dungeon anyone had ever been in. We actually WENT DOWN A TENTH OF AN INCH! It was amazing. We continued exploring the dungeons. We found the 'Tech Support' section covered in Cob-webs. Nothing out of the ordinary. Too, ordinary, we thought.

Suddenly, all the Funcom employees jumped in front of us! Each one was carrying guns called 'uber 1337 u r dead m337'! Many Trolls went down as 95% of us had the Take it easy! bug. However, the server crashed and a time warp occured so it didn't matter anyway.

9:30 PM

Everyone relogged. We finished off the Funcom employees, though we knew that those wern't the true Funcom employees. They had bad AI and slid all over the place. Wait. That made us think a lot. Maybe it was the Funcom employees.

9:45 PM

Spirits were beginning to deminish, as we went back up a tenth of an inch, ridding ourselves of the 3D dungeon. It was an extremely buggy area. Ancient programming scripts covered the wall as though it was caves from thousands of years ago.
We could smell the rot of the Funcom employees long before we could see them. We finally reached the FUNCOM ADMINISTRATIVE ZONE.

10:00 PM

The chatter was immense. Who would win? We were sure we would finally previal over funcom! The GMs summoned hundreds of massive Greature Ashun Terrors, massive bird mobs that had almost a million HPs. The trolls barely used all their specials before the monsters died.

10:10 PM

We began to call out to the Funcom employees. They shut down the server in fear.

11:00 PM

After they made up a plan, they put the servers back on. We relogged.

11:15 PM

Martin, the Lead Programmer, came out of the sheltered door. NTs had their fingers hovering over their primary nuke, Soldiers ready to cast Deflect Shield and do an Alpha-Strike. He raised his hands, tension ran high.

He chirped 'ru nubi'.

The zone crashed.

11:30

We all relogged and continued our conversation.

"We are the Trolls of Troll Co.�. We are part of Troll Corps�. We shall rid the world of the Filth known as Funcom," I told Martin.

Martin quickly replied 'lolz, funcom r0x0rs j001."

"Umm.. No.. they don't.." I replied, beginning to wonder about his mental state.

"lolz, liek yea dey d0, n00b1" Martin replied.

"If they do, why do we have," I make a gesture at the troops, "25000 armed warriors ready to attack you?'

"lol, liek how w00d our liek 1337 d00d survers hold up den, n00b/"

"Eh, they haven't.. really.."

A mindane conversation commenced.

12:30 AM

We were getting nowhere. I quickly rallied the troops. "This is fruitless! Let us destroy the corrupt evil known as Funcom!"

With that one phrase, Martin was saying 'ru nubi' to a reclaim booth at the edge of the complex inside the Funcom Employee Room we were about to enter.

12:40 AM

Tensions were high. THIS was it. We entered the room! The zone crashed.

1:00 AM

Everyone relogged. Funcom was there, armed and ready.

They began to chant 'ru nubi' at us. This is how it would all end...

We blasted them with all our powers! NTs spells were resisted, they dodged every normal attack. However, even their 'uber liek 0wn u' armor couldn't help against the Alpha Strikes and other specials. Lasers and bullets flew! Funcom body parts splattered on the wall!

1:30 AM

Finally, it was over. Funcom had been defeated. A regiment of 500 people stayed to ensure Funcom would forever be in a death loop.

The zone promptly crashed, ending our adventure against funcom.
