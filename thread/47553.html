Well, the location you pull from often determines the location of your destination. This is because the game tries to put missions farther and farther away as you get higher level.

So, you'd want to choose a mission terminal that tends to give missions at places that tend to be small. For Omni, I find that the mission terminals in Newland give me a good number of missions in Home and Southern Clon****. To my knowledge, those are the best spots for smaller missions.
