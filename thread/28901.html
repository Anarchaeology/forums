Info on burst recharge time
I did a few tests with the IP reset today to find out once and for all for myself just which skills affect the burst recharge timer. I haven't seen any definite conclusions yet and so, here are mine.

I didn't take exact measurements except for how long it took for burst to recharge, i'll leave that up to someone else, but heres the general gist of things. I did a full IP reset and raised only my base abilities, no other skills. At this point, I grabbed a ql 1 normal mausser and headed for a backyard. The recharge time on burst was 87 seconds with ranged init, smg, and burst all around 70ish. The first skill I raised was ranged init, I maxxed it out to around 380ish. I timed the recharge burst and it decreased by 8 seconds to 79. So ranged init does affect how fast you will burst. I then raised my burst skill, which maxxed out around 390ish. This further decreased the time by 5 seconds down to 74 seconds to recharge burst. So burst skill also affects how fast you burst. Raising SMG skill did not decrease the time any further.

Unfortunately I hadn't thought of it until afterward, to time my burst with my implants still in, but I know it was faster by 2-4 seconds as they are a decent mix of ranged init and burst, raising ranged init by a good 80 points or so, and burst by around 50ish and in an older post where my character was probably 5 levels or so lower, I had his burst timing with a mausser at 73 seconds.

A rough estimate would be every 40 points in ranged init takes 1 second off your burst recharge and every 60 points in burst takes off 1 second. It could still be more complicated than that because it doesn't answer the question why a regular supressor can burst 5 seconds faster than a regular mausser.

Anyhow, thats about it, if anyone has anything to add or get more exact or whatnot, go ahead. :P

/me shakes his head.

I've known burst recharge time decreased because of SOME skill for a long time, but until 14.2 with the IP reset I wasn't able to determine WHICH skill it was. I've done a LOT of burst timings, mostly with pure smg/burst weapons of course being a fixer. And yes, my timings I did yesterday were all with one weapon, a ql 1 normal mausser.

The recharge speed on a weapon does play a big role in how fast you will burst. It is also pretty consistent from weapon to weapon, at least in the pure smg/burst category. This may not hold true in all cases, but every .1 in recharge speed seems to equal 2 seconds. For example, if you have a normal mausser which has 3.0 recharge speed, and you burst every 73 seconds with it, then if you switch to an MTI Aleph, which has 2.8, you will burst every 69 seconds.

21 with .75s - A ql100 Duke Lugger SMP single wielded
32 with .95s - A ql20 Duke Lugger SMP single wielded
32 with .90s - A ql40 Duke Lugger SMP single wielded
40 with 1.3s - A ql39 Michael Patriot
69 with 2.8s - A ql124 MTI ALEPH 99
73 with 3.0s - A ql116 Mausser

You can see this in these burst timings I took a VERY long time ago which I pulled from this thread.

http://forums.anarchy-online.com/sho...?threadid=7279

The .2 difference equated to 4 seconds between mausser and aleph, the 1.5 difference between the patriot and the aleph ALMOST accounted for exactly 30 seconds, I may have been 1 second off somewhere. The .4 difference between the patriot and a ql 40 duke lugger was 8 seconds. Hence the theory that every .1 in recharge is equivalent to 2 seconds of burst recharge time. However, it does not hold true for some reason when going from the ql40 duke to the ql 100 duke which is a .15 difference.

Another bit of data that may help in determining the amount of skill needed to reduce burst recharge by 1 second may be the soldier nano, Riot Control. In game, i've only had it cast on me once and thought it was +100 burst, but my memory may be bad, according to anti-guardians it is +110 burst. As I said previously, I guesstimated that it would take 60 points in burst skill to reduce the time by 1 second. If we assume that Riot Control was made to give a specific bonus, namely -2 seconds of burst recharge, then we can say that every 55 points in burst will reduce burst recharge by 1 second.

Anyhow, I need to leave for work. :P This is just a quick guess here, but you can probably figure out the burst recharge time on a weapon when you have 800 init and 800 burst just by multiplying the recharge time on the weapon by 20.
