Hmm, I have mixed feelings about this. Selfbuffed duels in arenas may work and can be fun sometimes, but going selfbuffed into a 25% zone just gets you killed. Personally I never ever even step out into 25% unless I have at least behemoth and MoP running, but usually I have much more stuff in my NCU.

There is one thing that bothers me with selfbuffed though. Different professions have very different buffs to offer. Those that are more combat oriented, have very good offensive and deffensive buffs, others have great nano skill buffs/debuffs, yet others have trade skills buffs. Every profession has its strengths and weaknesses and I've always felt that one uses outside buffs form other professions to equal things out.. make it more fair.

I mean take a duel Enf vs NT and make it selfbuffed... I'd say enforcer would have about 99% chances of wining. Or take a Doctor without HE/Crunch and a MoP running. Sure doctor has good HP and can heal, but can he/she actually do any considerable damage without a crit buff running? Without HE/crunch doctor can't heal forever either, so it's just a matter of time before his/her nanopool runs out and then.. dead doctor.

On the other hand I think everyone has a personal list of buffs that are 'must have' and are 'good to have'. As long as you boost your HP a little and have slightly boosted offense/deffense it's allright by me. Though if someone asks you for a *duel* and you say: "sure, brb", go find a fixer for NCU buff and then ask every person you see for buffs.. it's kind of lame .

just my views on this.. .
