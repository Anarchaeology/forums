For other reasons they should be retractable.

In public teams ...

People like to ninja-invite you know... invite everyone they see in the right range until enough accept... but sometimes you end up with a team of 4 or 5 and are wanting that last vital member, like a doc or a tank... and you don't want that MP you invited earlier to accept and take the spot.

Or, if a few guildmates decide they want to team before its filled, but there's a bunch of people on the invite list (let's face it, very few people say "no I dont want to team" right away.. they let the invite sit there) that might take the spot(s).

Plus there's that more than 6 member team bug. you know, 3 ppl accept at the same time and your team of 4 goes to a team of 7, which could have easily been prevented if you could disinvite. (It sounds good, but it's a little buggy, and if anyone LDs then you don't know who to get rid of...)

Personally I wouldn't have the heart to kick someone after they accepted for these particular reasons of them not being the "right" member.
