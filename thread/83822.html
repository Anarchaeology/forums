Your doing fine. Most people with there first character don't see that mil mark until they are fairly high lvl. Just do as the above poster said and keep doing missions. Also pick up all the junk and sell it to a merchant outside city walls, you'd be suprised how much money that can bring in. I haven't done a single mission in several months myself but even picking up junk on team missions at lvl100ish they tipicly sell for several hundred thousand credits.

Also watch the shopping channel and see what kind of items are selling well, and then if you find a mission with that item as a reward, do it then sell on the shopping channel. Right now items that are selling well are graft and organic armor (if you get a full suit of it, thats even better but people are buying helms alone for quite a bit), also Notum Crystals and Shards are selling very well right now (and found fairly commonly in chests). Tank armor, Decus Coats (although not as well as they used to), commonly used weapons, Sealed Weapon Receptacles, found in chests, are very often used and sell very well, especially Sol Chironis and Pepper Pistol, more the latter.

The above are some suggestions, there are a lot of other items that sell very well too. Oh and one last suggestion, if your a Clanner, get your missions in Avelon (wompa from Athen, Bliss, Avelon) because they pay about 4x creds then any other spot in the cities and tend to give good locations as well.
