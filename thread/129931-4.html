I find it interesting that so many people are so intent on pointing fingers - stating the obvious about what happened 200 years ago, what happened at the meeting, who was responsible, who is the bad guys, who is the good guys - but strangely nobody tries to resolve any of the problems that have arisen. - primarily, what do we do now, to fix the situation we have once again been put in by malevolent leaders, this time Clan ones?

I'm leading a faction of Omni employees who are loyal to the corporation, and has no interest in direct confrontation with any peaceful Clanners - Our problem is the aggressive ones. Storm, Synergy Factor, RK-Mafia, Wen-Tzu, and more, have time and time again proven themselves to be the ones to destroy rather than build - and this planet is getting more and more worn down by the battle each day. Fact is, the fighting doesn't even matter much, due to our extensive insurance system, and in the end, those that live by the gun will have nothing else to show for it than their memories, when they face the exact same enemy for the 178th time. Whether it is aggressive Clans or employees, they are responsible for the lack of peace on this planet, the decreased flow of Notum, kreeping poverty due to lack of finances, and many other things - these are our enemies, and those are the things we should discuss - not whether Wolfe put another cap in Marlamin's butt.

And for the record - neutrals are protected by Omni-Tek regulations, and Chimera's views on neutrals are NOT shared by many guilds, nor even condoned of. Chimera are extremists with no loyalty or allegiance except to their own ideals, which they got from ancient readings of religious zealots. Neutrals are not part of this conflict, and should not, to whichever end, be forced to take any part, neither by Clan nor Omni-Tek - During the ancient wars on Terra, a nation called Schwitzerland chose to remain neutrals, and were thus not attacked neither by one side nor the other - This piece of history should remind us all that to attack those that have no quarrel with you, those that have done nothing against you, those that believe in true neutrality, is barbaric in its very nature.

A long time ago, Kithrak of Red Tape, now employed with Omni-Admin, and thus part of the organ which Healsike and his band of Omni-Tek employed rebels apparently wishes to rid us of, initiated an assembly. The assembly contained representatives from Clan, Omni-Tek, and neutral alike, all discussing to find out what to do, to bring peace to Rubi-Ka, at whatever means. It was known as the Diplomatic Assembly, and at its prime, it was having over 10 guilds attending, from all sides. Unfortunately, due to the rise of the Notum Wars, and other unfortunate events, the Diplomatic Assembly died out - but it sounds like those peaceful, those that believe that Rubi-Ka can actually have a future WITHOUT the everlasting presence of guns, bombs, terrorism, oppression, and genocide, should once again unite, and maybe unite to relieve itself of those that want nothing but to stir and destroy the peace.

Maybe it is the time that something like the Diplomatic Assembly was rised again, to aide in the peace process, to make this planet inhabitable by anything else than gun manufacturers, to protect those not able to protect themselves, and rid ourselves of those that seek to destroy any prospect of peace. Clanners, Neutrals, and Omni-Tek alike has been talking about obtaining peace here - so what about getting to it, and leave the finger-pointing business to the children, waving their father's guns, yelling centuries' old warcries, speaking of events long forgotten and buried...

The guild I represent, is militant, and at the moment I am one of two chairmens of the TRA, but our interest is strictly aggressors, and our goal is, naturally like MOST here, the total elimination of hostility on Rubi-Ka on all sides, and thus peace.

And whomever wishes to put words in my mouth, speaking of atrocities or whatnot, you are welcome to take this up with me - but you will find that there are many that know of my name and what we stand for, and what the Diplomatic Assembly stood for, and they will also tell you that what I say, is also what I mean. I have no hidden agendas in this matter, no secret goals - no means that take us over tattered carcasses of innocents - on the contrary. So whomever wishes to question what I say here, in public forum, will only do so to meet their own needs and hidden agendas, not to obtain the truth.

Anybody wishing to discuss this in a serious way, without the means of personal insults or political outcries, are welcome to contact me at any time.

~Zionai
