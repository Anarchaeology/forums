Way to improve single missions for all
A lot of people including myself have found problems with single missions. There are a number of factors which do cause major problem with players.

The reason we do single missions is often due to a lack of time, problems finding a group or to stock up on cash or items.

But at some levels even doing 50% missions becomes to hard, but for some classes once you reach about level 50 doing missions on your own become a real pain. I have a doc at level 69 now and for the last 12 levels it was impossible.

While I do believe the Over Equiping patch is good for the game I can see peoples view that it will make single missions even harder to do.

So I think we need a change to the single missions not to make them any easier as such but to give more scope. To enable the all classes to do some missions and give players at higher levels a chance to solo to.

I have noticed that the range the slider really works from is very small indee go below 45% and most of the monsters are grey and become pointless go above 60% and they are all red. So most of the scale in missions are wasted and pointless so why even have them.

Or why not make use of them, I think theres to fine a line between 50-60% going from full greens to reds. It would be much better to spread this out by balancing the number of yellows and organge in a mission up until about 75% when you would start seeing red.

But the biggest change should come at the bottom, doing grey monsters is pointless, no xp seems little reward from killing monsters even if you do out class them. First we could extented the missions down to about 40% which would be the bright greens. But why not have a level after that of say blue monsters, which are not worth as much. They are the normal monsters but well half the normal XP value of the mosters or make it a sliding scale from 75% xp light blue to 20% xp very dark blue.

This should hopefully allow all classes to do some missions with a reward, but cant taken advantage of it for power leveling due to the low XP values.


The other way of doing this is to have Class based missions, theres no way a doc doing a single mission is in the same league as an enforcer. I dont expect to be so, but I dont see why my class should prevent me doing solo missions. So maybe the missions should be handed out via class, maybe the missions are different some how, the docs less about fighting more about useling their skills. Or maybe the docs missions are just a bit easier but of course with less reward.

On the whole I think the simple and best way to improve the single missions would be the first one. But I am sure you have your views to ?

Well, you brought up Doctors as a profession that cannot solo very well.

The truth is, they can solo pretty well.

It's impossible to balance missions on a profession basis.

Some professions. like Agents, peak early and can solo incredibly well below level 100. Other professions like Crats have peaks and valleys when you look at their overeall effectiveness.

Some players overequip, while others are poorly equipped. How do you balance mission difficulty when there can be such a wide variance? If you tune the difficulty towards the optimal equippers, the other players with lesser gear and poor IP placement are going to complain that they are too difficult.

If you tune them toward the average player, the ones who min/max and use the best gear possbile may find them too easy.
Then they would have to balance risk vs reward on a class by class basis... which would be a nightmare.
