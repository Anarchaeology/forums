I don't know of any website's that have specific info on the dungeons, myself, but I can offer a tip - Do a search in these very forums. There's a 'Search' button in the top right-hand part of your screen... click on that, type in 'dungeon'... and you'll be a lot closer to where you want to be.

As for dungeon locations I *personally* know about? I know the Foreman's Office is in The Longest Road, West of Athen. I know that the Steppes of Madness are in Omni Forest.

Easiest of all to find is Camelot Castle, smack dab in the middle of Avalon. If you're in Avalon, and you can't see it... well, I dunno what to tell you

Level-wise, I understand that Steppes is the easiest of the lot, "offically rated" at around lvl 30-50... but that's not actually true. It's more along the lines of 50-70... and then, only if you're in a group.
