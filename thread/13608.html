Re-shelling pets for Engineers
Hi everyone,
just got an idea that would fix a tiny problem Engineers have, I'm speaking of losing pets upon logging out or crashing, pets that we have paid for!!
Since Eng pets cost (more and more with higher QL) money, why wouldn't it be possible to have a function to 're-shell' them back into our inventory so we would be able to use them again when we log back on?
That function could be triggered automatically if the game crashes so we wouldn't loose the financial investment we made by creating the shell. I don't know if that would be possible but I kinda like the idea. Pets are just a 'special effect' nano. Other Nanos such as buff are still on when you logg back. Why wouldn't it be the same for the pets (and agai I emphasize on the money aspect of the problem)?
But I'm not complaining, I really like my Eng and it's just a little annoyment compared to the fun.
