Possible Ninja Loot Fix?
When you kill a npc, one team auto get's loot, no one can ninja at that point with Loot Messages now.

The problem comes when you want to transfer the loot to another *certain* player, and you have 20 people standing around clicking as fast as they can.

Why not create a new command, or hotkey. When you have a Loot window open and you target a person within a certain range, and type that command or hotkey, it transfers the loot window from your screen to their screen.

/transferloot or something like that.

Then it could transfer the loot window regardless of how long the loot window has been open, so no corpses fading away before they are looted maybe?

I think this would stop a lot of fights... Those fights tend to get ugly and leave friends fighting one another. I think we need fighting/conflict in the game, but not this kind of fighting...

Really sad....

Anything bad about my suggestion? exploiting it some kind of way would maybe be a possibility, but i'm sure it could be done right... maybe someone already mentioned this? not sure...

Your thoughts...
