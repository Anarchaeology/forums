Press Release - SCHUEMANN & ASSOCIATES TO RELOCATE
For Immediate Release -- November 20, 29477

Rome, Atlantean

SCHUEMANN & ASSOCIATES TO RELOCATE MAIN OFFICES

Racheal Schuemann, senior partner at Schuemann & Associates legal firm, announced today the intention of her firm to relocate from the city of Rome to new offices in Jobe. The move is still pending final approval from Omni-Administration, but is scheduled to begin early next week.

"There was a wonderful coalescing of factors," Ms. Schuemann explained in a prepared statement. "First, we have been concerned for some time about access issues. As most people know, Schuemann and Associates has a diverse client base, including some Clan and Neutral citizens of Rubi-Ka who have had great difficulty getting to us. Given our interest in continuing our pro-bono work for economically disadvantaged citizens who have run afoul of Omni-Tek's sometimes-byzantine legal system, it seemed logical for us to consider offices that would be more widely accesible.

"Secondly," Ms. Schuemann continued, "JAME and the Jobe City Administration have bent over backwards to accomodate us, and we were able to secure a very favorable lease in Jobe. As much as I love Rome, the price/space ratio in Jobe was too good to pass up, and with our lease expiring in Rome, we were going to be faced with a decision soon anyway. Everything just pointed to Jobe. ^_^"

Schuemann & Associates will continue to maintain a skeleton staff at their Rome offices through the end of their lease agreement in mid-December. After that, all staff will be transferred to the Jobe office.

Schuemann and Associates was established on Rubi-Ka in 29475 and has been at the legal forefront of issues ranging from human and employee rights to reaching a sustainable peace settlement with the Clans. They are currently based in Rome Blue.

The woman pressed some buttons and spoke quietly into her headphone. Sundai couldn't make out exactly what she was saying. Several moments later, a dishevelled young man entered the room. His hair seemed to be reaching for the heavens.

"Right this way, miss Div." he said turning his back on her and retreating the way he came in. She stepped around the giant and followed the tall haired man down a long corridor with dark windows. They came to a crossroad and promptly made two right turns into a dimly lit large room.

"Welcome to Omni-Comm" he said. Sundai smiled at him, somehow managing to pull her eyes away from the growth on his head. She continued walking to the large apparatus in the center of the room. A glowing light flickered against the wall behind it.

"We haven't been able to capture everything" he said while tapping quickly on a keyboard, "but we have managed to intercept some of the communications." A list with dates appeared on the wall, presumably each one representing a different communication. "Some of this traffic was easy to get and some was completely scrambled" he continued.

As he selected each item, the message would appear in big black letters on the wall. The latest message from a person named Cedrick "Trybes" Clyburn, held some interest to her.

She has a robot for protection...

The rest appeared to be mostly technical and mundane. She quickly scanned through the remainder of the captured messages. She found it interesting that her new shelter was mentioned, she giggled quietly to herself and told the technician to permanently delete the records.

She heard a loud click, followed by a cascade of white noise. "As you know, several listening devices were planted in certain areas." he boasted, "We have captured several meetings; as well as, Comm-link transmissions."

She listened intently to the Comm-Link messages as they played. Mostly inconsequential, but she noted any mention of names in each conversation. The names might prove useful later.

The recordings captured by the listening devices didn't reveal much either, except a new name to add to the list. "That's just her co-worker" the tech offered. "Yes. Thank you. I got that." Sundai replied.

The sound clicked off. "Is that it?" she asked. "Yes Ma'am" he replied. Sundai faced the technician and began lecturing, "I don't think I need to tell you this, but I will. This is highly confidential information. Information which if it gets out could result in the loss of life. Including yours..." she paused for effect, "Please destroy all records you have immediately and contact me if you get anything else." "Yes Ma'am. I'm always willing to help InternOp's." he said as they both proceeded to exit the room.

As they reached the doorway of the waiting room, he watched her reach over to a large potted plant that adorned the entrance. In a quick and violent manner she pulled one of its long branches off. "I'm not InternOp's... anymore" she said as she walked out the door carrying the branch with her.

Such a bizarre culture he thought to himself.
