Political UPRISING -- Borealis
(The following refers to RK1)

The time is now! Know why this planet is
in such turmoil? Because no one is doing a
dang thing. It seems that the council and Mr. Ross
are just minding there own letting us kill
eachother and let the world go to hell
in a hand basket. Well I won't have it!
I am forming a political guild and we will
take matters into our own hands! I know this can
really work! Here are just a few things about it:

1: We will make contact with other guilds
Clan and OT alike and work out disputes.
2: Open trade with other guilds to benefit both
parties.
3: Role-play. This will be done in so many
ways that I can not begin to list them.
4: Internal Lotto System: That's right! Weekly
lottery drawings to win some major credits!
5: Friendly Family: Easy and great way to
make a lot of friends fast. You'll always have
someone to team with and help you when you need it.
6: Feedback from you!: Your voice will be heard!
All members will have a say in what goes on
in our guild. We are open to all ideas.

Those are just a few things. To learn more or
sign up, visit our website at http://maskedcrusaders.cjb.net

We will be forming in Borealis near the Whomp-pa
system on Thursday July 25th at 3pm Eastern Standard Time
Please be prompt! Look for Chizad. After the creation we'll
head to Reet's Retreat for a celebration!

(To get to Borealis--Find a whopa that goes to newland city, an easily accesible one is located in ICC. Once in Newland City, go to the whomp that says Borealis. You will zone right near the area you need to be.)

See you there!
