Scripts, macros, emotes and roleplaying
Hello!

My name is Prince "Morphex" Olveda and I would like to take a moment to share some of my thoughts on the future of our beloved planet, Rubi-Ka.

Before starting off I would like to say that I know that the people at FunCom have a lot to do already, but I want to discuss some of my ideas on how the future Rubi-Ka will be.

I'll start with the easy part. Scripts, macros and emotes. This is one of my favourite parts of Anarchy-Online. They are genious and make life so much better, easier and more fun!
F.ex. you can use scripts and macros in combat to talk to communicate quickly in team, if you are going to make a speech in front of your guild you can actually script the speech in advance with emotes to, when you are selling your items on the selling channel, you can make scripts for this too, and I could go on about the possibilities for ever, but you all know what you can use them for. However I feel that now is the time to expand this system, and let us be able to script more, and give us more emotes!

I would like to script my aggrobar, so I could turn it up and down with macros. I want to be able to display coords with a simple command like /coord. And there are lots of other things that could be done by expanding this possibility a bit, and I think that this part should not be too difficult to do for FunCom.

So after an easy start I'm going over to roleplay. I think that the world we call Rubi-Ka is a great place. It's a complete threedimensional world to move around in with fantastic landscapes and big cities. However we have a lack of things to do. People are just running around hunting the wildlife all day long, or shooting at each other in arenas or pvp-zones like 2ho or MMD. We have gotten a few places like Baboons and Rompa Bar (funny name if you are a norwegian) to hang out in, but still we get easily bored at these places...

I want to see more entertainment! And I want to see it run by players! What about a theatre where people could perform plays that they have made themselves, or old ancient plays from back on earth. Or stripjoints where people could make a living from dancing. I want to see gambling spots and I want to see more bars and clubs, and most importantly I want players to be in control of these spots!

This brings me on to my next topic. I want people to be able to make livings from doing jobs on Rubi-Ka. I want the trader to be able to own his own little shop, where he can sell stuff that usually is hard to get, or just totally regular stuff that you can get anywhere. This would make it possible for this trader to make a living selling stuff, and give him other things to do than running around shooting at mutants. Then the agents and fixers would have jobs to, supplying the traders in the city with items they want. I imagine there can't be that much trouble creating lots of stores in the big cities, just like apartments, that traders can buy, and run. The point is they have to be EXPENSIVE so that not the regular trader on the street can go shoot a leet and buy a store with the loot. This must be for the real high-level traders with lots of money, and then they can hire the lower-level traders to work there. And maybe one day this lower-level trader gets enough to buy his own place and start his own store The point is people can make a living from stuff like this and it will add a lot to the role-playing part of the game. Those who do not play AO for the roleplaying can still go hunt all day...

And FunCom! Please fix apartments! Make new apartments out of the backyards that can be bought (really expensive) by players! This would be really cool and really make it cool to play the game at high levels! Now you can go brag to you friends about your new house, in the same neighbourhood as Boss Ross lives, and it only cost you 500 million credits!

Make banks where crats can work, and where people can take loans! Make newspapers in-game with the latest news, and maybe players working in the newspapers, writing stories for these newspapers!

The possibilities are endless!!!!

I know this is a LOT of work for FunCom to eventually make, but it would make this already fantastic game so good I would actually consider leaving reallife to live on Rubi-Ka!
