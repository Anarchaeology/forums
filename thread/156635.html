BureauCon X: July 20, Reet's Retreat @ 22:00 GMT
Event name: BureauCon X
Date: July 20, 2003
Time: 22:00 GMT
Location: Reet's Retreat, Stret West Bank
Who may attend?: Everyone

Purpose: A chance for bureaucrats accross Rubi-Ka to get together and disscuss the issues plagueing our profession. There will be a look at issues that affected in the past, present and, possible future.

Current disscussion topics will be divided into two tracks.
quote:
Track 1
Track 1 will focus on more immediate and present issues such as implant setups, weapon types to use, etc.
quote:

General/Basic - Hosted by Szentasha

  • What weapons to use? Pro and Cons of each.
  • 30 minute break
  • Implant for Pets or Charm?



quote:
Track 2
Track 2 will focus on more theoretical and advanced topic matters.
quote:

Theoretical/Advanced - Hosted by Kithrak

  • Crowd control and immunities
  • 30 minute break
  • Our role in PvP



Both tracks will be running simultanously. The event will be structured as follows. 1 hour of disscussion followed by 30 minute break and 1 hour of disscussion. Total planned running time is 2 hours 30 minutes.

Directions: From the whompas in Borealis go to Stret West Bank.

Notes: This is an OOC(out of character) event. If you are interested in helping in the disscussion or the event please contact Charles or Szentasha via private message on the official
forums.

Topics for Track 1 may change slightly. The intended format will be a disscussion moderated by Szentasha and I for the topics, this means we will not be talking for the entire hour while everyone passively listens. We will be there to facilitate the exchange of viewpoints and ideas.

Weapons: Pistols
Szentasha asked me to talk about pistols on crats. Now, though I will explain it all there, here's the brief version so you can see what's covered. Disclaimer: This list is not complete. There's a lot more pistols out there that buff skills. I have not included them here cause I don't have or use them and deem them not practical for Crat use. Feel free to add them to this, maybe we can get a comprehensive pistol guide out of it.

- Daimoness


Pistols are supposed to be the Bueraucrat's weapon of choice, we even get that funny "Gunslinger" +20 Pistol buff. Now, pistols can be divided into two categories - damage or buffs.


Damage-wise, there is


QL 80 Customized IMI Desert Reet 1000

This pistol is nodrop and can be found in the Biomare (also known as "Foreman's") on the Lab Director. It'S a good pistol for lvl 41 - 100 if dual-wielded. Uses Burst and Fling specials.

QL 1 Parasitic Hecataleech

Another nodrop pistol that's found in the Inner Sanctum. I had no chance to test it yet but it sure looks good from the stats, especially the minimum damage on it. Burst Special.

Pain of Patricia

Now, this is easily the best pistol a crat could possibly equip, but it's only for level 175+. Not that you'd be able to equip it at a much lower level, and you won't ever be able to self-equip it, but you'll never be able to self-equip top equipment anyways. Burst and Fling Special.

QL 200 The Original Electronicum v3

This pistol is boss-loot and quite rare. The QL 200 version seems most common. The lowest QLs are available from the secondhand peddler in they Backyards and thus make for a very good startup weapon. Also it's got no special attacks, which makes it less favorable in PvP but saves IP for other things.

Summary: All good pistols are only to be found in special places. For a weapon that's supposed to be the basic one for so many classes (doctors, NTs, Crats) this is less than desirable, but other professions (Soldiers, Fixers) got similar problems.


Buffing Pistols are, among others:


QL 2 Second-Hand Old English Trading Co.

... as the lowest one I've ever seen, with +5 INT ...

QL 87 O.E.T. Co. Pelastio

... the next one with +10 INT, and there's also a Pelastio V2 with +15 ....

QL 100 O.E.T. Co. Jess

... and the higher one with +20 INT and +20 PSY. Then there's the Pelastio V3 with +20 INT and +25 PSY, and on the top end, the ...

QL 200 O.E.T. Co. Maharanee

... with +25 INT/PSY. They all give a Fling special.

QL 121 Polizziotto M203 Druid

This gun was one I used myself for a long time as I had the problem with implants always giving me more PM than SI due to the fact that I always preferred PM/SI layouts with Shining+Faded PM and Bright+Faded SI. This gun roughly equalled the skills again, besides a neat Sense buff. Fling special.

QL 12 MTI B-94

This neat pistol gives a XP buff, starting from +1% at QL 12 up to +5% on the ...

QL 12 MTI B-94

Besides, they all have a chance to cast "Elusive Target" on the user, a +50 Evades buff which can be quite nice in combat. However, these MTI series uses Burst and Full Auto specials, which will be quite IP-costly at higher QLs as both skills are dark blue.

All these pistols have one thing in common: Their damage threshold makes them of little more use in combat than a pillow or cushion, but they don't look as weird.

And - not yet seen in the game, but in the database:

Sleek Minor Fantastic 04

Looks okay and has some buffs to it, but we will see how it will be in action.

Just for your information: Pistols can be buffed by:

Engineer: "Extreme Prejudice" +140
Soldier: "Pistol Mastery" +40
Trader: "Team Skill Wrangler (Premium)" +132
General: "Pistol Expertise" +20
Attribute buffs (Sense+Agility Boost, Enhanced Senses, Feline Grace) up to +8
Total: +340

Burst can be buffed by +110 from a Soldier "Riot Control"
