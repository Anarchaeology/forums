Leetas
I think there would be problems with weapon modifications, which is what you were calling upgrades. Someone else has already discussed this, and the mechanics FC uses wouldn't support it.

One the other hand, how about being able to upgrade QL? You have a weapon you love, but you're outleveling it and can't find another. Take it in to the weapon shop, and for a rediculous amount of money the weaponsmith will raise the QL of the weapon. Same with armor, implants or other QL dependent items. Use some kind of sliding scale to incease cost as the QL goes up.

Certainly easier to implement, just don't know if it would be a positive or negative in the long run.

I like the idea of upgrading the QL of weapons/armor. Maybe you buy a generic upgrade tool, kinda like an advanced hacking device that you shift + rightclick on the weapon/armor you wish to upgrade and it raises it 1 QL at a set money cost. Youd need a higher level QL upgrade device to work on higher QL items. You could even show Engineers some love by making the money cost modified by ranks in Weaponsmithing and Mechanical Engineering. We already have costs in stores modified by Comp Lit, so it shouldnt be so difficult to tack on skill modifiers to cost. Then also make Engineers get another bonus discount to money cost for improving QL of items so you set up a nice service Engineers could sell out to others. Maybe certain classes get special bonuses/discounts to modifying certain sets of weapons. Im open to suggestions.

Maybe the base cost would equal to the difference between your item and the same item 1 QL more. This would be a great money saver overall, considering youd get MUCH less back selling your base item and they buying a higher QL item. Ive sold back weapons that cost 100k for piddly amounts, its just tragic. Maybe increase the cost to represent the fact youre trading convenience for best value.

Items shouldnt have unlimited refinement. Maybe like 30% over base with a max of 30 overall, so once past QL 100 it caps out at +30. Maybe make that improvement max also based on class, Engineers do it better, MPs do it worst, or something. Itll be more cost effective at lower levels to just chuck the item and buy a better QL, but as the item has a higher base QL it might be more worthwhile to refine it. Maybe make unique/nodrop/special QL kinda items non-refineable. Im not sure if the upgrade could also apply to implants/clusters.

I mean in such a hugely nano-dependant world, maybe to a degree a bunch of items are living and upgradeable. Heck youre already able to digitize and summon various QLs of gear, either when you die and get reclaimed or when you use that silly line of fixer summon gear nanos.

Also make it a yes/no popup once you shift+Rclick the upgrade tool on the item, so you can decide whether or not you want to pay the listed cost.
