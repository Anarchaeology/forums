Black Market
I have read all 14 pages of this thread as I am greatly interested in seeing this implemented. The ideas presented here are fantastic. I have a few of my own I would like to share.

In addition to all of the rare items that would be found in the black market, all shady normal item should be moved here as well. (Lockpicks, bomb disarm tools, hacker tools, ID hacking tools, etc.) Why would any legitimate business ever carry these items? The unfortunate reality behind moving these items is that newbies still need access to lockpicks and such. So in addition to the black market (Fixer and FP:Fixer only), a few shady characters should be added that would be accessible to everyone to carry these current "general availability" questionable items. They should cost much more than the black market equivalent however. Likely, these things will remain in the standard machines but it's still something I would like to see. Something that should definitely go into the black market would be higher quality level break & enter tools. Higher level lockpicks and bomb disarm tools that give bonuses.

There should be a clothes machine that carries disguises. This is more of an agent thing, but I think the black market would be the best place for it. You can already dress up like a omni medic, or omni-pol guard. What about disguise kits that give the appearance of other professions or city positions? They should be non-bonus back armor so that you could wear normal armor but have a different appearance. Another option would be a HUD item that generated a illusion of different disguises.

There are a couple of ways to implement buying and transferring no-drop items if this is something Funcom decides to do. As has already been suggested, one would be to ID encode it before use. Another way that I have thought of is to put it inside of a "wrapper" that has to be removed before use, similar to some instruction manuals. The "wrapped" item could be bought and traded. Once it reached it's final destination, the buyer would "unwrap" it by right-clicking on it. The item would be replaced with the no-drop equivalent and could then be used as normal.

I love the ideas given here regarding risk and punishment. Unfortunately, it has been stated that the penal system is no longer envisioned. However, I will share my thoughts in hopes that Funcom may reconsider at some point. In addition to jail (which I think is a great punishment for both rich and poor alike as long as it is limited to max 20 mins and options are given for escape, bail, and such), there could also be community service. Maybe the offender could be stuck with a mission to kill 20 leets or deliver some papers. Until the community service was completed, some basic privilege could be withheld. Cannot use shops, cannot insure, not allowed inside a major city zone, not allowed to grid, etc. Something along those lines. Getting caught and punished for trading illegal goods is probably too difficult to code at this point, but it sure would add to the excitement in my opinion.

Finally, I'd like to address the issue of false profession used to access this area. As someone that has both an agent and a fixer, I hope that you realize that this is something agents were designed to do. Access to restricted areas by means of assuming someone else's identity was the most exciting idea that agents posed for me. Make it dangerous to do so if you want (this only adds excitement for agents), but make it possible.
