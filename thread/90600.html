I know exactly what you mean and the lag is unbearable at times.

I have no idea what the specs are on your computer, but I have a P4 2.0 with 1gig ram and a GeForce3 and I lag like a mofo in large tower battles.

What I've been doing is turning off: clouds, environmental effects (most of them), cutting view distance to 50% and character view distance to 40%. If its still bad, I turn off nano effects (you may have to zone for some of these, not sure yet). While you may not be able to *see* the characters, you can still tab them.

Surprisingly, it helps more than you can imagine. There's a lot of stuff your computer is drawing when you're at 100% view/char distance w/ all effects on. I told people at yesterday's battle to do the same and they all yelled at me with things like "Yea right, my computer's uber, this is server lag." Finally a couple of them made the changes and were quite surprised, then thanked me privately

Sure its a pain to fumble with graphics settings before entering a battle, but its less of a pain than dying due to lag and becoming frustrated to the point where you put the game down for a bit.
