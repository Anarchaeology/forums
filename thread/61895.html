Layer Nano--some change needed?
Ok, this post is about both Enforcer and NanoTech Layer Nano lines.

Currently, the NT layer is consider as junk because of its recharge time (and since NT uses nukes, they'd rather use the time to nuke the enemy instead of recasting layer).

A second problem would be the NCU cost problem. Layer nano costs way too many NCUs, and it's a big problem for NTs and Enforcers because they already have a bunch buffs to have with them all the time. Besides, for a nano program that become useless after 2~3 hits, 40~50ish NCU is just a bit too much.

So here's my suggestion, reduce the recharge time (to something like 3.5~3 second recharge) and NCU cost of the Layer Nano lines (1/2 or 1/3 of the original NCU cost).
