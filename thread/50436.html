A serious issue: Harrasement
I would like to make the suggestion for an ingame feature that would help temper ingame harrasement and hopefully leed to players being more responsible for their behaviour and actions ingame.

Those who follow the social going-ons and read the roleplaying forum will be aware of several recent situations where leaders in the roleplaying community have been stalked and harrassed by anonymous alternates. What I mean is that people have been taking advantage of the ability to create an alternate who is in no way associated with a main character to harrass another player. If you put the character on ignore, then the player can create another alt, and use it to harrass you again. This also plays out with organizations where you can kick someone out of an org because of their bad behaviour and then they are back again and regoin the org with an alt just to continue the same behaviour.

I think that by somehow giving us a way to relate two characters to the same account, it will curb this malicious behaviour. I don't know if I would go as far as having a link to alts under a character profile. I would recommend the following though. Have a way to get an account id from a character name, so that you can check to see if this id is the same for two characters. Also, I think you should be able to use /ignore on an account basis so that if someone is harrasing you, you can continue to ignore that person even if they create an alternate.

I would like to finish by saying that I hope you understand the seriousness of this issue. I know for certain that people have left AO because of harrassment, and that harrassment most often targets prominent figures in the community. I think Funcom should be very concerned about not only losing player accounts, but those of important community leaders because of this type of harrassment.
