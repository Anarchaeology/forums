Agreed. Special NPCs, quest NPCs, dungeon bosses and so on are heavily overcamped, and it is no fun! At the same time they shouldn't make spawn times shorter. It should be some degree of difficulty getting an item.

Right now it is like "So you have QL 200 armor no at level 50. Look what I got! This "Motha****ing Cool Special Item" is just great!" "HAHAHAHAHAHA! What is the glory in that?"

What I want is a reply more like this: "OMFG! How did you get that??? I heard all Dark Carnival went to get it yesterday and failed!"

There is no glory in camping a mob for three weeks to get a special item you can show your friends.

What I would like to see is for outdoor special mobs to have random respawn time and random respawn place. Maybe even random respawn zone to make it extra difficult. There could be NPCs around giving you hints about where the mob is every time he spawns.

For example:

You are out looking for special NPCs. You come across this NPC called Linus "Penguin" Thorvalds, and you start asking him the usual questions like "quest" "mission" "what can you tell me?" and so on. When asking him what he can tell you, he tells you about his old friend Bill "Monkeylover" Gates, that went out for a trip earlier this day. He gives you a hint about where he went, like for example he wanted to collect some carbonrich rock. So if you are a skilled adventurer, or have traveled a lot, you know that there is a zone where there is carbonrich rock laying on the ground. You pick up a few friends, and after a short hunt you find him and kill him. You get the "Motha****ing Cool Special Item".

Now, for all you know "Monkeylover" has already respawned somewhere, or for all you know it will take 24 hours till next spawn. Next time he spawns and someone talks to Linus, he will give them a hint too. Sometimes the hint can be a riddle, or maybe the name of another NPC you have to find, that will give you a hint about "Monkeylover".

Sometimes maybe "Monkeylover" spawns somewhere with a heavy concentration of high level Sand Mantis, so you need a powerful team to get there, or maybe some other time, he spawns somewhere hard to find, or maybe you get a hint or riddle from Linus "Penguin" Thorvalds that is hard to figure out. Anyway it would add a lot of difficulty, and maybe fun and prestige to finding a special mob, and killing him for a special item.

As for quest mobs, or dungeon bosses, I currently have no ideas of how to remove the camping of them, but I am sure there are someone with a good idea out there. My point is just that we need change. FunCom need to think new.

I still believe the best idea would be to spawn a whole new dungeon for every group or player that enters, but this requires a few things in place first.

There would have to be some trigger item to get in, most likely a key that could be duplicated and everyone that has this item (same item duplicated) will appear in the same mission. Something like the childhood memory teddybears of the SOM comes to mind. First, this would solve the grief and competing for ridiculus spawn times. Second, it would ensure those who group up and decide to do something together have a "guaranteed" attempt at it. The "group" could easily be 1 person, a 6 players group, or several groups together - as long as they all have a copy of "item-to-get-into-mission" #4229.

So far, with a unique missions for each group/guild/faction, you need to eliminate some other issues like item harvesting and spawn camping. This also seems quite easy to fix - what if each spawned dungeon only has 1 boss in it (ie, there's only one TIM) and if you manage to kill it, it wont spawn again ever. Until you leave the mission and request another one which will be fully fresh with.

That would make it an actual challenge to get to these particular boss mobs (as well as giving the players some good adventure), as you couldnt just sit around camping the boss spawn for hours and hours knowing it would eventually be yours. Every time you wanted a go at that item, you would have to spawn a new fully loaded dungeon, fight your way through it to the end, and try to take on the boss. If you your group did this, as was intented, you'd be guaranteed a reward - no grief, no waiting around for 12 hours, no getting the same item over and over again without defeating the whole dungeon again, because you logged out at the boss spawn.

In this system nodrops would actually work as intented and higher levels harvesting items for their lower friends and members would not do so at the expense of other players. Neither would it be possible to harvest an item and flood the market, but everyone would have an equal opportunity for reward.
