It's that SF thingie....
Is there life after AO?
I don't know...
At first this game is overwhelming and very complex but once you get the hang of it, it is very very addictive.
The possibillities are endless with this game and when I get bored and go into another mmorpg or rpg where I have to hack and slash with axes, swords, bows, I feel this eerie cheezy feeling within my stomach and I really miss my Manex, my NovaFlow and my Chem Maussers... AO is insane programming in an insane world. and when I'm bored I station myself unto spots where things happen and just look and buff all those who ask me.. or fly in my yalm over the country or go into Omni-cities bugging the guards and people there...
What I think is awesome and this really made this game is that for once we leave the "Earth and Fantasy" scenario like "A Long time ago..blablabla"...

The other great thing of AO and MMORPG's in general is that you cannot cheat or ubercheat like that there is a fixer running around at level 500 with all the other char's nano's he/she suddenly can use. If you develop a character you really develop one and it becomes your online alter ego. This alter-ego gets himself involved with other people and it is by your actions that people will remember you, like real life... It is so cool to watch your alter-ego grow and become a real nasty killing (healing) - machine and show some great gameplay.

One can sometimes get bored and one can leave AO for some time but evidence has shown us many many times that people DO come back even when they say that they will leave permanently. They mostly come back because AO is insane...it is one of the best out there on the Internet at the moment (my 2 cents) and people all want the best

I have great expectations of other sf-mmorpg's but only time will tell if they can surpass AO.
