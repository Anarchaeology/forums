OE + IP reset
a while back, i would have been for this patch... the hardcore OE has become somewhat annoying and unpleasant as far as dealing with people goes [you greedy, nasty buff people ]. this game is frustrating to begin with, and i really can't fathom why anyone would want to subject themselves to the additional torture of standing around for hours looking for implants, stacking this or that, or degrading themselves for buffs =]

HOWEVER, after reading the post by gaute i am no longer in favor of the solution AS PRESENTED. it's just needlessly complicated. i don't see why we need equations and percentages to solve the OE problem. we know funcom has a hard time with issues of complexity [no offense i think you guys have been doing well lately] and this easily rivals 12.6 levels of potential malfunction. i'm really not sure what technical issues are involved, but it seems to me like they should just reduce the amount gained from the buffs themselves if they're hell bent on solving the problem. we know that the bonuses they give to ATK ratings don't do much of anything -- they're for OE. hack down the amount the buffs give you; wrangles boost less, mocham's/masteries/etc boost less, essences, on and on. for the people who have already OEd weapons... well. not sure. a mandatory unequip of all items would be good, if that's possible... and it should be, since you're making mandatory unequip of all items a requirement for IP reset. if you can't make people unequip all their gear, just combine a decrease in buff potential with a hard level cap on all the weapons, which we know can be done as a lot of unique guns/armor have level requirements. put the cap on implants too and stop the stacking crap. if you don't meet the cap and item is somehow equipped, weapon/armor/implant disabled, just like a yalmaha in doors. since you'd still be working with the current mechanics and fixed buff amounts, it'd be easy to figure out what level cap an item should have, too. and i'm not buying that it'd be too hard to input the info into the database. it's data munky work, man... if you miss one or two items, that can be easily fixed without screwing up the whole game. if some idiots find an item that was missed or whatever and dump IP to exploit, who cares, their problem. to me this makes more sense.

IP RESET. ok. i don't like this as presented, either. i know a lot of skills are screwed up and an IP reclaim would be nice ... i've got 250 points wasted in riposte, like 150 in sharp objects, etc etc. but the whole idea of making it an ongoing part of the game is just weird... again, it's just too complicated. why create all this needless headache? man, with people being allowed to reset skills that often you are just asking for exploits. i don't understand why this would even be considered. make the IP reset a one time deal for EACH character... sort of like the /command claim for a pioneer backpack. that way any new players who screw up can do it once, and all the current players can do it once. not to mention it's got to involve less coding, and as mentioned previously, less room for a repeated 12.6 apocalypse. to me this also makes more sense.

FC = great ideas, bad execution. Q.E.D., man... we've SEEN it =]

solutions like this preserve the existing game mechanics, satisfy the people who hate OE, and give the existing OErs the same system on a reduced level. simple to understand even for new players, less confusion, less exploit potential, blah blah blah. FC's proposal is just nutty... everyone's already covered the potential problems with it even if their system DOES work without huge bugs.
