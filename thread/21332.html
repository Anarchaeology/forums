Nano Nerf...
This game is well designed for the most part, especially when it comes to breeds. It's shown that each breed has certain advantages. Atrox get good melee skills, good strength, great HP, etc. Opifex get good agility, ranged skills, etc. Solitus are the best of all worlds, but with no exceptional advantages.

Notice the lack of the Nano. Now just by the name, it infers that the Nano should have higher nano skills. Well, I've played the game for 7 almost 8 months now... Lemme tell you, the Nano's "uber nano skills" have only a negligible advantage. Any solitus MP could have the same skills as my Nano MP at the same lvl with the right imp's. Even then, the solitus get's more HP.

Now, it is well known that enforcers have the lowest cost increase for body-dev. It's a bit sick though. I have a friend who is a lvl 48 solitus enforcer who is far from twinked, and much the opposite really. He has no body-dev or life implants in him. At that lvl, with his buffs he nearly doubles my nano MP's HP. This is only one example, but you must see how, resultingly, this leads to slaughter in PvP.

This excuse can be made that MP's are meant to have their pets tank. And this is very very true. But in PvP, who in their right mind would attack the MP's pet and not the MP first? It's damn near impossible for an MP to win in PvP when you're a nano. Excuses can also be made that we get healing pets. Well that works well for the solitus and atrox MP's who actually have enough HP for it to matter. But when you have 700 HP and your mobs hit for 60-100 and fast, that pet doesn't do anything for you and is really only useful to heal your atk. pet.

Well, mission mobs can be solved with the mezz pet, right? Wrong. We all have experience with the 3 mobs in the main room of the missions. You send a pet to atk one of them and the other 2 attack you. Nice, nice, nice. Which, once again, may be okay for solitus' and atrox, but when you're nano, you don't even have time to get out the door sometimes. And it isn't like you can defend yourself in that situations very well either. MP nukes are sucky and I don't see much point to them, and you get awful weapon skills. People buzzed to me about the Creations, like crotalus, etc. When you're nano MP, you don't want to go in the battle and add that minute amount of extra damage even if you CAN hit them. Only useful creations are the shields like the Vital Buckler that give you that little extra HP boost. GRRR! It isn't like our nano skills are so uber that we get access to the next nuke so soon before a solitus that it can make a difference. Even then, nuking will only get you attacked in many situations.

Eh, I'm rambling. If you think Nanos are nerfed to the extreme, gimme some of that bumpy stuff. Ugh, and no flames plz. I don't want a bunch of whiney Atrox MP's on here ranting about they can't use masteries at lvl 50.....

Are you guys nuts? All breeds are the same? Oh hell no!

Most people think an Atrox will have the most HP in the long run BZZZZT, wrong. The amount of extra HP is litereally negligable (in the 20s) for Nano Professions. For Professions with a ligher color Body Dev, its a different story.

People are dumb in making things like Atrox MPs. Atrox MPs get *no* HP advantage over solitus. Both professions get 3 HP per Body Dev. However Solitus will have incredibly higher nano and much higher Nano Skills.

An Atrox Intelligence caps at 400, Solitus at 480 and Nanomage at 512.

A Nanomage will always be able to cast things faster then any other breed especially in the high end game for nano casting classes. Why? It's simple. Most level 100-200 are skill capped. The higher int gives you that extra umph to make it. Nanomage also lacks in HP.

For most oter professions where weapons can be overequipped, all things are pretty mcuh equal. You choose the breed that gives the most HP.

But for classes dependant on Nano Skills, you dont want to choose a Breed with a Low Intelligence cap IE Opifex or Atrox. It makes no sense.
