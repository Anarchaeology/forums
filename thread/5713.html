I agree with you both - and I'll clarify this:
I believe there should be a balance between BREEDS and between PROFESSIONS. No breed should be inherently better than the other, and so with proffesions, so all players will start from a more-or-less equal point, and from there their choices will influence how their character will turn out ==> creating the imbalance between CHARACTERS Marlark described.
I think that such balance/imbalance greatly adds to the fun of the game, and gives equal opprtunity to all players, though still making place for each character to remain different.
With time, creative players also show that classic breed/profession combination can be done better, like an Atrox/Doctor or an Atrox/Engineer, both I've seen were astonishingly excellent characters. The choices we make during the game dictate how how good or bad our characters will become.
