How about a little Law and Order?
Today I filed official complaints with the appropriate organizations in Omni-1 and Athen. I encourage other neutrals to do the same.

Yesterday, neutrals all over Rubi-Ka were the subject to unprovoked attack by junior clansmen and omni-tek organization employees. For example, as I walked out of the Whompa into OmniTrade from ICC, dressed in social silvers and headed to Rompa's to meet friends, a couple Rookie OTs opened fire and blasted me back to the reclaim in Borealis. Geesh, I didn't even have time to get my bearings before I was dead!

Neutral friends in Athen reported similar events throughout that city.

The professional, military, well-trained Clan and Omni guards who regularly patrol the whompa, grid terms, and gates stood by and did nothing about this. They continued to engage incoming opposition per their standing orders, but obviously didn't have anything in their Rules of Engagement about friendlies ganking neutrals.

Eventually I did win through to Rompa, but only after donning my best battle armor, moving to full defense, and having every runspeed, HOT and defensive nano going possible for a poor little ol' 16th level fixer. Even then, if I'd been just a little slower, I'd have been a one dead duckie again.

If the omni and clan cities are going to be about as safe as a Mission Site, lots of people are going to start making pre-emptive strikes, and pretty soon every city will look just like a mission site. For example, I had to take down a variety of omni pedestrians, just to insure they didn't gang up on me and take me down! If things started to get bad, I'd call on higher powered friends to help blow open a route for me, and who knows where all that escalation might lead?

To prevent this senseless violence, official Clan and Omni agencies should start policing their junior staff better, and make the streets safe once more for free travel by appropriately factioned individuals.

Now, I freely admit the low-level neutral employees are starting to return the favor against Omni and Clan travellers, and that should stop too. Not to mention Loren Warr. Putting him in Newland didn't help a thing. Sure the Sentinels in Tir are wrong. But two wrongs never made a right. All they make is violence and hatred, generation after generation.

Besides, how's a good fixer to get ahead with all these bullets flying? It's much easier to acquire all those things I "need" with a little peace and quiet.

- Needful in Borealis
