Steps of Madness
I think it is time that Steps of Madness is revisited and revised as an area of the game sorely in need of tweaking. I mean this in several ways and I think that the lack of people using the area would support me in this. First is the fact that when it was made the people of Rubi-Ka were very different, many people were using weapons way beyond their normal ability range and controling pets significantly in excess of what the same type people control now. Due to this the average strength of a creature in Steps of Madness is way over powered, as an example a few weeks ago I went into steps with 3 other people all of us a bit nervous since not having been in there in a long time. The first creature we meet was a yellowish green ( lvl 39 NT at the time) Pillar of sanity, I hit him with my highest power damage I could (Glacial) to lead off the battle, and everyone else joins in. In the fight we lose our adventurer (4 on 1 who is yellow and we lose a guy) and decide to move on while waiting for him to rejoin us. I move ahead alone and encounter another Pillar and decide I wanna see a bit more about what these guys are like this one is again yellowish green to me. After 4 min of fighting I have died and barely managed to reduce the mob by 1/3 of his HP, he shurgged off the damage from my best nanos and broke root in secondswhen I tried to hold him back. At this point the other 2 people said they were leaving that the dungeon is too tough for them and could hunt easier elsewhere. I ask them if they want to come back later and they respond by telling me that by the time they can handle the dungeon it gives no experience so it's not worth it.

The other reason that Steps needs a revision is that upon the creation of temple of Three Winds most of the loot in Steps has been made of comparatively low value and use. If you were to take a small group and have them go over the stats on the creatures in Steps and maybe tweak a few of the loot items it could easily become a viable part of the mid game experience instead of an area avoided by the player base. Ironically at the levels that Steps if intended for most people are looking for a place to adventure since they have gotten most of what they need from the Temple already and a bit of loot to draw them in that direction or even a clue pointing them to Steps from the Temple could provide that push.

When it was originally put in game, Steps of Madness was intended to be done by a full team of level 50-60 chars. It's not at all urprising that your team had problems. It was never intended to be a place where you'd go for exp.

Your point about revising old statics over time is a good one. I think FunCom has changed a few things. If I recall correctly, the spawn time of some mobs has changed - not just TK, I think Neleb and TIM, too. Some drops changes - the ring tri-plumbo used to drop now drops from Ian Warr for goodness sake.

There are still a few worthwhile things in SoM: Nelebs Cloak, Gentle Hands, Brutal Hands, Fractured Sanity rings and generic TL2/3 rings and bracers. Maybe the jealousy, too. But I think the jealousy augmented aggro enhancers are as broken as other taunting tools these days.
