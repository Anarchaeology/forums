a few ideas not out of the ordinary and quite useful stuff
this may be my first post upon this forum but i have been in the game for quite some time and have been asking around over the past few weeks about ideas that would be of great use this are mainly proffesion based nano skills or changes etc.

Doctor: Above anything else the doctor should get a ressurection nano, all other MMORPG's have healing classes that all possess this ability if not this, why not a rezz effects healing nano?

Fixer: The ability to pickpocket from mobs on some occastions they can pickpocket rare items that would only be obtainable via pickpocketing.

Trader: Haggling ability to gain more money from selling junk to the vendor or lower price on items in the vendor!

NT: an area nano that freezes everything allowing time for the nt and his/her team to escape the nt and team members cannot attack during this nano and it cannot be cancelled as its classed a hostile timed.

Soldier: A beserk rage nano ranged int and crit % are increased for a short period of time upon nano cancellation/time out nano skills are locked down for a period of time and ranged int is decreased by a specific amount for a few seconds

Crats: The ability to travel into opposite conflict areas without being aggroed by guards and other NPC's if unarmed, also a second assistant droid they can summon to go and buy nano chargers, treament kits, ammo etc while they carry on about there duties (it could opperate as a portable vendor)

MA: Kick either more often in combat loop or it could become a move activated like brawl that does more dmg but takes longer to charge and is only availble to MA's also an attack much like dimach that takes longer to charge and can kill in one blow.

Adventurer: Increased ability to navigate maps with ease, areas only an adventuer can access or simply new areas that only they can find! also something different other than pit dragon, dragons are wayyy too comon as a final form how about a gender specific form like banshee for female and minotuar for atrox and males

MPs: Mind reading ability, detects mobs hp, aggro, and increases dodging capabilities.

Eng: weapon building fixed and armor building integrated!

these are just a few proffesion ideas tell me what you think and if FC reads this boards if you like em take em on board!!!
