In the case of the Hen and the Egg, the Egg Indeed did come first. As the evoloutionary process you refered to allows progression of species through generations. The first full fledged member of the Hen specied was indeed from an Egg, so "The egg came first".

In a sense of life itself on this planet. You have to look at what you consider an "egg". The forming of life through a single cell organism, you may state that the organism has no egg and was created in essence without a fetal stage. But you can also argue that being a single cell organism progressing to a state of animated life we would recognize today was it's fetal period, and it was indeed an "egg" itself. It's all very grey when speaking in terms of life itself and origins.
