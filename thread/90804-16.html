Little long friend... but well put.
It is a terrific setting to remind me of the days of old. And still daily every time I'm asked for credits I offer the fishing pole. Too often these people turn it down in favor of finding the quick score instead of the intelligent path of knowledge and power.

So much has been improved in the game since I started. Those who are new to the game have only one thing to base their ideas on. A lack of knowledge of the game gives them the impression that it is difficult. Money becomes a major demand in their eyes. Being someone who never held onto more than 10M in the game ever (well... before recently) I learned how to make do with what I had.

Before... I mean WAY before a SINGLE BULLET cost 30 creds. This did not change for many days. About 2 months after release they got the servers stable enough where you could acutally go thru 50 bullets an hour. After that bullets got an upgrade to 25 or 10. I forget which. Ammo was a major concern for all. Money came in extremely tiny portions then as well. Nobody even heard let alone owned a Yalmaha. Weapons were concerned with 3 things. Quality, speed, and ammo. Anything that didn't have difficulty in equiping at low levels (since nobody was 100th level yet) was prized. Buying it for 10k was a serious investment. Having a good gun speed ment that things would die faster. This increased your income, thus was a good thing. And ammo... by god ammo was the biggest pain everybody ever had. Any weapon that didn't use expensive ammo was good. Anything that didn't use ammo at all (martial artist techniques included) was godlike. However most Nano programs were unused, untested, and worst of all... without implants.

I was arround assembling minor implants back then. I never understood their benefits at first. And building anything was impossible. But people must learn the basics before they begin the path to wealth.

Before you do anything I strongly suggest that you learn the ancient technique... BLITZIN'

The art of getting much for very little. It's quite simple to do really. Takes minimal reflexes (point and click, and use the keyboard directional arrows) and offers multiple rewards (credits, armor, weapons, and other interesting items) with very minimal thought. Thus the origin of clicksaver was begun when someone thought "Gee... there must be an easier way to read the missions than going thru each one and reading all this junk."

All you need to do is get a mission, find the end point (either an object to grab, an item to target, an object to use something on, or a person to target) and quickly end it. Thus building on much with little.

This brave and powerful person laid the foundation for another terrific program. NanoNanny. Someone had money, and wanted to build implants. Not knowing what to do next one decided it was time to make a list, check it twice, and find out exactly what would be nice.

http://www.timezulu.com.au/NanoNanny/

check it out newbies everywere and even high levels. This is a needed bit of information till shadowlands.

Some market news for other new players from someone in the game for about 3 weeks. Apparently AO has seen a big influx of new players this month or this thread has been recently rooted.

Market prices on QL10 Concrete Cushions have dropped by almost 50% in the last two weeks. Toxsus posted in this thread that he sold a pair for 650K. Friends that joined this game when I did were selling them for 500K/pair two weeks ago. Last week I was able to sell them 400K/pair and over the past weekend I've seen people advertising them for 250K. One person I talked to was so desperate to sell that he wanted me to buy his stock to sell at higher prices later.

On a brighter note, the QL1 Old English Trading Co. pistols that add +5 INT are sellling for much more than TheDeacon was seeing. He suggested 50K/pair, but I've been selling them for 200K/pair fairly easily. I've even seen others selling slightly higher quality pistols (QL2-3) for up to 250K/pair.

I can highly recommend Jayde's Anarchy Mainframe (http://aomainframe.info) to find other items that have low requirements for buffing purposes.

I've sold the QL1 Tsakachumi rifle that adds +4 AGL for 100K. I've not been able to sell the QL46 pistol I have that adds +20 to CompLit and QFTheory but I've seen people advertising the QL44 version for 400K/pair. Also, the QL51 Pillow with Important Stripes that adds +15 to three nano skills I sold for 400K.

If you can't sell your Concrete Cushions, try to branch out into another line that might not be as saturated with competition.
