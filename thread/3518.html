Why some weapons don't go to level 200?
Why is it that some weapons don't go up to quality 200?

I'm thinking of agent's weapons, rifles, but I assume this must be a problem with other weapons as well.

The only rifles which apparently actually exist up to level 200 are stigma rifles and disafilliation rifles, both of which have serious weaknesses. The stigma rifle is a fast firing, lower damage weapon, which is not what you want when you're high level and have a super high ranged initiative.

In fact, as of level 70, I have >already< got my ranged initiative high enough so that my stigma rifle (not a 200) is maxxed out in its speed, it fires as fast as it can. So why would I ever want to raise my ranged init again?

The disafilliation rifle only holds 5 bullets, which is >also< not what you want when you have a high ranged initiative. What good does it do you if your gun fires extremely fast, but spends 30% of it's time reloading?

Is there some reason why the better rifles like the Tsakachumi rifle or the MTI flechette don't go up to 200?

I understand that overall rifles are balanced compared to weapons of other professions, and yes, this is proven by the fact that agents use many different rifles.

But I'm not complaining about gripocoms only going to level 50. What I'm wondering is, why do those other rifles stop at level 160, 175, or 188? Why don't they go to 200?
