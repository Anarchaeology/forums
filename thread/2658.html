Assassin in Vegas
K, for those that never read my site (ie all but the odd person that walks up to me in a blue moon and says "Is that YOUR site on the OT intranet???) i'll do a quick re-cap...

My names Lev, Lev "Levrivers" Rivers, professional assassin, at your service....

im soon to update my intranet site advertising my services, and i thought i'd get a few things straightened out....


When i arrived to this planet, it was because i had been guaranteed work. Omni-Tek had heard of my reputation as the "assassin with a concience" and knew i wouldnt backstab an employer.....

With my sister moved to Newland a year before, it was thru her that i heard there may be work out here for the guy that killed the Chancellor of Mars government...

So, there i was, neutral, foot loose and fancy free...

Then, before the conflict had really kicked in, work was slow.

"So..." i thought "...better get some work in the mean time..."

I found myself a month later working for the Baboons franchise as a night host at the famous nightclub. Making friends and getting into the often seedy RubiKa nightlife....

Despite this, i also met a girl who can only be described as a goddess, aptly named "Purity"....

A car, two apartments (one in Rome, another in Borealis) and a skill change later i find myself now getting more work as an assassin in the amnesty than ever before, although i wish i'd never made my work for OT public, id of liked to remain a neutral....


But, i've certainly not been out of the limelight by any means, I was branded a clan sympathizer by AO stratics and an Omni Traitor by AO Basher by a group claiming to hunt out and root out clanners within omnitek.
The truth is, that none of that transpired, that night was spent with them holding me up and demanding my money and possessions, in short, a robbery....

So, i was mugged, albeit i ran and kept all my stuff. But that wasnt the last time i was to be news in lil ol RubiKa.....


I got married,...
to Purity, yes yes,,........she's now the lovely Mrs.Lev "Purity" Rivers
and we're both really happy. Although work doesnt allow us to see each other as much as we want, we try out best to see each other whenever we can.

Im also known to many as "That guy in that stag night video" a night that cannot be described in words.....
(link to be added once ive asked Tappert again)
It seems one person in every room/zone seems to have seen that blasted video of me doing the YMCA in a naughty costume....Thankyou Solar, thankyou very much......

I was also present at the Radiman Clone shooting,. Most people would think i should be jealous at Aberic for getting their first...
but why should i? I wasnt on a contract to kill Radiman, if no money is involved (OT hasnt asked me to kill Radiman yet) then im not interested, its a job, plain and simple.

So, that about wraps it up for me at the moment.
apart from Omni-Tek Employee's have been screwing with my ID chip recently.....

Stay safe people, and *always* look behind your back.....
