ooc-

The primary reasons for staying neutral are:

1) if you like a challenge (it's not an easy life being neutral).

2) if you disagree with Omni and Clan's goals/behavior/dogma/etc. enough to want to stay out of picking allegiance entirely and take your own independent path.

3) Its much easier to travel, with the exception of Tir and a perhaps Bliss (at low levels).

If your desire for a token board is more than enough to cancel out the appeal of neutrality, then by all means grab an application. In this game your own happiness comes first. If you're still hesitant to pick a side, keep in mind the disadvantage of not having toke boards is part of the price for the freedoms neutrality has to offer. That will probably never change.

Personally I’ve accepted all the disadvantages and still have a great time with my characters (all neutral).
