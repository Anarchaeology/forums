((I know this happened a long time ago. But I just had an idea.))

This only applies to Homo-Solitus:

Part of the reason offplanet travel may be restricted is because of the special Nanobots in use on Rubi-Ka. The nanobots on Rubi-Ka are adapted to use Notum. So it would make sense to me that if you left Rubi-Ka you would be left with millions of nonfunctioning nanobots all through your body. Your immune system would see these as foreign objects and try to destroy them. This would cause serious problems with the functioning of your body. I heard somewhere that the inhabitants of Rubi-Ka have to have their nanobots flushed every 6 years or so. If this is true and nanobots can be flushed then it is just a simple procedure of evacuating the nanobots and not replacing them. Then your body will be clean of and nanobots. After that problem has been solved there is still another. Your body was created and has had small notum powered nanobots in it your whole life on Rubi-Ka. So in effect you had a long term immune system, and muscle booster. I beleive there are still nanobots on other planets, but not of the same type as the notum tuned ones on Rubi-Ka. This may cause your current Homo-Solitus which has been tuned for use with Notum Attuned Nanobots to be incompatible with other nanobots. This would also cause problems with the body's immune system. I think I might start a research paper on this...

Let me know what ya think

As a point of related interest, there might be a way that a Nano breed could leave the planet. I once saw Dr Omeneal Certes, former CoT sponsored scientific mind, turn a Homo Solitus into a Blubag. And then turn him back. Sure, I know what you are thinking, and yes, I had been drinking. But not enough to not know what I was seeing, and besides, there were other witnesses. After the demonstration I spoke with Dr Certes about the possibility of permanent breed change. He believed it possible, with little to no side effects. So, it might be possible for a Homo Nano to change itself to a Homo Solitus and thereby leave the planet. But, that would depend upon the Homo Nano in question being able to find Dr Certes, and convince him to help, which would be no mean feat.
