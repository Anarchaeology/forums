Agent Starling
The end is near !
Well, I will just reiterate the numerous concerns voiced on the previous posts. Agent's bread 'n butter is CONCEALMENT/Stealth. Other classes rely on sheer firepower or bruteforce and a good chunk of AC to back them up. Some need to resort to pets and others to powerfull nanos. This is called class balance.

Not everyone is supposed to go hacking and slashing the good ole fashioned way, and thats what attracted me and many others (I'm sure) to this game. INOVATION. Funcom managed to reshape the formulae of MMORPG's by creating an extremelly attractive world filled with exotic wildlife, outstanding storyline (when it's out, that is). and their "piece de resistance" : a myriad of VERY UNIQUE professions. Each supposed to contribute to a group using their own PARTICULAR abilities. Funcom has also stated numerous times that one doesn't necessarily need to be in a group to play this game. You can solo critters, do missions etc. Now, that is something, or so I thought.

Agents are supposed to use their core abilities to get past obstacles. We just lost ours. Concealment was the number one reason to be accepted in a group. I could pull mobs (the ones that aid each other) from a distance using sneak+aimed. It doesn't work anymore because mob perception is simply unbeatable! The strange thing is, try and use aimed while on sneak mode and you get a message that the monster is aware of your presence. Now get out of sneak mode and use aimed. OMG, it works!!!! Well, not exactly. Since I was not concealed the other mobs "saw" me shooting at their buddy and 15 seconds later I have 5 other group members giving me a stern look at the reclaim terminal. Now, do they need an agent? Not really. Not for the time being :/



Ok, high concealment + solo missions seems to be the number one reason for this nerf. Many others have said it already but I'm going to sound like a parreet (pun intended) and say it again. This is NOT and exploit. It is MY own business if I decide to forfeit ALL the experience a mission has to offer in favor of a material reward such as a piece of armor ( that wont be 5 levels higher than myself , anyways). Going straight for the pot of gold is the worst way to advance your character. You get jack didly for XP. Secondly, you can get all the XP from an easier mission +reward and of course all the cash and loot from mobs. You make just as much ( much more is more likely) and advance your character at the same time. Also, after a number of solo missions the rewards do become repetitive, so it's pretty much pointless going back for more.

However, the main premise with AO was to do as you please with your character. If you want to powerlevel and become an unstopable PvP machine? Thats great. Want to do missions with your buddies and level every now and then? That's also great. Casual player? Not enough hours to get on a group and hunt? Not a problem. Try the solo missions and have just as much fun. Want to become a trader (not the in-game profession) and spend countless hours sifting through the spam on the trade channel just to make a buck? WHy not? let it be. He chose not to advance his character in favor of cash. This is not an exploit.

KNOWN EXPLOITS :

- Shooting fish (and other mobs too) from land.
- Using the LoS (line of sight) bug to fight w/o being harmed.
- Duping items.
- Grouping with people 30 levels higher and using him/her to blast people your own level range on a PvP situation.
- Engaging in PvP combat while one of the parties is out of the political zone BUT within range!!! (go figure that one out)
- Figuring out the spots where a monster gets stuck.

These are serious exploits that need to be fixed ASAP.


PRIORITIES :



I think Funcom should rethink the way they adress issues in this game. IMHO I truly believe that playability should be the MAIN concern before any other known issue, be it an exploit or not.
Basically we are the beta testers here and we are getting our trial by fire at this very moment. We are the ones enduring several problems concerning gameplay. This game it still in its infancy but in the future , WE are likely to become its best advocates/PR's. No ad campaign compares to a satisfied costumer spreading the word!


Here's a list of several known issues that hinder gameplay :


- Memory Leaks. Not possible to play the game w/o rebooting your machine every 2-3 hours (256 megs here)

- Being unable to move near the Whompas durink peak hours, due to lag ( I'm on a T3 line, btw).

- Getting stuck inside mission rooms because of the Hall of Mirrors (resynch) problems. Often times this leads to death.

- Getting a monster to 10% health only to find out it had a"trick" up its sleeve as he unleashed a series of consecutive hits on you, ultimately leading to your death. Lag/Sync again.

- Not being able to get more ammo from a bag/backpack while on a fight.

- Not being able to leave ammo outside of the bag because of it's weight bug.

- Not being able to zone out when under attack.

- Getting fall damage for 256!!! when I jump a tree trunk!!

- Not being able to complete those "bring back" type of missions since many terminals are broken and WONT accept the item.(my bank vault is the living proof of that).

- NCU upgrades cause your NCU readout to display an incorrect number. I was actually called names by a player which I sold a unit to. He zoned for the 1st time and noticed the readout was showing only 6 units when he was supposed to have 12. Took me some time and a lot of patience to explain .


There are many other gameplay issues, but these are good and serve the purpose to ilustrate the state of the game.


CONCEALMENT!


Ok, I'm only lvl 19 and I don't have any cloaking devices or leet form nano. My concealment base is 104. I could get past mobs at ease while doing missions (easisest). Not so while on default dificulty mission, so I used Concealment Expertise to help me move along ( +20 boost). Tried a mission at 75% dificulty but no dice, so I upped it with Shadow Crown and was then able to move undetected past several mobs. I then tried moving the slider all the way to 100% dificulty and went in (187 concealment). Most mobs (all reds) could easily spot me from a mile away. In some cases I would manage to move past a red mob every now and then. That was BALANCED in my opinion. All this was prior to the last patch. As of this writing, I cannot move past a GREY mob on a mission set to easiest. Please, show me the logic on that!!


If something needs to be fixed is the ability to sneak before you zone into the mission area and remain in sneak mode, but able to run at full speed. That's the issue to be addressed, imo. A player in TRUE sneak mode can take 45 minutes to 1 hour to go through a mission zone. In other words , this is THE bug/exploit that should have been looked into. Your "Fix" did nothing but destroy an extremelly interesting profession.


Now, last but not least, a little background history .

I used to play UO and when EQ came out our whole guild migrated to it. We have a total of 67 members,so consider it a medium sized guild if you will. We have endured nerf after nerf for 2 1/2 years (yes, we still play it), but only because there was nothing better than EQ. All in all it was a good place to hang out with our friends. Now we all got excited when the first news of AO came out. Ever since then we have been eagerly following its development and wishing for a better place to be.

Now, whats the purpose of making a definitive move to AO when its bound (seemingly) to become another EQ where developers always look for the easy solution and not the right one?

Verant was not foolish enough to start nerfing before the game was stable and comunities were already formed and had strong bonds!

There are 6 officers of our guild playing/testing AO at the moment. What do we say to our guildies now?

"Hey, it's got better graphics and music than EQ but it's pretty much the same hack 'n slash and, yes they NERF! Oh, and one more thing, its nearly unplayable at the moment due to lag and bugs galore".

Do you really think 67 people will move to a new "world" just because of its snazy graphics and music? We want to be ofered variety. That WAS the main premise of this game.

Yes, 67 people are nothing when you look at the big picture ( aprox. 35,000 players according to Funcom), but it sure adds up and Im absolutely positive many others would rather stay with EQ and WAIT for a better place to become available.DAoC, Shadowbane, Project Entropia come to mind.

We as a guild, have paid over US$ 26,000.00 in fees to Verant over the course of 2 1/2 years. I see no reason why you should get our money if you're not offering what you advocated for the past year.

To the ones calling us whiners I say this :


THEY OPENED A PRECEDENT!!


Agents today. It could have been (and will be in the future) your pet, or your 45m SMG, or that kick arse nuke 'em all nano.


On second thought I think it is good that it happened at such an earlier stage of the game. An eye opener indeed. At least we know where they stand and we can opt to stay or leave. Verant never gave us that option, since nerfs didn't start until the game was well under way and solid groups had been formed by then.
