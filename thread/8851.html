Dynamic Lightening FX please!
Hi!

I don't know whether this idea has been posted yet, but I think it would be cool to add some cool light effects to the game engine.

For instance when u fire your gun at night or cast a nano at night the surrounding area doesn't get any brighter.
Just imagine how cool it would be to lighten up the darkness with explosions, NFs... whatever.
Fighting at night or dark places would be awesome. We all know the effects I'm talking about of other games, e.g. Q3, UT and the likes...

To Funcom: I hope that wouldn't be an impossible change to the gfx-engine. I'm not a programmer myself, but I hope that you guys will add this feature. Btw, this feature should be optional so that low-end systems wouldn't suffer a great breakdown in fps.
