Cooool!
I think this rocks from a PvP standpoint, from an RP standpoint and from every other conceivable standpoint.

AO isn't a carebear game. It does a fantastic job of catering to a carebear audience while remaining decidedly confrontational.

At the moment (pre-pack) the war can't end, becase no-one has anything to lose. I fight, I die, I res, I fight again. I do this until I get bored (well, I don't personally because I'm a 'crat - but you get the idea).

Any RP group can tack on their own reasons for fighting and I say best of luck to them!

But this gives us reason for fighting. Objectives. And with that comes the possibility of loss.

* * *

If the Clans and Omni-Tek were to declare unconditional peace and live together in peace and harmony while carefully tending notum gardens, then I'd quit AO rather quickly.

I'm a big fan of promoting exactly that in-game - peace is a noble cause to strive for. But the irony is that if that goal is achieved, the game becomes extremely boring

So I welcome anything FC throw at us to make that task more challenging (and more realistic).

Hooray!
