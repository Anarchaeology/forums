The Mockers Store Grand Opening - 9/21 20GMT
The Mockers Grand Opening for their new Store this Saturday the 21st, 20:00 GMT till 24:00 GMT!

The Mockers, founders of the popular item order business, is proud to bring you a brand new store!

SPEEDY's!
The Mockers West Athen Pawn and Grid Exchange

We'll be celebrating our Grand Opening Celebration like this:
GridStream Productions' DJ Tristalyn!
Dancing Girls!
And Free Drinks and Food!

Also, we'll have the following contests:
DJ Tristalyn will be asking Trivia of anyone in the store, any correct answers will win one FREE blitz by The Mockers right then and there!
There will be a Raffle that the winner will win one FULL set of Blitzable armor for FREE!
And finally, Every 10th customer will also win a FREE Blitz right then and there, or one free item from what we have in stock!

But, What does Speedy's offer?
We try and sell ANYTHING that we have, however, since it's impossible to have everything, we gaurantee NCU and NCU Belts on a regular basis. Items such as armor, weapons, nanodiscs, jewelry and a wide variety of other items are available, yet we have a harder time keeping them in stock.
However! Should we not have the item you are looking for, we can place an order for you! The order should be available within the next few days of its placement.

As well, Speedy's may be offering some Grid Services, such as:
-Ordering Items in person, and having them delivered as soon as Fixerly possible while you wait.
-On the spot entrance to the Grid.
-Summoned Armors and Weapons for Rent.
-Implant Stripping

Speedy's is conveniently located just EAST of the West Athen Grid Terminals. So, no need to hike kilometers and kilometers, just a short trip through the Grid or Whompas and you can be at Speedy's, Rubi-Ka's finest retailer!

Schedule's are: Again, it's Saturday, the 21st, 20:00 GMT to 24:00 GMT, or 3:00 PM EST to 7:00 PM EST.

Coordinates are: West Athen= 491, 415, the door just EAST of the Grid Terminal.

We hope to see you all soon,
The Mockers

More info at www.mockers.net/speedys (still under construction )
