Announcement from Mayhem
I come before you today to inform you of a great and also sad event. I will have to leave AO for some time due to national service and due to this I will step down as president of Mayhem.
It is not an easy thing to do, but I will not be able to spend enough time in AO to lead our guild.
I have chosen a long time friend in AO (from DRAP days) to take over my postition and I am sure you will recognize him.
The person I have chosen is Beartwo, I thank you for the time we have spent here and wish you well.

/me bows to Mayhem
