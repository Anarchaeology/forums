Give players a reason to play.
I've played for almost a year now, and in that year I've done just about everything worth doing in the game (at least for me). I've roleplayed, done pvp, hunted outdoors until I could predict spawn times and locations for exactly the mob I wanted to kill. I've dressed up my characters in some of the most silly outfits imaginable, just to have soemthing to laugh at.

After all that is done, what's left? Paying $12.95/month to sit around waiting for the next patch, and hope it does something to revive gameplay? I've been doing that for one month now, and I think I'll spend next month's $12.95 on a sandwich and a 12 pack instead. At least that way I'll be getting something for my money.

Until recently, I had plenty of fun playing the game. I found ways around bugs and enjoyed each of my characters. Sure I didn't get any of them to level 200, but I don't have a giant hamster wheel in my living room either. I took a character from the backyards to soloing lifebleeders in 3 days, which told me, I could have gotten level 200, if I had really tried. However, I lost interest in that, and ultimately in the entire game.

So my ultimate suggestion to Funcom would be, to give their players, a reason to play, make the game enjoyable, and not just a money/time sink while you sort out your corperate issues and hope you have enough subscribers with expendable income to just leave their account active while they wait for something to happen. I've seen countless suggestions pass across these boards that would have been viable gameplay adjustments/changes, in-game money/time sinks, added to the social environment of Rubi-Ka, and pretty much every single one of them were ignored or brushed off. You have these messageboards for a reason, to get feedback from your customer base. Listen to them and give them a product they will enjoy, and continue to use.
