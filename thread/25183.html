My suggestions
I am just a newbie both to AO as well as MMORPGs. In fact I have just started playing about a week ago and overall I love the game but..here are my suggestions:

The online character Bios are great i think they would be even better if we could persobalize them and write our own bios/philospy etc. much like the orginazations section.

Currently, I really do not undestand the relavance of the apartment. It would make sense if I could decorate it more and if I could have my bank items available in my apartment as well. For that matter should all apartments come with insurance scanners? all of that would make apartments much more relavant but currently I sleep on the streets of newland, i see no point in wasting load time on an apartment.

I also have a few things that I do not understand about being Nuetral.

Now I am still catching up on the storyline and all but when i signed up to be nuetral I thought the Nuetral zone was going to be a place swarming with Mercenaries and Traders I was hoping that some of the best soldiers would reside in the nuetral zone reason being that they are the best guns for hire. cCaring more about profit than who wins the war.

It would seem to me that since nuetrals have the capabilities to travel to both omni and clan territory why wouldn't both of those parties hire individuals from mercenary clans to covertly strike their enemy?

And speaking of that I think their should be an ability for players,especially agents, to use their nanos to hack the system so they can appear as a clanner when they are omni and vice versa, it would really add to the idea of working behind enemy lines.

I would also like to see guide missions, help this trade caravan get from point A to point B those would make great group missions.

Anyway that is my two cents for now. I love the game it is awesome and I plan to play it for quite some time.
