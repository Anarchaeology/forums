Anarchy Arcanum
Greetings fellow players,

Just wanted to announce the opening of a brand new Anarchy Online site - Anarchy Arcanum, located at http://www.anarchyarcanum.com

Created by two guys who has played since the start, and amazingly still is. Brought on by the creeping feeling that AO is coming into it's right these days made us want to give something back if we could.

All the little things that make AO great - the things you might not know about, the things you might have forgotten, or just the odd little things that you just want to know about or even find funny.

Right now the site is a little empty, but we hope to remedy that in the coming days. Even so, for the launch, we have put up a guide to creating Bronto Hide Armor, a guide to creating Treatment Libraries, a guide on Shadowrift shrines found in missions, and even the start of an extensive look at humanoid mobs and how to identify and slap them around! - All with a touch of humour and spirit.

Hope you all like it!
