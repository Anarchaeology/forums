A long lost song
A tune plays in my head
It fades as I sleep in my bed
Drums
Guitars
And a simple melody
A sweet song of life is what it was to me
Time goes by
My eyes slowly close
The song disappears from my mind
Where it is now
No one knows
Maybe one day the song will return
Until that day I can only sit and yearn
To long for a song
To play my life happy
