The problem with pistols as I understand it only begins to become apparent after lvl 60 or so...

The problem is armor... weapons that hit fast for small amounts of damage (i.e. pistols) are less effective against the ungodly amounts of armor that NPCs (and PCs) have at the higher levels... the result is that you only ever hit for minimum damage.

That's why high level characters prefer weapons that, while very slow, have a very high upper limit to their damage range (Sledgehammers come to mind).

Even the best pistols have a very limited upper range of damage compared to the "big hitters" of the upper QLs.

Sentinels ETE Strike guns are one of the few useable pistols.

Good damage, decent ROF, and reasonable requirements (one of the few that can be realistically multi wielded).



They may be great for you at level 49. Heck, I have a level 40ish pistol Soldier who uses them and is quite effective. The only problem is that when you get higher level (75+), you will start to find that they are not very effective against mobs with high AC.

The sore spot with pistols and most (pretty much all 1H weapons) is that they have low min/max damage. The way armor class absorption works in Anarchy Online makes these quick, low damage weapons all but useless at higher levels when mob AC gets astronomical.

Lets say you're fighting a mob whose AC absorbs 80 damage per hit.
You're using a QL120 Sentinels ETE whose avg damage (before modifiers) is around 75...
The advent beside you is using Rider Execs whose avg damage (before modifiers) is around 160.

When you factor in modifiers, your Pistol will be doing 4-20 damage excluding crits.
When you factor in modifiers, the Riders will be doing 7-100 damage excluding crits.



As you get higher in level this becomes more pronounced.
