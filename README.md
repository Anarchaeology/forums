# Anarchy Online Bulletin Board archive, 2001-2003

A read-only rebuild of Funcom's official Anarchy Online forums from the game's first years, from the beta in May 2001
to the end of 2003, shown the way the forum looked in 2002. Published at https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/,
alongside the [Anarchaeology](https://anarchaeology.github.io/) screenshot gallery.

Everything comes from copies the Internet Archive's [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/) saved:

- Funcom's forum archive (forums-archive.anarchy-online.com), saved in 2025 before it went offline
- pages saved from the live forum itself, mostly in 2001-2004
- the 2001 launch forum (aoforums.funcom.com)

Every thread links to the saved copy it was rebuilt from. The site's [About page](about.html) explains how the
boards are arranged and what didn't survive.

The posts are their writers' own. If you wrote something here and want it taken down, please open an issue.

Not affiliated with Funcom. Anarchy Online is a trademark of Funcom.

## How it's built

`tools/` holds the two programs that turn the saved pages into this site, and the data files that place every
thread on the 2002 board list. See [tools/README.md](tools/README.md).
