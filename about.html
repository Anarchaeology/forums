About this archive

Funcom ran the official Anarchy Online forums from before the game's launch in June 2001. Years later the old forum became a read-only archive at forums-archive.anarchy-online.com, which went offline in 2025. This site rebuilds the forum's first years, from the beta in May 2001 to the end of 2003, from the copies the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine saved, and shows them the way the forum looked in 2002.

Where the threads come from

  • Funcom's forum archive, saved by the Wayback Machine in 2025: 24,014 threads.
  • The live forum: 312 threads the Wayback Machine saved from the forum itself, mostly while it was in use and some years later, that Funcom's archive didn't keep. Pages saved by 2004 keep the signatures people used at the time.
  • The 2001 launch forum (aoforums.funcom.com, frozen in September 2001): 71 threads, from the beta in May 2001 to that September. The Wayback Machine saved very little of it; it had 34,458 threads.

Every thread links to the saved copy it was rebuilt from.

How the boards are arranged

Every thread is shown on the board list of August 2002. Threads from the launch forum, and from the boards Funcom introduced in February 2003, are placed on the 2002 board that matches: the 2003 Atlantean and Rimor "Buzz" boards on Role-Playing, which they replaced; the Profession Academies on their professions; New Arrivals on Beginners' Corner; Knowledge Database on The World of Rubi-Ka; Tech Help on Other Technical Issues; and so on. Shade and Keeper, the professions added with Shadowlands in 2003, have boards of their own. The launch forum's General board was sorted thread by thread.

What didn't survive

  • Most of the launch forum, and some whole boards, which Funcom's archive didn't keep. Their sizes in August 2002: Can't get the game running (889), Other Technical Issues (1,262), Beginners' Corner (1,949), Rubi-Ka 1 Exchange (7,509), Rubi-Ka 2 Exchange (1,446), Player Polls (305), Bulletin Board (200), Adventurer (1,177), Agent (783), Bureaucrat (997), Doctor (687), Enforcer (1,228), Engineer (945), Fixer (2,183), Martial Artist (1,255), Meta-Physicist (1,130), Nano-Technician (1,382), Soldier (1,001), Trader (752), Test Server Bugs (199), Test Server Discussions (245), Non-AO Related (308).
  • Much of 2003. After the forum was reorganized in February 2003, Funcom's archive kept only part of the new boards.
  • Pages here and there that the Wayback Machine never saved; threads say where.
  • Replies written after 2003 to these threads are left out.
  • Pictures and sites. Pictures in posts link to the Anarchaeology gallery when it has them, or else to the Wayback Machine's saved copy; pictures it never saved are left as plain text. Links to other sites go to the Wayback Machine as of the post's date, though it didn't save every page. Links to the item databases of the time (AODB, Anarchy Mainframe, antiguardians.org, HackersQuest) go to the same item on auno.org, which still uses the game's item numbers; it shows each item as it is today. Links to characters' pages on Funcom's site go to people.anarchy-online.com, which shows the characters that still exist. The archive's signatures, from later years, are left out.

Notes

  • The off-topic boards (Non-AO Related, and in 2003 Reet's Retreat) were read thread by thread: the ones about the game are here, on Non-AO Related; people's personal chatter is left out on purpose.
  • Times are GMT, as the forum showed them in 2002.
  • Under each name, as on the forum: the member's avatar, the last one the saved pages show for them; "Registered", the month they joined as the forum showed it, or the month of their first post here where no saved page shows it; and "Posts", how many of their posts are in this archive. That is fewer than the forum counted, since some boards and threads didn't survive. Each name opens the member's profile, with the threads they started.
  • Funcom's archive put runs of underscores in front of many names ("_____________Garnock"); they're shown without, as the forum showed them.

Removal

The posts are their writers' own. If you wrote something here and want it taken down, open an issue.
